Greetings pilgrims, space-travellers and fellow cult members. Hope you're all having a nice day.

Recently, I've begun making music with a drummer and found myself in a new jam space. Adorning the walls are plenty of classic rock and music posters. In a strange moment of serendipity, One of them stood out as something that's pretty familiar to all of us here. Here's an alternate version of the image, without the mandolin in the way. At first glance, it'd be easy to dismiss as just coincidence that a similar symbol was used but after learning that Lazlow had a deadhead phase (mentioned during his appearance on Dan Soder's podcast), I suspect there's a little more to this.

Despite my hippy proclivities, I've never really listened to any of the grateful Dead, but seeing a symbol reminiscent of the mural, I decided to look a little further into the grateful Dead. One of the first things I often ask about anything mystery related is "what does that name mean". The Grateful Dead, although originally named warlock, used a term from folklore.). the folklore of the term is often related to a traveler who finds a body that didn't receive proper burial. The traveler will assist in having the body buried and will be rewarded in some fashion, whether monetarily or through some guidance. Interestingly enough, this kind of coincides with some of what I see of the i8 mystery.

Whether it's Merle or one (or all) if the i8 bodies, I've felt that a spirit of some sort kinda joined the "source" and has been providing us clues in various ways. I feel this soul might also be chiliad8888 who sent the email on GTA IV to Niko, but I think it was intended for us - the player. On the site linked by chiliad8888, we can also find phone cheats, which on it's own doesn't seem to link to infinity but in rdr1 we can find a cheat written on a wall in a similar fashion to the writings at Merle's place - "I don't understand imnfinity". This suggests to me, where it's a cheat, engrained into the games code, that whoever that soul was, is now part of the "source" and may have reached out to us as chiliad8888. Maybe it's even Francis Sinclair from rdr2, considering his mural has a symbolic version of a "godhead" - a term also used by Chiliad8888.

Aside from the name "the grateful Dead", the band itself has an interesting rabbit hole relating to potential links with the CIA's mkultra program and it's use of LSD, the hippy movement and by proxy the whole Laurel canyon propaganda conspiracy that involves a bunch pro-lsd bands and movies (such as "the trip" and "easy Rider" both starring The Truth's voice actor, Peter Fonda). The Grateful Dead's own sound engineer, Owsley Stanley also manufactured a metric fuck tonne of the LSD that was passed around back in the day. this mini-doc goes into better detail than I can possibly provide. The dead, whether as a joke or a low key admittance has a song lyric, ""I'm Uncle Sam, that's who I am / Been hiding out in a rock and roll band" from the song U S. blues. Everything mentioned above here, also seems to have echoes found within the chiliad mystery and conspiracies within the world of GTA.

Anyway, that's about all I've got for today.
