Hey,

I am posting today to request a simple mod that could make our lives much easier in our hunt. I came across a trainer on the TransmetTeam website, where it has an option to check if "mysterious objects" has been loaded into the map. These objects are "prop_alien_egg_01" , "p_secret_weapon_02" , "prop_artifact_01", "prop_asteroid_01" and "hei_prop_heist_plinth".

The problem I have with this is that you have to load the trainer and select the button every time you want to check.

My idea to improve this mod would be to have a simple bit of text in the corner of our screens at all times that says something like "Mysterious objects loaded: Yes/No". This way we don't have to load the trainer every time we think we may be in the right circumstance, and we could get a live, constant check on if these objects are loaded.

Hopefully someone has the time and ability to create this, as I think it would be a great addition to our hunting tools.
