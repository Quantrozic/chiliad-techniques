[UPDATED 01/09/2014]

Compared to a few weeks ago there have been a lot of people that have turned into skeptics and they’re actually getting up-voted now on reddit, I see this as positive as I have been severely down-voted in the past for expressing my criticism to certain people’s findings.

However a lot of people want to start an argument by questioning why they are still hanging around. The way I see it, a discussion needs different sides. If everyone would just agree that every theory has some validity, albeit small, this mystery will never get solved, as the chance of getting sidetracked becomes greater. I find it weird that people are almost offended by people who claim there is nothing more to find (at the moment), it’s just a video game when it all comes down to it. If you feel protecting of ”the hunt” because you think you’ve spent a lot of time trying to find something, then please realize that you’ve done it of your own accord and that it doesn’t give you the right to just attack people who think differently. I’m sure most of the skeptics are not discouraging others from looking. It’s more about sharing an updated opinion or point of view.

Here is mine:

Let’s assume that the mystery is done for a moment,
then what are we to understand from this easter egg?
It does seem to imply more than it actually provides or rewards the player with.

The obvious "reward", in this case, are the three UFOs that appear upon 100% completion. There are no rewards that the player can actually interact with in a satisfying way. You can just look at them, which is a bit underwhelming considering the smaller details that surround them.

The first evidence at the existence of aliens in the game is an actual frozen alien in the prologue mission.

Given that, it could be hinting that Michael’s abduction is actually real. Although the ”reefer mission” would discredit that as he is under the influence at both times. But he is unconscious during the abduction, which could mean that WE are seeing the aliens rather than that he is.

And another find is obviously the mural at the top of Mt. Chiliad and the first UFO subsequently.

There are also FIB agents appearing in suspicious locations: by a satellite antenna and by a communications building near Humane Labs. With this going on, considering the eventual appearance of the UFOs, it gives the implication of some sort of story, a narrative, that is developing. It seems to imply that the FIB is surveilling and gathering information about the UFOs.

What is confusing though is that two of the UFOs actually have FIB logos on them. This seems to imply that the FIB isn't discovering these UFOs, but they are instead testing these aircrafts and are monitoring them from these locations (they own them). The Fort Zancudo UFO could be a military controlled aircraft and is instead being monitored by them, as it is right above their base. Also, the fact that it has writing in English on it further strengthens the implication that all these UFOs are man-made and not actual Alien UFOs. The Crashed UFO could be a former test of the same nature that failed and they are unable to salvage it as it would be too big to transport without looking suspicious.

But back to the matter of what this rewards us with exactly, other than the (vague) implication that the in-game government is performing secret tests with flying saucers. Well what says that there might be more is the actual frozen alien, as it confirms the existence of aliens in the GTA (HD) universe. The flying saucers are controlled by man but the government could either have salvaged these aircrafts and then put the FIB logo on them or they have been built after they salvaged an original, thus the testing. It could also be that they’re preparing to defend themselves against an alien invasion (a la The X-Files). The only thing to understand is that aliens are to be considered ”canon” or ”real" because of the prologue mission.

I won’t go into the game files that much, as I believe an easter egg is meant to be found by actually playing the game without scrambling around looking for it in code. But the findings of the Alien Egg and the Meteorite implies that there might be more to come. Especially considering that there is an egg on the mural as well.

Looking at the mural, you can interpret the bottom three images as signs or a sequence of events that will unravel for us. The first part being the UFOs, the second the egg and the third the jetpack flying man.
The lines are then interpreted like these are all connected events and should be considered as being part of the same easter egg. The ultimate reward is unclear. It could be all these events put together, a jetpack or something else.

I believe this will be continued with future DLC and/or updates. I do not think however it will be the main story or theme of that DLC, like it’s going to be titled ”ALIEN INVASION DLC” or something of the sort. But instead I think it this will be something that develops in the background, like it has up 'til now, and that it's definitely something that should be checked after each DLC and update. Are the saucers still there/in the same place? Are the FIB agents still there/in the same place? I think that if we find these things to have changed, then we finally have more to the easter egg. So I believe this to be an on-going narrative easter egg. Therefore I think it’s good that people are finding these little suspicious details, as it may point us in the right direction in the future, but right now it's relevance could be escaping us. Therefore I also think there is some truth in the argument that this is it. That doesn’t mean there won’t be more to come though.

Thank you for reading and I would like to add that I do hope for this mystery to continue, as it has been very exciting so far.

(Please note that I’ve tried to use the word ”implication" as much as possible. If you think that I’ve stated some speculation as fact, please know that this was not my intention. I don’t claim to know more than anyone else, but that I am seeing things through different eyes.)
