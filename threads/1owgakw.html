Hey guys. After getting 100% in GTA V enhanced edition I’ve found out something that is interesting.

Originally, on older gen consoles you would find scientists + FIB agents in suits at the radars near the prison.

Now you only see scientists instead of the scientists + FIB agents and the FIB unmarked car as well.

Before you complete the game at 100% on the enhanced edition you can see only one scientist roaming around the radar area. Essentially just taking data and going to each radar.

The interesting part is that now after 100% you can see 3 scientists and two white cars spawn in at around 10am. One car being a regular white car and the other being a white van.

After inspecting these scientists they have badges what seem to be from St. Rytkonen University. These are the same badges Humane Labs employees wear. This concludes that these scientists are in fact from Humane Labs as well conducting experiments.

If you guys know anything more about St. Rytkonen Univeristy and its affiliation with Humane Labs that would be great.

There also seems to be a lot of cross collaboration between Fort Zancudo, the IAA, FIB, Humane Labs, and Merryweather. Perhaps this is why you see the UFO’s only at the very end besides that one UFO in the water at Paleto Bay. To further add on it seems Rockstar also adds more clues not just in story mode but in online as well which they’ve showcased the Fort Zancudo underground lab as well and the lab also has a closed off prototyping door that you can’t enter. If you open the map while in the lab it shows that the lab is inside of Mt. Josiah which is the smaller mountation close to Mt. Chilliad.

This is not a conclusion rather an observation. Perhaps this could help unlock something later on.

Feel free to share any of your thoughts down below in the comments.
