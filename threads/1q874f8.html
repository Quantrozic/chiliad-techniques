I have always had a niggle in my head about the coincidence of the eye at top of the mountain in the Cairngorms in scotland. In the Nevis Range there is a cable car to the top of Aonoch Mor. This is where many world mountain bike championships have been held and the launch point is a huge wooden eye. i have tried overlaying the mountain bike trail from Aonoch Mor onto mount chiliad but no dice.

I know it's a coincidence but it drives me mad! Just wanted to share so others could feel my irritation haha.
