Dial 1-999-Guard me is a number off a Merryweather security patrol car from the docks near the sub under construction, and a ~55second tune will play. I know there is another couple other phone numbers that are out there that are the same tune as this. Has anyone else looked into what it possibly references? Its just a organ instrument(to me it sounds like one), also the tune seems to repeat several times and you cannot do much in that much time.

Could this be a pass code for somewheres?

The other number 1-999-chromer has a recording to view their website for more information, if you search eyefind for the chromer you get a list of companies from southern SA auto, merryweather and more as a result. Most times I search stuff and get no results but this word search delivers.

Have you tried searching yourdeadfamily.com for names of people in the game? trying Franklin Clinton at ages 25 and 26 are completely different. Might be some interesting results with some variations.

Truly alot of red herrings.
