My search for a secret island has only begun. I've started using all three characters at once because of the three flowers in the drawing and it also can't hurt for them to tag along. Only problem is I can't autosave so each time I fail I have to start from the beginning. Get them together and wait for the sun. Very annoying.

I've learned that the path of light spawns at 5am for the sunrise and 7pm for sunset. Still undetermined which I should do so I've just been switching around. All signs point to sunrise but the camp overlooks the sunset so it's driving me crazy.

It takes exactly one hour to hit the barrier in the east, leaving from the lighthouse in a Fogger. Not sure about west because the path vanishes before the barrier arrives.

I'm beginning to wonder what shape the barrier is. I've always assumed it was a circle but I've been adding points of interest each time I hit it and they are all over the place, seems to be a very jagged pattern.

Earlier I decided to sky fall over and over and found myself way beyond the barrier. I reached a point where the screen went dark and I spawned in Sandy Shores. The type of spawn that occurs when falling through the map.

I want to believe this island exists. Searching for it has rekindled my excitement for this mystery. I have a lot more to do but if possible, can anyone map the barrier using hacks or anything on PC that we can't do on consoles?

Good day and Kifflom.
