Anyway, there is a possibility that even my own current theory is already explored (huge chance of course), but I think I am on to something here but I need your help. I can't do this alone.

so, here it goes:

  1. One thing that always caught me in the mural was the part left in the middle. NOT the ufo icon, NOT the jetpack, but the combination of lines in the middle. because of the interconnections, I always assumed that these lines stand for ways to go, you can turn eather left or right at some point, and something (your choice) will get you somewhere.
  2. There are motorcycles on top of Chiliad.
  3. What most people seem to forget is that the mural has 1 icon on top, which represents the UFO hovering above, OR the actual red ufo icon beneth the wooden view thing on the top of chiliad.

Now for my theory:

*I believe that you need to stand on top of Mount chiliad at 3 am when its thundering, directly above the red ufo symbol, so the ufo appears. The UFO is your "start" mark, like: "GO!"

After that, you need to get a motorcycle (if you save before 3 am the motorcycles will dissapear AND the thunderstorm will be gone when you relead)

Once you get the motorcycle, you will need to run down Mount chiliad in a specific way. Once you reach the bottom unharmed and in a specific time, you will get the reward you have driven to.*

Now, to support my theory:

  • mount chiliad has multiple roads of the mountain, which are NOT on the map. One example is a path which will take you from the top to alamo sea/grapesead, and multiple others. These roads are also interconnected, which means you can go from one to another.
  • the glyps only say: Night (moon), Rain/thunder (rain) and the one that for now didnt made sense is the mountain one. However, this symbol could mean you need to get eather up or down the mountain (there is clearly a road on that symbol to/from the mountain)

I think the challenge is to go to a specific place, using a specific route, starting on 3 am while its thundering on mount chiliad, using one of the motorcycles provided on top, in a specific time

However, I tested this myself and kept dieing, its a challenge alright... (somehow i want to test this without invicibility cheat), also, I figured there are lots of different routes you can take with different outcomes.

However, make sure you save the game at 1 am or something so you have plenty of time to reinit the thunderstorm cheat (if you save/load it wont be thundering hard is my experience) and walk away and comeback for the motorcycles to respawn.
