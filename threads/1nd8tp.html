We discovered already that ELF stands for Extremely Low Frequency.

Some interesting things to note about these waves (taken from wikipedia):

  • "ELF radio waves are generated by lightning and natural disturbances in Earth's magnetic field, so they are a subject of research by atmospheric scientists"
  • "ELF waves can penetrate seawater, so the US and Russian militaries have used ELF transmission facilities to communicate with their submerged submarines."
  • "Some radio hams record ELF (or even lower) signals from very large homemade antennas, and play them back at higher speeds to catch natural fluctuations in the Earth's electromagnetic field. Increasing the playback increases the pitch, so that it is brought into the audio frequency range."
  • "They have also been tentatively identified on Saturn's moon Titan."

How could all this relate to the Mt. Chiliad Mystery?

  • ELF waves are referenced in the church sign in connection with 'the mountain'...Mt Chiliad most likely
  • Lightning naturally generates these waves...lightning is seen striking a mountain in the Mt. Chiliad Mural
  • Stormy weather has already triggered the arrival of this UFO
  • Submarines and other underwater activity were a big selling point of GTA 5
  • One of the UFOs is known to be sunken underwater near Paleto Bay
  • Saturn has a moon named Titan, where ELF waves have been detected
  • Quote from Titan's wiki: "It has been suggested that life could exist in the lakes of liquid methane on Titan, just as organisms on Earth live in water."
  • Fort Zancudo has a giant cargo plane called The Titan
  • The Titan is modeled after the C-130 Hercules
  • The Hercules has many uses, including "scientific research support, weather reconnaissance"
