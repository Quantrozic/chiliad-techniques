Canon Getaways: Could They Play a Role? (The Official Post on Canon Getaways)

So as most of us know, nations have been raging in major debates since 2013’s release of Grand Theft Auto V. One of the most major flavors of debate here have been on the topic of the classic question of the Canon Getaway.

To provide an example: in the known-&-favored “Dead Man Walking”, once Michael leaves the morgue thru the window, he is presented with a choice of two cars to use in getaway. Now, whether this choice has any impact is a debate for a different day. The fact of the matter is, once the choice is made, it’s fairly obvious by observing the dev-made routes, and practicing attachment to the path of least resistance, that the canon getaway in this mission most likely involved taking the train track tunnel to the LS Customs on the eastern part of the map and getting a respray.

Now, that one’s much more easy to determine than the one in Hood Safari.

Hood Safari, in fact, is the primary mission which led me towards committing to the creation of this very thread (which might I mention, truly is indeed the one and/or only official thread of this most heavily debated topic).

The reason for this is that the ending of Hood Safari presents the player with a FOUR-WAY CHOICE. One of the only of its kind inside the whole entire game. The developers are watching us and laughing their asses off over the fact that here we are discussing and attempting to decipher our OWN golden path, without making any mention, or taking any notice, of this very notion.

So I present to you the discussion point of the day: which “Hood Safari” get away do you hold to be canon? Which one do you guess to be?

  1. Be Trevor, escape with Lamar
  2. Be Trevor, escape with Franklin
  3. Be Franklin, escape with Lamer
  4. Be Franklin, escape with Trevor
  5. Escape alone

Of these, the escape route for 2 and 4 doesnt require you to kill any cops, which I find to be more realistic and fitting. At the same time, the dialogue with Lamar is in my opinion better, and provides a logical reason for Trevor to hang out with Lamar. If with Franklin, Lamar will talk about how he likes Trevor. If with Trevor, well then the two of them have a bonding experience.

So it’s a tough call. Something else I’ve noticed is that the 1 and 3 escape route , when you go up the road after reaching the top of the hill, there will always be 3 low rider type cars in the parking lot, all parked in a peculiar way as if there was a car meet going on there. I wonder if this is scripted? Is it only like that in this mission? Was that supposed to be where MC Clip and his crew were going to ride to, and was that going to be a part of the music video they were shooting?

Anyways, which of the four escape routes and dialogues do you think is most canon. And why?
