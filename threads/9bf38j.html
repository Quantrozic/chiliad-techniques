Anyone here familiar with LA?
I ask because the set-up at the Galileo observatory raises some questions for me. The real Griffiths observatory in LA features an armillary sphere and sundial on its lawn. The one in-game is pretty close to the real one except the sundial is on the north side of the obelisk, which is strange because they could have easily put it in the correct place. I'm wondering why they would do this, why go to so much effort then change this? The only answers I can think of is to allow for the seam/lines they have running along the lawn, or to put the sundial into shade for a period as a way to indicate a certain time (I have a vague idea that one of the elements of the alien code could be indicating this, which is about 1330 to 1400 each day).
And that got me wondering, are there any other landmarks, which are based on real life locations, that have a strange and unnecessary change? Not obvious shit like changing the Hollywood sign to Vinewood, but weird ones like the example above which have no obvious reason.
Just thinking about ways to 'reverse engineer' hints on what needs to be done/triggered.
