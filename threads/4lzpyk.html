Most of us have heard some dirty bum slur something about aliens at one time or another. Since NPCs have such varied dialogue, and much often goes unheard, I figured I'd dig around for homeless NPC audio clips.

In all, I found thirteen of these NPCs. All have either "skidrow", "tramp", or "trampbeac" in the file names. I went through all the audio, extracted any odd or interesting clips, then merged them all into individual sound files. (One for each NPC)

1) A woman who is convinced that she is Kraff.

2) Hard to understand this woman. Whales and UFOs.

3) Balloon animals everywhere!

4) This lady mentions abduction, has a long laugh after saying "Kifflom" (NOTE - The abduction line is one of the responses for being carjacked. I have no idea why, but all vagrants have car related dialogue.)

5) Be careful, they're watching!

6) The crazy cat lady.

7) United Negro Pizza Fund.

8) Fear of Zombies.

9) A pro draft hippy.

10) Alien Prophecies / Ancient Astronauts.

11) Sounds like this guy is a cannibal.

12) Veteran with a chip in his brain.

13) Severe Coulrophobia. (Fear of clowns)
