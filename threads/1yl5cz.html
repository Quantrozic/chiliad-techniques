It is pretty clear by now that finding the key to this mystery cannot be easily stumbled upon, or else it would have happened just by chance. However, there are so many permutations of factors that could be involved (e.g. time, location, weather, etc.) that it is hard to rule out completely anything that's been tried because maybe we didn't try it with the right combination of factors.

This brings me to my longshot idea. I am going to do all searching now with one other character via the hang out feature. I thought of this in that it would be interesting if one of the required factors to trigger an event was to "Take Two," after the name of Rockstar's parent company. Also, there seems to be little downside here. I can just imagine seeing the other character opening up some previously unopenable area.

That also brings me to a second, related idea. Perhaps "Rockstar North" will have something to do with solving the mystery, via a combination of finding the right rock (mountain) and then traveling north to a particular celestial body or star. I haven't fully fleshed this one out though.
