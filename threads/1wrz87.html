So there's been some discussion about the Paleto Bay sign that says:

JD O
19390
J6 JEFF
FIGO
%0Y

This has been brought up by a musician called Jeffrey Jackson, on twitter. His Twitter username is @Jeffj6 and he has been registered with twitter for a long time. He posted a picture of what he claims was himself as an NPC in the game, the sign related to him and also gave a clue as to this online tool being used to decipher the message.

So we don't know the keyword yet, but through process of elimination I have narrowed down 12 possible translations.

Before I post I shall say that I cannot figure out the last letter (Y) so I will leave it as a (-) to indicate it's unknown. Also it seems as though the numbers are meant to stay the same, as well as the % sign.

So have a read and tell me if any of these make sense to you or you can relate to any of these?

TO A 19390 T6 TREE EZRA %0-

HE A 19390 H6 HISS SODA %0-

HE A 19390 H6 HISS SOFA %0-

HE A 19390 H6 HISS SOYA %0-

BE A 19390 B6 BUTT TAXI %0-

BE A 19390 B6 BUTT THAI %0-

BE I 19390 B6 BASS SOFI %0-

BE I 19390 B6 BOSS SOFI %0-

BE I 19390 B6 BUTT TAXI %0-

so yeah, there we go guys, obv it's not actual proof it's just speculation, let me know what you think as this took me absolutely ages and just thought I'd share with you all.
