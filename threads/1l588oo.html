My views on the meaning of Lord Zapho

So one of the deepest and most hidden details on this game is the mention of Lord Zapho, whom we assume refers to perhap and aliens of sort, well I think it's meaning could be more storyline-related, and hermosa why:

It is heavily implied that Zapho was named and is praised by the Altruists, and well in the camp itself there are a lot of indication that he is seen as a deity of sorts, even when not named directly, but the correlation can be made by the symbol that seems to represent Zapho (The All seeing eye/U.F.O that is at the top of the mural).
So if we assume that Symbol is meant to be the representation or Zapho, and perhaps Altruist made contact with "it" through seeing it or hearing it on a radio, then we can make the connection that the Zapho they believe to be a sort of God, is simply the U.F.O owned by the FIB.

And that's where I got the theory, that perhaps Zapho is a reference or symbolism to the same storyline, because when the Bum calls us "Zapho agents" on the Repossesion mision, instead of random rambling it could be a foreshadowing to us becoming technically "FBI agents" during a great portion of the game, helping the FBI as informal agents, the same FBI who would be seen as Lord Zapho because of it's hidden (and presumably made for surveilance UFO).

Another HUGE reinforcement to this I believe may be the mission "Eye in the sky" (the name itself has implications), and it's on this mission where we get the second Zapho mention, which is by a bum who is talking in his sleep about him "getting in his glands" and asking him to get out of his ass, while all of this is fitting, we could even go further and correlate it to how the IAA was willing to do that to Mr K, and given that we don't treat him nice either under the FIB either, maybe we can assume the FIB also does those sorts of things. (Though I admit this later anus connection is more of a stretch).

But anyways I think I can resume my assesment of this, as Zapho being a symbolism and misinterpretation of government surveillance, being physically portrayed as the flying saucer of the FIB.

So when the hobo calls us Zapho agents, perhaps it's just a way of calling us the players on how we actually end up being Zapho agents by aiding the FIB.

It could go even deeper with all the anal and probe stuff having an even deeper (lol) meaning about maybe agencies like the IAA or FIB torturing or experimenting with hobos, via drugs and all of that which I believe is plausible, but that would be just speculation without more proof from what I've gathered.

I wanna know what you think about the way I approached this which I believe is somewhat original and fits very well, even more when we take into account the theories about the mural being a representation of the storyline as well (tho i'm not sure if I agree with that or not), but if that were the case then Zapho as the FIB would fit like a glove on top of it.
