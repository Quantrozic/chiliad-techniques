I’ve been looking into the DDH mural after reading posts by a certain controversial anonymous Reddit account, and after looking through hundreds of globe charts, I found the style hidden away in a place that is much more interesting than standard geography. It was used by CBS NEWS (famously has an eye as its logo) and it’s called the Cronkite Map. It was featured behind Walter Cronkite and used to refer to geographical locations as he delivered the news, as well as being featured in the intro to the show. After Cronkite left, CBS replaced the map in the intro with a map made of dots that look suspiciously like the Cliffford Dots on the mural. In the intro, the CBS logo also features a circle grid style globe (left “Internet” globe looking icon in red mural) as the pupil of the eye.
