HIPPIE CAMP INVESTIGATION- NEW CLUE!!! TRIGGERED DIALOGUE?

Documented under:Moon-Phase Condition Testing

I am sorry if this discovery is not new, but I've googled it, and couldn't find anything about it. I've read quite a bit about the Chilliad mystery at this point, and I believe I may have stumbled across something new about the nature of the mystery, these five months later. I'm also sorry-this is my first reddit post... If I'm doing something wrong, I hope the people that read this will make sure it becomes an up voted thread.

Recently my investigation of the FIB conspiracy (I refuse to call it "Chilliad Mystery", it's a bad name for this investigation) led me to the hippie camp. Many of my search attempts lately have been online. I've noticed that there are a few interesting differences between the online and story maps (online bans you from certain areas, could the reverse be true in conditional events?) . Last night I decided to investigate the hippie camp, because of all the characters in-game that might know something we don't they are certainly the most approachable and OBSERVABLE.

I figured the best way to learn for myself what was going on there is to really start to study the game and the design of NPCs. If ever there was a time to profile NPCs, why not start with the characters that have taken to living in the desert out of sheer belief in aliens. Honestly I'd never really taken the time to watch and wait for many things to happen in game. To really get into it, I dressed up in all Beach Bum gear, to go "undercover". It got me thinking about how clothing hasn't played much of a part of the conditional requirements we theorize about. What if the theoretical conditions aren't just location, time, and action= jetpack. If this mystery is worth a damn, we haven't even scratched the surface of what it is. What I discovered today, is that dressing like a bum in online mode and staying around the camp most of the time for a few days makes them all chill around me. When I show up with Franklin dressed like MIB and Chop in the Bravado, a lot of the hippies are hostile. As my online character, I have to pretty much bump into them for any of them to be aggressive to me. I'm not sure if I'm onto something or not. Didn't test other conditions, but what I did find was exciting.

At about 2am (didn't check what day it was in game, or what part of the lunar cycle it was) I was about to back my hippie paradise van (free w/beach bum pack rofl) up against the dead tree to see if I could pick up any weird signals on Rebel Radio. As I was making the rounds around the camp to maybe pick up any new dialogue (part of noticing out of place things is learning the normal routine of hippie camp AI), I noticed a blonde, bearded hippie walking into the Grand Senora Desert. I followed. After a few minutes, when approaching Sandy Shores, he takes a phone call and starts talking to a friend about some crazy stuff he saw in a documentary, and that there's an alien landing strip out in the desert. And he says his friend should "come by later" to check some of it out. He hangs up and all of a sudden I'm feeling like I discovered one of the conditional elements of the mystery. He walks to the nearest Quick Stop and by now it's morning. I'm waiting for him to get picked up or something. I've never seen this. Unsure if anyone has documented seeing NPC's that normally are known for staying put on their turf, just walking off, having a non-pointless phone call, and stopping at another spot. I tried to trigger the next event, hid from him, went right up to him, called my mechanic to have my paradise nearby in case he got in a car... Then I went on the roof of the Quik Stop and he disappeared. Now that I've followed a couple more of the hippie NPCs I've noticed that if you follow them far from their spawn point, they disappear when you look away from them. Following a character that's running away from havoc you caused will likely just end with them eventually running into traffic several minutes later in the chase. But I discovered MORE unique phone messages by following the same guy the next time I saw him.

The next day when I returned to the camp I saw him again. This time he ran out of the camp. I chased him, but realized he wasn't running from me. When I ran in front of him he still looked back at whatever he was (glitch or not) running from. Eventually, he slowed to a walk, and took another phone call. This time he was more paranoid. He was talking about how he suspected the whole network had been "compromised" and that he felt he was being watched at that very minute. He hung up and ran into traffic, instantly dying. I was even ready to follow the paramedics, but they just left him dead on the scene.

I didn't see him for days (in game). Later, I saw his avatar in three different spots in the camp simultaneously. I've spent basically all day in the camp online and comparing it to story mode. What's interesting about online is while there are many blonde hippies that look alike, I'm pretty sure the guy I'd been tailing is unique to online mode, I have lots of pictures of the spawned hippies in both modes, and I think he only exists online. This could be wrong, I haven't had time to disprove anything, only observe. The three blonde clones of who I now call CODENAME WEAVER (because he looks like this guy I know Weaver) eventually disappeared with the rest of the Hippies around camp bed time. But one new one remained in the back. He mentioned that government unleashed "the artifact" to a girl he was talking to (something I've heard another character say since). Then he took out his phone again, this time talking in the camp. He says something like "So I met up with this guy-" and then almost glitch-like he stops talking and puts the phone away.

Later I heard a different NPC, a glasses wearing nerdy hippie talk about a secret meeting, and that they shouldn't talk about it over the phone.

Over the course of the next few days in game, I didn't really progress with hearing any new phone calls. In the nights leading up to the full moon, the camp was almost and one one night completely deserted. I felt like I missed the clue where a meeting was taking place or something. Anyway, I have lots of pics, but don't know how to post them here. I'm lazy, and they're all inconclusive. I have pics of blonde guys, and dudes walking into the desert. You either believe my story about the unique, seemingly progressive phone calls, or not. It really seemed like the idea of trailing NPCs is new. If anyone can confirm that they'd heard these random calls made before and I just happened to hear them on a weird trailing mission, then maybe it's nothing. But I believe there is a sequence of paranoid phone calls to be heard and they may lead us somewhere to a scripted event in multiplayer. Like a crate drop, only it'll be a jetpack drop. Or maybe just the next clue in the puzzle.

ANYWAY. Maybe all this is just another dead end or interesting mystery. But my main thing is, this game rewards thinking like an undercover. Think about what the online lets us do. Think about how with moderating, and updates and content we don't know about, it may be ONLY online that we can solve the mystery. Who the hell has done extensive online experimenting at the Zancudo bunker? Who's thought about how dropping cigarettes, wearing flapper dresses, or using broken bottles could affect the outcomes of certain scenarios. Maybe we should be looking at unique scripted events in NPCs. Think like an in-world secret agent, not like a jet-pack hungry gamer. That is how we figure it out. I considered keeping this to myself and solving the mystery with this in mind. But I spent the second half of my investigation today realizing that it will take too long for me to investigate the whole damn map by myself.

I've discovered some other things too, but need more time to draw conclusions. Basically though, I think we're forgetting that this is not really Aliens we're fearing. It's an FIB conspiracy. I believe the glyph may be a plan of attack on the city, the government, and a plan for how to keep people safe inside the mountain (or possibly another stronghold). All great mysteries have a big twist. We've already seen the FIB twist on the UFOs. I think the upcoming DLC might not be an "invasion" at all. We may eventually be dealing with what some call the apocalypse. But more on that later, once I investigate more.
