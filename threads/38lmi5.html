I realize there are a lot of posts on the forum about the famous hamburgers sign already -- but I've been thinking about it -- not only does the sign have a little mysterious code attached to it, but it also DOES look like the "eye" in the mural.

maybe the mural has multiple meanings -- what we have already discovered, AND another meaning -- the first more obvious meanings we have already deduced, about the UFOs and characters and such, would be the FIRST place we look, and thus throw us off from actually solving the mystery.

obviously whoever designed this put a LOT of care and thought into the mysteries of this game. I think, together these two mysterious signs show us sort of a 'key' to unlocking the rest! while at the same time, leaving us wondering about each individually -- and having the UFO on the mountain, with a picture of a UFO drawn on the mural, may be there to detract from the true purpose and meaning so that we stay in the dark.

think about where the 'eye' is located, and how the lines stem out -- the first, obvious assumptions are some kind of map or electrical system -- and I think these DO apply somehow, im not sure how.

biggest thing about it is, I think we may be missing the point and the map may be related to the secrest of chiliad, BUT NOT DIRECTLY RELATED TO CHILIAD - I think its related more to the hamburgers sign!

basically my idea is that the hamburger sign IS the eye, the things drawn around the eye are also around the hamburger sign, and the line is coming up off the mountain -- like a sign would come off the ground.

I think somehow, lines and X's may lead us to something to help decode the sign maybe, and lead further into the mystery. I dont yet know how.

I do know, that in several places in this game, there are pictures representing other areas in game -- like at the art museum, there is a gallery outside, that has been investigated on youtube that shows artifacts that directly relate it to other nearby areas -- may also be a part of this (missing letters are involved in a motel sign if your into that)

but I really think the hamburgers sign is something big. I dont know how to decode it -- Ive been struggling with it myself. but it must mean something -- it absolutely cannot be gibberish. and with a puzzle THAT difficult, I dont think there is just some silly encoded message.

why put a professional level, real life cryptography technique to use in a video game? one that not even 1% of the smartest of us have been able to crack -- probably more than several hundred or thousand players?it seems too much -- unless the developers left it in for one specific person only to ever be able to read.

and remember, this is a complicated mystery -- with likely a complicated answer. they specifically disabled us from reading into any code to stop us from understanding this to begin with, and that DRIVES me to have a NEED to figure it out -- almost like im being held hostage by the developers. I just FEEL like I have to know whats in here. Like the day it all comes together a huge relief and excitement will draw over me.

I dont know what it means, but its GOT to be something important. I'll be very depressed if it turns out there is a cryptographer named Jeff who told them the algorithm and had them put it in game just to see if any one could crack it...
