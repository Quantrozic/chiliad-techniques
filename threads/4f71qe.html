Pescado Azul - A Familiar Pattern.

Once again, as I was organizing my extracted textures for a different post, I noticed a detail in one I have previously missed.

The texture is the signs for the shops Pescado Azu (no wiki entry) and Gutter & Blood.

They are located on Strawberry Ave, and are bordered by the FIB/IAA Plaza, Legion Square Park, and Textile City. (marked with a red X)

I had seen this texture a few times before going through the files. Something seemed odd about it, so I saved it in my "review folder", today I realized what it was.

Those bubbles create a pattern very similar to the X's on our mural.

Everything that follows this point is just observations and speculation, but I'm curious what connections other hunters might make.

Since I wasn't very familiar with the location, I decided to go have a look around. Turns out this is the building with the "Vote for Nobody" graffiti.

Going back to the files, I found the graffiti, and it turns out that it is a separate transparent model, very much like the "Foreclosure" decal that gets applied to the Maze Bank Billboards.

A Google translate of "Pescado Azul" will return "Blue Fish", but a more accurate translation is Oily Fish.

The only video I found seemed to see this location as an Easter Egg, but I don't speak Spanish, so I'm not sure why. Possibly the reference to fat, and maybe the "death pose" of the fish in the logo?

If you speak Spanish Portuguese,(?) and want to enlighten me, feel free!

Taken alone the bubbles resemble planets, due to the sizes implying depth. (IMHO)

Since "PESCADO AZUL" is a Spanish word, I went looking for a Spanish anagram, and got "ESCUDO PLAZA". (Shield Plaza)

Seems like it could reference to the FIB/IAA plaza right next door. (Shield/Police)

This was a rather unusual rabbit hole, so I figured I would share and see what other hunters might make of it.
