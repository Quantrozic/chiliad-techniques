The Return Of The Placements!! (INST & LOD)

Documented under:Shader and LOD Inspection

Kifflom Brothers (and maybe 1 or 2 sisters?), it's time for the next installment of File Wars...

A bit of a legend nowadays, there once stood a man (think he was sitting) who stood (sat) against the StarRock and parsed the (xbox 360) xmaps for us. Talkol was his name. However he perished in the stuggle and was lost to us (forever?) :, ( ... Another man then attempted to take his place. Transmet Team was his name. He also perished in the struggle though and left us with a hot mess of (PC) ymap placements that were never finished.

But now, I am proud to bring to you all some real (PC) ymap placement files!! From the man locked in my basement, tgascoigne xD He has yet again supplied us with an amazing resource for checking placements and which ymap files place them.

http://optimus.gascoigne.me/ymap_dump.7z

We know how much you all love your X, Y and Z coordinates so strap in... these .json files include the INST and LOD sections of the .ymap files (he is working on the other sections still). Each .ymap has its own .json file (of the same name as the .ymap name in the files) and inside each are the X, Y and Z coordinates of model (and texture and LOD) placements in that area.

As usual Glokon's most eggcellent map can be used to plot coordinates: http://gta5map.glokon.org/

I recommend the notepad++ find in files function (in the search tab) to search all the files at once for things and that will show you the coordinates for that item and which .ymap places it. (It just occurred to me that these can actually be loaded into memory with visual studio code and searched the same way you would scripts) xD

I'm gonna keep this short and sweet. Hopefully by now you all understand the XYZ coordinate system in GTA 5, but if you do have any questions at all then I am here to help.

(tgas has told me that in some cases there are 2 versions of the ymap. This is because in updates some files were replaced for new versions. He has included both for us)

TL;DR: World map PC placements (x, y and z)

Kifflom!
