I've spent quite of time as a lurker in this sub, but now I've found something worth showing my presence. The moon change its behavior in snowy weather. How, you ask. It moves faster. Well, at a first glance, just a bit faster. But when the clock ticks exactly four a.m. it turns on full speed and goes away across the sky. I did it plenty of times on top of Mt. Chiliad, where it seems to be the only spot where you can see the moon in snowy weather. It starts moving from behind de platform where you can see the UFO and then circles the mountain until it disappears in the middle of the clouds just above the Maze Bank building shadow.

As you may think, I did go to the top of Maze Bank building before and after the moon starts moving. Unfortunately, there's no vision of the sky by the city, just dark clouds.

Here's the most twisting part now: if you use the weather cheat once more, the moon will reappear in its original position, where it should be if you didn't changed the weather.
