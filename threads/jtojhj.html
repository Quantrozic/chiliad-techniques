Image 1: https://imgur.com/gallery/4LttjT9

Image 2: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galactic\_coordinate\_system

I'm thinking this "shoot for the stars" thing is quite literal, under certain conditions (rain/night) you must pick up the sniper on the observatory deck and shoot a specific star(s) or something along those lines.
Possibly even just observing the right part of the night sky during said correct conditions with the telescope or mobile phone camera.

This looks to be related to the yellow part of the mural.

The problem now being, having no idea of how to use galactic co-ordinates, or if there are any known co-ordinates that could be used in this way.

How this theory of galactic coordinates came to light:

This all came about due to the sand stone glyph, with the wow! Numbers and letters.
Looking up wow! On wikepedia, they seemed to isolate the signal's location from space somewhere around the saggitarius region, using galactic coordinates, which then led to this.
