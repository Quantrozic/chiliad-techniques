Walking Through the Spiral Staircase

Documented under:Golden Ratio / Fibonacci / Spiral Overlays

I've spent the past couple months strictly lurking. Reading, searching and digesting. I think at this point, there's nothing new I can bring to this discussion that hasn't already been found. I did some subreddit searching, and found a few articles regarding the spiral staircase located in the "sewer" area by the beach (near the ferris wheel). From what I can tell, it's still a mystery. Why is it there? What's it for? Why haven't they removed it yet.

So I stumble upon the area by accident, notice the out-of-place spiral staircase, and like every other hunter who sees it, became obsessed with it's purpose. But there's some sort of invisible wall/block around the fence, which prevents you from entering the area.

Well, I actually got through the fence yesterday, totally by accident. While trying to jump onto/over the pipes nearby, I fell "forward" in what I can only explain as glitchy way, and "slid" through the fence. Made it inside

The staircase is there, but you can only walk through it, not up it. The pipes are the same, you just walk through them. I spent 20 minutes trying to walk through the other walls, blow things up, etc just to try and make something happen. I'm not 100% yet though, so shrug if that makes a difference.

But the weirdest part of it all, is that I did this with Trevor. Eventually I wanted to get out, but couldn't hop the fence or leave at all. So I killed myself with a grenade. When I respawned at the hospital, my haircut was different. It immediately went back to Trevor's default, long/scruffy cut. It all seemed very glitchy to me, but it was certainly interesting.
