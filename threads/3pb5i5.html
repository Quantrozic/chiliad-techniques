Anyways, on with the show.

Firstly, with the no water mod, I've found at night there's a few specific things in the ocean that glow orange'ish.
I find it interesting that the UFO lights up the way it does because it doesn't strike me as random like the rest of the spots. Especially intriguing is the fact that the bottom, below the clip line illuminates as well.
I wouldn't have even posted the other spots, but there's only the few. If there'd been rocks scattered throughout glowing like that I wouldn't have thought anything of it, but it's just the two rocks, the patch, and a trapezoid shape (which is the only one that vanishes if you get close, the other anomalies remain no matter how close you get.)

Probably absolutely nothing, but didn't think it'd hurt to point out.

Next up, the Tivoli Theater light. I've seen a few small mentions of this, but I feel like this has to be something more. That light is the only visible light from that far up, and from every direction I've tried up there.

No other rocks formed like this anywhere, and what's more is the bottom part of it really looks like the Laguz symbol that's been discussed from the sand glyph. If so, could these rocks be placed here to possibly symbolize the moon rising above water? The circle of course being the moon (since there's no rocks to look like light rays) The line would denote the surface, and the water symbol happens to fall under that line.
