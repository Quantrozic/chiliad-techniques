Is the Heist Mural trying to tell us to look at Voyager for answers?

▲ 16 ptsu/destruDec 14, 201724 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Fourier Transform Audio Spectral Analysis

First time poster, but I wanted to drop a quick idea out there since I haven't seen it mentioned yet. Maybe someone more talented than I will be interested and can take a stab at this. The more eyes, the better.

The recently released wall texture inside chiliad got my mind going after I showed it to a friend that doesn't pay much attention to the mystery. He told me how the waveform in the yellow portion of the image reminded him of what's on the Voyager golden record that we sent out into space 40 years ago. I did a crude overlay of the waveform onto the record, and he's right! I had to squeeze the chiliad waveform a bit to make it overlay properly, but I think the resemblance is undeniable. The only point of the waveform on the golden record, anyway, is to give an intelligent species help figuring out how to decode the audio on the record (the waveform itself, afaik, does not decode to anything useful). Here's what NASA has to say about the images on the record. There is also a pulsar map in the yellow portion of the mural which has resemblance to what's on the voyager disc.

I was thinking, maybe it's a sign that R* is telling us that we need to decrypt audio from the game similar to how images are extracted from the golden record. Here's a blog post explaining how to get the images out of the golden record. Obviously, the first audio that comes to mind is the space docker horns. I know people have some audio spectrum analysis of the horn but I don't know if anyone has looked at this method.

What do you guys think? Thanks for your time!

Unrelated side note: There is also a sine/cosine image on the left side of the yellow portion of the chiliad texture. Maybe the dots on the wave helps to map something out.

EDIT: This didn't seem to get very good reception, so I'll look into it some more if anyone point me to the audio directly from the game files? I only own the game on PC and a ripped youtube clip may not suffice.
