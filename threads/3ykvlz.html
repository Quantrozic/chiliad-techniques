So since I ran out of clues to follow in the game, I've been digging around the game files hoping for some clues. Well, I think I hit something! I was looking for the files involved with the Fort Zancudo bunker elevator and found something interesting. There are 3 files for the elevator labeled elevdis1, elevdis2, and elevdis3. I'm assuming these are elevator display states. elevdis1 is the -1 that is always showing on the display. But take a look at elevdis2 and elevdis3: http://imgur.com/a/juJop. Now this doesn't prove anything for sure, but it's doubtful they would bother to make a -2 and -3 texture unless the elevator was actually able to go down further. Thoughts? Ideas? Where do I go from here?

EDIT: The Humane Labs elevator uses the same texture file, so it might have something to do with this as well.

EDIT2: After further digging I found this complete texture file.
The file name seems to indicate that it was meant for the Humane Labs elevator but all three industrial elevators in this game draw from this file. Human Labs also has a floor -3 as you can see in this video https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=by2KWovds7A at :10. This is discouraging, but there still may be more to this.
