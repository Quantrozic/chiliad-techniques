So, nothing of note was found, especially not at the exact coordinates. I followed the small gulch (I keep calling it that but I’m 90% sure that’s the wrong term) and it led to nothing of interest. There was a single saguaro visible from the coordinates, I investigated it but didn’t see anything out of the ordinary. I was out exploring for probably 2 hours, trying to access the coordinates from different routes as well as trying to find any sort of tracks that could indicate other people in the area recently, no luck. The whole time I was there I couldn’t help but to feel like Michael wandering the desert.

After a while of not finding anything interesting I just decided to explore the area which led to some cool finds you can see at the bottom of the Imgur album.

I also want to make it extremely clear that just because I didn’t find something that doesn’t mean it’s a dead lead, I was one person on foot, with limited time. On the flip side of this coin, it’s just as likely absolutely nothing. It was a fun adventure nonetheless and I hope you all enjoy.
