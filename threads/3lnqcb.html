Sasquatch isn't necessarily passive during The Last One

▲ 8 ptsu/JohnDangleSep 20, 20158 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Director Mode Scene & Spawn Testing ?

Kifflom brother-brothers and sister-mothers! In the hopes of getting that 22nd animal in Director mode, I've been replaying The Last One, as many of you are I'm sure! I noticed if you don't engage the squatch when he starts crouching on the cliff you can drive pretty much anywhere, so I thought to grab the dirt bike or buggy and head to the top of Chiliad with him there. No joy, the locations of peyote that make you into dogs are next on the list, but to get to the point...

On one of the runs, I accidentally bumped our costumed friend with my dirtbike and he seemed to come after me. I tried again and sure enough, he pulled me off my bike and fought me. Skip to 1:30 in this video, it's cut for my crappy channel or it would be right at 0:00. I tried and couldn't get Frank to talk to him during this, but it doesn't seem you can talk to any peds while traveling abroad in the mission.

As well, in the interest of staying non-lethal and to check this guy's claim, I tazed squatting squatch and he slid a bit down the cliff. Not off it, just about 3 feet down the rocky part. No footage as I didn't expect it to happen and didn't replicate it (yet), but the screen kinda faded black and we appeared here for the cutscene rather than on the cliff.

Forgive me if it's common knowledge we can roam around freely during the mission and that he'll fight you; obligatory statement about doing a search prior to posting but I wasn't sure what terms to even use. Felt like this merited sharing with all you fine folks on the hunt for the 'gold' peyote! Thanks for reading.
