Gitch under the Human Labs and Research loading bay area.

▲ 1 ptsu/HugenMooksNov 9, 20132 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Livestreamed Group Hunts ?

Last night I completed the "Monkey Business" mission and decided I would return to have a look around the Labs as I haven't even bothered to come here before I had to do the mission.

I remember that I exited the building via a loading bay, and this is around the area where you hook the refrigeration unit with the helicopter during the mission. I decided I would try and have a look around what could be stored in there, so I got in Trevor's helicopter and flew there, then parachuted down. Upon getting into there, the textures were just grey and flat, and as soon as I tried to get onto the 'concrete' section of the loading bay, Trevor did a diving action and then dove a good distance down into water below, glitching through the ground.

Under here, I couldn't see much, as it's a fair way down and your stuck in water. There is only a limited patch of water, so if you swim off it you will fall for some distance, and then be spawned back up in the car park of the area. If you were to dive though, you can see the pipe that the characters swim through to gain access into the labs. I'm sure if you were equipt with another parachute from just landing a chopper on the ground rather than falling you could use it, but you would still be a fair way down and I'm not sure what would be able to be seen.

I'm not sure what relevance this has to anything, I just thought I should share what happened.

Things to note:

  • I tried with all 3 characters, the glitch was still happening.
  • I'm ~54% completion.
  • If nobody can recreate this I will post pictures, but they will be a few days away as I don't have access to the game.
  • I searched for this but I couldn't find anyone else posting about it, if there is another thread please direct me.
