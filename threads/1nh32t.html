We should be looking more into movie plots for clues (Hear me out)

▲ 23 ptsu/dopestepSep 30, 201316 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Radio-Broadcast Subtext Concordance ?

I noticed there are a lot of movie references in GTA 5 (and all the other GTA's) and I think we might be able to use that to learn a little more about the ufo mystery. For example, the movie "Close Encounters of the Third Kind" is a famous alien movie where special goverment agents, and some hippy freaks (who claim to get messages from aliens), meet at a mountain in the Sonoran desert to make contact with a UFO. The government kept getting reports of UFO's and the witnesses all claimed to hear the same 5 tone sequence of notes. the government broadcast those notes into space and in return got a series of numbers in response which turned out to be the coordinates of the meeting place. Also, during the meeting, the aliens returned all of the people who had mysteriously vanished over the last 50 or so years and invited one man to board their ship and explore the universe with them.

Any of that sound familiar? It should. the references to mountains are constant in both plots, the government agents, paranoid conspiracy theorists, the Senora desert in GTA 5 and the Senoran desert in close encounters.

Many of the mission titles are references to other alien movies/tv shows. there are multiple references to the X files and it made me think of the first episode where Mulder gets out of his car after hearing a radio disturbance and sprays a red "X" on the ground exactly where it happened. Later in the episode Mulder and Scully are driving away from a scene they were investigating and they suddenly lose ten minutes without realizing it. Mulder stops the car where the event happened and looks on the ground and sees the red "X" that he sprayed earlier.

It may be stretch to say that the red X's in the mural are related to that scene but many people (including myself) have noticed that certain areas around the chilliad wilderness and surrounding mountains will cause interference on car radios. Since the radio in game isn't an actual radio it means that Rockstar had to program that in intentionally. It could just be a product of their meticulous attention to detail but it seems to me like it might be something more than that.

Thoughts? I'd like to start a discussion here for relevant movie plots. Maybe if we collectively analyze the movies we've seen we can find something that correlates in game.

Edit: I'd also like to point out that during the epsilon missions, the leader gives you a short speech and at the end he asks "Are you ready to meet your ancient ancestors, receive an egg from them, and form your new self out of it?" I don't think its a movie reference but it might be related to the egg in the mural. Whenever the epsilon people refer to aliens during other parts of the game they call them their "ancient ancestors".
