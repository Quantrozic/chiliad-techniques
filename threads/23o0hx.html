Hi all,

As this subreddit has progressed with age it's become a great resource for all hunters, whether we're in the hunt to find the hidden religious symbology or just want to look at dick jokes. However, over time, it's naturally become harder and harder to search for and find the easter eggs which exist within the game.

In fact, I'm aware that some long time contributors have only become aware of things like the snowman or Grease easter egg within the past few weeks. In order to get around this and to have a full list of all of the Easter Eggs, Secrets and References within the game, I've created a seperate subreddit - http://www.reddit.com/r/GTAVEasterEggs/

This is in no way an effort to detract from this sub. Regardless of whether or not we all get along, it's a great community. This is solely an effort to organise things in a matter which more befits my slightly OCD mind and to help other people out.

The subreddit is open to all contributors but I do ask that you follow the rules on the sidebar just so there is a more uniform way of posting. I've posted a few examples already.

Thanks!
