Hi all! Let me preface this by saying this is only speculation, and was intended purely as food for thought. However, I hope that sharing this will spark some additional investigations and theories.

With that said…there’s been a lot of recent speculation about what the Altruist Camp symbols mean. Meanwhile, we’ve yet to decipher where the Hippy Camp is pointing us toward, and what the red dots signify.

Backtracking, let’s look at the model of Mount Chiliad at the Hippy Camp. It has a red circle atop it, with the yellow eye/3am/moon glyph. Let’s think on that…

Why the red circle? Why the yellow glyph? Why are these two colors combined, and why here? Where else have we seen these colors used together?

Next, let’s look at the other red dots at the Hippy Camp. Collectively, we’ve spent a lot of time looking for other red dots in-game, but I think the answer may be right in front of us. Think like a developer. Think like a graphic designer. You’ve put a red dot on top of the model of Mount Chiliad. Then you have two red dots directly across from it. Why have this very purposeful repetition of color (red) and shape (circle)? It’s almost like you’re telling the player “Hey! That red dot you’re standing on? Look across from you. That’s where it is on the map.”

So, we must ask ourselves: If one of those dots represents the top of Mount Chiliad, what does the other red dot represent? For shits and giggles, let’s pretend the larger red dot with the station wagon on top of it represents the top of Mount Chiliad. Geographically-speaking, that means the other, smaller red dot must represent an area nearby.

Now, that second, smaller red dot could simply represent the Chiliad UFO. But, you know what else is really close to the top of Mount Chiliad? The Altruist Camp. Watch this video, specifically the 3:20 mark, where the overlay is shown. Could this other, little mountain (or somewhere around here) represent the second, smaller red dot? If so, what might it have to do with the rain glyph at the Hippy Camp?

Finally, what does the red arrow at the Hippy Camp represent? Again, think like a developer. Think like a graphic designer. Arrows point, but they also represent movement. What is moving, or what is supposed to move? The UFO? The player? And where is the arrow originating from, if it’s pointing toward the Altruist Camp and Mount Chiliad?

Again, just food for thought. Kudos to everyone who’s been investigating these areas recently. Happy hunting!
