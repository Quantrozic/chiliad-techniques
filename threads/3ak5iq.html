Hunt/follow this sub because you enjoy it!! Not just to solve the mystery

▲ 28 ptsu/adamiskingJun 21, 201516 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:DLC Manifest Pre-Drop Enumeration ?

I think at this point there's a big ol' elephant in this room, with a few recent topics about the mood of this sub and how people respond to, shall we say, poorly thought out topics..

There was quite a big build up before the nextgen release, then an even bigger build up before PC and lot of people saying things like 'I hope we solve this before the PC release and people crack the code open and ruin it!!!!'

And now we're here, a few months down the line and we still haven't found any SOLID next steps.

It's frustrating.

But this sub should be something fun, a pass time where people who enjoy the game can get a bit extra out of it by exploring the world, finding new things and trying to solve the mural.

I only started reading about a year ago and joined a bit later, maybe it's just my perception but it does seem that, particularly since the PC release, the general tone of the place has got a bit less friendly.

I've always said, hunt because you enjoy it and share your theories/experiences because you enjoy it.

It might sound silly but if you're here SOLELY to solve this, and you're no longer enjoying the process of hunting and the crazy theories/things people find, just stop. It feels like a minority of posters have become frustrated since the lack of new positive finds since the PC release.

There're plenty of other games and passtimes out there if you're no longer enjoying this.

Yes, we're here with the aim of solving a mystery.

But at the end of the day it should be fun, this isn't work. Try to keep that in mind.
