The mystery cannot be revealed yet..or ever.

▲ 0 ptsu/CRRPGMykaelMay 5, 20157 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Atbash Substitution Testing

First of all, I'd like to state that I love GTA and Rockstar Games and their work mentality and creativity and everything. This is also my first Reddit thread ever, but anyway, the devs have spent over 5 years on this game, and it's absolutely mesmerizing, the level of detail and atmosphere that is present in GTA V. One of the things that came out of that is, yes, the Chilliad Mystery. And you, the fans, who kept digging and asking questions to reveal its secrets (the PC release has really given this whole thing a big boost).
Now, I have read just about any piece of information regarding The Mystery directly and indirectly on this subreddit. There's the tech guys who write their own tools to find specific assets in the game and make important discoveries, and there's the guys who just create the craziest theories ever.
But the problem is, the mystery has not been revealed, and maybe it cannot be revealed yet. Or ever. The information that I personally regard as the most important, is the basic stuff, the mural. Also, the "JACK SHEEPE SERVICING TRI-MANIFOLD" thing being an anagram for "JETPACK CONFIRMED", and the actual name being used for a parody movie about jetpacks. The jetpack on the mural. The glyps being called "UFO CLUE" in the files. That's too much to ignore. But nobody has found a jetpack, or an egg for that matter (only in the game files). All we have so far are several UFO's, and a vague idea of what the red X's are.
Now, let's wind the clock back to 2011. Batman: Arkham City had just come out. But wait, what does that have to do with GTA? Well, just like GTA, Arkham City had its own easter eggs and secrets. I'm sure you all remember Scarecrow's boat, and his 3 number messages on the 'decoder'. But, who solved them? Some of them were solved by the fans, but wait, wind the clock back briefly to 2009, Arkham Asylum had come out. Now, one of the biggest secrets in that game was a room referencing the sequel, Arkham City, but that secret was only solved in 2011 after some aid from the developers. In Arkham City, Scarecrow's boat seems to be pretty difficult to just randomly stumble upon, but the first 2 number messages are relatively easy to crack. So what about the last one? It's been solved, again, with aid from the developers. Now, check this out:
There's a sequence of numbers said by a voice (just like the previous codes), that you have to translate to the alphabet (so 1 would be A, 2 would be B, etc). Cool, similar to the other codes.
After that, you need to use an Atbash cipher to reverse the letters (A becomes Z, B becomes Y, etc). Okay, getting difficult, but still plausible. Now here's when your mind gets fucking blown:
You have to use a Vigenere cipher (if you don't know what that is, it's a cipher where, similarly to what's been done before, you reassign the letters around, but unlike the Atbash which simply reverses them, Vigenere's can do anything, and needs a 'password' to do so) with the password SCARECROW, which finally unlocks the last message, once again referencing the sequel, Arkham Knight.
Bear in mind, these big secrets have only been revealed with help from Rocksteady (the developers) YEARS after the game came out, and I think we need Rockstar to unlock the mystery. Maybe a little helping hand, or maybe wait for them to update the game or release DLC with the adequate content. I mean, there's a god damn 'Split Sides West' comedy club interior in the game but it's inaccessible.
So, we have 3 options:
-The mystery is simple and can be revealed, we just somehow haven't done it yet.
-The mystery is FAR harder to solve than we expect (yes, even for those with the completely crazy theories about seemingly insignificant things).
-The mystery cannot be solved at this moment.

Think back to how the next-gen version of the game added new content, and not just the cool little things like 1st person view, the peyotes, or even the other collectibles which can only be accessed on NEXT GEN for RETURNING PLAYERS like the Monkey Mosaics (maybe the mystery is similar to that). There is a new murder mystery (not to be confused with the murder mysteries surrounding Jock's wife and her ghost or even the Vinewood Starlet mystery) that only RETURNING PLAYERS on NEXT GEN can solve. As Michael, you have to go around looking for 'clues', and the game doesn't "support" this 'mission'. There's no cutscene, there's no text, there's nothing about nothing. The only thing that happens is a small sound effect when you're near a clue. There's 4 messages on a wall on different parts of the map, there's a woman "in cement shoes" in the ocean, and only after gathering all 5 clues, you can go to Solomon Richards' office, but only after 9pm and it's not marked on the map, the only indication is a yellow marker near the door. There's a letter that you can read, but after you're done with it, again, there's NO INDICATION of what to do after that. You have to go to a mine shaft in the woods (inacessible in last gen), blow up the sealed entrance and approach a dead body, whereupon the game gives you a new phone 'contact', who if you call, enables 2 different "Noir" screen filters. I mean, that's it? I don't know what's more disappointing, to know that the mystery is over and that's all you get or that there's maybe an extra step that's even more random to uncover and just as unsupported by the game as the previous ones.

TL;DR The mystery is either super hard to solve, or cannot be solved yet at all.

My advice is for for the good people who know what they're doing (the ones finding all the interior coordinates, and uncovering the "-1 or 999" code, which unfortunately has been debunked as pertaining to the fact that you're playing singleplayer or multiplayer) to keep on searching the game files for anything (remember, even within the files, any mystery related stuff can be hard to find, as evidenced by the discovery that Medkid textures are kept in the file used for barbershop interiors).
Also, you need to be at 100%, and as I alluded to previously, you probably need to be a returning player too.
