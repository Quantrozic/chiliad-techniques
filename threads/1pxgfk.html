Mural interpretation:
All of the glyphs are telling us what we need to do to figure out the Mural. The Colors chosen are important. The fact that the eye at the top, the UFO, the egg and the jetpack guy are in red, because they're alluding to the day and that in order to use them, it has to be daylight. The alruist camp might associate red with the sun. . I think the clue at the top of the Mural is the RED eye, meaning for this clue, wait until daylight.

I contend the UFO on Chiliad is real because it has a shadow. Most projected holograms I've heard of require darkness or night to be seen. I also believe I've heard the same noise as the other UFOs when approaching the ufo at night. Also, when looking at the UFO with SNIPER rifle and the advanced scope, you can see a light coming out of the bottom, begging us to ask "Can we board it?". A logical place to board would be directly below it but we have no idea where "directly under" is though, do we?

Could it's shadow show us a way to find directly below it? Another thing that leads me to associate the use of shadows to look for a clue is the fact there is an eye on the obelisk at a certain time of the day. There are also many things that arguably can be used as rudimentary sundials in the game. The use of shadows to track down the location is something I'm personally investigating at the moment, but if anyone wants to look into it as well, go ahead. The shadow of the UFO could also just be a clue to it's reality and provide no indicator as to it's location, with the obelisk telling us to « look » at the shadows, but I'd rather cross it off before I dismiss a potential lead.

LOOK for a CLUE at the UFO on a RAINY NIGHT with the SNIPER at the TOP OF THE MOUNTAIN

With this being said, we’re left with three other symbols that are within the confine of the mountain on the mural. The UFO, The Egg and the Jetpack. This here guys is somewhere I'm very skeptical on and I'm only tossing it out there because I'm going to investigate it. The UFO is on the mural because it leads us to look for it on The Mountain. Now we also know the UFO on chiliad casts a shadow during the day and will stay in the sky throughout the day if you stay there long enough. I think this is giving us a hint that it’s always there – regardless of the conditions that you need to initiate it’s sight, also nullifying it’s potential as a hologram. If it were a hologram, we wouldn’t see it during the day. The Obelisk is also another potential clue to look towards the shadows. This might have some credibility. A recent thread posted this allegation and also how it bore a similarity to the top of the mural and it got my upvote.

The Egg is symbolism that is scattered throughout different areas of the game when you look for it, tying it to several of the in-game factions hinting that maybe we should look a little further into them if this theory doesn't pan out. I, personally, also saw the mountain in a (crude) shape of an egg, with old man’s crack representing the crack in the egg, but I'll be the first to admit that it could be just because I want to. It could also be another hint that we can crack this egg on the mountain. Just me rambling on about that.

On to the Jetpack. I’d hate to say it, because I was so sure there was a jetpack in-game, but I’m not so sure anymore, and here is why. While pouring over the epsilon tract before I took one of my pilgrimages up Mt. Gordo, they hinted that celebrities and director’s are epsilonists. Being curious of the real-world application to that hint, I looked up a famous Scientologists since they are the real world epsilonists. I also broadened the search to include potential, alleged or rumored scientologists. On the list of potential/rumored, one name popped up that I found particularly interesting, the actor, Daniel Craig. Who is Daniel craig? None other than the latest James Bond. James Bond, as we all know, is another famous Jetpack rider. I looked to the game and headed up to chiliad myself to watch the UFO and see if I could find anything. While looking up the cheat for the rain, I noticed a particularly oddly named cheat. Skyfall.

The SkyFall cheat is new to GTA V and is peculiar in the sense that it breaks from the game world to a black screen for a moment. It even takes a few seconds, like it’s searching for something, before it places you in the middle of the sky and let’s you fly through the air like Michael after he got ejected from his abduction. Now the skyfall cheat could’ve been named other things too. Skydrop, kiss the sky.. the list of potential names that Rockstar could’ve used and made reference to real world things could go on, but they chose Skyfall in particular. Yeah, I’ll admit, it sounds cool, but I think it might be a clue and a potential explanation of the jetpack on the mural. After all, James Bond is one of the most famous jetpackers in history.

One thing I was always curious about, was that one single line on the bottom of the mountain that was a line connecting the UFO and the Jetpack. Some people say that if we were to board a vehicle or had some game altering event as the egg, it might require a cutscene or a transition of sorts. The Skyfall cheat does just that. So, with that in mind, how is the entrance to a UFO depicted not only in-game but IRL hollywood? Abductions from beams of light directly below the UFO. So, looking at the UFO on Chiliad we can’t necessarily see the tractor beam unless we - LOOK at the UFO on a RAINY NIGHT with the SNIPER at the TOP OF THE MOUNTAIN . I understand the Skyfall analogy is a stretch, especially where it would require you to cheat, so it's quite possible there is a jetpack in the game still. Just wanted to throw the Skyfall idea out there because I'm going to play around with it for a bit once I can pinpoint a potential location.
