That other cryptic thing- the Richman Glen Mural

Good day everyone. Today I will be sharing what I have found out about this mysterious assortment of tiles called the Richman Glen Mural- it is a substitution cipher

To understand what alphabet I used is as simple as trial and error. To my astonishment, the International Telegraph Alphabet lauded no error. I counted each tile in groups of 5, and matched the pattern to the Telegraph alphabet (painted tile = black dot for telegraph patterns). All patterns on the mural match patterns with the telegraph alphabet.

The significance of why I decided to use the telegraph alphabet is an "end to a mean". You throw shit at the wall, and whatever sticks has to be considered physical proof. It spells out

  • IOTKABEIIMEUMAVKO

after clever spacing TRYING TO AID THE PROGRAM TO PRODUCE ANYTHING

  • IOT KABEII ME UM AVKO

Plugging that into quipqiup produces

  • SPY ACROSS TO IT CHAP

Therefore, I becomes S, O to P, T to Y, and so on.

This phrase makes sense. It's not gibberish. Lest not forget The Emissary, which means 'the foreign spy'. The phrase seems similar to "The Spy Who Came in From the Cold". It's open to interpretation, but at the same time, give me something more tangible. Solve the Famous Hamburger sign, come back to me.
