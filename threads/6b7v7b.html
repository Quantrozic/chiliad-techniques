Altruist Camp - Dead End Connections

▲ 97 ptsu/walkeronlineMay 15, 201796 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Save-State A/B Trigger TestingSave-State Differential Diffing

Hello once again!

Quick side note before we get too deep into this - I know some people are asking why i don't post videos anymore and the simple answer is i only post a video when there is something worth talking about, and despite all the clickbait going around (nothing new) i think most people by now know how to sort out the real from the bullshit. I have also noted that posting in certain threads here on reddit results in you being branded a buzzkill on other people's theory, but i want to stress the fact that most if not all of what we look into is supported by the game itself and not loosely based connections based on assumptions. We are looking at the intended use behind the game code to find new things. Sadly, new things have been few and far between in recent times.

So, lets get to the point.

The Altruists Camp has been hotly debated, but between testing and script/file digging, we have concluded that some things are just dead ends.

There is no set order or combo of victims that changes any outcomes

Starting from a testing perspective, i started from a clean 100% save and i dropped off every victim once, followed by every other victim combo for the second drop off. Here's the notes that i gathered from those attempts.

The Callbacks

We were looking at the audio files, and boy is there a lot of audio in there that we have never heard that have always been a mystery to us. Sadly, without knowing how all the audio system's work, we cannot say for certain it's unused. What we CAN say is that to our current knowledge of how audio is played in scripts, a majority of it is unused.

This leaves the callbacks themselves - the idea being that after a drop off, you hear "Someone has been brought! It is a sign..."

I, like many others, noted that there is more variation to those lines as well as many callback opportunities involved in all the possible drop off combinations. The idea being that the callback would change based on who we took in a certain order as a confirmation we are doing something correctly.

The issue is, at maximum we are making 4 trips to the altruists, and at minimum, 2. They do not care about how many trips as long as they get 4 people minimum. So, any combo of people can have 2, 3 or 4 trips (remember, trips = runs to the camp with people. There can be 1 or 2 people in any trip)

The first drop off will not have a callback, as in, nobody says anything after the cutscene is played.

The second drop off WILL have a callback UNLESS its the final drop off (making 2 trips with the 2 victims) because this would trigger the shootout.

The third follows the same rule as above, a callback unless its the final drop off (a combo of 4 victims in 3 trips) which starts the shootout.

The forth will never have a callback, because it will start the shootout no matter what you do. This means that we only have a chance for a callback on two drop offs that are dependent on the combos, and we may have one or NO callbacks at all. The idea of callbacks being useful to us is debunked by those points.

Looking at the audio itself gives a clue - In the audio files for the camp, two specific files are duplicated, and they just happen to be the only callbacks we have ever heard (Someone has been brought! It is a sign...) and the duplicate ones have a SLIGHT tone variance as they were taken from two different recordings of the line (something common in voice acting work - recording the same line multiple times so they can have different takes to mess around with)

It looks like the tone variance is attached specifically to the victims and more specifically to the groups of 1 or 2, AKA, one audio plays for every group that has 1 person, and the other plays for the groups of 2 (the two different trips with 2 victims, either the robbers or the drunk couple, have different audio for their callback compared to all the single victims)

So, as far as the audio goes, I have two hypothesis - The first is they intended for more to happen at the camp, but intentionally shortened it. The easy way to do this is to disable/delete the lines and write new code. They then duplicated the audio calls to use in the new (current) version of the missions so they wouldn't have any odd code overlap/activation happening. The second is, there is some other thing happening at the camp besides the entire victim drop off/shootout that we are unaware of that uses that audio. The latter can and should be looked into.

Is there other victims?

Not that we are aware of. We checked this by looking at the why/how the altruists logo pops up on the map and what missions its attached to. It appears to be called only on those drop offs we already know about.

What about the crate at the shootout?

We have found no evidence that the crate will ever load its "open" model under any circumstances. This does leave the question as to why we have that model, but it appears to not be called ANYWHERE that we can find. I haven't made any multiplayer maps, but is it possible its an asset used there?

What about the UFO Shadow?

Looking closely at the footage of the "UFO Shadow" and trying to replicate it in my own game showed that something was different - I wasn't seeing the same shadows at all in my game. I concluded that the graphic settings on the footage in question was set much lower than mine, and the UFO shadow is literally the shadow of a bush farther up the mountain. At that distance, a low def version of the camp would be loaded, if it loaded at all, and would not include shadows FROM the camp unless loaded. Look carefully at the footage in question (i don't have a link, but its easy to find) and you can see how blurry the edges of the shadow are, and how very bush-like the shadow is if you remove your imagination from the equation.

So why does the dialog image for the Altruists Camp show a UFO?

No clue, but these fit into my earlier hypothesizes - Either it was a bigger mission that was cut down (that had more involvement with the UFO's) or its something separate from the victim/shootout mission line that has not been activated properly yet. I will say that any currently found scripts for missions in that area include nothing but the victim/shootout information. Considering we have a lot of cut content and a lot of files we still cant get access to/read properly, it cannot be ruled out yet.

What about The Eye?

As i have posted about before, it seems the eye is more related to weather and time than location, however location is more lenient. The new options for ENT allow you to speed up the flow of time in the game which affects the weather/cloud movement. I set the weather to stormy, the time to go by very quickly and i waited at the camp. I saw the blue eye sunset every day doing this until the storm cleared (Weather cycles seem independent of the movement of time.) meaning weather and time are important. Moving around the map, you can see in several places the sky going blue for the sunset behind cloud cover. This makes it seem more like an environmental effect and not a scripted event. This also means that while it is observable only under specific conditions, its unclear if those conditions have any effect on anything related to the camp directly. The only thing i can say for sure is that the height the camp is at is the best viewing height. Any higher, and the eye gets washed out white, any lower and it starts disappearing behind the ground fog layer that accompanies storms.

It should also be noted here that seeing the eye sunset followed by the 3 AM UFO seems impossible to do without cheats, as no storm cycle lasts long enough to do both in a day. Its always possible that we are missing a trigger for weather, but script searching doesn't show any specific triggers in respect to storms (You will find the normal storm calls for the Chiliad UFO, as well as a few rain/storm calls for the cable car script, but those calls are related to effects on the cable car, such as rain hitting the top of the car, and sound effects played while riding it in the rain)

But you used trainers for some of your testing...

Yes i did. The use of mods/trainers is debated among many. I will stress again that while i am changing variables outside of normal play, i am showing that things are triggering under those conditions. The chiliad UFO will spawn with natural cheats OR trainer changes because they do the same thing. So far no testing shows that a natural occurrence of any instance is different than a cheat/trainer supported trigger of the same event. If you want to see the blue eye sunset naturally, you have to simply get to that area at the right time when storms are happening. Or, you can cheat the weather before that time frame and get the same effect. I will be looking into this soon to see if the game changes any flags for specific weather cheats to disable other things that may or may not trigger because of it.

Conclusion (TL;DR)

There is no specific order to the camp drop off victims.

Callbacks for drop offs only happen on trips that are not the first or last drop off. Meaning a maximum of 2 call backs, and a minimum of 0.

The call back audio that is in game is related to only 2 audio files. Both happen to be duplicates of other files, leaving doubt as to if the camp was changed before release, or has more things involved that we have never triggered.

There is no specific calls for any of the unused audio based on the methods we currently are aware of for audio placement. Without full access/knowledge of audio placement, we won't know for sure.

No other victims are involved besides the ones we already know about.

The open crate isn't called anywhere in scripts/placements.

UFO shadow is likely a bush in low graphics mode.

The eye is environmentally based, but still triggers under specific conditions, naturally or cheat/trainers used.

Anything else that may/may not happen at the camp is not related to the victim/shootout missions.

Trainers are not evil.
