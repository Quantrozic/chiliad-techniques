THEORY

The Mount Chiliad Mystery (MCM) be a marketing ploy formulated by Rockstar to advertise an upcoming game. The pay off most likely being a release teaser trailer to GTA 6.

When the MCM mystery is finally solved and door T01 opens, not only will we have access to the heart of the mountain and the jet pack, but we will also get some sort of announcement for the next game in the series.

Why do this? To keep up interest in the franchise and sell more games of course!

Konami / Kojima Productions have already done this. P.T Demo is released, and upon completion a trailer for a new Silent Hill game is revealed. Great now everyone is excited!

CHILIADMYSTERY.COM IS AN ALTERNATE REALITY GAME

When looking at all the deciphering and clue hunting this community does, I cannot help but compare the process to that of Alternate Reality Game (ARG). The principles of ARG are explained quite well on Wikipedia. In essence it’s a real life easter egg hunt for fans to follow, often over a long time period. An ARG can be altered by a users actions or ideas. Once a new clue is discovered, marketers will release the next.

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternate_reality_game

The Year Zero Game of 2007 is a good example of anARG. Nine Inch Nails used an ARG a way of promoting their album of the same name. Users deciphered a code on the back of a NIN tour t-shirt, which had the encoded domain name for a website which led users to another clue. And so on. After following through the ARG, NIN fans would discover leaked music tracks from the band.

Now let’s look at chiliadmystery.com, the mysterious website with ‘Door T01’ image as it’s singular item of content. Does this not work in a way similar to an ARG? Leading GTA players on a breadcrumb trail? But to what?

LET’S FOCUS MORE ON ROCKSTAR

First I believe, like some others on here, that Rockstar are patching the Mount Chiliad Mystery in at predetermined intervals. The Golden Peyote mystery is just an example of how Rockstar can programme GTA lore in at will.

By patching in the mystery, Rockstar can enforce their MCM over a set timeline. How coincidental will it be if more MCM clues are uncovered the closer we get to a GTA 6 announcement? Not very I think.

SO WHEN WILL THE MYSTERY BE SOLVED?

Looking at Rockstars previous releases, games are released every 2-3 years. As games get more detailed and complex, they begin to take longer. Take a look:

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Theft_Auto#Titles

Now here we have to do some guesswork. Assuming Rockstar are releasing games on their current timeline, Grand Theft Auto 6 will most likely be released 2018 - 2019 minimum. GTA 5 was announced in 2011, 2 years before the games release. By applying this logic to future releases, GTA 6 may or may not be announced sometime in 2017.

Which means (if the mystery will ever be solved) I would keep your eyes and ears open for Mount Chiliad Mystery developments around the first quarter of 2017, or any Rockstar game announcements.
