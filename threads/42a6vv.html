I've heard about the mystery in the past, but this is my first time really looking into it. I have put plenty of hours into the game though. I also did take some time to read up on things, though some of the information was pretty old, some of this may already be outdated. But one thing that was mentioned was trying to look at this mystery as solvable in an obvious way that has been overlooked, so I think a fresh perspective may be warranted here. Also, I agree that the mystery can be solved without cheating by examining the game's code, but that certain things found are still evidence for this being solvable.

My initial thoughts on the mural. I agree that the mural depicts Mt. Chiliad. Having compared the shape with screenshots it is very close for a hand drawing. I also agree that the red Xs depict the locations for the glyphs. I have seen people argue that their positions do not match the mural, but when rotated correctly, the points are very close to where they should be, and further, there is a reason why they aren't perfectly correct. Also, three of the glyphs are used to get UFOs, and that has been tested. The other two I believe are intentionally faded because they do not have coded events.

Before I move on the three bottom symbols, I wanna say that my theory on the conclusion of this mystery is to get and fly a UFO. And to do that you will need to get the jetpack, which would also solve that mystery. And honestly, I can't think of anything else that would be a realistic reward for completing this challenge, and I think that the three bottom symbols are, in order, how to achieve that. Also, I think that the alien HUD found in the game files is what this is used for, lending some credibility to this theory.

Now, the first symbol is a UFO, that is clear. I am sure that it is the Fort Zancudo one, due to the three dots underneath it and the fact that it has to be 3 AM to find the UFO.

The second symbol is a cracked egg. I believe this is also a reference to the Zancudo UFO. The reason for this is that the UFO says SEGREGATE AND REARRANGE on it. When you rearrange segregate, you get easter egg. This UFO itself is the egg. So the second symbol is telling you that you have to break the egg. You have to crash this UFO. Now, I do not have any idea how you are able to do that. There is probably one, and only one, way to achieve this. Personally, I think that the cellphone emp may be the way to do this, but I'm not convinced that it will work for sure. I am convinced however, that this is what has to be done, somehow.

The third symbol is a man flying a jetpack. I believe that only by crashing the Zancudo UFO, will you be able to get the jetpack. It wouldn't be a surprise that the military would have the jetpack in the first place, and it also makes sense that a high tech stealth aircraft would use a jetpack instead of a parachute for covert ops. Now, I won't know how exactly you get it until the UFO is destroyed. Maybe it just spawns at the crash site. Maybe a soldier using it jettisons from the aircraft.

Once you have the jetpack, you can go back to the rest of the mural, get a UFO to show up, and then use the jetpack to capture it. Maybe you have to fly up into it's tractor beam, not sure. This whole theory will obviously need some testing to back it up.
What do you guys think?

EDIT: Ah, one thing I forgot to elaborate on. The map of the mural. I think this might be part of it too, it seems like a complexity to the mural that wouldn't exist without a purpose. Perhaps this has something to do with how to take down the UFO.

Here is what I'm identifying as the core map, the parts of the map that are real. http://i.imgur.com/9MEynir.png The rest of the lines are either the glyphs or the symbols, which are not actual parts of the map, but an attempt at obfuscating the important part. Also, I believe that the line drawn at the bottom connecting the UFO and the jetpack is another hint that the jetpack will be on the UFO. Now, I have left the dead ends on the map because they may be useful, but I have a second theory that these three ends may correlate to the three characters, but that might be a red herring. If someone can identify what this core map is of, this mystery may yet be solved.

EDIT: Another quick edit, but the core map may actually be Fort Zancudo. http://i.imgur.com/IRfkTgK.png Not saying it is, because it definitely would be a little off, but quite a few key features there are lined up. Take it with a grain of salt. I still think checking the core map against everything would still be a good idea at this point.
