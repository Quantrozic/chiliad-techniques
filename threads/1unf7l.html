My personal theory, that sometimes the simplest explanation is the most likely.

▲ 27 ptsu/willxthexthrillJan 7, 201425 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:DLC Manifest Pre-Drop Enumeration ?

I'll start by saying this is just my opinion.

We know so far that the mural says "come back when your story is complete".

We also know that Rockstar is releasing more story mode DLC this year.

Therefore, our story is not yet complete, and until it is, we arent going to get the big reveal.

I do think there is more to this than what we are seeing, but I think if there was more to it that was available now, we would have found it by now. Personally I really hope there is, flyable UFOS, alien co-op missions, jetpacks, more airplanes cars and bikes, new cheat codes, and those empty spots on the wall of ammu-nation will be filled in with all sorts of goodies. But I just don't think we are there yet. I don't think rockstar took three years or longer to make this game, just to drop everything on us all at once. I hope they release things soon, but I think all these clues we are working so hard at are just a tease of things to come.

Im not trying to be a downer, I am subscribed to this sub and read all the new posts, but a lot of them seem to be grasping at straws. I did like that theory recently about the red Xs being events we control leading to bigger events. But again I'm very surprised if no one figured out all the different possible endings yet.

Anyways this is just my 2 cents, just wanted to put this theory out there.
