Facts:

  1. The bunker doors in Fort Zancudo (elevator stuck on -1) have been shown to open when people go into spectator/NPC mode Online, which some pointed out is the same behavior when a door is supposed to open from the other side. This makes sense considering the UFO lights seen above the bunker and the fact the elevator is clearly on a lower level, and that the area immediately outside the doors is a safe area unlike the rest of the fort.
  2. The motorcycles atop Mt. Chiliad where the UFO is observed are color-coded for Michael and Franklin (the two characters who represent the "new gamer" and the "gamer whose been doing this for too long" as opposed to Trevor, the "let's just fuck shit up" rep). Some observed that when you get both up there, the game auto-saves. Ding ding ding.
  3. The mural depicts a cracked egg, a UFO and a jetpack. Jetpack aside (ambiguous as to whether or not it's even in the current game or future DLC), the cracked egg seems to represent the 100% health requirement, just as the UFO is speculated to represent the fact you need 100% completion in order to advance further into "mystery territory." The thunder requirement is clearly shown as the Lightning surrounding the mural.
  4. The UFO interior has been confirmed to exist within the game assets, and further analysis of the decompiled code points to various nuances in how the UFO is rendered with respect to distance, in-mission status, etc.

My theory: A 100%er should be able to get Franklin and Michael up to the mountain top, ideally in Epsilon robes or the 100% shirt, and try to get into the UFO from the bottom or see if a cutscene triggers when it appears with both up there.

If Trevor is around it can't hurt to have the whole group up there, and some code analyzers speculate that some of the variables referenced depend on the option of preserving all three characters for this to work.

I think either the ability to fly or else fight the UFOs follows, as the endgame, with Fort Zancudo being a potential exit point. The jetpack would make sense here since the safe zone outside those exit doors gives you just enough lead time to get to where you can jetpack away, flying low enough to evade authorities in the base.

It's also possible a kidnapping may be in order, since the desert/hippie location seems tailored for Trevor, while the other locations (Epsilon for Michael?) may be tailored for the other characters for their abductions.

Keep in mind, Star Wars: Rogue Squadron came out for the Nintendo 64 years before The Phantom Menace came out and yet it was found to contain a Naboo fighter in it afterward, hidden because they basically hashed its asset files unlike the rest of the game (disguised as audio data if I recall correctly). So there are easy ways of hiding stuff in plain sight when you're developing a complex software project like this and R* wouldn't spare that expense here.

So if my humble theory is right, we still need...the jetpack. Or maybe it'll be available after a cutscene involving the group atop the mountain.

Just my thoughts and recap on what's known so far, feel free to refute with stuff I may have missed. I prefer fact-based findings over "there's a tree here that is pointed the wrong way!!!!" and such.
