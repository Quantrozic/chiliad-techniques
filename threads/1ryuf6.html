Possible "Our path is lit" solution?

▲ 33 ptsu/lolcifer666Dec 3, 201335 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Route Theory ?

Ok, bear with me, I'm a little stoned and I didn't take any pictures, so I'm sorry now if none of this makes any sense.

Anyway.

I was playing around at the shadow rock in the Altruists camp last night. Remembered that when they attack you in the final mission, the game loads you crouching behind the rock for cover. So I ducked for cover just to see if anything from that view stuck out. I finally noticed the zig zag stairs up to the radio tower (old news, I know). So I thought, hey, maybe the path needs to be lit...on fire.

Got up, pulled out the can, and laid gas all up and down the stairway. Lit it, nothing. Noticed it went out prematurely and shot around the middle of the stairs where it had gone out. Finished, still nothing. Oh well.

Also from the crouched position I noticed what looked like odd markings on a rock near the top of the radio tower hill. Here's where I wish I had pictures so I could indicate which rock precisely, but alas I do not. Anyway, looked like it could possibly be a glyph or something, but I couldn't really make it out from behind the shadow rock, so I made a note to check it out.

After my failed stairway torching, I decided to jump around over there and attempt to get up to that rock. I climbed up to the radio tower, took the stairs down the back (past the electricity shed) and looked at the hill to map my route. As soon as I got on the hill, I tried to jump up a space between two boulders. Mind you, this is a nearly vertical hill so I didn't have high hopes. I made it up to the ledge and started mapping out my next path...

All of a sudden, LOUD metal door sounds. Like the sounds when they open a vault or a huge metal security door. Including what sounded like tumblers in a lock. I freaked out and started to turn the camera around looking for what could have made the noise. Sadly, I also touched the other joystick and fell all the way down the mountain to my death.

I have yet to do this again (subsequent attempts resulted in premature death and dismemberment). Anyone want to give it a go?

EDIT: likely debunked. Probably sound effects from the Thelma and Louise event.

EDIT TWO: I finally got around to listening to the car crash and it's not that. It's very short and doesn't keep going forever. Plus that had a more hollow sound than what I heard.
