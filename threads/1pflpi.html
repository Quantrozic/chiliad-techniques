A Theory - The Real Ending

▲ 14 ptsu/TheUnitedKajOct 29, 201315 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Choice-Tree Ending Permutation MappingMission-Context Semantic TaggingNarrative Theme Frequency Coding

Alright - lets stop and reflect.

Out of all the possible endings in GTA V, option C is the most obvious cumulative and logical ending. All the bad guys are taken out and the three protagonists live happily ever after.

But does that ending tie up ALL the loose ends?

In my opinion, ending C is just as bad as options A and B. The only difference is that the players of the other side get killed. We all know Rockstar has a professional

writing team. Does it really make sense for this ending to be the ultimate cumulation and narrative payoff of all events preceding it? When I played it, I felt a little underwhelmed. In GTA: SA, Rockstar started out with four main characters and wove it into an excellent story of greed, power, and betrayal. More importantly, the story and
its events escalated into craziness (I mean really, who would have predicted flying a jetpack stolen from a government facility after playing the first several missions). Can the same be said about GTA V? I practically waltzed into the Union Depository, and taking out each of the bad guys seemed like a job Simeon could have given at the beginning of the game.

Aside from the anticlimactic and unfitting set of endings, we also have interesting loose ends. What was that "Something out there. Off the charts" that Dave mentioned. What really do the FIB, IAA, and Merryweather want to annihilate each other over to get to? And where do the Illuminati and aliens fit in with this story? As extras? Easter Eggs meant to be put on a post-game checklist to be retrieved with invincibility cheats? That can't be right. I mean for god's sake there's FIB flying saucers, an Annunaki UFO, and a secret bunker in Fort Zancudo.

The only answer to all these questions and loose ends (including things we consider to be easter eggs) is that there is a hidden, more climactic, and more perfect ending stashed away only to be unlocked under certain conditions.

Anyone who's explored the anomalies discussed on this subreddit can see how this would be plausible. Some have even proposed the three items in the mural relate to the three protagonists and so on. And they're partially right.

Wouldn't it make more sense to wait about taking out Devin Weston and Steve Hanes until some light had been shed on their big government conspiracy? Wouldn't it make sense for them to explain the existence of FIB flying saucers, aliens, and even the mural itself? Wouldn't it make sense if there was one last great attempt to catapult the GTA V story into the narrative payoffs and themes that were subtly being set up even since the very first mission (Aliens in the movie studio)?

Wouldn't it make more sense for Micheal and Franklin to do one last job for the government while Trevor sneaks into Fort Zancudo, takes the minigun from the watchtower, enables the elevators via a computer in the watchtower, proceeds to the bunkers while unleashing his revenge on the Air Force for never accepting him? While he goes down the bunker, gets the jetpack, and finally "earns his wings"?

I am not entirely sure how the rest of this mission would play out, and I have left out a lot of my own ideas that might be too speculative. The points here are these: 1) We have a bevy of unexplained content that doesn't seem right to be left out of the story, and 2) Rockstar has left many thematic narrative setups in the story open and without
meaningful payoffs.

TLDR:
Hidden fourth ending that has classic GTA and Rockstar attitude, more climactic ending, and incorporates much of what we think is easter egg material. The means by which we unlock this ending are up for speculation, but is a much better idea than combing the game world for an egg (or trying to decode tunnel lights ;) )

Thanks for reading!
