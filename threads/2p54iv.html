Follow up to the Petition post

Documented under:Expanded & Enhanced Build Diffing

Hello. So a helpful redditor pointed me to the website Change.org when I asked how to go about making a petition. I went to that site and wow it was easy! Pretty cool site. We are definitely lucky to have the internet in our lives.

Anyway, so next step was a box where I had to fill out why I was petitioning Rockstar games. And as I typed, well, it hit me that I was being a little ridiculous. I felt silly reading it back to myself. As a couple fellow hunters pointed out in my last post (albeit not very kindly, but that's the internet I guess), I suppose patience is the name of the game.

I still feel shafted by Rockstar, I still think we've found all there is to find (most of the mysteries loose ends feel more like the ghosts of cut content, honestly), and I still am grateful for this easter egg that's given me months of great reading, and weeks of frustrated, depressing searching/testing/and yes, honking (sigh). But yeah typing out my complaint in petition form, I couldn't help but feel... Bratty. Its my own opinion that there's nothing left. But until a new breaking development, I'm done hunting. I can't afford to join the new fun on next gen, which definitely contributes to my fuck you attitude towards Rockstar. But I'll frequent this site because I dig the vibe, man.

My last post devolved into name calling and accusations, as it so often does online. Sorry to the people I got snarky with.

Really wish that by now we would have found something in the files or anywhere in the game that is DEFINITIVE PROOF that there's more to this mystery. But we haven't. Its all based on peoples interpretations of the "evidence". The mural was proof there was more to the mystery. The glyphs were proof. The zancudo bunker was proof. Unfortunately we found all of them after we found the UFO'S. (Well except maybe the mural). We're all just San Andreas Bigfoot hunters again. Except this time Rockstar would rather stay quiet, and let intrigue build for the next game's sure-to-be-awesome easter egg.

If I'm wrong about all this, yayyy!!!
But if I'm right, man I feel dumb having wasted so much time on this. That's the only reason I wanted Rockstar to say, well....anything.

Kifflom brother uncle sister mothers.

Can't wait to go there and see how it inspired GTA 5's Sandy Shores (in real life its called Desert Shores). But this was off topic lol
