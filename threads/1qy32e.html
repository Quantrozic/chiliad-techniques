Okay, so after this dry spell my favorite theory so far has been that we are supposed to cause an earthquake.
Proof it could be a plausible theory:

  • There is proof of this on an in game website where it says 'Same as always, triple digit heat and moderate to severe chances of earthquakes.
  • There are also mentions of a fault line in game. California is known for earthquakes, but I think this is something different.
  • GTAV was advertised as having dynamic natural disasters.

Look at the mural, those wavy lines could be cracks in the crust and the lightning bolts a visual representation of the earth shaking/moving.
Perhaps the mural is a guide of where to detonate and where the fault lines are?

Two theories gleaned from different posts on how to cause an earthquake:

  • The Dam, there are quite a bit of doors on the dam, and it is designed interestingly to allow players to reach those doors.
  • The nuclear bomb from the heist mission. There are two bombs, and only one is stolen. Would it be possible to use the other one to cause an earthquake?

Can any of you think of a plausible way to cause an earthquake in game?
