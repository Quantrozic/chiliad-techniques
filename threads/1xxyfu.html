Probably time for all of us to start afresh.

I for one am going back to North Yankton (for the first time) this very lovely full-mooned Saint Valentine's night.

So here's a couple of things I am thinking of:

  • Don't use shared or modded saves and don't try to game the game with multiple versions of your playthrough in a dozen different files. These are the evil twins that Ron Jakowski talks about at length on his radio show. The lady who assured him it wasn't creepy to have sex with your twin must have been a devil in disguise, because it's a frickin' abomination!
  • Do take regular breaks, and while we all like puff or a pill now and then, especially while gaming, don't do so much IRL drugs that you lose touch with what your heart is telling you. One thing I plan to do is have a break at the end of each day in-game, and use that time to read about another piece of the puzzle, since there's so much to get through. But the writings have been given that we might KNOW.
  • Don't feel bad, it's forgiven, just come on over and be made whole.
