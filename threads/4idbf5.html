Unofficial guidelines for posting/chatting/hunting

▲ 27 ptsu/walkeronlineMay 8, 201623 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗

These are just a few personal things I've noticed with people who post on the sub/chat in the discord that needs to be addressed.

1. If your being downvoted/trolled, there is probably a reason for it.

Usually the reason is pretty straightforward - You are posting about something that's already discovered, you have a weak/incomplete/inconclusive/idiotic theory or you are suggesting something that has already been tried. Most of the time, people will simply downvote the post and let you know (sometimes with gruff) that its already been tried/done/talked about. Which leads me to...

2. Don't take it personally.

You may be posting about something you think is amazing/a breakthrough/something you feel nobody is paying attention to. Most of the time, we ARE paying attention, but we aren't doing anything because of reason 1. This is not a time to be upset about it. We are usually pretty fair about giving honest real feedback, but because this is Reddit, there will always be a few people to rain on your parade. Take it in stride and read the advice you are given as to why your ideas are being ignored. The mods here are usually pretty clear with people about the issues with posts. What you DON'T do is think that we are attacking you personally and retaliate with personal attacks. This is starting to happen more frequently and only serves to distract from the overall purpose. The only time you are going to get people to treat you badly is when you are obnoxiously asking for it by feeling ignored/entitled. It's a bad move and it will come with consequences if you don't comply with the mods. We are trying to cut back on the number of angry discussions and personal attacks we make both in the sub and on the discord chat, but know that if you end up in one, its probably of your own doing.

3. Everyone has a theory. Everyone.

You more than likely have a theory, complete with pictures and a wall of text explaining why your theory is the one theory. It probably isn't. We have nearly 3 years of searching this game and for everything we find, 5 people have a theory that it fits into. We have yet to find a theory that unifies things in a way that unlocks/advances the mystery. That doesn't mean you shouldn't go ahead and post it, just realize that rule #1 and #2 apply to it. This also includes posting on other people's theories about how "Ah! This fits with my theory" type posts. Sometimes its ok, most of the time its simply to steal attention to your theory. Its a super fine line, so its easier to be humble and stay on topic in the thread you are in.

4. We are not a personal army - Test your own theories

This is actually a reddit rule in general, but I think we should restate it here: We are not here to do everything you need done. If you come in with theories expecting others to test it, your going to have a bad time. In general, posts about you actually testing a theory get more attention and further testing than posting about a theory you want tested. The idea is people who sit back and theorize can throw out a million different ideas until one sticks, but people are usually busy testing their own things. It is better to test your theories yourself and show proof. We have had quite a few people posting about theories who don't even play/own the game, which usually ends up badly. The overall rule is, don't post without testing yourself. If you can't test yourself, you need the worlds best theory or a very good reason you can't.

5. Everything is NOT a clue

Rockstar is great about providing a lot of inside jokes and little details in their games meant for people to find. As far as easter eggs go, there's a TON of them that might or might not be related to the overall mystery. It is easier to error on the side of caution than certainty in these cases. People will post about how they see glitches in textures, oddities in lighting, a random anagram from an obscure piece of text or see patterns that just aren't there. We cannot state that anything you find isn't a part of the mystery, but common sense says that most things are not.

6. Read the rules, the FAQ's, sidebars and use the search function

A lot of really bad situations can be avoided by reading up and searching to see if something you want to talk about exists already. The search function is certainly not the best it can be, but it's what we got. Follow the rules, post, collaborate, and don't be an ass. Pretty simple.
