A few notes...
Based on discussion here and my own observation, I think it's safe to conclude that the Chiliad Mural is supposed to be aligned to the west. The glyph under the observation platform AND the mural itself are both facing perfectly west...the same direction the cable car runs. This side of the mountain contains 4/5 of the glyphs. The boxes with red 'x's are likely just telling us 'somewhere on this mountain you'll find clues that ultimately lead to viewing the UFO at the peak'.
