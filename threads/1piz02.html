The 'river' that runs from the mountain to Alamo Sea.

I've been following this from the start, reading/testing as many posts as possible, and I don't think I've seen any speculate about this river, sorry if I missed it if anyone did.

The glyph near the 'tank' with an arrow on top that is, to my knowledge, the end of the trail of the little arrow signs, is the glyph of the curvy trail. Whether or not is related, what I found might be connected to something. The river that runs from the east side of Chiliad down to Alamo Sea doesn't even have water flowing down, none visible, except there is animation/sound from your feet that shows you're treading through water. Okay, so maybe it's a very slow moving river. But the water animations/sound continue on up the mountain past the 'river' marking on the map. Try for yourself? It is the river flowing from Chiliad curving down SouthEastward into the Alamo Sea. Go to where the river ends on Chiliad on the map and continue walking up the mountain to find the 'real' start of the river, perhaps? There has been talks of draining/earthquake/something at the dam, perhaps you can trigger more water to flow, or the earthquake triggers more water to flow, there is a rain glyph after all. Has anyone checked the river during a 3am rain that triggers the UFO? Or anything else involving this river?
