Let me start by saying that I love lurking this sub, physically checking out peoples findings and reading theories. I would love to test a lot of ideas from our contributors, but some are hard if not impossible to look at if I cant find the location. Even when its nicknamed I can have a hard time finding it.

.

So, I propose we start using coordinates to share locations when talking about or theories and findings. gta-5-map.com allows for the easy use of coordinates. In the left bar underneath the on/off switch there is a butoon with a chain symbol.Clicking that gives you the coordinates in a hand url form.
