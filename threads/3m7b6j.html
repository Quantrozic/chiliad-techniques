I found this symbol very interesting. It is of an M, with a lightning bolt in the symbol. The numbers written on the wall next to it are '5369'. Doing a cursory search for the phrase '5369 meaning' brings up as the first result, a word called philédonos (φιλήδονοι). This word means 'lover of pleasure'. Its Greek Strong's number is 5369, which literally means "lover of pleasure". Philédonos is made up of the words philos and hedone. It is actually from the Greek Lexicon Bible, and It leads to verses in 2 Timothy 3-4.

"This know also, that in the last days perilous times shall come. 2 For men shall be lovers of their own selves, covetous, boasters, proud, blasphemers, disobedient to parents, unthankful, unholy, 3 Without natural affection, trucebreakers, false accusers, incontinent, fierce, despisers of those that are good, 4 Traitors, heady, highminded, lovers of pleasures more than lovers of God;"

Somehow I feel this is exactly what this symbol is trying to communicate. It is fitting for Michael, and describes his nature also. This also fits the theme of everything else I have found regarding symbolism in the game.

This is the Rod of Asclepius, which has the same meaning as the Caduceus. In the mystery schools, the serpent, also known as a dragon, is a symbol of Lucifer, which is wrapped around the symbolic representation of the phallus. This confirms to me the dragon sign directly across the street. Everything here seems interrelated.
