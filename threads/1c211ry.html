Hello community and mystery hunters of GTAV.

I was in the vicinity of the Altruist Camp with Trevor, conducting investigations using the radio frequencies that you can pick up with Trevor. It occurred to me that the SoulWaxFM logo might be related, which is a tower with some rays. These rays are the same ones that are on the Chiliad mural. So, I thought, why not go to the Altruist camp? Upon arrival, I noticed that the radio tower at the top of the camp is identical to the one at the Soulwax FM lake. Nevertheless, I proceeded to input the classic codes to change the weather and put lightning or a storm to match the Soulwax logo. Unfortunately, I didn't find or hear anything relevant. I thought about leaving, but instead, I activated the cheats once again to change the weather condition to snowy.
Here's when I start narrating the events of the video I posted at the beginning:
Feeling a bit frustrated for not finding anything, I entered the code to change the weather to snow. After a few seconds, I noticed that to my left, on a hill with a cliff, several police patrols had appeared. At that moment, I only saw that. It's worth noting that it was 7:00 PM on a Sunday. I entered the weather change code seconds before it turned 7:00 PM. Additionally, this save file is one that I've completed 100%, using years of research to make decisions, etc. Also, I want to highlight that while this save file is at 100%, I mean 100% in the sense that I only need a couple of online trophies to say that I've fully explored the content accessible in GTA, including hunting the beast...
I also want to highlight that I've spent hours researching on this forum and countless hours on YouTube, and I hadn't seen this Easter egg before. Please comment if you've seen it before.

Anyway, seeing this event, I started conducting tests, like trying the process again to repeat this event. After a couple of attempts like the initial one, I managed to trigger the event again. But this time, I just approached to get more details, ignoring factors like if you get too close with the zoom of a weapon or your phone, this event triggers, or eventually, with your character approaching, it triggers. In the scene, we have two or three police patrols along with a helicopter, a convertible vehicle with Mexican guitars in the back, as well as several police officers, two or three, two women who are in the vehicle, along with a man from the IAA or sometimes with a blue jacket.
The event seems to be a scene that occurs at 7:00 PM while it's snowing on a Sunday (at least that's how I managed it), where several police patrols, a helicopter, and the aforementioned vehicle appear. It seems that they force the women in the vehicle to commit suicide by jumping off the cliff with the vehicle.
After learning these details, I proceeded to conduct tests like shooting at the helicopter, killing the passengers of the vehicle to see what happens, or killing the police officers who seem to only approach the vehicle, causing it to accelerate and jump into the void. I also went to check how the vehicle wreckage looked when it jumped without much progress. I also want to emphasize that none of the NPCs interacted with Trevor. You can see the rest in the video.
I've been playing this game since its release, and this Easter egg is new to me, which makes me wonder if it's just a scene or if perhaps something needs to be done, or maybe I should be with another character, I don't know, I feel like I need ideas to continue investigating.
I'd also like to know if anyone has GTA San Andreas to capture a clip. It's very simple, in the desert, there's a giant radio telescope. When you approach it with a vehicle, listening to RadioX and with a wanted level, you can pick up an interference that sounds like a Cluckin Bell ad, but in the background, you can clearly hear the horn of the Space Docker. I would appreciate it if someone could help me with that clip as it's not on YouTube.
Mystery hunters, if you have any recent theories, not the usual ones, please leave your comment. I'll be delighted to investigate everything you have in mind.
