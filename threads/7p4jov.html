Yo yo sup everyone. Happy New Year. Whenever I think of the "Jetpack Shadow" on the Altruist Stone, it makes me think of the Mayans and their temples. On a the day of the equinox, a shadow would form on the side of the temple representing the feathered serpent coming down. Here's one example, Kukulkan. So I wonder if Rockstar was inspired by the Mayans, when creating the Altruist Camp and Jetpack Shadow? There is a hippie in game that says" The Mayans had it right, but where did they go?"

In Didsomebodysayyoga we see Xenomorphs beaming up Michael, so what is the Xenomorph connection? They say Xenomorphs need a host organism in order to reproduce. Is Michael holding an Alien inside him? Or gave birth to one already. There is a comedy show in GTAIV, with Katt Williams, and he has a joke where he mentions a person buying a car with bad credit looked at like he is an Alien, makes me think of Michael's son, Jimmy.

The other day I was watching a show on the History channel about the Zodiac Killer in San Francisco. They were trying to decode his cryptic messages and had a supercomputer helping them. They were feeding the computer with information and different programs to answer their questions, but the computer was no help. Someone then cracked it, or at least part of the Zodiac murders. All the victims were killed on the day of the "Dali", or a new moon. Some people believe the infinite 8 killer is a representation of the Zodiac killer. Maybe we have to take hikers to the Altruist camp, on the day of the new moon flip.

The other day, while in GTAonline, I was wearing a sweater with a white tiger and the word Yogarishima on it. There is a store in GTAV with this name also. Crustpunker and his son translated Yogarishima for me, it's an island between Heaven and Hell. Pretty interesting, since some people believe GTAIV and GTAV to be in some sort of purgatory, which would make Karma a factor in the world if you wanted to get to Heaven.
