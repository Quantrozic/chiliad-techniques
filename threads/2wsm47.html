The main reason why Karma Theory is implausible

▲ 12 ptsu/LlamaGuy69Feb 22, 201525 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Choice-Branch Outcome Tabulation ?

Lets just entertain the idea that Rockstar intended for the mystery to be solved by a series of "correct" choices made throughout the game. This is the main idea of the Karma theorists. It's important to consider the big picture here. Lets just imagine that its several years from now and that the mystery have been solved and the jetpack has been found. First of all, this is going to be huge news in the gaming world and most people will associate GTA V with the mystery and the secret jetpack itself. Of course, most people who play the game years from now are going to want the jetpack. If Karma theory is true, this would force future players into making all of the choices that lead to the jetpack and completely eliminate freedom of choice throughout the game. The concept of making your own choices is the heart and soul of GTA V, and a Karma system would completely ruin this. R* was very careful to make sure that the player has freedom of choice, but that no matter what choices the player makes in game they are still able to reach 100% and take part in the mystery. Its a pretty big stretch to believe that R* would let players start the mystery and not be able to finish it.

To sum it up, karma theory seems pretty implausible when you consider the nasty impacts it would have on GTA V such as 1) Eliminating freedom of choice for future players 2) Forcing future players who want the jetpack to search online for "correct" choices and completely spoil the story for themselves on their first play through 3) Completely piss off nearly everybody who put in many hours to reach 100% only to realize that they have to do it all over again.

This is absolutely not a bash on Karma theorists, and I am still open to the idea however big a stretch it may be. However, when rationalizing this theory, its a good idea to consider all of these impacts on the game, because Rockstar without a doubt considered them as well. Cheers!
