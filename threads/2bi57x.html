I just want to post(reiterate) some important facts and also relay some thoughts I have about the glyphs/"clues" on the side of the mountain.

For the past week or so I have re-delved myself into this mystery hoping that maybe we can solve it before it is released on PC. (When we expect to be bombarded with new things that will undoubted help solve the mystery in very little time). To do this I use only the facts as a base and attempt to build on them with theories and then test them to see if they lead to anything or fail (and most of the time they have failed). My focus has been primarily on the glyphs displayed on the mountain and mountain mural and determining their meaning.

So first I want to lay out some facts we already know and some we knew but didn't exactly realize we knew <- I will explain that part below.

What we already know about the mural and glyphs:
Mural-

-Depicts Mt. Chiliad, showing the mountain and a squared top (the skyrail building); The image of the mountain drawn on the mural is taken from a far northwestern or north-northwestern position and incorporates 2-3 faces (as it is a 2-D image).

-Has a red glyph on top, uniquely shown with five red hashes about the top and two red hashes at the bottom

-Has five boxes connected by lines, each box containing a red X; These boxes indicate where on the mountain you can find five (5) glyphs or "clues" (explained later).

-Has three symbols on the bottom, also inside of boxes and connected by lines to the boxes with X's; Inside each box is a different symbol- The first presumably a flying saucer vehicle, as it depicts a saucer shaped craft with a person inside; The second an egg, presumably alien in origin as it mimics the iconic egg from the movie 'Alien' (as well, the "aliens" that are shown in the game also resemble the iconic Alien from the movie "Alien".); And the third a person wearing something on their back that resembles a jetpack, presumably so the player makes the connection between that image and the famous much-loved Jetpack vehicle that appeared in the earlier game GTA:San Andreas.

-The meaning of the lines on the mural has not yet been 100% determined; The small lines immediately coming off of the boxes that indicate where the glyphs/clues are 100% correspond to the direction in which you must face in order to see the glyphs in those indicated locations.

The glyphs/"clues"-
THIS IS WHERE SOME NEW/RE-REALIZED SHIT IS

-In the game files the textures for the only 3 glyphs that anyone has found refer to the textures as "clue" in their filenames.

-We have 5 glyphs, (I don't give a damn what people have called these glyphs before, but I will be referring them to the following:) The Rain glyph, The Moon glyph, The Mountain glyph, The Faded glyph, and the Incomplete glyph.

-The three glyphs/clues that were found in the texture files are called different things: The glyph with the cloud and rain drops is called "rain" in the filename; The glyph with a moon and 3 lines is called "moon" in the filename; And the glyph with the triangle that has a square on top and a squiggly line running down the center is called "mountain" in the filename.

[ THIS IS VERY IMPORTANT BECAUSE THIS MEANS THAT THE "MOUNTAIN" GLYPH 100% UNDENIABLY REPRESENTS A MOUNTAIN- This means no more jackass theories about it resembling any thing else ]

[ALSO, This means that the "Moon" glyph might have more meaning to it rather than just 3:00 during nighttime. I would still rule out anything dealing with moon phases, it seems unlikely because if they wanted a full moon we would never be able to tell it was a full moon if they just drew a circle, as well the moon shape depicted is a STEREOTYPICAL moon shape- This leads me to believe, in addition to the 3 lines indicating 3:00, if maybe the moon has to simply be out (with no particular phase), just OUT!

Now I want continue exclusively with my thoughts- My focus recently has been on the Mountain glyph since it has not yet been 100% proven to signify anything at this point. My focus has been on it and it's corresponding sand glyph, which appears to try and explain the glyph. Now other than FINALLY realizing/knowing that the triangle and box is a mountain, I still don't know which mountain it means Chiliad or Gordo (either is very possible, and neither are proven) AND I still have no idea what the rest of it means: the squiggly line, the wow signal characters, and the semi arrow with possible N.

BUT, I have realized some other things about this glyph. When you take the rail car up to the mountain, right before you reach the top your camera view is changed and it is forced on that glyph, LIKE THEY INTENTIONALLY WANT YOU TO SEE IT. Now what even weirder is if you go to where the sand glyph is you see it oriented in a very strange direction, BUT if you face that direction then look at the map you find it leads DIRECTLY to the rail car station at the bottom of the mountain, which when you take it you are forced to see the exact same glyph right before reaching the top. This leads me to believe that the orientation of the glyph was supposed to direct you to finding/seeing the same glyph on the mountain. This still doesn't explain the squiggly line on both glyphs, or the wow signal and half-arrow and N on the Sand glyph version. These are the things I am currently still trying to figure out because I think figuring it out will obviously get us a step further. I just wanted to reiterate some things for people who are unaware of them and shed some light on things that I have heard no one yet discuss, rather than keeping this all to myself I thought I'd share it and hope someone else's mind might come up with some other ideas using this information that I have not and get things moving on the right track again! :)
