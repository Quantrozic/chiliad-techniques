Miasma Chorale

How's it going, ChiliadMystery?

This time I've got something a bit strange that I noticed at Betsy O'Neil Pavilion, Little Seoul.
Well, in this theatre, there's a kind of 'orchestra' that you can hear if you go near the entrances, close to a kind of ticket office. It's quite faint and easily drowned out by ambient noise or traffic. However, I was wandering around the area and noticed something curious; apart from the Miasma Chorale advertisement (which is very interesting because of all the strangeness surrounding the Miasma), if we follow the path from the car park to a kind of basement with glass doors, the sound of that strange 'orchestra' becomes much louder and easier to hear. As I followed this path, I noticed heat emanating from some kind of vents. These vents continue underground and are accompanied by blue lights, and there are NPCs driving through this lower area in their cars. Personally, I had never noticed this situation before, as I had not paid particular attention to this building.

Later, I switched to Franklin and took the Space Docker to this basement, and then it started making that strange sound that it only emits in very specific parts of the map. I have no idea what it means, if there is something here we should do or if it is a point of interest to consider.

I am not sure if anyone else had already noticed those heat emissions and the sound of the Docker in this part of the map.

Anyway, I'll leave some images of these "emanations" (which remind me of a symbol on the red mural) in the comments, along with a couple of links about the Miasma Chorale and the sound of the Space Docker that I recorded myself.

Thanks for reading the post, best regards, and shoot for the stars.
