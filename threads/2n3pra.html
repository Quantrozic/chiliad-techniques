Rockstar's roadmap to singleplayer mysteries?

▲ 6 ptsu/the_monotonistNov 22, 20145 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Named-Entity Extraction Across Patch Notes and Newswire

As you probably know, the murder mystery was solved:

http://youtu.be/_3Jznik7N9A

Now think back to the 'A Picket Fence and a Dog Named Skip' video that Rockstar released awhile back:

http://youtu.be/rb1vOrm9SX4

The video begins with Solomon, who is closely related to the current murder mystery. You can see the 'letter on the desk of a friend' in the video:

http://i.imgur.com/jFcESOy.png

Rockstar said that there would eventually be more single player content in the future, so maybe the purpose of this video was to act as a roadmap to future mysteries?

I think we should take note of what exactly is shown in this video so we can keep an eye on them each time the game updates.

  • Solomon Richards "Nothing but make-believe will ever feel quite real again"
  • Peter Dreyfuss and a mime on Vespucci Beach "Tragedy, pathos, tedium...I'm bored"

(since he is next 'in order' maybe we should be looking into him next?)
