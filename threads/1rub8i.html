One nagging thing I can't stop thinking about.

▲ 18 ptsu/k4rm4tt4ckDec 1, 201323 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Hidden Map Art & Environmental Pattern Recognition

So let me preface by saying I've never been fully convinced that the Chilead mural is meant to be overlaid or referenced beyond the mountain itself. That said, I've also never been able to erase some of the images people have made of the mural superimposed on top of the whole map. So the idea that keeps nagging me is that if you place the mural's Eye above Chilead's observation deck, and then place the UFO box above Zancudo, then that puts the jetpack box over The Hippie Camp, and the cracked egg in The Alamo Sea.

The little oddity about that which keeps nagging me is thinking first about how fascinated Rockstar had become with The Salton Sea while making this game, which is why The Alamo Sea is such a rich feature. Combine that with the fact that the real San Andreas Fault runs basically right into The Salton Sea, and the image of the cracked egg floating above The Alamo Sea... I can't shake it. It's probably just me seeing Jesus on toast, but until this mystery is solved that image will feel too ironic.
