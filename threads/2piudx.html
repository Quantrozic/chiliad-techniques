Stuff we are waiting for(updates)

▲ 12 ptsu/toeknee1Dec 17, 20145 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Audio Bank Delta Monitoring

We know that online heists are coming soon, and they were announced about a year ago(or more), it got me thinking about the other stuff that has been announced(or more likely) rumoured to be coming as updates.

I know I read here, and saw a video containing some audio files, where Lester is talking about the new casino that's opened up in LS(Im assuming the one near the racetrack), and horse racing. The same audio had Lester talking about car racing inside the Maze Bank Arena(like San Andreas and Vice City had- stock car ring, destruction derby, and the motorbike stunt course).

We also know about the much anticipated(by us here especially) update to single player. New missions for Frank, Mikey and Trevor. Personally, this is what Im most excited about.

Other things that have been found in either game files, or patch/update files, etc. Hydra plane, jetpack, flyable ufo, Solomon mission that cant be triggered as yet.

What else has been on the rumour tree for a while that hasn't yet been delivered??

Then theres stuff like Trevor and Maudes bounty missions, having more of these would've made the games playability last a lot longer. You get an email with a description of the person, and a satellite picture of the area the person is hiding in(so much like the treasure maps on RDR, where you need to use your knowledge of the place to find where they are hiding, and arrest them). In my eyes, R* missed the opportunity to go big with this one.
Franklins assassination missions from Lester, could've been a lot more to this too.

I know theres a lot of ranting here, and Im sorry, but we know Rockstar watch us on here from time to time, and I hope they read this and can give us some more fun things to do, its truly an amazing game with so much in it already.
