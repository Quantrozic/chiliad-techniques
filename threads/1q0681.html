What if the EYE is not an EYE?

▲ 23 ptsu/halfstache78Nov 6, 201332 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Color-Theory Palette Significance StudyJungian Archetype Mapping of Glyphs ?Semiotics

I took a step back from all of this and decided to ask my gf for her input on the whole Chiliad mystery. She helped me reach a few new conclusions concerning the very core of this mystery. Just about every theory and opinion has been predicated on the accepted definitions of the elements of the mural. The EYE glyph being the unifying factor. But, what if the EYE is not actually an EYE?

Without any preface I asked my gf what she saw when I showed her the mural. Her first reaction was the main symbol looked like a SUN. We began discussing the rest of it and the more we did the more it made sense the the EYE glyph was in fact a SUN symbol. If you look through the wiki under Symbolism all the real world counterparts all point to sun references. In fact, the topmost glyph is almost identical to the sun symbol that means "Zenith of the Sun." The zenith being the highest point in the sky the sun reaches. In other words, 12 o'clock high, or straight up. With this interpretation all of the other glyphs make a bit more sense. The SUN ZENITH with a rain cloud, for example, means while it raining look towards the sun's zenith. Even if the sun is not present (ie. night time) the sun's zenith remains the same. It's just the point where the sun reaches its highest point.

This interpretation might also give us an explanation for the partial-faded glyph. We have a partial SUN ZENITH symbol. This could mean that this glyph is telling us to look for something that sits somewhere not quite as high as the sun's highest point in the sky. Doesn't the Ft. Zancudo ufo sit about 2/3rds the way up, not quite the highest point you can go? That's just one possibility.

Since most of the clues dealing with these glyphs have already been discovered it probably doesn't help much in terms of understanding the conditions they're describing. We figured it out regardless. However, it may help us in ways I haven't fully considered yet. A lot of recent theories have been focusing on the moon, moon phases, and the sun. With this interpretation of the mural glyphs those theories seem to be much more on point than perhaps previously thought. We already know about the strange plane that appears when you shoot the sun from the Altruist camp. I wonder what other strange things might happen while interacting the with moon and sun in other places, conditions, and times. Thinking about the glyphs this way just might open up a few more doors, both figuratively and literally.

The more research I did on ancient glyphs, pictographs, symbols, etc. the more it made sense that what we're dealing with are most likely astrological and/or elemental symbolism. These things are very common themes and all closely tied together. These types of glyphs and drawings are more likely to represent something in the environment, in the sky, or in the stars. They drew things they would see around them (sun, moon, trees, birds, stars, etc.) more-so than symbols that represent how they interact with their world (ie. an eye glyph).

To further support this, the alien glyphs at the hippy camp, when compared to real astrological symbols, just look like a mash-up of various sun and moon symbols. Whether they mean anything or not, the similarities are great.
Also, the Altruist glyphs are obviously a collection of sun and moon symbols. So, throughout the game, it looks like we have a vast array of references to both the sun and moon. I believe those are the keys. Figure out the role the sun and moon play and solve the mystery. I know a lot of this stuff has been discussed, but it felt disjointed and unconnected. Viewing the EYE as a SUN symbol seems to tie it all together into a unifying theory that might help set a foundation for further exploration.

I ran out of tinfoil, but this mixing bowl fits just fine.

We also developed some new ideas about the meaning of the larger glyphs at the bottom (ufo/egg/jetpack), but I'll save that for another time so this doesn't turn into a novel.
