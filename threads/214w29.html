Searching random character's names for any reference and found something you may be able to use when testing theories:

(A) Where do we want to be?
(B) How will we know when we get there?
(C) Where are we right now?
(D) How do we get there from here?, and
(E) What is changing in the future external environment, that we need to take into account?

Take his meaning from it and apply ours. Example:

(A) At the end of this mystery
(B) When there's a rocket strapped to our back
(C) Sitting on top of mount Chiliad crying
(D) A giant drill? A volcano? Divine intervention?
(E) Well, there's a dog with an Australian accent in my room now and he's put this 'thing' on my bed.

This is a joke. Lighten up.
