Okay, you'll have to forgive me, I have a decent but not expert level of mapping. I may be wrong, but as far as I know, my explanation is correct.

It's old, but a classic, and brilliant. I'm hoping most of you know Halo 3? Well, in several maps, most famously Sandtrap and Sandbox, there were "The Da Vinci Eggs". Mysterious egg shapes located at the top of each tower surrounding the map. They were named that because of their texture, which was a blue/purple/mulitcolor group of letters.

tl;dr Halo 3 has the same kinda thing

What they did, however, explains their purpose. In Sandtrap, they would fire a light orange beam at the ground under you (only really visible if you drive and turn the camera to face the tower, the beam sort of lights the vehicle up making it seem gold/orange). What this does is give away the position of you, and spawns mines which pop up and kill you. Easier than adding individual mines and lagging the game and restricting things in the map. For those who dont know the map, picture a circle pile of sand, and thats pretty much it. Now put six towers around equidistant and thats what keeps you in the map.

In Sandbox, they were made much more violent. The beams themselves became weapons, and were basically instakill lasers. There's a collectible hidden at the edge of the map, meaning you need to build a map to protect yourself from the weapons. Or haul ass and drift.

Also, in other maps, these can be seen as eggs or cubes, most known to be under the man-cannons built into the map (antigravity pads that push you a certain distance/direction, used to hop up to higher levels)

tl;dr they're triggers for weapons. gravity/speed/direction changes. hidden objects (GTAV) etc

Basically, what you're seeing under the map, is no different to a skybox, or the game's coding. You are seeing what you're not supposed to see. It is just a simple tool to control the game. The spinning textures you see are the Da Vinci Eggs. They are the tools of "Hey other Rockstar employees, leave this alone, but if you need to edit the UFO, this is where all the coding/info is" and "Hi, I'm a spinning texture, I am what generates the UFOs and lights when my coding detects that my requirements are met. Thank you for seeing the cool thing I made. But please dont clip under the map and see me because I'm self conscious and youre not supposed to be down here". Thank you (/-\) "

tl;dr you're wasting your time looking at the developer object creating the UFOs in game and not actually looking at the UFO itself and stuff related to it. We aren't gonna get anywhere knowing about the texture, UNLESS we find more under the map and then find more clues or UFOs from that. Ignore it's existence, it's just there to put the UFO in the map once the requirements are met. (and obviously for FZ its the corresponding light effect)

Thanks, I'm just trying to clear stuff up, since I keep seeing the same stuff as Halo 3 with the "wooo mystery thing must go somewhere. It is there to control the UFOs, and to make it easier to edit them for R*. Think of it as the projector, that is only relevant to R*. The mystery itself, and the UFOs themselves, are what we need to deal with. As I said, only two things can we use these for. One, you just learnt something from me, yay. And two, if we find any more, we know its a trigger with requirements (lights = time, chiliad UFO = weather, time, player position etc).

(note, that's assuming my knowledge of map making and development is correct about this, but it does seem the most likely explanation, otherwise we'd be able to legitimately see the texture. It's hidden because it's irrelevant to us. As far as I'm aware, I am fully correct, but anyone older and more experienced is welcome to chip in. I did basic stuff I taught myself, I'm not opening GTA's files and making programs, and I'm certainly not pretending to be an expert. Just my two cents from everything I know about developing this kinda thing.

Thanks :)
