Crown Images

Lester Revelation and Moses dialogue, alien with a crown

It starts with an intriguing piece of graffiti. The word CODE with cracks running through it. Crack the code. Also, there is a Crown. There are a lot of repeated shapes used in the game, on products, places etc. The crown is one of them, so I located them all (I think). It seems there are 26 places or product lines where you can find crowns.

  1. Albany car manufacturer
  2. Ammunation - on four weapons
  3. Betsy O'Neill Pavilion - Quid Novi? 6 banner
  4. Blêuter'd champagne
  5. Buckingham Luxor jet - tail-fin artwork
  6. Crown Jewels Motel
  7. Del Perro Pier Entrance
  8. Estancia Cigars
  9. Gamelords - on the Drag-On 6 posters
  10. Garde Ta Foy - motto above the Little Seoul Church doors
  11. Heavy Flow exhaust systems signs
  12. High Life Credit Card
  13. Jewels sign
  14. Kingz team logo
  15. Kortz - Russian literature relative to the political changes in Europe banner
  16. Movie posters - An American Divorce, Shoulder of Orion II awards
  17. Overflod car manufacturer
  18. Pisswasser beer
  19. Ponsonbys - three argyle sweaters
  20. Ragga Rum and Pride Brew - on the same billboard
  21. Richards Majestic
  22. Ring Midas jewellery store
  23. The Feud logo
  24. The Royale apartments
  25. The Viceroy hotel
  26. Wags To Riches pet store

A few notes:

  • I thought maybe Vangelicos also, but I believe they are tiaras in their images, not crowns, although I'm not sure about the differences.
  • The hippy camp when the mirror-effect-trick thing is applied forms an alien with a crown.

So what to do with 26 crowns? Well, there happens to be another piece of prominent graffiti that says 26 SET 51 34. 26 sets of crowns? But what about the numbers?

Next step: as Ron says on The Blaine County Community Hour, "OPEN YOUR EARS!" I had an idea ages ago (different account name) that there may be a book code/cipher hidden in the game after listening to one of the Chakra Attack episodes. In it Cheryl responds to Ray at one stage with, "Oh, stop making things up. I'm not barren. Who even uses this word - barren? What are you - King James Bible?" I figured the numbers could be pointing to words in the King James Bible.

The King James Bible - which fits the crown theme - is one of the most commonly used book for book-ciphers due to its availability, there are books within it (named, and they can be numbered), chapter and verse numbers for easy encoding, and the existence of Bible concordances which let you look up any word in the bible and where it can be found. The numbers could be pointing to books of the bible and a word number, or some other combination.

So then on to try and find sets of numbers related to each of the Crown products and places listed above. And searching through each brought up some nice 'coincidences'.

The first one that got my attention was The Viceroy. Looking at its sign the T and the Y are larger than the rest (and I find having the last letter larger than the rest strange), and there are four stars. I tried using the TY to mean Timothy, and with the fours stars (after trying using it in different ways I remembered a dialogue somewhere in the game that mentions 'squared') I went to Chapter 4 word 4, which is "Speaketh". Now Ron was speaking to me, "See? See, see? See, see? Hear. Open your ears!" In the Beyond Insemination radio program you will hear Duane say the following: "Next caller! Inseminate me! No, no, no. That's wrong. Speaketh to me." Okay now that's a strange and seemingly pointless piece of dialogue, and a strange coincidence. But not much to get excited about yet.

The next 'coincidence' came via The Feud. Looking for a way to find numbers by searching through Feud assets lead me to a t-shirt you can buy at Suburban, the 75th anniversary edition. Oddly, the Roman numerals for 75 are split across the artwork, making a LX (60) and a XV (15). So what does Book 60 (1Peter) word 15 give you? Asia. Duane Earl again: "I served it on top of my stepdaughter just like they do in Asia. I am particularly interested in visiting Asia. I've always been kind to the Asiatic cultures, I've always enjoyed them - the kung fu, martial arts, and fucking throwing stars." Note here the repeated use of the word for emphasis.

Next the Buckingham Luxor jet (see also there is LUXER graffiti around the place, a hint for where to look). The crown on the tail is part of the coat of arms for Rudolph II, Holy Roman Emperor 1576 – 1612. Taking 1576 to be Book 15 (Ezra) word 76 gives you ME, and 1612 becomes TO. Not exciting, but you can't make a message using only keywords so that didn't worry me. But when I tried playing with the AJ89 on the plane - again something weird. There is no book that begins with A and ends with J, but if you reverse them (from Omega to Alpha and back again and from Z to A and all that shit) you could get JoshuA. Joshua word 89 is EVEN, again not exciting but the word right before it is Lebanon. Jock Cranley on Bless Your Heart: "Whatever, whatever. What do you think about affirmative action? Hey, you ain't a Lebanon, is you? I mean…ehhh…ain't nothin' wrong with it. Some of my best friends was Lebanons, but it's unnatural, and a sin!"

So more rather strange dialogue, repeated use of a word. Have they used Lebanon as a way to let us know we are on the right track? I did a search on one of those concordances mentioned earlier and there are 64 cases of 'Lebanon' in the KJVB. That bible has just under 800,000 words. 'Asia' appears 20 times. 'Speaketh' 66.

Continuing on. The Drag-On 6 poster has a crown on the 'Gamelords' at the bottom and the number six appears twice. Book six, word six gives you 'Moses'. Lester dialogue: "Moses, ironically, he found Jesus." Moses appears 784 times, but out of an almost 800,000 word book that still gives you a roughly 1 in 800 chance of picking it if you were to grab a random word. And then on top of that Lester mentions that name in a section of dialogue, a name that has no other relevance to the game?

Richards Majestic, founded 1921. Book 19 (Psalms) word 21 is 'Nor'. Duane Earl: "For the record, not me, nor no one in my family was ever a monkey, nor have they been with a monkey, not ever, not even at the zoo! Nor pretty much any animal aside from usual ones we all do practice on." A lot of emphasis on a word that is rarely used today (side note: using years is an easy way to give you 4 digits. Maybe the 1849 that was recently found on the Leopolds building is a hint for what we are looking for).

Now for a few different types of hints (I think): Ray D'Angelo on Chakra Attack, "Knit one purl two, all that shit. Gangsta style! Blam blam blam blam blam! And I can say: "Dad, I made you a sweater". Posonbys has three Argyle sweaters, with a crown on them, and their prices are 4 digits.

In the mission I Fought The Law they Overflod (crown on logo) Entity XF has MKB 652 for a number plate, which as far as I can tell means nothing in leet speak or whatever. So why have they given that vehicle a special plate? MKB equals Modern King James Bible maybe? I think they want us to search for each little riddle relating to each crown instance.

At the Betsy O'Neill pavilion there is a sign for a show called 'Quid Novi? 6'. Quid Novi means 'what news'. News can be a synonym for Revelation. Lester dialogue, same mission as before: "And in revelation of that..."

Blêuter'd. Created 1855, since 1869, bottle label 1969. Words become 'Of This Season'. Trevor to Cletus in their first mission: "Tis the season for it." Just more OPEN YOUR EARS.

The Meltdown mission. They hop in an Albany Stretch Limo with the number-plate V1N3W88D. Shouldn't that be V1NEW00D in correct leet or 1337 or whatever it's called? Maybe they changed it so it fit the word they were after. Anyway book 13 (1Chronicles) word 88 is 'Nimrod'. It feels out of place, but looking into Nimrod it seems he is considered, at least in the Jesus HD universe, to be the first King.

Right, I think I've had enough for now (still recovering from the flu followed by a weekend of too much booze). If you'd like a part 2 with the rest of my guesswork let me know. It's obviously not finished, and I'm still trying to find rules and solutions for each Crown, if there's a process etc. But it seems like each one may have its own way to be solved, and the 26 SET 51 34 was a way to nudge us towards a bible cipher.

Summary or TLDR:

  • There is a 'crack the code' graffiti with a crown.
  • There are 26 objects, places or product lines that feature a crown.
  • The 26 SET 51 34 graffiti nudges us towards a bible cipher, hints being in the audio.
  • Each Crown thing is a riddle to find numbers/directions to a bible word.
  • Hopefully these words lead to a message. For example, I think I have found, among others: speaketh Asia heat clothes sixscore. I have not found an order for the words, that's just an example of what it could be, I suppose. So it could become something like 'Speak to an asian person in Heat clothes at 1:20 (sixscore is 120).

Have a good one.
