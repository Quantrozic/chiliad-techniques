In Paleto Bay underneath the Famous Hamburgers sign, there is this cryptic sign: http://imgur.com/g6LAZAL. At this point I firmly believe this to either be some sort of obfuscated clue/easter egg, or a very deliberate, very well-crafted red herring; there are simply too many anomalies for me to disregard it as run-of-the-mill random gibberish. "Normal" garbage text that people generate almost always has telltale signs that say, "this person put all of 2 seconds' worth of effort into creating this." Whoever made this put more time and thought into it than that--enough for someone with a tech background like myself to sit up and take notice--and I want to know why.

I haven't been able to make any notable progress on it yet, but I do have a small handful of wacky theories I've been exploring and I believe further investigation is warranted before this is written off entirely. I seem to be the only one giving this any real attention, and I think a few more pairs of eyes on it could really help get some ideas flowing. Anyone interested in joining forces on this to see if we can tease anything out of it? I'm not making any assumptions about where this might lead if it is in fact legit, so I can't offer any promises of a jetpack.
