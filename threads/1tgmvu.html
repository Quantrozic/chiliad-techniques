Regarding the glow-in-the-dark symbol, just wanted to point out that the top of the oil drop has a glow-in-the-dark marking on it as well, and is part of the symbol as a whole. (http://i.imgur.com/3jZmtMM.jpg). This part is absent during the day time so it should be considered part of the whole symbol. (http://i.imgur.com/afUyYFf.jpg). Not sure if too many people noticed this or took it into account when trying to interpret the symbol. Keeping this in mind I still have no idea what the hell its supposed to symbolize, any fresh ideas?

EDIT: I also want to point out that of the three RON signs, the most Western one is the only one with a clear image of the symbol. The middle one has the symbol but it is a lot more faded compared to the one most West, and the most Eastern one has no symbol at all. Is this significant?
