This giant ad will probably be familiar to some of you who've used the "Fly a helicopter under the map" glitch, as this building has a missing clipping plane large enough to pass a buzzard through.

I was struck by how much the cracks resembled the crack in the egg, particularly when illuminated at night, with the curved highlighting provided by the spotlights.

Does this mean anything? I have no idea. Even if it does, I don't have 100% to meaningfully check. What I did notice was that when purposefully clipping into the building, it appears to have a textured (but not modelled) interior.
