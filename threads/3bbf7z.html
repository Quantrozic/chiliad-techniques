Issac the dead body from Murder Mystery speaks like Graham the zombie.

▲ 5 ptsu/amoshydraJun 27, 201510 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Modding & In-Game Manipulation (Trainers / Script Hooks)

If you manage the change yourself to Issac using a trainer/mod and make him speak, he will say something like

  • "Arrrrrrgh"
  • "Brainless loser"
  • "Sucker!"
  • "I will eat your brains!"
  • "Don't give me irony"
  • "I got on stage fighting corpses" <- not sure
  • "Tourist!" <- not sure
  • "Run!" <- not sure

These will be played when the character get into a fight or chasing someone.

However unlike Graham the zombie, Issac does not have a way to respond to threat, conversation or to greet people.
