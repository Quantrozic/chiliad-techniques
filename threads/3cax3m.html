How to see the eclipse

Hey guys,

I realized i should have posted a detailed tutorial on how to see the eclipse by yourself depending on the date in your game so here it goes.

Step 1 : find your next new moon phase. You need to see the moon at that particular moment where it flips from left to right and note down the day you're in. In this image, the new moon is on tuesday.

Step 2 : where did you see the new moon flip ? If you saw it near sunset (by the cable car station) that means you're starting a south orientation. If you saw it near sunrise, you're on the north orientation. See this for more details.

Step 3 : determine what month you're in regarding your new moon day. You need to use 2 things to be sure : first one is your new moon day and second is your moon orientation. Let's say my new moon was south. So i'm looking for a new moon south on a tuesday. I circled the day using this calendar. In this exemple, i'm on day 256 in the 6th month.

Step 4 : Use this other calendar to determine the eclipse days. In blue you have the sunrise eclipses and in green the sunset eclipses. Advance the days by sleeping or killing yourself until you reach the eclipse.

Step 5 : Be carefull about the timings. The sunrise eclipse starts at 7:07 and the sunset one starts around 18:25. You need to trigger the rain (by cheat or sex or whatever) just before those timings.

Notes :

  • Apparently you can see it from everywhere. I tried successfully from chiliad platform, mount gordo, altruist camp, observatory and hippie camp.
  • Apparently you can see it with everyone. I saw it with all 3 characters.
  • I only tried it with the rain cheat at 1.6% and at 69% so you guys are free to try at 100%, in front of ufo, in the space docker...
  • I tried to see the eclipse in a full moon day (day 107) and on a new moon day (day 877) but nothin more happened.
  • The calendar only covers the first moon year but the patterns goes on if you continue.
