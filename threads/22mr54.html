Question: Try Packet Sniffing?

Documented under:Network Packet Inspection (Online Sessions)Network Packet Inspection in Online

Long time lurker, first time poster.

No collision or texture models for the jet pack in the on-disc game files? Game includes tons of online content (social club, online stories, etc)? What about grabbing the models from an off-host resource?

As a developer I've hidden a few easter eggs in software I've shipped and I always hid them behind a network call so the things that could get me fired weren't shipped with the product or visible in the source code tree. I'm only at 92% in this game so I haven't had the chance, but I wonder if anyone has bothered to do a little packet sniffing with Wire Shark when the Chilliad UFO appears? Maybe a URL will show up to an external resource that's only downloaded and installed on the fly under certain conditions? R* has to be able to do just that with online games and custom character appearance, right...? Maybe they use it for more than characters? Just a thought.
