Different Sizes of The Sun.

▲ 2 ptsu/myinnertrevorMar 15, 20179 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Vanishing-Point Perspective Recovery

Yo yo sup everyone. The Altruists Camp has weird paintings of the sun in different sizes. Why is that? How can the sun change size? Does that prove the sun revolves around the Earth, Geocentric model, or is the Earth flat and sun circles around on top in a circle? Are we under a dome?

Here is a quick slideshow video of the sun. I tried to keep the sun centered in screen for the shots but from 10:00am to 1:00pm, the game will not let you do this. I tried to keep it centered because when you move camera to make the sun appear on side of your screen it will make it bigger, so keeping it in center of screen is how I tried to keep a constant for my video.
You can notice the sun getting bigger as it rises, and smaller as it sets.

Based on perspective, my best guess is the sun is travelling around the Earth. Others have mentioned this before early on in hunt, I just wanted to bring up this idea again to discuss, and put together a quick video showing the sun.

Let me know your thoughts on this, on why the sun changes in size?
