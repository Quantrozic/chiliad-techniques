"You've gotta wack that f*cker because it makes sense"

▲ 54 ptsu/BionicWheelJun 15, 202043 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Choice-Tree Ending Permutation Mapping

I'm currently watching an Instagram Live video of Ned Luke \[Michael's voice actor\] (I know, I know, but don't downvote just yet!) signing autographs and talking live on cam with fans. Whilst speaking with one he just said something that I found extremely interesting. The fan who was on live with him was talking about the game endings and Ned revealed that the first time he played the game he killed Trevor. (I'm paraphrasing here but trying to get this as close to what he said as possible from memory, if I can go back on the video later I will update this post to write exactly what he said) But in a round about way, he said:

"I actually killed Trevor on my first playthrough...because it makes sense... you've gotta wack that f\*cker because it makes sense... and here's a little insider tip for you, you've gotta wait because that's how it originally went, Franklin can't do it, he can't kill him \[Ned tries to gesture in a way to show that Franklin doesn't want to/have the nerve to do it\] you've gotta let Michael kill Trevor because that's how the scene really went when we filmed it. That's my little tip for you today."

In no way am I suggesting that Ned Luke knows any next step towards solving this mystery (he never even mentioned it), BUT, inadvertadly this could be a super crucial bit of information for us or anyone looking into Karma playthroughs. Personally I've always believed that if you choose ending C, your reward is still being able to play with all 3 characters.
