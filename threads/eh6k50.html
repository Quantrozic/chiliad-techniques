Possible Arcade random event equivalent to the Nightclub Epsilon Robe & the tattoo/underwear/ufo as obtained via the Macbeth Shot blackout — pure speculation, but wanted to share

Documented under:Manhunt-Universe Easter-Egg Cross-Reference

This post began as an edit to my original post titled 'Tipping your Nightclub Bathroom Attendent', but it veered more into the realm of speculation, so I figured it would be best to start anew;

As most of us are aware, once you tip your attendent $577 dollars, you unlock the Epsilon Robes as worn by Michael in SP. It takes about 15 minutes in one go, and seems rather arbitrary... but it's worth noting (and I'm sure I'm not the first to notice this...so if there's an original post related to this matter, I implore you to link it because I have not been able to find it as of yet) that the fifth and seventh letter of the alphabet are E and G, respectively. Therefore 577 spells out EGG.

It's also common knowledge that once you've unlocked the Alien Egg via the Gunrunning source mission, your character is eligible to unlock another 'random event' Easter Egg via the Nightclub; drink a Macbeth whiskey shot during the hours of 01:00 and 04:00 while there's thunder and lightening going on outside... and you have the chance of blacking out and waking up atop Chiliad Mountain in your underwear (just like Trevor in SP), with a new tattoo and a UFO flying off in the distance.

Cool and all, but not nothing groundbreaking. All in all it feels like more of a nod towards the Chiliad Mystery community than anything explicitly mystery or clue related.

But this got me thinking — is there an Arcade equivalent?

My first post simply asked, can anybody think of something similar to tipping the bathroom attendant that can be done in the Arcade that may possibly yield a similar outcome? Could the Macbeth shot lead anywhere?

AS far as a bathroom attendent equivalent goes, the only things we can interact within the arcade that are unique only to the Arcade (so ruling out the dartboard, jukebox, etc) are... well, the arcade games. But what's unique about the arcade game selection is that only one is NOT a game; the fortune-teller, Madam Nazar.

There have been multiple posts where her scripts are broken down and analyzed; they contain references to both the GTA universe (both SP content as well as MP; ie, infinite eight killers all the way to the dirty dupers) and the RDR universe (tall trees, man in a tall hat, and well, Madam Nazar herself, right?). The number that can be called (123 764 5112) has been discussed lately, but I'm not going to touch on that other than to simply alude to it as 'evidence' that there may be something unique there, just like the bathroom attendant is unique in the sense that no other DLC has had some random NPC that you can give money to for no reason other than to just 'see what happens'.

So far, the Arcade Macbeth blackout hasn't brought us anything too farout; everyone has reported that they've either woken up in the bathroom, the basement or somewhere within a blocks radius of the Arcade exterior.

But again, all of this got me thinking; the Nightclub Macbeth easter egg requires the aforementioned conditions; rainy weather, between the hours of 1 and 4am, and unlocking the Alien Egg via a Gunrunning Mission (which I might add, ALSO required the same weather and time conditions), so perhaps the Arcade has similar conditions to be met.

Well, here's how I've been thinking of it;

You don't go to a Nightclub and drink whiskey during the day, so it seems fitting that you'd only only discover the tattoo/ufo easter egg at night.

And perhaps the converse can be said for the arcade ; it's more of a daytime rainy-day sort of event, right? Maybe more of a weekend thing, even? Obviously this is pure speculation, but it's worth considering.

So, simply... maybe there are conditions to be met at the arcade? Maybe they're the same as the the Nightclub and the SP chiliad ufo, or maybe they're different? What if we try between the hours of 13:00 - 16:00?

Personally, I think thunderstorm/rain is still an important aspect, as those weather conditions are simply more rare than the usual California Sun that beats down on Los Santos, so it would make this EE equally as difficult to obtain.

Again, I'm going on nothing but speculation here... but i think it worth exploring. Same goes for the Casino even, but I personally can't think of anything in the Casino that is comparable to either Madan Nazar of the Bathroom Attendant, so I'm not delving into that here.

Besides, the Arcade is, all in all... an extension of the casino DLC.

Anyway, I have some other things I may edit in here to gloss over, but im out of time at the moment and don't want to risk losing my progress on this post as I have the other two times I've put my phone in my pocket.. so I'm going to end here.
