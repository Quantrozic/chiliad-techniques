The Mural as I see it, and how to go about decoding its meanings.

I was going to post something in another, now-deleted thread asking for the next step in the mystery after you have seen the chiliad UFO, because it seems like the mural might just be pointing at the known UFOS, but it got really long. Writing this helped me clarify some thoughts for myself though, so I've expanded it, brought in some of my notes, and here it is.

I hope this is helpful for beginners, and anyone else who lacks a framework for investigation. It is all speculation and informed opinion that may well turn out to be wrong (I already know lots of people will disagree with much of it), but I wouldn't put it here if I didn't believe it personally.

The mural doesn't really tell us the location of any UFOs, but instead describes a system by which we can unlock a flyable UFO, a jetpack, and a third thing represented by an egg, that you get for unlocking either of the other items. This might be as simple as a trophy or outfit, but could be as interesting as a mission set, an apocalyptic scenario and/or changes in the map.

The Xs on each side of the centre line are spaces in which to plug one of the five major glyphs found on Mount Chiliad: the weathered/faded eye, the partial eye, the eye with "mountain", the eye with crescent moon and the eye with raincloud.

Each glyph may have multiple valid interpretations, and may represent a series of steps or conditions rather than a single feat.

The Xs are each linked to each other and the rest of the mural by straight lines. I believe all such lines are decision paths the player can follow. Start at the eye atop the mountain, then choose a path to either the jetpack or UFO. Each time you come to path linked to an X, you may not pass until the relevant glyph has been satisfied, nor may you cross the central egg line once you have chosen your path.

Now, there's a couple of extra paths into the mural maze from other places - above the jetpack, from the mountain's left side, and beside the UFO (though this one does not bypass all the glyphs). These are other routes to the prizes, that do not include the trophy or additional features represented by the egg. Since they don't all have glyphs, they might be time-based (such as... everyone gets a jetpack at the end of 2015, regardless), or require a different kind of thinking altogether. Radio in all its forms is very prevalent in the game, as is electrical energy. The mural could be showing both or either of these, so they might be relevant to an alternate path.

A glyph condition could be as simple as "it must be 3am", or it could be as complex as "it must be 3am on a Tuesday, and you must be wearing appropriate clothing with a specific character in a specific location. Then, once the trigger has occurred, go to a second location and shoot at the crescent moon three times, bringing down the cloaked UFO in front of it." And that's for a single glyph. (example only!)

That we can rationalise most or all the clues as pointing at the Chiliad UFO and other extant finds with varying degrees of satisfaction is immaterial. I believe it was deliberately set up that way to encourage debate. If we believe in a jetpack, we have to also believe that R* are giving us the mighty puzzle everyone regrets was not in GTA SA.

So to solve the glyphs' true meanings we have to play again and again, examining the story, the characters, the setting, the dialogue, the structure of missions, the radio and TV shows, the everything. They are all saying things both on the thematic level and as a subtext. Once this is completely decoded, we will all be able to replay the game as if seeing the Sixth Sense for the second time - it will be blatantly obvious that Tyler and Jack are sharing a body ;)

"Did Somebody Say Yoga?" could be dissected in this way. Is it saying to Michael, do yoga or don't do yoga? Do drugs or don't? Aliens are real or imagined? Michael needs Amanda or forget her and fuck strippers? I think do, don't, real, strippers. Your answers may vary, but that mission is certainly saying a lot about those particular questions and others I missed.

One of the things "Exercising The Truth" seems to be saying is that Cris somehow knows you are really running in the desert - he calls you with encouraging messages, knowing precisely how far you have travelled no matter where you end up! Whether that is because he is watching from hidden cameras, a UFO or jetpack, uses Trackify, or he has a gps device in the medal is up for debate.

Look at other missions and see what questions they might be answering, and for which character the information is intended. Patterns might emerge, and there will be times that a character is saying one thing to the other people in the scene, but you will hear him or her saying something else to you the player, as a subtext.

For a couple of examples of the game getting personal, listen to Chakra Attack in its entirety. If you are listening carefully you will hear Dr Ray DeAngelo Harris "talking to YOU" directly with some secret messages intended only for dedicated gunters.

on youtube: http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=weS19eV9OUE

I haven't noted the times to listen, because you might hear things I didn't. The whole show is full of hints related to spirituality in the GTA world, and yoga specifically. Dr Harris seems to be an authority on this topic. I think of Michael (the Yoga protagonist), his story and his options in game while listening, and try to apply all the little ideas explored by the hosts. This includes the ones that seem like nothing more than crude jokes or new age parody. That is exactly where R* would put the good stuff, knowing most people would overlook it.

"For," as the tract tells us, "that which has higher meaning must be told in all manner possible, by oracles, by brother to brother, and condensed into phrases that others need understand while trapped in traffic and seeking escape."
