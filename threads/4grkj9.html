So yeah I was reading about some Video Game Easter Egg's that took a very long time for players to discover. One that I did not know about was Final Fantasy 9's Nero Bros. Sidequest. Which took 13 Years to discover...

So why am I posting about this you ask?

Well mainly because this particular Easter Egg involved 16 'Advancement Points' that were scattered throughout the story-line and you would have to do very specific things during your game progression in order to advance this hidden side-quest. Also if you advanced to far in the game, it was not possible to complete this side-quest.

This has always been, what I imagined our elusive Chiliad-Mystery would consist of...A set number of specific actions that must be done at certain points in our story-line, and if you go to far then you missed it. I have seen many people ponder similar ideas on this thread.

If a game made 16 years ago can have something as complex as Final Fantasy 9 did, and took people 13 years to solve...then there is no doubt in my mind GTA 5 could have implemented a similar model...

I know this doesn't really help our mission..but I found it interesting and, if anything, it gives me hope that this Chiliad Mystery can one day be solved...even if it's by our grandchildren, playing GTA 5 Emulators on the HTC Vive 4.

Link to Original Discovery of FF-IX Easter Egg in 2013 - http://www.gamefaqs.com/boards/197338-final-fantasy-ix/66283454

Edit - One of the 16 Advancement Points listed is - "After you hear about the giant eye in the sky..." - Woah...Strangely Relevant
