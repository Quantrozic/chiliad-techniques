"Franklin and Lamar" Repo Car Choice: License Plate Contains "3AM"

▲ 19 ptsu/SpatCatDec 17, 201412 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Choice-Branch Outcome Tabulation

Sup everyone! Just got my PS4 GTAV Black Friday bundle at the gamestoop. Finally joining the next gen hunt.

When I played the first mission, "Franklin and Lamar," Lamar's dialogue about making a choice/decision between the two repo cars stuck out to me. I decided to examine the vehicles. You have a choice between a red car and a white car. They both have license plates which do not change randomly when you replay the missions or start a new game. Imgur links to the license plates are below. I apologize for the potato quality on the white car pic, it seems like plasma screen whites and cameras don't go together.

The red car's plate, the one closest to Franklin, reads "2603AM56."

The white car's plate is "5912FL34."

Now, this may be grasping at straws, but when I see 3AM anywhere in the game, I take notice. The dialogue is different depending on which car you pick, and in my opinion Lamar seems more emphatic about your decision to pick the red (3AM) car. Any idea on what the numbers 260 and 56 could mean, if anything? Any idea on what the other license plate could mean?
