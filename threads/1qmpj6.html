Anyone still thinking about these questions?

▲ 22 ptsu/Dog_BreadNov 14, 201348 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Interrogative Prompting of NPC Trigger Conditions

I've been thinking about the following a lot lately as I struggle to replay 100% with no pedestrian casualties :0

  • What would the entirety of the easter egg be? I think there will be several things you can trigger, probably in different ways: An alien abduction; access to a jetpack; access to a flyable ufo (zancudo imo); ufo mid air battle.
  • Moon and sun phases suggest to me there will be an eclipse of one or the other at some point in the timeline. That could constitute "seeing the eye", ensure "your path is lit", or give meaning to the hippy code. It might be possible to work out when or where an eclipse can be viewed. (The illuminati eye on a dollar bill looks like an eclipse too).
  • The famous full moon party; often discussed, never located. Is it completely mythical or do we just need a better organised search party (EDIT: Pretty sure that this party will happen on Mount Gordo, and is only accessible with the right karma. Hippies are very sensitive to "unkind spirits", so stop killing them!)
  • The significance of the plane spawned when aiming at the sun in the altruist camp - could it be followed by a second character in an aircraft... More thorough analysis is needed. (EDIT: this may be a timed spawn and reference to LOST).
  • A full on investigation of the karma theory, including combinations of life/death decisions, innocent playthroughs, psychiatry, yoga and/or pilgrimages to cleanse the spirit. As far as I know, the only required decisions are shrink advice, and the life/death of Ortega, Beverly and Dreyfuss. What would you choose for each, and how would you explain that combination? (EDIT: shrink advice is either completely or mostly optional.)
  • more weather triggers, can we get thunder and lightning without cheats? (EDIT: It has been pointed out by rafman400 that Trevor dying on the altar during the Altruist shootout will induce copious thunder and lightning that will last forever as long as you remain in the camp. inconsistent reports suggest that sleeping with Ursula "sometimes" makes lightning - perhaps dependent on karma?)
  • is there a way to control radio signals via manipulation of power supplies, radio towers and the space docker? (EDIT: CB broadcasts are received in some areas of the map, but not others. While in a CB district, broadcasts are received at least every two minutes (real time). If you aren't getting such a signal, and are using the right car, then something weird (and probably awesome) is happening
  • the effect of hang outs on relationships - are there hidden like/respect meters that can be filled to trigger benefits? This feature wasn't pushed in GTA V but there might be more to it that first appears. Does anyone have a list of what times characters are available for hang outs, and even which characters this works for? (EDIT: Still no news on this, but hangouts will provide extra clues in the form of dialogue - there are lots of new conversations you can hear if you haven't used hangouts much)
  • the role of clothing, hair and tattoos; Kifflom robes, stealth suits, cop uniforms, weather wear... wearing some of these in the right circumstance might prompt new access, npc interactions or ambient events. (EDIT: Trevor's underpants-only outfit is unlocked at 100%)
  • planning and multi-character strategies; getting the guys in position at different locations in sequence may be required to make bigger things happen. (EDIT: I now believe this will be thing for Ending C, whereas unlocks in the other two endings will be solo character work).
  • there is still an island in the R* social club map that nobody has been able to see or visit. I think it will be a matter of rain and time, maybe karma, maybe how you travel there.

Has anyone else considered some of these or have any ideas for further testing?
