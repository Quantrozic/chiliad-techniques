The Framing of Merle Abrahams

Greetings fooligans, spacetime travellers and fellow gumshoes. I hope you're all having a nice day.

Through the years, I've had a bit of a fascination with the infinite 8 mystery and I've been suspicious that what we've been told about Merle Abrahams isn't entirely accurate. I'd like to share a list of things that lead me to believe that Merle was possibly framed. To do this, I'm going to go over some discrepancies of the "official report" and what we can find in game.

  1. The Newspaper article - At Merle's home we can find an article from "The Senora beacon", dated December 2004. We can find one major discrepancy between what was written and what actually happened. In the article, it's indicated that the victims were 8 single MALE joggers. This differs from the bodies actually found which were 8 FEMALE bodies.

The senora beacon also "ran cover" for Jock Cranley during the death of Jolene in an article dated March 5th 1978, written by Allen Smithy. Interestingly, the name "Allan Smithee" was a pseudonym used in Hollywood by directors who didn't want their real name associated with the film. This suggests that the Senora Beacon likely isn't a bastion of honesty to begin with.

We also have IAA ties to the United Liberty paper which echoes the real world Operation Mockingbird which is CIA involvement with newsmedia for domestic propaganda purposes. Did the senora beacon help frame Merle? Let's not forget the only thing Merle confessed to, according to the article, was having an obsession with the number 8 - not that he was involved in any killings.

  1. The bodies - Aside from the previously mentioned discrepancy of the gender, the fact that the bodies exist in as pristine condition as they do is contradictory to stages of decomposition. Throughout the ocean floor, we can find whale skeletons with no meat on the bones. Via Michael's murder mystery we find the body of Isaac) in a decomposed state. The i8 bodies should also have some level of decomposition between 1999 (the "murder" year) and 2013 (GTA V setting). The i8 bodies were wrapped in plastic, but not airtight, leaving them exposed to the water.

Although only found by examining the prop model, we can also see that there are no wounds on the bodies but they all have an 8 painted on them. Their hands are ritualistically posed with 3 bodies having their hands tied in front and 5 with their hands tied in back. This is also echoed on the 8s found painted at Merle's. You can find groupings of eight 8s, with 3 in white and 5 in black.

  1. The graffiti at Merle's - believed to be the work of Merle and his neighbors. Given that the graffiti attributed to Merle bears a similar font to the graffiti attributed to his "neighbors", I have to question if any of the graffiti was actually written by Merle. Also of interest, only 3 colors were used - black, white and red. Another place we can find only black, white and red being used would be the chiliad mural, it's glyphs and "come back when your story is complete". The font of "come back when your story is complete" is also similar, as is the font used on another abandoned building found in red dead 1 which also refers to infinity "I don't understand imnfinity ".
  2. Circumstantial evidence - Noted by the senora beacon, Merle was arrested due to circumstantial evidence, none of which is mentioned in the Senora Beacon article. While circumstantial evidence can be used in court, it isn't direct evidence. Circumstantial evidence relies on an inference to connect it to a conclusion of fact. Direct evidence is evidence that doesn't require any inference. The circumstantial evidence may only be Merle's obsession with the number 8, where direct evidence would be something like video proof of Merle actually committing the murders. While sufficient circumstantial evidence can be enough to get a conviction, it can also be insufficient to incriminate someone. An obsession with a number that looks similar to the infinity symbol doesn't necessarily provide direct enough evidence that Merle actually killed the joggers.

Given the above discrepancies, I don't think there is enough to prove beyond a reasonable doubt that Merle was the killer, if any killings even took place, considering the bodies might not be bodies and could just be mannequins or "props". In a meta sense, the bodies literally are game props but from an in-game perspective, we can't wholly confirm they were humans either, since we can't perform an autopsy. The modus operandi of 8 male joggers does fit better with Eddie Low who we meet in GTA IV but even still, we can't confirm that 8 male joggers were actually murdered in 1999 because there aren't 8 male bodies that we can find.

Why is any of this important? Well, that's where we get into more theoretical territory. I suspect Merle was framed to hide something he knew, making him out to be a crazy person and to prevent other citizens of San Andreas or even us from looking into what Merle knew. That admin being Chiliad8888. I get that the evidence to support that is as circumstantial as what got him locked up in the first place, but it would make sense in some fashion considering the content of chiliad8888's email to Niko/the player and how cheats also appear through both red dead and GTA V. It's like once Merle's soul became a ghost in the machine, he started to reach out to the player through the cheats. Maybe Merle/chiliad8888 was the one talking to us through the 4th wall breaks through the course of the GTA games such as " There are no Easter eggs here" in GTA:SA.

I dunno, I just definitely feel there's too many discrepancies between what we're told about the infinity killings and the evidence presented. Hope you enjoyed reading and I hope you have a great rest of your day.
