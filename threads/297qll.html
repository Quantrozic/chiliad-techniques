Mural Interpretation: Linking the Glyphs to the UFOs

I've been a lurker (like many others on this sub) for about a month now. I recently began rethinking the mystery/mural and this is my idea of how the mystery works. Note: There are so many theories/interpretations of the mural on this sub. Thus, many of the aforementioned theories are touched upon or may be repeated in this post to link it together.

The mural is seemingly a depiction of Mount Chiliad with the eye at the top representing the Observation deck. The X's would represent the glyphs on the mountain which serve as instructions to trigger the Mount Chiliad UFO to spawn. We would later discover the lights at the Zanudo Bunker. We would then use the light on the bunker to discover the UFO above the fort. Here we find the phrase "Segregate and Rearrange". Using "Segregate" as an anagram, we unscramble the word to say "easter egg". This is where the mural comes into play. If I'm correct (which I might not be) the Egg on the mural would then represent the Zancudo UFO while the UFO glyph represents the Chiliad craft due to it's similarities (I know the two don't look perfectly alike but they do appear similar). It was also the first craft we discovered so it's position on the left side of the mural would make sense as we read from left to right. This would then leave the Jet Pack glyph to represent the Hippy Camp craft. If this is how we were meant to interpret the mural, then we may have already solved the mystery by finding the three craft. Or perhaps there is one last step after the discovery of the Hippy Camp UFO that would tie them together and reward us. The lines and the lightning bolts on the mural itself are confusing to me and I'm not sure what to make of them. If anyone has any ideas to add on to this theory or has any ideas that may disprove it then feel free to post, I'm open to suggestions/criticism.

Sadly, that's all I have. I know many of those ideas have been reiterated from previous posts and I apologize if this exact theory has been posted before. I have read many other theories of the mural and didn't find any that had the exact same details in this particular order.
