Beaver Day

▲ 4 ptsu/Jetpack_JonesSep 19, 201520 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Choice-Branch Outcome Tabulation

Yeah sorry about the title, I couldn't think of a more apt one but you may understand why in a moment...

So how many times now have you reached 100% expecting to find something different? Maybe a slight variation of what you did last time, yet nothing. Do you get the impression this ending we all know is fake, as fake as a UFO that cannot be touched, an anticlimax. It feels like a bad dream I can't escape, perhaps the mystery is not so dissimilar?

If any of what I just said sounds at all familiar then you've probably seen the movie Groundhog Day. The premise being the main character, an arrogant weatherman played by Bill Murray is stuck reliving the same day over and over in a strange town called Punxsutawney. He eventually escapes the time loop when he's truly changed as a person. The Groundhog is the town mascot which is also believed to predict the weather.

Would it be kind of a dramatic irony if Rockstar knew we would keep repeating the same mistakes over and over, only to realise one day the whole mystery could be solved by making the right choices? Yeah you're right, probably not.
