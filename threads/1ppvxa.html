Been following and looking with you guys but havnt really posted anything. I am on my second play through on the game (make sure I didn't miss anything) and I was going to do the mission where Michael shoots the aliens with the mini gun after smoking some dope.

When driving there I drove up the sidewalk on one side of legion square and instead of leaving black tire marks it left impressions into the ground as if it was dirt. Acted as dirt but was tiles or concrete. I havnt seen this in the game before and it happened only for that section so it's probably just a glitch but I thought I would just mention it.

Second thing I noticed was the "dont say nope to dope" sign. All of the "O"s had a line though them like the marks on the uv map. The mission for Michael has the Hollywood aliens in it and the one for Trevor has clowns, but the one for frank doesn't have anything because he doesn't feel anything and the guy ditches on the next mission with him. Maybe there is something there since it is one of the weirder moments in the game but just a observation.
