Not meaning to take any thunder from milnah23, I have done the same, attempting a replay to see if I could notice anything out of the ordinary, and here are some of my observations thus far;
(I'm currently at around 50%)

  1. In the first heist (and at Lester's) the calendars are all dated 2006. Certain dates are hilighted in the First heist.
  2. At the completion of the first heist (drop off) there was an Altruist sign nearby.
  3. There is a bunker that you can see down into on the corner near where Trevor kills Johnny. What's down there?
  4. Could free tattoos (or a combination there of eg: “Saty Lucky”) influence anything?
  5. There is a massive Thunderstorm on Trevor’s mission to Los Santos with the Lost clean up.
  6. Could you be required to do a task in certain buildings that you can only enter once on missions (eg: FiB, Tequila Bar)
  7. At the end of the mission where Michael meets Dave at the Observatory, there is a discussion about the FiB and IIA “finding something”. What could this be?
  8. Michael sees Aliens everytime he does drugs (except smoking in his house)
  9. When Michael is on drugs, he flys. Could he have to actually go somewhere in particular?
  10. In Trevor’s first heist mission from the freighter there is mention of 3 containers. What’s in the other two?
  11. In Lester’s dialogue he mentions “Secret Technology”
  12. If you replay missions, often you get different dialogue.
  13. Omega’s T-Shirt
  14. What if there are clues hidden in the movies/stories at the cinema?
  15. Audio clues hidden in the ‘Breaking News’ radio announcements

So thats where I'm at. Apologies if some are vague, these are notes from my phone, tapped out in haste.
If any one has any suggestions, observations, comments, please let me know! :)
