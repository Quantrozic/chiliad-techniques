[Speculation] Lets get this hunt back on track.

▲ 107 ptsu/ChippitaOct 21, 2013132 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Spatial Autocorrelation Testing of Prop Placement

First of all, a thanks should be given to those whom have discovered the various bits of the mystery. Your hard work has accumulated into one of the best easter egg hunts I've ever seen since GTA:SA. And a bigger thanks to the code parsers whom have given us hope that there's more to be found.

Lets start this with a quick summary on what we already know about:

The Mural: The first discovered and most important part of this puzzle.

Overlays:
Mural lines up with the mountain if you use the cable cars as a reference point with the center line. This is likely the only correct overlay for this mural, but it may have other meanings.

The "Eye"
While this may represent the UFO, it also hints to the glyph on top of the mountain leading us to the "Come back when your story is complete" message. We've seen this image everywhere since, but we definitely can assume any other locations with similar imagery may be hints.

The 5 X boxes (Mural)
5 Glyphs on the mountain are found when used with above overlay, but that can't be all there is to it.

  • They may be repesenting the 5 100%'s required to see the ufos.

The 3 "Reward" boxes
There's so many possible meanings to these, so here's my idea:

UFO box
4 UFOs so far have been found. I feel I lack the need to list them because they're common knowledge now. They are only one of the rewards for following the clues.

Egg Box
The egg is heavily mentioned in epsilonism. What it references, we don't know, but Cris does mention receiving an egg from your ancestors. Aliens are often considered our ancestors, and the reason humanity exists among conspiracy theorists. So from that I present:

gta5\props\lev_des\lev_des_rpf\prop_alien_egg_01.xdr :
 prop_eggprop_egg_alpha
 prop_emmisive_anim3
 prop_alien_egg_01.
 Prop_Alien_Egg_01
 prop_egg_alpha_n
 prop_egg_alpha

The _alpha bit likely represents transparency, much like the UFOs. It also has an "emmisive" animation. So it radiates something, likely light or another effect.

Jetpack Box
This is not a hitchhiker with straps on his backpack. This is not scuba gear. This is a jetpack, because Rockstar isn't a bunch of morons. They know anything to do with San Andreas will have any of the mystery hunters wanting a Jetpack. Who else spent countless hours exploring the back end of GTA:SA we were never meant to see using our trusty jetpack in some poorly mapped tattoo parlors? The jetpack was more then a vehicle. It was a tool many of us used to explore.

The UFO locations
I do not feel that there is anything else to the UFOs other than simply existing. They're there to make us hunt for more, or simply for Rockstar to give us the UFOs we thought we saw in GTA:SA.

Mount Chiliad -
The first and most important UFO, as it's what got us all here, reading this right now.

Alien Camp -
This camp is littered with alien stuff, including the UFO. Likely has other pieces of the puzzle within it, but some of the shit users come up with are getting pretty out there.

Fort Z -
Another likely place to check for aliens or jetpacks, so this UFO was also found rather quickly. Red lights come on before the UFO's light show starts, almost like an indication to stick around.

Underwater -
The wrecked UFO that littered San Andreas with space craft parts. Nothing else major about it besides it was our first "UFO" we discovered, as it can be seen before the game is 100%.

My current thoughts:

I've been following the hunt since it first began, and had to deal with the stupid theories just like everyone else. Now that we've had public access to the code, I think the hunt should be focused away in seeing random things and thinking they're unrelated, and working on what we know to find the next "reward", the Egg. Once we find this Egg the code is mentioning, maybe we will have hints to the next piece. But all efforts should be focused on finding this egg.

I speculate the movie set may have something to do with it, especially since coordinates (-1124.392,-514.7001,33.21493) that Woovie originally posted from the ambient_ufos.txt file, points to the movie studio. No other coordinates matching this set in any of the other file has been found, so if these were fake coordinates, how he got it to now direct us to the movie studio is anyone's guess.

I found a Window (after thinking about the good ol' easter egg hiding in a window) that radiates light, but isn't actually on. It radiates light like a lamp or something, but no other windows in the area does this. Even the ones that are brightly lit. It's located just below the highest point in the "Deep Inside" set.

The studio also has red spinning lights much like Fort Z. While some may say its likely just the standard "Shooting in progress", that doesn't really explain why all the studios have it on after 10 pm (Might be 9), which is the same time the windows in the set light up, leading me to discover this window.

Lastly, "Deep Inside" is also supposed to be a spy movie, but there's only aliens/astronauts actors on the set. I don't think this should be overlooked as simply just "it's a movie studio", but as a hint from Rockstar to be searching anything alien related. The rooftops have some fairly obvious and hand crafted paths on them, but they're never actually used for anything. There are some "Desert" crates on the path to the window like the one Omega is using as a workstation, that have a logo for a stout company, state it's earl grey tea, but also has a skull and crossbones warning, so they're quite bizarre, and I haven't seen them anywhere else in the game that I can recall.

TL;DR: We need to find the egg, and I'm thinking the movie studio might just be the place to look. At least until we discover what the coordinates Woovie posted are pointing to.
