Aloha to everyone desperately trying to work out this mystery, new owner of several sleepless nights due to the intregue. I'm at 100% so in a position to test things, however, I'm leaning towards whatever 'trigger' needs performing, will not be done in a single game. But I'll get to that... so, cent #1.

The egg... people are trying a lot of interpretations and theories. Hopefully this will help direct things, I don't think it's metaphorical and we need to be looking for something VERY similar in the game. This is because of the single straight line at the bottom of it. What purpose does this line provide? They could have drawn an egg on its side, or upright how it is now, with no need for a line to signify anything (I've seen it used with scrabble tiles in real life, the line is there to make sure you can differentiate between say, a W and M. The line is there to show us the correct orientation of the object imo. Is it grounded, or at the bottom of something? ;)

Cent #2... I did a quick googling and did find a wee mention of what I'm about to suggest here and there, buried in mountains of pages. Basically the more I look at it, the more it looks not like tunnels in a mountain, but like a timeline... with alternate pathways and check boxes for important events. I'm going to try and work out how many heists and 'direction changing' events there are and if they correspond to the check boxes. Just how non-linear is the game?

R* are definitely trying to tell us something with those pictures of eyeballs & lightning (notice the eye at the top of the mural and lightning around it). And maybe 'come back when your story is complete' means there are different play routes? I don't understand how anyone would ever know if their newer route had caused any effect.

I think the next thing I need to try is, replaying through and choose a different choice for the heists each time. I'll let the /s/ know which route I take, then it's just the long slog to get each one up to 100% and see if that has any noticeable effect on the 'weird' stuff that we can see everywhere but can't get to do anything.

Kifflom to you all, brother brothers and sister sisters.

Good grief, this little CD has all but taken over my life.
