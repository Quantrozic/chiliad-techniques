Disclaimer:

I did a search for any discussions regarding the beta images that were found, and not much turns up.

This is all purely speculation/for reference, and the linked images were not included in the final game, so any connections between the images and the mystery may be irrelevant.

The Seal:

Beta - http://imgur.com/lav7nUs

Release - http://imgur.com/wIVk3gF

Some noteworthy things about the seal:

  • Epsilon logo
  • 3 Fruits
  • Sasquatch
  • Owl holding mouse
  • Beaver with hammers/RS logo
  • American flag.
  • Stars

I believe the beaver is purely an homage to the developers. (RS montreal, hammers signifying the work put in, RS logo being in the beta logo, etc.)

The castle in the beta Sasquatch part of the seal may be referencing the shed.

Owls are symbolic of watching your path closely, moon magic, and warnings.

3 fruits -- 3 fruit trees

The Logos:

White Bolt RR - http://imgur.com/rKgscHq

Santos Spirit Railway Co - http://imgur.com/1gcvPnE

Los Santos Pounders - http://imgur.com/dmCJGhl

Noteworthy things:

  • Lightning Bolts
  • Stars
  • Same Owl Image

I find it interesting that both railroad concepts had imagery related to the mystery/seal, perhaps there is something we missed with the train?
