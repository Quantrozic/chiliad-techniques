Hovering underneath Sandy Shores UFO (Deathray)

▲ 13 ptsu/midwestlunaticJul 29, 201521 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Rockstar Editor Time-Lapse Capture

From RadioNeat's post, I remembered messing around with the Hydra Jet as Franklin (PC Hard 100%, All-Gold) and the UFO's.

This answers the question if it's still there on Next-gen, how long it lasts and exactly what it looks like. I have an exact POI of every UFO and with this one I lined up perfectly and kept ascending slowly until the UFO EMP'ed me. When I go close or hit the UFO, this does not happen, only when underneath.

Deathray Recorded with Rockstar Editor, I do not own a capture card.

Also for anyone having trouble finding the UFO spots, a good tip I learned: Go to your in-game settings, find audio, and turn the music volume all the way down, and SFX volume all the way up. Turn your volume up loud. When you are in range of a UFO, there is a dip in the sound effect volume and the UFO effect starts to play.

I've tried using this to find other UFO's but no luck so far; this is taking into account if there IS another one.

Happy hunting.
