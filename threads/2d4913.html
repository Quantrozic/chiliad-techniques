So people have been saying shoot for the stars on the UV map references penris, whose logo is an eye surrounded by stars. Also penris anagrams to sniper, and this got me thinking, a sniper spawns at the observatory, but maybe that's not powerful enough. So that got me thinking about that mission where you have that super sniper in the van parked next to the observatory, maybe you have to shoot the penris logo with that sniper. So I loaded up that mission, Caida Libre, and I used a normal sniper before I got into the van to look for the penris building and its way way too far to actually load the logo. So then I got into the van thinking more would load, but whats really weird is that even though the van is parked next to the observatory your view gets switched to looking along that main highway, so you're looking sideways at the penris building instead of right at it like you would if you were looking from the observatory. Here's a quickly drawn paint illustration of what I mean http://imgur.com/Xsh3Lhh (BLUE CIRICLE = PENRIS BUILDING). But I still tried to shoot the side of the penris building and still nothing. So I guess you don't have to shoot for the stars in the mission after all.
