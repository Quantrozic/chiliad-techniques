A note on noclipping

▲ 40 ptsu/GiiiraffeMay 10, 20153 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Collision-Mesh Probing

Since a lot of people are now noclipping and removing water and such I just wanted to note that, when noclipping, although there may not seems to be anything under/behind the map, various interiors and rooms only load when approached from a certain direction/position. For example, if you remember the neuro-toxin mission, with noclip you can revisit the water cooling channel you swim into as Michael. You can't see it from a distance because it won't load, but if you follow it closely from the entrance, you can actually see the whole mission interior. Once you enter the basin and try to climb the ladder it dissapears though. If you move ever so slightly away from the cooling channel, the whole interior also dissapears.

So what I'm trying to say is, even though it may not seem like there's anything when noclipping for secrets, there still may be something hidden.
