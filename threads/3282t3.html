Why any karma theory doesn't make any sense.

▲ 2 ptsu/kosm93Apr 11, 201519 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Poisson Rate Testing of Sighting Reports ?

I keep seeing these karma theories pop up on here and in my own personal opinion I think they would never work. I get this idea thinking from a game design perspective. GTA is and always will be an open world game where you play as you want. It is a series based on creating destruction and havoc. Having a specific way to obtain a reward would destroy the inner game design.

For example lets say there is a way to get a "jetpack" or "ufo" based on a specific way to play. If this was the case, everyone would play through the story exactly the same way to obtain the reward. It just doesn't make any sense at all being what GTA was designed to be.

Lastly, after following this mystery for such a long time it's becoming more and more clear that there most likely is no jet pack or flyable ufo in the game as of now. I think that the "come after your story is complete" might show more after we finish all the DLC.
