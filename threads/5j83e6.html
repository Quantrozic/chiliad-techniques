Geology

▲ 49 ptsu/TrevorsbuzzardDec 19, 201627 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Geological / Topographical Realism Analysis

It has been suggested that Mount Chiliad is based on Mount Shasta in California. I decided to conduct a little geological study to see if there is any similarity. My Brother is an exploration Geologist with ten years experience in the field evaluating rock formations. He is not familiar with GTA V and so I asked hime to take a look at the rock formations found in the game. He was surprised to find so much Geological detail incorporated into a game, after surveying Mount Josiah he said the sedimentary rock formations are very in keeping with the islands in Paleto Cove which show evidence of techtonic upheaval, whatever that means.
He also confirmed that Mount Chiliad is different to every other mountain in the game as the central portion at the peak is entirely volcanic in structure. This can be seen on the North West corner adjacent to Paleto bay, where the molten cores have cooled to form pillars of volcanic rock.
I found this interesting as this confirms that Mount Chiliad is or was a volcano.
I asked him where he thought a crater would be but he says the mount looks very weathered since it's last eruption and the rocks surrounding any crater looks eroded away.
