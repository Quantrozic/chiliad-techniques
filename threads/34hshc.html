Items that unlock when you hit 100% may be the roadblock for the UFO

▲ 51 ptsu/KuztomXMay 1, 201536 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Crowdsourced 100%-Save Testing Matrix ?Save-State A/B Trigger Testing ?

So I was looking at the scripts today and found some items that unlock when you hit 100% completion. Included in the items are some TORSO resources, as well as a "100% completion" t-shirt. Why does this matter? Well, we know that the UFO scripts unlock on 100%, so why wouldn't we assume these other items to have relevance.

In another thread it was mentioned that there is a variable that holds the key to unlocking some UFO interior. This variable could either have a value of -1 or 999. If it was either of those values, the rest of the code could be executed when the Chiliad UFO is spawned.

So what if the variable represents your outfit? We know that the Altruist camp has ties to the UFO, and they are nudists. So what can we take from that? Maybe in order to "praise and give thanks", you have to follow their advice and be nude when spawning the UFO. -1 could be the value for not wearing clothes at all, which happens to unlock once you complete 100% (probably what the TORSO resources are). Why would they unlock the ability to be mostly nude at 100% completion? Alternatively, the value of 999 might be if you are wearing the "100%" t-shirt. Both of these outfits will "prove" to the UFO your worthiness, and that you have completed your story.

Likewise, if we continue with the scripts, it looks as if there are some other checks based on how close you are to the UFO. Some of this could be the automatic hiding of the UFO as you approach it, but there is another interesting trigger. If you look at the UFO through an aggressive scope, the sniper rifle, the bottom will open up and shine a light, however, the UFO will disappear. However, if you view the UFO through the telescopes which happen to conveniently be located right at the peak, the UFO will NOT disappear and will still open up. This is a message telling us something, and is reinforced at the Altruist camp where there is a painting of three UFOs, one just as small as a star, one medium, and one fully zoomed. This tells us that zooming in and "seeing the eye" is key to this, and there are scripts that look to reinforce this.

So my ask is this. Can someone with 100% test this out. Go to the site and reveal the UFO with either no outfit on or the 100% shirt. Then, watch the UFO through the telescope and see what happens.

Finally, there is an additional check in the code that ensures that your player is not injured. While naked with full health, injure yourself in front of the UFO as a sacrifice and see if a reaction occurs...but only do this after the UFO has been revealed. There are two things that reinforce this thought. One, the script appears to execute code if you are fully healed, which may include "hiding" code, so injuring yourself after the UFO has been launched may prevent the hiding part. Second, near the Altruist camp there is a cave with a painting of the UFO. Right in front of the UFO is a camp fire, positioned in such a way that if you were to walk up to the UFO, you will walk into the fire, injuring yourself.

If any of you 100%ers are up for testing, I think trying different combinations of the above could possibly be fruitful for the hunt. Huge thanks for those that do try and maybe we can finally put this mystery to rest!

PS: Many of the glyphs show the trail as a clue. It might be possible that the only way to truly show your "praise" for the UFO is to actually walk the trail fully, potentially while nude and getting there uninjured. Just a thought worth trying.
