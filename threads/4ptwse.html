I've always wondered, whenever you replay a mission, you actually have your "present" stats, weapons, clothes,even cellphone ringtone, tatoos, haircut and body armor, but the money you had at the time doing the mission first time(you cannot bring your money from the bank account into the past).
So my question is, with the recent bigfoot thing, is there any possible way of replaying mission as bigfoot? Following the earlier logic, this should be possible. And by doing so it might be the key of the other mysteries. For example we could trigger a cutscene in which bigfoot would save dom from falling cause of its unnatural powers. I'm still thinking about missions that would have another ending if bigfoot made an appearence(probably the epsilon one). What if bigfoot was abducted instead of michael himself? He might be strong enough to escape the ETs and destroy the ufo from the inside??? Like the sunken ufo, that might be the case, a failure of an abduction.

This should also solve the time travel mysteries. In fact, replaying missions is the time travel itself since it keeps all of the stuff i've mentioned earlier. Actions in the past have consequences in the present, so if bigfoot altered the past, the present would be different.

Furthermore, those X's on the mural might symbolise the missions you HAVE to discover the alternate ending in order to achieve the reward shown there. Just like backtracking, lets say those lines are the story progression lines. Each character had made a wrong turn somewhere, ending up on an X. If my theory is correct, alternating a specific mission's ending would have as a consequence in the present the CORRECTION of the path. There are more than 1 X's per line, so if you alternate a mission's ending, it would also create a different outcome for another mission.

I would love to see your thoughts regarding this. I might be onto something.
