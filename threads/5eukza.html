So basically I had this idea to map the chiliad mural to a coordinate plane. I downloaded an app called GeoGebra graphing calculator and I started plotting the points on the plane to what I thought was the correct way to represent the chiliad mural on a coordinate plane. Its not very complicated, but It was a fun challenge and I think if it were to be mapped on a plane this is it how it should be done, I also wanted everyone's opinion on what they think the mural would be like if they were too try and map it them selves.

I started from the origin (0,0) to (0,1000) since the meaning of chiliad is 1000, then used common sense and halfed 1000 to get the next point (0,500) then I halfed that to get to the next point 0,(250), next (0,125) and the rest is history..

I used half because on the mural it looks like half from point A (0,1000) to (0,500). On the chiliad mural point A (0,1000) looks like the peak of Mount chiliad to me and on top of that is another segment then it's the eye/oval and circle with the line through the middle , so everything is exactly the size it's supposed to be I believe , and if anyone wants the file to explore each point I can give it to you but you need GeoGebra graphing calculator.
