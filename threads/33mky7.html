As I understand, it's still inconclusive if GTA:O plays a part in the mystery.

In my opinion, I believe it is highly likely the last part of the mystery, or even a completely new mystery, lies in GTA:O. The only reason I believe this is simply because it's something developers can /do/ now.

Easter Eggs used to be a single player thing. Finding developer pictures in hidden sections of the map. Hall of Developers and such. Then developers started hiding shout outs to the campaign or other games in the form of objects or texts hidden throughout multiplayer maps. Today, secrets in multiplayer games are just as complex and intricate as single player secrets. Just look at Battlefield 4. A Megaladon? Like Rockstar aint gunna see someone put a hidden Megaladon in their game, see how the whole community freaks out over trying to find it, and NOT do something just as big in their game.

That being said, anyone else interested in searching together in GTA:O for any clues should post here. Related: http://i.imgur.com/tPaG5CF.png
