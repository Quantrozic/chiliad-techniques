The East Alamo View

I'm not sure if this has been found before, if it has I haven't seen it brought up much but it seems awfully interesting. I did search for East Alamo View but nothing related to this came up in the search. If it has been brought up feel free to downvote to hell lol. To make things short I was riding a bike to the first Epsilon mission when I noticed this spot on the map. Zoomed out version

Since you can't see it from this is a road that is off of the road that runs parallel to the river in Ranton Canyon. The three roads all lead to dead ends, two of which have a little observatory deck (no telescopes though) with the third one just being a dead end. The interesting thing about this is all three of the roads point twords locations that are affiliated with the mystery. The one to the right is looking straight at Mt. Gordo/hippie camp. The one to the North is looking at Mt. Chilliad, and the third is looking right at the Altruist Camp. As soon as I got up there I had a thunderstorm so there also may be a weather trigger associated with the location. Obviously this is interesting because there are 3 spots (3 main characters) and all of them are looking at "hot spots" for the mystery. I don't have 100% myself but I'm going to try and get Micheal, Franklin, and Trevor down here to see what happens.

I recorded a 5 minute clip, but it isn't showing up on Xboxclips.com yet so sorry for the lack of pictures. If it shows up I will post it here. If anyone goes to the location you should see what I'm talking about. If anyone else gets rain/thunderstorms please let me know. Wasn't sure whether to tag this as Analysis, Observation, or Discovery. I thought Analysis fit best because I can't imagine no one has found this spot before.
