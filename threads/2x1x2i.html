I saw this interesting theory on the GTA Forums. I know some people get butthurt if you don't mention that their theory came from another place (because I have time to give a fuck about that) so for the sake of the butt hurt... I saw this on the GTA forums

"I would like to re-mention my "Sandy Shores Prison = Old Military Base(Area 69) location" theory for the 3rd time and perhaps the final time :)

The SA military top secret base was south of the desert airstrip. The sandy shores airstrip has the same exact alignment, same location of hanger and watchtower(sort of). The prison is south of it like the Area 69 base was in SA. Perhaps area 69 was demolished for whatever reason and Fort Zancudo was built instead of it. Also the Ft Zancudo ufo says segregate and rearrange which becomes 'easter egg near grand ear'. The two things at grand ear are the satellite dishes and the prison. 1 dish points back at Ft. Zancudo. Why do we get a wanted lvl flying over the prison? Why does the prison not show up on the in-game map? Its invisible and unmarked on the map like Ft. Zancudo."

This is interesting for a few obvious reasons:

  • It aligns with the cannon from SA
  • The segregate and rearrange clue could refer to the rearranging of the bases. I've never bought that seg. & Rear. just stood for "easter egg" that's just lazy.
  • This theory also ties in the satellite dishes which have always been super mysterious.
  • No in-game map for the prison? I thought there was one on last gen but i'm certain their isn't on current gen. The prison is so damn hard to explore that it would be a great place to hide something. Of course it's been explored a lot but I wouldn't be surprised if something was missed. No one ever talks about searching the prison during thunderstorms or at three am... throw in some episilon robes and the space docker for good measure... drive in circles around the prison for 88 secs? I'm just throwing shit out... The prison could use some more investigating.
  • Also if the prison did hold something like a jet-pack or egg it would make sense to be located near the hippie camp and "beam me up" writing, to perhaps interact with the UFO after retrieving whatever could be in the prison.

What are your thoughts? Share!

Oh and for the people who may also be butthurt by this potentially being rehashed material I will let you know that zeroes fucks are given... at least I'm bringing up something GOOD to discuss.
