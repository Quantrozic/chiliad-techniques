Everyone seems to have accepted that the mural depicts a UFO, a cracked egg, and a jetpack. What is the thing at the top of the mural, then? Is it an eye? Is it a crude depiction of the UFO?

Is it supposed to be the sun or the moon above the peak on Mt. Chiliad? Just kind of thinking about it.
