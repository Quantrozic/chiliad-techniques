Chiliad Secret Hidden Cave?

I decided to take a flight around Chiliad for no good reason when something caught my eye.

Here is the album of my find

First two pictures show the locations. There are two rocky ridges on the back of Chiliad that resemble the ridges on other mountains. My initial search he was for areas that could be landed and stood on by parachute.

Images 2 and 3 show the area in question. There's a tiny outcropping of trees with a different kind of textured wall behind it. This texture matches the normal rock structures found around the mountain, except they didn't do a great job of blending it in.

The rest of the images show the spot itself. Notice how the edges are all badly blended to the landscape and clearly show a divide of some kind. I believe this would be one of the entrances on the mural.

I saw the UFO on top then parachuted down to it, it was the same. Used explosives to no avail. I don't have a mod for glitching into areas, but it should be tried.

What gets me is it's placement. It's partially hidden behind trees. Could be intentional, or it could be a bad dev trying to hide the bad lines in his mountain.

Its also low enough on the mountain that its possible to get down by foot without too much trouble. This i believe is a key to finding the areas on chiliad, because the exit's should not force a death.

I am unsure what we can do for this, as we still haven't figured out the trigger for ...whatever it is we need, but it's something that should be looked at.
