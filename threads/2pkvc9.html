New thoughts on The Hatch - Michelle and the IAA

▲ 11 ptsu/TommatonDec 17, 201435 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:In-Game Phone Dialing Sweeps ?Morse Code Analysis ?Pitch-Contour Morse Extraction ?

So it seems everyone has accepted that the underwater hatch is unrelated to the greater mystery, but I'm not so sure.. I still think there is more there. Where to begin...?

I get that the Mt. Chiliad mural's egg could just reference a general Easter egg, but the hatch from Lost is just so iconic. "The Hatch"! That show was wrapping up around the time this game began development and it was so riddled with mystery and fan speculation that I think R* wouldn't have just ended it at "Hey, you never call. How d'you fancy going bowling?" (this is the message heard near the in-game hatch via Morse code.)

It's this Morse code message that has me intrigued. Everyone is writing this off as an Easter egg for Niko's cousin Roman, but that doesn't add up for me. "Fancy a bowl?" doesn't sound like something he'd say. No, I suspect this message may be from Michelle AKA Karen, Niko's back-stabbing gf from GTA IV. In that game it is revealed that she works for the IAA (GTA's CIA parallel). We know she is in Los Santos, as she can be seen interrogating the suspect in the mission Three's Company in GTA V. During that mission, she escapes, cannot be killed, and isn't heard from again AFAIK. That mission name is interesting because her first appearance in GTA IV is in the mission Three's A Crowd, which results in Niko TAKING HER BOWLING. Having played through both games, I never even noticed the characters were the same person and assumed "Three's Company" was a reference to it being the first mission in which you control all three of GTA V's protagonists. I think the name is purposefully misleading and the clever call back just shows a little too much TLC for this character on R*'s part, considering I didn't catch it at all and I played both games. It's worth noting the GTA V mission was originally called "The Extraction" or something and was changed later in development.

My thought is, we should GIVE HER A CALL, right?? Like she says! Does anyone remember if you could ever see what her number was in GTA IV? Maybe calling that number when the hatch light turns on would trigger something, maybe open the door. I was messing around in my submarine trying to take photos of fish the other day... realized the phone doesn't work in there, but INTERESTINGLY I could still PRESS the phone keys. You can hear them being pushed even though you can't see the phone, so memorizing the button presses might work.

If her number isn't findable, is there an IAA number in the game? Or a United Liberty Paper #? (ULP was the undercover front in GTA IV for the IAA. I read in one of the wikis that she provided the voice mail prompt when calling that office). Or even a number with a Liberty City area code?? If we're considering Michelle/Karen the link between the "Three's A Crowd" and "Three's Company", titles, perhaps the "company" refers to the IAA or ULP.

I also read another poster mention he died near the hatch and it spawned him at the Humane Research Facility even though it's nowhere near the hatch, certainly not the nearest shore. He speculated this was due to a connection to the hatch, perhaps an underground tunnel. And as we all know, Humane is yet another front for the IAA. It is also based off of the real Santa Susana Field Laboratory, which does lots of military R&D, including work with LIQUID-PROPELLANT ROCKET ENGINES.

I don't know man, it's all just food for thought, but it really got my gears turning. I guess I was just sad to see everyone chalk up the hatch message to Roman without a second guess. Gotta be more to that. Unfortunately, I am not at 100% and won't be for some time, so any and all help y'all can provide would be just wonderful. And apologies for the lack of links but all my info comes from the GTA wikis (except the dude respawning at Humane, forget where I read that.) Thoughts?
