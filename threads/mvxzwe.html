I know some people think this can be solved in single player, however if Rockstar were to give any clues, they would have to give it through online DLC’s, however since Rockstar never released a single player DLC they only way they could give clues is through online. Madam Nazar came out in the casino update and let players know some clues (many that are still unknown) and she ensured that there is still a mystery to be unraveled.

The Cayo Perico update which is considered “the biggest update in GTA ever” however has no new clues? Many people have searched the new island without finding much that seemed relevant. So, then I started looking at other things that this update brought, Different cars, Kozatka, a couple different places for prep-missions but still couldn’t find anything. Then it hit me something that I entirely looked over was Tattoos.

I went to the nearest Tattoo parlor and there were some new tattoos that came with the Cayo Perico update. Many of them were just tropical designs but one stood out to me, it was sound waves. At first, I took no second thought thinking it looked cool, but then later I went back to it and took a closer look, it wasn’t just random sound waves it looked like some sort of pattern.

After some research I found that some websites can play audio from sound wave tattoos. (https://www.skinmotion.com/) I don’t have the resources to test this theory for myself. I did some additional research, and I couldn’t find anyone else talking about this. If anyone could try to figure out what the audio from the tattoo is it may have a clue. It may also just be some Easter egg, but I just thought that you all might be interested in this information.

If I have any errors, please let me know, also if you can play the audio let me know what is says. If I remember right, it was under tattoos for my characters leg, I can’t remember which one. Even if it isn’t a clue, it would be kind of cool to know you were the first to find an easter egg. Thank you for your help.
