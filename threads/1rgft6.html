Hey everyone,

I am following this mystery since GTA 5 came out and lurk around reddit in general and this subreddit in particular for quite some time now.

Yesterday, while reading criss-cross through the posts here I stumbled upon the website "www.chiliadmystery.com", which seems to been recently setup. On the 24th Sept. 2013, to be specific and according to the whois-data of the domain. This happens to be 7 days after the official release of GTA 5.

I was curious and looked at the source-code of the webpage, for reasons that I have no non-tinfoil-related explanation for. The index.html source-code is not very spectacular. Just some Google Analytics tracker script (Property ID: UA-44419405-1, can someone reverse engineer and link it to an Analytics account, Rockstar?) and well of course the displayed image.
But even this small site used a CSS-file (style sheet) for it's format and checked this one out, eager to find some copyright or anything.
Instead I found the comment: "/* IT'S HERE! */"
And I was slightly blown away... :)

Screenshots can be found here: http://imgur.com/a/uZKtd

Realistically it could be
a) either someone trolling hard or
b) Rockstar giving a hint to all of us.

The fact that the domain was registered 7 days after the game's release makes me think it was Rockstar. At 7 days in the game not many people were talking about the potential conspiracy. And not many gamers were already planning a several-months long scavenger hunt. And if someone was, they would more like have planned to host a wiki or alike.
After a week the first people surely noticed the mural and the name "chiliadmystery" got quite established, especially amonst youtube-videos. So it makes kind of sense for rockstar to wait a few days and then pick the first established name and use it as a domain.
It would have been to obvious to have the domain registered before the game's release as well.

According to the whois-data it is using admin-c privacy, which is a way to get around using your real credentials for the registration. So I can't link it to rockstar. Usually this service costs a bit extra. But domains are cheap in general.

I then whois'd rockstargames.com and gta5.com, both official domains, which are managed by markmonitor.com, domain and brand management. I couldn't link them to chiliadmystery.com, but to be honest that would have been too easy.

Is someone in the mood to try and send a mail to info@chiliadmystery.com? Maybe they'll respond...who knows.
It's late at night over here...got to work tomorrow.

Cheers
