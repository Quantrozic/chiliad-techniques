The Mt.Chiliad Military Base could be based off Cheyenne Mountain's Military Base

▲ 40 ptsu/rainthemfkiddOct 4, 20218 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:RAG Knowledge-Base Querying ?Wiki-Based Claim Registry Curation ?

In GTAO, The doomsday Act 3 (AKA "The Doomsday Scenario"), you go to Mt.Chiliad and into this top secret Military Base to track down Avon Hertz and there a Giant door used to get into the Military Base just like The Cheyenne Mountain Military Base.

The layout is strikingly simular to the Mt.Chiliad Base. I say that it could be based off this due to both the door and the Base located inside the Mountain, and also the fact that Cheyenne Mountain is very well known as really the only Mountain base in the U.S. (At least to my knowledge).

If it isn't based, I'm sure they at least had SOME inspiration!
