Then I tried to rearrange the second number in the same way and got 4210. Google said it was another irl prototype of an in-game tranctor ih 4210.
I also tried to rearrange these numbers in different ways but got nothing.
In fact I have no idea what it means but I think it has something to do with the back to the future. Perhaps we should use a tractor to reach 88 mph?
