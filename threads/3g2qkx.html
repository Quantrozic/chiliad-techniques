Map of the power grid and Power Theory

I made a map of all the power stations I could find and how they link to each other: http://i.imgur.com/74ATSUR.jpg.

This is to test whether or not the 5 Xs on the mural represent power stations. Here are some points I want to make before moving on to the analysis of the map:

A. I use the term "Power Station" very loosely here. It may be a bunch of power transformers or a transmission substation or a distribution station. I use the same symbol for them all.

B. I mapped the tall power towers (yellow dots) but not the small wooden power poles (blue dots) except a bit around the station #14. The local grid (blue) gets complicated and maybe someone could finish the map if no major clues were found. I'd say that would be a waste of time though.

C. There is a total of 20 power stations on this map. Some are linked and some are not. I named them from 1 to 20 based on whether they are very connected or not :

1 & 2: these two power transformers are located inside the Palmer-Taylor station. #1 Key point if you're talking power and electricity IMHO. These 2 could be considered one entity.

3 through 7: these 5 stations are located within the RON Alternates Wind Farm.

8: this station is the one close to the famous Radars.

9: the most southern station.

10: this one is linked to the local grid with power poles on one side. It links to the rest of the stations on the other side.

11 & 12: these two are connected, but only 12 is linked to a local grid.

13: this is the station on top of Mount Chiliad. I only included it to see if it represents the eye on the mural or not (the eye has a descending line coming from it) and also because there are "Hight Voltage" sings on it.

14: the aerial tramway station on the base of the mountain. It's the only location where I tried to map the local grid as well (wooden power poles) to see if it's linked to station #12 before giving up (so that local grid is incomplete).

15: the station that powers the Altruist camp. Smaller than the other stations. I added it because of the significance of the Altruist camp to the story and the Chiliad Mystery.

16: a station on Sandy Shore that is only linked to a local grid (there are two power towers nearby indirectly connected to it though, which is weird).

17: the station close to the Galileo Observatory. Disappointingly not connected to anything.

18: a pretty big station inside the Department of Water and Power. Not connected to anything, not even the local grid (there are many wooden power poles around it but no connections).

19 & 20: two stations that are not connected to anything even though the power line goes near them.

Analysis:

Here is map with only the stations and their connections: http://i.imgur.com/O4OCAIx.jpg. Let's start by eliminating all the stations that don't seem important (always regarding the 5 Xs). I made a petty attempt to make a diagram out of the connections: http://imgur.com/SpEwQHp (the mural seems like a simplified diagram). Some stations could be combined into one component.

I'm sure you guys could come up with more interesting finds as the two possibilities of confirming or debunking the Power theory are equally interesting.

Here are some more ideas:

  1. The one issue I have with this theory is that I can't seem to destroy the power stations or their key components. So what should we do with the stations once we know which ones are represented by the 5 Xs?
  2. If you look closely at the mural, the connections between the 5 Xs seem more like blocks than simple lines. So maybe we'll need more components to have a similarity between the map and the mural (zones and regions maybe?).

p.s. I can send the PSD of the map (71 Mb) to anyone who wants to contribute or is interested.

TL;DR: I mapped the "power stations" and how they're linked. See if that would help confirm or debunk the power theory that says the 5 Xs on the murals represent power stations.
