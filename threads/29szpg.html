Let's talk about karma (in an intelligent way this time)

▲ 4 ptsu/fucking_chiliadJul 4, 201411 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Karma / Pacifist Playthrough Protocol ?

At first it seems like karma in this game is bullshit. I haven't seen a single place where doing something morally right changed the outcome. But there are LOTS of references to karma. It's brought up in a convo with Trevor near the beginning of the game, its written all over the hippy camp, Jesus at the pier talks about "lame people" and stuff, and the concept of karma is implied in a lot of conversations, especially ones with Michael. A completely "morally good" playthrough with no killing is probably not the solution to this mystery, that would be ridiculous in a game based on robbing banks, shooting cops, killing hookers, and everything else we love about GTA. However, it still seems possible that some choices can influence things in a positive way. I've noticed if you choose to let Ortega live, he just ends up showing up during the meth lab attack. And a lot of random events have a choice between who you help, the criminal or the victim (sometimes police officers) No matter how ridiculous it sounds in a game full of crime, karma very well might exist IN SOME WAY in GTA. I want to hear any observations or speculations from anybody playing the game in a relatively nice way. Tell me what decisions you've made, tell me ways you've gotten around killing someone (for example, the guard in the prologue. I've heard he can be injured and not killed, but haven't found evidence. Tell me any changes to the story line you've seen that were based on your good choices. Tell me ANYTHING
