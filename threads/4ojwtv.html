The Chiliad UFO is Lord Zapho.

▲ 35 ptsu/UndeadOPJun 17, 201647 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Sun-Shadow Azimuth Triangulation ?

No, I'm not high right now. This is a legit theory that I've had for a while.

The Altruists worship Lord Zapho, who they depict as a sun in their drawings. You know, if you think about it...that sun looks a lot like the eyes on the mural and in the glyphs. We know that those eyes are supposed to symbolize the Chiliad UFO. So, is there a connection here? I think so.

I think the Altruists could be a nocturnal cult if you think about it. I know they're more active in the daytime, but there's a couple things that point towards them worshipping Lord Zapho at night.

  • The "jetpack shadow" on the rock doesn't appear until sunset.
  • The leader of the cult is always staring out into the ocean where the actual sun sets. I believe that he is waiting for the actual sun to set so that their godly sun (the Chiliad UFO) can rise.
  • Their website shows the moon in the sky, blocking the sun. They're basically prioritizing the nighttime over the daytime with that banner.

Let's examine the glyphs now:

  • "See the eye!" As I previously mentioned, their sun looks a lot like the eye from the mural. This just further confirms the connection.
  • "Our path is lit!" As we all know, the Chiliad UFO shines a path of light from its underside.
  • "He is risen!" Okay, so that glyph looks like the actual sun rising over the water. But, it could also represent the sun setting under the water. As I previously mentioned, I believe the cult leader is looking out into the distance at the water waiting for the sun to set so that Lord Zapho can rise.
  • "His light shines on us all!" Just another reference to the UFO's light.

So I believe Rockstar intentionally made these glyphs look like the sun to throw us off. If you still don't believe this theory, there's one more thing I could to do to prove it to you:

When the Altruist Cult speaks to Trevor when he delivers hitchhikers, pause your screen and look at the dialogue. This is the best picture I could find online. Their symbol is someone looking up in awe at a UFO.

So yeah. I believe Lord Zapho is nothing but the Chiliad UFO. We know the Chiliad UFO is man-made and is a fake, so basically innocent people are getting murdered to please a fake deity. Sounds like something Rockstar would put in their game, right?
