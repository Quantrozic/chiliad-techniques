Let's Make Groups

▲ 26 ptsu/SteezymannMar 11, 201634 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Livestreamed Group HuntsModel Vertex-Group Inspection ?

I feel like it would be way easier for us to work our way down the checklist of things to try or theories to test and things to debunk if we all grouped up.

If people who believe the altruist holds the answer, they all have a group and test certain things by member of the group.

Example: Member 1 will test respawn times today. Member 2 will listen to all npc dialogue in the camp. Member 3 will watch all npc movements throughout the camp.
Etc...

Do that for every group of hunters and we can haul ass through the "checklist" . So say you believe the hippy camp has more to find, you do the same as the listed example and more and you meet back up here on a daily hint hunt thread.

I gave up hope on there being anything a little while ago but came back and found you all had pulled the sub out of the rut it seemed to be in and became active about things and discovered new leads and just seem more enthusiastic again. Let's not waste your good progress by being disorganized.

Things always work best when people work together. Not just bounce ideas off of each other, but when they actually get together and collectively test their ideas. You're all smart and very creative. If you all can channel that creativity we can figure out once and for all what there is or isn't there to find.

What do y'all think? I know most everyone has different views of this whole mystery, but enough of us share a common interest in certain areas and theories. There are enough of us to do this and if you really want to buckle down and do this, let's do it.

If this is something y'all want to do, we have to figure out what groups there will be. Take a poll on what area interests the user base here the most, then do another poll for top theory. Then do another poll on which we would like to start first. Then get volunteers for the first group to study the top polled area and or theory.

Like I said above there can be group altruist. Then group hippy camp, then group maze, then group observatory, group lost camp, group chiliad, group paleto and so on.

Like I said, you all are great, you are all smart and again you are very creative. We just need to get organized.

Anyways. It may not be what anyone wants but I figured I would throw the idea out there.

Thanks for reading.
