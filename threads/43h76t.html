If you reload the North Yankton Bank post-prologue, you can enter the surveillance room

▲ 40 ptsu/spaceleviathanJan 31, 20168 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:GTA Online Update Diff Surveillance

If you using a trainer you reload the North Yankton Bank area post-prologue, you can enter the surveillance room which is fully modeled & textured.

I've always wondered if you could enter as it shows up on the minimap and it turns out if you go back not in the mission you can open the door.

To note in my exploration I did not find anything of note it's just monitors and footage controls and a cabinet / chair. There is some sort of paper posted behind the door you have to close the door to see it, but its so low res i doubt it means anything.

Then started a new game to try shooting out all the cameras before taking ONLY the cash in the corner (shot a way the cash on the floor), in hopes of perhaps not having the guard see us, I tried to avoid the cameras before shooting them out but the placement means pretty much the moment after the explosion you are seen by the camera behind the double doors.
(will post screenshots tomorrow morning as i have to run out right now already running late).

didn't find anything on search about this so thought i'd post and perhaps share an interesting discovery and debunk a possible theory at the same time
