More ocean, fully mooning, more like ZancuNo amirite? -- Huntathon update: The sequel.

Documented under:Multi-Save Parallel A/B Testing ?Tide & Water-Level Observation

You know what makes hours of hunting seem less tedious? Listening to the world cup while you're doing it. If I were a bigger soccer fan, I might actually be watching the games, but I'm not, so fuck it.

Anyway, I got working on the theories people sent me in messages, but didn't find anything too interesting there. Those theories mostly took place in the city, though, so I managed to spend a lot of the time there also checking for false walls. I've already spent tens of hours looking for them, but there's still plenty of space I hadn't checked. No finds, but I've covered quite a bit of ground, so there's that.

I also did this shit:

More Ocean Stuff

  • I spent a bunch more time scouring the ocean floor, but haven't found anything. I can't say that I've seen every square inch, but I checked the hell out of what we can reach. I've checked what I think is all the interior space that allows for crush depth (as opposed to the very edges), and what I'm confident is a very large part of everywhere else. Visibility gets rough at night, but isn't terrible. I searched specifically for small caves and unique objects. I'm pretty confident myself that there's probably nothing down there that we don't already know about, and I think I've exhausted my will to search there again.

Full Moon Stuff

  • Made a save with a full moon, and started with easy stuff. Went to all UFOs to see if something might change. No dice.
  • Checked all Chiliad glyphs to see if they would change somehow. They didn't seem to.
  • Checked the hippy camp for some kind of change, but it didn't seem to. No real changes in NPCs either.
  • Flew around the desert looking for what could be a full moon party, but didn't find anything.
  • Checked the lighthouse, and parachuted down on top of it to mess around with it some, but nada.
  • Checked the Gordo power shack, the small pond next to it, and the single grave on the mountain south of there, but nothing changed.
  • Checked the Altruist camp, and lit myself on fire in the cave, but nothing happened.

(This is all small stuff I'm sure people have either tried directly, or just in regularly playing the game. There's more complicated stuff I want to try, but I'm always open to ideas.)

Zancudo Stuff

  • Went to the bunker elevator on a full moon to check if anything happened. Nothing. Tried it while the light was on, then tried it all with no wanted level. Strangely, the only place I've managed to cheat the wanted level down within the main area is in the bunker, even if there were soldiers outside of it.
  • Walked around the control tower pressing right on the d-pad to see if there was a hidden switch around, but there didn't seem to be.
  • Investigated the elevator in the tower to see how it functioned, and it seems like the elevator isn't an elevator bunch a bunch of rooms made to look like one. The floor marker always says 1, and I'm pretty sure there's an elevator room behind every elevator door. Checked the old vid where someone got the bunker elevator doors to open online, and the floor light always said -1. It's the same floor indicator from the elevator in Humane Labs, and that one changed with floor. If there's a way to trigger that elevator to "move" up, getting in would trigger a cutscene, or a break like when you enter your apartment online. This should mean wherever it leads would be somewhere under the map, or outside of it. Maybe the elevator door opens, and whatever there is to find is just sitting there inside, and there's no extra room to go to. All I know is that it's interesting.

I know many of these things have been tried, but fuck it. In my opinion, the most solid lead we have is that the bunker elevator light is the kind from Humane Labs, and not the kind from the control tower. This may be a result of it being an elevator that leads far down, like the one at Humane, but it's something to go on.

Tons of variables, so I'm keeping at it for now. I've been doing lots of searching for a false wall, but I think I'm going to quit that. Still working on stuff, but want to be more thorough with them.
