What does 333 mean??

▲ 8 pts[deleted]Dec 31, 20183 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:In-Game Phone Dialing Sweeps

Still testing stuff (PS3) and just noticed that when you get a text or an e-mail, the number "333" comes up on all three characters phones, does anyone know why??

I Googled "333" and this is what came up-"Angel number 333 symbolizes aid and encouragement. It means that your angels are just nearby, ready to help and reassure you that your plans are going well. It sends the message that your prayers have been answered, and seeing 333 means that whatever you requested for is on its way to you."

I thought that it might have been an "Angel number" Easter Egg as I noticed it happening after getting a "need help" type text from the Hen House, but I tested it a little more and noticed it comes up with any e-mail or any text that I tried it with, it's strange and considering it's the number "3" thought that it might mean something.

To test this yourself (For those who don't know/if it hasn't been noticed before), get out your phone and go to dial a number manually, but instead press the star button, this brings up the last number you manually dialed and called on the phone and you'll see it'll be empty. Go purchase a business like a Cinema or something and they will send you an e-mail afterwards (Most businesses do, or just trigger an e-mail/text any other way). Now check your phone again the same way as before and the number now comes up as "333". Switch to any other character and check their phones and they too will have the "333", even though they didn't get the e-mail or text themselves...

It didn't work with the phone calls I received (From Lemar and Ms. Madroza) so I'm assuming that phone calls don't trigger it and only receiving texts and e-mails do-and I tested to see if say you normally dial a number with Franklin, it'll save on his phone when pressing the star button, but switch to Mike and Trevor, press the star button on their phones and the number Franklin dialed won't be on their phones, it'll only happen with the "333" thing when getting an e-mail or text, then it effects all 3 phones-it's strange and might be worth testing some more, but I didn't want to waste my time if it's already been found.

Could be just a weird glitch type thing or something, but could also be a possible source of clues too, like maybe different texts/e-mails bring up different numbers sometimes, or something to do with the Badger Dishes, or it's telling us to dial 333 somewhere, or maybe something to do with the "shoot for the stars" stuff (You're pressing the star button to recall the number)-I'm not really sure what "333" would mean in the game, if anything so I have no idea atm, but thought it worth a mention and my apologies if it's been brought up before (I did search, no results about it). Happy holiday's ppl.....
