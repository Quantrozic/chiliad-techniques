Evidence That "Karma" Affects NPC's Behaviors

▲ 6 ptsu/SkruffeeSep 25, 201425 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Karma / Pacifist Playthrough Protocol ?NPC Pathfinding Observation LoggingShared-Engine Behavior Comparison

On my first playthrough, I killed relentlessly on all characters. On that save I noticed that hippies didn't fight. Then on my no-kill as Micheal playthrough, I noticed they were a lot ballsier, and would attack me with little provocation on my part. I decided to make sure I wasn't imagining things, and played both saves one after the other. The following video shows that test and its results. http://youtu.be/gOQ4A-X3VoQ

The hippies weren't the only ones, some of the big women in the desert will also fight Micheal. This happens way to often to be coincidence. My theory is that it's mainly his strength stat being so low that makes weaker NPCs willing to fight him. But in any case I say "karma" in quotes because I assume it's his stats causing this(which are the way they are because he's good). I'm at 79% on my Karma playthrough, I'll make a post detailing it and asking for suggestions when I'm at 100%.

Also, before you dismiss it, it's not that hippies don't fight my bad Micheal, it's that they do it almost never, far, far less than they do to good Micheal.

sorry for the crappy recording, my Xbox is modified to use with headphones, so I just stuck together some songs I made to fill the silence. OH and just FYI, I was just walking up too close to them, I didn't talk or insult them.
