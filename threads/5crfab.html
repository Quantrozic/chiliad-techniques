A few things that stand out to me on a new playthrough.

▲ 19 ptsu/DragontearNov 13, 201616 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Karma / Pacifist Playthrough Protocol ?

Okay so I've been following this sub for a while and there are a few interesting theories and elements that haven't really lead anywhere. It's clear that if there is a solvable case here there clearly have been some clues overlooked and the solution is going to be something crazy obscure. So I've started a new playthrough and am just going to go over some details I've noticed thus far:

  • The beginning mission, We are lead on some pretty strict lines here, mostly as a tutorial, but it does force the player to make decisions they maynot otherwise make. Threatening people and killing a guard as well as many police officers. But we are often rewarded for going off the beaten path, the secret room, the frozen alien and the hidden webs. Of course seeing these often results in failing the mission at hand. So I feel like trying to break the game and seek out alternatives to missions is something important overall.
  • Next there is also the cut scene with the Dr. Friedlander where phrenology is subtly referenced, basically its the concept that different parts of the brain handle different functions with very simplified definitions. I won't get into huge detail here, but the statue in the background is a chart and I think this is part of a key that we should use to decipher the rest of the story as allegorical. The Psych report is a huge element of the game, so I wouldn't be so quick to rule the karma theory possibility out.
  • After that is the introduction mission of Franklin and Lamar repossessing the doctors car. Has anyone ever tried to race Lamar there while keeping the car in perfect condition?
  • So first thing I notice as soon as i'm in the sandbox is the local gang members who have an affinity toward Franklin, if you walk toward Grove street you'll eventually find yourself in enemy turf, no matter how many gangfights I instigate I can't seem to capture the hood. I also notice that different NPCs act differently depending on your player character. Some will have full blown conversations with you, others may take offence or fear you. In San Andreas this was based on the outfit you wore but I haven't been able to find any concrete evidence in V to confirm any kind of solid pattern for NPC behavior.
  • Certain parts of the map change along with the story. For example the old farm house you burn down, or the lost camp you raid. Visiting these spots earlier in the plot shows different results than later on, so in addition to the newscasts, there is very distinct possibility that there are a lot of subtle changes happening we maynot even notice.
  • The subway and sewers man these are kind of elaborate and there are a lot of fake doors and maps and symbols. I'm not saying we should start hunting for Ratman or anything, but there is kind of a faint heartbeat sound that was debated on the sub a while back.
  • Those little bits of steam that come out of the ground. What's up with those? Is that like a thing in real life or something?
  • Alternate dialogue/story options, You can get a haircut as Franklin early on in the game to impress Lamar, I've noticed a few other things as well, would be prudent to try to get a list or chart of what choices we actually have and start from there.
  • I'll take my tinfoil hat now, but I'm starting to indeed suspect there might be a karma/groundhogs day thing going on where you need to play out the game perfectly making moralistic choices while speedrunning + maybe some hidden stuff to unlock a fourth ending+perks.

Just a couple of thoughts, nothing solid, but I would like to have an overview of gamemap changes based on plot progression, NPC behaviors, and optional choices that result in dialogue or mission structure changes.
