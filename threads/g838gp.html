Below is a rough summary of how to unlock them, what you need to do, and what they unlock. There might be more to the Epsilon Tract. It could be a mistake but it seems like an odd one. I'm going to look through the scripts to see what I can find. Unless someone already has?

Letter Scraps

Unlocks after Repossession. Only Franklin can complete it. Collecting all the Letter Scraps reveals Peter Dreyfus was the one who mutilated and killed the actress Leonora Johnson and unlocks A Starlet in Vinewood where you can chose to kill or spare Peter Dreyfus.

Spaceship Parts

Mission for Franklin. Becomes available after Fame and Shame. Franklin has to meet Omega at his trailer, who says he was almost abducted but the spaceship crashed. Omega has the same device that the Epsilons use to detect Aliens. He asks you to collect all 50 spaceship parts and return them to Omega in his lab/trailer near RON wind farm. Unlocks the Space Docker.

Epsilon Tract

Mission for Michael. Unlocks after completing Unknowing the Truth (final Epsilon mission). Collecting 10 Epsilon Tracts unlocks a website in-game (www.epsilonprogram.com/tract ) for viewing the complete tract. Seems anti-climactic? This is also the only hidden package collectible where the label (visible max) doesn’t match up with the true max value. Max value is apparently 15 but we only collect 10. Interesting to note that for a very brief period 6 years ago the Social Club website listed Epsilon tracks as totaling 15 but it got quickly changed. There was a lot of speculation about hidden tracts.The game has only ever said 10 but this line says the max value is 15 and to only display 10 as max. This is from a recent version of the game. The label value is what gets shown in the UI.

Nuclear Waste

Available after purchasing the Sonar Collections Dock after completing The Merryweather Heist. Collecting all 30 nuclear awards you $250k + $690k from the barrels.

Submarine Wreckage

Available to Michael after purchasing the Sonar Collections Dock and completing Blitz Play or Monkey Business. Abigail asks you to investigate the death of her husband. Collect all 30 submarine parts and Abigail gives you $10 and a photo of her husband Frank Mathers. When presented with evidence of sabotage she claims the police are in on it and she plans to tell the FBI. If you follow her she goes to the airport instead and claims you are harassing her giving you 3 star wanted.

Peyote

Eating a peyote plant turns you in to 1 of 27 animals. Collecting 21 unlocks controllable animals in Director Mode and an achievement. Collecting all 27 starts the spawning of Golden Peyotes and opens up doing the Beast Hunt

Monkey Mosaic graffiti

Unlocks after Chop mission. Franklin is sent the text but any character can take the pictures. Take a photo of all 50 monkey mosaic graffiti. Unlocks Monkey Mosaic random event where you can take a photo of the graffiti artist in action to unlock monkey themed clothes and the Go Go Monkey Blista car

Wildlife Photography

Unlocks after Franklin helps Beverly take pictures of celebrities in Paparazzo. Take photos of 20 wildlife and Franklin gets the Kraken submersible. The message you get from the tourism board after is a little odd. It references the peyotes, people falling off cliffs, people eaten by mountain lions, then ends with “…we’d love you to continue your adventures in the seas.”

Destroyed Signs

This is related to the Strangers and Freaks mission Extra Commision where Josh Bernstein asks Trevor to break real estate signs to sabotage Lenny Avery. Breaking all the signs unlocks further missions with Josh and you get to sleep with his wife twice. The final mission (Breach of Contract) you can chose to kill Josh or spare.

3 of the scenarios lead to choosing to kill or spare someone.

3 of them you can use Chop to help you find pieces.

3 of them involve taking pictures of stuff with your phone.

3 of them unlock a unique vehicle.

Lots of audio cues.

Not trying to make a point about 3 it's just funny how it worked out. Let me know if I should add any relevant information, correct anything, or if you want something investigated a little more.
