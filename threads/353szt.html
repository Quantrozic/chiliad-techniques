New Take on The Mural Being a Trap & The Symbol Meanings

▲ 1 pts[deleted]May 6, 20157 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Comparative Mural Iconography Cataloging ?

Good morning/evening fellow hunters, new poster here, I however have been skimming through this sub-Reddit for about a year now, on-and-off. After finding this great mystery once again the other week, I have decided to pick the game back up and try to help on the adventure. I digress, after reading the theory that the 5 red crosses on the mural are traps in the story, I thought over what could they could mean, and I came up with this. Sticking to the idea that the red crosses are traps in the story which will prevent the secret from occurring, I came up with the theory that the traps might be recognisable due to thunder or bad weather in the cut scene of said missions. I know this is far fetched, as I haven't done a story mission in a long time, but this idea came along due to the thunder striking the mountain. I know the whole basis of there being thunder in order to see the UFO, but this theory is just a suggestion which could be tested.

Moving on to the next topic, the images of the UFO, Egg and jet pack man are widely known to be a depiction of the three characters (M being the UFO, F the egg and T the jet pack), however due to my prolonged vacation from the mystery I never quite heard whether anyone explained why the characters were these select images, therefore I came up with my own belief. I am lead to believe that by looking at the mural, the UFO and jet pack are connected under the egg, signifying Michael and Trevor's past before the game started & the first story mission. However, Franklin could be the egg as a metaphor to a new person, a young, petty criminal which is signified by the cracking open of a egg, birthing a young person in the midsts of 2 old criminals.

I know these suggestions don't really effect whether we get closer to the end of the mystery or not, but I felt my suggestions might clear up anything untold.

Last point, if anyone is able to tell me how the symbols got connected to the 3 characters, it will be gratefully appreciated!

tldr; thunder might be seen in the missions which are the traps & Franklin is the Egg as he is the youngest.
