The original WOW signal happened in Ohio on Monday, August 15, 1977 at 11:16:00 PM EDT. If you do a time zone conversion for LA, this time would be 8:00 PM PDT. The time the Chiliad UFO appears is 3:00am. I had my time conversion backwards when I first looked this up, and it lined up to 3:00am, but since it has FIB on the side I assume it's not extra terrestrial in origin so I'll still give this a go just to say it's been done.
