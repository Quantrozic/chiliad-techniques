Altruist camp / Sun plane findings... Ozark Mountain Daredevils?!

▲ 13 ptsu/Dog_BreadOct 31, 20135 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Bit-Plane Steganalysis of Game Assets ?

After the great video posted by FartSausage, I had a good look round the altruist camp this morning, instead of going to bed.

I saw a lot of yellow and red sun/eye glyphs, heard the mysterious music as I walked around, and read the messages like "embrace the light", "your path is lit", "praise him" "we are his children".

I discovered that it is very easy to trigger the mystery airplane by aiming at the sun with a sniper rifle and zooming in on its center. Hold it for a few seconds and you should hear the engines of the plane. Zoom out a bit and you will soon see it.

You can stay zoomed in and follow the plane to see where it is heading. If you zoom out, or stop aiming, the plane soon gets out of range, and disappears much like the Chiliad UFO when you stray too far from the viewing platform. Stay zoomed in from a good vantage point and you can see it go quite far. I was mainly watching it from the top of the hill with the altruist radio tower.

You can trigger it again and again by aiming at things. Once you have seen it once, it seems to come again and again whenever you zoom in on something. Allow about an hour of in game time between zoomings, and you should get it every time. I was aiming at all sorts of stuff, the moon, the altruist radio tower, a plane flying through Raton Canyon, I'm not sure it matters. Perhaps only the first time.

anyway, having followed the plane's path from the top of the hill, I noticed that it is going in a line towards the San Chianski mountains, in the direction of the Humane Labs. One time I shot at the plane a few times, and it made a definite veer to the right, heading more towards Sandy Shores before disappearing.

Slightly weirder than all that was the fact that when I zoomed in on Sandy shores after watching the plane yet again, I could hear Ozark Mountain Daredevils from Rebel Radio playing "If You Wanna Get to Heaven" on a loop.

I was zoomed in all the way, and the music was very faint. However, it was definitely there, and faded out as I panned away from Sandy Shores, and back in again as I returned. The loudness seemed to be centred on the large radio tower in the centre of Sandy Shores.

I stayed zoomed in for at least a couple of playthroughs of the song, to verify the loop, and it just kept going with no ads or DJ. There wasn't anyone else or any vehicles on the hilltop with me, and the sound was only present while zoomed in. I could hear the plane spawning in a few times while I was zoomed in on the town, but I didn't want to pan away to it because I was into the music.

I tried to recreate the situation by reloading my save, killing all the altruists, triggering the plane and going to the hilltop, but it hasn't worked. Perhaps it's a weird glitch, perhaps it's a clue to investigate Sandy Shores (and/or the radio tower there), perhaps it's an obvious game feature which my sleep deprived brain has overlooked.

Anyone else had something like this happen?
