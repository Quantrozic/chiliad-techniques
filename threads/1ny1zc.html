So I figured a great way to figure out where to go next would to be to literally follow the egg from discovery to where it would lead in terms of clues.

For example:

Gta V screens of a giant mountain with a shed everyone wants to check out.

Check out shed see weird mural must be an easter egg.

Notice the same red eye is underneath the wood at the top of the mountain.

Now im guessing after the point in which we found the eye at the top of the mountain people knew there was more according to the map. THe next logical step I would assume that happend was people were investigating the platform and noticed a "return when your story is complete" message and thus thought that you must complete the game for something to happen.

So the next thing I would think that would be discovered if it wasnt for a massive community would be the glyphs on the mountain while searching for entrances to the mountain as that is how it looks.

Now this is where I get lost. How does the mural say at all that you will find UFO's at zancudo or the hippy camp. It doesn't.

There has to be a logical next step we would take as if we were oblivious to the things found on the internet. Not just scour the entire map hoping for something to pop up.

So, forget everything that was ever found so far. Done? Good.
Now all we have found so far in this hypothetical game we are playing is a Mural at the top of the mountain, which led us to the eye at the very top, to the return when game is complete, to glyphs on the mountain.

WHERE DO WE GO NOW? Based solely upon the clues we have so far.

I am assuming we realize that at 100% we must return to the top to see what happens and we just happen to be there when raining at 3am (go figure) and see a UFO. What now?

I hope this is able to throw some new ideas into the mix and help us solve this thing.
