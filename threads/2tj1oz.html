So, after fishing through some of the threads on here, I found this overlay. It has the Eye at the Observatory and the jetpack man at the Altruists. I decided to go into my game and go to where the other symbols are to see if anything else overlayed perfectly.

First off, the lightning bolts do not overlay to anywhere of interest. So ignore those.

All of the X's overlayed to very interesting places. They didn't exactly overlay on to these places, but rather overlayed to places nearby where the point of interest could be seen clearly.

Bottom-right of eye - takes you to a funeral home with a graveyard in the backyard. Nothing really mystery-related here, but a random event spawns here where you drive a girl home away from her fiance. She can be taken to the Altruist camp.

Bottom-left of the eye - a little wandering around this area takes you to none other than the Rebel Radio station building. If you don't know what's so special about this building, its logo is a ram skull, the same skull seen in the cave at the Altruist camp.

Above jetpack - a road on Mount Josiah. This particular road (well, path as it's not on the in-game map) takes you straight to the tip-top of Mount Josiah. Once there, you have a perfect view of Fort Zancudo. I also heard that you can see the Zancudo UFO from here under the circumstances needed to trigger it, so maybe this means something.

Above egg - a shallow part of the Alamo Sea near the road. Nothing of interest here, but you get a glimpse of the alien silos, which are down this same road. Only one slash of this X is visible on the mural, so perhaps this is telling us to only acknowledge the right silo? By the way, this is the silo with that "Caution: Liquid" box on top of it.

Above UFO - some rundown trailers with nothing too mysterious about them. Out in the distance, you get a clear view of the satellite array, so maybe this is where it's pointing us to go/see.

Jetpack - obviously, this leads us to the Altruist camp.

Cracked egg - I'm still not that convinced this is supposed to represent a location/prize. But if it does, this overlay has the egg as a dock out around Mount Gordo. Interesting to note that on the same road that leads to this dock, Ursula's random event can be triggered.

UFO - just a redneck camp with a bunch of rundown houses/trailers. Interesting to note, when viewed at certain times of the day, the telephone poles at this place give off the same shadows seen at the hippy camp. If you don't know what I'm talking about, if you look at the ground next to the poles at certain times of the day you can see a shadow of a pole with the Eye of Providence on top. This same shadow appears at the hippy camp and at the Observatory. Interesting to note, if you follow the telephone wires from this location, you'll wind up at the hippy camp.

So, if we figure that the UFO is taking us to the hippy camp, then that means this mural was presumably drawn by both the Altruists and the hippies. The locations the X's bring us to are there to help us solve the mystery. All of the locations the X's brought us to somehow tie in with the Altruists and the hippies. The runaway bride & docks/Ursula tie in to the Altruists because these two can be brought there; Ursula can also tie in with the fact that she can trigger rain to view the Chiliad UFO; the satellite array ties in with the hippies; Rebel Radio ties in with the Altruists due to the ram skull; Fort Zancudo ties in with the hippies because they constantly talk about the "artifact" and an FIB-owned UFO can be seen above their camp; and the silos obviously tie in with the hippies.

I think the whole mystery revolves around the hippies, the Altruists, and the FIB. The Altruists are sort of an Illuminati cult, the hippies are against the government and believe in aliens, and the FIB are always watching us & also seem to have made alien contact.

Tell me what you guys think. Out of all overlays, this one seems to the best.
