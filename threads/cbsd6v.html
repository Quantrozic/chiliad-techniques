Has anyone found UFOs' creepy sound files yet? i mean extract from game's files not a youtube record. I find it interesting no one has posted it yet if they found it. Im a sound modder for GTA V (name is Suspect on gta5mods) and i can say ive listened to tons of sounds in game files still i havent heard them. I know some thing about how sounds are used in GTA V that might interest you.

Did you know that firing sound of Assault Rifle (AK) Is actually an extremely high pitched version of another sound thats totally different? Gta v heavily relies on audio system that takes one sound and modifies the shit out of it. This doesnt only happen to Assault Rifle, lots of other guns use heavily modified sounds as well:

AP Pistol is actually Bullpup shotgun sound but again high pitched and some other sound altering effects used on it.

Grenade Launcher firing sound is the same as tear gas going off sound but low pitched.

the core boom of explosions use firing sound of Desert Eagle but fades in with other explosion sounds i found it out one time when i was modding Desert eagles sound to laser pistol sound and whenever explosions happened in my game i heard that laser pistol but with lower tone which again shows how rockstars implemented a system into the game which adds many uses to one audio file. Thats why guns sound like shit in the game because they are 70% remixes of other sound effects. High pitch, low pitch,low tone, high tone and other effects used.

My overall point with telling you this fact is that since UFO sounds arent in the open in game files then its using a heavily modified version of something else. If we could find which sound it uses maybe it would be a big clue to discovering something. I read here before that as with New gen become the beast easter egg, rockstar loves to hide clues in game files too. With texts with file names with whatever.

Just wanted to share this cool info about rockstars audio secret Happy hunting!

EXTRA: Does anybody else remember that...before the game's release, there were many articles online about how Rockstar hired a special team of coders or programmers to "hide" game files so they are well hidden/encrypted for Gta V? i really wonder now if that was true or not since seeing how some sound files are non existant in game files but can be heard in game because it combines modified versions of different sounds from different folders. Maybe scripts also do that in the game?
