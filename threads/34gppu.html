2013 Epsilon Contest? Archived Version of epsilonprogram.com

▲ 9 ptsu/EnergyTurtle23May 1, 201527 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Archived-Thread Rescue and Mirroring

Remember those five "missing people" reportedly abducted by the Epsilon Program? This Cultstoppers website appeared around September, 2013. I've seen it linked many times in other threads, and I think I may have found some sort of explanation for it.

It seems that in 2013, Rockstar was setting up some sort of promotional event involving GTAV and the Epsilon Program. Here's one of the first posts, released on April 22nd, 2013, which mentioned that more info would be posted in a week:

  • KIFFLOM

So I found this post in the Newswire archive, then crawled ahead a week to find this invitation:

  • The Enlightenment Seminar Invitation

Rockstar posted this on April 29, 2013. Reportedly five contestants would be "chosen" for something. They had to enter the contest on the Epsilon Program website, which made its first appearance around this time, as well as submit photos of themselves using the #KIFFLOM twitter tag.

Using the Wayback Machine, this was one of the earliest functional snapshots I could get of the Epsilon website:

  • http://www.epsilonprogram.com" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">epsilonprogram.com May 27th, 2013 Snapshot: Entry Form

This is what epsilonprogram.com looked like from April 29th to May 29th. There is an entry form on the front page for this contest. Then the application period closes one month later, on May 29th:

  • http://www.epsilonprogram.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">epsilonprogram.com May 29th, 2013 Snapshot: Applications Closed

This is how the page stays for a few months. Then in August, the page is updated with an announcement. Five winners have been chosen, and they are being prepared "for indoctrination at our Los Santos Learning Center":

  • http://www.epsilonprogram.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">epsilonprogram.com August 6th, 2013 Snapshot: Winners Chosen

At this point, the Epsilon Program website is apparently abandoned. It stays like this until January 2015, and then disappears from the web entirely.

So I turned to the Epsilon twitter page. This account was created around 2012, but nothing really significant was posted here until the contest was announced. The twitter page follows the contest up until May 29th, and then the contest is never mentioned again. Here is the last time that it is mentioned on Twitter:

Epsilon Program @EpsilonismToday:
We are currently reviewing your photos and will be in contact with those we deem worthy very soon. #KIFFLOM http://www.epsilonprogram.com
12:26 PM - 28 May 2013

From this point until around September, the Epsilon twitter page reverts to posting regular links to their promotional screensaver. Then Epsilon makes a few posts claiming that they are being harassed by CultStoppers.com:

Epsilon Program @EpsilonismToday:
Listen we are NOT a cult
8:21 AM - 5 Sep 2013

Epsilon Program @EpsilonismToday:
Cultstoppers are harassing us. We will harass back.
8:31 AM - 5 Sep 2013

Epsilon Program @EpsilonismToday:
Hello, non-people at http://Cultstoppers.com – get a life – we are NOT a cult.
10:28 AM - 5 Sep 2013

So I checked the Wayback Machine, and this date is the first appearance of cultstoppers.com on the net. The Epsilon Program website has since been taken down, but cultstoppers.com is still being maintained by Rockstar.

So I would guess that the five abductees are actually the winners of the Epsilon contest held in 2013, but this raises several more questions for me. Why was there such a long delay between the end of the contest and the appearance of cultstoppers.com? What was the "indoctrination" that the winners were given? Was the contest prize simply to be rendered as a GTAV character and used on promo website? I'm wondering if there's more to this. Have these five people ever come forward and talked about the contest? Plotwise, plenty of people are recruited to Epsilon all the time, why were these five recruited via a contest, and why did they later disappear?

Any more info you guys have would be much appreciated, just some interesting stuff that I found when going through the Newswire. Not likely related to the Chiliad Mural I would think.

UPDATE: Commenters below have provided links to several of these guys' twitter pages, as well as photos of their characters in-game. They have never described the full details of their prize, but we do know that they appear in-game as randomly spawned Epsilon Program NPCs, and they appear on the Cultstoppers website, starting in 2013. There was quite a long period between the end of the contest, when it was announced that five people had won, and the appearance of the Cultstoppers website. Four months in fact. The full details of their prize were never made public by Rockstar either, in my opinion it was treated rather mysteriously.

From the sound of it, I'm not the only one who noticed this. When the Chiliad Mural was discovered, a lot of people tried to ask the contest winners if they knew anything about it. As far as we can tell, the winners never responded to any mystery-related questions on Twitter. Take from that what you will. It's possible that they know something about the secrets of GTAV, possibly even something about the Chiliad Mural itself. If they were bound by a NDA, then they would not be able to confirm or deny anything related to their NDA, which could explain why they never respond to Mural-related questions. On the other hand, without an NDA, they could theoretically respond to these questions, even if it's just to say "I don't know anything about the mural, that wasn't part of the prize."

On the other hand, they might ignore mystery-related questions because they think we're nuts. Just saying. :D
