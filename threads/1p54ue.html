Full Moon Party Tonight!

▲ 8 ptsu/MudnartOct 24, 201312 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Moon-Phase Condition Testing

During the day, before a full moon night, a hippie at the alien camp can be overheard saying there’s a “full moon party tonight.” With the NPC recognizing the changing moon phase, and the UFO high above, it seems possible that something could happen here. From this location, the full moon moves directly over Mt. Chiliad at 3am (when the Holo-UFO can appear on Mt. Chiliad). I’ve stood on the red ufo glyph, the indented circle near the alien faces, and the platform above the hippie camp (watch out for cougars!) at this time. I have tried all weather patterns. I have found nothing. I even found that welcome banner one of the space ship parts was found near hoping the “full moon party” was in a different location. Nothing.

Before I save over Franklin loitering at the hippie camp at 2am during a full moon, does anyone have any further suggestions for this particular time and place?
Oh, I shot at the moon too. Nothing happened.
