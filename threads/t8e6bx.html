I'm not yet one of the big fans of transposing the mural over the map but the idea always intrigued me but it seemed like a big hassle. This solution that I just coded is lacking in many ways BUT at least it opens the possibility up to more people. Mostly, the max resolution sucks (480x360) so all maps dropped in need to be drastically crappified first. I still think it beats nothing?

It currently contains a small handful of maps I found or made. Want to see a specific part of the map? Get me a 480x360 image and I'll add it. Or you can let me know the area you want to explore somehow (and hope for the best)

What is Scratch?

Scratch Project Controls

click the green flag to start the project / click the X shaped arrows to the flags right to full screen

OVERLAY CONTROLS-

click to place

space - changes rotation center

A/D or arrows - rotate (hold 1 or 2 for finer rotation)

p - opens dialogue box to enter angle numerically

mouse wheel - zooms (hold 1 or 2 for finer rotation)

wheel + c - adjusts color

wheel + t - adjusts transparency

wheel + b - adjusts brightness

MAP CONTROLS-

m - cycles maps

UHJK scrolls map

n + mouse wheel - zooms map

n + c - centers and scales to 100%

Details that I don't think you'd likely notice at a glance. Also, I was also playing GTA 1 online and wondered if we might need a map from a different version? Adding maps/images to any Scratch project is pretty easy. If you want your own copy of this project to play with I can show you how to get one and you can stick any map you like in. Or if you'd prefer to do it without my help, you can..

  1. Start your own Scratch account (always free), open my project and click Remix. Then you will have your own private copy on your account to play with.
  2. You can Download MIT's offline Scratch app, See Inside my project and click File/Save to your computer. Then you'll have your own copy on your PC, just run your Scratch app and use it to open the project.

Scratch will auto-resize added images to fit if they are too big. If you need a free, simple app for cropping/ simple painting on/ resizing your own map, etc images I recommend Irfanview. It also has a great feature (Image/color corrections) that lets you tweak sliders to adjust an image's color, contrast, RGB balance, Gamma, etc, all while comparing the live results to the original image. A great tool for researching any mystery involving image manipulation.
