So, now to the theory:

My theory is that we need to get five stars on top of the Penris building to trigger our sixth star.

You may think this is crazy, but I have some reasoning behind this theory. First off, there is a Spaceship Part located on the roof of the Penris building. Why else would there be one up here than to point us to this roof? We have to use a chopper to get here, and we wouldn't even bother coming up here for anything else than this collectible. Secondly, there's the shoot for the stars message. It's by a telescope at the observatory that points to the Penris building, and there's a sniper next to it. In order to get the five stars on top of the Penris building, we'd need a sniper for everything pretty much. We'd need to snipe pedestrians down below to get the first-third star, then we'd need the sniper to shoot out the pilots of the incoming police choppers. I'm not saying in order for this to work, you need to use ONLY a sniper. I'm just stating why I think the sniper at the observatory is hinting towards this hypothesis of mine.
