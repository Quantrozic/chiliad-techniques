Hope all is well in this great community. This is the first time I’m posting here, so I hope to at least provoke some further exploration into the Easter eggs in GTA V.

We all know that in the green mural, BIGFOOT27 can be spelled out, which makes sense because the Easter egg has already been solved. I’d like to think it’s Franklins Easter egg going off of the colors of the mural, and with that I matched the red with Trevor and Purple with Michael. It’s the purple one that really interests me, as most of it seems involves psychology, spirituality, balance, wisdom and honor.

Furthermore, in the purple mural, the phrenology model has sections labeled with the letters A-J. Could it be possible that matching these labels with a real model of phrenology could spell out something or give us a clue? I attempted to do so, but I couldn’t find a clear model. There is a phrenology model in Dr. Friedlanders office if I’m not mistaken. Maybe somebody could match it to that if they do some editing or dig into the game files.

It’s been said that they will be expanding aspects of the story mode for the ps5, so maybe they will give more. Can’t wait for that, but for right now let’s keep seeing what we can solve. Happy Hunting!
