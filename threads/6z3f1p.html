  • The Pirate Ship lines up almost perfectly with the big Cave, Goonies Confirmed.
  • If you put Big Foot in it's Peyote Location the Sea Serpent's head is roughly over the Oriental Theater.
  • Putting the Treasure at the Altruist Camp lets you get a path that lands almost EXACTLY at the edge of the map in a cove.
  • Putting the Skull and Crossbones Camp at the Hippie Camp the Sasquatch is in the Quarry.
  • Putting the Skull and Cross Bones over the Military Base puts the Pirate Ship over the Land Act Reservoir
