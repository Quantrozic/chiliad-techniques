Moon phase testing was so tedious I think I've burned myself right the hell out. I haven't tested any of the lightning stuff yet, but I think it's too interesting not to. I think I've tested just about everything else that I've wanted to, and all the ideas I got in messages, so I don't know where else to go from here besides the lightning stuff. Anyway, here's what I did with these couple of things.

Tides

I went to a low lying crack in the shoreline near the lighthouse figuring it would be a good spot to see changes in the water level. I got there, and the tiny inlet thing was pretty full looking, but I let it sit a while and made myself something to eat. When I sat back down, I was waist-high in water. Then, at about 4am (like 20 mins later, real time) the water had sunk back down. By 6am there was barely any water there at all, and the water came back in sometime in late evening. I was ready to put tides into the "confirmed" pile, but I decided to wait.

The "tide" never went back out. I reloaded the save to see if I could replicate what I saw originally, but I couldn't. Water level does go down sometimes, but it's definitely not on any timer or anything like that. It's actually pretty rare that the water level gets really low, you could go game-days without it happening. Weather seems to play a part in making the water swell up from beneath the ground architecture, but I couldn't find a way to manipulate the water level to shrink down low. Maybe it's super calm seas that does it, I'm not sure. I know for sure that it's definitely not a tide leaving, though.

Moon Phase Stuff

I did a few things with a full moon, but I created yet another file with a new moon, and tried those things out again. Everything I've done here was done under both new and full moons with no obvious results or changes seen:

  • Drove back up and down the street that one person claimed to have seen an NPC being abducted. Started at 11pm, and ended at like 4:30am. Did 5 cycles of this.
  • Did a simple check to see if even something small would change between the two phases on the UFOs. Also checked to see if something would change at Chiliad platform, hippy camp, or Zancudo bunker.
  • Took all three characters to the three separate UFO spots at 3am. Then cycled it so that all combinations of character and UFO spot were covered. This was mostly to check the bunker elevator, I don't know if you can even switch back to other characters from the bunker without cheating. I've parachute-landed right on the steps down to the bunker with no stars, and I always got them once I was inside. I did cheat the stars down, though, and checked the other spots.
  • Took all 3 characters to all 3 UFO spots.
  • Checked the gas station sign with the glitch/symbol on it, the lighthouse, the observatory, the altruist camp, and the alien graffiti water tanks.
  • Lit all three characters on fire at Altruist camp cave, and sacrificed all 3 on the rock then checked Chiliad and the Zancudo bunker.
  • Took the SD to just about all the places I've listed, then accidentally deleted the SD save I had while meaning to delete another.

Then I just got frustrated, threw cheats on, and shot up Zancudo with extreme prejudice. Had to switch back and forth between my 60 gig and slim PS3s because my 60 gig main one kept overheating, and I've already had to reapply thermal paste in the damn thing twice.

I'm not sure what more I even want to do with moon phases. I'm open to ideas, but I'm probably just going to move onto lightning stuff. Between searching the shit out of the seafloor, and fucking with moon phases, I think I'm pretty burnt on the tedious stuff.
