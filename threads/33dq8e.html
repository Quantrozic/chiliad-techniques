Followed the path to the sun

▲ 10 ptsu/head_bussinApr 21, 20159 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Moon-Phase Condition TestingStatistical Power Analysis for Spawn Experiments ?

i'm sure this has been tested extensively but, a few months back i decided i would follow the only path of light i could find in the game, and followed the path of light the sun makes on the water as it was rising.

i started at the light house and followed the reflection off of the water, hoping it would expose a secret island or something, but no luck.

although i did this particular experiment online, i did notice something interesting. i had seemed to make my way a lot further out than usual.

what surprised me was the size of mt chiliad compared to the rest of the map, and how far i had seem to make it out over the ocean.

i would like to do further testing. if anyone has any particular times they would like me to test let me know.

when the sun is setting or rising over the horizion it looks like the eye. i feel the sun is an important step to the mystery that hasn't been tested enough. not sure i'll ever be able to %100 my file because this game is a huge glitchfest on xbox one.

if someone with a 100% wants to test it, i would recommend starting at the lighthouse.
