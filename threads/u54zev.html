The Fort Zancudo Redux: A Retrospective Look At This Area and it's Mysterious Lore.

Disclaimer & Background

Let me start with a disclaimer: I'm not too bothered about what we've found in the game files.

Of course, there's always going to be the notion of cut content and unused assets during our discussion, but my purpose for this little investigation isn't to simply state the obvious.

Instead, what I'm interested in is the logic behind the design of what we actually can see and interact with.

For the sake of context, I've been looking into this mystery since the beginning, so I hope I've got enough experience and I hope that you don't dismiss my observations as being amateur. Now, let's get into it!

Part 1: The Bunker

Why, lads, is there a power outlet on the inner wall of the fort zancudo bunker, next to the elevator?

What purpose does it serve? Or, said another way, what purpose would it serve in real life?

Last night I was revisiting an old classic; the guy who walks into the bunker and says "malfunction", something which I've been able to replicate by entering the bunker without a wanted level and making enough noise to draw his attention.

I've noticed that if I throw a smoke grenade while standing on the bunker, I can gain a one star wanted level and be arrested on the spot. It's possible to also do this in the elevator room itself.

The area seems to give the player various options to trigger a wanted level. Step out of the boundary and you will get the 4 star wanted level. Feel like shooting up the red gas cylinder? There's a 2 or 3 star level.

It seems as though we can initiate a sequence of events, all while standing in the green light.

I've played around with this, though I must admit that I haven't been skilled enough to get very far. During the lightshow, while standing in the very middle, I've thrown the smoke grenade, triggering the 1 star. This causes one trooper to appear by the gas cylinder, which I then shoot to cause an explosion.

After that, vehicles start appearing from the side of the runway, and after taking out a few of them I haven't got much further.

Another curiosity: one can also walk around on the grassy areas around the bunker.

And didn't someone confirm that the green lights won't show after visiting the fort zancudo ufo?

Part 2: The Surrounding Base & Tower (misc).

I don't know how the control tower fares on current gen or pc, as I've been playing on the xbox 360.

One thing that I remember, though, is how glitchy it can be. I've watched a fair few videos over the years of textures not loading, people getting stuck in walls, trooper NPC spawning being unpredictable.

Is it still the same?

If you really want the mini gun, go to the top of the tower. Right. But is it worth it? Perhaps I'm a little spoiled now by years of playing the game, because I'm sure it was a lot of fun the first time around.

Perhaps this whole base is an after thought. The idea of storming the base never really seemed worth it. Sure, we all tried, but I can't help and wonder if rockstar dropped the ball somewhere.

Combine the strength of the army with some long reload times and you've got yourself a hassle. Sure, you may technically be able to steal a jet but it's not really worth it.

However, the existence of an area like the bunker is a way in. Though why bother if the only reason is to stand by an elevator, transmitter dish or spinning green light that only appears between 3 and 4?

To bring a final observation to the discussion, I've never seen it mentioned that landing pads on the runway are outlined in black and have an eerie resemblance with the 3 larger boxes on the mural when viewed from above. Particularly the color of the lines against the greyish background, though it's clear to me that an overlay does not quite fit, even if we were to represent the eye with the spinning green sun.

I now want to open the floor to any anecdotal stories or fun facts about fort zancudo, the bunker, and the ufo.

Keep hunting and shoot for the stars.
