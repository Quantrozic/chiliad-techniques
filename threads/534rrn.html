Radio not Lightning

▲ 44 ptsu/BigKongIndustriesSep 16, 201620 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Morse Code Analysis ?

I'm a rail signal engineer from Scotland and this week I have been working on upgrading a signalling system which works by radio , its called Radio Electronic Token Block and afaik is only used in Scotland. The diagrams we had for the plans showed the same "lightning" symbols that are on the mural but as the symbol for radiowaves going between cell towers. The inverted radio wave on the drawing I had was from the main transmission tower receiving a radio signal from the last cell tower in the chain , all other symbols were for transmitting.

I know that this has been mentioned before I just thought I would add what I know from an engineering perspective,I believe 100% the symbols are radio and not lightning. I believe that cell/radio towers are key or at least play a part in the mystery. As I'm away from home I can't investigate more right now , but my theory is that we would have to activate a series of cell towers somehow possibly with the space docker. As we know this may have to get done on a certain day at a certain time in a certain order. Do we know if the radio towers with red lights ever change over time or anything?

The RETB system works by only allowing train into a section of single line at a time. I'm not sure how much detail went into the design of the railway on gta as some equipment that is in game that I have seen makes sense to have and others doesn't. I believe there is still some sort of mystery or possibly just unfinished/deleted content to the railway , whether or not this has anything to do with the chiliad mystery I don't know.

I think more effort and searching has to be done in relation to radio , whether its selecting a radio channel at a certain place or honking the sd horn with epsilon robes on... maybe its just confirming that its Mt Chiliad thats on the mural as its the highest point in the game and would make sense to have it broadcasting/boosting radio signals.
