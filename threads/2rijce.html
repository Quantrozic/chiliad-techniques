The intriguing Mt. Chiliad mural.

▲ 4 ptsu/LamalarsJan 6, 201515 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Mural & Map Mirroring ?

The mural is a very interesting and intriguing thing. But understanding it, is a completely different story. I do not easily jump to conclusions about it's meaning. The only thing i know is what i see. And it raises a couple of questions. In my opinion its important to know these answers before even jumping into all kinds of theories. I know its fun hunting Easter eggs, but we need the right tools, in this case knowledge of the meaning of the mural. I'm not saying that all the theories you've come up with are wrong. But we can go on and trying stuff out and come up with all kinds of theories that leeds to more questions. In my opinion we first need to understand the truth about the mural.

So what do we see ?

It is shaped like a mountain, but the lower left side is open.
Question: Why is this open and is it telling us it is not a mountain on the mural,but just a line wrapped around the the x's and the 3 boxes?

There are 3 images in the boxes( UFO, cracked egg, jet-pack stick man), 5 X's, 7 dashes on the UFO/ eye on top
Question: Is there a pattern (3-5-7) or is this pure coincidence?

There are 2 colors used in the mural, red and black.
Question: Why are all the things i mentioned in point 2 colored red , and the rest in black?
Do we need to rearrange the red colored things in the mural ? And is it telleing us only to use the red ones ?

There are 10 lightning bolts on the mural, but one is mirrored incorrectly. If You let the wrong mirrored lightning bolt out then there are 9 lightning bolts, fits in the 3-5-7 pattern ( 3-5-7-9 )
Question: Is this piece of the mural telling us it needs to be mirrored or it needs to be rearranged? And if there is a pattern (3-5-7-9 ) why is the 9 (bolts) colored black ?

We need to understand the truth about this mural in order to use its knowledge.
We need to take small steps at the time, and once we know how to read it, we'll be able to use it.
