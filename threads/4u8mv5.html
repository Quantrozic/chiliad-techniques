Just Noticed This Sly Foreshadowing From FlyLoFM - Flying Lotus & MF Doom - Masquatch

▲ 44 ptsu/the_stoned_apeJul 23, 201633 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Narrative Foreshadowing Audit

Was listening to FlyLo when I heard a song I have heard countless times before, but never really listened to. The song I am referencing is Flying Lotus & MF Doom - Masquatch and was released specifically for GTA V. It ends with the line 'While You at it get the Masquatch' I found this interesting in relation to all the Sasquatch shit that happened over the past year and thought it was a nice bit of foreshadowing. Although I think it specifically relates to the Sasquatch Mission where you find out that Sasquatch is in fact a Man aka Masquatch.

Here are some other interesting songs that were specifically released for GTA V.

Health - High Pressure Dave - This song was based on the song 'High Pressure Days' but they intentionally changed Days to Dave, perhaps hinting at Dave Norton?

Wavves - Nine is God - Many people have theorized on this one, thinking it relates to the Infinite 8 Mystery

OFF! - Whats Next - This particular song includes the line 'Plan B Doesn't Exit' which many have thought could be a hint to not Kill Michael which is Option B in the final mission.

Nite Jewel - Nowhere to Go - Has the line 'Hallucinatory power....Desert flower. A Cactus. ' I.E. Peyote.

I only looked at songs that were made for the GTA V Official Soundtrack, I could have made some vague connections with other songs on the soundtrack, but I only wanted to include the most blatant. Let me know if you catch any other connections.
