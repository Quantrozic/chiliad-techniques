Coded Message in Zancudo UFO Sounds?

▲ 24 ptsu/superpancakeOct 19, 201317 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Audio Spectrogram Reverse-Search

Hey all. I thought I'd try to figure this out before posting it here, but I think I've hit a wall with it and need others' advice. I found the sounds the Zancudo UFO makes a bit strange and repeating, so I sat on top of it for around 8-9 minutes just recording the sound, no music or sfx clogging it up, direct line in. I then put the sound into Ableton Live and put the sound clip into Izotope Iris to get a spectrogram of the sound.

What I got was fairly interesting. I believe there is a coded message in the sound pattern the UFO makes. It plays 2 low blaring G notes and then a high pitched note afterward, which then repeats with different high pitched frequency every time. It comes out to what looks like something hidden in the audio. Here's a few pictures from the spectrogram:

The other thing to note: I thought I would find a repeating pattern with the sound, where the audio loops. But after recording 9 minutes of the UFO sounds, I can't seem to find where the pattern starts and loops. If there is a message in there, it's either really long, or dynamic.

Anybody have any insights on how to decode a possible message in the UFO sounds?
