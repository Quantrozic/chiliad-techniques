I'm not sure if it's been said before, but perhaps the Mural isn't a Map, or Cipher for this mystery, but is just meant to represent the Mystery, like another Glyph.

What I mean is, what if it's basically saying "You have found an Easter egg (Kind've the same motif as the "There are no easter eggs up here, go away" signs), congratulations. But there is more to bee seen." Each of the lines down it, (Independent of the center line), represent... basically this sub-reddit, and hunters, searching, and digging. The X's, and Sudden Stops representing debunking theories, and leaving Loose-Ends.

Now mind you, I still believe that the Three Boxes represent three distinct rewards. The Easter eggs representing the base of the myth (The three UFO's), The UFO is perhaps a "flyable" UFO (More on why I quoted flyable later.), and lastly a Jet Pack.

Bonus information:

The Number 7: Why are there 7 lines, (5 on top, and 2 on bottom) of the eye at the top of the Mural? They seem to be of a similar style to the three lines underneath the 3 o' clock UFO glyph. Also, please take notice that GTA:V (At least by my count) is the 7th large Title in the GTA series. GTA, GTA 2, GTA 3, GTA:VC, GTA:SA, GTA 4, GTA V.
