The Kortz Center and other places that aren't explored nearly enough

▲ 14 ptsu/bblazer24Feb 28, 201517 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Phase-Inversion Center Channel Extraction ?

so last night, I was just dicking around in single player and decided I would try and find that mansion with the creepy giant chess board and check that out again (I made another post about getting sidetracked at the playboy mansion and hearing some interesting dialogue).

while exploring the area, I saw the Kortz Center off in the distance and realized that I never really looked into it much. I think that place definitely needs to be explored more.

some interesting things I found there:

  • Exhibits about the pharaohs (complete with an Egyptian eye icon), Russian literature, the gangs of liberty city (indicating that IV has some relevance to this?), and a Chinese dynasty
  • numerous egg shaped sculptures around the place, and another that sorta resembles a bong maybe
  • a massive sculpture that kinda reminded me of the Fort Zancudo UFO
  • huge satellite dishes and walkways on the roof
  • has a sorta interesting layout
  • lots of restricted areas
  • the hedge maze in the back (connection with maze bank? maybe an overlay for something? can this maze be solved?)
  • loads of suspicious security cameras
  • a mission that took place here involved various government agencies (including a character from IV if I remember correctly)

in addition the Kortz Center, what other places do you guys think should be looked into?

EDIT: the Richman area with all the mansions is worth exploring also

that chess board always freaked me out, and maybe there's a possible connection to epsilon since rich people live in the area

even if a place isn't potentially mysterious and important, everybody should explore more because there is a lot of really cool detail in this game
