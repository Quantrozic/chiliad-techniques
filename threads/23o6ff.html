Strange radio message

▲ 17 ptsu/ShadowPuppet1Apr 22, 201429 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Radio-Broadcast Subtext Concordance

I recently started a new game to see if I could anything I missed the first time. Last night, I reached the point where the altruists try to kill Trevor.

I died immediately, and when I respawned, there was a random, torrential downpour. We all know about this.

What I did not previously know about, however, was the radio broadcast. After killing the altruists and stealing their money, I went off-roading in the storm in the mountains to the east of the altruist camp. My radio was off and there was no one around, but I suddenly heard what sounds like a guy on a handheld radio saying "Ok, he got it." Or "ok, we got it." I wasn't expecting to hear anything, so it caught me off guard so I wasn't actively listening.

And then, right after that broadcast, the torrential downpour immediately stopped.

This may already be known, of course, but if it's not then I think it's a clear indication that someone (FIB?) is directly controlling the weather. Of course, we all know it has to rain at 3 am to cue part of the mystery, so I wonder if there is some kind of link there?

Also, the only other time I can remember a mission cueing specific weather is when Trevor drives to kill the Lost bikers before heading to LS to find Michael.

It's a ridiculous long shot, but it might be worth checking out that biker camp again?
