Right so, ive been coming up with some what of a theory over the past week, it might be a little long winded but just bare with me. It started with when I a heard about the UFO that shines a light directly onto the bunker in Fort Zancudo and I realized that the song Rockstar used after Michaels abduction "Shine a light" cant just be a coincidence. I realize the song wasn't just made for the game but its put there for a reason. Its possibly not a clue but im not going to disregard it, the song is remixed by 'Flight Facilities' now put 2 and 2 together, when thinking about something shining a light onto perhaps a secret flight facility, this is not just all coincidence but getting to my point, have a look at the picture at this link that i posted Imgur it is 4 things I took notice of during the game and read from websites, the 1st one is a UFO 'shining a light' above a moon, now im sure people have seen this but i interpret it as perhaps at a specific stage of the lunar cycle (the one depicted in the picture) you can perhaps get into this 'flight facility' in the bunker at the Fort or perhaps you would be able to be abducted some how (don't judge me im just throwing ideas out there) but ofcourse after 100% completion and usually around 3am, applying to both theory's. Reasoning behind this is if you notice the rest of the pictures in the link there is a symbol of a UFO with a beam coming out the bottom of it, on the side of Mt Chiliad, then there is the words 'Embrace the light' written on some of the walls in the Altruist village which brings me back to the beam of light out of the UFO's.

So my main 2 points are these

  1. at a certain point of the lunar cycle at 3am after 100% completion, the beam of light ontop of the bunker could perhaps assist you on getting in to what i call the 'secret flight facility' hence there being a jetpack in it

Let me know what you guys think
