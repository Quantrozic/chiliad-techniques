Ok, so I was replaying a few missions just to try out the alternate paths I didn't originally and also make a few movies :D. On the Merryweather Heist submarine mission with Trevor, I went far off\-shore and as deep as I could possibly go and started recording, with the aim of making a spooky "void" video.

This was the result \(EPILEPSY WARNING \- RAPID FLASHING FOR THE WHOLE VIDEO!\) Now, there are obviously filters and edited camera angles in that video, but the crazy choppy visual effects and the constant "flashing" of the sub were there with the standard game camera, and showed no matter what angle or filter you chose. Also, whenever you pointed the camera at Trevor these flat, untextured "layers" would cut through his body, as you can see at 0:42 in the video. The weird thing is, none of this was visible in game as I was recording it, everything seemed totally normal.

Sadly I deleted the original footage to make room for more, but I'm thinking about recreating it. I doubt it means anything, but it's bloody strange. Also it probably has nothing to do with the Chiliad mystery but I didn't really know where else to post it :P

Another thing I noticed is that during this mission, while you will receive the extreme low pressure alert constantly at this depth, you don't actually ever die. It could just be coded for this mission, I don't know.

EDIT: Also the constant sound of explosions you can hear through the video were not added by me, and I definitely couldn't hear it while I was recording in\-game. So weird! Could it be the sound of the submarine trying to implode but not being able to do so because of some script in the mission? :S
