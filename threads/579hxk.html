Thinking of Easter eggs in other games, I think to one that I really used to enjoy doing and many of the mechanics translate to GTA as well. COD Zombies is a multiplayer wave-based survival game. Each of four players take control of a unique protagonist as they kill Zombies to collect points that unlock weapons and new sections of the map. On the maps are usually power terminals that must be activated in order to turn on power-up machines offering both character boosts and weapon boosts. The process to unlock the weapon boosting machine is sometimes quite complex and usually provides clue to the story as to why the Zombies are there and why the characters are fighting.

These mechanics, finding objects and activating them, traveling to different corners of the map and finding symbols to use in another combination lock, shooting specific things in certain orders and others are ways that other 'Easter eggs' are hidden. In the latest 'Shadows of Evil' map, the symbols rotate on a per game basis, so if you all die you have to start again and find a different set of symbols. To unlock certain songs and hidden pieces of the story, you can activate certain objects to 'pick them up' and when you have them all, a completed piece can be found somewhere else that you can activate to play the music. After a lot of play and file hunting themselves, there turns out to be an even bigger hidden Easter egg in each level, in SoE it unlocked a cutscene. Now, players think that there's an even bigger cutscenes that takes into account play across all of the different maps. After several games worth of Zombies, there is a rich and deep backstory with alternate timelines, pocket universes and mysterious alien artifacts. There's an entire community of YouTubers trying to put all these pieces together into a coherent story, some of them last two hours or more. It's incredible and closely mirrors some of the best parts of this community.

But those mechanics lead me to things in GTA that I've noticed to be very similar, but primarily it comes down to where we can use the Knteraction button (right on the D-Pad IIRC, the Horn button). There's many places where a hint appears to indicate that you should use it, and in the Bigfoot Easter Egg it was the primary but hidden mechanic used to find the path to the Airplane Graveyard. Many speculate that the horn on the Space Docker might do something similar, but thats yet to produce any results. You can use the Interaction button to use computers (like the ones in the FIB building, which surely must have access to at least something different) and talk to pedestrians, in the Prologue, Trevor uses it to open a garage door before running into the police. Its also used to activate telescopes. It's clearly very useful and there are many open uses for it but many hidden ones, too.

Is there a way to extract a list of all the interactable objects in the game? Perhaps they all have scripts attached to them or there might be a master list like handholds.xml? Are there any areas where there might be something to use the Interact button on where there have been some results or could be?

In addition, Rockstar showed with the Bigfoot mystery that they incorporate the Interact button and many other mechanics like timing and symbols and story being found through the mystery hunt. Perhaps rather than the smaller scale theories we need to be thinking bigger and in a multiplayer sort of way but with Franklin, Michael and Trevor. Electricity plays a big part in Zombies as it does in GTA, and Online EMPs can be used to knock out the power grid. R* built GTA SP with all those mechanics and have been able to extend and add to them online, but they are still likely able to be used, at least in a rough form, in SP. Each character, as in the later Zombies puzzles, might have their own activatable objects (all three have unique vehicle rewards (sub, hot rod atv and Space Docker and the the Epsilon vehicles and Trevor's unique helicopters) and unique puzzles to solve simultaneously during a certain time.

Thanks for reading. In addition, here's a few things I've been bothered by over the years:

  1. The phone number: 328-555-0174. It shows up in my games often enough that it's become a bit of an oddity. There's been discussion of it, but I think it's been unlocked too early for some and that there are other triggers needed to 'activate' it. Sort of how you can get to the mineshaft but don't get the filters until you actually solve the mystery.
  2. In the files there's one that starts dt1_tc_ce that is a simple box. It's contained inside the same files as the Zancudo UFO (IIRC) and it's coordinates are out to sea somewhere to the west. With all the talk of the Altruist camp and Praising the Sun and the radio station that's slogan is "Flying into the sunset", I think it's worth looking at again. It might be simply the reentry point for Trevor after leaving Yankton and Michael with the Chinese, but maybe not.
  3. The tracts. I had the Epsilon tracts open while looking through OpenIV yesterday and noticed that the Chapter and Verse markings seemed to correlate with the naming of a lot of the files (ch3_xxx and so on). Perhaps the Tracts (from the religion claiming everything to be a simulation, pretty much) might be clues for the file hunters to use for looking through them. I've been thinking for a while that if there is anything hidden in the files, it's scattered across all of them so deeply that you'd need a map to start hunting. Perhaps the tracts are that map.
  4. PENRIS. At night, from the Observatory through the sniper rifle pickup telescope or the rifle, if you look towards the PENRIS building you'll see two blue dots highlighting a star and a point in the N. If you fly towards them they remain visible until you get to City Hall. Facing PENRIS from behind City Hall you can see them until you pass over. Just like the Chiliad UFO. Since being passed off as a lighting glitch, it's been pretty much uninvestigated, but an interview I've read recently changed my mind(here) where he says that every light you can see in GTA's new engine has a bulb that you can fly out and find. Well, not those two lights. And not the one on top of City Hall either.
  5. If the Chiliad UFO is a hologram (projected light) what bulb casts the light (see #4)? The Beacon Theater looks a likely spot as I think it's the brightest light on the map.
  6. From the steps leading up to the Altruist radio tower you can shoot at two electrical hookups and they catch fire after you do. I'm almost positive I saw the light go out on top of that radio tower after that. Maybe "gush gush gusher" involves pumping electricity into it three times, or there might be three 'interactable' radio towers when done in a certain order unlock something else. I'm damn sure electricity plays a huge part somewhere, like the plant in downtown.

Thanks again for reading, I hope someone can help me out with that list!
