Cement Factory dog scratching at trailer door.

▲ 19 ptsu/TeeniestbeansFeb 10, 202623 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Trailer Metadata and Encode Forensics

https://imgur.com/a/OYvUBL2

There is a cement factory located in Blaine county that has a security guard and patrol dog that wander around at night. I was in the area investigating the two trees I found across the street at the loading docks as they are the exact same model of tree that’s found at the alien camp just without the paint which to me feels very odd.

I kept hearing barking off in the distance and decided to go check it out. After sneaking in an climbing to the top of one of the silos/cement process towers I saw this dog stuck in an animation loop of barking and scratching at the door of the job site trailer. Too see if it was a bug I got the dogs attention and had it chase me around for a few minutes but once I got far enough away from the trailer it would immediately go back and start scratching at the door again.

I was playing as Michael in director mode with the time set at 1:01 am during a full moon.
