How I Got To This Theory:

I don't post a lot on reddit or the internet at all about the Mount Chiliad mystery, but I do read a lot on this sub-reddit and anywhere else I can find info.
I also have never had a 100% play through, however that is irrelevant when discussing my theory and I will explain why. Based on all of the information gathered by the community since the game was released, there is enough liable information to go by when conducting my own hunting test and theories.

Lately I've been reading a lot on the "SEGREGATE AND REARRANGE" topics as I believe that clue is key to solving the mystery. Most people brush it off as simply, an anagram for "EASTER EGG" but I believe it's more than that. If we brushed things off that easily then we would have almost nothing, as far as reliable information to go by. So I have spent the last few days trying to rearrange all of the underlined words scattered on the artwork throughout Los Santos. I will be honest, you can come up with probably several different phrases, however many of them don't really make a lot of sense. I tried to determine which words could go together and possibly form a cohesive sentence. This is what I came up with.

"FACT, FRUIT TREE LIES. KARMA IS BROKEN. YOU SUE (Seek/Request) FACT. SECEDE YOURSELF, YES THIS IS MANDITORY. DREAM MURRY(HAPPY, MERRY).
DEFEAT LOS SANTOS IMPOSSIBLE DREAM. EVER DEFEAT US, THE WORLD REBORN".

The issue that I initially had was where does "Sue Murry" fit into all of this, so I decided to look and see if "Sue" and "Murry" had any meanings, other than someone’s name. What I found was that "Sue" could be used to mean to "Seek" or "Request" (obviously other than to "sue" someone). I also found that "Murry" could be used to mean "Merry" typically in a British dialect. Using these definitions in place of "Sue" and "Murry" makes the sentence begin to make sense.

Sue:

  1. To seek justice or right from. (http://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/sue)
  2. To make a request or application. (http://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/sue)
  3. Appeal formally to a person for something. (GOOGLE)

Murry:

  1. Dialectal British variant of merry. (http://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/murry)
  2. Merry Meaning: cheerful and lively. (GOOGLE)
  3. Very happy and cheerful, feeling or showing joy and happiness, causing joy and happiness. (http://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/merry)

Once I had figured out my solution to Segregate and Rearrange, that is when I began trying to figure out what it could possibly mean. Then my brain kind of exploded with ideas. I know that everyone has their own opinion on these topics but I will list them anyways.

  1. The mysterious green alien egg, clearly this is the next step right? Even though the map is enormous, at the end of the day it can only be in so many places. After thinking about everything that I have ever read on this subreddit I came to the conclusion that it can only be Hidden Underground, Underwater, A secret cave on one of the several mountains, one of the many mysterious locations around the map (you know which ones), or possibly some of the location based anagrams associated with Segregate and Rearrange. However, Maybe it is hidden somewhere in Los Santos, but that would need a miracle to find, so I believe that it generates somewhere outside of the city.
  2. Then I thought to myself how much of the sky has been explored, like way up in the air. How much of the ocean has been explored WITH NO MODS OR CHEATS. How many of the tallest buildings in Los Santos have been explored. I mean the tallest buildings that you can only access via a helicopter or plane. Has anyone tried getting the space docker up to the correct mileage at Trevor's Airstrip? Have any of you 100% guys revisited all of the space ship parts locations after achieving 100%. Then I continued to think as a lot of us have, is it a DLC or has it been there since day 1.
  3. Then I thought that there is absolutely no way that there is a secret hidden city on the bottom of the ocean floor, or a massive earthquake with lava all over the place, no traveling in time and no zombie/alien themed apocalyptic missions. We have to consider the technical limitations of last gen hardware. We also have to consider whether or not the mystery is exactly the same on current gen and last gen. Being logical and using a realistic point of view, there is no way that Rockstar could fit that much content onto last gen systems. The game alone pushed last gen to its limits, just like San Andreas did in 2004. If there is a hidden city underwater/underground then it would have to be very small. If there is extra content then it can't possibly be a whole other game entirely, that's just not possible or even a remotely reasonable assumption.
  4. Then I thought about Fort Zancudo, and guys I don't think there is anything to be found here (at least not yet). I have explored this place up and down (using Cletus Second Hunting Mission, which isn't a perfect method but it's the most efficient) and unless a door unlocks somewhere or my theory ends up being correct then there is absolutely nothing to find here. I mean think about it Rockstar said they didn't think we would find all Easter eggs; obviously the jet-pack is what everyone wants. They know that we played San Andreas and that the first place we would look is Fort Zancudo, seems like a perfect distraction to me. Why put the Jet-Pack in the place where everyone expects it to be, they even have the Zancudo UFO Beam and a very aggressive CPU intelligence. I think it's all there as a distraction, and it may have worked for a year and a half, but if my theory is correct maybe something could eventually spawn at Zancudo. As of right now I don't believe there is anything there.
  5. Then it all came together, read this sentence one more time.

"FACT, FRUIT TREE LIES. KARMA IS BROKEN. YOU SUE (Seek/Request) FACT. SECEDE YOURSELF, YES THIS IS MANDATORY. DREAM MURRY (HAPPY/MERRY).
DEFEAT LOS SANTOS IMPOSSIBLE DREAM. EVER DEFEAT US THE WORLD REBORN.

What could it possibly mean, and then it all hit me at once. We've been overcomplicating things way too much the last year and a half.
I believe it's a lot simpler than we all previously thought.

WHAT DOES "THE WORLD REBORN" mean? After we solve the mystery what could possibly be triggered in the in-game world. Immediately I thought about snow, we never see snow at all at any point in time other than using cheats and the Ludendorff missions. It doesn't snow much in California, however it is possible. Maybe "THE WORLD REBORN" means that we can trigger snow in Los Santos without using cheats, (I'm trying to use realism here and keep the technical aspects of the game into perspective). If this happens maybe the snow could possibly trigger something else in the game world. Maybe there is no snow for following the right steps, but maybe something else is triggered by following my theory. (Once again I apologize to those who have completed a play through like this).

The "LEGENDARY HUNTER" THEORY:

My theory is very simple but it seems to make sense to me. Basically the concept behind it is to complete absolutely every single side quest as early in the story as possible. What I mean by side quest are every strangers and freaks, all collectibles (space ship parts, letter scraps, wild life challenge, races, altruist camp missions, epsilon mission, Maude missions, etc. COMPLETE ALL OF THE SIDE QUEST AS EARLY IN THE STORY AS POSSIBLE. I know everything isn't unlocked when you start the game, but as an example: Once you meet Omega go find all space ships parts and return them before you go on your next story mission.
As soon as you unlock a mission type, or meet a certain character and they give you a mission, certain event, or quest. Finish their mission completely before you go on your next story mission. Like I said, basically just complete as much as you can as early as humanly possible. I feel as though using this method the Mural starts to make a lot more sense, and I will explain that in a minute.

Think about it Rockstar said that they didn't think we would find all of the unlockables/easter eggs in the game. They also knew that as soon as we saw
"Come Back When Your Story Is Complete" that we would immediately rush to 100% without grinding the game out and taking our time to find all collectibles and completing all side missions first. I believe that the closer you get to 100% (Early in the story as possible/allowed), that possibly these actions could unlock alternate cut scenes, alternate options/decisions throughout story missions, or could possibly trigger something somewhere along the lines.
I also think that Simeon Must Die is important, but that's a whole other discussion.

THE MURAL ITSELF

Now my breakdown on what I believe the mural represents. First of all I believe that the X's on the mural are meant to represent the "MAJOR" Heist in the story mode.

  1. The Jewel Store Job
  2. The Merryweather Heist
  3. The Paleto Score
  4. The Bureau Raid
  5. The Big Score

Source: (http://www.gta5cheats.com/heists-guide/)

I also believe that the locations of the red X's on the Mural is irrelevant, no matter which direction that you take they all lead to the same location. I believe that the lines and puzzles in between the X's are meant to represent the players choice in exactly when to partake in these heist, and that all of the extra stuff like where the lines lead is just there to confuse you. If you look at the first two X's, they are duplicates. In my opinion I believe that this is the first point in time where the tangents that decide whether you can possibly trigger something or not began to separate. I think that the left side of the Mural Represents the summary of all of the time and effort spent researching, testing, and trying to decipher the mystery basically the last year and a half. Following this side of the Mural Leads to the
UFO. A lot of people believe that there is no mystery, that the Zancudo, Hippy Camp, Sunken, and Chiliad UFO is all there is to the mystery. I believe that this is interpreted wrong I believe that the UFO on the Mural is there to represent Segregate and Rearrange, the final factual concrete evidence that we have that is not speculation, it's right there on the bottom of the Zancudo UFO. I think that the line linking the UFO and Jetpack is meant to represent finding out the truth. Like I said, no matter where the lines on the mural start at, they all lead to the same place Mount Chiliad. Clues on the UFO will lead you to the Jet-Pack and clues on the Jet-Pack will lead you to the UFO.
They all lead to the same place, with the egg in the middle representing the gaming community attempting to "crack" the mystery/mural. Our journey also begins with the Egg, leading directly down from the Chiliad UFO; every direction begins with the Chiliad UFO and ends with it also.

THE RIGHT SIDE OF THE MURAL:

I think that what the right side of the Mural is representing is what I like to call a "Legendary Hunter" Play through (because you will need a legendary effort to complete the game this way. I believe that if you do all of the side quest by certain points in the story (THE 5 MAJOR HEIST) then possibly something will trigger.
What that triggers, I honestly don't know but I do believe that it will be solved this way. I have never had a 100% completion; I just don't have the time to dedicate to doing it. However, that doesn't stop me from hunting or attempting to solve the mystery. Just think about it, it makes perfect sense. Why else would they say that we would never find everything, they knew that virtually no-one would play the game this way. A lot of people believe that there is a series of events that must take place in a certain order to either find the next clue or solve the mural completely. I believe that this is the process in which we must use; I mean we have virtually no way of knowing if we are following the right steps or not. My hope is that by completing a "Legendary Hunter" play through that we can unlock something along the way, or at least find the next clue in the Chiliad Mystery. I also included an image of my breakdown of the Mural.

To summarize everything that I said, I just want people to have an open mind but keep it grounded in realism. I think that completing as many of the side quest as early as possible in the story could possibly trigger the results that we are looking for, or at least help us find the next clue. I think that the red X's on the mural represent the 5 Major Heist in the story and could possibly act as triggers after completing the side missions ASAP. Remember as soon as you unlock a new set of mission types complete it right away before advancing in the story. Find all collectibles ASAP, right at the beginning of the game. Complete all strangers and freaks, races, and everything else as early as possible. This in my opinion is the 1 and only true way to 100%. Now I don't know if anyone has tried this already but if not, then that is why I believe we haven't been able to solve this mystery because I doubt anyone would ever complete the entire game playing it this way. Grinding hard and putting a bunch of hours in the side quest before you even get very far in the story missions. Hopefully this can spark some minds and lead to a great discussion and possibly solving the mystery and finally attaining the Jet-Pack, Flyable UFO, Green Alien Egg, or whatever it is that Rockstar is hiding from us (possibly the illusive “6 STAR WANTED LEVEL”).
