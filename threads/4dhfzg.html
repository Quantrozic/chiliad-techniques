On the top of Mount Chiliad, there is a second wall beside the one with the Mural (on the left). That got me thinking, and so I overlayed a mirrored version of the mural over it.

When the eyes on the top of the mountain are aligned, the two images overlay in a magical and creepy way. (image 1)

-The 5 lines over the eye matches, but the two underneath the eye are doubled, this is a voluntary positioning of those lines.

-A small X is formed underneath the eye. Just under the top of the mountain.

-The two eggs form a single hatching egg with an alien face over it.

-The two Jetpack guys are getting inside there spaceships.

-8 markers (X) are visible.This number is written everywhere in gta V.

When the two eggs are aligned (image 2)

-There is a tunnel leading from the top of the mountain to the unhatched egg.

-Some X markers are doubled.

When you align the eyes over the mountain to form a figure of 8 (image 3)

-Two X appear over and under the eye

-The center lines go through 4 X markers.

The most compelling to me is the first one, where an alien face appears over the egg.
I’ve put an other image with 4 layers of the chiliad mural overlayed, just for fun (image 4).

I leave you to your deductions.
