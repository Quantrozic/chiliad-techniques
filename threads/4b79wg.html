4. Floater

When the shootout starts, the crate slightly floats until it's touched in any way by anything. Bullets (yours or theirs), the player character, or even the Altruists themselves can cause it to fall just by touching it. It's very possible to clear the camp without triggering its gravity. Seems like this could be part of solving the mystery of the crate... or it's just a load glitch. Still worth looking into further IMO.
