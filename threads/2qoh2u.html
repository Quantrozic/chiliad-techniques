Secondly, I've researched this so I'll try not to cover ground we've covered. However, we've all spoke about the 5 eye logo on the penris building. You know, the 5 stars in the eye. Pentra = 5. Iris = eye. Penris = 5 eye. All referencing to the UKUSA agreement. Again however I was searching the vinwood sign and came across a tally chart on the letter I. How many lines? You gussed it, 5 (as in IIII with a line through them). Funnily enough, no such tally chart appears on any other letter. Another reference I believe is on Mt chiliad. The 5 glyhps that are read as UFO's or EYE's Or both! People claim they represent the x's on the map. How many? Again 5. Either there's a lot of 5 eye/illuminati references for nothing or we're missing something. My second find, is I noticed around the door of the observatory, there are markings of 3. Like this (III) scratched around the door. I figured, like the glyhps, this was hinting towards 3am. I went there are 3 am. Nothing, looked through the scope (telescope or sniper it doesnt matter) towards the penris building. Nothing. Switched the weather to thundery and then that's when something strange happened. The penris logo faded dramatically leaving 2 stars lit up. One being one of the 5 in the eye. The other being in the position of the letter N. But you can't see the star in the N at another time. Strange huh? I figured to the two remaining lit up stars maybe linked with the two lit up doors out of the 4 on the entrance to the observatory, but I might be grasping at straws here. However I'd also like to add I checked this twice and it happened both times. I tried at 3am and other times without the thundery weather and othing happened. If you find that the same happens but without the thundery weather and or at a different time than 3 am the please post and debunk this so that nobody wastes more time!
