So I keep bumping up on weird things in SP as I play it over. I noticed these weird things in the Humane Labs mission.

  1. If you start killing the caged monkeys, Michael suspiciously says “This is for the greater good. [pause].. Allegedly”
  2. The office labs are littered with sticky notes that say “This needs an extra push to close!” And some that have an equation on them (that I can’t make out because of low quality textures on XBOX One S) This phrase is even on the whiteboards. Could just be a repeated asset I know. It’s just suspicious that it’s so obviously repeated not even to show variety.

[Stretch incoming: the words “push” and “close” could mean we have to work harder to close the mystery?]

  1. When you switch over to Trevor and start landing the helicopter, instead of picking up the container, just land it on the ground. As you land it, Dave starts walking backwards and if you exit the helicopter you are prompted to get back in the vehicle. But for some reason Dave starts walking towards the helicopter looking off into one direction while his weapon stays pointed.

3.2 If you get back into the helicopter, Dave will stop walking towards you and freeze. If you leave the helicopter again after reentry, you won’t incur any penalties for walking far from the vehicle and can now basically free roam while on an active mission. (Maybe we can go somewhere else and try some stuff out?) My controller batteries are charging right now so I can’t try anything else just yet.

What do you guys think? Also, if this is old news I apologize and will kindly crawl back into the hole I came from.

Edit: You’re able to go to fort zancudo and there will be no wanted level even if you hop in the vehicles there. So again practically free roam while on a mission with 0 penalties. Also paleto bay is EMPTY.
