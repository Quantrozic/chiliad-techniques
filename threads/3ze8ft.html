The recent discovery of the six additional places for finding the golden peyote was based on coordinates from users of this forum. I remembered that there were leftover coordinates in the animal controller script from the original discovery, see http://i.imgur.com/JioTvCl.png .

Since I did not read about how the coordinates where found anywhere (maybe I missed it) I investigated and found that the coordinates in that file were indeed the coordinates for the golden peyote (see link later on).
However, there are still leftover coordinates in this file! What are these coordinates for?

I do not have GTA V installed at the moment so I would love to see someone experimenting with time/weather/character conditions to see if there still is something to be found here.
