What the hell triggers 3 Alta Street's lights?

▲ 22 ptsu/FlarestrikerFeb 10, 202320 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Memory-Scanning for Hidden State FlagsSave-State A/B Trigger Testing ?

The building furthest to the South-West of the Downtown 'Orchard' is simply called 3 Alta Street. You might know it as the building that has Epsilon-Blue rims that sorta indicate an X if seen from above. Plus, it has a weirdly double-lit Broadcasting Tower on a sweet landing pad and it is the only building in that orchard that shares the Eye-shaped Lights of Penris. Alongside more obscure theories, I have reason to assume that 3 Alta Street plays some sort of role in the 'Penris Mystery'.

What confuses me since months however is its entrance lighting. This is how it's supposed to look when its lit up. Notice the vaguely egg-like structures within the windows and, hey, the Eye-Lights are lighting up the building! I've never seen Penris's lights do that - but maybe they have the ability, too...?

Either way, I've only ever seen the building light up like that once by absolute chance. I never checked time or date or weather or moon phase - which is a detriment, since all I see when I drive past it at night is this version.

I've tried every setting in Director Mode to get this lighting to spawn but it seems as if 3 Alta Street has a mind of its own. Has anyone else seen it spawned/can make it spawn? If so, please do tell your conditions and if you've noticed anything else off about it.

Bonus: the Eye-Light depicted in my picture is the only one that points straight at this entrance. The general 3-Lights-at-Pillars structure can be found on every side, but only the entrance side has this lonely light chilling smack in the middle of it. Oh, also - if you find these Eye-Lights again anywhere else, also do tell.
