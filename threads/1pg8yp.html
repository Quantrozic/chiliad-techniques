Timeline of GTAV Easter Egg discoveries - Can you help?

A few days ago I started a thread over at GTA Forums:
http://gtaforums.com/topic/637631-easter-egg-origins-history-of-the-gtav-treasure-hunt/

Over the last few weeks I've gotten a little tired of the rampant speculation about jetpacks and the endless re-announcements of old clues on the Forums. I thought it would help to think about the game in terms of provable, real world facts. I decided to make a timeline that shows when and where the easter eggs (UFOs, alien[s?], movie references, etc.) were first mentioned or rumored online, and then to try find exactly where they were first proven with photo or video links.

I started a preliminary list in the thread linked above, but the list is far from complete and I'm sure there are earlier or more accurate links for a lot of the discoveries I've listed. I'd love it if you would take a look at the list and tell me if you have any information that I am missing.

I doubt this will help us find the next easter egg or predict the date of any upcoming in-game alien invasion/apocalypse/earthquake (lol!), but I believe it would help if everyone knows where these clues came from before we try to move forward. And it's just really interesting to look at how all this information has traveled in the last month and a half!

Please take a look, and thanks a lot!
