This is an hypothesis that I would like to propose you, I do not know if it has already been discussed here.

Firstly, I think most of you know one of the easter eggs of Vice City, which is a chocolate egg that says "Happy Easter" on it. This egg is hidden in a secret room in a building, accessible by passing through one of the windows. Here's a video:
http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pP6Ufk3KtbE

But the most interesting is what sustains him. As you can see the chocolate egg is on a pedestal.

In GTA V, just off the isolated house near the Mont Gordo, next to the lighthouse, there is a pedestal that looks similar to the one in Vice City:
http://prod.cloud.rockstargames.com/ugc/gta5photo/4965/UyoYPeg8PEqkz0nlnz4-Ag/0_0.jpg

Don't you find it suspect, an isolated pedestal like that, with nothing on it ?

So this is my theory, this base could be the spawn place of the alien egg. This is a track to keep in mind, until we find how to get the cracked egg. Maybe R* wanted to make a reference of the Vice City egg, with a more "futuristic" or "Science fiction" version of it.

Another important thing to note, I may be wrong, but the egg is related to Epsilon (if you have read the tracts), and the isolated house near Mount Gordo is probably related to this sect, because we can find the Kifflom old tractor in a bush.

Things seem to coincide.
