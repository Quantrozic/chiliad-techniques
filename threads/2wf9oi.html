Mods, please pin this post if you believe this developing method is relevant / interesting for our hunt.

TL;DR: A brief explanation of the scientific method. You don't have a theory, you probably have an hypothesis. Test before you share your ideas so they can become theories. If you can't test it, flair it as a untested hypothesis so hunters that are in some kind of lead don't lose focus. Some good resources explained in step 2). Some quick answers to most common excuses for not testing in step 3).

"It is a capital mistake to theorize before one has data. Insensibly one begins to twist facts to suit theories, instead of theories to suit facts." - Sherlock Holmes, A Scandal in Bohemia.

Guys, I know that there is a lot of hope going on (myself included), but I'd like to leave some opinions regarding new threads and 'theories'.

An idea is not a theory. On the scientific method, in order to develop a theory, you first need to have a QUESTION (this is the step we're all into. We do have questions: "Is there a jetpack in the game?", "What is the correct interpretation of the Chiliad mural?", "Is there a flyable UFO in the game?").

Then, based on that, you raise an HYPOTHESIS (and this is what most of the posts in this sub are). An hypothesis, in turn, must be TESTED first in order to become a theory. If the FACTS make sense with the hypothesis on your tests, then it is secure to say that you can share this to the community so they can test it too. If the facts align with the hypothesis for all experiments, ONLY THEN WE HAVE A THEORY. If they don't, all we have is background research for new hypothesis that can be raised:

http://www.cdn.sciencebuddies.org/Files/5084/7/2013-updated_scientific-method-steps_v6_noheader.png

What I'm trying to say is: Please, I don't want to be this guy, but I believe that most of our Jesus toasts and lame 'theories' were made by people that missed step 3 (testing). They have a lot of ideas (and that's a good thing), but for some reason they don't test it. I know that there are people in this sub that don't even has a console. I know that there are people that are not to 100% yet. And I know that there are people that have some good insights while not available to test ('I'm at work right now', 'I'm not at home right now', 'My console is broken', 'I've already taken 4 victims to the altruists'). But I'd like to give some ideas to better save our time, and help organize hunters by different approaches:

  1. Try to answer smaller questions before getting to a big one. Instead of "What does the mural means?", try to be more specific, like:
  • What does the X's in the mural means?
  • What does the lightning bolts in the mural means?
  • What does the figures in the mural means?
  • Is that really a jetpack in the man's back?

This way we'll have smaller hypothesis, smaller tests and finally, easier theories to debunk or confirm. More productivity!

  1. Research before creating an hypothesis: Don't be lazy to research! Don't just look at the first page of the sub and then "I didn't find this anywhere in this sub so here it goes my theory"! Don't just "I believe it would be cool if the game features this" and then here comes a giant post with tons of conspiracy! Even if you don't have the game, you can research and see if the mural can overlay that specific area, for example. You have a lot of external resources to help you out, such:
  • http://gta-5-map.com : Amazing google maps-like application with four different views (atlas, satellite, road and UV invert), location of items and collectibles and much more.
  • http://gta.wikia.com/ : Character backgrounds, names, locations, images. Well it's the wikia for Kraff's sake.
  • http://www.reddit.com/r/chiliadmystery : Yes, you read it correctly: This subreddit contains A TON of information. Search in it!. Game hunters are about not being lazy!
  1. TEST IT. Test it again. Test it with different conditions. Then, only then, share the hypothesis.

Just a thought: If you post your idea without testing it and, let's say, it takes 10 minutes to read your hypothesis + 10 minutes to test it, it could take a minimum of 10 * 13903 (number of members at the moment of this post) minutes just to read the idea. It would take 20 * 13903 (193 PRODUCTIVE DAYS!) in the worst case for the team to investigate it. If you test it yourself, it takes only 10 minutes (if it fails you still save 10 minutes because you don't need to make a post about it.)

Bonus: Some quick answers to most common excuses:

  • I'm at work right now / I'm not at home right now: Wait, go home, test it. We're waiting for more than a year now, we can wait a few hours for you to test your ideas before posting. Even if you take a couple of days to be available, that's OK. Calm down and wait for when you're available.
  • My console is broken: Wait, fix your console, then test. See reasons on the previous excuse.
  • I'm not 100%: If your idea requires an 100% save, you can either download an 100% save and test it, or you can get to 100% yourself. If your idea doesn't require an 100% save, you can do it right away.

Ok, now if you don't have the game / don't have a console and are on this sub just for the joy of investigation, then I'd recommend you to flair your post as a untested hypothesis. This will alert people that: a) They need to put their tinfoil hat before clicking on the post; b) They are willing to test an untested idea, that may or not be a good one; c) They are aware that the Nothing Happens trademark may appear.

  1. Post your findings in case of success with resources : Please gives us photo, video, game files. I don't care, just prove that your hypothesis is aligned with facts. It's the theory that must adapt to facts, and not facts that must adapt to hypothesis.
  2. Once confirmed, the lead could be fixed on the sub's pinned posts. This way we will have a good place to access hot leads that has some proven facts in it.

I believe that this is an efficient, well known method for testing and sharing things, now that we got to a point when we're not making huge discoveries. We need to keep this sub clean with good information. It's the wikia's job to have all possible information about a building, or a tree, a noise or a location. Our job is to use this information to build rails in which we can ride on. Investigation rails are made with facts.

Kifflom!
