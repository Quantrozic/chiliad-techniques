It seems as if (well in my game anyway) that whenever you go to the Epsilon building, only one gender spawn. I'm not yet sure if this is random, but I kept killing myself a few times as Michael and kept driving back up to there, and it was always just a single gender for me, I never saw them mix.
I also noticed that the spawns were different. I could sometimes kill myself 3 or 4 times and it would still be the same gender, but most of the times the gender that is outside would swap. I also notice that at the entrance of the building only two types of NPCs spawn, so there is a clone of each one and they stand opposite each other for me all the time. Could this just be a little inside thing where they only let certain genders out at certain times? I remember seeing that Epsilon is a mix of Scientology and a bit Heaven's Gate, with the Heaven's gate part being that they say "Possible descendant of Kraff" on Epsilon and with HG saying "How a Member of the Kingdom of Heaven
might appear"

(http://www.heavensgate.com/misc/member.htm)

I personally feel that we should keep on our current path, but occasionally look for Heaven's Gate references anyway. I mean heck, there are ufos in the game, michael gets taken up. Heaven's Gate was essentially a UFO cult that committed mass suicide to get on board a space ship trailing the Hale-Bopp comet, and there's a comet in the game.

Feel free to offer thoughts, and if it's your first time seeing Heaven's Gate, I would recommend looking them up and checking out some documentaries. I saw one that mentioned that they always kept the same people outside and the rest inside to avoid suspicion by police and to make it look like very few people live there when quite a few lived there. Could the people standing outside be doing a similar thing?

"in March 1997, 39 members of the cult Heaven's Gate committed mass suicide with the intention of teleporting to a spaceship they believed was flying behind the comet"

EDIT: and for people thinking that Heaven's Gate could be referencing the Altruists, sure their website looks similar, but the members of Heaven's Gate lived in a big mansion and not out on a mountain. I personally feel that The Epsilon Program could represent Heaven's Gate far more, but I also feel that it is a mix of Scientology AND Heaven's Gate.

EDIT: Just a little quote from someone else: "In some point in the game the Weasel News report that an asteroid is approaching the Earth. They say it will be visible after one week or so. I don’t know whether it’s just made up news or if it actually is visible one in-game night. Based on my research the news come out very early on in the story (under 10% completion). I’ve also heard/read that “asteroid” was found in some of the game files."

While I believe this could be similar to what we are observing, I prefer to think of Heaven's Gate as Epsilon even though EVERYONE seems to relate them to the Altruists. Sure, the website IS similar, but nothing else is. Heaven's Gate members never went around naked, Heaven's Gate members weren't all men or all white men, many Heaven's Gate members were pretty young, while the Altruists dislike the young. HG didn't praise the sun or live in shacks, most of them were white sure, but there were also some african americans in HG. Also in a documentary they mention that the members inside were very pale, so they clearly didn't praise the sun, they praised ufos and more. I'd like to think that you could bring one of them to Mount Chiliad or something. I just feel that there could be something more behind this.
