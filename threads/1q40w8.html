This is just speculation (sorry, I couldn't figure out how to tag the post as such), and I looked through as many pages as I could stand to see if it has been said. Apologies in advance if this has been said already.

Why would the mural have X's in boxes, and that do the lines mean? With all the overlays, I fail to see how the lines would fit in, unless the underground tunnels existed. Since no one has found an entrance yet, I find that to be unlikely.

Now... I speculate that the lines are a kind of branching game logic for choices during missions (the lines are paths/choices in the missions. Like option A or B, etc... Or maybe more subtle than that) the X's could indicate that at certain paths you could take, there is either temporary access to a clue... OR (and this is more likely as I will explain) making a certain choice blocks you from progressing down the path.

Has anyone mapped this out somehow to see if any resemblance to the mural paths could be reached? If this theory is true, then it would mean in the end after decisions are made at crucial parts of the game, either you have access to one of the rewards, or two of them (the connecting line at the bottom). In other words, either the easter egg, or the jetpack and UFO vehicle. Or none. This might or might not be character unique. As in, for Trevor you could have access to unlocking the egg, and for Michael nothing.

Support for this theory is the fact that each box with an X is a dead end... They don't lead to the rewards in any way. Also, the beginning (top) has 3 starting paths. This could be our three playable characters. As you can see, for each character there would be a clear path of decisions that would lead to no rewards at all.

If this is the case, many of you would have made some situations or events that would not be repeatable by others. Hence, a different direction would have to be taken to debunk people claiming an anomaly taking place that can't be repeatable by folks. We'd have to draw a clear map of paths or a set of paths that might fit with the mural, then ask for this information before debunking a submission. This would also mean that in order to debunk or claim false a submission, someone with the same path map would have to try and repeat the findings.

Anyway, just thought I'd help the cause. Again sorry if I'm repeating anything.
