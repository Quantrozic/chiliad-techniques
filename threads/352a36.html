UFO lights could mean something

The UFO light sequence is made of 7 steps repeating endlessely :
RED | WHITE | RED | break | WHITE | RED | break

What make me think it could be a hidden message is :

  • The altruist webpage uses morse code
  • The 3 dashes on the mountain glyphs standed for 3 A.M.
  • The mystery has always been about observation, not so much thinking. Once you discover the UFO, this would be the perfect next step to do : watching it.
  • The FIB logo spinning the wrong way has never been fixed, even in PC version. Is it a hint from Rockstar to look at the UFO ?
  • There is 5+2 dashes on the mural glyph, and 5 light blinks coming from the UFO + 2 breaks. It could also mean that we have to translate the 5 dashes into 2 letters, using morse code. If you look at the glyph, you can see the UFO is made of a line and a dot, which are morse chars.

First I believed that the second red blink lasted longer. So the first interpretation I made was done by converting time to morse, and I got the letters SA. But with more investigations, I noticed I was wrong : the 7 steps last the same time.

So the only way to translate this sequence is by using the colors. Doing that, I found something that might be interesting...
If red = - and white = . we get the letters KA in morse
If red = . and white = - we get the letters NR in morse

What is strange is that KA and NR are the intials of two similar NPC in the game :
Karl Abolaji and Norm Richards.
Those two characters are two possible gunmen for the last mission : The Big Score. Abolaji is an epsilonist and can only be employed on that mission. Moreover, if you look at all the crew members available in the game by alphabetical order, one is just under the other.

In ancient Egypt, Ka is the supposed spiritual part of an individual human being or god, which survived (with the soul) after death and could reside in a statue of the person.
This is interesting too because the map is inspired by the illuminati pyramid, as you all know.
Does it mean we have to kill one of the three characters to get the object ? In this case, that would also mean we can kill Franklin in a secret ending... Or maybe it just means that the reward is under a grave or a sculpture ?
