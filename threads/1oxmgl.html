Hello everyone! Just noticed a few interesting things that you may want to look into, since material is thin on the ground at the moment.

I was flying over the Kortz Center I noticed the only lights that were highly visible from my altitude resembled the jetpack that is on the stickmans back in the mural.

Pic 2.

So down I went to investigate, and there were some interesting sculptures. One of which could resemble the "Eye" or UFO when viewed from the correct angle, or perhaps even casting a shadow.
"UFO" Sculpture and it's Shadow.

The second interesting thing in there are the egg sculptures. There are two fat "eggs" and one curious slim egg with a hole in it.

Pic2 and Pic3.

Last but not least, just south of there if you are flying high enough you can see an "eye" made of lights. It is interesting to note that nearby there is a telescope aiming at the buildings producing these lights.
