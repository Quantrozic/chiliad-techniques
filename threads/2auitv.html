The dinosaur eye

▲ 7 pts[deleted]Jul 16, 201416 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Moon-Phase Condition Testing

The cave near Palmer Taylor power station is the eye of a dinosaur.
It just has to be viewed at the right angle. To see it you have to stand on the land across the water, facing the cave. If you move to a certain spot, the cave looks like an eye. The large rock in the centre of the cave is the pupil. The mountain around the cave makes it look like a dinosaur with its head laid down, looking out towards the ocean, or maybe its drinking ( in the drink?)
and if you look at the volcano shaped mountain in the background, it looks like a saddle on the dinosaurs neck, it even looks like there's a path leading up to the saddle.
If you wait until a night when the moon is passing around the south of the map, when the moon goes in line with the cave, the eye lights up. Is the cracked egg the moon?
The best time to see the eye lit up is when the moon looks like its above the power station.
If the moon rises in the south of the map it will be too low in the sky, and the eye will not be lit, even if its above the power station.
Wait for a night when the moon rises around the sonar docks, by the time it gets to the power station it should be high enough in the sky.
And if anyone thinks its Jesus toast, why is there a dinosaur statue at the otherside of the mountain, and its right eye lights up at night?
The diner even has a volcano on the roof ( the saddle )
Also, if you notice the Altruist eye, in some of the drawings the dot in the centre of the eye is painted above the line, and in others its painted below. When the rock in the centre of the cave is lit, some nights its lit below the waterline, some nights its lit above. ( he is risen. the dinosaur?) Even the mural looks like its half underwater.

But if someone has a save where the moon rises in the east of the map, they could upload one of the eye lit up.
