Textures with coordinates in Mirror Park and strange disappearance of a bench

▲ 19 ptsu/Diegox64chatJan 15, 20266 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Coordinate Pair Extraction and GeocodingMural & Map MirroringPlayer-Coordinate Telemetry Clustering

I was investigating Mirror Park when I accidentally died. I returned in my helicopter to the same spot and realized that a green bench was missing.

I started recording (PS4 - 100% completion). At the spot where the missing object should have been, I found a strange texture on the ground that seemed to indicate some kind of cords. What struck me most was that it had the symbol of the eye of Chiliad.

Or is it just a coincidence?

Image: https://imgur.com/a/C3d1z9V

Something I noticed later is that there was a glare on my phone and around my character (it's noticeable when I walk near the texture), which is why the phone looks so faded (probably because I died at night and it glitched due to the smog weather).

I set the floor on fire to see if the texture would darken a little. That's when I realized the bench wasn't invisible, it was just not there. I decided to get in the helicopter and fly high enough to reload the map and see if it would reappear. And it did; even the texture was still there.

After that I had problems with the police. I didn't cut the video so it would be clear that I wasn't using cheats or mods; but the video itself doesn't add anything after I shoot the first policeman.

Video: https://youtu.be/G\_NMKIgsBxU

If anyone could upload this texture in better quality, I'd appreciate it.

Kifflom!
