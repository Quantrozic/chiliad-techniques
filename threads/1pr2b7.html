"Pharoes Riches" [sic] sign at Kortz

▲ 16 ptsu/lendotNov 2, 201322 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Anagram and Constraint Word-Solving ?

Made a curious discovery while roaming Kortz. All along Kortz Drive and the rotunda area there are banners for "Pharoah's Riches" (http://socialclub.rockstargames.com/games/gtav/snapmatic/photo/YDLp1d9XrUiFvF8Vh68bpQ). At first glance I didn't think much of that until I double-checked the spelling and it should be "Pharaoh." Mildly interesting, but that's such a common misspelling even I missed it the first several times I looked at it.

Now if you head back to where the hedge maze is, there's a sign on the building that says "Pharoes Riches" (http://socialclub.rockstargames.com/games/gtav/snapmatic/photo/L6MGA9UH7k2Tws2fHNuoig). That's the one that really caught my eye. What jumps out at me even more than the blatant misspelling is the lack of an apostrophe. If it were just the misspelling, I might have been inclined to write this off as an easter egg poking fun at the fact that it's a tricky word that no one can ever spell correctly without looking it up first. But when combined with the missing apostrophe, I think it warrants a closer look.

My current suspicion is that this is an anagram. I ran it through an anagram generator but there are a lot of possible combinations and a cursory look didn't turn up anything exciting (although "search pier hos" gave me a good chuckle). Anyone else wanna take a stab and see if you can turn up anything?

Related to this, a while back I went looking for a tool to facilitate poring over large sets of anagram results and found this one: http://www.easypeasy.com/anagrams/input.php. The cool thing about it is instead of just dumping out all the results, it gives you a list of unique keywords it found and then you can select one and see only the anagrams that contain that word. Very useful for weeding out all the garbage. Just be prepared to click past the annoying pages asking you to make a PayPal donation.
