My theory

▲ 17 ptsu/Lauren_of_LoreSep 24, 201320 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Moon-Phase Condition Testing ?

The UFO that is found at Mt. Chiliad seems to just be a hologram, and because of that I think what "they" are trying to tell us to do is to go interact with that UFO.

The UFO over Chiliad is a hologram of the one that appears over the hippy camp in Sandy Shores, so that means the first one we need to interact with might be that one.

There are plenty of clues making me think you have to get abducted by the FIB UFO:

  • The UFO opens and closes its hatch over and over, turning on its tractor beam
  • In the hippy camp there's writing saying "Beam me up!"
  • You get abducted by the same UFO during one of Michael's trips, after he drinks the spiked drink his son gave him

For anyone who already has a 100% save loaded up, go to the hippy camp in Sandy Shores [[pic]](http://i.imgur.com/IweRAiv.jpg) and look around. You'll see a bunch of stuff but the two things that should stand out are two glyphs.

The first glyph is a UFO with a rain cloud under it, the second is a UFO with 3 lines and a moon under it.

The first obviously means it needs to be raining, and the second could mean several things but probably just means it needs to be at night.
