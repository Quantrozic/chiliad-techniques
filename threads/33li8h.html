So, this is kind of "out there" (though I've seen wilder ideas), but I noticed something about the ladders in the game.

I saw a YouTube video about the area near Dutch London Street right above the Port of South Los Santos:

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SD79aRcaLmU)

so i decided to go check out the area with Franklin. While there, I figured I should climb the towers around the area for a better look. While I'm climbing them (specifically this one http://imgur.com/S7w3qIY) I notice that the ladder is green (Franklin's color). I didn't think much of it until later, when I'm dicking around as Trevor in Paleto Bay near the Clucking Bell Factory. While up there, I decide to climb the tallest tower (water tower i think) on the side of the train tracks behind the factory. What was surprising was that the ladder was orange (Trevor's color) --- I also heard weird buzzing noises at the top of the tower but I don't think it was anything. Additionally, I was at O'Neil's Farm (from the Trevor mission) climbing around on the silos there (also as Trevor) and the ladders there were orange as well (Trevor's color).

Now, I haven't checked out any other ladders and I'm sure this is probably nothing. Just thought I'd share it with you guys.
