Thank you for taking the time to review my ideas. I know this is a lot to read but I really believe this could lead somewhere with proper testing and better review of the source code. Also I apologize for the long winded nature of this post. I’ve tried to make it as short as possible but there is some much information needed to make full sense of my theory it’s difficult to sum up quickly.

I’ve been part of the chiliad mystery community for roughly 5 years. Sometime about 8 months ago I decided to start from the beginning and completel 100 percent all gtav story mode and online content. Specifically with the idea being to test certain ideas by creating the necessary save points for back tracking and brute forcing different completion states. During this journey I started to notice certain similarities and hints that I believe are a type of instructions to complete/interact with a secret or Easter Egg. Hopefully if I can do a coherent enough job explaining what I believe exist it will seem just as obvious to you as it does to me now.

To start by laying out a basic explanation, I believe the Games engine has potentially several script sequences that are waiting for a certain interactions to be registered together. A deeper explanation of why I believe this to be true I will detail in a separate part of this post. Essentially all three player characters Michael, Franklin and Trevor need to used together in order to trigger whatever secret I believe likely to exist. I’m not talking about using the hangout feature to get all three characters together. I believe they need to be controlled and positioned independently of each other.

Before getting lost going into what I believe to be evidence I want to on some core game mechanics that allow my theory to be possible and the existence of these mechanics make little sense not just narratively but especially from a development sense. The game allows us to switch between our different protagonist during free roam by use of the characters select wheel. If you wait longer than 90-120 seconds two switch back to a previously controlled character they go into a type of sleep state until you switch to the again. If you however keep switching before that window expires you can control all three players on the map at the same time. Additional & Importantly the game engine is tracking a truly vast amount of different factors such as the time of day, map location, regional proximity, character proximity, recent events, traffic, pedestrians, wanted level, vehicle, game completion percentage, field of view, Level of detail scan position just to name a few. Depending on these different factors a RNG roll will activate different characters transition animations, some rarer that other. Trevor by far has the most.

Additionally there exists an engine tracking script tied to ped density and player proximity that operates independently of the animation program. When certain proximity conditions are met such as having switched between all three characters within a 90 second window so all 3 protagonists remain in an active player controlled state and they are within a certain close proximity relative to each other the games engine disables a few standard features. Including the character animation sequence, the zoom in/zoom out effect, LOD scan and extends the low res background. It does all of this while completely maintaining each characters unique user interface, map area restriction/permissions, dialogue differences, unique player internet interaction, mission markers and map icon data.

From a development perspective this is insanely resource intensive for almost no perceptible benefits and back in 2013 getting this working as smoothly as it appears in game is a small miracle on top of the monetary expense that went into getting it all working. I can’t help but think why go through all the effort? A single experienced programmer could have just set the characters selection so if you tried to quickly switch between all three during free roam one would call or text saying “hey I’m busy and can’t be available right now” or “I’m with so and so, I’ll brb” then give you a 30-60 second cool down until you can control that character again. Saving a huge amount of performance resources. However they chose the harder path. Outside of two story missions that barely use the full feature it goes completely unused. Unless of course a hidden secret or Easter egg exist.

It would explain why no one just randomly has stumbled across anything. Most people play only as the same favorite single character. Social club stats places Franklin then Michael and Trevor last as time spent playing.
Most players probably don’t fully understand how this character switch mechanic actually works.
Okay if you’re still with me here let move on.

Let’s look at some of the many reasons why I think this all to be true.
Starting with the yellow doomsday mural we can see the silhouette of a person standing which I believe directly references the player characters. To the direct side of the silhouette there is a waveform with three dots positioned sequentially along the waves track. These each refer to one of the three main characters. If we count the peaks and valleys of the wave their are 13 peaks and a total of 26 pulses. If we assume each pulse is 10 seconds we get 4:20..besides the obvious reference I believe this is the window of time we have before the sequence restarts. Looking under the waveform there exist what appears to be an encrypted message or possibly mapping data that appears as a series of dots,blips,lines and dashes. I don’t believe this has ever been verified as solved/translated and could possibly hold importance.

Shifting focus to the next part of the doomsday mural we see a reproduction of part of the voyager probe message in the form of a line array, under which a symbol of a ufo is represented. To the right a figure of a face with expanding dotted lines looking towards the line array signifies our player character facing a certain direction and looking at a converging point. Taking a closer look at the line array you will notice 3 of the line segments stand out as potentially significant. These 3 sections share the same wide rectangular symbol and I believe instruct us to have each character (Michael,Franklin,Trevor) positioned at a certain point on the map where their field of views can converge. Around Chiliad seems like the obvious positions. Someone once claimed that the line array segments represented different coordinates around Blaine country/observatory and when standing within a certain area of these locations a faint unique radio static transmission could be heard. There is a YouTube video of them showing the locations but I can’t remember the title.

Additionally looking to the top right of the line array on the mural semicircles appear sequentially which I believe are three 1/3 sections of a circle placed to further hint towards the use of using all thee characters independently of each other during a specific amount of time.

Next is my investigations into the games source code. For a brief time last year I had access to a Remote Desktop with a few different tools and a version of the leaked source code. I tried my best to understand what I was able to access but I’m no expert level developer. While roaming free mode coming into a certain very close close radius of one of the other characters several flags exist as proximity triggers. While the obvious function is to trigger the hangout mode via a dialogue interaction there also exists a flag for a “triple switch active” that doesn’t seem to be directly scripted or connected to any file paths other than a few location attached nodes. The engine is definitely tracking this specifically and I remember that one of the flag node was attached to the yellow jack inn. At the time I believed it was related to darts but several other locations have the same node format such as the ft zan cudo and the chiliad lift station. There is also direct mention of a “3 part clew” within a section of the developer notes.

I also want to mention the how. As in the how in the hell do we figure this out? I think real world testing for those who have the time but I’ve always wanted to use script analysis tools to brute force character states in code walker while monitoring performance spikes to see if any big files load in. Also thinking with the use of three characters active on the map at the same time. It possible we are on the right track with this but are overlooking obvious details. For now I will leave it here and just say thank you for taking the time to entertain this idea. I’ve wanted to make a post like this for quite some time now but have been too nervous.
