We've been a little scattered lately in our search. I think we should start with what we already have then extend to other realms once they are all debunked.

R* intended for us to see the mural, the glyphs, and the UFOs. So that's really all we have to go on initially. We know an Egg exists in game, and we know there is a texture for an alien spacecraft HUD of some sort. So let's apply deductive reasoning...

THE GLYPHS

The Eye -> All Seeing, top of the mountain. Omnipresence (could relate to the switching ability between characters, the user is omnipresent)

The Eye Above Rain Cloud -> Clouds obscure vision, limits perception, clouds judgement. (When switching characters you can't see the map, due to cloud cover)

The Eye Above Waning Cresent Moon - > Waning Crescent occurs once in 28 day moon cycle in game. There are full moon parties in game, so we know lunar cycles are an interactive game mechanic. Also implies night time, lines indicate time, 3 lines, 3am or 03:00 the "Magic Hour".

Eye Above Light Beam(Radio Tower) - > My interpretation of the mountain or radio tower glyph is that it is actually a UFO with a beam under it. The FZ lights may be key here since they are a timed event and obviously hold back the NPCs from engaging you on the bunker...

Half Eye -> Perhaps this suggests that the Mt. Chiliad UFO is only half real, since it is only light(hologram) and not mass.

THE UFOS

Fort Zancudo UFO-> Beams a green light down on a bunker at the base. Lights begin at 01:00. At 02:00 secondary lights show. At 03:00 full intensity light is shown over bunker until 04:00. No Wanted Level if you parachute in from 00:00 to 04:00 and stay on top of the bunker. NPCs do not react automatically, no airspace warnings triggered. R* purposefully made it so the way is clear. This is key because the game mechanics actually change here. R* wants us at that bunker...

Mt. Chiliad UFO -> Appears at 03:00 in the rain. Is only a hologram, not solid. Approach the UFO and it's lights go out in sequence in the same pattern each time, one by one. It is the "image" of the hippy camp UFO.

Hippy Camp UFO-> The missing link, it hovers over hippy camp, but shows no lights at the camp and doesn't really do anything in terms of timing or weather...yet.

OBSERVATIONS

  1. Fort Zancudo UFO & Mt Chiliad UFO are linked in time. 03:00-04:00 Magic Hour for FZ UFO to shine light at fullest, and Mt.Chiliad UFO Appears at 03:00.
  2. Mt. Chiliad UFO has Rain Condition to Appear, FZ and Hippy Camp Don't.
  3. The question is what links the FZ & Mt.Chiliad UFOs to the Hippy Camp UFO?
  4. Mt. Chiliad UFO is the IMAGE of the Hippy Camp UFO (Half Glyph, "Projection" Glyph)
  5. Hippy Camp UFO doesn't interact with the surface...no lights at hippy camp. Could it be that the hippy camp ufo is itself projecting to Mt. Chiliad?

Where does that leave us?
