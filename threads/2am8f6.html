With regard to this ol' mystery, the radio shows can be very informative if you listen carefully, keeping in mind that virtually everything has a hidden meaning. The shows contain many illuminating details on how to pursue some of the nirvanas GTAV has to offer.

I've spoken often about how there are at least three nirvanas available, one for each protagonist. I believe Chakra Attack provides plenty of clues on Michael's nirvana in particular (Dr Ray seems to identify with the blue side of humanity), but also has some pointers on the mystery as a whole.

Here's a breakdown of all the clues I've spotted and what they could mean. They aren't necessarily in chronological order, rather I have put related quotes together.

“Ohhmm”

  • A reference to Om, the dharmic mantra. Here, it seems to represent yoga.

“Every morning – before you get started – we always ohhmm.”

  • You have to do yoga on a daily basis when pursuing Michael's nirvana.

“While you're blowing on a sausage, you wanna 'ohhmm'”

  • Yoga is a metaphor for sucking dick... Kraff's dick.

“Ignorant fools will say, are you really humming on that sausage? No, I'm cleansing myself. Like wheatgrass on your radio. That's how you cleanse yourself.”

  • Both wheatgrass and yoga are ways to gain Epsilon/blue karma, either for its own sake or for redemptive purposes.

“Don't you go direspecting coconut water. That's the nectar of the gods.”

  • Coconut water is a metaphor for sperm, the thing that Epsilonists deny exists. They call it nectar because being blown is part of their religion.

“Don't set things free that are gonna kill other things. You're attacking the world in the wrong way!”

  • Think of cause and effect when choosing who to kill or spare. Bad guys should die. Don't regret putting them down, they will only kill again.

“You cannot go to heaven as a human, you can only go to heaven as a damn angel!”

  • Somehow you have to die to reach nirvana.

“You're giving life and you're taking life away at the same time. It's two things going on here; it's a hot and a cold.”

“I am a compassionate lover and a fierce warrior.”

  • Epsilonists want to kill gingers and sleep with multiple women. Compare this with Duane Earl on Beyond Insemination, who says “it is never about that woman's pleasure, NEVER.”

“There is a duality. Du-ality. TWO-ality.”

“We are all one, but some of us are also two.”

  • He's talking about the origins of humanity from the red and the blue, Zapho and Kraff.

“When one becomes two, problems can arise”

“You have a disdained look for everybody in MY universe.”

  • Referring to the underlying race tension between those identifying with red or blue. Seems like Cheryl is a red.

“Enjoy the view. There's mountains, peaks and valleys on that body.”

  • It's okay to look at strippers.

“There's an in and an out motion going on here.”

“Share the women, but just don't leave no stains 'cause that ain't nice.”

  • The Epsilonist directive towards sex seems pretty important. They have a liking for blowjobs and facials. Also a reminder that some of the girls can be enjoyed by more than one protagonist.

“You have to give yourself, and your money, to public radio.”

“I am for-profit, but many are not.”

  • Donations to blue causes, like Epsilon, are definitely appreciated.

“We cleansing everything: mind, body, and your spirit. We going big. We going all the way. Nirvana...”

“This is a full set, it's something all-encompassing...”

“Spiritual things come in threes. A three-parter. Only not sleaze, and no cross 'em swords and no worrying about if it's right or wrong or if you enjoyed it too damn much, because you're supposed to enjoy every last bit of it.”

  • The three nirvanas are Michael = Mind. Trevor = Body. Franklin = Spirit. Every part of the game is included in at least one of the paths. You can work on them all together, just match the activity to the character. Mistakes are allowed if the overall style is consistent.

“Sense and nonsense together do not agree with each other.”

“You put sense and nonsense together and what do you got? You got some bullshit going on.”

  • Beware of having characters act in ways contradictory to their overall philosophy. For example, Michael wouldn't go after Merryweather because (in his words) “that's Trevor's thing” (phone call after the Bureau Raid).

“A karma compass, that tells you street shit and real shit.”

  • There are karma indicators in the game to keep you on the right path. You just have to figure out what they are and calibrate behaviour to them.

“Turn that [rainstick] upside down. Hear them soothing sounds?”

  • Rain is a karma indicator for blue. Rain is a good sign for Epsilonism.

“Why are you obsessed with pinatas?” “You either is one, or you isn't one. And sometimes, you both is and you isn't.”

  • Alluding to the race war again. For Epsilonists, Trevor is a pinata (of ginger blood), Michael is not. Franklin is a hybrid, half and half.

“You can hang yourself in the public square and let people hit you with sticks 'til candy and coins drop out yo' ass. Or you can sit on the mountaintop and ohhmm. It's simple. I like both.”

  • Because Trevor is “a pinata”, if he gets killed this is good for Michael's nirvana. For example, it often triggers a thunderstorm if he gets killed at the altar in the Altruist camp. Franklin might also count for this, but to a lesser extent. And the other part is about Michael doing yoga.
