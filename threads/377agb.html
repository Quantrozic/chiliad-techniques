Hey, I was just dicking around in singleplayer when I stole a truck on the highway next to the horse tracks. It had a trailer next to it but it wasn't connected anymore because the cars were crashing into each other. When I got in I just quickly changed the radio station, and it had some kind of talk show playing, but it had a buzzing noise. When I started driving forward (South) the buzzing got louder, but I went back North so I could get the trailer attached to it, the buzzing noise got weaker.

Then I attached the trailer, drove North, buzzing got louder and eventually the talk show couldn't be heard anymore and it started pushing another station in behind the buzzing noise. Then the buzzing stopped and I could just hear the other station normally.
Unfortunately recorded videos on PS4 don't save the radio audio so I can't prove this in any way :(

Has this happened to anyone else? It wasn't just random buzzing that would eventually change the station, it specifically got louder when driving South and weaker when driving North.
