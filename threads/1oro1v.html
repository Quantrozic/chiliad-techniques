It is possible to found every single meanings to all teh drawing/text on the internet and I personnally went on verifying everything.

"So what does it all mean ?", you'll ask because i think i might sound a bit condescending (if it's a word in english...) :

  • Crosses - Has written in the bottom-right, they are some, not all, location to find the nuclear wastes/submersible part/random weapon or dosh location.
  • These slashed O - Has written, it indicates some location where a Gruppe 6 truck can spawn for you to steal their money.
  • Red triangles - Has written, online only buyable properties.
  • Red circles - Has supposed, shop you can attack.
  • Red boxes - Has written, Los Santos Custom location.

Now blue text and drawings from top to bottom (roughly) :

  • Stash + Cannabis leaf + UFO symbol : N-E of the map, indicates a repeatable random event in solomode where you can steal money from some drug dealers, also point out a spaceship part location.
  • Frank Mathers : Random encounter with Micheal, leading to a side mission quite unsatisfying
  • Between the crates + Sun symbol : RPG and various weapon located in the altruist camp between crates next to a house with a sun painted on it.
  • Rail Cabin + spreading lines : Indicates the location of a shotgun in the red cabin with a wagon in it.
  • Exploding symbol north of Alamo Sea : Grenades on a dock.
  • They are here + UFO symbol : Spaceship part location (You can believe the text has some meanings but i'm sure that it hasn't any)
  • CBL = Omega symbol = UFO symbol : Location of Omega encounter, CBL is what he calls Franklin when they first met if I recall, UFO symbol indicates the mission he gives you as well as explaining the signification of the other UFO symbol on the map.
  • The whole Zancudo thing : Location of the 3 cited vehicles, as well as the Minigun in the control tower (The symbol is still weird I admit, maybe elevator related ?)

  • In the drink : Spaceship part and letter scrap in the cave.
  • Under the rock face : Letter or spaceship part location (I don't remember which)
  • 2 other UFOs symbol with no text : spaceship part location.
  • Shoot for the stars + Star Symbol + UFO Symbol : Sniper and spaceship part location (I'm pretty sure that there is nothing else here atleast from the map)
  • 2 more UFOs symbol with no text : bleh.
  • Open grave : a gun you surely already have by the time you find it...
  • Pink pool : Protection location.
  • Ladder symbol + SW rooftop : There is a ladder SW of the building leading to the rooftop where you can find a weapon (RPG i think) and also an armed Buzzard.
  • Backyard / Davis & Grove St : Equipment in some of the backyards.
  • Bridge symbol with arrow : RPG location.
  • Big red circle is obviously the online special car delivery to Simeon.
