New to this - is Devin Weston connected to anything yet?

Hi! Been vaguely aware of all the clues and puzzles and secrets around GTAV in this community for a while but never dug into it myself.

However, just recently I started reading a book: Out of the Silent Planet by C.S. Lewis - yes, the Narnia guy, he did a lesser-known Sci-Fi trilogy.

In the first few chapters the plot gets going fairly swiftly - the protagonist, a linguist named Ransom, out on a walking holiday stumbles on a house where he meets an old, less-liked schoolmate and a somewhat rude scientist. These two promptly drug him, kidnap him, and he wakes up on board a spacecraft flying to Mars (although because he's a linguist and apparently loosely based on Lewis's friend JRR Tolkein, he immediately nerds out and teaches himself all the alien language and terms and only refers to the place as Malacandra, the local name)

But in reading this (I've only got 12 or so chapters in) it struck me suddenly that the names of the two villains were very familiar: the posh financier/businessman who seems in it for exploiting other planets is named Devine, and the scientist is named Weston.

Playing GTAV, it always struck me as odd that, considering how much Rockstar love putting in little gags and pun names and references, that the main story antagonist Devin Weston had such a relatively unusual name, yet not for any obvious reason.

Suddenly seeing these two characters abducting a third into space, so often referred to in the book together as 'Devine and Weston', and remembering the whole Chilead Mystery clues and easter eggs... I can't help but think this MUST be a reference to C.S. Lewis's Cosmic Trilogy - yet beyond that I can't see any connection between Devin Weston, who seems very down-to-earth and linked to financial matters and Merryweather, and not the Chilead aliens/sci-fi/UFOs clues...

I'll keep reading the book, see if any other obvious references pop out, but I also couldn't find any reference to this name connection anywhere, maybe I'm just making it up.
