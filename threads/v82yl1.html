There's a house on the edge of a cliff in Banham Canyon that has a telescope that's in alignment with the 3rd Eclipse in the Moon Cycle.

Here's a pic showing this alignment.

After completing the mission “Far Out” a Spaceship Part will spawn right next to this telescope.

The player will automatically collect this Spaceship Part when choosing to look through the telescope. Perhaps we're meant to collect this part during the 3rd Eclipse or perhaps it was simply put here as a clue towards its alignment.

The 3rd Eye is visible through the telescope without making any adjustments to it, though it's possible to center in and focus on it at maximum zoom. I think this is likely what we need to do if there's some kind of trigger behind this.
