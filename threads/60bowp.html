As you might know, there is an odd beam/line that appears in the SKY / clouds during certain weather/times.

IMAGE OF SKY BEAM & RELEVANT STUFF

It can be seen from the observatory Sniper Telescope looking south between the skyscrapers. It's above the Gas Domes when viewed from there. At first look, it seems like a seam in the cloud texture, and it might be that but..

  • The Official R* wallpapers depicts a similar line and an thunder cloud eye above the 2 Gas Domes,
  • here is some art that shows similar lines and stuff
  • [the supposed R* employee has told us about this on 4chan] (http://i.imgur.com/g5i0jzz.jpg)

I also seen in a video that there is another line opposite this one in the North that is only visible when the other line is. If the leaker is just making stuff up, he sure picked a weird thing to make important
