Hi all, not sure if this has already been mentioned but I was looking around online at the mural overlay pictures, and a lot that I've seen tend to mean that there's nothing where there should be something according to the mural, and seeing way too much speculation surrounding the all seeing eye/UFO/sun/whatever at the top of the mural being linked to the crashed UFO on the north coast.
Having given this a look, I thought about reversing the mural, so the jetpack glyph is on the bottom left instead of the bottom right, thinking that the mural may need to be read from the north face of the mountain.

After that, I noticed a diner at the foot of mount chiliad (north eastern foothills) that has a rocket outside it and thought that even though it isn't a flying saucer as seen in the mural, it IS a spaceship.

Head west from that point and you'll find the cluckin bell farm, I know a lot of speculation has suggested that this could be representative of the egg in the mural, so continued west and then the map curves and well....nothing but a few islands, so I investigated with the sub and found no underwater tunnels or anything so came up dry.

However, if you head Head virtually straight south of the diner with the rocket out front and you'll find Rex's diner (the one with the dinosaur outside it), and this is tenuous I know but dinosaurs laid eggs, so if there's an audience for the cluckin bell farm representing the egg, then im OK with the dinosaur also potentially being representative of the egg. Also, if you go on the map when you're at Rex's diner and head a straight line west, it lines up with the mouth of the zancudo river, where you hundred percenters'll all know theres a UFO hovering above FZ.

So so far I've been thinking reverse the mural left to right, then rotate it 90° to the left, and its still holding some relevance, so I followed it, and using that logic and following south to get to the jetpack panel, due south of Rex's diner..... Is the NOOSE HQ (the one with the attack chopper on the roof that you don't face any resistance in) so I went in and had a poke around , noticed that at least one of the doors around the bottom of the building is an openable texture (its part of a set of double doors and where one has that "look at me! I'm interactive" shine to it, the other doesn't. Couple that with the helipads having a few "invisible walls" at the top of ladders so that you have to climb up onto the helipads from the back, and I think that our jetpack may be contained there to be flown out of the building and away from one of the helipads.

Now I just need to see if thheres any points of interest on the general map that tie in with the boxes on the mural....I'll do that another day, but its just my two cents and hopefully helpful, if this has been discussed before then sorry for repeat posting
