Reverse WHOIS on Take-Two Domain Names

▲ 25 ptsu/zuccsJul 8, 20195 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Frequency-Domain Watermark Search

Hey all,

I haven’t played this game for 5+ years, but I do enjoy reading a good conspiracy post on here. So please don’t crucify me if this has already been covered or isn’t relevant at all.

As far as I understand, whattheydonotwantyoutoknow.com was a website provided to you in GTA IV?

So I did a WHOIS on that domain name and it’s clearly owned by Take-Two Interactive Software, Inc. with the email address domains@take2games.com.

Then I did a reverse WHOIS search on that email address (https://viewdns.info/reversewhois/?q=domains%40take2games.com) which gives 1,000+ domains owned by them. You can see the first 500 for free on that site - or if someone wants to chip in to buy the rest of that list.

There are a few interesting ones that stood out to me:

altruistsunite.com

bankofliberty.net

grainoftruth.net

etc.

Someone better versed in this mystery might spot some others that are more relevant.

I should also clarify that this list is not exhaustive. There could be another 1,000 domain names not picked up by this service. Or if there was a website we were supposed to go to, the domain name could be marked as 'private' in which case there would be no relevant details on the WHOIS for us to see.

As far as I can tell, these just redirect to https://www.rockstargames.com/V/, but we are only looking at the root domain name here.

There might be a prefix (like "wwww", or folder/file required in the URL (e.g. “altruistsunite.com/clickme” to redirect to somewhere more useful that isn’t the generic GTA V homepage. There’s not really any method to find that out though.

Or this could just be Take-Two protecting their brand and sitting on a bunch of relevant domain names. Who knows!

Anyway, hope this inspires someone out there!

Happy hunting.
