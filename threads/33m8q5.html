###Why I'm not Qualified
.

So I just recently started hunting and am trying to get to 100% as fast as I can. The thing that stands in the way is the stupid Epsilon missions and a couple of random events. So as I've been reading everything that has been posted on here and playing the game I think I may have an idea for a different way to hunt for the truth.

My theory is that the Epsilon program is the closest we could ever get close to the aliens in a controlled environment, save the space rover. The other times were when we smoked weed and when we got drugged by Jimmy. Therefore, I think it makes sense to go back to basics and look at what we know to be true.

###San Andreas Department of Water and Power
.

Right after you were dropped off in Shandy Shores at the satellite dishes near the prison there was a group of scientists, like many people report seeing early in the morning with FIB agents. The other people that were there in my game. Were four employees of the San Andreas Department of Water and Power. This was my first exposure to the Epsilon program so I made sure to make careful note of it. I went online and researched where they were based in game.

##Elgin Ave Substation
.

They have two buildings. One is on Elgin Avenue, right across the highway from the Casino and race track. What else is there. The message in the back about the dinosaur where you later interact with Marnie. (I definitely think there is a connection here) My personal opinion for what it's worth (which may be shit) is that the SADWP Company may be related to the lightning bolts that we see in the mural on Mount Chiliad. Perhaps, it represents the need for electricity to sustain that hologram. I haven't one hundred percented yet but it may be possible to look for a source of the hologram and then disable it. Like you cracked the mystery (egg) of the UFO, but alas this is all speculation.

##Little Bighorn Street and Jamestown Street Substation
.

The next power substation is located at Little Bighorn Street and Jamestown Street. Over near where you bring the hot cars for Devin. I looked around this power substation for a while and there wasn't much, and here's where I'm gonna start grasping at straws, but there is a billboard for the children of the mountain. The billboard has an eerily similar look as the mural on Mount Chiliad. I know it's not the Epsilon program, or event the Altruist Cult, but that's what I have. Now I'm trying to do some Nicholas Cage National Treasure bullshit and follow lines. I feel as though if you have two points linked to the mountain nearby there's something going on. So I hoped off the top of the SADWP substation and went straight past where the sign was. I ended up at a Self Storage warehouse, the Pisswasser factory, and some cold storage building. Now sounds like a dead end, but! I have a different idea.

###The Fish
a.k.a. Where I went Batshit Crazy

.

The only other symbol that I get from the Epsilon program is those boxes inside that little warehouse where they store all the posters. The all have the same (Chinese?) writing on top and a dead fish on the side. These boxes aren't found everywhere in game, but they were found all over the Storage Warehouse. No idea if that means anything. I searched and didn't see anything myself.

So to elaborate on that fish I started looking for Chinese characters all over the city, namely where the Triad liked to hangout during the story, but couldn't find anything. So I was driving through Little Seoul looking to see if I couldn't find some Chinese as well. The one thing I did find was right across the street from the Ammunation in Little Seoul is a building that had a giant H on it. I remember the alleged Rockstar Employee leaving a cryptic H at the end of his second and final post ever on his account, so I always make sure to be looking at new ways to understand his message. I stop and look around. On the top of the building with the H is a giant Epsilon billboard, know I know I was in the right place. I look around some more and I saw the Grand Asian hotel I believe it's called, and the character on the outside of the building looked more Chinese to me than Korean like the rest of the area so I go to investigate.

##Or did I?
.

Before I can even get there I find a building on the far corner towards the Grand Asian Hotel that had a red fish logo on it's front doors. I stop and investigate. On the top of the building there is an advertisement for an Asian printing service I was like maybe that's where the posters are from. Anyway I can't find anything around the building and start looking at the Grand Asian Hotel and another Asian strip mall catches my eye and what do I find but the printing place at the far corner. RIGHT WHERE EPSILON TRACK 5 takes place!

I think that instead of looking at all of these hippies who don't understand the fact that the government controls three of the four UFOs it makes more sense to follow the government.

TL;DR let's look at the clues that are present in the Epsilon missions!
Disclaimer: If anyone knows where else the printing boxes might come from that would be greatly appreciated! I tried looking on the boardwalk and Vespucci beach and the Great Ocean Highway but didn't find anything startling.

Edit: That's the end of my wild speculation, but I did want to add one more thing. Trying to piece together some clues others have found. I think if you draw a line between the lighthouse and the signs on the Ron Oil signs on the Great Ocean Highway near Procopio Beach it points almost directly to the sunken UFO. Could be something to look into.
