DISCLAIMER: I do not own GTA 5, nor any system capable of playing it. I'm only at about 23% completion. Any info/opinions in this thread are either gathered from watching this mystery from the internet.

I personally think the whole Karma thing will lead us no where. Let's just get that out of the way.

Reading the Mural: I believe the mural is supposed to represent MC, and the X's lead to the glyphs, giving you clues on how to view the first UFO. Following this map will lead you to the top of Chilliad, and hopefully the signs on/around the viewing platform. After getting the hint "Come back when your story is complete." Followed by finding the Glyphs to view the MC UFO.

Cracked Egg: I believe the cracked egg glyph at the bottom is just to show you've "figured out" the puzzle of 100% completion, raining, 3 a.m., etc.

The UFO: I think the UFO is supposed to represent the other two UFO's, leaving the player to hopefully figure out where they are using the lights on the FZ elevator, and the arrows leading to the arrow at the HC.

finally

The Jet Pack: The key to obtaining the Jetpack, at least to me, Omega.

The reasons for this are, Omega has tie ins with The Truth from San Andreas, Including only giving a mission to Franklin, who has tie-ins with CJ. (Both being from Grove Street territory.)
This theory is strengthened by the fact that some people have trudged up some game files that imply an extra mission with Omega, in which a phone call will trigger him to parachute to you. This leads me to believe that there is some trigger, be it calling a certain number, or being at a certain place, at a certain time and getting called. At which point Omega will drop in, and give you a mission to steal the Jetpack from FZ, similar to the mission "Black Project" in San Andreas.
