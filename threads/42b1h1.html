Weird Spawn Locations for Michael

▲ 18 ptsu/UndeadOPJan 23, 20168 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Director Mode Scene & Spawn Testing

Hey guys. I'd like to bring up a subject that's been making me wonder for a while. So I know that a lot of people are interested in Trevor's spawn locations, but I think there might be better clues in Michael's.

So I just recently completed all the Epsilon missions on Xbox One, and ever since then I've had Michael spawning the same way at different locations. He has a white tank-top and blue boxers on, and he's sitting on the hood of his parked car. He'll then step down from the hood of his car and just look around. There's nothing strange about this; however, the places it occurs at are the strange parts.

I've had it happen, so far, at:

  • The hippie camp
  • A shop in Grapeseed
  • Under a bridge down the road from the alien silos in Sandy Shores

So, as you can see, these are pretty weird locations. I've never had this spawn happen to me until after I completed the Epsilon missions. I am not 100%, I kept all three protagonists alive, and I have not collected any of the Epsilon tracts yet.

Let me know if you guys have ever experienced this and/or what you think about it.

Kifflom, brother-brothers!
