I got into this mystery after pretty much everything that we know has been discovered so my perspective is tainted in a way, and I know many of you are the same way. Let's try to imagine this from a fresh start.

Obviously the mural is the starting point. From there, we might think that the cable car is the straight line down the depicted mountain and search the mountainside for clues, which would apparently turn up the glyphs.
Looking at two or three of the glyphs would eventually lead to the conditions needed atop Chilliad.
The next logical step to connect the viewing of the apparently holographic saucer to the "real" one above the hippy camp would be that there are glyphs on top of the "mountains" in the hippy camp. So why would we deduce from the clues we've found to go to maximum elevation? Just a hunch? Okay, I can accept that... But where to now?

Something that bothers me is that the hippy camp glyphs are the only ones needed to see the holographic saucer on Chilliad, but there are three other glyphs on the mountain that aren't really needed for the fake one. The real FIB UFO is just always there, no special conditions needed to view it, and there doesn't seem to be one single glyphic clue at all pertaining to the fort Z UFO. It seems like that one was only ever discovered by fluke.
What are we missing? There should be more clues out there, but what aren't we seeing? How can we expect to make it any further if we're not even sure how we got where we are? It seemed like we somehow found ourselves on third base without ever having been to second, so we don't know where home plate is...
