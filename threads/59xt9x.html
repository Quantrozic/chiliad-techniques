McKenzie Airfield Jack Sheepe logo: R-108 is a Soviet CB Radio

Documented under:Radio-Broadcast Subtext Concordance

Hi all. I've been scouring this subreddit for ages now, so I thought I'd sign up and try and contribute a little. I'm sure we are all aware of the Jack Sheepe logo at McKenzie Airfield and the whole segregate and rearrange aspect of it, where it can be anagrammed to "Jet Pack Confirmed Lies In His Grave".
Upon reading through some of the older posts on here regarding the meaning of R-108, a lot of people came to the conclusion it represented the run time in the movie Cockateer - indeed it is. But given the whole Jetpack Mystery, I undertook some research of my own to try and figure out what R-108 means. After some research, I found that R-108 is actually a type of FM (ultra short) CB Radio. So do you reckon this could be a reference to the CB radio hint we have on the radio antennas towards the mystery - I very much think so.

But CB also stands for Clucking Bell, of which the Festival location at Paleto Bay bears witness to a Moon Eclipse at a certain time - so whether this is a separate easter egg altogether or conjoined I have no idea.

Once again I apologise if this has already been found out. If it hasn't been found out to the extent of the radio.
