Won't be posting a picture of Doomsday Murals as can be seen in a quick google search but here's a thought I just had mulling over the Doomsday Murals.
In my opinion they aren't instructions. They are representations of our stories. 3 main characters and the player ourselves.

Yellow Top Row represents the player
Red Second Row reprsents Trevor
Green Third Row represents Franklin
Blue Fourth Row represents Michael

Symbols in Yellow Top Row (Us)

\- swirl of dots \- connecting paths made by players who believe their actions can lead to unlocking a secret ending or easter egg. like how the theories going around is to complete GTA main story by making every action perfect, following signs left by characters and dialogue, going to the right place at the right time. It looks like all the possibilities, only the "sun" symbol in the middle of the swirls is the correct path to unlock the "secret" ending. It could very well be a true method of unlocking secrets baked into the game engine but if not this symbol is kind of making fun of anyone who believes this theory
\- solar system \- a circular feedback loop of players in the community trying to find out the secrets of GTA V, where these theories revolve around a central idea. also more space related symbolism.
\- chilliad mural \- the infamous chilliad mystery which occupies the minds of players like myself from 2013 to the present day. i believe the chilliad mural itself is simply instructions to the 4 flying (/sunken) saucers
\- human with zig zag and arrow pointing right \- our progression on trying to understand the chilliad mural moving forward with time, with 3 dots in the zig zag as the years progress possibly representing 3 big updates related to chilliad, which were done as fan service. Specifically in GTA online with halloween alien update, alien egg in the 600th resupply mission and jetpack in doomsday heist. The alien egg and jetpack named in the data files were likely repurposed for these updates with the only thing, the alien glyphs HUD for some kind of flyable craft not being used (yet) in an update.
\- eye with some kind of > with a box \- I don't have a great theory with this just thinking its kind of the reverse of a following symbol (see below) where the eye is being observed. i.e. rockstar is watching us as we come up with theories, which would mean we are the eye, the observer? Perhaps related to securoserve, like our GTA online character using that as a boss. Rockstar saw us flock to GTA online over single player and repurposed their future DLCs for online rather than the abandoned Trevor secret agent thing.
\- hand with globe with swirls like satelites \- could represent us having the World Wide Web in the palm of our hands, to combine forces and tackle these hidden mysteries and easter eggs Rockstar left for us.
\- singularity atom flair with ufo below it \- based on that space engraving done by NASA incase we come in contact with aliens. reminds us how the central idea of the mystery is about space, aliens and UFO/IAPs but the theories flair out and branch off to things like infinity 8 killer, bigfoot, ghosts, spiders etc. Note the straight lines running through the singularity look like sound waves (see below) and the 2 circumference lines make a shape of a great UFO or a planet like saturn. It could be a solar system like earth circling the sun or the moon circling the earth with the second circumference being aliens circling earth - again tying into the community's interest in alien life or advanced technology
\- three half moons/reverse waves \- the symbol to the top right of the singularity flare, basically looks like a moon phase cut off from the top, but if you look at it inverted, like imagine below it is water and they spike up like half ))) waves, could represent that theory people had of a giant tsunami wave taking over los santos like in that movie poster and weird sunken continent map

\- shapes with swirls, halved circle, dotted circumference and a greater orbiting dot with arrow following \- super hard to give this symbol a short name, it's right above the left facing head profile. it begins to have a bit of the golden ratio in it (see symbol on the very right of the mural, explained below) but then it cut in half with a dotted circumference and then a greater circle or oval going around it with the dot and arrow behind. something is going around but I have no good explanation. to me it's just another space, scifi symbolism. nature, space, moons, shapes, its all related to our reality, to occult and to religion and superstition in some way. maybe it ties back to the other things here.
\- moon phases \- the beliefs of fans that particular phases of the moon and a time cycle were necessary to unlock a secret, related to swirl of dots, since this is related to doing the EXACT right thing, EVERY single action perfect in order to unlock the "true" ending. the very first moon symbol is just a circle "eclipse" and fans have been looking for a way to make an eclipse happen to trigger a certain event. again, space and occult symbolism.
\- "field of view" mesh forcefield and 4 arrows above it - i don't have a great theory here because if there were 3 arrows above and 1 below i could say "thats the alien ships, see the field is the skybox, 3 above, 1 below." but no, theres a field of view and 4 arrows above. \- "golden ratio" swirl \- a circle surrounded with swirls looking like that beautiful pattern in nature made by the golden ration like in flowers or shells, with arrow and diving lines like a clock inside the swirls. the arrow could be a spaceship heading towards the sun or a black hole, with time space spinning around. the dividing lines are like the divisions of a clock representing time. what does all this mean? it could just be more esoteric space symbolism. what can it specifically represent in our context? maybe how the community is swirling around this black hole, we are getting sucked in to this mystery in this world rockstar made for us, all paths lead to this place, some people are on the outside of the swirl (not invested in the mysteries) others, like you and me, are that tiny little arrow about to be pulled into a void, a realm, a final understanding. it's terrifying and beautiful. or it's nothing. the middle is either black or yellow depending on which version of the mural you are looking. the black circle looks like the void. the yellow circle would be the sun. we're either about to enter nothingness i.e. nothing comes of this mystery after all these years or we are going to be enlightened by a bright sun of knowledge. kifflom!

So it's basically a nod to "remember how you wanted to get inside Mt Chiliad by trying all these theories, but you're in here now lol"
\- lined circle of different fading thicknesses \- looks like an artistic depiction of a sun or sunset. more space imagery. could also be those shiny circular stickers they used to put on game boxes for "game of the year" or something like that, as thats how they kind of shined and reflected. IMO, represents the idea of 'this is a game'

In conclusion to the yellow section, I believe these symbols represent different ideas players have come up with to try to solve the Mt Chiliad mystery, with the mural, various paths, choices made by players, internet in our hands, observers being our community and rockstar witnessing our responses, alien theories, wave theories
