Ok, forgive me...I'm still trying to work this out in my head as I type, in hopes someone will be able to expand upon what I'm thinking:

Based on reading A LOT of theories regarding the mystery and stuff over the past few weeks, something dawned on me. There seems to be possible similarities presented between the sand glyph and the Chiliad mural. I didn't feel the need to post pics since I'm sure we're all familiar with the two. Just follow me for a moment...as always...I apologize if this has been covered elsewhere (I haven't come across this theory specifically):

The Sand Glyph is made up of: the rune laguz (meaning water, or body of water...I'm pretty sure it may be where we get the english word "Lagoon"), the "mountain" (per the file name) glyph with the "eye" above a box, and the WOW! radio signal.

The Chiliad Mural is made up of: lightning bolts (which some have hypothesized as being radio waves), a mountain outline with the "eye" above a box, and the squiggly lines at the bottom (which some have hypothesized as being water).

I think it's possible both are pointing us in the same direction or to a location. I'm trying to figure out how the lines and boxes on the Chiliad mural play into it because they are too deliberate. But that's why I'm putting it up here...in hopes it may spark an idea in someone else.

EDIT: Wait...even the hippy camp shares the same Water/Mountain with eye on top above a box (on the ledge)/Radio signal motif.

Some other things to consider:

-- We should consider the composition of the two as well. Why arrange the images in that deliberate of a manner? Most good art work (if that's what you want to consider the mural) tends to lead the eye around the piece...to one focal point.

-- Has anyone else noticed the slight break and line width change in the line leading from the egg to the eye on the mural? Why not just a continuous line or width like the rest of them? It's probably nothing, but just figured I'd mention it.
