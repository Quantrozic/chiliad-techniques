###Peyotes
We've also confirmed that different peyotes on different locations (e.g on Mt Chiliad, and the one in the city). The one in the city is for different land animals. We've also confirmed that you get different hallucinations for the 3 different characters in random orders. So far we've only tested with the peyote in the city.

Franklin hallucinates about "owned wild animals". We only tried it once with Franklin, and he hallucinated about being a Cow that one time. But the guy that guided us to the Peyote, also said that Franklin would hallucinate about being a Cat.

And Trevor hallucinates about different dog breeds. We didn't really recognize 2 of them, but one of them was a Pug.

And Michael is hallucinating about different wild animals. He hallucinates about being what is believed to be an Wolf (Michael indicates that he thinks he's an husky though.), A Cow, and an Coyote.
