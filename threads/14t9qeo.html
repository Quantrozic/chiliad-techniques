Has anyone else ever seen this Green Goo Event in Online before?

▲ 9 ptsu/FlarestrikerJul 7, 202314 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:String Table Cross-Locale Diffing

Happened to be fucking about in Online yesterday when I flew over Chiliad and saw a very strong green light emanate from the middle of Grapeseed. You could see it easily from the mountaintop, it out-glows everything else, even the purple-lit airstrip. When I arrived, I saw five big puddles of green goo leaking out of toxic waste barrels, surrounded by staff from Humane Labs.

Here's what I saw.

  • The people standing there have guns and warn you to not cross into their investigation scene. If you still do, they will attack you with guns.
  • The barrels leaking the goo and the vans at the spot have the Humane Labs logo on it (three overlapping eyes - one yellow, one red, one blue).
  • You can stand in the goo, it won't do anything to you.
  • Nothing else happens if you kill everyone. This is not a mission.
  • There are five puddles plus a white trailer. And if you look at it from above, they form an arrow... that points right at the path leading up to the Grapeseed Eye. Five Xs, anyone?
  • Time was circa 9PM on a Wednesday, waxing moon.

I tried to look this up on YouTube or find something about it on the Subreddit, but nothing pops up. Is this new DLC content?
