The main things I wanted to say are in the picture, but I also needed to say this; I honestly believe people are thinking about this way too hard. Especially with the tunnels. If you've ever played a source game and have gone under the map or have experience with mapping you'll know that textures are one-sided, the side you view them from is the only side you'll see them, which is why people can shoot through walls when they glitch into buildings. If there was a tunnel, there would be textures on every angle, and anything under the map would be visible from one angle or another, and people have been under the map and through the code and found no such ones, but can easily view the interior for shops and other textured surfaces.
I loaded up GMod to get an example
As you can see, the nearby walls are see through because the texture is one sided, but the others are still visible, this is how they easily do textures in underground, and they did this in GTAIV and GTA:SA, the only way they could hide this is if they used occlusion blocks, but this would be found easily by clipping through them or through everything else missing.

If a hacker went under the map and found a tunnel system, do you really think they wouldn't share it and brag about discovering it? If something was found under there by one of the many hackers, it would be well-known by now.

Also, the fact that it says "Come back when your story is complete" is obviously referring to the story campaign, and being so close to the mural do you not think they aren't related a single bit? It's a mural - not a map. Take your tinfoil hats off gentlemen, Rockstar has almost laughed themselves to death. This does not however mean there's no real mystery out there, there's a lot of things yet to be explained, but I just believe that the Mural should no longer be one of them.
