Purple Mural (My observations)

Anybody who’s been here a while will have seen the various posts I’ve made about this, and seen how one by one I’ve changed and adapted my perspective to try find what each icon actually represents on this mural in-game.

I am now at a point where I think I can provide enough help that somebody, who’s got 100% completion and/or plenty of hours play time on SM after the ending on option C, would be able to utilise this information to help us move forward on solving it.

So here goes.

This mural is based on karma, psychology and self realisation.

Before we start, I would like to note this mystery is specific to just Michael, but may be possible to complete with Franklin if you killed Michael, depending what you do after SM to build his karma back up (killing Michael is a bad karma option, killing Trevor instead may still make this possible but evidently more difficult)

Icon 1 - Transition Symbol - The symbol at the start of this mural represents a karmatic shift in time and events, in other words, it signifies the end of story mode, and that this mystery can only be completed afterwards. I spent any hours researching hundreds of similar symbols, and the combined meaning of what I found leads me to that presumption.

Icon 2 - Ying/Yang symbol - This symbols represents your balance of karma after the end transition, and indicates you have to play through the game perfectly balanced with your decisions, the fact this appears next to the transition symbol implies you need to continue to good deeds even after SM is completed, and continue to do so while playing through this mystery, i would say having all yoga activities done may also contribute to this, and I believe whichever way you do it, wether you do yoga before SM completion , or after, it allows you to build up the correct karma for Michael regardless of you game choices, over time of course, not just from one session of yoga.

Icon 3 & 4 - The Keyhole and 3 tier symbol - okay these two took a long while to research and realise what their meaning was, They are actually one in the same, Let’s start with the 3 tier cake type icon, this as far as I can find, signifies financial growth and development, “starting from
the bottom, getting to the top” type of thing, and it focuses solely on implying you need to buy each business. the bottom piece being the biggest because it’s the easiest business, and the top being the golf club as that’s the hardest to purchase. The key pairs with this because it is an instruction that having met the previous icon requirements, and owning all relevant businesses with Michael unlocks the next step, which is by far the most complex one of this whole mural.

It’s worth noting, these murals are structured as a timeline of preset events, if they share the same space on the mural, they generally are one in the same or apart of the same situation or step.

Icon 5 - Karma and Psychology Icon - Alright, this one right here, has by far taken me the longest to research, I have read hundreds of articles about the psychology behind karma, and how one specific brain function can cover numerous outcomes with different end results. Depending on your human perspective, but since were doing this with Michael, it’s obvious that this is a purposeful way for Michael to exercise his demons and move past them, the letters on the brain of this go from A-J, and of you split them into groups of 3, they actually cover specific parts of the brains emotional functions. I found a simplified model of a brain, which breaks down what each of these are, which are as follows:

Front of the brain (Letters H&J) correspond to the brain functions, Motor Control, Concentration, Planning, problem solving, Speech and smell. Obviously things like smell are redundant as they are not a feature in this game, which is why that section compared to the real life model does not have a letter in it. H and J specifically focus on planning, problem solving and speech.

Middle of the brain - The letter I - this corresponds to the brains hearing function, and implies this one will require you to listen.

Letters G, B and C which are just adjacent from the middle of the brain, correspond to touch and pressure, taste and body awareness, it could also be said facial recognition could also be apart of this category, since it shares roughly the same positional alignment as G and C.

A and D correspond to language and reading, and also overlap a little into the vision section of the brains functions. So I would say all those 3 things combined are what these letters represent.

E and F are a combination of Vision and Awareness functions found at then lower back of the brain.

All in all it’s evident these letters represent choices, choices that happen in an order and require specific judgement from each corresponding section of the brain to count it as a “correct” decision on the players part. Which specific random events or choices these letters represent are still a mystery to me, as there’s only letters A-J but a shit ton of programmed random events and choices in the game, however if you pair the letters with the brain functions, I guess you could deduce which individual events they are.

This mystery cannot be progressed until this step is completed, which is why it’s centred in the middle of the mural and why it blocks the next steps in the timeline.its worth noting that the halo type structure around the head implies an “outreach” perspective, in other words it’s telling you to explore to find those specific events to utilise those brain functions with Michael.

Okay so now we’ve focused on the first half which are the steps to activate the last part of this mural, let’s get onto those last few symbols.

The last ones were very difficult for me to understand their meaning in the game, even though visually they are obvious to understand.

Icon 6 & 7, The weighing scale and the brain - these sharing the same space represents Law and Justice, and balance, all in one, I’m not 100% sure what this means in the game itself, but given there’s an electric symbol next to itI would presume it has something to do with being at a bank, or a police station and using the stun gun to trigger something. Which would allow the next steps to be possible.

Symbols 8, 9 and 10 - The stairs, the maze, and the frequency, okay so on the last icons I believed you had to do something relating to law and justice to trigger the next step, these 3 combined imply after that step something will happen, idk of its a stair case opening, or the maze bank building itself moves round or if it’s just telling you to go around that general area looking for a recently activated opening or random event, which has happened due to a transition you triggered on the previous step, it’s basically saying you need to search that area for an opening. Or something to activate t he next step.

Symbol 11 - Psi Symbol - this was discovered long ago to be Dr. Friedlander symbol, and in this instance, I believe it shows We need to have all his missions completed in order for the next bit to be possible, as I genuinely believe he is the sole end focus of this mystery given it’s psychological and karmatic structure.

Symbols 12, 13, and 14 - The Eye, the hatch, and the end. Okay providing you pieced together everything else, these last 3 are one in the same, the eye is telling you to look and observe, as shown by the eyelash type lines above it, all other “all seeing eye” representations are suggested to look from above, this one is a direct view indication, either in first person or just in general looking around on the ground, the weird box thing is actually a hatch, it took me a while to figure that out, and it implies the aforementioned hatch raises from the ground, and as far as I can tell, there are only two hatches worth noting in the entire game which could correlate to this mystery, and they can be found directly round the corner from the textile city mural which points directly to maze bank tower, there is one on either side of the road and they are the only ones like that on the entire game, and conveniently sized and identifiable with yellow/black lines round the edges and are big enough to literally walk down into them if they were open. I believe these are what open after all previous steps, and are where we need to go to conclude this mystery, what the conclusion is I have no idea, but there will definitely be some sort of cutscene or transition to a location not otherwise available in the game, as shown by the squiggly line symbol at the end of the mural.

Combining this with the textile city mural, it is evident playing story mode properly with Michael, killing a bare minimum, and making the right choices to make him a better person, is ultimately the goal and key to this mystery.

A majority of this may be inaccurate or can be proven to not be true, but from my own observations of playing for many thousands of hours, and doing a lot of research, I really think this post may help somebody more progressed than I am actually be able to compete this mystery.

Thank you for reading and I’d appreciate feedback on what you guys think of my observations and research findings. Or any additional info you can provide to help fill any gaps in what I’m saying timeline wise.
