The First 10 Missions: Mission Order, Golden Path, Gold/Red vehicles, etc.

Documented under:Mission Order / Narrative Flow Analysis ?Vehicle Spawn-Table Auditing ?

Hello Everyone, For the last week I’ve been playing around with the first 10 missions with recent posts in mind. I think I have found some interesting stuff that goes with some old ideas, so I am going to share it all here in case it inspires someone.

First off, I like the idea that the in-game time is a hint to the order that missions should be played. I think the first gold/red car switch in C*omplications* is a hint to that. Following that theory, I came up with the following order of the missions and early game activities.

1. Franklin and Lamar \- Saturday 8:00

\-Get a Haircut

2. Repossession – Saturday after haircut (done around 15:00)

\-Pulling Favors (Done around 17:00 – 18:00)

\-Paparazzo

\-Shift Work

3. Complications – Sunday 8:00 (Should start around 4:00 after side missions, Time shifts forward, and you get the golden car in cutscene)

\-Take back Jimmy’s BJXL and steal the other one that spawns after mission

4. Chop – Sunday Evening

5. Father/Son – Monday 9:00 (Time shifts forward)

\-Respray Amanda’s Car Matte Yellow for $1500

6. Long Stretch – Start Monday between 19:00 and 20:00 (Ends Tuesday 1:00)

\-Buy gold tint for $1000

7. Marriage Counseling \- Start Tuesday between 1:00 and 8:00 (time shifts forward to Tuesday 11:00 after mission)

\-Exercising Demons

\-Grass Roots – Michael

8. Daddy’s Little Girl – Wednesday 9:00 (Time Shifts forward)

\-Choose yellow race bike and beat jimmy to pier for golden bike to appear after mission

\-Therapist Visit

9. Friend Request – Thursday 9:00 (Start after 0:00)

10. The Jewel Store Heist – Friday 9:00 (Take a cab to mission after 0:00 to fast forward time)

Here are some other random notes related to each mission:

Repossession

· The unique gold schwartzer parked in back at start

· A song is scripted to play if you shoot the guy off bike before he gets away

Complications

· After multiple playthroughs the only car that consistently respawns in the dealership afterwards is the gold BJXL that takes the place of the red one that was there during the mission. This car also has a red tint on the carbon roof that seems unique.

· Jimmy posts about getting another BJXL on life invader, so I stole both and put them in Michael’s garage.

Chop

· I have this mission at this spot in the order because it needs to be done after complications to trigger different dialogue from Franklin and before Father/Son to have The Long Stretch become available Tuesday night.

Father/Son

· The main reason that I chose to spray paint the car matte yellow over gold is because it costs $1000. Primary and Secondary combined costs $1500

· Gold Shower neon Lights

· “Invest in the red, it is in your interest” billboard.

· Respraying the car is the only option that has unique audio. “Mom was never big on the red”

· Jimmy sometimes says “take your time, you want to do this right” while inside LSC

The Long Stretch

· Must start between 19:00 and 20:00 because the mission is weird with time. If you start before 19:00 the time will move back a whole day after the mission. If you start during the 19:00 hour the time will move forward during the mission, but it will move back to the correct time afterwards. I think the fact that the time is frozen for a good portion of the mission shows that exact time during missions is unimportant.

· If you do the mission order above, this mission should appear right after the completion of father/son. If not, this mission takes longer to activate which resulted in a long period of time with no missions to do.

· Lamar always makes sure you have $1500 even if you don’t need to buy anything at the gun store. Both the shotgun and the gold tint cost $1000. Every time I’ve done the mission, I already had the shotgun and bought the flashlight and gold tint. This leaves franklin with $28.

· Mission seems Foggy at night

Marriage Counseling

· In my last play though when started with franklin, he says the line “This n\\\\ knows what time it is” after pulling the house down. Probably nothing but caught my attention because it was the first time I got the timing right.

· The weather seems foggy/hazy no matter what after the mission and calling Lester. Could be a sign that this mission comes after The Long Stretch. The mission itself definitely seemed foggier than normal when I played it in my last playthrough the morning after doing The Long Stretch. Haven’t tested enough to confirm though.

Daddy’s Little Girl

· Another mission with Gold / Red vehicles. In my playthroughs a red bike always spawned after the mission unless I chose the yellow race bike and won the race, then a gold/yellow bike would spawn. Other scenarios I tried was winning the race on the red and black bikes and losing the race to Jimmy on the yellow race bike. All resulted in a red bike spawning. I haven’t tried with any of the other bikes yet.

· I believe you are supposed to do the therapist mission right after this mission. Mainly because you can still see the yacht in the background and the dialogue matches up. Maybe you are supposed to ride the golden bike there.

· I put this mission before friend request mainly because if you do the therapist session after friend request it talks about meeting back up with Trevor which makes no sense at this point in the game.

Friend Request

· Nothing much for this mission, other than the game tries to push you to go out the front door on the way out.

Update: I did another run-through just to confirm everything, it turns out that simply beating Jimmy to the pier on the yellow bike doesn't spawn the golden bike at the end. I have definitely had it spawn, but I'm only getting the red bike this time through. I'm thinking it might be because I didn't do the first epsilon mission. I will keep playing around with it.

Update 2: I still haven't figured out how to make the gold bike spawn on the pier again after Daddy's Little Girl. I'm not going to move on to the next 10 missions until we figure out what triggers it. Although I am getting a bit tired of these missions LOL. Also, Im not 100% convinced that mission timing matters yet so Im going to see if different mission orders spawn the bike.
