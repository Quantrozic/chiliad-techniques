Hey everyone!

So in my last post I mentioned that I had a golden bicycle spawn at the end of the pier after the mission Daddy's Little Girl. I wish I had taken a video of it because after probably another 10 or so play throughs I haven't been able to get it to spawn again, instead a red bicycle spawns.

Now when I got the golden bike to spawn the first time, I used the neon yellow racing bike and beat Jimmy to the pier. However, it really feels like you're supposed to pick the black or red bike and let Jimmy beat you to the end of the pier. There are two main reasons for this: 1) According to GTA Wiki, no matter what Jimmy complains later on that Michael still hasn't bought him a TV even if Jimmy lost the race and the bet for a new TV. 2) if you let Jimmy win the race to the pier, Michael's bicycle is featured prominently in the background of the cutscene. This is not the case if Michael win's the race. You can replay the mission to confirm this for yourself. This makes me wonder if Michael's bicycle is meant to switch from red to gold in the background of this cutscene, similar to the car in Simeon's lot in Complications.

I propose we all put our head's together and figure out how to get this gold bicycle to spawn. I'm thinking we could set something up where we log exactly how we play the first 10 or so missions. That way maybe we could figure it out by a process of elimination. I have 3 ideas for what could trigger the spawn: mission order/timing, karma/violence levels, or a small detail (such as how you mod Amanda's car in Father/Son). What do you all think?
