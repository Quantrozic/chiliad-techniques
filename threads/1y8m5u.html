Glow-in-the-dark Blue Goo found on Mt Chiliad

▲ 16 ptsu/LlamaGuy69Feb 18, 201412 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Shadow Tracking

I remember that way back in September somebody on GTA Forums made a small post about finding blue goo on Chiliad that only appeared at night. Here is the image of the glow-in-the-dark blue goo taken from that post (http://i.imgur.com/KRNfJzT.png). One problem is that the original poster failed to mention the location where he found the goo. I believe that this blue goo could be a clue that tells us that the symbols found in Paleto Bay are significant to the mystery. The symbols found at the RON gas station in Paleto Bay are painted with the same exact blue goo that's found splattered on Mt Chiliad. (http://i.imgur.com/3jZmtMM.jpg). Given this information, I think its safe to say that the symbols are indeed part of the Chiliad Mystery, and not just a weird signature of some kind left by a designer. What are your current thoughts on the meaning of these symbols?....and has anybody found the location of this mysterious blue goo on the mountain?

EDIT: Could possibly be debunked as some kind of lighting glitch, check RedEydFcd's comment.
