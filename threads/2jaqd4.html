Three lights above Hippie Camp observation deck

▲ 16 ptsu/ERich2010Oct 15, 20148 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Tide & Water-Level Observation ?

hey all, so i'm new to the game, but have seen this subreddit before through links from other threads. anyway, i just started playing (360) and saw something strange when i was playing as Trevor.

i had just finished talking to whoever that large older lady is that sends you info on people to find for the first time. it was night time, and i started moving towards the next mission. i saw these three big stars forming a triangle in the sky, and noticed they were actually "moving" unlike the other stars (they were stationary, but just closer than the other stars; a matter of perspective.)

at this point, i remembered this subreddit and got kinda excited, so i made my way towards them. i started climbing a hill to get underneath them, but they disappeared when i got close enough. there was a radio tower in the distance whose lights were off, and right as the three lights disappeared, the lights on the radio tower turned on.

i looked around (since i was mostly looking at the sky) and found an observation deck. after like an hour of digging through this subreddit/wiki/etc, i've determined this to be the observation deck above the Hippie Camp (the painted ground was below it, and there was a plaque dedicated to Mt Chiliad).

i looked through the telescope things and didnt see anything. immediately after, i was mauled by a fucking puma or something and died. i dont think that has anything to do with this though, it just kinda sucked.

i'm nowhere near completing the game, as is a generally agreed upon prerequisite. as i said, i just got control of Trevor. also, im pretty sure it wasn't raining. but i swear, there were three low hovering lights (maybe three stories above the observation deck), and the radio tower lights came on as soon as these lights disappeared.

i've scoured this subreddit/other forums and haven't seen anyone mention anything similar. but it seems strange that these lights would lead me to exactly the right location if it were actually just a glitch.
