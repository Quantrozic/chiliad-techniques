Possible story paths & save game notes

▲ 0 ptsu/Hangar13Mar 17, 201423 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Route Theory ?

So I was entertaining the idea of another playthrough on single player. Already have a platinum on PS3 but before I go through again wanted to setup a plan. I went through the save game editor to see what different choices have already been found. Below is the list I came up with, anyone else have any other game changing decisions or thoughts on these?

SPOILERS BELOW

Heists
Approaches change missions/prep leading up to heist. Using different crew members affect cut and have the possibility to die (EX: norm in jewel store job) As far as I can tell there is no canon path that the story uses with any of these.

  • The Jewel Store Job
  • Agency Heist
  • The Port of LS Heist
  • The Paleto Score
  • The Big Score

Kill or Save
These are what interests me most, especially the options to kill that have no effect on missions.

  • AL Di Napoli (kill for gold medal)
  • Dr Frielander (news reports killed either way)
  • Ortega (dies either way)

Other points of interest
Options missing from save game editor (might be an unknown)

  • Kill or Save Abigail (sub parts)
  • Kill or save Beverly. (paparazzi)
  • Kill or save Dreyfuss (letter scraps)

Rampages are different than normal medals. No requirements listed so can be 100% but bronze.

Other notes on vehicles
Just random stuff while going through editor

  • Chiliad Cable car has a vehicles hash? Does this mean it can take damage?
  • Clown Van from trevors trip is a real car, can it be stolen/driven from S&F mission without blowing it up?
  • Train Freight cars are Empty, have a Lando/D-Rail container on them, or is a Grain Car. Anything else may be an actual container ontop of car. Can those be opened/stolen with cargobob?
  • Barracks Semi - Spawns only in Fort Z. Is there any special cargo to pull with this?
  • Humane Labs has their own box truck. Is anything special in the back?
  • FIB Buffalo / Granger
