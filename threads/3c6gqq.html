From the beginning i saw many people tried to overlay the mural to almost every zone in the map.
I tried myself to understand better what People was trying to do.
But here we have some logical problem I think.

The mural image to use ?
The best image you can get came from game files. But proportion are completely different from the screenshot you can take in game.
Considering that if there's a mistery for everyone to be completed I'm pretty sure R* didn't wan't us to take image files, so i'm also sure this can't be used for overlay.
The most obvious to be used is the image you get in game with snapmatic. But here we may have proportion problem as well for example if you change Field of view. So probably the default FOV may be the right choice.

Another problem are the dimension of the overlay used on the map. We must consider that if the mural is too big the simple x squares may cover square miles. Giving us nearly no clue about location. After all, the meaning of a mural overlay is to find unknown locations

I don't know if the mural can bring some clue. In 2 years it didn't but who knows.

I'd like to know what you think about this problems.
