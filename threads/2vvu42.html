Possible foreshadowing in start-up screen?

▲ 0 ptsu/AltruistsConfirmedFeb 14, 20154 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Narrative Foreshadowing Audit

Hey guys. I just wanted to post this theory of mine that I've had for a while. The theory is that the start-up screen we get every time we load up our game is actually instructing us to certain missions in the game that have some significance to them, and possibly hidden secrets/easter eggs.

Here are the slides from the slideshow during start-up:

*Nervous Ron (Trevor on ATV holding a rifle; Ron next to Trevor's trailer with some type of radio transmitter)

*The Bureau Raid - Roof Entry option (Michael on Franklin's back-patio holding a stack of cash)

*Bail Bonds (Maude standing in front of her house; the guy on the newspaper she's holding is not one of the bail jumpers so it isn't directing to a specific bail bond)

*Friends Reunited (Wade standing in front of a building)

*Trevor Phillips Industries (Trevor standing in front of the Yellow Jack Inn)

*Casing the Jewel Store (Michael standing in Los Santos in a suit holding a gun)

*Chop (Franklin holding a baseball bat while walking an angry Chop)

*I'm not sure of this one (Michael standing on a hill that is overlooking Los Santos, wearing a suit and wielding a pistol)

*Franklin and Lamar (Simeon standing in front of his dealership)

*Daddy's Little Girl (girl in red bikini taking selfies on the beach by the pier)

*The Hotel Assassination (Franklin standing in front of his new house holding a baseball bat)

*I'm not sure about this one (random girl blowing a kiss on the streets of Los Santos)

*Chop (Franklin holding a gun and wearing a green bandanna)

*Shift Work, or any of Hao's street races (Hao standing in the middle of a street; it appears as if he's standing in Sandy Shores, though none of his street races take place in this part of the map)

*Casing the Jewel Store (Lester standing in his warehouse next to the heist blueprint for The Jewel Store Job)

*Did Somebody Say Yoga? (Amanda doing yoga in the backyard of Michael's mansion)

*Mr. Phillips (Trevor standing in front of the O'Neil house with a lighter and a jerry can)

*I'm not sure about this one (female cop arresting a girl with sunglasses in Los Santos)

*Dead Man Walking (Dave Norton standing in front of the Observatory talking on the phone)

So yeah, tell me guys what you think. It kind of makes sense to me, except those two images with the unknown female characters. Let me know what you think! KIFFLOM!
