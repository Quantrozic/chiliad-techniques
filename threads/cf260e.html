TL;DR

This isn't a true summary but what I think are the most interesting points of this post.

There are 2 stores on a corner of Little Seoul with a symbol reminiscent of the UFO symbol. The symbol is on the flaps and stores' signs. The flaps and the signs are on both sides of the corner. The signs also have Korean text that translates as "Big Sky" which, according to Wikipedia, can mean "two randomly flying bodies are very unlikely to collide, as the three-dimensional space is so large relative to the bodies." Whereas other store signs in Little Seoul are pretty much generic, such as "Sports shop" or "clothes shop".

Based on the interpretation of UFO symbols where 3 lines mean 3 AM, this symbol has 6 lines so 6 AM. At 6 AM the city lights turn off, sun light appears and shadows from the buildings appear. Around 8 AM a shadow whips by in the street next to the store. The shadow is from the crane atop the unfinished skyscraper. The shadow's course (the tip of the crane) is a straight line down the street.

FACT

There are 2 stores on a corner of Little Seoul (https://imgur.com/iD4JVbp) with a symbol reminiscent of the UFO symbol. The symbol is on the flaps and stores' signs (https://imgur.com/ViUt6Zt). The flaps and the signs are on both sides of the corner (https://imgur.com/O7i264T). The signs also have Korean text (https://imgur.com/xRfwCW1) that translates as "Big Sky" which, according to Wikipedia, can mean "two randomly flying bodies are very unlikely to collide, as the three-dimensional space is so large relative to the bodies." Whereas other store signs in Little Seoul are mostly generic, such as "Sports shop" or "clothes shop". I basically wondered around Little Seoul and used google translate to translate signs in Korean at random. I translated somewhere between 10 and 20 signs and when available they all translated to the same as their English counterpart (some signs are in both Korean and English). They are all generic and don't contain any jokes as could be expected from R\*. The sign with the symbol seems to be an exception as its meaning seems unusual.

According to Wikipedia), The Big Sky is a 1947 Western novel by A. B. Guthrie, Jr.

According to Wikipedia Big Sky may refer to a bunch of things.

The symbol is repeated extensively. It is on the signs and on the flaps, both on top and underneath of the flaps on both sides of the street.

Based on the interpretation of UFO symbols where 3 lines mean 3 AM, this symbol has 6 lines so 6 AM. At 6 AM in the passageway (https://imgur.com/m2MwyhA) that allows you to go behind the store, at 6 AM sharp, when facing the city, a strong beam of sunlight appears shining through a tree.

On top the building there is an Epsilon billboard (https://imgur.com/n04HHdG).

Around the back, in the courtyard, there are ladders all over the place allowing you to climb on to various rooves. The roof of the store has, to the best of my knowledge, a unique texture of tiles (https://imgur.com/TjzeYIR) and has a unique sound when you walk on it. It is at least true for the area. From a bird's eye view it stands out compared to other buildings.

The building has, in GTA online, a low budget apartment, with mystery themed elements (moon decoration, Celtic symbols book, etc.)

The store has a strangely placed light inside (https://imgur.com/mRCJBck) that goes off after other lights or never goes off, I can't remember. You may think this is just a reflection but either go there or watch the previously linked GTA o'clock video.

Around 8 AM a shadow whips by in the street adjacent to the store (https://imgur.com/moYCz45). The shadow is from the crane atop the unfinished skyscraper known as the Mile High Club (https://imgur.com/RIv8sxy). The crane can be reached by going up a ridiculously long ladder that could be seen as being similar the infinite ladder in the in-game movie "Capolavoro". Once on the crane you walk though pyramid shapes formed by the frame of the crane to reach the end from where you can see the shadow it casts. The shadow's course (the tip of the crane) is a straight line down the middle of the street. Using the crane could be an inconspicuous way of casting a precise shadow at a precise point on a precise course. Big sky could mean big skyscraper (this is what made me realise where the shadow was coming from). It could even be "big sky" instead of big skyscraper with the "scraper" part missing as the building is unfinished.

It may be relevant that all areas mentioned are accessible with ladders so no cheat codes are necessary.

Thoughts

The big sky theory is interesting as the currently known UFOs are stationary but a moving object at a specific time, in specific weather conditions could make it very hard to find which would be a condition to making a mystery that isn't easily solved.

Using shadows as an element of the mystery could also be interesting as it would also be hard to find and fits well into the astronomy and secret society theme of the mystery.
