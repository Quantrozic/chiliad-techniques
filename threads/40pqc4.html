I'm not sure if I found something or not...

▲ 0 ptsu/ReignOfTerrorJan 13, 201618 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:In-Game Phone Dialing Sweeps ?

I just had something VERY interesting happen while I was searching around the area of my last post about the portable generator. In the picture, I crashed it near the Marlowe Valley building in the northeast of the map. I decided to search this area in SP for another one of those trailers, but was unable to. Instead I started hearing church bells. I noted that the church bells coincide with the time on the phone. I found it pretty odd that Rockstar would take the time to code something so small. so I started searching around the building. Around the back side, I found a very large door, with an odd mark on it. Almost as if there was a service door inside of the larger door, but not large enough for that. Anyways, I waited near that door until 2:00 am and when the dual bells chimed, I tried to set off the EMP. However the bomb didn't go off even though it was my first time entering it. I then started walking around the building and reached a patio with what looked like a fireplace. As I stood in front of this fireplace I heard cars crashing in the background and someone yelling. I was being told to "put the weapon down" and when I turned to see what was happening, there were a group of Merryweather people storming my location. I noticed that it had just turned 3:00am on the smartphone when this happened. I killed the first 4 Merryweather people, walked over to their vehicle, and was immediately shotgunned in the back of the head by someone who I didn't see anywhere. I'm going to attempt to replicate this again when the time on the smartphone reaches 3:00am

EDIT: It happened again when I switched to GTA:O and went back to the building. Here's a picture I took with my phone right before the Merryweather people killed me. This time (obviously) I couldn't input the EMPDROP number, however I did try calling it. So it may just be the act of dialing that number, even if it doesn't "work" has something to do with this. From now on I will assume it has nothing to do with it, however, and I will stop dialing it to see if it happens without it.
