Hey guys, I've been lurking here for a while now and it seems things have unfortunately become pretty stagnant lately.

The big feeling at the moment seems to be the karma approach, but personally I cannot see this being the case. I feel like there is too much scope for error in it. Personally, I don't want to repeatedly go back to previous autosaves because I accidentally ran over a coyote, evading the police on the way to a mission? But hell, maybe it is!

Anyhow, I've been looking through a lot of previous posts on various easter egg related topics and the recurring element of finding the big easter eggs depends on what time it is (Thelma and Louise, FIB agents being at the satellite dishes etc). However, with the Chiliad UFO it depends upon the time, where you stand and the weather conditions. This got me thinking, perhaps the formula for finding the elusive jetpack depends on this and more? Maybe even to what day it is (In game) and which character you are.

This led me going back to the glyph's at the hippy and altruist camps. I noticed that while the hippy camps glyph's relate entirely to the formula for seeing the Chiliad UFO those at the altruist camp have no correlation. Altruist camp glyph's

Now these are all pretty biblical but the only clue I see here is SUN OF GOD which is a double entendre of Jesus and the sun itself. I'm not much of a religious person so Google was my friend here. But by some divine miracle the first phrase I entered was GIVE THANKS which led me to this page: http://www.biblegateway.com/blog/2012/04/hallelujah-he-is-risen-2/

Already within the name of that website was HE IS RISEN.. Which is coincidentally enough, a phrase used within the altruist camp glyph's. You can imagine my excitement, however that was nothing after I read the first few lines of Matthew 28.

After the Sabbath, at dawn on the first day of the week, Mary Magdalene and the other Mary came to look at the tomb. Look, there was a great earthquake, for an angel from the Lord came down from heaven.

Now if I'm to interpret 'an angel from the Lord came down from heaven' as one of the UFO's or aliens coming down from the sky then it makes some form of sense? Also the fact of it being a description of Easter morning was further enticement!

So if I'm going to take this at face value, have I just been given everything I was looking for in a single paragraph of text!? If so then my guess at the formula is:

Where

Presumably in the cave beneath the altruist camp.
Possibly the cave north of Palmer-Taylor Power Station or even a church?

When

A Sunday/Monday at 12 (The clock's hour hand being stuck at 12 in Paleto Bay would then have some relevance?) and also 12 being when the sun is highest in the sky adds relevance to the glyph HIS LIGHT SHINES ON US ALL.

Who

I'd presume Franklin as he's the one protagonist who cannot die in the story.
Possibly all three in order to give the three flowered glyph meaning?

Weather

I don't really have an idea for this but I guess sunny would be the logical approach?

Now I shall try every variant of this until I undoubtedly become disappointed again and start looking under rocks!

Edit: Tried each location including those in the comments singularly with Franklin in sunny and overcast conditions at both 8 and 12 on Sunday and Monday but no find yet unfortunately. My motorbike did vanish when I visited the Altruist cave on Sunday at 12 after walking inside and back out but it could simply be that it rolled down the hill when I wasn't looking so I wouldn't read too much into it.
