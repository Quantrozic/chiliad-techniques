I have made a video of things I've noticed while I searched for possible clues towards the mystery. Some things might be of interest to you.

  1. Entering Fort Zancudo Without Any Stars https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zbztyVgSnYk Simply drive there when Trevor is supposed to take Patricia Madrazo home. This only works directly after the Monkey Business mission, so you will have to re-do that again if you mess this one up. Don't stray too far from her. Also seems to work at the prison, but I couldn't find a way in.
  2. Saving Thelma & Louise https://youtu.be/zbztyVgSnYk?t=6m14s If you time it right you can snatch their car up using the Cargobob. Maybe you have to take them somewhere?
  3. The Dog That Sits By A Specific Grave Every Morning https://youtu.be/zbztyVgSnYk?t=7m35s Quite sad really. Poor doggy misses it's owner.
  4. Noisy Lamp In The Graveyard https://youtu.be/zbztyVgSnYk?t=9m24s Probably just a nice little detail, but still made me curious.
  5. "Safe" Buildings Aligned In Fort Zancudo https://youtu.be/zbztyVgSnYk?t=11m10s The fire tower points directly at the bunker.
  6. Two Hoots Falls Waterfall Secret Cave https://youtu.be/zbztyVgSnYk?t=12m42s Almost certainly just a lighting illusion, but it's looks like a cave entrance!
