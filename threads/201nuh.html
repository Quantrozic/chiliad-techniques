Here are some questions/thoughts I've been pondering lately... and although people may think "what's the point", I'm actually addressing something much larger:

Is the mural really an intricate, thought-out design... Or a brief, simple reference to the Chiliad UFO?

Let's analyze... for fun.

1. How was the mural drawn?

  • Literally, how did Rockstar draw the mural? I've thought about this countless times and I'm torn. Sometimes I think Photoshop (with the brush tool etc) to make it look worn.

*BUT - Other times it almost looks like magic marker... As if it was drawn on computer paper with a Sharpie and scanned. What do you guys think? (Don't worry, a "point" is coming haha).

2. Why are some lines uneven?

  • Ok. After mentioning the "magic marker" above, my thoughts turn to the uneven lines scattered throughout. NO, I'm not referring to every line on the mural (when I say 'uneven') - I'm referring to lines that look deliberately drawn in with a purpose. For example: Follow the jetpack line UP and look at the first left...

Can you see how a line is drawn OVER the line? To me, it looks like the 'connection' was added after the fact: on purpose.

Better example: Look at the bottom left 'x' (right by the UFO glyph). Look at the line attaching the 'x' to the rest of the mural lines.

To me, it undoubtedly looks like he entire 'x' branching off was completely drawn AFTER the line it connects to. Does that make sense? It's not a smooth line from 'x' to the center line (above the egg) - it looks like it's own separate piece.

So...

  • With all of this said, what do you guys think? Personally, when I look at the 'added' pieces and overlapping lines; analysis suggests the lines ARE intentional and have a purpose. The reason I'm going on and on about this is because at times, I tend to think the lines/x's are ambiguous or mean nothing.

However, I now truly believe the 'paths' do in fact mean something - and it's almost as if somebody meticulously used a ruler and marker to go piece by piece, make a point - and make it accurate.

Thoughts, suggestions? (Other than hate and thinking I'm losing my mind... Don't worry - I've already accepted that I have and my feelings will not be hurt lol).
