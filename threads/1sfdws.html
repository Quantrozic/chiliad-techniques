Some Questions After a Few Weeks of Game File Trawling

Hey all, so since we've had some very talented searchers making some neat tools for us, we've made some steam into understanding the UFO phenomena in Grand Theft Auto V.

Just a few questions to have linger for further study:

  • Have we found the interior for the abduction scene in the files? For that matter, have we found any of the textures or models? There are definitely some unique effects (light table, that green orb on the ceiling, the room itself, those circular doors under the table)
  • I noticed there is talk about a set of coordinates far under the middle of Ft. Zancudo (Around -120 I believe) so is there any means to find a trigger for this, or what it represents?
  • I've also recently come to understand that it is possible to view the Zancudo craft from Mt. Josiah, which might be why it is so much closer to the ground than the Salvation Mountain saucer. Is it possible to view the saucer above the hippie camp from anywhere else, including Mt. Chiliad? Is there a set of coordinates in the LOD file that point towards viewing the craft from anywhere else? Can the smaller telescopes found on viewing platforms be used to find the flying saucers? What are the coordinates for the yoga mat? (They should be easy as there are only a handful of places to do Yoga)
  • What are the filenames for the radio towers at the various places? Are they all uniform or are they unique? Is the name of the model of the dish that is skewed in the array different than the others?
  • Now that we have the names of the clue files, what sorts of tests have been done on the UFO phenomena regarding rain patterns and moon phases? Has anyone attempted to use the snow weather instead of the rain, since thunder seems to work for the Chiliad saucer anyways? For that matter, has the thunder weather been researched?
  • Are there strings for the unlockables after 100% completion? If so are there any odd files unlocked after this requirement is met? (IE the flying saucers, Bigfoot mission, t-shirt ETC)
  • Has anyone tried to spawn the Zancudo craft model anywhere else? I know it's in 20 parts but it could be possible, and has anyone found the way the EMP works on this craft? (I say EMP because that's the wording in the files and that's sort of what happens to the engine)
  • Has anyone also found the audio files for the Zancudo craft? Someone said they might be procedurally generated, which might make some headway into solving the anagram riddle. Bonus: Has anyone found the audio files for the abduction scene? Or for that matter, just the dialogue? I had some fun screwing around with the dialogue of the aliens and thought I got pretty close, can't do much more with the thumping heartbeat in the background and a low-quality recording.
  • Has anyone wondered if the Downtown UFO coordinate might be referencing the abduction scene in "Did Somebody Say Yoga?" I don't know much about the interiors in this game but is it possible it loads in there? They don't need any part of the inside of the spinning anus craft to have collisions or a backface so you may not be able to crash into it when going under the map?
  • Twice now I've had this happen, which makes me think there is something to it, but the last two times I went to see the Zancudo saucer and then fell down to look at the lights at 3AM, they did not show up. Is there a specific trick to getting them to spawn? (as in you have to be there before 1, or after 1, or you have to see the lights before the saucer) So it helps, those two times I went up and landed on the craft with the invincibility cheat and a helicopter, walked around and it threw me off. I opened my parachute to look at the bottom of the craft and after this, I just cut the chute and fall down until I hit the ground. Twice now, I have missed those lights. The red klaxon light still spins on, and it's 3:00 but I see nothing. There aren't even the smaller lights at all. :/
  • How the hell can Omega know and understand alien specifications? What is the name of the model of the little craft that moves around? And what is with the unused dialogue in Omega's file? DLC or maybe an unused space mission where you need to shoot a large asteroid? (This is purely fanservice)

Thanks everyone, I want to help but at this point all I have are my reasoning skills and the sets of evidence you all have laid out. Anything new helps.
Man, does this remind me of my days at the "Hunt for Bigfoot and Other Strange Phenomenon" forum haha those were the days. I would hope at least some of you were involved with that, talented bunch of men they were.
