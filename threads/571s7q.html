Possible new Morse code found!

Documented under:DTMF and Morse Tone DecodingMorse Code AnalysisPitch-Contour Morse ExtractionSpectrogram Pareidolia Cross-Check

Alright, so I decided to hop onto GTA for the first time in a while, and started out in sandy shores. I jacked one of those old dune buggy lookin cars, and started to make my way to the city. But I was going under one of the roads into a tunnel type thing at about here on the map, and when I came out of it I heard a strange beeping noise. I went in and out of it again and heard the same noise. At this point it was obvious it wasn't some off thing with my headphones, so I googled around a bit for anything related to Morse code and going in/out of that bridge/tunnel, and found nothing. (Note: I could just be horrible at googling, in which case I apologize for this post). So I whipped out Fraps and recorded it. Take a look for yourselves!


Here's a link to the enhanced audio file, I'm hoping someone can transcribe the message from either the sound or spectrograph of it! Just for some additional information, I tried it with one or two other cars and they didn't seem to work (though your results may vary), every station seemed to work, as long as the music volume was up, and sometimes exiting at the top would work, and others exiting at the bottom would. Kinda tricky to get consistently, but there must be something to it.

Once again, my apologies if this is old news or has been posted before, I checked everywhere and couldn't find anything on it, so I guess it's better to be sure than to be sorry!

*Re-posted because I didn't add a flair the first time around :p

Edit: Alot of people are saying it's simply interference added in to make the game more realistic, so I've moved the flair down to speculation. The last time I heard that sound in real life was probably over 5 years ago, and in my 254 hours playing this game I hadn't heard it once up until this point, so I'm sorry if I jumped to conclusions. That being said, I still think there might be more to this than "It's just a sound added to the game", and with something as big as the Chiliad mystery, I don't think it's the best decision to completely dismiss anything.

Edit 2: Alright, so I just hopped online and tried it. To my surprise, it didn't happen! That raises the question, if it was to add a touch of realism, why wouldn't it be in online like the GPS cutting out? I still think there's something to this yet.
