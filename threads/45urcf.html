Unrelated Easter Egg Solved on the Water Tanks!

Hey guys. I'm pretty sure I solved a new easter egg, although it's unrelated to the Chiliad Mystery. I thought you all would still enjoy it because it involves one of the big "mystery hot spots."

So today I stumbled across those tanks by the Alamo Sea with a bunch of hippie/alien graffiti on them. I started taking a weird interested in the strange sign next to them.

Here is the sign. Being a Chiliad Mystery hunter myself, I've always known about this sign and thought it was a reference to needing 88 MPH to time travel, especially since there are train tracks right behind said sign.

However, I then randomly noticed some graffiti out of the corner of my eye while wandering around. You see the little Pac-Man to the left? Well underneath it is the word "Bubble", and the two b's at the end are written like 8's to make the number 88. This was getting interesting.

It then dawned on me that that little symbol could be a pie graph and not Pac-Man. I pulled up a pie graph website on my computer and the first thing that came to mind was the 78 and 10. So I put 78 and 10 into the chart.

My mind was blown.

Not only does it come out to 88, but it has the exact same design as that graffiti. This was a clever little Easter egg and I love it just because of its minority compared to the other ones. It's small details like this that make me love this game even more.

Again, I know this is not helping us solve this mystery, but I still wanted to share this with you all. If you guys feels this post is unnecessary, then I will take it down. I just figured you guys would be interested in it. :)
