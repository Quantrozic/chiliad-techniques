The Space Docker thread

▲ 15 ptsu/Lauren_of_LoreSep 25, 201313 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:SSTV (Slow-Scan TV) Signal Decoding

Okay so a few things are already known about the space docker:

  • It's unlocked by collecting all the UFO parts
  • It has unique "honks" which sound a lot like toy space guns going off
  • One of these honks which is rarer than the others (you literally have to honk about 100 times before you hear it) is a series of high pitched squeals and sounds almost like some sort of data transmission?
  • It has green headlights

That's about all that's known about it. You get it from Omega, in his shed, after putting together the last piece of the little UFO. I've never done the collection mission myself but I have seen the cutscene, and he doesn't seem to explain anything about the space docker or what it's for.

If you think about its name "Space Docker", it suggests it might dock with something and it could have something to do with the UFOs. The only thing that makes me doubt that is the fact that the space docker doesn't respawn if you lose it.

Any ideas?
