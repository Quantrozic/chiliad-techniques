Not sure if this was ever done in the past, but I decided to go back and go through the GTA V manual to see if I spot anything. A couple things caught my eye.

  1. When viewing their interactive map on the digital manual, I noticed they highlighted all places of interest including stores to rob, tattoo shops, barber shops etc. One real random suspicious spot that stood out was Maze Bank Arena. Why would it be highlighted as a place of interest if nothing goes on there other then Fame or Shame auditions during SP. Also notice its location, La Puerta latin for The Door. Should we visit this area?
  2. On the Mr. Spoke Rental sign, notice the wheel that kind of looks like the spider webs everyone been seeing.
  3. Dr. Fielders logo has that infamous "U" I keep spotting everywhere. Refer to my last post.
  4. Finally on the Cult Stopper website I found it weird that LifeInvader was listed as a cult. Maybe we need more focus on there?
