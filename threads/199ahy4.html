What if the original intent of the Chiliad Mystery is to reference the Truth's unexplained plotlines in GTA: San Andreas?

▲ 16 ptsu/Latter-Pudding1029Jan 17, 20244 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Developer Meta-Intent & Interview ArchaeologyDeveloper-Interview Intent SynthesisManhunt-Universe Easter-Egg Cross-Reference ?

Think about the three main points of the mural. A UFO, an egg, and a jetpack. Now, let's all consider the notion that the jetpack in GTA: Online wasn't the primary intent of the reference in the drawing, the fact that the UFO's do appear in single player with very little explanation, and the egg being datamined early into the hunt for answers. Let's recount what the Truth made CJ do in the GTA: SA storyline.

"The Egg" was mentioned pretty early into the Truth's missions. When CJ stole that combine harvester, The Truth once remarked that "The karmic circle closes. All is as it should be, back to the egg." The cracked egg is the second main branch of the mural.

The jetpack, while many seem to be satisfied that it just became part of a DLC, was one of the mysteries that DIDN'T have an actual model in the game files when people started data mining early in the hunt. But this also may be related to another Truth mission in GTA: SA. CJ was tasked to steal a jetpack from the military, one he also ends up using to steal a mysterious green goo. Which leads me to the last unmentioned part of the main branches of the drawing.

The green goo has never been explained, though the implications of the area where it was taken from (Area 69 being a parody of Area 51, a facility closely associated to alien conspiracy theories), one can theorize that aliens are a possible source of this substance. Maybe that's what the UFO symbolizes?

I don't know. It could all be just coincidences but, to me they line up pretty well at least? What do you guys think?
