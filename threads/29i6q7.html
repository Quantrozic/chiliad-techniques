Calling An Ambulance After You Run A Ped Over

▲ 42 pts[deleted]Jun 30, 201443 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Karma / Pacifist Playthrough Protocol ?

I did it as Franklin during a squeaky-clean playthrough I'm experimenting with. Backed out of a parking lot, accidentally hit a ped, got out, called paramedics. When they arrived, I stood there while they pronounced him dead. I hit the talk function on the d-pad; Franklin said "Well that takes care of that, I guess" and "I did my best".

Additionally, if you give yourself up after catching a star instead of running, "Busted" is displayed in blue. While wanted, you'll notice your HUD flashes red/blue; you'd think this is to represent the police lights. Seems there's more to it than just that. Even running from the cops or giving yourself up is a red/blue decision.

When shooting at someone or otherwise attacking them, you'll usually notice a red X and an onscreen flash. But not when you're only putting down someone who is an immediate, unavoidable threat to you (doesn't matter if someone is firing at you - it only matters if they're successfully hitting you, and/or pursuing you at close range). In that instance, it's a white x, no flash.

The billboard at Vespucci that has a spaceship part on top of it depicts a vodka bottle branded "Nogo" with a red x. Its slogan? "X Marks The Shot".

Just a few observations to keep your gears turning in regards to how five years of game development easily achieves rabbit holes. Do not underestimate this game's brains. If you're especially familiar with R*'s productions, you probably already know that.
