PS5 version will end the mystery

▲ 77 ptsu/ChiliadWillNeverEndDec 31, 202035 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Old-Gen vs. New-Gen Version Diff Analysis ?

I know this is not what anyone wants to hear, including myself, but I am pretty sure we won't see the solution for the Chiliad mystery or any unlockable single player content until the PS5 expanded version comes out. Rockstar said they were going to make single player DLC in 2013 then they abandoned it to focus on GTA online, where they have progressively included clues, hints, unlockables, such as peyote/Bigfoot, doomsday heist murals, and now Cayo Perico monster and alien egg, that all refer to the Chiliad mystery. It only makes sense Rockstar would include whatever they had planned for the single player DLC content in the new expanded ps5 version. My bet is that version is where we will find our answers / finally solve the mystery. Think about it, If they don't put a jetpack and some cool alien post-game content in the PS5 version then it would be a disappointment so they probably will.
