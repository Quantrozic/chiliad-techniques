I can't help but notice the correlations between these 4 elements to the mystery...

jetpack glyph, zancudo ufo, altruist cult, trevor

Trevor has fundamentally insane behavior (like the Altruists), was a member of the military (Fort Zancudo), and is an expert pilot (jetpack glyph).

egg glyph, mt chiliad ufo, epsilon cult, michael

Michael has a lot of money, and has lived a life of lies and deception. Epsilon is founded on the idea of "turning your wallet to Epsilonism" in order to live a happy life. They are the only one of the three cults that don't actually believe in aliens, but instead use fabricated stories to fool people into giving them money. The Tract makes numerous mentions of an egg hatching, "and out of that egg came forth a whole world". Michael was 'reborn' after his death was faked. Epsilon is structured like a mountain (Mt. Chiliad), with Cris being the peak. The other two cults are more communal and structureless.

ship glyph, hippie camp ufo, children of the mountain, franklin

Franklin is young, relatively innocent, and seeks independence from his environment of gang violence. The Children of the Mountain are dedicated to peace and togetherness. They want to escape. Franklin specializes in vehicles, so the ship glyph correlates with that. The hippie camp contains a truck with a fake UFO attached to it. Franklin is also the one who receives the Space Docker.

All that I can conclude from all of this, is that the character you choose may be more important to this whole mystery than we originally thought. Perhaps there is some specific task that each character needs to perform for their respective cult and UFO location?
