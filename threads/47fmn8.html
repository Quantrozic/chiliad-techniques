I am going to explain what I think the 3 bottom figures of the mural mean (the egg, jet pack, and ufo).

Most of this is just based off of symbolism that I am connecting to the 3 main characters of the game, but from what I've found, it all makes a lot of sense.

UFO: http://www.auntyflo.com/dream-dictionary/ufo
A UFO symbolizes the idea of accomplishments in life. To be abducted by aliens on a UFO means that you acknowledge the setbacks in life and suggests the need to uncover new aspects of your life positive or negative. This relates to Michael because he was very successful and rich but he struggled with his family a lot. Michael's story during GTA 5 was dealing with making Michael realize that his family was falling apart and had the potential to fix it or let it fall apart.

EGG: http://visualblessings.blogspot.com/2011/04/symbolism-of-egg.html
The egg symbolizes birth and a hope for growth and new life. This relates to Franklin because he became a new part of Michael and Trevor's crew and all he wanted to do was get out of his previous life of selling drugs to gangsters. Him joining Michael started his new life and he grew and got rich throughout the game.

Jetpack: http://dreammoods.com/cgibin/dreamdictionarysearch.pl?method=exact&header=dreamsymbol&search=rocke
The jetpack symbolizes being propelled into a position of power, no one can get in your way, you will overcome all obstacles and limitations. This relates to Trevor because he gave no fucks about anything and murdered everyone in his path to get what he wanted.

This interpretation doesn't really solve anything but I do think it reinforces the idea that the jetpack, egg, and ufo are referring to the 3 characters and may not be a physical object in the game.
