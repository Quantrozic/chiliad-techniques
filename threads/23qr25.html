Using Content Creator To Create Scenarios/ Trigger Event/ Solve Mystery

▲ 1 ptsu/Agynt47Apr 23, 20140 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Narrative Theme Frequency Coding ?

I've been making a lot of missions lately and it's been making me think of narrative situations as I create them. An example is an assassination mission I made, designed for 1 vs. 1 capture, two hitmen competing for a hit. I'm doing a series of missions where you can (as an FBI stooge), help cover up parts of the Chilliad Mystery. (Mission where Omega is being busted in Davis Quartz, Epsilom Assassination, etc.).

I then got to thinking that perhaps Rock Star anticipated we would start to tell our own stories, and there could be more Easter eggs to be found.

During my Epsilom Hit capture mission "Killflom", when I was hiding in the Epsilom guys backyard, waiting for the Player 2 assassin to make his move (and hiding from the Epsilom members nine girlfriends), when I noticed that the Epsilom guy started talking about Cris Formage.

I've heard random NPC's reveal that they are Epsilom followers before, and many times they look like normal NPCs in nicer parts of town. But I wondered if Epsilom NPCs dialogue and habits and patterns have been documented very much, because there's no known way to trigger them or predict their appearance. Anyway, it made me wonder if these new NPC's can give any new clues.

We now have the ability to create situations in game with different factions. You can place Altruist cult members at the little cave and observe them.

You can put a woman on the altar and boot up the mission with current time and weather settings to see what they do during a thunderstorm.

You can create theoretical Full Moon parties, the place and way you design the party could be the difference between something happening or not. I've been creating 1 vs. 1 capture maps that last 30 minutes and investigating the properties and dialogue of the new NPCs we've been given.

Interesting idea... Even if nothing comes of it, it'll be fun for people to play a Chilliad Mystery themed set of capture missions. Also it'd be cool to see creativity being the key to unlocking more elements of the mystery.

Hopefully when Mission creator comes out it's even easier to create these extended universe stories. It's actually quite easy to shoot entire cutscenes with Capture Creator. An example is this Merryweather recruitment video, made mostly from the control given by rockstar's content creator.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2Dx-4cAfAyk

Anyway, just a reminder that we have a bunch of new abilities with the content creator.
