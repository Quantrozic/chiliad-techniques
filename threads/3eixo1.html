Could the mountain glyph be Old Man's Crack?

▲ 7 ptsu/JohnDangleJul 25, 201539 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Frame-Step Glyph Photography ?Symbolic Glyph Frequency Counting

Brothers!

Riding the tram the other day, looking around in 1st person to see if I could spy anything new. Caught a nice, close-up glimpse of the mountain glyph during the short arrival cutscene at the top, and it got me thinking again about that glyph and how we interpret it. That is, that it's simply a sign we need to go to the top of Mt. Chiliad.

For me, that step was kind of a no brainer, and with strict conditions like time and weather to see the UFO (we might not think they're that hard to figure out or meet, I have friends on R* SC playing regularly with zero knowledge of these easter eggs, who would never have had a clue about any glyphs or aliens until I asked if they follow this sub) I think they were counting us hunters being the ones finding the meat of the mystery. But why not include this little peek at the glyph on the way up, to maybe get some gears turning in peoples heads as they get up there, see the mural, and maybe feel like looking around more.

I think that was the intent of the placement of the glyph, but not necessarily how we should interpret the glyph itself. Anyways...

Just now I've come upon this picture of Old Man's Crack on the wiki, and it really struck me; if we regard the square on the top of the mural as the summit tramway station, we would do so again for the mountain glyph, right? So, if we're viewing the mountain from the bottom of the tramway, we can see the pattern of lines roughly matches the shape of Old Man's Crack. Hopefully, this crappy graphic will help demonstrate what I mean.

As you can see, it roughly lines up. I also put the line onto this big map which you can find in full and unsullied here

So what does it mean? Is it indicating Old Man's Crack will be what opens the mountain? Will it be the crack on the mural egg? Can we hasten the crackening?

If everything lines up so nice, why does the crack go in the opposite direction on the egg as from the glyph? Do the 7 switchbacks have any significance to anything? Probably not, but you know.

Maybe it's indicating we need to take Old Man's Crack down from the mountain, on the motor bikes, after seeing the UFO at 3am, considering it too has the 3 lines above the eye symbol like we used to find the UFO originally. Where we would go from there, I don't know.

Maybe follow the 10 minute red-line path in the park up north from the crack (forgive me for forgetting exactly where, I don't wanna say at Gordo unless I'm sure) or another path, like past Up-n-Atom.

Just wanted to start a conversation on the topic, I couldn't find any speculation on this which I was surprised to see, but I guess I'm probably bad at indexing. I'll be jumping on later, hopefully, to get some POV shots from different locations along the crack and of course footage of the tram ride up and the cutscene clearly showing the glyph. Kifflom, Kraff and Zaph' guide ye.
