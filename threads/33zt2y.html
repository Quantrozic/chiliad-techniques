Solid proof of FIB monitoring using "Maverick" helicopters

▲ 17 ptsu/trainwreck42oApr 27, 201512 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Time-Lapse Environmental Monitoring

Playing the online heist setup "Humane labs - Key Codes" you meet with an IAA agent who is being followed by the FIB and according to Agent 14 is "trying to get rid of her aerial tail" when you are driving to the meeting.

When she gets to the parking lot, every time there is a Maverick helicopter that is scripted to come from the southwest and fly up to Maze bank tower.

This is not a random NPC helicopter, it is scripted to happen everytime in this mission because this is the FIB aerial tail Agent 14 was talking about. As proof of this, if you snipe the chopper, you fail the mission. You can snipe anything else and nothing happens.
