Hear me out. I'm only discussing facts and logic here - this is not wishful thinking, denial, or baseless speculation.

Now, take a good look at the first shot of the jetpacks in flight:

https://i.imgur.com/TDTcDo2.jpg

That's the building on top of Mount Chiliad in the exact center. Just a few seconds after the protagonists are seen being strapped in, they're flying directly upward from that building.

Rockstar obviously knows we've been trying to open the doors next to the mural in that building this whole time. If no jetpack ever existed in the mountain before, then why would they choose this location for their big reveal?

I know, a lot of you will say they're just mocking us for going on a wild goose chase for 4 years. I sincerely doubt that Rockstar would intentionally alienate some of their most dedicated fans like that. If anything, that would only ensure a significant loss of future sales.

Isn't it far more likely that they're just laying it all out for us, since we've been at a dead end for so long?

This is from The Doomsday Heist video description: "You and your criminal crew are enlisted to un-tangle mysteries and eradicate threats spanning from the bustling streets of downtown Los Santos to the ocean floor and all the way to the inner depths of Mount Chiliad..."
