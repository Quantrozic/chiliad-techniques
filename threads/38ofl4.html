Ok, this one may be wayyyy out there. I used ASCII values to instead convert the original numbers on the sign into letters. So 9=TAB, 6=ACK, etc. Combining the result with all the letters on the board, one possible anagram is "Back and to the left." Another is "Back To The Island," a possible Lost reference? I have a strong feeling that "Back to the" is part of the message.
