Hey there, been lurking here for a week now and have a few theories.

Firstly, R* have done an excellent job of hiding this secret (if there is one) or leading us on (if there isn't), however - I feel users on this sub-reddit are giving them too much credit. Personally, I believe the logo at the top of the mural to be the 100% UFO - Lunar cycles, the sun, all those other theories I feel are crediting R* a tad too much, and are missing the fact that the glyph appears to resemble a UFO on top of mount chilliad where, once at 100%, there actually IS a UFO.

However, I still don't believe that this is the only egg, but maybe this is the initial trigger? The lines on the mural cannot be coincidental, as complex and random as they are, they must mean something. And I believe that the UFO on mount chilled is what sets all the gears in motion.

Another user on this reddit (I apologise I cannot seem to find the post or the user) posted a picture of what he believed to be a red line coming from the Mount Chilliad UFO directly to the maze bank. Maybe this is the next destination?

So here's the theory - maybe we have to see the UFO on mt.chilliad to trigger some event, and that to progress to the next eggs you need to travel from the UFO directly to a set location (Maze bank?). There may be other conditions too, like you have to arrive there without crashing/getting hurt, or in a specific vehicle.

I understand the markings on the maze bank are a bit dubious, but that doesn't discredit my theory - maybe we just have to travel somewhere within the city that better resembles a cracked egg? The red line from the Mt.Chilliad UFO was fairly vague.

While I'm curious as to what the recent developments with regards to having the characters "stay" at certain spots will bring, I'm not convinced - the fact the characters stay is not necessarily indicative that the camp is "special" - I've had characters stay in random spots in the city before. I believe the only reason that the characters seem to "stay" in the ufo camp is due to there not being a clear mapping-route for them to leave, and so they just stay still.
