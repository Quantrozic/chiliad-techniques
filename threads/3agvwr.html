So, first of all, let me just say that I'm not yet done looking into my findings, and don't have any solid conclusions yet, but it's nearly 5am where I live and I'm about to fall asleep on my keyboard, so I figured I'll just give you guys the raw info and you can look into it too. Always good to have another set of eyes, or 700 of them for that matter.

BEAR IN MIND THIS IS A VERY LONG READ, FEEL FREE TO SKIP TO THE TL;DR AT THE END.

Now, on to the actual topic:
I took upon myself to look into the tunnel lights after seeing a few posts about them over the week, for anyone interested, this is the series of lights if you read them from the direction of T01 to T02:
01000101010010011001000101010010011001000101010010011001010010001001000100110010010101000100110010010101100010011001000100100010010001100100101010001001100100101010001001100100101010001(0)
(I'll explain the last 0 later on)

This is the series with all half lit lights considered as off and the missing lights ignored(I'll get to that in a moment), totalling in 186 bits.
You might want to use this series for further research, as the series listed in the other posts here is missing a digit(Regardless of the last 0).

Ok, so all of this info is already known, I know.
But, did anyone notice yet that we're looking at a palindrome?
Or rather, almost a Palindrome.

I'll explain:
I noticed that the 2 parts of the tunnel that are built of brick and have the T01 and T02 entrances are completely palinomdrical to one another.
T01 is the sequence 1010010001001000, and;
T02 is the sequence 0001001000100101.

At this point I figured that if the entire sequence is a palindrome then the lights will most likely be debunked as texture reuse, so I started extending out the parallel sequences from T02 through to one end of the tunnel and from T01 to the other end.
What I found is that both sequences are completely palinomdrical, with the single exception of the end of(The one closer to T02) is actually missing a light, a 0 light. If you add that, both sequences are entirely palinomdrical.

Ok, this might be just a consequence of the tunnel being a little short to fill in another light, right?
Well, what about the sequence between T01 and T02 is it also palinomdrical?

Now here comes the interesting part, the 2 missing lights are directly next to the T01 wall, right?
If you just ignore them the sub-sequence between the 2 service doors also starts looking palinomdrical...
Until you spot exactly 2 mismatches in the sequence.
Both mismatches are a place where there are supposed to be a 000 and there are is only a 00.

This is really hard to put in words, but I can split up the series to reflect what I mean and you can go ahead and check it in game for yourself(Check the end of the post for the sequence split).

Another interesting point is that there are exactly those 2 mismatches in the sequence, which, to me, would imply that they have something to do with the 2 missing lights after T01, but who knows what that means if anything.

The final point of interest that I found is about the mismatch spot marked __ on the sequence below.
If you go with your character directly under the place where this light is missing, this is the exact spot on the map where the region indicator swaps from Mount Chiliad to Chiliad Mountain State Wilderness.
This is of course repeatable if you want to check it out, just go and move over between the 2 lights that are supposed to neighbor this one.

Lastly, I have tried a few character encoding systems to translate the binary, but none seem to fit.
Given the (0) addition, we are looking at either 188 or 186 bits, depending on whether we include the 2 missing lights as the mismatches or not.
186 is divisable by 6 and 188 is divisable by 4, meaning that the best guess for an encoding scheme would be one based on 4 or 6 bits, but a 4 bit scheme doesn't exist as 16 letters don't cover the alphabet, and 6 bit encoding(Such as BCD) yield no result.
Further research is required.
(And on that note, just to enlighten the other hunters, the ascii scheme is actually 7 bits long, not 8, 8 bits would be extended ascii, or UTF-8 could also be a possibility, but neigther will fit as the sequence isn't devisable by neither 7 nor 8).

And with that, I'll be going to sleep and once again count on you guys to figure something out.
Good night.

TL;DR:
There's definitely something up with the tunnel lights that is very much intended, this isn't a texture duplicate.
The tunnel lights form an almost perfect palindrome, but there are a few complications, as described by the split sequence below:
0100010101001001100100010101001001100100010101001001100*1010010001001000*100110010_010101000100110010__01010100010011001*0001001000100101*001100100101010001001100100101010001001100100101010001(0)

Italics are the T01 and T02 brick sections.
Bold 0 are places that are supposed to be "extras".
Bold _ are places that are missing a character to mirror.
(0) is the "Missing" light from the edge of the tunnel, required to mirror the other end.

(Add the last 0 and remove the 2 excess 0s(Or add them instead), and you are left with a perfect palindrome).

The spot in the tunnel where __ is is precisely the spot where the region transitions from Mount Chiliad to Chiliad Mountain State Wilderness.
