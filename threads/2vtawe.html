Theory test: Sacrificing at Altruist camp...

▲ 2 ptsu/AltruistsConfirmedFeb 13, 201510 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Network Resilience Theory Stress-Testing ?

Hey guys. I was bored today and decided to get back on the game and do some more theory-testing. The first theory I decided to test out was the theory that one of the characters need to sacrifice another character at the Altruist camp's sacrificial stone or fire. I did just this with every single combination, at both sacrificial places.

If you weren't aware of all the combinations, here's a quick list.

Michael can sacrifice: Amanda, Franklin, Jimmy, and Trevor

Franklin can sacrifice: Jimmy, Lamar, Michael, and Trevor

Trevor can sacrifice: Franklin, Jimmy, Lamar, and Michael

So, the only people that can be hung out with by all three characters are Michael, Franklin, Trevor, and Jimmy. I guess this is where the theory that Jimmy needs to be sacrificed spawned from.

Anyways, when I say I did every combination - I mean I did EVERY combination. Every two-way & three-way combination. So I got everybody to hang out with each other.

Not a lot of mystery-related dialogue, but during my sacrificing of Frank by Mike & Trevor, as soon as I killed Frank, Mike & Trevor started talking about how they made it in the business and Trevor started calling Frank "that kid." It was as if they were happy he was gone so they could talk... But then again, Franklin didn't actually die he was just injured.

Also interesting to note is that the first attempt I tried (Frank, Mike, and Trevor with T killing F & M at the same time) I made sure that all three were in their underpants outfit. (Mike doesn't have one, he was in his Bed outfit). When I went to pick up F & M, they were in different outfits. But during an earlier theory I tested with M, F, & T invading Fort Zancudo with the Stealth outfit on, they all kept their outfits on when I went to pick them up. So they cannot be hung out with their Underpants/Bed outfit on.

So yeah. This has been "debunked," unless we need to trigger something first before sacrificing someone.

Any other hang-out theories you guys have? KIFFLOM!
