The Mural & The Glyphs

▲ 10 ptsu/ellem52Jul 8, 201810 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Edge-Detection Glyph Tracing ?Frame-Step Glyph Photography ?

The Mural has 6 points (not including the Spaceship, Egg & Jetpack) and there are 6 glyphs. I am aware of a few theories regarding how the Mural is an overlay of Mt Chiliad. Something like a sock over the mountain. There’s also the idea that the Mural is a 2D representation for where the Glyphs are on Mt Chiliad - like if the Mural were inserted into the mountain.

In either of those are correct then the Glyph by Great Ocean Highway and Procopio Beach is NOT part of the Mural. We know there are other Glyphs on the map. Usually duplicates of whats on Mt Chiliad. This would mean there’s a “missing” Glyph. In the “sock” theory the Glyph should be closer to train yard. In the “2D” theory the Glyph should be further down the tramway, basically under the (west of) the mountain trail to the spaceship Glyph. Those locations would match the Mural better than the 6 locations we have currently.

Do you think there could be an unfound Glyph, or maybe like the hatch on the east side of Mt Chiliad - R* hasn’t revealed it yet? While they haven’t updated offline much, we did get 4 new murals in GTAO - so maybe?
