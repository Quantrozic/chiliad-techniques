Emperor Triggers Autosave

▲ 18 ptsu/DRUMIINATORJan 13, 201626 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:In-Game Phone Dialing SweepsSave-State A/B Trigger Testing ?

During my quick test with the emperor number I noticed that dialing it after loading a saved game triggers an auto save.

Changes all phone themes to Grey with a black background.

Change the theme and the background remains black but the trim/buttons change.

Dial again to reset the background, there will be no explosion.

Once again you can call the number, set the black background and cause the explosion.

Seems to enable/disable the background with each entry but the theme remains Grey until manually changed.

Trying to figure out the importance of activating the black phone and why it auto saves.

Note that upon loading this saved file I can kill, change characters, etc and never prompt an auto save.
