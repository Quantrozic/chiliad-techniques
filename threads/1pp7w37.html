What is sine-wave speech

Sine wave speech is a synthetic form of degraded speech created by replacing natural speech with 3 or 4 time-varying sinusoids that mimic the original frequency and amplitudes. It sounds like beeps initially but becomes intelligible as speech once listeners are primed (thank you AI)
