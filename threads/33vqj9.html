I replayed the "Did Somebody Say Yoga?" mission with LinGon's updated trainer running to record coordinates. This is the mission in which Michael is drugged, dragged into an alley surrounded by chimps, abducted, then dropped off. I recorded coordinates for the Alley, Probing (the scene in which you see the inside of a UFO and aliens), where they released him, and where he came to.

Alley: -20.2, -220.2, 45.3

Probing: 41.4, -779.8, 836.9

Drop: -900, 100.3, 880-ish

Final: -966.9, 309.3, 70.4

See how much he traveled and the altitude at which the UFO was located? The Z coordinate for the Probing is in the sky and far away from the alley.

Is this proof Michael was actually abducted and it wasn't just a hallucination? Or is it just where the interior for the cutscene is located and is at an irrelevant position?
