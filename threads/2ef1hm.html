Is it possible that the SpaceDocker is tied to LifeInvader?

▲ 2 ptsu/N1GHT666HAWKAug 24, 201414 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:In-Game Internet & Website Crawling

This is something that has bothered me for a while. The company LifeInvader uses the term "docking" a lot. The main instance that comes to mind is when you do the assassination mission for Lester, the one where Michael has to get into the L.I. building and plant an explosive on the new prototype phone. Later, when you're watching the dude present the phone on TV, he references being able to "dock" with each other. He also says "it looks like someone is trying to dock with [him]" when you call the phone (which inevitably explodes). I just think it's rather curious that really the only time the term "dock" shows up is with L.I. and then in SpaceDOCKER. Thoughts?
