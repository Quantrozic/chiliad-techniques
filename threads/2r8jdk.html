My interpretation of the Epsilon riddle...

I think that message may (somewhat) be referring to time-travel. Just think about it. It says "dream of a planet of fire and a dinosaur with a saddle." The part about fires and dinosaurs is talking about the time of the dinosaurs on Earth. Saddles (well, at least the one depicted in the drawing) were not invented back when dinosaurs were around, as far as I understand. So are they saying that it would be such a great feat to be able to ride something from the past...?
There are a lot of theories regarding the Chiliad Mystery with the Space Docker being involved. One of them is going "Back to the Future" style, and getting the Docker to 88 MPH. Once this is accomplished, we will be sent back in time to the version of San Andreas seen in the game, "GTA: San Andreas." Here, CJ will somehow award us with the jetpack he equips in that game. So, thus bringing CJ's jetpack to our time and using it will be like riding a dinosaur with a saddle: "riding something from the past."
Kind of a far-fetched theory, I know. But it could work. Plus, spaceships are mentioned in the riddle which I think ties this into the mystery even more.
But regarding the fruit tree...here's an interesting idea. So, we all should know the famous Portal quote, "the cake is a lie." Well, if you look deeper into that quote, it is actually using cake as a motivating perk to solve intricate puzzles. When you have "broken out" of the game's initial testing phase (from threat of death), you find scrawls on the innards of the testing center warning you that the "cake is a lie."
So, here, "fruit tree" is used in place of cake. I don't know why that is, it could have deeper meaning. But I think that the fruit tree is, like the cake in Portal, being used as a motivating perk to solve intricate puzzles, such as maybe the Chiliad Mystery?
