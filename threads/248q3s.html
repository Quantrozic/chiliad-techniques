Hey everyone! Sorry, but I'm thorough on my posts.

Haven't posted in awhile. I was once a PS3 100%er, now I am going for 100% on 360.

I just completed the mission where you rescue Lamar from the Sawmill... on the way back while taking Lamar home to LS, I crashed and stuck my car into a basin / drainage tunnel off Mt. Chiliad. I ended up in a peculiar drainage tunnel on the south side of the mount. During the mission, there is a bright yellow dotted line inside of this tunnel, and many parts of the line flash with bright yellow x's intermittently. The time in which you take Lamar home after the sawmill shootout is later in the afternoon, into the evening.

I returned to this location as Trevor, prior to the Union Depository heist. The drainage tunnel is located at the base of Mt. Chiliad, just north of a very circular peninsula on the northwest corner of Lake Zancudo. The "mysterious exploring music" that sometimes plays in the background while flying above places such as the Vinewood sign and the Altruist camp starts playing as soon as you fly above the round peninsula. The peninsula is home to a wrecked rowboat on its south shore as well.

I stayed in the tunnel from 2pm until 10pm, only to find that the glitch disappears at 9pm. I feel like it's probably a lighting glitch. However, there are very clearly x's that appear over and instead of the dotted line.
