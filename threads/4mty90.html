Hey guys. I've been browsing the sub-reddit's wiki, and one page really interested me - the Game Files page. There's some interesting things in here that we still never came to a definitive conclusion on. So I'm asking all OG code hunters what they all personally think about these things.

1.) Alien Runes / UFO HUD

What ever came of this? After some research, I saw some people saying it was faked/modded in, it was removed from next-gen versions of the game, or it's still there and we don't know what it's for. Can anyone confirm any of this?

2.) P_SECRET_WEAPON_02

Did we ever find this? Why is "prop_secret_weapon_crate01.xdr (textures - prop_chrome_dirty_02 and prop_wood_plank_05)" listed as one of the unfound props? I'm so confused about that. If there is a separate name for the secret weapon crate, why is the secret weapon itself just a crate? Where's the weapon itself?

3.) A51 Breakable Vent

This one really piques my interest. Why have we not found what we look like? This seems like a huge lead, especially since blast doors are involved. I understand that it says these textures/files/whatevers are also in GTA: San Andreas, but that doesn't mean they're just being reused. Rockstar could've just happened to name some things from this game the same way they did in that game. This seems extremely likely in my opinion since there are two Area 51-esque military bases in both games.

So yeah, do we have any new information or any new thoughts about these things?
