Maze in the purple mural

▲ 8 ptsu/NotarealMustacheAug 30, 202025 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Engine-Constant Threshold ExtractionRAGE Format Parity Auditing Against RDR2

I'm not sure if it has been mentioned before but as I was watching through TheEpicNate's video on this wonderful gaming mystery it occurred to me that within the purple mural there is a pretty clear reference to The Maze which is also featured in Westworld.

The maze is a representation of gaining consciousness / awareness. Actually most of the pictures on the purple mural have to do with the unlocking of higher consciousness. A keyhole to represent something locked. Not one but two examples of the dichotomy of man, good and evil, law and order, yin and yang.

It's also very interesting that they used peyote for the Bigfoot transformations, seeing as Peyote was historically used by indigenous people in rites that were intended to 'unlock the third eye'.. or in none hippy terms, to uncover new realms of consciousness.

I imagine some of this has been posted but these were things that stuck out to me.
