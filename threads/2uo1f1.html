Heya guys & gals.

Tempting as it is to write stuff like "omgz I haz solved it, big find" we've had enough let-downs for a lifetime via this subreddit so trying to keep my feet on the ground about this. But without further ado, here's what I've noticed that's got me semi-excited (although I'm already trying to poke holes in it myself):

With Trevor, and ONLY with Trevor, and ONLY if I use a chopper to reach the cablecar (Chiliad mountain end), as soon as I land (next to the two motox bikes) the character wheel blanks out, like it does when being chased by the cops or on a mission of some kind. This state persists until I takeoff and leave the immediate area.

Exciting, right? Except... and this is where you lovely people come in, to try to work out if this is 'something' or a glitch... is it possible that it's triggering remnants of the bounty hunter mission?

On this particular save file, which is at 100%, I'd lost out on the bounty hunter stuff, it all just seemed to disappear (known glitch when reaching 100% without completing all the side missions, they sometimes just flat out vanish). This is why I'm slightly wary of it being a significant find.

What I tested:

  • Tried various save files. 60% save, nothing happened. 98% save, 'ambient mission' triggered. Same on 100%.
  • Interesting to note that any police interaction en route seems to disable it.
  • Checked with Mikey / Franklin, nothing happened
  • I shot dead the bounty hunter escapee on my 60% save, perhaps this is why it only happens on the 98% / 100% (they are both different versions of the same game). HOWEVER, another very salient point is that I chose option B on the 60% save, option C on the 98 / 100% save.

WHAT I'D LIKE FROM YOU PEOPLE

Ok so I'd like people from the following groups to chopper their way as Trev to the top of Chiliad, land next to the two motox bikes, and see if their character wheel is active or blank...

  • people with 100%, who have completed all the bounty hunter missions, option C
  • people with 100%, who have completed all the bounty hunter missions, option B / A
  • people with any % who have completed all the bounty hunter missions.

If some, none or all of you can replicate my findings that will give us a much clearer answer on whether this is a glitch, or the fabled ambient mission we've all been searching for.

Fingers x'd!
