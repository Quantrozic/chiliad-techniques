[Discussion] Could the mural on Mt. Chiliad be taken from a movie or piece of pop culture?

▲ 0 pts[deleted]Sep 29, 20131 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Cross-Title Symbol Lexicon Reconciliation ?

I put up a post today on an event I came across while exploring the Altruist communications bunker and it got me thinking. I thought I was looking at something that related to the Mt. Chiliad mystery, but it was an homage to the movie Thelma and Louise. Could the mural itself be a big homage to a movie, book or something else in pop culture? I did some searching and was able to find a little something.

I tried to find something that resembled the mural from movie, books, news, art, anything. I found this painting by artist Daniel Martin Diaz that looks similar.

Here is Daniel Martin Diaz's painting. http://i.imgur.com/s3SVVck.jpg

Here is the mural we have all seen. I've enhanced the lines. http://i.imgur.com/CDkZDsE.jpg

Here is the overlay. http://i.imgur.com/MtXAu9m.jpg

Now, there are a lot of things that line up:

  1. The cross on the crown aligns with the center of the red eye.
  2. The eye in the painting aligns with the intersection of two major lines on the mural.
  3. The red X on the painting aligns with the far red X on the mural.
  4. The three symbols at the bottom of the painting line up with the images on the mural.
  5. The bottom most line of the mural aligns to the bottom of the painting.
  6. The cross on the right side of the painting lines up with an intersection on the mural.
  7. The mural itself is surrounded by a box. The mural would look just fine without that box around it. Yet it's there.

Here is Daniel Martin Diaz official website. The painting is there. http://danielmartindiaz.com/

And Wikipedia page https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Martin_Diaz

This caught my attention:
"The multi-platinum band P.O.D. commissioned Diaz to design and paint the cover of their self-titled album Payable on Death. The album went gold in four weeks and caused a controversy across the country due to the cover artwork Diaz created."

GTA games are known for being controversial. Diaz is a controversial artist. He has also done work for a well known band, so helping with a game wouldn't be unusual.

I don't know if Diaz is in the credits for the game or maybe someone at Rockstar was just inspired by him. Some more research is needed.

Lay it on me if I'm wrong on this one. Maybe the mural is taken from some other form of media. Thoughts?
