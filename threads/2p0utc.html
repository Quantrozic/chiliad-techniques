A different weird sound. Some research done, more needed!

Documented under:Beat-Grid Tempo Forensics

I have been lurking this sub on the daily, and haven’t seen anyone mention this strange sound I hear often.

I’ve noticed a strange chattering while flying in specific directions. the sound comes in even waves of chatter and no chatter. the sound lasts about a second, then quiet for a second, then another chattering sound, then quiet etc. Might be easier to hear with the radio off, until you first notice it. Now I can’t un-notice it!

Its almost like it leads somewhere. the sound can be followed, and easily lost. It can be heard in planes and helicopters, but since its easiest to maneuver a heli, thats my vehicle of choice for this yet unsolved mystery. If I hear the sound, and stay put, the sound goes on indefinitely. When I first hear this noise in a helicopter, I stop moving forward, can turn left or right, and the sound fades away, and returns when facing the right direction.

I have been able to recreate this sound. Take a helicopter to paleto bay, and face Chilliad, you should hear this chatter once you start ascending, and it goes away once you hit the top. I’ve heard it at the sandy shore airstrip, from the helicopter spawn spot. I think I was facing north.

I’ve heard it in many locations, facing various directions. This sound might form a grid, or map which I haven’t been able to nail down. Next time I play, I was thinking of trying to follow these sounds while wearing headphones to help pinpoint direction. I have noticed this noise for the better part of a year at this point. 100% is not needed. No specific character or timing or weather. Thoughts? Anyone else able to recreate?
