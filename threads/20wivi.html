I hiked up Mt. Chiliad by foot after following the trail from the lighthouse and doing yoga (at sunrise 6 AM) at Mt. Gordo as suggested by a poster on here a few days ago.

When I arrived at Mt. Chiliad I was about to just continue the trail (as it never really leads to the cable station on the mini map) but I went to the platform anyway and saw this guy. He stands around like he is looking for something, hears different sounds and shrug his shoulders like "now what do I do?" and he also greets the sky with his hand that resembles the "peace" symbol.

Video.

He's also holding a map of Los Santos but it looks like a smaller island compared to "our" map and seems to have a big lake in the area where Fort Zancudo would be and a island just off the coast. (Why would he need a map of the city on top of Chiliad anyway??)

I tried talking to him but M would not say anything. I bumped into him and he threatened to call the police so I bumped into him again and he ran towards the dead end trail beside the cable car station and fell off the side of the mountain.

I've never seen this guy (or other npc's) do this kind of thing before. Usally they just stand around with the phone or a pair of binoculars. Anyone else seen this guy or kind of behviour?
