So this sub is still dealing with meta karma posts on a regular basis. There are a number of karma theory supporters here and many more (including myself) who feel discussing the theory is not helping us solve the mystery.

I'm sure karma theorists are tired and frustrated by others dismissing their ideas and even condemning their participation. As a "karma denier," I feel the theory, because it isn't tied to clues like glyphs and murals, detracts from testable theories and divides us socially.

I think karma theorists would benefit from having their own sub where they can discuss and debate their theory in a supportive environment without having to wade through competing theories or receiving antagonism. I bet the mods here would even post a link in the sidebar directing people to this sub generating even more interest in the theory.

What do you all think? Do you support the karma theorists having their own sub?
