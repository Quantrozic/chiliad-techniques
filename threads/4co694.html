Strangely modified texture found in files. Odd text and a mirrored man in a window?

▲ 8 ptsu/denturedocelotMar 31, 201642 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Mural & Map Mirroring

Sorry about the strange title, but it's a bit hard to describe.

I'm still spending a good portion of my free time with my nose in the GTA V file structure. As I was sorting some saved texture files into appropriate directories, I noticed some very odd details in one of them.

This is the original texture.

The reason I saved this texture, originally, was the strange graffiti/text. When I went to sort it into a category, I noticed that there were two men in the window.

Here is a blow up of the text, and a reversed image. I made this image because I was having trouble reading the text.

Take my word, after looking at literally thousands of textures in this game, this just is not at all normal. The closest thing to images of actual humans, that you can find in the textures, are seemingly accidental. It's always some skewed reflection of a person on a glass surface, or something similar.

This might be a good time to mention that a large portion of the textures in the game files are actually photographs of real life locations. I have spent some time modding older game engines, so I'm not aware when this became a normal practice, but there is no doubt that a many of the textures in GTA V are actual photographs.

So when I saw the men in the window, I naturally assumed it was another "accidental human" pic that someone didn't edit out.

Then I took a closer look, and noticed another odd detail.

The men in the window were intentionally added to the image. If you look very closely, you'll notice that one of the men doesn't have a back, and that it is the same person, mirrored.

Look closer, and you can see the line that resulted from the images being pasted together.

Aside from the text, that I haven't yet been able to interpret, why would they intentionally add images of an actual person to an in game texture.

Could these an images of a developer added as a small Easter egg? Notice that he seems to be in the standard "actively using a computer" posture.

I am not prone to flights of fancy when it comes to things that are generally considered toasty, but since this texture that has obviously been manipulated in a very abnormal manor, I wonder if some of the other oddness in this image might be worth noting. (Look closely at the "reflections".)

All in all, I wanted to bring attention to this oddity. I appreciate any feedback, as I am a bit stumped.
