GTAO, Morse Code, in the tunnel under Ft. Zancudo

▲ 3 ptsu/D_TroxApr 23, 20155 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Morse Code Analysis

You know the tunnel under Ft. Zancudo? The yellow traffic dividers? The ones you want to hit every time you drive through. I noticed that in online the lines of dividers are missing pieces. If you drive through them fast you'll hear Morse code. You can also just look at them from the side and read them. It's short long short repeated three times. Which translates to: 'R R R'. Maybe it's someone adding a nod to Rockstar's name and logo. Or maybe if you say Rockstar three times in a row (like in beetlejuice) the jet pack will appear.
