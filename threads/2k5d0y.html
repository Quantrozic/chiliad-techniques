6
E

6
33
77
88

So assuming that we can look at it as a phone number we're dialling it becomes 6-33-77-88-55.
Couple of things jump out here, the first being that if all the letters are displayed as numeric characters we get repetition in numbers with the exception of 6, double 3/double 7 etc, so let's deconstruct the numbers and look at the pattern forming:
6
33(+27?)
77(+44)
88(+11)
55(-33)
So if we allow ourselves to assume that this is the pattern, or more appropriately assume that this is the pattern WITHOUT the +27 jump for the space between 6 and 33, and assume that it adds 11 to get back to 66 allowing the cycle to start around, we end up with a slightly different yet cyclical number pattern
6(6)
(-33)=33
(+44)=77
(+11)=88
(-33)=55
(Potentially(+11))=66
Making that if we carry the pattern over we see
66-33-77-88-55-66-33-77-88-55-66
And so on, but this is something that jumps out at me because other elements of the mystery and mythos centre around cycles, just as the recurrence of the number 5 (I know that'll happen with it being the fifth gta game), but also touches on ELF-after all, a frequency is a "cycle" of sound, and the cycles of weather/day/night/time that seem to bind the ufos and the conditions of their appearance are all working in a frequency with one another.
