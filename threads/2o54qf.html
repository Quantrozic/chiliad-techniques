Trying to turn New leaves, Getting ready to Wrap it up, Reuniting with the family.

▲ 10 ptsu/myinnertrevorDec 3, 201419 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Choice-Branch Outcome Tabulation ?

Yo yo. After being on a hunt like this with some very smart people, you begin to question everything and my detective skills have increased. Some people say this game is like a musical album, I say its more like a Choose Your own Adventure Book.

A couple of days ago, I was around 80%, I just did Mike's reunting the family mission and still had my FIB heist, rooftop option, to do. When going to do it I noticed Lester talking about turning New Leaves to Mike. So I began to think was I supposed to do this mission before reuniting the family. I looked in the guide book and noticed, they have the FIB heist and a mission called The Wrap Up before doing the Reuniting Mission. So I began to think should Mike wrap things up with the FIB before going to reunite with his family, luckily I had a 70% save file to go back to.

So I went back making sure this could be done, and it could. But when choosing the FIB heist option I decided to do the fireman approach. So when going to start the heist , I figure I'm going to hear Lester talk about New Leaves again, but he doesn't. He just talks about the heist and what Mike has to do, then Mike says the whole plan sounds idiotic, and Lester tells him, "Remember you choose this". So now I beginning to think something is up. The heist choices could be involved. Let me know what you think. Peace and Love. Cheers and Kifflom.
