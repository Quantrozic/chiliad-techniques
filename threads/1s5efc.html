Sand Glyph\Altruist Graffiti linked?

Alright, I know this has been done to death, but hear me out.

Sand Glyph (Image credit: Mojoe128):
http://i.imgur.com/c3KRZtB.png

4 parts to this, many possible meanings, but this is my take on it.

N - North, as in, head north.
Water Rune (Logr) - Near the water.
Mountain - Double meaning to this, but overall "Mountain"
Radio Signal (Wow! Signal) - Radio tower.

Now, as much as I hate to beat this dead horse, do some Yoga on the top of Mount Gordo. You'll notice you have a clear sight of 3 things: Water, Mountain and a Radio Tower. Not only that, but the starting camera shows Mt. Chiliad (with a winding trail leading up the observation deck similar to the glyph) on the left, and the radio tower on the right.

Also, I cannot confirm, but I definitely heard something not quite right while doing yoga. I first heard it and it stuck with me, because it sounded forced and not with the music. Could have been the alleged Wow! Signal previously mentioned, but there's definitely something different with the audio. It sounds like a hum of sorts, but not like a singing bowl like others have assumed in the past. (Come from a spiritual family, have heard many through my life.)

Now, this is the part I'm a bit stumped with, the Altruist Glyphs.

http://i.imgur.com/dnFk77y.png

If there's any order to them, this is what I believe it to be.

  • Sunrise\He is Risen!\His Light shines on us all: The sun rises on the east, same place where our yoga mat is.
  • See the eye: Look at the sun, the flare makes it look like an eye.
  • Embrace the Light: Embrace it, bathe in it, get sweaty and flexible in it.
  • Our path is lit!: There's a very obvious path on chiliad during the day that you see constantly while doing yoga. There is also a dirtbike pointing directly at the start of that path next to the radio tower.
  • We are his children!: Could mean the altruist camp, which if you were to follow the path from Gordo on a dirt bike without looking at the map, you would be 90% likely to get to the gates. There is only one crossroad in the direction you're heading that will take you in a different direction, and if you take the brighter and higher path, you will end up at the altruist camp.
  • Praise Him/Give Thanks: Could simply be hints to do yoga, or it could require you to slaughter the altruists.

As for the sun symbols, look the sun during the day. I did it with "Extra Sunny" weather, and I definitely saw an eye, as well as similarity to all the symbols over the course of the hours of 5am-12pm.

So far my thoughts are along the lines of dirt biking the trail to either Chiliad, Altruist camp, or watching the sun set on the far coast on the hill (north of the sonar docks) with a path and two "talking" (bugs) trees, or the island south west of it with the hidden hut (surrounded by trees, didn't know it existed until I flew out there) that has one entrance with a few kegs and a mattress, and a boarded up back room.

I've done the hike twice now (once stopping at chiliad), but I don't have the altruists killed on my save, so I need someone to test that out. The journey is a hell of a lot of fun, so you shouldn't feel like you're wasting your time too much.
