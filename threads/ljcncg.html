Red Gold and Green Theory + Shawn Fonteno's Instagram Post

▲ 42 ptsu/CreathianFeb 14, 202110 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Color Theory & HSV Analysis

Kifflom!

I wanted to share a connection I made after browsing the subreddit for a bit.

A while ago, this post was made on the subreddit, sharing an Instagram post from Franklin's VA. The map of Los Santos looked off, color-wise, to me, so I took it into Photoshop and started messing around with it. The map looked too blue, even for an image of the city at night. Folks also mentioned that Mount Chiliad's shadow (as well as other shadows) looked way too dark. This tipped me off to the fact that there might have been some color editing done to this image.

So, I messed with the saturation and ended up with this. Outside of Blue, the only colors visible on the map are Red, Gold, and Green. Part of me felt like this looked like a heat map, especially since the red areas are around the lights of the city more than anywhere else.

Further proof that color correction was done to this image comes when it's compared to what I assume is the same exact image that I found with a quick google search. Applying the same Saturation settings to both maps leads to very different results.
(Image posted by Franklin's VA is labeled as "Instagram Image")

Now I don't know for certain if the exact areas that are red/green/yellow actually mean anything since they seem to be pretty similar on both maps, though the colors green and gold hardly show up on the second image at all. What I am curious about is if this might help people pull anything from the Instragram image, since now we have evidence that suggests the image was altered in some way.

I am curious what locations are highlighted gold/green here if anyone can figure that out. Also, would it be possible to get an in-game screenshot of the entirety of Los Santos, with the term vision filter applied? I want to check to see if there's something either missing or not showing up as heat.
