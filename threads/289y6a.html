I was digging through the game files out of sheer boredom, just simply opening and exploring what I can since I'm in no way smart with the deeper side of file exploration, and then I came across some audio files that popped up after I had converted a series of in-game sound effects (horns, gunfire, that sort of stuff) into mp3s. And among them was this frankly out of place piece which I have taken the decency to put up on drop box for easier access. https://dl.dropboxusercontent.com/u/124191071/224SC.mp3

It's no song I've heard on the radio stations nor does it sound like it fits into anywhere else I've heard songs currently. At first I was simply going to close it but then after a while I heard the lyrics begin so I sat through it, it may just be my tinfoil hat syndrome at work but the lyrics sound awfully cryptic regarding the whole altruist/chiliad mystery we have on our hands. What with the lines about moon and sunlight.

Edit: I could be wrong, but this may be something that plays in hippy's earphones from time to time. I walked past some just now and I could hear music coming from one of them at least, whether it was this song or not though I am stumped on.

Edit 2: It turns out this is a song from the gamecube game F Zero GX, a profile theme for the character 'Jack Levin'. Whether this is intentional or a mistake on a dev's part is all up for debate.
