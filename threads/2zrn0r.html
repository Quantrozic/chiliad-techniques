The GTA V Mystery:

So recently i have started thinking of the mystery and also how to write a mystery & what must happen in the story to create
a mystery. Then i applied what i have learned to GTA V so far and here is what i have come up with and would like to share.

So the way i feel the mystery was written:

You must go back to the hype surrounding the game upon release for PS3, and XBOX 360. I remember the internet buzz
about the massive open world and R* hyped the open world and the massive amount of Activities you can do even join a cult.
So as a gamer i have got my game day of release turned it on and opened up the map to see what was in store for
me, i opened to a screen and it was black i had to explore to the map to open so i beat up a few people got some cash lost
the cops and hailed a cab to the other side of the map, i got out and the moment i saw the mountain i knew that wanted to
get to the top to see the view. Now R* knew the moment we saw the mountain we would go to the top and check out the view
from the View platform, that on the back of conviently left "Come back when your story is complete" which is left in plane
sight as a starting point the top of the mountain. We then find a mural that is left in a very obvious place were we would
know to find it again, but we don't quite understand the mural some X's lighting UFO's? EGG? Jetpack man? what does it all
mean, well our story is not complete yet so that makes sense, now after playing the storyline there are obvious hints at
Aliens,FIB conspricies, Hippies who love Aliens, Cults that are kinda fishy, a ski lift that passes past a glyph on the
mountain. All things that help us understand the mural a little more. So after see the Glyphs on the mountain and the
Glyphs at HC that basically tells 3 am in the rain and is a time to be some where but where well that was already given to
us in the very beginning "Come back when your story is complete". So to the top of mountain at 3 am when it rains. Then bam
there it is in all its glory a UFO but when we apporach it disappear the only information about the UFO is the FIB letters
on it. So intrigued about the ufos we go to the mural again but now its seems to make alot more since seeing the star type
thing at the top, the x's being glyphs, the lighting indicating it has to be raining, but whats with The UFO, The EGG, and
STICKMAN, still unanswered, so being we all do things from left to right, we see the ufo that looks exact to the HC car UFO
so at this point we have helicopters we fly up on the HC to see a RED arrow pointing to a Red Dot, that right above that
DOT but at the Top of the world there is a UFO, that we can interact with but not very much,it shuts you down and throws
you off, so all we can do is observe because that is what R* wants us to do with the HC UFO observe, well we take 2 things from
this observation.

1: It is the same ufo at 3am on top of the mountain
2: The FIB have UFO's

So back to the mountain nothing changes same thing, but we know the FIB have UFO's so where would they keep them? Well that is easy a Air Force base. Which the game has FT.Z,

Re-CAP

So from a writers perspective we have:
Lead the player to the clues from the beginning with "Come back when you story is complete" by placing it on the tallest mountain.
The player plays the story line learning of all the subliminal clues placed through out the story line, that hints at Alien,UFOs, and other ET like Things, Helping understand the mural a little more
After they are done with the Storyline all the clues we know at this point lead us to the Hologram UFO at the Top of Chillad.

So were do we hide our next Clue? at the one place that has the most UFO references in the game, and why not put a giant arrow pointing to the spot where the UFO is. Knowing UFO references the UFO on the Mural Matching the UFO at hippy camp we would surely head there. Follow Giant arrow to Anothor UFO that they can observe it is a Functional UFO that Looks like the Hologram UFO but all we know for sure is the FIB have UFO's. The fact that there is a holograph UFO and a identical UFO that is physical is enough to set up for another clue, so write FIB on the UFO to show they belong to the goverment. It is pretty obvious where to look for Goverment stored UFO'S a Air Force base.

Now to find the next clue have the player use what they know again. The 3 am Trigger. So place a UFO above the Air Base and have a light shine down at 3 a.m to keep the player using what they know to find the clues.

When they find the ufo they will investigate and the only thing that They can find is: SEGREGATE AND REARRANGE
which is the next clue, which is the word SEGREGATE AND REARRANGE which is a short definition of a Anagram one of the most commonly used puzzles in mystery related writings.

But it all seems to easy at this point thats because i have not brought into play all the RED HERRINGS in the Game/mystery and every good mystery has them, all mystery novels have you think one guy is the killer or multiple guys are the killers, here is a list of what i believe R* entered as red Herrings:

The Tract

I feel are there to confuse us and make us think they have something to do with the mystery but the mural does not depict any of these things. So they are Red Herrings.

Stick with what we KNOW.

Now the Lines on the mural, I believe they represent Tangents of the path it takes to complete the mural, some parts are confusing and will lead us to the same thing over and over again until we find something we missed, that is the section on the left that just seems to go in circles, but it doesnt lead any where but to the ufo's, the egg, or back to the top of mountain (which is what we have been doing for the past year or so but cant find the egg) and the UFO's Lead us to a Anagram that has lots of Green EGG answers in it and a few location based clues about a EGG, that is the next thing on the mural unexplained (and found in the game files) and I believe is the Next Clue because it fits the flow of the current leads that have lead us to the mystery.
