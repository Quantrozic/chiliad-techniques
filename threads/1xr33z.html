Potentially hidden messages in "Nine is God"...

So the song "Nine is God" was written specifically for GTA5. In addition, the complex mystery of the infinity killer has been considered solved, but has not led us anywhere. I'm making this thread on the assumption that the song must be related to the infinity killer. 9 is such a random number to pick for a song like that (it never has to rhyme with anything), and the only other number they could have picked that would have meant something would be 8. The lyrics of the song are also somewhat related to the murders ("Your lungs" repeated over and over -> all victims were found underwater).

So, what if there are hidden messages in the song? What if that's where we'll find the next step to the mystery? The song has a long instrumental section at the end, which would definitely contain something hidden. In addition, I think we should be listening to the vocal parts backwards to see if anything pops up there.
