The Great Seal (Mt. Chiliad Mural)

\-

Intro

\-
From the perspective of a player at the peak of Mt. Gordo the Sun sets directly behind the peak of Mt. Chiliad.

This creates the image of the Great Seal found on the back of a US $1 bill during sunset, where the triangular shape of Mt. Chiliad represents the triangular shape of the pyramid and the Sun represents the All-Seeing Eye.

This is the same image the Mt. Chiliad Mural is based on and there are many other allusions to the All-Seeing Eye and the Great Seal found throughout the game as well (including the picture in Franklin's safehouse, the poster in Lester's house, Omega's shirt etc.). From certain perspectives the baby blue Eye of the PENRIS building forms another Great Seal with the building directly North of it which has a pyramid atop its roof.

From the peak of Mt. Gordo, the Sun is perfectly aligned with the peak of Mt. Chiliad at exactly 18:45, forming the image of the Great Seal.

Doing Yoga freezes time. This means that if a player does Yoga at this time the image of the Sun and Mt. Chiliad forming the Great Seal will be seen in the background during the entire Yoga session.
Many clues point to the fact that Yoga is the answer to walking part of the Golden Path.

These clues hint at the fact that holding the Downward Dog position for a certain amount of time is key to performing Yoga correctly.

While performing Yoga, every time Michael switches to a new pose the camera angle will change. If the player decides to hold a pose the camera will remain at that angle for the entire duration. The Downward Dog is the final holdable position. While holding this position Michael faces away from Mt. Chiliad and the camera is placed showing the image of the Sun and Mt. Chiliad forming the Great Seal in the background for the entire pose.

Holding the Downward Dog atop Mt. Gordo while the Great Seal is formed lets the Sun shine on “your anus”.

There are many clues pointing to the fact that this is key, including Fabien's apparent anal fixation.
In “Did Somebody Say Yoga?” Fabien says “We have the Sun...and the murribunda...the asshole...now we begin and put the asshole to the Earth”. He also says “...mouth and anus are perfectly in line...” at one point during the session. Later on in the mission he instructs Amanda to do the Downward Dog, causing Michael to become enraged and attack him. After Michael falls into the pool Fabien claims that “yoga is the answer”. In “Reuniting the Family” Jimmy tells Amanda that she can do a lot better than a “yoga instructor with an anal fixation”.

The secret alien “Eye Tattoo” that can be unlocked in GTA Online has an arrow that points directly to your anus.

There are also many clues that point to the planet Uranus... which is often used as a joke, having the same pronunciation as the words “Your Anus”.

The phrase “Take me up Uranus” can be found painted at the Hippie Camp right next to the spot where the Hippies perform Yoga.

The Galileo Observatory has Golden plates around the Sun dial that represent only the planet Uranus. This differs from the Griffith Observatory, the real-life observatory that it's based on, which has a representation of all of the planets in our Solar System.

The brightest ring around Uranus is called “Epsilon”.

Many clues allude to the fact that the practice of Yoga and Meditation is a big part of going through the Epsilon Program, including the fact that Fabien is shown to worship the Sun, the fact that he has a baby blue website design, and the fact that in “Assuming the Truth” we find Epsilonist Marnie Allen meditating on a Yoga mat in Grapeseed.

In GTA: San Andreas there's an Epsilonist pedestrian who became widely known as the “Epsilon Backpacker” due to the fact that he carries a baby blue Yoga mat on his back. (Full explanation of Epsilon's connection with Yoga in Part 4)

The Weatherman Technique

\-

Although the time freezes while performing Yoga, the weather mechanic does not freeze.

Clouds will form/disappear (even though they don't move) and the natural weather cycle will unfold as normal.

This means that Yoga can be used by the player to manipulate the time and weather to align to his wishes.

This can be achieved by using the “Weatherman Technique”... where the player turns off Auto Save and Manually Saves the game every so often, allowing him to load a recent save file if a change in weather occurs and effectively “predict” the upcoming weather.

This means that for example, if the time is 2:00 and you can predict that rain or a thunderstorm will occur at 5:00, you can instead freeze the time and cause the storm to occur at 2:00 by doing Yoga for 6 minutes (the equivalent of 3 in-game hours). In this scenario the player is able to go and observe the Mt. Chiliad UFO under a natural thunderstorm using this technique.

The transition from Clear weather to Rain/Thunderstorm causes the sky to rapidly change over a period of several seconds, even when the time is frozen while performing Yoga.

Holding the Downward Dog atop Mt. Gordo during Sunset (18:45) under Clear weather conditions and waiting for Rain or a Thunderstorm to occur causes the entire sky around Mt. Chiliad to turn an extremely bright Golden Yellow color during this transition.

The bright Golden Yellow sky lasts for several seconds and then gradually disappears, revealing the Great Eye.
