Just looking at the mural after a few beers last night and resulting hangover this morning.

Just a few points;

  1. The mural appears to be saying that when the UFO appears above Chilliad there is some kind of either radio or electrical disturbance happening in game affecting various locations, two of which hold the jetpack and UFO.
  2. The other 5 boxes may relate to strange things occurring at that time elsewhere within the game. Either the X represents something else to be found, or something that needs to be done.

For example, at 3am after the UFO appears above Chilliad has anyone tried getting into the lift structure at the observatory. That has a radio dish on it, which maybe triggers the door to open (if the mural is about radio signals) has anyone tried this?

That's all for now - I know there are many interpretations of the mural but maybe it really just is that simple.
