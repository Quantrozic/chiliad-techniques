[Investigation] Opening doors

Documented under:Dependency-Graph Prerequisite Mapping ?

Hey guys,

I hope my flair worked, I would have put [long shot] in there but I was unsure if it would work.

I am working through my xbox one play though, being overly nice as everyone just started the second mission for the cult and went into the book depository. This got me to wondering, there are hundreds if not thousands of closed doors in the city, part of a wall with something behind it but inaccessible the player unless part at an exact time (like the book depository) or the elevator at Ft Zancudo (never used to our knowledge, but if we walked up to them at the right time, would they swing open just that easily?)

I am reminded of the Borderlands secret room {https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dX26ppFxiyU} that was discovered a full 2 or 3 years after the games release. You needed to go to a locked door, dead part of town, nothing in it you would ever go there for, during a mission that takes you nowhere near there {rack hive} If you miss it, its closed forever. and you walk past it every day!!

My (quite long winded) point is this, while using PC mods to clip in and out of these places and tell us they exist is all well and good... there could be a SILLY set of prerequisites to open a door we already know exists, there could only be something behind it at

  1. At a certain time of day (may be a locked time mission)
  2. A door not on the path of the mission.
  3. (even worse) a door that is locked but in view every time you use said character.
  4. 2 or more of the main characters together, or less if you are 'supposed' to off one of them.
  5. if another list of silly prerequisites are met, clothes, pacifist run, wanted level, weather and so forth.

and if we don't have all these things right at the right time and try the right door, its locked and we missed it.

So, who wants to try every door at every stage of the game with every character with me? anyone? :-/
