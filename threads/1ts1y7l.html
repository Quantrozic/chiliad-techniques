My findings of the Purple Doomsday Mural

Evening all, I want to preface this by saying i have been working on the Purple Mural specifically since around 2019 and this is an amalgamation of years of scouring the whole map and playing/picking apart story mode - the only reason i am posting is people here recently shared things which may assist in my findings eventually and since that element of this was found by others it made sense to post and see what everyone thinks of my coincidental findings and hopefully somebody can take this info and run with it to a solution.

Here are some basic criteria you need to participate in this Mural, This is not optional and will prevent you from doing certain things post-story if not done correctly during and after it at the earliest time possible in each instance.

  1. Attain 100% Completion
  2. Complete all activities with family such as tennis with amanda and general hobbies/past times for michael such as yoga.
  3. Operate only on a permission to kill basis during campaign as michael, use other characters every instance where necessary instead. Exemptions are missions like the one where you rescue tracy or jimmy and have to defend them - note down to explicitly only defend them or others, it's important.
  4. Complete all collectibles to ensure you have the space docker and go-go monkey blista.
  5. During the campaign choose every option that leaves you with no money or not much reward.
  6. Complete all phone internet webpage interactions such as epsilon program, stock markets etc, this is very important.

Right, i think that'll do for mandatory per-requisites, i will say that although i can't prove these are essential it's abundantly clear (to me) that they very well are.

Okay, Onto actual stuff now:

I began researching this mural after becoming obsessed with the Textile City one many years ago and realizing eventually that the Textile City and Purple Doomsday murals share the same theme in essence - Basically two pieces of the same puzzle.

Looking at the story, the endings, the general theme of the way the characters influence not just the city but also the people around them it became clear there was an element of karmaic progression for michael and his character throughout and after the story depending on choices - with some of his family/acquaintances it's very subtle but with time in-game and the right choices the way they speak to and treat him slightly shifts. This is only true to the game with a playthrough deemed to have good karma for michael. Any other choice or too many wrong decisions and they permanently despise him and it's harder to rectify post-story (i believe intentionally)

I believe throughout the story its not required to make every decision matter but i believe there is an invisible tally that keeps tabs on what mentality you have played with and if the minimum amount of "karma" is not reached then this will not work at the end of the game as you have not earned your reward.

With that said - we all know what the purple mural looks like, So i'll start with the simplest thing - it's colour.

Purple is a colour that represents wealth, power, spirituality and creativity - historically it was very expensive to make so only the powerful and wealthy could really use it for general things like dye and such, which is also why it historically doesn't appear on national flags too.

Now, we must ask where on the map can we find something purple that is associated with all of those things? The answer is simple - Next to the union depository there is a red and white apartment building and on top there is a purple light which is borderline a beacon.

Why is this important? i saw here recently somebody share a snippet from a drunk dev that said pay attention to the lights in the city as one will be visible more than others from chilliad, or something like that - anyway, this light is visible from chilliad on both xbox one and enhanced and i assume would be on 360 too.

It's in such a place you wouldn't think much about it but i believe this is what that dev was referencing and i've had this theory years before i saw that post the other day, it pretty much just added a layer of confirmation for me.

This light also comes on much earlier than usual and much later at random times/days, the most interesting time i saw it coming on was 19:00-19:03 and this happened during the start of the story so one would assume whatever monthly calendar the game uses that it would be towards the start maybe if we wanted to replicate it consistently? Why this stood out is that it didn't come on at exactly 19:00 and flickered until it became solid colour at 19:03. I have also seen it do this online too at that time so its a programmed thing at a set time and not a one off bug or glitch for SM.

Okay so on the foundational basis that this could be ground 0 for the purple mural i then began looking round the area for things that would match the iconography of the purple mural and separated ones like the ying/yang symbol, friedlanders logo etc as i believe they are pre-requisites and not icons.

What was left was a handful of icons to look for such as the keyhole, the circular maze, the stairs etc.

Now, this next part for your visual understanding i need you all to go visit the car park behind the union depository and find the one singular manhole cover that is square which has the letters M.F.S on it - also has a road marker arrow pointing to it, can't miss it really.. Stand on that manhole cover, face the UD building and use your brain to look around your FOV and search for things that match the iconography on the purple mural, you'll have to really look but once you see them - it's hard to unsee.

You should find from this location within your view range without moving

The keyhole icon, the stair icon, a metaphorical but existing representation of the circular maze (and surrounding you is everything else themed such as law, order & finance etc.

Anyhow, Across the street under the overpass directly visible from this location is a billboard that reads "What will you do?"

Alongside this next to this car park there are drains that say "Direct Sea Pipeline" or something on them as well as a hatch that says "Exit, may open suddenly" which has an M on it too - what's interesting is these are sporadically placed in questionable locations around this area and around the textile city mural.

The only icon that is not accounted for in this location directly is the pacific standard bank icon and i believe that is the conclusionary location to this after this first initial part is complete and where we will receive our reward.

So, That's basically the location and relevant things, now, we look at the smaller details that add credence to this location such as posters which can be found on the wooden partition, These posters are seen numerous places but are hard to find in-tact and not torn, one in particular (a phone number on a green poster) that says something along the lines of "Play with me" or "be my friend" has another poster i found which is full intact and is opposite the marina you can buy as michael alongside a bunch of other complete posters which are usually torn. This number on the poster has an almost dial up like tone when called - not immediately either it rings for a little bit. Not sure if relevant was just strange to find in the same location i was looking guess.

Another poster that's common but oddly and conveniently placed here is "softly softly catchy monkey"

and there's a couple others that could have connotative meanings to the mural too.

all-in-all i believed this location to be vital to the purple mural all things considered and felt like i had the place down but not the time or series or actions to progress- THEN i saw a post about golden hour and maze bank casting a shadow like a sundial and given this places location sitting right in that shadow (at least im 100% sure it does given its position) I felt that was just another layer of confirmation that i was in the right place. The reason why is they said golden hour is 18:50 onwards and of course i've seen the purple light comes on at 19:03. (the pacific standard banks founding date was 1903 too i believe)

So, what do i think this mural is? Well.. have any of you ever watched any of the old kids and family shows where they basically solve puzzles and riddles in temples/jungles, have to blindly navigate things through obstacles to get gems or diamonds etc? examples from the UK are Jungle Run, Crystal Maze or Fort Boyard, i truly 100% think this is the theme they went for with this and the textile city mural. I believe with the criteria met you qualify to solve a puzzle or timed event using this location as the start and then have to complete or navigate it within a set amount of time, whatever the process of this is it will eventually lead us to the pacific standard bank where one final puzzle must be solved using the busts in the building (which have some interesting destructible mechanics depending on progress and completion, as do the light fixtures on tables which when damaged look exactly like scales of justice and can be tilted by shooting them with a sniper rifle or stun gun) i believe this is eluded to subliminally by michael having a bust with no head near his front door too. (i know it means hes lost his head but equally, things can mean more than one thing)

I only believe this to be the case because the devs would have grown up and/or have their kids grow up watching these shows as they were very, very popular in their day and in truth it would make an awesome concept for an easter egg such as this.

Another thing to note about the textile city mural is that if you face it directly it points to the maze bank, but theres a spawn with i think michael? (maybe franklin) where you spawn under the adjacent binco clothes stores price tag/arrow logo and from that angle the hands do actually point exactly straight to where the purple light is. Just a side note really for extra credence.
