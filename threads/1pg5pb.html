[Investigation] Penris Building: "Shoot for the Stars." MAY BE ON TO SOMETHING!!!

▲ 14 pts[deleted]Oct 29, 201326 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Sub-Pixel Star Pattern Mapping ?

Go downtown to the Penris Building in a helicopter. Hover above the statue of the blue, half-sunken eye and five stars.

Now, think about the mural and then look at the placement of the 5 stars at the blue eye/stars statue:

  • The statue has 5 stars.
  • The mural has 5 Xs.
  • The stars line up roughly in the same place as the Xs would on the mural.
  • There are dots and other types of geometry that could match some of the angular lines in the mural (doubtful).

I managed to get a faraway shot, but it doesn't do it much justice. A heli works best for observing this. Any thoughts?

Next, check out the eagle statue in the nearby plaza:

  • The plaza looks like an eye.
  • When the light hits it just right, the eagle's wings create two shadows that look like hands on a clock.
  • There are three boxy-looking umbrellas with Xs on them (representing the three major easter eggs on the mural?).
  • The eagle is surrounded by stars and, when you get up close to them, they exhibit high-res textures. Very sharp. Seems purposeful.
  • The eagle itself sits atop a metallic, circular structure. Looks like it could be a UFO, or could open up to reveal a descending, spiral staircase. I saw no texture seams that would suggest this, however.

I shot the stars with a pistol. Nothing. But I didn't try it long, or under many different conditions. If anything, my money's on some sort of interaction with the stars in the eagle statue/fountain. Again, those suckers look crisp up front (or from a distance). Why?
