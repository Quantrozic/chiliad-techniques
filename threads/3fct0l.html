Reading the mural differently

▲ 22 ptsu/GuydoAug 1, 201510 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Mural & Map Mirroring

I see the mural as a clear indication of something readable and cryptic. I believe the bottom three objects are all found in game and we just need to scrutinize exactly what Rockstar gave us here.

After looking at the mural, you may notice aspects of symmetry in shapes and lines. This is where I think we may want to try something new. At first, I thought the two blocky "L" shapes between the leftmost x's could be cut out and slid like tiles to form a box. Essentially, I thought maybe the entire mural could be rearranged like a physical jigsaw puzzle. Then I realized there aren't enough closed geometric shapes for this.

Next, I tossed around the idea of using the lines as folds. If you printed out the mural, and folded it on paper down the vertical line that bisects the diagram, the upper x's seem to match almost. Then you can repeat this with the horizontal lines between the bottom two x's and top three.

My thought process is that there may be something implied on this mural that may reveal a symbol we would understand. I plan on playing with a print of the mural when I have time, but my hope is that someone else might see a pattern here before I do. Thanks!
