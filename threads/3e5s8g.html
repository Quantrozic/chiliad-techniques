Elements for a good (reasonable) theory

▲ 5 ptsu/gteyeJul 22, 201514 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Alchemical Element Correspondence Analysis

Seems like theories are going too far. People are seeing mysteries where are none - all outdoor messages are being read as a hint to solve the chiliad mystery among many other misinterpretations of ordinary elements of the game. Seems that we are running away from the source of the mystery and creating a collective illusion, a puzzle that is far more complicated in our minds that is in the game.

To try get the things back to a coherent line of analysis of chiliad mystery, I would like to SUGGEST to return to the basics, and to accept some elements that all theories should have in order to be a good theory:

  1. First of all, those arguing that the mural is just a hoax created by R*, or that the mystery is already solved have to explain the reason to exist an real egg in the game (a fact confirmed in the game files) and where it is (once I saw a youtube video of a guy that claimed to have found files of an asteroid, but I do not know if it is true). Obvious, the egg files may be part of a possible hoax but if R* could expend so much effort in create a hoax why not create a legitimate and solvable mystery?

So, the next points presupposes that there is a solvable mystery in the game.

  1. The source of the mystery is the mural. All theories has to explain or speculate, in a reasonable way, the elements of the mural to make a minimum sense. The curves in the mural are clearly the mount chiliad silhouette; the squared shape in its top is the wooden platform (because the symbol of the eye is under it).
  2. Every other element that is not in the mural should have an explained connection with the mural to be part of a good theory. So, for example, a theory that claim that altruist camp or the observatory are parts of the mystery has to demonstrate a clear connection or representation of these places within the mural. What in the mural leads me to the observatory or the altruist camp or zancudo etc.? What in the mural tells me to search in these places?
  3. If there is a jetpack in the game, it will not be the "ultimate reward", because the jetpack figure on the mural are in the same level (of importance) of the egg and the "spaceship" (whatever it represents). Thus, ideas like "jetpack hunt" are misleading and do not focus on the whole. The main idea here is "understand the mural".

[some speculation]

I'm not really certain about it, but I would suggest that since the lines of the mural are pictured INSIDE chiliad mount, maybe there is a way inside. Note that a line connects with chiliad on the left part of its silhouette and another line connects with the jetpack figure on the right (entrances?)

Based on that, the most probable entrance would be the T01 and T02 doors in chiliad tunnel. And a possible connection could exist with the IAA and FIB towers that has T01 and T02 painted on it. Maybe the trigger to open the tunnel doors are in the towers? As I said, just speculation...
