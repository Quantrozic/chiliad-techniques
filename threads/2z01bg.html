The 5 step theory

▲ 29 ptsu/BipolaricecapMar 14, 201515 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Frame-Step Cutscene Forensics ?Frame-Step Glyph Photography ?Frame-Step Visual FX Decomposition ?Route Theory ?

Hey guys, as much as I think this site is great for sharing finds and theories, it can also lead you to be thinking of too many theories at one time clouding your mind in the process, so I decided to not visit this sub for a week in the hope of unknowing the things I already know to clear my mind in preparation for building a theory. Through countless hours of game time and research, I'm starting to come up with a theory that fits although there are a few bits here and there that still require some thought so here goes.

My theory is all about the number 5. Obviously we have 5 X's on the mural but we are still yet to figure out exactly what these mean. Now, imagine if this mural was some sort of checklist, the X's representing the things yet to be done, and once one step is completed it will be clearly visible on the mural, maybe a tick in a box or a green X? For each step, each protagonist must meet the required needs for that certain step, once this is achieved each protagonist can then begin their second step but only if each one has completed the first step. But what could these steps be? I think the mural is related to the 3 cults, stating that each protagonist must complete 5 steps to achieve the hidden goodies. So what do we have in relation to 5 steps within the cults?

TREVOR: Trevor is the only guy who can deliver people to the Altruists and we have a few to choose from. The shootout is activated when you deliver the 4th person but there are random events with 2 people so it is possible to deliver 5 although you dont get paid for the fifth. I know every combination has probably been tried but maybe they have to be delivered at certain points in the storyline depending which step the protagonists are on, so for example the first would be delivered when each protagonist is currently on their first step and so on. Trevor also has 5 rampages and there's the 5 vinewood souvenir missions so these may have to be done in steps too.

FRANKLIN: He is the only protagonist contacted by the CoM cult. It talks a lot about self actualization which I think is the key to cracking this. Looking a little more into self actualization I stumbled upon Maslow's hierarchy of needs. I've recently noticed that I'm not the only one on the maslow trail, Kingshit481 posted something the other week, hopefully thats good minds thinking alike!! Abraham Maslow was an american psychologist who believed that one must go through 5 stages to achieve self actualization and that all criteria must be met to be able to progress to the next stage. The 5 stages, or needs, are physiological, safety, love/belonging, esteem and self actualization. I will go into a little more detail on each and state how I link them to Franklin in-game.

Physiological: These are the physical requirements for a human to live. Air (lung capacity?), water (sprunk, cola, pisswasser??), food (sweets), and then protection from the elements, clothes (clothes), shelter (Franklin lives with his aunt). Maslow also places sex in this category (nail a hooker).

Safety: Personal security (weapons and armour), financial security (purchasing a property), health and well being (Stamina?)

Love/belonging: Humans need to feel a sense of belonging, acceptance and intimacy (hang out with all friends and do all activities with them, get booty calls from strippers, maybe if the first 2 steps are a success there could be a way of getting tanisha back??).

Esteem: The need to be respected by others, people often do this through a profession or hobby (do all stunt jumps, parachutes, races, under the bridge, knife flights and triathlons all with franklin, win against every possible player at golf, get all medals from shooting range and flight school.)

Self actualization: What a man can be he must be. The desire to accomplish everything that one can, to become the most one can be. (Purchase all the properties linked to franklin plus all the others available to him, buy all clothing, buy marina and hangar and everything to go in them, fill both garages with fully modded cars, find all scrap letters, spaceship parts and nuclear waste with franklin, pretty much do everything possible with him to the max) Maslow describes self actualizing as finding one's self at the summit, sounds an awful lot like the sign outside the CoM church, find out about yourself on the mountain. Im thinking the sign has 2 meanings, self actualization but also telling us to go to the mountain.

There are also 5 towing missions for Tonya so these may need to be done step by step along with the others.

MICHAEL: He is the only protagonist not deemed unsaveable. With the epsilon missions, there are references to the number 5. He has to deliver 5 cars, he also has to run 5 miles in the robes but as these two cant be done side by side I looked for an alternative. Over the course of the epsilon missions, Michael makes 5 payments to Epsilon. The first being $500, second being $5000, third being the vehicles, fourth being the $10000 and finally the $25000 payment for the robes. Michael also has his visits to the shrink where advice is sometimes given and Michael can accept or reject the advice, this happens on 5 different occasions.

So there seems to be a link to the number 5 for all three protagonists within their given cults, and also other 5 related things for each protagonist. Now thinking about the mural and it being a checklist, find out about yourself on the mountain certainly links in with that idea. Also Dr Friedlander states in one of his very first texts that through progress you will see the results, is this again implying a checklist? I havent noticed anything like this regarding Trevor as of yet. I'm guna start bashing through the game again and test this theory so will let you guys know if I get anywhere, but if anyone sees any flaws in this theory, or if anyone thinks of anything to add to this please throw them at me.

I truly believe the 'come back when your story is complete' isnt referring to 100%, I believe its when all 3 protagonists hidden stories are solved, when their 5 steps are complete. If I was to guess the outcome, with all the mention of earthquakes/tsunamis/floods I think when we have completed the story all 3 protagonists will stand atop chiliad in a big ass thunderstorm and watch as an earthquake rumbles and creates a tsunami which floods the entire map. Would explain the lightning bolts and what looks like water crashing up the mountain on the mural.

Happy hunting, kifflom!!
