"We are no closer to finding anything than we were 3 years ago" My response + To me these clues/theories say otherwise.

▲ 3 ptsu/FuckinRickyJan 20, 201713 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Choice-Branch Outcome Tabulation ?Moral Choice & Playstyle Branching Analysis ?

It seems to me that a lot of people here are constantly saying that we have come absolutely no closer to solving this mystery than we were almost 4 years ago, which to a degree seems true but in my opinion is completely wrong. Personally I feel that a main contributor to just the lack of observable progress has to do with the willingness to actually put these theory's to the test. Yes I know that a good amount of people actually do try some of them out to no avail but anytime I see a very solid or plausible theory posted, I see a whole lot of "Maybe now if you try this, this could happen" and "Wow great theory! Maybe if you do this, go here, try that, shoot this, rearrange that, etc etc..." A lot of suggestions (which is great!) but not a lot of actually going out at testing the original theory posted, logging and then updating with results to know if anything happens when its actually attempted. Just comments adding a lot of maybe's and next steps to something before even exploring and testing it in the first place (Which I know can also be part of getting closer to the solution. The community and such) just feel like maybe we should spend a little more time debunking or proving an original theory, before we try to add onto that theory and make it more convoluted and harder to attempt.
Here's some examples I think are big steps and do make us closer to the solution than when we started, IF we actually put them to the test.

EYE IN THE SKY (Relating to the cave paintings near altruist camp) - https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0dm1aKCsfLs - Also contains solar eclipse which we already know is referenced a lot in game.

A POTENTIAL "CORRECT" ORDER TO DO THE HEISTS - https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dNPbSEoxw9Y (Note that in the first heist when you climb the ladder to take a picture of the AC unit, an open hatch that also contains a big "X" was found similar to the one in this video as you get to the top of the ladder.) This may be implying that there is a correct order and correct choices you need to make through the game. I am currently looking for these "X's" in each heist. Not only that but on the Chiliad mural there are 5 X's with illustrations of potentially branching paths and choices. There are 5 Heists with branching paths and choices.

A HIDDEN 3rd SPECTROMETER AT ALTUIST CAMP - https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k_rwWPirdgg&t=332s

MOON ECLIPSE (Which again we know eclipses are referenced a lot all over the map including 2 pretty big eclipse hints in the city near the Pegasus building - https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vJdvtbkU0es&t=241s

(Probably late to the party with this one) PSYCHICSHOUTOUT.COM - Something I just noticed while typing this. In the GTA browser, on a website called psychic shoutout.com, the background is completely covered in those same "Lightning bolts" that is all over that chiliad mural, also the logo for the website has a little glass ball with an (all seeing?) eye and a little radio antenna on the top with those same bolts emitting from the antenna, implying that it is electrical power/radio signals and being that it is the exact same bolt as the mural, I think its safe to say that those bolts on the mural are definitely power based from some sort of antenna or radio tower. I don't believe it has to do with weather if anyone still thought that.

THE "SPECIAL" TELESCOPE - https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3QSi6zW9lHY

(I know most of these were from the same YouTube channel but that is only because this guy is one of the few guys actually posting legitimate non clickbait clues, theory's and general findings.

In conclusion, I feel like if someone posts an interesting theory that seems plausible, we should be trying to test out that theory instead of typing "Well maybe if we take the mural and flip it on its side then backwards and add 2 it will give us the flyable UFO" we should be using the testable and plausible theory's to report back with the results so we know if any of it is even possible before adding more things on top of it, or just moving onto a different theory altogether.
