To summarize, the same short set of 4 characters is repeated over 90% of the camp. A couple characters that are not a part of that set can be seen on the hood of the UFO car, and paired with alien graphics. There is also a custom plate on the back of the truck that says "BEE PEA", which is the only instance of this plate in the whole camp.

I personally don't think us figuring this out would be anything major relative to the Chiliad mystery. Knowing Rockstar, it could just translate to "penis" or something.
