Something weird with SnapMatic...

▲ 6 ptsu/Kingshit481Jul 9, 20145 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Snapmatic Photography & Location-Data Cataloguing ?

Kifflom, fellow hunters. Just something I noticed while adventuring tonight.

I am currently on the north side of the military base, outside of the fence, so no wanted level. I am directly west of the barracks, but standing directly across from the first observation tower, and there is one soldier standing on it with field glasses.

This is where it gets kind of weird:

Just for shits'n'gigs, I was playing around with the phone's camera, zooming in to possibly get a better look. I decided I would take this lone soldier's photograph. When I went to take the picture, the camera clicked as it should, but I was not given the option to save the photo, and the phone went back into Inventory (so, not on the screen, in other words). I did this a couple of times, but was unable to actually save the photo. To see if my game was glitching, I turned around and took a picture; this time, I was given the option to save the photo to the gallery. I turned slightly towards the direction of the barracks and, again, was able to save the photo I took. It was only when I was trying to take a picture of the observation tower (and I do not mean the control tower!) that my camera did not allow me to save anything.

Not sure if this is a glitch, but also having a hard time remembering how casing places before heists worked. If other hunters would like to test this out for themselves, please-- I would love to see if what is happening is isolated to my game at the moment. If need be, I will upload a map with my current location.

Kifflom!

EDIT: Okay, ran through with this a couple more times. Didn't realize the fitness yard was right behind the watch tower, so if you want to look at what I'm seeing, that's the one to look for. Now, I've noticed that it only closes the phone instantly if you get the guy on top of the observation tower (with field glasses) that the phone immediately exits. The fitness yard, the ground in front of the tower, whatever-- is able to be photographed/saved. It is only when the guy on the tower is in the photo, even if it is just his feet, you will not even be given the option to save the photo.

EDIT EDIT: Changed orientation, it would let me save only a few times. Another soldier appeared on the other side of the tower, first soldier disappeared, and was able to save photos again. Sorry to lead anyone on a wild goose chase. Probably a bug, but part of me wants to believe!
