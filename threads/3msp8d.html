Nine is God, No Happy Endings, I Get Lifted Mural Paths.

▲ 2 ptsu/A_CryptarchSep 29, 201512 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Choice-Tree Ending Permutation MappingMoral Choice & Playstyle Branching Analysis

I've been listening to the GTA soundtrack for a while now before I started obsessively shadowing this forum and before I presented any of my thoughts to you.

Welcome to some random tangent of absolute insanity festering within me. The mystery is only solvable by a specific character and all of your choices determine who that character is or can be. You must follow the Mural Path properly, of course, but at the end, you get to decide which character can properly complete the mystery.

"Nine is God" is my theory that, in order for Trevor to be able to solve the mystery, Michael must die. You must proceed along the path I outlined with Franklin (Properly killing/not killing the appropriate characters?) and Trevor but when the time comes to choose between Michael and Trevor, Franklin chooses Michael.

"I Get Lifted" is the inverse. Michael must proceed along the path properly, as well as Franklin but you choose Trevor to die.

"No Happy Endings" is the theory that nobody has played Franklin properly at all. Beverly and Richard Dreyfuss are, most likely, killed by all players who encounter them. In order for Franklin to be the proper character, you must select Death Wish, as most players do. Avoid killing one, the other or both. It's also possible you may need to kill both prior to the choice but who knows?

A couple of random thoughts about all of this;

You may need to complete all aspects of the game prior to the choice but this isn't concrete.

You may need to make all the right choices with all three characters except the final choice.

You probably need to encounter the UFO with your chosen character first.

Gold Medals are probably bad but no stone should be left unturned. Testing kill variables -- Killing Bev but not Dreyfuss, Killing Dreyfuss but not Bev, Killing Both and Killing Neither should all be considered.

Greed is your enemy. Do not seek the largest cash payout (like with Al, for instance).

It's possible that you can only acquire one reward per game (the one depicted).
