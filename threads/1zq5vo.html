The reason WHY rain is significant - (Rain Fade)

▲ 41 ptsu/LlamaGuy69Mar 6, 201417 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Poisson Rate Testing of Sighting Reports ?

We know that rain is significant to our mystery, but we havn't really discovered WHY it's significant. We know that rain is required to view the Chiliad UFO. This most simple hypothesis of WHY is that it adds an extra requirement along with 3 am and makes the UFO harder to find, rather than just appearing every single night. Another simple hypothesis would be that rain adds drama and excitement, and makes the UFO seem more ominous and spooky. I happen to agree with both of these reasons, however I believe that the significance of the rain goes much deeper.
For starters, the Chiliad UFO is NOT the only aspect of the mystery that is affected by rain. In fact, the group of FIB and scientists DO NOT show up at the satellite dishes at 10am like usual if it is raining. Instead, if you go there during the rain there will only be one lonely FIB member instead of the group, plus the cars that spawn are not present. I have tested this numerous times and this holds to be true every single time. I have even tried other adverse weather conditions such as snow...the FIB group will show up in any condition besides rain.
Going back to Chiliad, the radio antenna on top of the shack constantly has a puddle of water under it, which I believe HAS to be a clue.
Considering all of these facts, I believe the significance of the rain is its direct effect on radio signals. Radio waves are just light waves at a lower frequency. They travel at the speed of light and can be absorbed by particles of matter that it comes in contact with, much like the visible light we know and love. There is a phenomenon called "Rain Fade", which is when rain absorbs radio signals and causes them to become weaker once they reach their destination. Rain Fade is the reason why you get a shitty cell phone/internet connection sometimes when there is a storm. Considering the puddle of water under the radio antenna and the strange FIB behavior during rain at the radio telescopes (dishes), I believe that Rain Fade could be the reason WHY rain is significant.

That being said I have a few hypotheses of how Rain Fade comes into play. Maybe the radio antenna at the shack is constantly receiving a signal from the FIB to "cloak" the UFO...and when Rain Fade occurs this signal dosn't get received and the UFO becomes visible. Maybe the green light that the FZ UFO shines on the bunker's dish is sending radio signals as well as light...and this is what is being disturbed.

The most important fact to consider is that the Chiliad UFO stops making sound the moment it stops raining...this is extremely important, and it shows us that rain is more significant than we thought. Please share your thoughts, comments and ideas. Hopefully we can riff on each other's thoughts and get one step closer to solving this!
