Rayfire is a realistic collision simulator.

Although it's called several known times in scripts, there's an event at (x="-1234.810059" y="-1105.000000" z="10.370000"), in Vespucci Beach, right in front of Prawn Vivant and a very short distance from the pier.

So the goal would be to figure out exactly what's going on. What model is being called, what animation it plays, and when this whole thing is used. There's a number of Natives that have it in their name, but as far as I know, none of them take place at that location, and are associated with the storyline and its use of natives. So is there some hidden or obfuscated event with large scale destruction?

Somebody said there's a strange object in the code located above the nearby apartment building with the giant V on it, but that's all the info I have on it right now.

Anybody who wants to help take a crack at this would be greatly appreciated. We've done a lot of work on it on the Discord and kind of hit a wall.
