Shoot for the stars!

▲ 13 ptsu/Large_Progress71Aug 28, 20248 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:UV Light Analysis

Hi! A few weeks ago, I got interested again in the topic of Easter eggs from the GTA V collector's map. I’ve seen that most of the mysteries have already been solved, but this one seems to remain unsolved. I’ve searched everywhere, even posted here, and all I find are videos from over 7 years ago showing that where the UV light appears on the blue map is the observatory. There's a sniper there, strangely enough, and if you aim at night, you can see the "PENRIS" building (sniper with the letters scrambled), and above it, there’s an eye with stars around it and a bright blue light in the middle. To me, it’s like, oh my god! It’s obvious you need to shoot there, the map's name itself says so, and I’m surprised it hasn’t been solved yet!

As if that wasn't enough, I found another video that shows that at the real-life observatory, there's a statue in memory of an actor who filmed a scene right where the viewpoint is (JAMES DEAN), and guess what? The statue has a star! Additionally, in another video, you can see that from the Vinewood sign, the eye’s light is much more centered for you to shoot at. Others say that sometimes a helicopter appears, and there's even a line of code found that references something happening if you shoot between certain coordinates. Has anyone made any progress? I’d like to remind you that the videos I watched are OVER 10 YEARS OLD, it seems like this mystery just stopped being investigated! I can’t go any further because my PlayStation broke. :(
