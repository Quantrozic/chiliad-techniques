Shadows in Paleto Bay and The Altruist Camp

▲ 3 ptsu/bakinsammykNov 27, 20136 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Shadow TrackingShadow-Length Solar Position ModelingSun-Shadow Azimuth Triangulation

Just a few discoveries I want to share. I was exploring Paleto bay on the weekend. Time was morning, after sunrise but before you could see the sun from behind chiliad.

I was looking at shadows. A lot of shadows in the game move according to the suns position (like in real life). Such as the pole shadows in the hippy camp that seem to fill the boxes at one time or another.

Anyway, all of a sudden, the shadows of a tree and other surrounding shadows I was looking at sped up. Like it was being fast forwarded. I have no idea why, but I chased the shadows and ran towards them. Then they stopped for about 10-25 seconds. Not sure how long because I was still in a state of WTF! Then they sped up again and I started running toward them again. Then it stopped again and just resumed normal shadow movement for the rest of the day. When the second fast forward stopped, it was around 10am. I finished up at the railway yard behind the clucken bell factory.

I started just outside the town, near the clock that is shaped like an egg, my character facing chiliad and the shadows were moving to my left (hence why I ended up in the rail yard from following them).

In hindisght, I should have been looking at the sun when I noticed this, but my instinct was to follow the shadows. So far, I've been able to replicate this once, but not everyday, and not for the last 3 days.

Another thing unrelated, not too far from where I started, if you go right, instead of left towards paleto bay, there is a start of a mountain trail to the altruist camp. There is a wooden map sign with a castle carved onto it, which I believe represents the altruist camp. However in the bottom right corner, the word ACTUALITY is scratched onto the wood, and it's covered a bit by a piece of paper.

Actuality is one of the passwords for the Children of the Mountain cult. There has to be something Children of the Mountain related up there. The word is carved the same way as the castle the seems to represent the Altruist camp.

One last thing. The Altruists worship the sun. The morse code from their website says they hate the sun. Is there two cults there? Maybe something living under the Altruist camp? Has anyone else heard the predator noises outside the front of the Altruist camp?

That's what have to add for now.
