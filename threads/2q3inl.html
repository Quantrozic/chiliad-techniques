I'd like to point out a theory of mine concerning the mural. So the top portion of the mural represents the Chiliad UFO, with the eye atop the mountain being the UFO itself. The X's on the mural represent the glyphs found on Mt. Chiliad that show how to see the UFO. The lightning represents the storm that you have to be in to see the UFO.

Alright, so now for the bottom of the mural. The UFO with the alien inside represents the Sandy Shores UFO. This is because this location is the main hot spot for seeing any type of "alien" in the game, albeit only paintings/graffiti of them.

The cracked egg represents only one thing, and this one thing is not even on the map. It just represents "cracking the easter egg," which is why all of the symbols eventually link to it, and the Chiliad UFO glyph leads directly down into it.

Finally, I believe the jetpack symbol represents the Zancudo UFO and nothing else. Why? Because this game has so many tie-ins with GTA:SA, and in that game the military base is where a jetpack was contained.

So, after viewing all of these UFOs in order at specific times and specific moon phases (as seen by the moon phase glyphs on the Beam Me Up camp), then the Zancudo bunker will be opened through a trigger. Inside, I'm not sure if there will be a jetpack or an alien egg...but I believe personally there will be a drive-able UFO and maybe a new Strangers and Freaks mission for Omega. Just a thought. Or maybe you actually literally get everything the mural helped you find out. You get a UFO as a vehicle (the UFO glyph), an alien egg that can hatch and sprout an alien (the cracked egg), and you get a jetpack (the jetpack man).

This is all just a theory, but it works. We just have to dig deeper into these glyphs and find out what times to view these places. And, also, I believe the sunken UFO has no significance whatsoever in this mystery. Perhaps it has something to do with the aftermath of solving it, but you do not need it to solve the actual mystery. It was put there to throw us off, if anything. Now for these other mysterious places...Penris, observatory, Altruists, etc....R* has stated that there are mysteries/easter eggs/secrets in the game so brutal and secretive that only they will ever know what they are. So everywhere/everything in this wonderful and brain-busting game most likely has its own mystery surrounding it. We have solved some mysteries already, like the Monkey Mosaic, Michael Murder Mystery, Infinite 8 Mystery, Space Docker, etc. But maybe with the power of the jetpack and flyable UFO we will finally be able to solve some of the greatest mysteries in gaming history!
