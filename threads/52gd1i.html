The red eye and a question

Documented under:Frame-Step Glyph Photography ?

Now for this mystery there is one basic question...well for me anyway which hinders any basic search forcing you to look at the mural in either one of 2 ways.

  • 1 as a representation of something
  • 2 as a literal depiction of something

What I mean is if the red eye's placement underneath the viewing platform on top of chiliad has to be one of the above.

If you pick number 1 and think it is just random then the possibilities are endless which the sub sort of represents.

But if you believe the placement is not random and reflects the mural correctly then it means one of two things as it is hard to ascertain exactly which way the eye faces but it only leaves two possibilities which is either NW or SE.

SE it lines up with grapeseed and roughly the nest egg house from the paleto bay bank sign.

NW it looks out to paleto forest water tower and on to the small island state wilderness.

Now I know we been here before, this is more about an old question asked in a new light as we are now rumbling about under GTA V's hood , as it's 2 straight lines one NW and one SE with not much in the way maybe a check for co-ordinates directly.

It has to be my biggest issue when looking at the mystery, is the eye randomly placed or placed with intent to reflect it's position on the mural.
