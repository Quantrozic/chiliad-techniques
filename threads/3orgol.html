True apologies if any of this isn't original, I'm not very regular on reddit & i did my searches on this sub and found little comparisons for like an hour, but i'm sick and weary.

First:
I've always felt Maze Bank building represented the eye at the top of the mural, for a number of reasons, including the "Egg of Life" fountain at it's base. Messing around the other day, I discovered that Bishop's WTF?! UFO and the Maze Bank Arena are exactly 2/3 mile from Maze building, and form a straight line with all 3 locations struck-through. Interesting, but not much else...except the roof of the arena is mirrored. More interesting when considering the Epsilon Tracts.

Speaking of the mirrored lake: the fountain at the base of Maze building has a perfect mirror on it's flat surfaces. At night, only the closest buildings' lit windows will reflect in the water: http://imgur.com/f60d2C6
From same spot, http://imgur.com/6KuQyhE the building the "crack" points to has brilliant blue lights illuminating the top, but nothing reflects. Hence my distance-from-fountain theory.

I've tried discerning some text from the lit windows' reflections in the fountain. If i warp my brain a little I see "PANIC," the Arena's home team, but even I think that's stretchin' it.

I realize these are open-ended and go nowhere, I'm only hoping to put others onto the path of the Tracts' mirrored lake, etc.

If you haven't read the Tracts, or it's been a while, read them all in reverse. Got my cogs spinning.

Second:
The cavern beneath the Altruist complex. Hadn't explored it personally until today, and what I take away from it is this: The only "sun" glyphs at the hippy camp indicate rain, and "3" (AM watever). They're the only ones spelling out the UFO conditions, besides 100%.

Bringing that logic to the cave w/ the skull and bonfire, the only "sun" glyphs indicate a beaming, full sun EXCEPT one, cut vertically in half by a dark blue line swallowing it up, speaking to me about the Eclipse. I know some think that particular glyph indicates a setting sun over water, but then why would it be VERTICAL in the cave? Plus, we've proven PREDICTABLE eclipses in-game.

Take that into account with the written words: (See the Eye!") the Eclipse; and then ("Praise Him!" & "Give Thanks!") = Burn yourself alive in the cave, upon the eclipse. Bask in His light, and Give thanks!

Not saying you're gonna respawn with a jetpack or some shit, but i'm only on 78% in my game, due to extremely low tolerances for collectibles...so somebody's gotta test it. Also, the sacrificial part of that theory is open to more interpretation, but the eclipse is a certainty in my mind.

Third:
I'd made a previous post theorizing the half-built skyscraper as the cracked Egg in the mural. I find it intriguing there's a means to reach the top with a straight line, the crane, despite needing a parachute - which it provides - to get to the top of the site. Also intriguing, a set of horizontal braces halfway up the crane lead straight into mirrored windows...what tinfoil hat? the one I just crumpled into my back pocket?

Also, the view is a bit too nice from the top of the crane...http://imgur.com/xCVATYV

anyways, take away what you will. Here's to Jack Sheepe, and to getting the flu outta the way early in the season...
