1999-Mountain

Documented under:In-Game Phone Dialing Sweeps

So building off the black phones cheat I thought I'd play around with phone words to see what I could find. Things like jet-pack and i-can-fly didn't work. There's 3-4 automated responses to incorrect numbers. They are someone hanging up, a company telling you to visit their website, and the others slip my mind. However, 1999-Mountain (1999-6686-8246) plays this elevator music on a loop for three or four times. This happens for all three characters, including online. I can't tell where the song is from but it sounds familiar. It seems to trigger nothing for me, but I am not a 100%er. It could be nothing useful, however the consistent response elicits a potential query. Happy hunting. Kifflom
