An Important Reminder.

Documented under:Collision-Mesh Probing

This is coming up as the result of a sort of accidental discovery, not exactly on my part either. After lurking this sub for an age, I got the hunter's fever. Easter eggs like this make some games truly beautiful in a lot of ways - both due to the necessity of community involvement to really get anywhere, and the possibility that it could all be shite!

Anywho, that aside, I recently discovered that Payday 2's engine works in a similar way to GTA V's. The engine itself will not load an interior until a prerequisite/simple trigger is met. There are several instances where this is utilized in a "normal" way during Payday 2 gameplay - several heists won't load the interiors of the vaults until they've actually been opened. Only then does the engine get called to load said interior. It doesn't even really "see" the interior or "know about it" (its personification relax) until it's absolutely necessary.

GTA V's engine works in much the same way - that objects or interiors don't even really exist in the game world until a prerequisite or trigger is met.

These are things I have read and come to understand between the two different communities. If I am wrong about any of this, please let me know and I will either modify or delete this post to accommodate new information.

Regardless, AFAIK this has been a known "feature" of GTA V's and PD2's respective engines, and really adds a lot of fat to the meat that is this mystery.
