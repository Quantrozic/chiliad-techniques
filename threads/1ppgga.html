Exploring the "Chiliad Cobweb"

▲ 21 ptsu/EvoorgEbutNov 1, 201326 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Reused-Asset Lineage Tracing ?Shader & Procedural Asset Analysis ?

I have avoided posting here, but have thoroughly enjoyed keeping up with some of the wild speculation here. As a developer with some experience, a lot of the “discoveries” can be easily explained as rendering glitches, inside jokes, or apophenia.

But the “Chiliad Cobweb” piqued my interest.

DISCLAIMER: I do not think the cobweb is in any way connected to the Mural Mystery... at least not in any specific jetpack producing way. I believe it is just a curiosity.

When I first heard of the cobweb I wrongfully assumed it was just a rendering glitch that someone was referring to. Possibly a moiré pattern caused by the floor grate texture or the shadow map glitch when it hits a wall on edge. Then I saw a screen shot. It's real?!

I fiddled with the image in Photoshop just to convince myself it was not someone's idea of a joke. Some red herring made in MSPaint. It certainly looked like a crude mock up of a spider's web. Definitely not a professional texture artist's work. But there it was. Photoshop showed no significant tampering. I had to see this in game for myself.

Now there are a lot of logical possibilities for its existence that do not involve crazy conspiracies. It could be “Programmer Art”. Often when a designer or programmer want to implement an object or system they will mock it up without the help of an artist. It is usually visually very crude. The intention that it will be assigned to an artist after the proof of concept is approved. In some cases the object will be placed “in world” but “hidden” in code so the artist can access it, but it won't be sitting around looking ugly or getting the QA team sending bug reports about it. This could explain the childlike quality of the art asset and only showing up at a certain time?

Anyway, I made my way to the mountain. Sure enough, it appeared at the same spot under the gondola shed at 1 am. I thought maybe its appearance was due to a shift in lighting at that time, but no. There was no noticeable lighting change and the texture appeared to be flatly lit no matter the time or angle viewed. That's went I noticed something odd. It's moving!

It moves like the sails on the sail boats! This led me to believe it was using a displacement shader of some kind. That would imply that it is more than a simple quad. It would require a much larger tessellation to get that kind of movement. Not a big deal. It could just be the asset request specified wind movement.

So then I started panning around, just in case it did line up with something. When I managed to get the cobweb to clip with the game camera... Wow! It's not a simple plane! It's a complex mesh! More than that. It appears to be constructed from multiple segments of “Cable”. (Here's how the source engine describes them https://developer.valvesoftware.com/wiki/Cables_and_Ropes .) This implies that who ever made this did so rather painstakingly. Very odd!

What does this all mean? I have no idea... I am guessing it is simply a silly easter egg. Probably left by a level designer as a funny “I was here” to his friends. An entertaining find none the less.

Or maybe we need to shine the Docker's headlights on it...?
