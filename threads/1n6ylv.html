Things I would like to see someone try @ Penris tower (with 100% completion):

  • Shooting each of the five stars in the Penris tower sign, using a sniper rifle.
  • Standing on top of Penris tower (and/or a building near it) and shooting at stars in the sky using a sniper rifle. Try a lot of different stars.
  • Exploring Penris tower thoroughly.

Regarding Maze Tower:

  • Stand on top of a tower nearby the Maze tower, and look at the fountain (which looks like a cracked egg) and attempt to line up the "map" found at Mt Chiliad with the egg. Supposedly the jetpack lines up with the "Mile High Club".
  • Try and follow the lines around the Maze tower to see if they line-up with the lines found on the "map" at Mt Chiliad.
