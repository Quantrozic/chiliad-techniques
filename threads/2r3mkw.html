Here are some maps that show a possible overlay for the Mural and how it might be translated from a diagram into directions and active areas. I have orientated the vertical axis at 15.5CW from north, in line with the observatory. Also the Mural centre, is positioned on the observatory obelisk, which is called Centerpoint. The crack in the egg lines up with the Vespucci canals and the Jetpack man's arms line up with the fork in the surface drain. Is this just coincidence?

A lot of the elements seem to fit between roads and terrain, e.g. the lighting bolt over the radio telescopes, hitting the prison. It might need tweaking and there must be more to it, but before I speculate, I think it would be good to get some comments and a discussion going.
