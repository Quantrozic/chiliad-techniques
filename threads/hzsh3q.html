Let's solve the yellow mural

Hi all, let's solve this yellow mural once and for all. Here's everything I've got, I'd love it if everyone could post on here everything they have on the yellow mural, whether it's theories, facts, speculation or progress, I want to see it.

Introduction

First of all, here is a picture of the yellow mural - http://imgur.com/gallery/ZJhk73w
The yellow mural is directly linked to the chiliad mystery, we know this from the chiliad mural seen here - http://imgur.com/gallery/7XmM4Ea
The general theme with this mural seems to be time and space. Other than the chiliad mural itself, this mural is seen as the only hint towards the mystery, and is R*'s way of telling us that the 100% completion chiliad ufo isn't the entirety of the mystery.

Theories

Left side of the mural -
http://imgur.com/gallery/jh8nfCt I'm most unsure about the 8 dots (top left), it seems to be part of a larger picture showing various curved paths. The middle dot seems to be highlighted and linked to the dot before it. To hazard a really stray guess, this could be hinting at multiple paths all leading to the same end, I'm really stuck with this one.
The bottom left image shows planets orbiting the sun, perhaps this particular alignment holds importance or maybe it's simply just showing us the picture as a hint. Of course we have the chiliad mural, but underneath it is a human figure and a lightwave with an arrow running along the top of it, pointing forward (the exact length of the wave). Interestingly, this lightwave has 26 ups and downs on the wavelengths and 3 dots on different parts of the waves. When you assign each wave a letter you get the letters, E, N and U. The nearest reference I could find to ENU was "East North Up", which is a common method of navigation to get anywhere at any height (you can use - for up to be down, east to be west etc). As for the arrow, it's either telling us to look in one direction in a straight line or telling us to decode the waves from left to right. I personally think it's the latter. Underneath these waves are 5 lines in which I can tell you absolutely naff all about because I have no clue. The only images I can find of these are really pixelated, I'm sure someone on here knows what these actually are. Lastly, the eye in the top right of the picture caught my attention due to the pupil actually looking like a crescent moon, I think this is a reference to looking at the moon.

Middle of the mural -
http://imgur.com/gallery/dvlNt4h starting from the left, we have a hand with an atom in it (The power is in your hands perhaps?), a barcode or random vertical lines and an angle leading to a square. The square itself looks a lot like the map icon of the cart that leads up to mount chiliad, but I think this is showing light reflecting on to a surface. In the middle we have a huge star map, this shows a stars location based off the stars around it, except this isn't our star, there's also an alien pointing to the the designation. I think this is showing us the star system of an alien planet. I'm yet to do this, but I'd be interested to see if the night sky in gta has this exact star constellation. There's 3 cut off semi circles towards the top middle that I don't think hold much relevance. On the right side of the picture we have a human head and lines to show the viewing range of it, this is facing to the left. There seems to be a lot in this mural about viewing things in particular ways.

Right side of the mural - http://imgur.com/gallery/7bl8uJh This side I think will be the most informative once deciphered, to the bottom we have an audio wave that should be able to be deciphered, here's a closer picture of it incase any of you sound tech guys can translate it - http://imgur.com/gallery/erNDJSK To the top left is what looks like a satellite gravity map where the gravitational effects and orbits of planets and moons are taken into consideration. In the middle of this is in image showing the bending of space with arrows pointing to different phases of the moon. To hazard yet another guess, perhaps this means space time or maybe that the moon phases affecting the space around it. So maybe, things in the GTA world physically change depending on the moon and we need to look out for them. Lastly, to the far right is a diagram of sorts that I am clueless about as well as what looks like a circle finger print (I'm sure this is something obvious but I can't figure it out).

This is everything I have on this, I know its not much but I'd love to see your thoughts on this mural and everything you have on it. The more we work together the more chance we have of solving it.
