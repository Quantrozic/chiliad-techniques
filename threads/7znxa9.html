For that you need:

  • trainer with custom teleport function. I used this.
  • coordinates (see below)
  • understanding beast logic =)

After you ate latest golden peyote you will spawn at "b0 START" and see your first corpse. Second body will be only one of these - b1 deer, b3 near LHouse, b4 bridge. You need to teleport to next body only through all intermediate points.

For example, you assume that second body will be b3 near LHouse, you need to teleport in this way:

0->3 (1)
0->3 (2)
0->3 (3)
b3 near LHouse

If there is no deadly corpses just teleport back to your previous body and try another way.
Now you assume that second body will be at b4 bridge, you need to teleport in this way:

0->4 (1)
0->4 (2)
0->4 (3)
b4 bridge

Suppose that you find second body at b4 bridge. Next body will be only one of these - b5 car, b6 puma,
b0 START.

Yes, game can force you to get back and you can walk through some points several times.

Suppose that now you are at b6 puma. Next body will be only one of these - b3 near LHouse, b4 bridge, b7 containers, b8 hippie. If next body is b3 near LHouse, so teleport in this way:

3->6 (3)
3->6 (2)
3->6 (1)
b3 near LHouse

So, next "body number" can be either more or less and it must be with current number of point in my names(for 7th body number "b7 containers": 6->7, 7->8, 7->9, 7->10).
Not sure, but if you will be trying teleport to dead bodies at once for faster detecting the right way - it may cause problems and long quest...
