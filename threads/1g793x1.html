The Doomsday Heist, Gave us what Chili's was supposed to be, but I believe Clifford will make a return in GTA 6 or Future DLC
My speculation stems from Multiple DLCs Post doomsday heist

  1. The Clifford Clones, are Active, working independently. They appear during the diamond casino heist setup mission where we need to get the laser cutter, for whatever reason they have it. My speculation was to get into mt chiliad silo perhaps, as nobody goes in there until the ULP Missions

During ULP, after retrieving Clifford's code on USB from Mt chiliad, Clifford's clones come after you.

  1. Ending of ULP Paperclip is left ominous. Clifford is in IAA hands, but possibly with nefarious intentions, we know the IAA, will use weapons, and terrorism to boost funding, as shown with nerve gas we steal in single player
  2. Zancudo bunker In the Zancudo bunker there is alien technology, coveted with Clifford's Eye logo all over possibly hinting Clifford had something to do with the reverse engineering of Alien technology, and The Weapons. Rockstar is known for environmental story telling. I even thought possibly it's a slight nod but The San Andreas mercenaries dlc introduced A Clifford juggernaut that hinted towards the upcoming criminal enterprises dlc (may be wrong on dlc name) featuring ULP missions.
  3. Coil Weapons Division In multiple late DLCs we are introduced to Coil Weapons Divisions Crates. Avon Hertz owned Coil.

I could go on and on but I feel like rockstar is trying to hint towards Clifford's return in future dlc or gta 6 dlc
