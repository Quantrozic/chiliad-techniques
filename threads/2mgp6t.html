This is the last and only remaining theory that I have pertaining to the mystery. I've wanted to try it for a while but didn't have the time back then and now I can't play the game for at least a month. If anyone is interested in the proposal I hope you'll try to verify it. It's a long and boring process but it has some solid ground I believe. I'll do it eventually because I want to get my closure on this mystery (until someone finally solve it/or it's released on pc and the modders do their work...) and this is, for me, the last thing to try.

So, someone needs to take Michael, in Epsilon robe (it might me be a condition, if not it won't block things at least), and go to mount Gordo. Then make sure to never leave the area until you're done with the testing (like in the Epsilon quest in Sandy Shores). You'll have to stay here for at least a whole moon cycle, so approximately 30 ingame days. And I would say to do it during two in game months to be sure (if it starts on a particular moon phase or something like that). Every day you must do yoga and not leave the area. I would advise to limit the area from the yoga spot to the lake on the down side of the cabin. The lake has been suggested before to be what the hippie camp represents (near a mountain, the tree, the yoga spot, facing Chiliad, the lake itself). Do yoga every day when the sun rises and maybe at 23:00 also. When you've finished the yoga session go near the lake, maybe you have to swim. Take care of the cougar if need be but avoid shooting too much. But be cautious not get killed. Do a lot of saves. Endurance is the key here.

There may be other variables (if anything), be imaginative but whatever you try don't leave the area, don't change characters and don't miss a yoga session per day. If it doesn't provide any results I guess the red dots at the hippie camp just represents the location of the underwater UFO. And the main mountain in the hippie camp represents Chiliad, not Gordo. Even though everything points to Gordo (the geography, the hippie camp, yoga, the full moon party, the abduction).

If anyone who knows how to read the code please take a look at anything that is related to Gordo, to yoga, and if there's anything similar to the working mechanics of the Epsilon mission where you have to walk the five miles in the desert and not leave the area.
