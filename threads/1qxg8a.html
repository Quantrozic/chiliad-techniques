I'm certainly perplexed by this mystery. Often, I find myself coming back to the drawing board looking for anything I may have missed during the first hundred reviews.

That said, although maybe not directly related to the mural, the UV Map reveals secrets and seems to be mostly related to GTA:Online. There are somethings that seem to be referenced in SP as well.

First question: http://imgur.com/831kyCs,tnf7Bg9,Rfm2TSi#0 - I have not been able to figure out what this means. "In the drink". After searching, I've seen posts about it referring to the small cave area on the beach where multiple items can be found such as a spaceship part; but, other spaceship parts are marked with the little alien symbol on the UV Map, so I don't think that's the answer.

Second question: http://imgur.com/831kyCs,tnf7Bg9,Rfm2TSi#1 - I've read that "Rail Cabin" refers to a shotgun, but upon inspection, I haven't been able to find a shotgun nor anything that seems to be of interest.

Third question: http://imgur.com/831kyCs,tnf7Bg9,Rfm2TSi#2 - What's this refer to?

Additionally, I found a sniper rifle on top of a tower at the Egg factory. It's not marked on the UV Map as I understand the general consensus is the UV Map is used to show where weapons are hidden.

I did use the search function in this subreddit but I didn't find anything related. Apologies if this is a revisit on some of these items.

In terms of the Mural: I believe the UFO depicted refers to the sunken UFO. The egg, I think, either refers to the egg factory although I'm not nearly certain. I think the egg may refer to a rebirth or something in relation to the Altruists as I believe the Altruists may have drawn the Mural since some of the glyphs and the eye at the top of the Mural are similar to the sun and eye graffiti in the camp and on the UV Map http://imgur.com/2aoJUFr . This makes me think the eye is not the hologram UFO but rather the sun. Therefore I think there's a possibility it could mean that something is to happen when the sun is high above the mountain - noon. Perhaps that's a time where the player is required to start some procedure leading to unraveling the mystery. These are all shots in the dark as I review the evidence and attempt to make connections. I've been exploring and attempting to debunk my idea before posting here. But, this is a place of collaboration and I thought some of you may share the connections.

Furthermore: Does this graffiti look like the Space Docker? http://socialclub.rockstargames.com/games/gtav/snapmatic/myphotos/photo/UZJ0hbPoTkacXPCyd0fnsQ

Thoughts?

Edit: I've found that the graffiti that resembles the Space Docker is repeated elsewhere and is not significant.
