The first thing I thought when I saw the mural is that there are 2 or 3 prizes, and we have to complete steps for each before going to the ufo on chiliad.

Maybe not prizes, maybe starting points of a sequence.

Reading through the history of posts it seems most people are trying to solve it by trying to do 5 things at once.

Wear robes, when it's raining, when it's 3am, at a location, on a certain day. 5 things at once.

To me it looks like 5 things to do in a sequence. Go here, do this. Go here, do this. Go here, do this. Etc.

Has there been much investigation into the sequences or has it been mainly just 5 overlapping items at once in most cases?

With all the timed events it only makes sense to do things at certain times, meaning you can't do 5 things at once. They should be done separately.

Anyways, just my thoughts on it. I'm sure I'm way off the mark but wanted to share.
