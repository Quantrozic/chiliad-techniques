What do you feel to be the canonical story? (spoilers for storyline)

Documented under:Choice-Tree Ending Permutation MappingManhunt-Universe Easter-Egg Cross-Reference ?Moral Choice & Playstyle Branching Analysis ?

This is something that has been bugging me for a while and it would be nice to sound it out and get an idea as to what others feel would be the actual canonical storyline to the game. Please note - This ignores any side missions not impacting upon the main narrative, easter eggs or anything relating to UFOs and the like.

As we know, there are multiple options throughout the storyline for how to approach the heists, who to hire, who to kill and who to let live.

It's clear at the moment from Rockstars advertising of the upcoming GTAV DLC that their intention was that all three main characters would survive until after the events of the game (unless it is set during the events of the main storyline). Therefore, ending C is the canonical end.

But playing through the game, I've noticed little things that don't reflect how I've played it. I've discounted them as bugs but maybe they hint towards a canonical story. For example, I took the submarine option for the Merryweather Heist. After the heist, I saw online reports that a cargo ship had sunken in the docks, which I understand to be the result of the other option. So is the game hinting at the other option being the correct one?
Another 'bug' is Amanda constantly saying that Michael cheated on her and to stop visiting strippers. I never did with Michael so I guessed this happened before I took control of him, but maybe the game was suggesting that I should have for it to be canon.

Has anyone else had similar issues? Do you have an idea as to what should be the canon storyline?
