My second post, and I'm trying to post only really solid / useful stuff - obviously lots been suggested but would just like to hear other ideas based on this. I think there is a few very strong (in my opinion) clues that give us a general area where the 'jetpack guy' could mean for us to search.

Basically, the 'red-eye; atop the Mt, under the platform is facing west, so if you take the mural and turn it on it's side, so that the 'egg' is over Paleto Bay. We now have the location of the 'egg' over the egg-clock (see wiki). Now is this the only broken clock in the game? if so, why at 12 o clock? well isn't the crack on the mural egg coming from the 12 position, and a crack means a break, break = broken. broken egg clock.
From this same position, if you look up at the mountain, don't all the 'x' marks match to the glyph positions and isn't the 'ufo box' roughly in the same position of the sunken one. There is also a small one-off (at least i've not seen others) set of floating piers near the beach, which sorta, would make the bottom line under the egg on the mural (a bit clutching on this one) - but all in all, taking into account those weird RON symbols, the various egg related things (cluckin bell factory and the town adverts which say 'nest egg') Paleto is mysterious place.

That leaves the 'jetpack' location, either out at sea north-west of the cable-car, or near the Altruist camp. Roughly in that circular area. Now it's not exact, but the mural is rather rudimentary and not exactly pinpoint, so it's meant to be rough I think, so that you go and search. All comments welcome, lets do this, keep up all the good work fellow hunters (i love reading other posts on this sub) (plus I like parenthesis) (like a lot)
