  • The anagram "Jetpack Confirmed" exists in the Jack Sheepe Servicing sign (as we all know) - but several letters are unaccounted for...

I was bored at work today and decided to tackle the other letters. Here are some of my findings - and although I'm not a big believer in the 'Jack Sheepe' theory, I came up with some compelling results.

Using EVERY LETTER of the sign:

  1. Receive jetpack filming sonar dish
  2. Receive jetpack from shining dials
  3. Receive jetpack from in dish signal

-> Or as SuperMaruo suggested: "Receive jetpack from signal in dish" makes more grammatical sense! Probably my favorite one...

Yes, all 3 phrases use every letter (including tri-manifold etc) and I was actually pretty shocked... I'm a big believer in coincidence - but I wanted to share these nevertheless.

Here's some more excluding a letter or two...

  1. Receive jetpack in formal shindigs
  2. Receive jetpack and film origins
  3. Receive jetpack and film risings
  4. Receieve jetpack from his island (missing a 'g' from sign)
  5. Jetpack confirmed, shine laser
  6. Jetpack Confirmed, Revise Signal
  7. Receieve jetpack in flashing dorms (Zancudo dorms?)
  8. Receive jetpack riding on flash (Back to the Future!)
  9. Receive jetpack from dish signal (minus the word 'in') -----------------------------------------------------------

*Again, I'm not saying these are anything beyond coincidence - but some of my findings were actually pretty shocking... The ability to make coherent sentences like "Receive jetpack from dish signal" is pretty wild (considering the controversy surrounding the FIB and huge dishes). I plan on finding more soon and I'll post what I come across!
