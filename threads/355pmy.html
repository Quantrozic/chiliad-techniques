There is a point in the game where Amanda calls you and asks you to think about your decisions and to please be good from now on and he says he will but the player makes the final decision on the matter. It is around the end of the game when there is just a couple of missions left before and setting up the big one. She makes a point to tell Michael, and us by proxy, to stop doing the things he is (we are) doing. I theorize that at this point in the game, this is when your decisions begin to matter. Late in the game, after they've hinted at things for a long time. I am replaying to get back to this part of the game, and will provide vids when I get there again, I couldn't find the call online anywhere yet. The idea is that you get the bulk of the percentages, and the over-100 that is possible is because of this area where you can begin to decide for yourself.
