Orange Crush depth, the ol' hook-'n-tug, the Chiliad Fuckery: Test-tacular huntathon update #1.

▲ 19 ptsu/RdEydFcdJun 16, 201413 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Modding & In-Game Manipulation (Trainers / Script Hooks)

So I've started working my way through the "simpler" ideas, or the ideas that don't involve more setup than an Ocean's movie. A handful of them were ideas I've been sitting on like a mama bird for months, and a handful of the ideas people sent my way either in the last thread, or through PMs. Still a lot more to check out, but here's the things I've managed so far.

Underwater/Ocean Stuff

  • I went around the entire edge of the map at near-crush depth. I kept as far out as I could while still having seafloor to explore, and stayed against the edge of this figuring it was less likely that people would have explored the very edge than the areas closer to land. Kept an eye out for caves and out-of-place objects, but found none.
  • There is, however, an arch in the Eastern ocean that's right at crush depth that you can fit under. There's a few underwater arches out there, but this was the only one that you could enter, and even then, you still start crushing. I don't know that it's something, but it was the most interesting thing I found. You can find it on your map, going to parachute jump 11, then going dead right with your cursor until it stops moving off the Eastern coast. It's roughly where the cursor stops, at crush depth.
  • I made runs closer to shore when the topography seemed interesting, but didn't find anything. There's not a ton of searchable real estate down there, but visibility is an issue, so there still could be something there. Given the number of people playing the game, though, I'm pretty satisfied I hit the spots that are the least likely to have been searched.
  • I tried going farther out to sea than you can by boat by repeatedly using the Skyfall cheat, but at a certain distance the game no longer lets you use it.

Cargobob Stuff

  • Thinking that maybe you needed to take something vaguely egg-shaped/related to the top of the mountain, I tried hooking the ball from the juice stand, and the Cluckin' Bell chicken/bell. Also tried hooking the dinosaur from the diner, figuring fuck it, they lay eggs too. No dice on any of them.
  • I've tried hooking a few interesting things, thinking that maybe something needed to be pulled to activate something. The statue in front of the observatory, the Sandy Shores satellite dishes, the UFO station wagon and painted truck at the hippy camp, and the top of the lighthouse were all things I tried. No luck getting anything to hook.

(Open to suggestions here, I'm sure there's still plenty of shit to try hooking.)

Mt. Chiliad/UFO Stuff

  • Tried making it to the Altruist camp with the UFO spawn window with a parachute, but could only get there as soon as 4:10 am. Definitely not possible on dirtbike. It's possible with a chopper, but as far as I can tell, nothing happens. Checked the barn and the shack on the hillside specifically, but no doors open.
  • Checked the mountainside clue glyphs in a chopper during the UFO spawn window, and nothing seems to change. Can't reach most of them on foot, though.
  • To follow through on an old theory I had, I tried to see if, were I to land a chopper on the Hippy camp UFO, it would show on the Chiliad UFO. Landed the chopper on the UFO, switched to a character on Chiliad, but no chopper showed up. I'm still all-in on those two UFOs being one and the same, but this test came up short.
  • What I did notice is that even when the Chiliad UFO isn't visible to you, the reflection of its lights still show up in the mounted binocs on the platform. I don't know exactly what this could mean (if anything,) but it's interesting.
  • I had an idea that maybe the Chiliad UFO would stay visible as long as one character was always in a position to see it, so I tried bringing 2 characters to the platform. However, the game froze every single time I tried to switch between characters on the top of Chiliad. I could switch back and forth if only one character was there, but when 2 characters were both at the top of Chiliad, it never let me switch one to the other. This is the only place in the game I've come across this bug occurring so consistently.

The freeze-glitch has be soured on a bunch of the ideas I have involving more than one character in the same place, but there's still plenty of other places to try it at. Still lots of other variables to look into, too (moon phases?) so no worries so far. To the people who sent me messages: I'm still looking into those things, just want to take my time with them.

In the meantime, I have enough e-cig juice to put Lucile Ball's ghost into a nicotine coma, and more Gatorade than an NFL sideline in Florida, so I'm ready to keep going.
