tl;dr can someone try raising the water level (ie a reverse of the most water mod) to make it easier to explore and hopefully to be able to see things from the ground/under the water parts themselves

also if you can confirm it is done another way and this won't work, that'd be helpful too, but I've noticed in some cases viewing things from where you shouldn't makes things invisible. An example being how HL2's dirty toxic water is only meant to be seen from above. If you crouch in it with god mode so it doesn't kill you, you can find a shallow spot where your POV is level with the surface of the water, so you're looking exactly along the surface. Also occasionally viewing liquid from underneath also makes it invisible and makes things seem to be floating.
