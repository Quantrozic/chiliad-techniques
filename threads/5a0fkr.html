Hill Valley Cemetery, tile marker, and Halloween

▲ 18 ptsu/grininjokerOct 29, 201617 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Orthographic Map-Tile Mosaic Stitching

First, and foremost, all due credit to walkeronline for seeing this in one of his great videos.

I did a post search for this area, and didn't find much other than a couple mentions that were dismissed rather quickly. (unless I missed them?) However, as walker spotted one of those cube markers just under the pond surrounding the small island with a couple tombstones, I think there may be some importance to this area.

Being that it's a graveyard, and Halloween, maybe there's some timing trigger there? SP or online. Zombies, ghosts, aliens, chupacabra...who knows.

Has anything ever happened here to explain the place marker? None that I can find, or recall in the single player. Though there is a R* designated team deathmatch here online.

A couple other things that may be worth a mention;

*Up the hill, close by, is a small memorial to artist Chris Edwards. (discovered here, I believe)

*Randomly, a small dog appears to sit in front of one of the nearby tombstones.

*Some of the graves have odd dates, with "10-62" instead. (mentioned here before)

*Nearby is the open grave with the handgun (widely known, I assume).

*A letter scrap was nearby (bringing attention to the area, maybe?)

Again, just bringing some attention to the area, spurred on by walker's video, in hopes of sharing some thoughts and ideas on what exactly this might be for.
