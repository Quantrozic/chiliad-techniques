[Speculation] Paleto Bay Eggs

▲ 57 ptsu/d_winchOct 8, 201326 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Livestreamed Group Hunts ?

I was just going to the parachute jump in Paleto Bay, and I noticed there is a structure with egg shaped clocks on. Oddly, the structure has three sides but clocks on only two sides. One also has two 'beams' attaching to the clock, while the other doesn't.

After seeing this I decided to see what other egg themed things were around. Obviously there is the Cluckin' Bell factory, and Paleto Bay hosts a chicken festival. Check the imgur album for a few other egg based pictures. I would imagine that the egg on the mural must be based here, there's just too much of a focus on eggs.

I looked back at the mural image and thought that maybe the 'cracks' represented two hands of a clock, so I was going to wait until the hands matched the 'time' on the image. That's when I noticed that the hour hand doesn't move from 12. It looks like it moves slightly, but I think it just goes back and forth. Seems pretty weird!
Also, if you face Mount Chiliad from the clock area the sunken UFO is to your left, as on the mural.

It may be worth also pointing out that while driving from the area I saw fruit trees, which I haven't seen anywhere else. If you hit them fruit will drop from them. The fruit tree is a lie... Kifflom!

I'm not yet at 100%, so it seems pointless to look around too much.
