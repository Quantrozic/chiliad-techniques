Radio Mirror Park DJ Host monologues - Hidden code?

▲ 40 ptsu/DifficultylevelMay 22, 202110 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Mural & Map Mirroring

I've transcribed the Radio Mirror Park DJ Host segues in the hopes that there is a hidden code contained within it and perhaps working out what it is that we might be able solve.

The reason for this is that these monologues are scripted and appear to contain instructions or locations. I think if we can brainstorm their contents we can unlock a set of actions to get us closer to an easter egg or two?

All help here or on discord, welcome.

\------------

You're listening to Radio Mirror Park, which means either you're a tryhard or someone far into the future, just rediscovering this on an old playlist somewhere. So those of you from the future, I welcome you.

Those who are tryhards from the present, turn over. Listen to something more in keeping with your demographic.

I gave up wearing antique demin ok, I'm not one of you, it's over.

\-------------

My God I can't stand you people, I'm not even going to give you a name.

You know who you are and you know what you are, even though you try to deny it.

Oh not me, I just happen to be into wax moustaches and looking like a street urchin from Charles Dickens.

It's over, move on, the party has been shut down and your name was never on the list.

\-------------

I cannot believe it, someone stole my bike last week. What's wrong with people?

I was just at the farmer's market, trying to find some locally made organic cat litter,

I dont have a cat, I just don't believe in water rights, so I use the stuff myself, it's really freeing.

Anyway, the bike yeah. I was gone maybe an hour, I came back, the bike gone.

And before you ask no, it was not a Fixy I am not a cliche, don't be ridiculous.

It was a penny farthing, that is a bike with meaning.

\-------------

I mean what is wrong with people? Everyone has become such a cliche.

I'm over all of it, really over it. I mean there's noone original left, everyone is into the same things.

Foreign movies, plaid shirts and facial hair...and authenticity.

I am the original authentic, I was here first, I was here before anyone even knew here was a place, I am here!

\-------------

The problem is I suppose friends, is that when everyone wants to be alternative, then noone is alternative and when everyone is original in exactly the same way, then noone is very original.

And I've been worrying about this alot and occassionally playing some free form jazz while thinking about it.

I sold my llama farm a long time ago, it was a metaphorical llama farm ofcourse, very post irony.

\-------------

I'm not one of you, you want to be like me. There's a difference you see, a big difference.

I'm the person, people like you follow. I am authentic and that's why I'm selling my place in Mirror Park to someone just like you and no, I won't tell you where I am moving next.

\-------------

This is Radio Mirror Park broadcasting nothing but music you'll want to pretend you discovered.

Don't worry, your secret's safe with me. I protect my sources too and I'm a hundred percent locavore about everything.

\-------------

Hey where'd you get that antique plaid shirt? None of your business.

Where did you get that hand pressed Tofu? At a shop called none of your business.

What about that amazing creamy fresh Mozarella? From a humanely raised, emotionally stable loco buffalo I know, called none of your damned business.

These are my choices people, make your own.

\-------------

I'm the urban ninja of cool and the thing that best defines me is the fact that I'm not a vain self-absorbed, plaid shirt and skinny jeans wearing, crowd following narcisist.

I plow my own furrow, I liked the 80's before you did and I like it in the right way. I know it sucked...and I know why.

\-------------

I'm gonna check out this fantastic performance art piece later, where a group of people in black onesies take apart an antique

typewriter. They eat each piece, defacate it out and put it back together and type a letter to future generations.

Really original stuff, you probably wouldn't comprehend.

You're on Radio Mirror Park. We're miles ahead of the music industry, once these bands make it big, it's over.

\-------------

Radio Mirror Park, if you have a request, we don't take emails or bleets or even phone calls.

Get a fountain pen and some paper and write us a letter. Seal it with wax from your own special seal.

Make a collage, do something artistic for once. Art is whatever you say it is, especially if you're charging other people for it.

But Radio Mirror Park is the ultimate art, art that disappears a few seconds later and you can tell you friends how good it was and they can all wish they were there, that's what fuels irony, envy.

\-------------

I can't bear the idea of genetically modified food, despite the fact that I am allowing a smart phone and computer to digitally and genetically modify my life into a place where I pay attention to nothing, am constantly interrupted and never devote my attention to anything for more than 30 seconds.

What was I saying? Oh yeah, tofu crabcake recipe.

\-------------

I don't follow anyone on LifeInvader, I don't follow anyone period and neither should you.

Why be a follower, when you can be out there on your own, in the desert of life, wrapped in your own self loathing, battling binges of going slightly too far with the drink and the drugs, carrying a lunchbox, using a cane and wearing a Top Hat and a unique suit.

People will remember you, you're one of a kind.
