What is up guys, its been awhile since I've posted, mainly for the gross amount of new people to this reddit since the new-gen release and the sheer lack of knowledge that has been previously provided by this reddit.

So here is where I'm at. What is the one clue that we have that guarantees our minds of all the alien easter eggs and the theory of a jetpack? The mural on top of mt. chiliad. I believe that when your looking at the mural the x's need to be looked at in a muli-dimensional vision. Meaning, look at them in a flat version then look at them as if they are all around the mountain. The x's represent choices and they also represent places.

Lets start with the places:
Bottom Left- Ft. Zancudo
Top Left- Altruist Camp
Bottom Right- The Observatory
Middle Right- Hippie Camp
Top Right- Mt. Gordo (yoga)

These places aren't just hints for the eggs that are located there but they symbolize ways of living your life. To unlock the egg means you unlock everything. So you have play the game right down the middle. The mural matches the saying "Some look left, some look right, but the answer is right in front of us."

I'm not saying this is a karma playthrough, or you must play the whole game the way you think it should be played. There is one correct path. That any or all the characters can take. The line in the middle represents the correct path. If you take the line to can lead you astray, just the same for the path to the right.

So there you go, 1 game, 5 choices, WHAT ARE THEY?
