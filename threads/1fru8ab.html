Extended dialogue chances

▲ 21 ptsu/TontorsSep 29, 20245 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Whisper Bulk Transcription of Dialogue Banks

Im sure most here know there is alternate dialogue in many missions after failing a mission. Mostly just 2nd takes with slight variations but occasionally its quite different. A few years ago when I was playing I was doing the mission "did somebody say yoga" and noticed something I had not known before. Once Jimmy gets the drugs from the friendly neighborhood drug dealer and you drive away everyone hears the dialogue that starts with Michael saying "Alrighty then now what?" and ends with Jimmy saying "You're too crazy dad bye" before the Alien cut scene starts. This is what I assume 99% of people hear as thats how the mission works but if you leave the parking lot of the burger shot to start the dialogue and then go right back into it and park the mission cant end. So Michael drinks the drink and gets fucked up and the dialogue plays as normal but it keeps going as the mission cant end in the parking lot. Michael sits there stoned babbling about things like the steering wheel is as big as a farris wheel and how everything looks like its in 2D. There was another thing or 2 he says that you wont hear if you play it regularly. Anyway Im doing my first playthrough in years and got to that mission and did it again and sure enough there is an extra minute or two of dialogue that wont normally play. I checked a few videos on YT to be sure I was not mistaken but everyone had the standard lines in them. Nothing new was said that I can say is related to the CM but it got me to thinking if there are any other missions like this one where if you dont let it end more might be said. Im not talking about slowing down to let the dialogue finish before reaching the yellow markers like we all probably do but playing it "wrong" to force more talking. So can anyone think of another mission where there is a chance of extended talking?
