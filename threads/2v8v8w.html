The Worn Glyph, an examination detailing the partial moon.

▲ 30 ptsu/orlin002Feb 9, 201511 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Moon-Phase Condition Testing ?

There have been various threads/posts that have made both correct and incorrect references to the worn glyph (bottom right X on the mural). The main goal of this post is simply to provide some visual information about the glyph to help people better understand the appearance of the glyph, and of course use this thread to reference the information in the future if it is needed.

My focus in this thread will be on the moon portion of the glyph.

Here is the glyph highlighting the moon portion with selection: http://i.imgur.com/Dbpn6fEh.jpg

As one can see, there are lines in the paint that forms this section of the glyph. When compared to the moon glyph (upper right X on the mural) we can see these lines on this section of the worn glyph match lines in the moon portion of the moon glyph. Moreover, we also see the shape of this portion of the worn glyph is also a match to the corresponding part of the moon glyph that contains the matching lines in the texture.

Here is a comparison of the matching lines on the two glyphs; left is The moon glyph, right is the worn glyph: http://i.imgur.com/2LlFxEkl.jpg

Here the portion of the moon glyph is rotated and adjusted proportionally only and overlaid onto the moon portion of the moon glyph: http://i.imgur.com/MNGF7O6.gif

Now, for additional perspective, here is what the texture looks like laid flat like the moon glyph texture; black is the part of the glyph that is not shown in-game (note I have not accounted for the missing part of the UFO/eye portion of the glyph): http://i.imgur.com/cymuNJk.png

Here is a comparison of the above image with the worn glyph as it appears in-game in order to help people understand what is being shown texture-wise: http://i.imgur.com/Lfevqytl.jpg

I hope this more in-depth look at the worn glyph is useful in the future. I know I have provided information and images on this topic before, but today I decided to formulate this new collection of material that helps exhibit the glyph better and is, I hope, easier for people to understand visually.
