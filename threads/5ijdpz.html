Revisiting the UFOs - is there a fifth?

▲ 6 ptsu/nitheoristDec 15, 201613 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Dependency-Graph Prerequisite MappingHidden Map Art & Environmental Pattern Recognition

I've just stumbled onto this having reached 100% in GTA V, and there's an observation I haven't seen, or at least my trawling hasn't unearthed it. I’m a dab hand at pattern recognition, frequently spotting connections in my life that are too abstract for most.

There are three boxes on the mural that seem to signify pathway stages. This is important, we should rule out nothing as accidental in the fundamental design of the mural.
We know that one UFO is found outside of the 100% requirement, and can be found at any time in the game. The underwater UFO. Therefore our current knowledge proceeds in such a manner:

Procipio bay UFO > 100% > Three other UFOs.

We must also consider that the five boxes marked X mirror the five unique glyphs located on Chiliad itself. The pattern matches too well to be a coincidence, therefore we can safely assume these boxes equate to the five glyphs. Source:

The sequence is unclear but what we know for sure is the 100% requirement is the prerequisite to satisfy the UFO icon stage.

When the community found the Zancudo, Hippy and Chiliad UFO sightings the pattern began to emerge alongside the glyphs.

I have but one conclusion, there is a fifth UFO, or a fifth indicator, but would stand to reason it follows suit and another UFO remains.

The community has not progressed in its hunt, because we have not satisfied the condition to move past the current stage. The correlation between the glyphs and the UFO locations are practically undeniable. It might be worth investigating the height of the boxes as they correspond to the glyphs as the underwater one would be one of the two lowest and the Chiliad and hippy camp ones would be the highest. If there is a pattern there, it might suggest the relative y axis. Since one UFO is submerged, perhaps the final one is hidden even moreso, either further in the ocean depths, or a little higher, in line with land mass.

Of course this is speculation but the patterns scream out at me. The five white glyphs are a piece of the puzzle, the red glyph atop the mountain is the beginning of the road (and perhaps the end). There is no way Rockstar simply left this at finding three UFOs at 100%, which happen to so elegantly fit the glyph indications.

We’re not done here.

I do not see how the glyphs can be eyes - with a line intersecting them.
