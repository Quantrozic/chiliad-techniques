The West Coast Is... An Island?

▲ 45 ptsu/doofy102Oct 26, 202020 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Manhunt-Universe Easter-Egg Cross-Reference

If GTAV is set on an island that’s in the middle of the ocean (that has western and eastern coasts) why do they say that the island the western coast of a larger continent? I know that GTA has always been set on islands in the middle of the ocean, but what if V turned it into a mystery?

I wonder if the land of V was once an actual west coast of a larger continent as the game’s characters itself would claim it to be. Has the West Coast been snapped off of the mainland, taken somewhere else and looped around to make an island? Is that the explanation? Is that why there’s a freeway that circles the island and doesn’t really go anywhere? Is that why, North of Chilliad, by the Up an Atom place, there’s a jarring split down that freeway that you can see from above which looks like two seperate freeways that have been smashed together? Is that why Trevor smuggles guns to Mexicans, bizarrely, north of Los Santos? Was the West Coast itself segregated and rearranged by FIB UFOs? Are we meant to unpack the entire island map so that it resembles an actual western coast? Is part of the map actually meant run south of Los Santos? I spot some suspect and abrupt change in terrain amongst the freeway just south of the wind farm - green to desert. Right by the hand.

Why would any of this be true? What has this to do with the aliens, with chilliad, with the meaning of the story?

It would mean that V’s map is no ordinary island, it’s, among other things, penning Michael and Trevor to Los Santos and its surroundings. They’re unable to leave (plane always crashes if you try to fly out) unless it’s to North Yankton which is where, outside of Los Santos, the most important events of their story happen. Yes, I think this is to do with their story. Their choices.

The FIB guide the major beats of Michael and Trevor’s story by giving opportunities and forcing big choices. They give Michael an opportunity to betray Trevor, he takes it. There are consequences. They give Franklin (the character I still have no idea about) the opportunity to betray Michael/Trevor, the player decides. You see how to FIB open and close the story, hovering over the characters for most of the game. And they have a UFO. And some of them even have reptilian shapeshifter eyes, apparently? I read that somewhere? Anyway, the FIB/aliens are a force, it seems to me, that lock Trevor and Michael on V’s island, only letting them cross the void to go to North Yankton because that place is tied up with their conflict, their choices. This is why there’s a frozen alien in North Yankton. They’re stuck there, literally and metaphorically. You can think of it like “Lost”. That’s my theory, but I still have many nagging questions I don’t have answers to. Why does the real ending of the game (Franklin meets the Bigfoot suit guy) have the Bigfoot guy literally bark like a dog? Nobody ever asked about that. I have no idea about Franklin, man.
