Now for my theories and speculation for the "mysteries"
and ill try not to rehash things already gone over and deduct from what we can confirm.

the Chiliad mural-
Let me break down its components/

The mural itself is a conundrum of interpretation but theres things that stick out to us naturally. The three icons at the bottom, what do they represent. I think they are quite literal. The ufo is infact a real ufo in the game world for us to either see or pilot. the jet pack obviously for us to find and fly. The egg, a bit more confusing but believe it just to mean Solving the Easter Egg. Hence why it is in the centre and connected to everything else on the mural.

The Eye/Ufo at the top. maybe it isn't that at all. We see this symbol at the hippy camp and altruist camp, both seemingly revolving around yoga practices to praise the "sun" even in tract text the eye has been described as being the sun. I believe the epsilon tract tho convoluted has to do heavily with this mystery, even following rockstar tweets referencing lines from the tract as well as the wallpaper with the woman's arm tattooed with a phrase from it.

The X'ed out boxes. ill be honest i have no clue to what these can be. What i think we do know is that they are the keys or "steps" to get to the bottom of that mural. For a minute I was thinking From doing the epsilon missions and hearing there are a supposed 5 hidden or missing tracts is a befitting theory to this however, since it seems no way to really do we may rule it out. Someone mentioned they are the five glyphs to be found.

When talking about the glyphs we can confirm a few things. We do know what one of them means: full moon, 3am, raining and we see the ufo. with that let us look at what we can confirm as reference for the others to interpret. The lines around it indicated the time of day, and if accompanied by a cloud that is a weather condition.

some one also noticed the color combinations to them with red and black corresponding glyphs.

We need to determine the reason for color.
Red = an action to be done at the corresponding glyph
Black = what we need to see, or something of the like
(open to hearing suggestions)

everything about this is so subjective, and what about the connecting lines between the boxes? I don't have the answer to these yet but searching with you all.

The Hippy camp/

But, it seems it has its own thing to be done there with the Red Dots and Big Red Arrow giving. All in redline the 2 other glyphs. The color may signify that this is a command, something to be done. Find out what to do with the markings..

Another connection I haven't seen any one mention is the mound to the right with windows.. is ACTUALLY the chiliad mural.
With the eye/ufo glyph right at the top-painted on the roof floor, the windows on it - those are the boxes with x's all spaced just like in the picture.

it gives us another way to view the mural in a 3 dimensional space or perhaps something even more we haven't figured out yet but i am certain that it is the mural in a physical form.

This has to be something! i originally thought sense this was the only other place with representation of the mural, maybe we interact with this mound some how. Especially since it has the red glyph painted on it signifying an action, or we have to stand on it with the right conditions etc.

With all the yoga alien art work around the place its a clear identifier that yoga is a key to this puzzle. Otherwise there would be no reason for only a single other location asides Michael's house for it.
