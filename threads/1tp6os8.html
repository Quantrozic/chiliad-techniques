Omega Unused Lines - Debunk (Sorta)

▲ 25 ptsu/HeySlickThatsMeMay 27, 20264 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Debunk-First Review WorkflowPSO/META Schema Mining for Dormant Fields

One of the longest mysteries were unused lines for Omega left in scrapau.gxt2, these are also listed on the wiki for this subreddit, sometimes people still bring these up to "prove their point" or whatever.

By cracking the hashes, the purpose of these lines has been revealed, and for those who expected some deep conspiracy, a hidden mission or whatever - prepare to be disappointed, as expected!

It seems the original plan was for Omega to remain as an NPC after "The Final Frontier" until you get away from the area causing him to despawn, you could mess around with him which would trigger these lines which now are unused. These lines are variations for pointing a gun at him, bumping into him, hitting him or causing him to go away, that's all there is to it...

Before somebody makes another conspiracy theory - it's worth pointing out that "MESS" voice lines have number skips because there's a pause delay before each line, as those lines would play when standing still next to Omega as one long conversation, essentially him telling you to go away like any other NPC does in some missions. Although i have no clue what SHH and SHM voice lines are for, but i can assume they could be related to explosions or something similar.
