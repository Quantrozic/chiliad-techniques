First of all, I’m a huge fan of this mystery and have been reading your posts for of a long time now.

I do believe that there is a mystery here, but I think we have jumped to a conclusion that is incorrect. Way to often people is talking about finding the jetpack after solving the puzzle, however I don’t see it. When looking at the mural I do see something that could be a picture of a jetpack (I’ve also have a theory about it being a picture of a hiker, which would refer to ‘mount chiliad tourist trail’….but that’s not what I’m trying to say here). I don’t see how any logical way that this should be the goal of the hunt: To get one of the three things at the bottom of the mural. Doesn’t make any sense.

It is possible that you have to find:

  1. The Ufo
  2. The green egg
  3. A jetpack

But this would not be the end of the mystery, but rather the start point to reach the “all seeing eye”…what ever the hell that would be. Wouldn’t that be more logical?

This leaves us with two scenarios, (A) either we are suppose to find them from left to right (logical) or (B) that they are three separated things we need to find complete the hunt (once again logical).

(A) Then we need to find out how segregate and rearrange or FIB connects to finding the green egg somehow…

(B) Go back and try to solve this the same way that you guys once solved the UFO on top of MC. I would guess that it would take the same kind of clue logic.

So this is maybe not the best post to solve the mystery, but my hopes is to lay the jetpack reward theory to bed!
