So, there has been suggestions before that the cable car in game is modeled on the real life Palm Springs Aerial Tramway and this seems true.
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palm_Springs_Aerial_Tramway

If you look at the old style cars they used to use, they match the cars seen on the cable car in game (minus the advertising), also if you look at the valley station at the bottom, it holds a resemblance to the bottom station in GTA V.

At the top (Mountain Station in RL), the station was redesigned in 2000 to take the new rotating cars, but the old station (if you google, you will find a picture) again matches the style of the mountain top station in GTA V.

Now, obviously I wanted to know if there was a link to a Mural or anything in the RL station. And I found this.

http://www.desertsun.com/story/money/business/tourism/2014/08/08/palm-springs-tramway-muralist-transforms/13762635/ with the mural called trompe l'oeil, a French expression which means "to fool the eye."

But wait I hear you scream, GTA V was released in Sept 2013 before this was painted. Well it's not the first mural in RL in the mountain station.
http://www.avant-palette.com/about/news/news.html

I cannot find a picture however of the finished mural.

To fool the Eye is a interesting line as obviously we know the UFO above the mountain is a hologram/mirage to "fool" the eye. Also, there is a "eye" in the mural.
