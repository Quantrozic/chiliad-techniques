What's up fellow descendants of Kraff? Quick little post today. I've been replaying GTA doing a no cheats playthrough, but that's besides the point. Anyways, today I've been running the Epsilon missions. After the mission Chasing the Truth ended, I walked to the Grapeseed airfield just in time to see the Yellow Cropduster land at the runway.

For those of you wondering why this is important, the name Jack Sheepe appears on the side of the Grapeseed hangar. Jack Sheepe was a porn star who acted in the movie "The Cockateer" which was a parody of the movie "The Rocketeer"

The Rocketeer is about a young pilot who finds a jetpack in the back of a Yellow Plane. The Rocketeer is set in LA, which is where GTA V is based off of. All of this information is obviously already known, I just wanted to put some background.

After taking off in the plane, I flew around the map a little bit, going over the observatory and such, like he did in the movie, but nothing happened. Then again, I'm not 100% on this save yet, so I might not have been able to trigger anything mystery related. Just thought I would put this out there for others to test and see if it spawns for them. If this is actually a trigger, then we may actually have some kind of starting point for a posible Rocketeer easter egg.
