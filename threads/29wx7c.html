Karma translates directly to "Action", when someone says "That is your Karma" they are saying "That is your own doing."

Westerners have twisted Karma into some kind of "Karmic justice" which does not exist outside of our Hollywood version of Karma. If you want to play with "Karma" then your goal would be to play the game from the perspective of the character, and do what THEY would do, completely ignoring your own Ego's rambling. Forget "Karmic justice", only Michael would be concerned with it in that sense, hes the "Good guy" as the game says a million times over. Trev killing people is "His Karma", Franklin being a smart ass sharp tonged know it all is his Karma.

Taking into account Rockstar's level of detail in everything, I've found it really interesting how accurate their depictions of Yoga, hippies, and spirituality are. They obviously play a key role, so getting the notion of "Karma" right would have been just part of that process.
