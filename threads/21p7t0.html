I've been looking at the ELF sign a little bit and noticed that the S is dropped below the N.

http://i.imgur.com/ph66A8G.jpg

Then I saw this sign also with a dropped S, but its coincidentally under the N again.

http://prod.cloud.rockstargames.com/ugc/gta5photo/2207/rYzGG3JqnU6NpkiHfrNw2g/0_0.jpg

I know this is a long shot, but could there possibly be a code worked out from that. For example, you get an arrangement of words and then a hint to the coding of the message such as:

FIND OUT ABOUT YOUR ELF ON THE MOUNTAIN

KEY: N = S

So as we fill in the letters it looks like

ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUVWXYZ

________________S_________________

N is our first clue. There might be other letters given to us but this clue is repeated twice, it seems more plausible than an anagram. It's actually showing us a clue, where did anagrams even come from?

I don't remember quite how to figure though out, like working out what the other letter represent but I feel like I remember doing puzzles like this in grade school.

Also, maybe it doesn't necessarily mean every sign is coded, but some strange messages might be coded using this key.

Let me know what you think, I feel like it's not a coincidence.
