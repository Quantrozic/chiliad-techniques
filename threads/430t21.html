Finding the right ending

▲ 11 ptsu/BipolaricecapJan 28, 201616 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Choice-Tree Ending Permutation Mapping ?Moral Choice & Playstyle Branching Analysis ?

I've just finished my current playthrough by finishing all available random events/strangers & freaks before progressing into the next mission, doing the lester assassinations just before the final mission, purchasing all properties and doing all property missions. I saved it at a point where the only things left to do were the final mission, mrs Philips/damaged goods and the last one, hoping that achieving 100% on the final mission would make a difference, nothing noticeable as of yet. However, I have done the final mission all 5 currently known possible ways to see if anything stands out and it does. Firstly I killed Trevor, after the mission I changed to Franklin and he spawns outside his house saying bye to a girl in a taxi. He is wearing the 100% shirt, the only time in my own experience I've seen him wearing it without me actually choosing it. Next I tried letting Michael kill Trevor, again I changed to Franklin after the mission, same scene only he's wearing a league of basketball shirt. Next I tried killing Michael, changed to Franklin after the mission, same scene with league of basketball shirt. Next I tried letting M kill himself, changed to Franklin after the mission, same scene only he's wearing the 100% shirt again. Finally the deathwish option, this time when I changed to Franklin after the mission he spawns inside his house wearing what he was previously wearing in the final mission. Now, I have repeated these endings multiple times and it is the same every time. If there was a certain ending or endings that are required to progress in this mystery I'm sure there would be some indication, regardless of how big or small, to let us know which is the right option(s). The fact that we require 100% to progress in the mystery makes these two situations where Franklin spawns in the 100% shirt stand out to me. Could this be the indication we need to be able to choose the correct ending? Not sure where to go from here, I'm guna keep playing about with all 5 saves to see if anything happens.

One more thing, I think the whole Friedlander report can be put to bed completely, the report is completely different when finishing the game twice in the exact same way. When trying out different endings I had repeated each of them 3 times from the exact same save and for example, each time I killed T it was done in the same way and each time had completely different reports.

And also, completely irrelevant but something I noticed that doesn't appear on the wiki, the final mission when devins in the trunk the radio is playing I wouldn't wanna be like you, blatantly a dig at the 'be like devin weston' website! Ha!
