Golden Rules of Clues

▲ 15 ptsu/DRUMIINATORFeb 29, 201617 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Sacred Geometry Proportion Testing ?

The keys for unlocking hidden secrets in the game have required one or more of the following: 100%, Time, Day, Weather and Range. Our purpose is to find the right combination at a precise location.

The Chiliad UFO requires all of these. The ones at Zancudo and Hippy Camp only need 100% and Range. Peyote needs all of them but Range. The ghost needs Time and Range. The clues from the murder mystery require Range. Infinite 8 requires none that we know.

There are other factors like how the Peyote requires you to keep Bigfoot alive in a mission or how you can't access Solomon's office in the murder mystery before beating a mission.
