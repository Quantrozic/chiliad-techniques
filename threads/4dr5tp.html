Confirmation bias? A crazy idea I just had.

▲ 2 ptsu/oxpoleonApr 7, 201610 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Sentiment-Weighted Theory Triage

So, I sort of stumbled across the Chiliad mystery a while back, didn't really think much of it. Well, something suddenly came to me today. VERY IMPORTANT: It's only speculation but there's a reasoned argument here. Bear in mind that therefore there is not any concrete evidence to substantiate this theory.

I'm going to assume, to start with, that the generally agreed consensus regarding the mural is true, that it points to three major Easter eggs: the Space Docker, the murder mystery, and the third unsolved mystery depicted by the jetpack.

Now, possibly because most people on this sub have played SA when it was relevant, what they see here is what they want to see, namely, a jetpack. I want to question that assumption. As others have discussed, for the other two secrets, what is depicted by each box on the mural is merely a clue about how to find the Easter egg, and is categorically not the reward itself. So, in the third box, what do we see? A jetpack. Or, if you will, a jet pack. As in, quite literally, a pack of jets.

Why do I think this is relevant? Because it fits with other loose ends.
Primarily, we have the oft mentioned idents of Los Santos Rock Radio - always talking about "sailing into the sunset" and how you'd need "a very fast boat". Now, the host of LSRR is Kenny Loggins, probably best known for Danger Zone, which not only is a reference to a pack of jet pilots (Top Gun) but also actually appears on the radio in game. This suggests to me that the answer may lie not with a fast boat, but with a jet. Also, given the fact that the jetpack takes off vertically, I am speculating as to whether the Hydra is perhaps relevant. The reasoning behind my thinking will become clear later in this post.

We've also got rumours of an SSTV image of some kind of HUD. This might be fake and totally coincidental, but if not, to me it points towards the same thing, the answer involves a jet.

Now, I have a few ideas about what this could mean:

  • Cut content - there was supposed to be something involving jets, but it got cut. Perhaps the Hydra was supposed to be in single player as a reward?
  • All three protagonists are needed to fly jets into the sunset simultaneously for something to happen. This seems unlikely as it would be hard to coordinate.
  • Something in GTA Online.

It's this last one I am most intrigued by, for the following reasons:

  • Pretty much the only time in the game in which a number of jets appear is one of the heist setups for Humane Labs.
  • It's also the only mission in which the Hydra appears, AND the only mission involving going significantly beyond the landmass of the game.
  • Once the opposing jets are defeated, the radio is locked to Rock Radio playing Danger Zone. There is the obvious explanation for this, but taken in the context of everything else here, there's scope for interpreting this as a reminder.
  • This mission is centred around a boat which, if the time of day is favourable, is pointed towards the sunset, just like the radio suggests.

Perhaps it's total coincidence, but I can't help feeling that there might actually be something here.
