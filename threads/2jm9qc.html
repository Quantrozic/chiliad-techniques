Hi guys, it's been a while.

The other day I started doing a total skip playthrough. Will let you know if anything happens at the end.

Honestly, I think it might be the way. Ideally you should play the game first. Basically you would complete the game, work out all the clues to find the holographic ufo, look at the mural again (like we did, in frustration lol), and logically deduce from the symbols that to get a jetpack and/or ufo depending on perhaps some of the choices, you can play the game from scratch by skipping all missions to end up with a different prize. Here's why I think skipping all missions is the answer:

  1. The Mural: The Egg is a non-skipped playthrough, it's path is clear of skipping, so to speak, so there are no X's in it's way. The UFO & Egg however, are blocked by X's, i.e the path is filled with X's. The difference between obtaining a UFO or a jetpack may lie in which ending you choose.
  2. The power of three: Three bottom symbols on the mural (in the boxes), 3am for the holographic ufo (another hint), 3 characters, and 3 times failing the mission section before you can skip.
  3. The X's are obviously what you get on each mission if you skip each section; you essentially get bronze and no recorded mission time or stats, so to speak.
  4. The sinners passage mural: "For when the great scorer comes to write against your name, he marks not that you won, but how you played the game." Even the word mark in this quote, mark's are usually made by X's. Marks are either made by X's or ticks, generally. So he "marks NOT that you won", meaning he marks that you skipped/failed everything, he marks that you lost.
  5. Another world is possible, the fruit tree is a lie: A mission world of complete failure, where all is skipped and no merit is achieved throughout the entire story, thus balancing the karma aspect; you as a player chose to skip all the events and the story came back around to balance things out again, depending on the end of course. This makes sense to me as per all the karma references. As a player, you are choosing to do the opposite of what the game is making you do: essentially you aren't killing anyone and your not listening to the voice of evil that tells you to do bad things, instead you refuse and skip everything.
  6. The buttons for consoles: X on xbox, square on ps: these are the buttons to skip and they are included together at the same time on the mural.
  7. Epsilon: even the epsilon tract seems to be some bizarre analogy for this kind of playthrough; I'm not gonna try and go into detail because it's just my opinion, but read it with this kind of playthrough in mind and you might see some hints.

There's probably a lot more. But I think this is the most sensible approach to the mystery, it all seems to fit quite well. So far I've skipped every single section of every mission by failing; either by running off or blowing things up lol.

It sounds so simple but it does kind of take its toll, but I'm gonna try and stick with it and let you know what happens!

Has anyone tried this? I've noticed that some heist choices result in things that you can't skip; the Vangelico one for example, curiously the loud & dumb option has a big X all over it, if you choose that then you can't skip the part where you have to hijack the LSPD vehicle. But option B allows you to skip the bugstars van mission.

Little things like this are starting to make it seem like I'm on the right track.

Thoughts, opinions, stories, etc?
