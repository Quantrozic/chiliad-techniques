An idea about the Xs

I'm sure we've all seen numerous images of the GTA V map with the mural placed on top. But I'm not sure anyone knows the correct way it is meant to be placed on. Most start by putting the top of the mountain on the mural on the top of Mt Chiliad on the real map, but that's about it. No one knows which way it is meant to be rotated.

So maybe we could put the mural on the map, with the peaks corresponding to each other, and rotate the mural in a full circle whilst drawing a line along the Xs.

Basically, say the map was on paper and you could put the mural on top, match the peaks, then put a pencil on one X, and rotate it so it makes a circle. Now if the X is on the map, it'll have to be somewhere on the circle. Then repeat for the other Xs, and follow the cirlces around.

This may seem like a long shot, but I feel like it's worth a try. I'm not able to play, but if someone thinks it sounds logical, maybe they could try it. Just another theory
