Has anyone else witnessed an American Indian soapboxer in GTAO?

TLDR: A few years back, I heard an American Indian soapboxer in GTAO during a mission but can’t find any reference to it. I think the mission was “Chemical Extraction” which has since been removed. It seemed like the triggering of the speech was a bug. Has anyone else experienced this or know of the audio file?

—

During an online mission, in humane labs, while in the garage that opens (see wiki walkthrough video for exact location), the sound changed like with soapboxers (other sounds are reduced so that the speaker can be clearly heard) and a guy who sounded like an American Indian started talking about his people’s history. It seemed like a bug, as there was no soapboxer around and no reason for the speech to have been triggered. I’m pretty sure it was the mission “Chemical Extraction” which has since been removed.

I was playing with a friend who was talking at the same time, plus we were under heavy fire. As I was trying to juggle not dying, listening to my friend, completing the mission and this curious speech, it was pretty hectic, so I wasn’t able to listen to the whole thing. We had spent quite a while in the garage as we had been pinned down and were low on health, it was only after a bit of time that the speech triggered. As I remember it, the sound faded when I left the garage, as if I was walking away from the person talking, just like with soapboxers.

It was probably 2018 and at the time I was pretty intrigued and went back to the spot, looked around but didn’t think to try repeating the mission. I recently started playing again and thought I should look into it more as it was the only strange thing I’d actually witnessed in GTAO, plus it seems unreferenced. That’s when I discovered that the mission that I think it is, had been removed.

According to the wiki page the mission, along with a few others, was removed, reintroduced, then removed again, which is kind of curious. Also, there are no soapboxers online. Maybe it was cut content that somehow got triggered by a buggy mission. Still, I’d like to listen to it in full, as it might provide some insight. I don’t know how the patching process works, but it could have been completely removed. I did a quick look through the game files, but no luck. So, if anybody else heard this or which file it is, please let me know.
