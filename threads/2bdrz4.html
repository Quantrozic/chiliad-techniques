The Jewel Store Job

Paleto Score-no choice available, possibly certain gunman you have to hire? I don't know, I didn't research this heist

Bureau Raid

Big Score

I think the mural definitely represents some kind of play style, coupled with the posts lately about karma, back stories, and personalities of the 3 characters, I think that not only one of these two paths I have chosen for heists is correct, but also the above three things are somehow implemented.
