I think I may have found the next step in solving the puzzle that reveals the correct interaction that Trevor needs to have with the Altruists in order to trigger some kind of alternate ending.

In addition to bringing the Altruists the correct victims in the correct order its highly likely that we need to deliver them under the correct time and weather conditions as well. There are many clues that hint at the fact that triggering an Eclipse during the delivery is one of the hidden conditions that must be met.

All of the paintings and graffiti referencing the Sun are a clue towards this, with the tag “See the Eye!” being the most direct clue as half way through the duration of an Eclipse it will form the shape of an Eye. Since this cult is heavily based on Heaven's Gate it's highly likely that they believe the Sun or the Eclipse itself is some kind of spacecraft that they need to sacrifice their victims to. Many of the Sun symbols around the camp closely resemble a UFO, with the painting in their hidden cave bearing the closest resemblance.

There are 6 Eclipses that occur within the 55 Day Position Cycle of the Moon. The Eye only forms if the Weather = Rainy during one of these 6 moments that the Sun and Moon cross paths. The Eye will NOT form if the Weather = Thunderstorm or if the Weather = Clearing (weather type triggered after banging stripper Juliet).

This means that our weather condition is solved, the weather must be Rainy in order to trigger the Eye.

The next step of the puzzle is figuring out which of the 6 Eclipses is the correct one to trigger during the deliveries.

The 1st Eclipse and the 2nd Eclipse in the Moon Cycle occur at Sunrise and are unable to be seen during the Altruist delivery cutscenes. This likely means that we can rule these Eclipses out as far as being the correct ones.

The 3rd, 4th, 5th, and 6th Eclipses all occur at Sunset and are visible if triggered during the delivery cutscenes. These Eclipses all appear at slightly different times of day and in slightly different positions in the sky.

There are two telephone poles that are prominently seen in the background of every Altruist delivery cutscene. During these cutscenes the 3rd Eclipse appears in perfect alignment between these two telephone poles while the other Eclipses are slightly off center appearing either behind one of the poles or completely outside of the two poles.

I think this may be a clue that the 3rd Eclipse is the correct one to deliver the victims under! The 3rd Eclipse is also the Eclipse that's in perfect alignment with Mt. Chiliad from the perspective of Michael while doing Eclipse Yoga atop Mt. Gordo! This is all likely a nod to the concept of the “3rd Eye”.
