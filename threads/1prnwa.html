Theory: Observation decks with telescopes

▲ 25 ptsu/talkolNov 2, 201334 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Hidden Map Art & Environmental Pattern Recognition

There are a few observation decks with telescopes in the game.

The most important one is the observation deck on the peak of Chiliad, right next to the mural. This is where the biggest Easter egg so far, the Chiliad ufo, appears under the correct conditions. The ufo is "limited" to an observer on the deck, and as soon as you step off, it disappears.

What if the other important locations we are looking for - are observation decks as well? and an observer has to stand on them under the correct conditions to see the next piece of the puzzle?

I was looking for the next one, and I had a feeling it is related to the hippie camp (this is the only other place with Chiliad-like glyphs). I keep expecting the hippie camp ufo to descend from the sky and do something near the ground.

And indeed, there is another observation deck near the hippie camp which is a perfect match to the Chiliad one.

It is located about 400 ft south east of the hippie camp. It has 2 telescopes (like Chiliad) and it even has the same "Chiliad View" sign in the middle. It is overlooking the hippie camp with mount Chiliad in the background, a view too perfect to just be a coincidence. The hippie camp is not much of a view, isn't it weird that there are telescopes pointed right at it?

I stood there with 100% on 3 am in the rain but so far didn't see anything. But I have a feeling there is something out there.

Do you recall any other interesting decks with telescopes? I think there's one overlooking the penris building too (another glyph-related spot).
