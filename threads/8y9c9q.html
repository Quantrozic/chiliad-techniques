Alien code at hippy camp

▲ 12 ptsu/VegemiteGeckoJul 12, 201810 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Iconographic Color-Code Analysis

I'm fairly new to this mystery (weird after 5 years I know) so if my thoughts on this code are old I apologise. I tried searching it but couldn't find anything the same, and I don't have time to watch 3000 hours of youTube (kids and work and shit), so here goes.
Coming into this mystery fresh the three things that stand out like dogs balls are the Chiliad mural, the wow signal (why put it in the sand as well as the camp otherwise) and the alien code around the hippy camp.
I've mainly been thinking about the alien code and how it could be applied within the constraints of the game, and what we can influence or follow, the main one's being time, place and some sort of condition. I'm trying to put together a list of what they could be, so i'm hoping someone has other ideas of what the code might mean from a visual perspective. Imgur
For 1 in the image above I'm thinking it may represent a time of day. I thought this because of the way the obelisk/armillary sphere at the observatory puts the ball in the sundial into shade for a short period each day, and the ball turns almost black. R* did such a great job recreating that area I couldn't help wondering why they placed the sundial on the north side when in real life it is on the south so it is never in shade (unless they had to move it so it didn't cover that seam in the path, which opens up to reveal an alien spacecraft hanger).
For 2 I'm guessing the lines on the outer may mean 'between' or 'within'. The circles within, I thought possibly a place, being the three domes at the front of the observatory building (the outer two have openings in real life). My other thought was time related again, as in the moon phases or something. Between the second quarter to full to fourth quarter or some such shit.
For 3 I thought place, the obvious one being a representation of the water towers from above. There's a couple of other landmarks that would look similar from a UFO's eye view, like the plaza outside the Weazel building, and a couple of tanks at the dock area.
But 4, fuck knows. I was trying to think of how this could represent a condition to be met, then I could try a time of day, time of moon cycle, a place and then... something.
Any thoughts?
