I feel like the jet pack shadow on top of Chiliad was meant to point us in that direction. For those that don’t know, there’s a bat on top of Chiliad. When the sun hits it just right, it creates a shadow that looks like the jet pack symbol from the mural.

Maybe it’s just Jesus Toast, but I’ve always thought it would be a pretty good way to hide something. You’d have to be in the right place at the right time and even then, when’s the last time you actually looked at shadows? I’m not talking about looking for shade on a hot day, I mean actually paying attention to the shape a shadow made.

Idk. There’s probably nothing left to be found, but it’s fun to think about.
