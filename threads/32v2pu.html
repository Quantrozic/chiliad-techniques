Can we collaborate with other groups of hunters outside of Reddit?

▲ 12 ptsu/_TheCredibleHulk_Apr 16, 20158 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Livestreamed Group Hunts

Hey guys,

I just want to put a thought out there. We know that there are other communities out there searching for easter eggs in this game, but there seems to be minimal crossover/collaboration. People on GTA forums and other places are searching too, imagine if we were all posting our ideas in one place, if we were all working together? So many needless repostings of the same thories could be cut down, and if we joined up with others we may come to new conclusions simply by talking to people outside of our group. Talking to the same people about the same things tends to lead to stagnation and prevent innovation. I think we need fresh perspectives, not just from new hunters but people that have been doing this for as long as us... Anyway, if anyone has any ideas on how we could all meet up and share ideas I think it'd be great. It can't hurt, can it?

If you think this is a crap idea I understand, but please don't post negative things, it doesn't help. Just downvote or move on and read something else please.

Kifflom brother brothers.
