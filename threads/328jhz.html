Some random thoughts on "Segregate & Rearrange"

Documented under:Anagram and Constraint Word-Solving

Just some random brainstorming here.

Has anyone ever figured this:

On the Chiliad and Sandy Shores UFOs, "FIB" is written on them.

On the Zancudo UFO, "FIB" is nowhere to be seen, and instead the only writing we see is "segregate and rearrange."

What if it's telling us to segregate and rearrange the word "FIB"? Which, obviously, we know one obvious answer is "FBI." But there also the other outcome of "fib," which is another word for a lie.

So what if it's telling us that the UFOs are a "lie," or in other words, the UFOs are man-made by the FIB? I'm not sure where this would lead us, but it definitely makes the Sunken UFO stand out more.

I also don't think that seeing the UFOs after 100% has anything to do with a 4th-wall-prize. Think about it. The three main protagonists mess with the FIB the whole storyline. So maybe the three UFOs were cloaked the whole time, and after something that our three guys did the FIB decided to uncloak them? IDK, just some food for thought.

Tell me what you think! I'm not trying to disprove anyone else's theory, I'm just putting out my own. Like I said, I don't know where this will lead us (if it's a right answer) but it's just an interesting observation I had.
