Why is the Chiliad UFO a glitchy mess?

▲ 30 ptsu/DRUMIINATOROct 15, 201523 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Director Mode Scene & Spawn Testing

After looking at it numerous times and from many angles and distances in Director Mode, this thing seems really glitchy.

It begins to disappear once you walk towards it, leaving only the lights, eventually you get almost directly below it and the entire thing is gone but actually still there (you can get a lot closer in Director Mode than Story). The beam of light only appears using a sniper or phone and if you do this while its faded you can see where the light is produced within the texture. Also the sound effects for it eventually fade out after an hour in game time.

Not to mention the FIB logo spins the opposite direction than in the old version of the game. You can see a red dot that was intended to be covered. This has never been fixed or even addressed.

I don't understand why it works the way it does for an end game reward??
