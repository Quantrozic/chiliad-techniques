Change in the 'scientists & FIB at the satellite array' pattern after much messing around

Hola.

This may turn out to be yet another nothing but I've been drawn recently to investigating the scientists / FIB agents at the satellites. It just seemed odd that it would repeat day after day, almost as if it's waiting for us to interact with it in a certain way. Anyway, to the observation.

After trying various methods of clearing the agents without scaring off the scientists failed, I changed tack and with all 3 of the guys together, I decided to just sit there for 24hr (gametime! lol), in one of the FIB vehicles, to see if anything different happened. Bear in mind at this point there are 3x dead agents on the floor, 2x vehicles which I had moved off the spawn point to allow for respawn.

What happened next was new (for me). A single scientist turned up, no bodyguards. He looked older than the previous scientists who in fact looked the spit of Ray Wise/?ref=tt_cl_t6

I had T as the leader of the party. When I approached this lone scientist, he said: "Woah bra, you need to find peace". Seems like a bit of an odd thing for a scientist to say. Unfortunately he then started getting a bit agro so M and F gunned him down immediately.

When I waited another 24 ingame hours, a guy with a clipboard arrived, he too met the same fate as all the others who dared to square up to Uncle T (ie gunned down by M & F).

I'm going to have another go at this line of enquiry later today, would be great to get some confirmation from others who've gone down this line that this is not random occurrence and that something of note is actually happening!

Kifflom to all hunters.
