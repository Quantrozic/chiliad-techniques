Hangout with A triggers drug-induced stumbling at Old Man's Crack with autosave & Capolovaro hints

Hi all, I'm still intrigued with Hangouts atop Chiliad involving M. tldr My suspicion is that there is a trigger that will allow one of the characters to tumble down Old Man's Crack as in the movie, and interact with the boulder in the pass, triggering a mystery event.

Here are the steps I have taken to see the result (glitch?) in title:

  • Watched Capolovaro on a Hangout with F during "Chaos"
  • Taken the minigun from Zancudo tower and escaped with 4 stars wanted level using the off-roader which you find inside the dock near Lake Z.
  • Setup a Hangout with A who will meet in Sandy Shores. A says "Michael you look well" or something to that effect on meetup. There is other interesting dialogue during the hangout.
  • Take A to tennis match at Michael's house, played 4 sets lost all. A says something to the effect they should do this more often.
  • Take A to Cable Car. Day is Wednesday time is approaching 1am with full moon. Use the different views while taking the ride to see the radio tower glyph.
  • Take pics of A in front of the mural using different filters. Noticed that A can and did glitch into the mystery room beside it sometimes, but I could not do the same with M at all.
  • Wander over to "Bell's End" looking for the seagull peyote, didn't find. Peyote may not be discoverable during hangouts.
  • Wander over to Old Man's crack pier which has a barrier on a pier jutting off of Mt C. Time is just after 3am. Walk around the barrier with A who will follow.

Here's the difficult part. I wanted to get M to jump off the barrier and A as well (random idea) and then engage M's parachute. Because of the short distance if Meven steps forward off the pier he will usually commando roll to recover as if dropping from a short height, but he normally will then tumble down Mt. C. and get wasted for his efforts.

On my attempt described above, M stepped of the pier and then the animation for drug-induced (similar to when taking the weed from J or when taking peyote) started and M stumbled around a bit as music played. I used the controller to try and bring him back onto the pier but that sent him tumbling down Mt. C. A followed screaming (horrifically) and autosave occured during the Wasted portion.

Analysis I have done a lot of testing atop Mt. C trying to determine what the purpose of apparent stunt jumps and edges are in relation to mystery triggers. Here's what I've uncovered

  • There are times when the number plates on the dirtbikes atop the mountain add up to 100. E.g. 44 and 56. Colours vary.
  • Taking one of the bikes off Old Man's crack and down the mountain reveals a pass with a moveable bouder. Continuing on past that gets you across a railroad track into Mt. C. national park. Continuing further would land you in Paleto Bay I believe.
  • In the film Capolovaro we are shown many times the image of the protagonist rolling down the side of a cliff. (moved to tldr)

Kifflom brothers let me know your thoughts. edit:list formatting; add tldr.
