ENGINES

The brain works in similiar ways but have different bodies, just like the engine of the games and the whole game itself.

ENGINE DEVELOPMENT

Dozens of developers spend a whole year building or modifying the engine that will be used on a game, all they do on ther MACs is storaged at the servers at the engine data center (yes, they have one DC just for that). Now, lets see if you could debug an engine on your PC at your home...

The developers use a powerful MAC with special editions of softwares to develop or modify an engine. All they do is storaged at the servers. When everything is done they have to compile the files to build the engine. With a Rack with 12 Dell servers R640, with 1TB of RAM each and HD of 10TB each, plus a Dell Blade Series containing the systens and virtual images, plus a Sotage Server with 100TB on HD's take 2 days to compile the first part of the engine, and 3 days for the second. To compile the whole engine with the body, it takes a whole week...SO, a modern powerful rack with 12 servers would take 1 and a half week to compile, wich is the exactly amout of time it will take to debug...can you do that at your home?
