Best anagram tool?

▲ 3 ptsu/dwlaterJan 12, 20150 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Anagram and Constraint Word-SolvingAnagram, Acronym & Linguistic Pattern Analysis

I've been using this one:
http://www.wordplays.com/anagrammer

It lets you add words that aren't in its database (eg. Chiliad, Trevor), also lets you filter OUT words that you're pretty sure won't be a part of what you're looking for (eg. emphysema, blameworthiness).

Change the Max Answers drop-down from 200 to 1000, but remember, it will easily reach 1000 as you approach 30-letter source phrases. Just keep dragging stupid words into the Exclude list, till you get a readable sized list.

Anyone got a better one?
