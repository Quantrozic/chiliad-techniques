Possible explanations for the symbology behind the mural itself.

Look at it this way:

The whole thing is a big symbol. A sort of visual sentence, composed of many words, which are symbols within a symbol. Like words in a sentence.

  • The eye - the one that sees / the viewer / us
  • Paths - dead ends - wrong turns (Xs) - and 3 pictures which could be a symbol of 3 possible conclusions, 3 different endings, three different states (id, ego, super-ego) which was a theme in the game.
  • The direct path leads directly to a cracked egg. This only seems like an alien egg because it is next to a man on a jetpack, which gives a size reference impression. But i'm pretty sure it's just a symbol of a cracked egg mistaken to be a "huge dinosaur egg". The cracked egg could mean a breakthrough, a cracked easter egg. Or perhaps, given the references to personal development and "when your story is complete" stuff, it could mean a coming to senses and figuring out how to make it all work out, the breakthrough, ending C maybe.
  • ufo - ufo sighting. Can this be achieved regardless of the ending, just as long as you reach 100%?
  • multiple paths - could mean different switches have to be done in certain times to accomplish certain things in a certain way.
  • some of these Xs aren't Xs. One of them is a partial X and has a dead end path come out the other end of it. I'm sure some of these could represent certain paths to take during playthrough. You do get different bits of conversations depending on who you choose to go to the mission with. Sometimes michael and franklin arrive together having talked in the car the whole way while trevor was just sitting there waiting for them. Sometimes trevor and franklin have a funny conversation in the car, and when you arrive you notice that Michael had been talking with the FIB guy for a while. (So if you chose him instead maybe you'd have heard the FIB conversation? Maybe there are other, more subtle choices to be made during gameplay that most people don't notice.
  • the ufo and the jetpack are linked by a path for some reason.
  • lightning bolts all around, and lines at the bottom seem to wrap the mountain up. Happenings... entropy.
  • the mountain is often seen as a metaphor with paths to navigate it and "it's not making it there that matters, it's the journey."
  • top of the mountain is square, signifying man made structure perhaps. But it doesn't seem to hold a very important part besides identifying the shed.
  • the jetpack could have been a horse or a running man if it were meant to represent speed. Still not sure what it could mean.

Feel free to share your thoughts.
