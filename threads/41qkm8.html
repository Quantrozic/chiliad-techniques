I believe in the GTA universe, the moon was knocked off track, causing great floods to wipe out a lot of huge cities around the map.

If the moon was ever knocked off track and brought closer to Earth, here's what would happen. I found this from this website.

"If the Moon got much closer, say 20 times closer, it would exert a gravitational force 400 times greater than what we are used to. A mighty tidal bulge would be created, hitting the land and causing great flooding, with cities such as London and New York disappearing under water. When the Moon moves on however, the flooding subsides and the water will retreat."

Look at GTA 5's world map.

Now look at this world map.

The proximity of the Moon and the water levels it brings with it also explains why Los Santos is a massive island, and why Liberty City is a floating shithole in the middle of nowhere.
