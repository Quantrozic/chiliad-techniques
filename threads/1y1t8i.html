Time for me to stop lurking and start helping with this search. I have read a lot of theories about the lines, the most common ones being:

  1. They are a map overlay (assumed debunked)
  2. They represent logic decisions regarding game choices. Where I think this theory falls down relates to my thoughts below.

I think it is safe to say that the lines and glyphs were not needed for the 100pct UFO to show itself, therefore, we must consider that the diagram flows downwards. What I mean, is that the glyphs represent something that may or may not lead to the discovery of the 3 items detailed on the picture. This brings me to my key observations.

  1. The core line coming from the chiliad ufo suggests an interaction, path or connection. Given the line continues in the air, suggests any path would be by means of air travel on the part of the player or UFO.
  2. The x's represent glyphs that must be visited or interacted with somehow on the path of travel.
  3. The lines that come out the side of the mountain diagram are no coincidence, I believe these represent failed paths or interactions, I.e exit points in the metaphorical sense.
  4. This point is a theory unto itself, have you ever tried unfolding the cube at the entrance of the kortz center? Anyway, that's another story for another day.

My main point is that simply seeing a UFO upon game completion is all a bit too cliched and convenient, it must have a purpose. Going back to my point about the downwards flow of the diagram, I believe the UFO can be interacted with via sound, light or some kind of action to trigger something. E.g. Has anyone tried lighting the glyphs during the UFO visiting period?
