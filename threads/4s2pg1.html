Taking another look at "nothing"

▲ 30 ptsu/cbwysicJul 9, 201632 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Texture-UV Hidden-Message Unwrapping ?

The glass triangle at the top of the FIB building has always stood out to me. When you look directly at it, it looks like a triangle or pyramid (link to pics below), which we know has significance. It seems that pyramid is synonymous with Chiliad in this game. But, saying this is a link to Chiliad seems like a bit of a stretch without more to go on.

Walking up to it and peering through the glass, you are staring directly at the FIB logo. The logo has a bat in front of the birds face, which was previously written off as being nothing, but it could also be linking itself to the Altruist camp, which also has significance. Why would a bat/baton/whatever be in a FIB/FBI logo? They don't carry them. It would make more sense in a police logo, unless it were a clue.

Probably nothing again, right? But, there's a couple more things to notice when you are looking down through the glass where more links to the mystery begin to emerge.

The FIB logo used in the hippie camp UFO texture is basically cropped from the full FIB logo. You can see the extra lines around the letters, and when peering down through the glass at this logo I noticed the same lines, which were for the bird's feathers, wings and tail. Why crop out an image when there's so much detail put into this game, and such simple black and white lettering could have been cleaned up or even completely recreated from scratch in just minutes?

So, while standing on top of the FIB building, looking down at this logo, we now have ties to Chiliad, the Altruist camp, and the hippie camp, all places that are involved in the mystery. But wait, there's more!

The bottom of the logo has four stars, and unless you always play with your wanted level frozen, you'll know that four stars is what you get when entering restricted areas like Humane Labs, the Merryweather port, Ft. Zancudo, or the prison (which would make the most sense given the scales of justice behind the birds head, but that's probably a legit reference to the real FBI logo).

Perhaps this is all a coincidence, but you know what they say: once is a mistake, twice is a coincidence, but three times is a pattern. There's too many coincidences here to not be a pattern.

Here's a link to some pics: https://imgur.com/a/7lMO6 The hippie camp UFO texture was enlarged a bit to also point out the incredibly small letters hidden in the texture, I haven't seen a reference to these before. The one of the right looks like "SL152". The one on the left is a bit more unclear, but looks like "SL141". The leters are too small to be noticed in game so probably not meant to be noticed, unless a rogue developer threw a hint in there. These stood out because of the odd graffiti that is seen around the city that says "SC1_006".
