Idea for knowledge gathering

▲ 47 ptsu/trycrackonceMar 9, 201723 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Knowledge Graph Analysis ?

Been lurking a while now, the mystery is so great I just wanna find more! However as of right now, I feel like myself and probably a lot of other people dont really know what we can do to contribute. I certainly am not going to understand wiring diagrams, Morse code or be able to read the games code for clues. However I think there is a ton of lost potential in that this community only seems to have a few people really finding stuff while the rest kinda wander around aimlessly (myself included).

So my idea is this, lets have the moderators spot light a weekly theory or area that needs further investigation. The mystery might just be too big for one person to ever unfold on their own. We know Rockstar likes to time things according to Clock, day of the week, weather etc. Thats a lot of ground, a lot of ingame time to cover and an absolute shit ton to review. Lets start gathering data like a fishing vessel collects sea creatures. Each week the admins pick a theory of general direction to dictate to the community, then community members search out, find and upload any and all information they find on it. For example in one of the Tesla/Electricity threads, people were suspecting that the power line towers might be labeled abcd, put out a directive to find and label all the power towers with those markings on them on a map. Next week its something new.

The more information that is collected the easier it is to review and search for new clues, patterns etc.
