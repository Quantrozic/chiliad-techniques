[Speculation] Radio Mirror Park Song Clues

Documented under:Radio-Broadcast Subtext Concordance

Okay, im sure you all know about the references to a "lake with a mirror" in the epsilon tracts, and you may have even correlated this with Mirror Park. I have even more reason to believe Mirror Park is significant somehow.

After completing the story, i immediately noticed that images of significant locations appeared throughout the credits, accompanied by a very electronic/alienish sounding song (Colours-Age of Consent), as well as a couple other songs. These songs all share something in common, they all feature on the station "Radio Mirror Park".

After investigating the mission "Did Somebody Say Yoga?" for potential clues, i noticed something that makes me feel more than 100% that this is an intentional clue, the same song that plays during the credit sequence plays on the radio as you drive Jimmy to Burgershot, shortly before Micheal is drugged and abducted. As i have already mentioned in another thread, i already believe this mission's purpose is to hint towards the relation between yoga and aliens.

Considering the song features on Radio Mirror Park (as do a large variety of electronic/spacey songs), i am quite sure that Mirror Park may hold another clue towards the next step in cracking this. I dont think this has been discussed, but the more people seatching places that may actually be relevant, the better!

Oh, and i think we should all keep an ear out for location/activity specific background music (Epsilon building, Altruist camp, flying a helicopter at different altitudes, yoga, etc), as these music cues seem to signify clues or significant easter egg locations.
