Why is there a 1KB nw_countryside.ymt file separate from countryside_nw.ymt that spawns a pigeon on top of the Chiliad cabin?

▲ 44 ptsu/TaupyFeb 6, 202619 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Director Mode Scene & Spawn Testing

I might be reaching here, but I found something strange while digging through the scenario files in \\x64a.rpf\\levels\\gta5\\scenario\\

While searching for chicken spawns in the countryside YMT files, I noticed something odd in the northwest region file.

There are two separate YMT files referring to the NW countryside:

- countryside\_nw.ymt (29.57 KB)

- nw\_countryside.ymt (1.071 KB)

That immediately stood out to me.

Most of the other countryside region files follow a consistent naming structure, with the direction at the end:

- countryside.ymt (9.662 KB)

- countryside\_construction.ymt (29.2 KB)

- countryside\_ne.ymt (37.87 KB)

- countryside\_se.ymt (29.57 KB)

- countryside\_sw.ymt (56 KB)

\- northcountrysidebeach.ymt (22.59 KB)

So why is there a reversed format nw\_countryside.ymt instead of sticking with countryside\_nw? Even stranger: it’s only 1 KB. That’s tiny compared to the others, and could probably have been merged into countryside\_nw.ymt.

The file only contains two spawn definitions.

1. world\_human\_smoking

- Spawns near the Ron Oil gas station by Fort Zancudo (<offsetPosition x="-2566.87476" y="2313.82275" z="32.21674" />)

- Active between 11:00 and 15:00? (<start value="11" /> and <end value="15" />)

- Available in both Single Player and Multiplayer (<availableInMpSp>kBoth</availableInMpSp>)

Nothing special there.

2. world\_pigeon\_standing

- Spawns directly on top of the Chiliad cabin where the mural is. (<offsetPosition x="450.538757" y="5572.129" z="795.516541" />)

- Active between 07:00 and 22:00 (<start value="7" /> and <end value="22" />)

- Single Player only (<availableInMpSp>kOnlySp</availableInMpSp>)

This is where it gets interesting in my opinion.

The Chiliad cabin is one of the core mystery locations in GTA V. And this file which is oddly named and unusually small contains a pigeon spawn placed precisely on top of that building.

I don’t remember ever seeing a pigeon there even after spending some time there trying to get it to spawn.

I tried forcing spawn in Director Mode but couldn't get the pigeon to appear. Interestingly, I was able to force the smoking ped to spawn outside of its defined time window (at midnight), so either Director Mode ignores time restrictions or I'm misunderstanding how those values are enforced.

It all feel suspicious to me:

- Two NW countryside files instead of one combined file

- Reversed naming convention (nw\_countryside vs countryside\_nw)

- Extremely small file size (1 KB, only two spawns in it)

- One of those entries is a Single Player-only pigeon placed exactly on the Chiliad mural cabin.

- "NW" was also found recently in RDR2 (tree carving that says NW).

If anyone with deeper knowledge of scenario YMT behavior could help understand why that file would be separated instead of merged and whether Single Player-only flags are meaningful in this context?
