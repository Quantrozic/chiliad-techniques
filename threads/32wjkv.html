Hi guys. I've never posted on this subreddit but had an idea that I just wanted to share.

So I was playing through the campaign again since I bought GTA V for the PS4 (I previously had it on PS3) and whilst completing "Accepting the Truth" I noticed that the cardboard boxes in the room that you meet Marnie have barcodes on them.

Not having really taking any notice of these before I thought I'd try and use a barcode scanner on my phone to see if anything came up.

After a few tries I managed a match and I used the app to match it with products and/or a web search. The following came up:

http://imgur.com/entFYdz
http://imgur.com/udjgkic
http://imgur.com/CSeFBJq

Now don't get me wrong, I'm not suggesting that this in itself is of any significance. I just thought it may be an interesting idea that others may want to think about.

My main issue with scanning was that the barcodes were too blurry. With the PC version having come out though, I thought that this may make them clearer and more easily scannable.

Other matches that came up (although these weren't accurate matches) gave me locations.

Location 1 was some place in Joao Pessoa in Brazil, and the other was 4783 Talmadge Rd, Moorpark, CA.

As I say, I doubt that this is significant, but couldn't see anything else on the subreddit about it and wanted to share my ideas. Thanks for reading!

TL:DR: Can barcodes be scanned accurately ingame leading to clues? Better PC graphics would make this easier to do.
