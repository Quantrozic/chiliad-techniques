Hey group this weekend I got in contact with reddit user "themysteryhunter" in regards to his treasure map speculation post that was deleted a few weeks ago. He's forwarded me a copy of his work and after a brief read I think he could be on to something regarding a casino dlc. I will leave his work here and you can be the judge.but quick question for the code walkers, I know casino strings were found a couple years ago, has there been any new information found recently?

Hey guys after taking a look around the treaure map https://imgur.com/a/cEvB8 I believe the mountain with the trail at the top of the map would be the Mount Chiliad stream it just happens to be pointing in a different direction to throw us off. The tepees/skull and bones would be Stab City because we do not have tepees or a camp ground with tents in those numbers but we do have trailers and there's 2 banners floating in the wind one with a skull http://imgur.com/a/mLFDS and the other is a Pirate Music flag http://imgur.com/a/ZFoDL there's also a few skull posters hanging around http://imgur.com/a/GTIv1 http://imgur.com/a/SdewY and the stage itself has 2 skulls http://imgur.com/a/8X6UL lets not forget the purple skull EE. The hint for the pirate ship location can be found on the sign at Marlowes Vineyard https://imgur.com/a/CfN0b the wall the sign sits on is designed like an arrow https://imgur.com/a/4Q0zq which points directly at the cove where the cry cave is located https://imgur.com/a/r4XD3 The big dead tree would be the dead tree at the hippy camp because it's the only tree that's on par or in a straight line with Stab City. The spider is pointing to the property just south of Redwood Race Track that looks like its from the Red Dead era with the old wagon wheels, we can see a spider web on the bottom of the water tower https://imgur.com/a/WvnI7 http://imgur.com/a/VO0GE (Note: It's also the location of a gang attack involving The Lost) http://imgur.com/a/Jj7lr The beast would be where the peyote plant is found at Two Hoot Falls http://imgur.com/a/eCg46 The arch in the rock NW of the beast would be the Lago Zancudo tunnel under Fort Zancudo http://imgur.com/a/bPVKs The guy in the hole would be referring to the casino as the casino term for losing all your money is "I'm in the hole" http://imgur.com/a/A7ML6 The treasure chest with the "X" would be at the altruists camp, but the words "treasure chest" can also be found on a poster at the Pirate Music store https://imgur.com/a/eunXg

I feel the clues hint to online DLC's that we've received and some we haven't yet!. The arch tunnel or cave would be hinting towards the Gunrunning DLC and the bunker we can purchase just outside Fort Zancudo. The beast would be hinting to the Teen Wolf update which was released in the Free Mode Events DLC. The spider would be hinting to the spider webs added in either the Heist DLC or the Ill Gotten Gains DLC having been discovered by the community in the first half of 2015. The skull and crossbones/weed leaf would be hinting towards the Bikers DLC which gave us the opportunity to be in a biker gang and grow/distribute weed. The pirate ship would be hinting to the yachts released in the Executives and Other Criminals DLC. The hippy camp tree could just be a landmark as I cannot think of a DLC it could be pointing to.

Now if the guy in the hole is the casino that would mean an entertainment DLC, which could include Bahama Mamas considering its interior has yet to be used. There's a video on YT with the characters we could see in the casino from dealers to pedestrians. I've been busy lately so haven't had much time to work on this but I'm still trying to figure out what DLC's the treasure chest and the snake are tied to.
