In every thread I see multiple ideas that I cannot see the basis to. Things like "maybe sacrifice someone on the stone to turn on a radio signal to get abducted". We need to stick with the facts. We have no evidence for a radio tower being involved. What clue points us to needing to sacrifice anyone? Why is getting abducted part of the process?
People are finding things like the elevator at Zancudo and coming up with a theory and then finding "clues" that only slightly relate.

Clarification time.

The sand glyph

The arrow and the N means the direction north. There is no 'body of water' and it's not a sideways 'Z'. The N and the arrow has already been used in the game for finding the infinite 8 murder victims. photo. It means north.

The zig-zag is not a radio tower. It may not necessarily be a path up a mountain. Could be a river. Could be tracks. But it is not a radio tower. The zigs do not touch the diverging lines, the diverging lines themselves are simply at the wrong angles, and the box at the top has nothing to do with the symbol for a radio tower.

This leads nicely onto a slight rant. People! Why are you going on about the company that makes the dishes? Badger is a company in game that makes dishes. Ooh, fort zanduco uses badger dishes and so does that one guy who mentioned aliens, and the sandy shore radio tower does too and it even has a platform to stand on at the top! It's a conspiracy! /facepalm. The sandy shores tower may have a platform but it also has a ladder going to it that isn't even climbable. Not to mention there are no direct clues involving the tower in the first place! The company that makes the dishes has no involvement in UFO conspiracies. Also, finding a red light in Zancudo and another one in some car tunnel is not some mysterious clue, it's game designers using a cool looking game entity in other places where it would also look cool. Did you expect the game designer to use a different size red light each time they needed one? Or have each light a different hue of red?Repeated assets mean nothing until we find a UFO with "look for repeated assets" written on the window.

The WoW signal is interesting. It's understandable why it could mean nothing in regards to the hippy camp (it's the sort of thing you'd expect) but having it in the sand glyph directly involves it in the mystery.

The Mural

Now this is something I have been studying for a long time. The actual game file indicates how the mural was actually designed in the first place. The designer used a ruler and pencil, and was then scanned and edited to be put in the game. Now, whilst that seems like useless information it actually means a lot. You can see that lines were added and gone over in the drawing process. Lines that should be one straight line stop at T-junctions, and are then continued at a later point. If the lines were deliberate and based off an actual schematic, they would continue straight and the perpendicular line would be made after. It's hard to explain in words without images but I'm on my phone at the moment (I'll add them later). This indicates that the lines themselves are merely "doodles". Random lines in order to make the mural more interesting than it would be without just the shape of a mountain and red 'x's.
To support this, you see many other lines and lightning bolts in what would be blank space. It's a filler, it makes the mural more interesting. We should only be focusing on what is drawn in red on the mural. That's the vital information. There are no tunnels in the mountain.

The jet pack, egg and UFO do not represent the 3 characters, and they're not metaphors. There's no reason to think so. Sure, the egg could be a metaphor for rebirth, but that line of thinking ends there. Since when was a jet pack a metaphor for anything? Why the heck would Trevor be a jet pack? Because he's erratic like a jet pack? No. Stop it.

The jet-pack shadow

Now this is the biggest lead (in my eyes) so far. Rockstar knew the game files would be searched. Sure, we've found alien egg textures but these mean nothing if we don't know how to find it in game. Having dynamic clues that cannot be found in the files is the only way to make this mystery a mystery that cannot be cheated or hacked.

Now here's where I put my hypothesis forward.

We need to find the egg and the UFO. Now these can be shadows. It could be reflections. It could be looking a certain set of uninteresting objects at a certain point of view. The point is they will not be able to be found now matter how far you look in the game files. You need to look for them in game only.

So far this is all thinking on the actual evidence we have so far. The following is where I think outside the box.

We've found a bat being part of the jet pack shadow. I believe that when we find the "UFO" and "egg", there'll be a object involved. A gun maybe, or health. 3 objects that in turn will be the next set of clues. What these clues would mean we can't be sure yet. Maybe the object names will need rearranging, who knows. But in my eye that's a solid place to start.

Now! I want input, criticisms and ideas! Discussion is vital. Go!
