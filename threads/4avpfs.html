Trying to crack the code on the Famous Hamburgers sign.

▲ 24 ptsu/infidelcastro13Mar 17, 201630 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Iconographic Color-Code AnalysisRoman Numeral and Symbol Substitution Decoding

So, of course, when approaching and analyzing mysterious things in-game, there's some things you just can't shake off. This is one of them for me :

http://i.imgur.com/IQVOHpK.jpg

This seems to be a pretty obvious message, right?
I mean, there's this apparent code, JD O 19390 JG JEFF FIGO %0Y, and sitting on top of it, is this:

http://vignette4.wikia.nocookie.net/gtawiki/images/2/2d/FamousHamburgers-GTAV.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20140303004145

So, you know... Obviously, this looks familiar right? I'm fully aware this is nothing new. It's also totally unsolved.

Maybe it is nothing. You know, It is located on Pyrite (fool's gold) Ave. In reality, though, aren't we all chasing fool's gold here?

So trudging forward past the preamble stated above... This is where I'm at with it, and maybe someone else is fool enough to follow me down this rabbit hole -

Looking at the code itself, it's kinda clear that this can't be just an unscramble, a segregate and rearrange type solve to this. So next you look at types of ciphers. Being that it's an alpha/numeric code, you can rule certain types of ciphers out. But then there's also that percent symbol thrown in there which lead me to, " Maybe it's ASCII based?"
http://www.braingle.com/brainteasers/codes/ascii.php

So in doing that -

JD O 19390 JG JEFF FIGO %0Y

becomes

74 68 32 79 32 49 57 51 57 48 32 74 71 32 74 69 70 70 32 70 73 71 79 32 37 48 89

-which is still a bunch of nonsense.

So where do you go from there?

I found this -

http://www.cryptool-online.org/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=68&Itemid=78&lang=en

Which is a Homophonic Cipher, and is a little out of my league at first glance, and I'll have to do some more research on exactly how this type of cipher works. you can test it here -

http://www.cryptool-online.org/index.php?option=com_cto&view=tool&Itemid=79&lang=en

But as you can see it's a little more complicated than what it seems. And, also, it could be completely the wrong way to decipher. So there's that, too.

So if anyone want's to help take a crack at this, it would be much appreciated. I'm pretty sure I have the first step right. I won't be sad if I don't. It very well could be Pyrite, too. I really think we need to rule this one out or SOLVE IT, to get any further.

Thanks for reading. Kifflom.
