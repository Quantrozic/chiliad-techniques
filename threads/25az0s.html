Hey guys, not posted here in a while because I've genuinely had nothing to contribute to the search. This weekend however, I've had a couple of interesting idea's/finds. None of these are related to each other but I didn't want to make three seperate posts.

  1. Orion correlation theory

This one is a bit more on the raving lunatic side of things so if it doesn't tickle your pickle feel free to skip rather than abuse. I include this more on a whim of it being useful to someone else or just another thing to tick of the checklist as I can't come to any form of conclusion to it myself.
That said it pretty interesting read and I can see a handful of crossovers from this into Gta. The crosses in boxes (I know in this instance are to portray pyramids) are also used on the mural. The sun god is mentioned which is also prominent with the altruists. The sphinx was obviously used in the original San Andreas however its more what the sphinx represents in this instance for me. A creature which asks difficult riddles in order to protect a treasure or passage? That feels a lot like this mystery as a whole. Maybe its just me but when I saw it, I instantly thought of Chiliad Mystery myself. There's a couple more similarities if you use the links in the wiki article.

  1. FZ Bunker

Not this again, right? I read a comment on GTA forums months ago about how someone who after entering the bunker of Fort Zancudo without attaining any wanted level, had three guards enter the bunker and do something in the morning but I can't remember the entire post and cant find it again. I'm not sure if he/she was simply trolling but I have also entered without attaining a wanted level with no cheating, glitching etc. (If you want to do so too and don't know how what I did was put on stealth clothing and using a helicopter north of FZ I would parachute right at the bunker entrance and run straight in) I'm now waiting inside the bunker having autosaved waiting to see if any guards enter or basically something to happen without the alarms sounding.   1. Underwater and an idea on the egg

I've had a thought about the egg and the fact it glows in the dark. If this function had been specifically put onto the egg then surely it must be of importance. I thought maybe it can only be found at night? But in that case why bother making it revert back in sunlight, why not just make it glow continuously? Then it struck me that if it was to be found in the depths of the ocean where it is always dark it would help you to find it. However when going back up to the surface, depending on the time of day it could revert back to its chrome look.

So this weekend after reading a few posts about the ocean I weren't sure how much it had actually been searched without the use of the no water mod. So I set about having a look myself and spent around ten hours looking and finally found something that after checking on here I don't think has been posted before.
It could be just a texture mess up but there is an approximately 10 meter long stretch of floor which has sort of a translucent look to it and when you go beneath it, it has sort of a chameleon effect on your character where you can see your outline but you become part of the texture yourself. I find it really weird and the poor picture quality doesn't do it justice but its worth a little look. I should point out if you look at it from above you'll never notice it so swim along the floor.
