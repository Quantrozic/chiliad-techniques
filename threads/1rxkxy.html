TLDR: I don't think the Jetpack is DLC because that would be a dick move by R*.

Okay, so...Many people believe around 4 different things.

1.) The Jetpack is DLC

2.) The Jetpack is nothing but a nod to San Andreas, or it's a complete troll by Rockstar.

3.) The Jetpack is a backpack, or somebody possibly ascending to the stars.

4.) The Jetpack IS indeed in the game, somewhere. (This is my choice)

I believe Rockstar knew exactly what they were doing. I mean for all we know, maybe they delayed the game 5 months just to add onto OR create the Chiliad mystery. Rockstar knows about their fan base, and how much we all love their Easter Eggs, no matter how big or small. We can obviously tell that they took many cues from the old San Andreas, with Bigfoot, UFO's and being in a certain place at a certain time under certain conditions, etc...
My point is that Rockstar HAS to follow through with this mystery, or else their fan base will never trust them again. Sure, they may appease a few DLC gods if they add in a Jetpack in an update somewhere down the line, but for others who have painstakingly spent hours, if not days and weeks on this mystery, a DLC Jetpack would literally shatter trust with many fans. In the end, my biggest proof (for myself, anyways) that Rockstar added a Jetpack is not only the picture on the mural, but the fact that they are staying completely silent about the whole thing. Anyways, sorry for the novel. But I must know, what do YOU believe?
