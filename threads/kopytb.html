The Paleto Score mission does not have a fail condition

▲ 70 ptsu/CreathianJan 2, 202120 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Coordinate Teleport Sweep ?

Hey all, I've been apart of this community for a long time, and I recently learned something that I couldn't find mention of on the sub that relates back to an old theory.

Basically, back when people still thought the jetpack could be unlocked in the singe player, there was a running theory that we had to get all three protagonists in the same place at the same time. This was shown to be theoretically possible, though incredibly difficult to pull off.

Well, I recently discovered a YouTube vid from the GTA V speedrunner DarkViper that showed that the Paleto Score mission doesn't have any fail conditions during the part where you're driving the bulldozer as Franklin, with Trevor and Michael in the bucket (Scoop? Plow?) of the dozer.

Basically, you can go anywhere on the map during this part, and all three protagonists are present. Only downside is that you'd have a constant 5 star wanted level that may make exploration difficult. I'm wondering if this is something people have looked into at all? I personally couldn't find mention of this on the sub, but I wouldn't doubt that I may have just missed it.
