What other clues are we missing? (Cutscenes & Clues)

▲ 9 ptsu/ChiliadVibeTBDMay 22, 202027 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Frame-Step Cutscene Forensics

Basically, I think we are missing a lot of clues in dialogue or animations that the characters do. Whether its someone saying a line or two thats also lyrics to a song, or the characters doing certain animations that could reference a scene in a movie, I believe there are a lot of references and clues we're missing. Heres my example that made me very sure of this and kind of creeped me out:

At the end of the mission Fame or Shame, we all remember when Trevor and Michael watch Lazlow dance around in his underwear while Trevor films him on his phone. When I was playing the mission on my 100% playthrough, I noticed Trevor say "Alright, alright, now drop it like its hot, alright?" to Lazlow while he was dancing, and for some reason it stuck out to me so I looked up the lyrics to the Snoop Dogg song since its the most well known use of that line. I was more than surprised by what I found, and it made me wonder how much we're missing in the rest of the game.

Lyrics in the song:

"The Phantom, exterior like fish eggs"

Is it a coincidence that Rockstar referenced a song that talks about a black (fish eggs - caviar is dark colored) Phantom, while we're driving a black Phantom? And the lyrics reference eggs in the same line? I don't think so at all. Is this part of segregating and rearranging? Segregating the pop culture references and arranging the clues we find? This seems like a lot of work, but this one example made me think there might be more to this idea.

Another thing:

I think the fact that Bertolt Beach House is the first place we visit in Los Santos was a big clue to how the game was written. Bertolt Brecht was known for what he brought to "epic theater" and there was a lot of stuff on his wiki page about things that seem very relevant to the game. The "V-effect" stands out for sure, also known as the "alienation effect." From what I learned, it deals a lot with the "fourth wall" and how the "artist (Rockstar) never acts as if there were a fourth wall besides the three surrounding him ... The audience can no longer have the illusion of being the unseen spectator (the painted eye everywhere/on the mural?) at an event which is really taking place." There are a lot more connections and interesting things about Bertolt you can find on his wiki page so I won't list everything here, but this is one last quote I saw on there that I think connects to the Fame or Shame dialogue and the rest of the game and also how detailed Rockstar gets:

"Each scene, and each section within a scene, must be perfected and played as rigorously and with as much discipline as if it were a short play, complete in itself. Without any smudges. And without there being the slightest suggestion that another scene, or section within a scene, is to follow those that have gone before."

I thought it was too weird that Trevor casually saying "drop it like its hot" led to lyrics that reference the exact vehicle we're driving in the mission, and eggs. Theres also the connection of phantoms being related to ghosts, which could be related to the Jolene Cranley easter egg. Is the Phantom used for part of an easter egg? Either way, there are probably so many other "hidden" references throughout the story in the game, and I think it would be worth seeing what else we can find considering how directly the line Trevor says connects to "eggs" and what we were actually doing in the mission. I'm curious what everyone else thinks!
