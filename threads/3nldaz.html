Secret door in current gen GTA V mine?

▲ 34 ptsu/NormanQuacks345Oct 5, 201525 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Old-Gen vs. New-Gen Version Diff Analysis ?

Gameclip-https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q36-1aMetNE#t=63

When you blow open the mine door on XB1/PS4/PC GTA, and go into the mine, take the left paths at the forks. Eventually you will find a path that branches off to the right. Take that path, and once you get to the dead end, shoot a flare at the right side. If you do it right, it the flare will be on the other side of the door where the tunnel continues. I believe there may be something back there.
