This is going to be somewhat difficult for me to explain through text but I'll try:

The 3 bottom boxes, ufo, egg, jetpack, are the 3 domes on the observatory, with the egg being the center one and the biggest.

The squares with the x'es, the one on the bottom left is situated where the elevator is at the observatory, and on top of that, the X looks more like a satellite than an X, and the elevator at the Observatory has a satellite on it.

If you look on this square, you can see there is a line going down, then to the left, then down again. This would be the stairs near the elevator that go down one floor to some sort of restaurant.

If you look on the jetpack symbol, the line from it goes up, then to the right, down, and right again. This would be the area with all the power electricity stuff.

From the egg, the line that goes all the way up with the sun/eye/ufo thing, the thing at the very top, is the pillar thing at the center of the Observatory. This pillar has something that looks like some sort of eye on top of it, and it can be lined perfectly with mount chiliad if you stand at a certain angle.

And I remember the middle dome, the big one, had a crack in it when it was viewed through some texture viewing program, just like the egg.

I'm very sure that the Mural is the Observatory
