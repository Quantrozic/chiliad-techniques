Thoughts on Michael's big choice, and question about Run Epsilon.exe during Lifeinvader mission

Alright so I'm replaying the game for the 1st time, going to go for 100% again. Keeping an eye on the details this go' round.

FIRST

For the people analyzing the storyline, and the karma players, and the perfect playthrough people- i thought it was interesting that when people discuss the fact that Michael gets pulled back into a life of crime by having to pay Madrazo, he doesnt really have a choice. While its correct that he doesnt really have a choice, I'd suggest people replay the mission where he pulls the deck down. The whole car ride back afterwards, pay attention to Michaels dialogue. He's ecstatic. He sounds happier than he ever does at any other point in the game, and he claims that (and im paraphrasing) "this feels good! to be doing something again"

now im hazy on the exact wording but while in this truly happy state he goes onto claim that he should get back into the life of crime, with franklin kind of questioning him. i should probably replay this myself to find the actual quotes. but nothings the same as hearing it yourself.

Michael definitely has his mind made up about going back into the life, before he even knows that something is wrong. He admits and accepts his true self for this one mission, then spends the rest of the game trying to be something else, failing, and bitching and moaning about how he tries to be good but cant help it and its not his fault.

i know im rambling somewhat but it seemed the general consensus was Michael was thrust back into the life willingly. I'd argue he was ready to jump back in with Franklin before he even knew he was in trouble, and once he needed money the decision was inevitable.

SECOND

I did a quick search, found nothing.

HOW THE HELL IS NOONE TALKING ABOUT THE ANTIVIRUS SOFTWARE ON LIFEINVADER.

I've been here a while. Longer than most, shorter than some. But I've never seen anyone mention this.

When Michael does the Lifeinvader mission, hes let inside by an employee that wants him to fix his computer, bogged down with porn pop-ups. you, as michael, have to close all the windows and run the antivirus software. when you run the software, a window pops up with a loading bar, showing you all the files this antivirus software is either just scanning, or deleting.

it goes pretty fast, but i definitely saw Run Epsilon.exe flash by.

anyone see this? can you link me to a post about it? is there a breakdown of all the files it lists?
