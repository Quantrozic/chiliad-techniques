The underwater UFO has no FiB logo and appears at all times whilst the others appear only after 100% and have either an FiB logo or some other text. This leads me to believe the UFO(s) like the ones underwater have been taken in somewhere (Likely Zancudo as it is the only known place with enough space and privacy to hide a UFO) for testing into UFO flight capabilities. The military have a budget of around, '3 trillion dollars,' and if they are bringing in UFOs for testing and have a fancy one above their fort whereas the FiB ones are much more primitive so to speak, then perhaps, as before mentioned, the military at Zancudo test, and give away (or sell) the useless ones to the FiB. This may explain why the UFO and the jet pack are linked (assuming the jet pack is on an underground level of the fort, like in San Andreas) on the mural. Also a hangar recently opened in online around the same time as heists, which include hydras. The hydras are described as out dated and the jet pack does go a long way back, but assuming my thoughts are correct, the experimenting could have taken place at any point before the release of GTA V. This ties in because the hydra, UFO and jetpack all seem to have the same or similar methods of flight.

An observation of the mural also reveals that if lined up properly, the UFO, cluckin' bell (which seems to be the best explanation for the egg in my eyes) and Fort Zancudo all roughly line up meaning that if the two are linked then each cross too is linked either geographically or figuratively.

That concludes my thoughts for now. Please do expand on this. I personally aren't sure where to look so any opinions are appreciated.
