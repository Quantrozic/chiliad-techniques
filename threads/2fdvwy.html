Let's look back at GTA SA

▲ 3 ptsu/LovoskeaSep 3, 201412 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:San Andreas Legacy Myth Regression Testing ?

Hi everyone

Someone on this subreddit linked a video of a guy by the name of DeadHead_Skater_OG420! on Youtube. The video is 15 minutes long so I'll post a short TL;DR version here.

TL;DR VERSION: The guy says in the video that Franklin is actually a throwback of CJ from GTA SA. Franklin is involved with the Bigfoot easter egg. Bigfoot was one of the biggest easter eggs of SA, but he didn't exist... Then Franklin needs to collect 50 spaceship parts for Omega, who is also a throwback to "The Truth" from San Andreas. From that, the guy in the video thinks that Franklin is the one who is supposed to find the jetpack in GTA V, because Franklin and CJ have many similarities, even on the "easter egg" level. The guy also thinks that the control tower must somehow open the bunker in FZ.

Now, I'm not going to say the guy is right or wrong, it's just a theory. However, it might be a good idea to take a look back at GTA SA for clues. Yes, Everything has been found in San Andreas, but maybe there is a huge clue in some smaller easter eggs in San Andreas. Don't forget that GTA SA was the first GTA to have an extremely dedicated group of myth hunters. Some of the (fake) myths in GTA SA were:

  • Loch Ness
  • Bigfoot
  • Ufo's
  • Chainsaw Killer

Wouldn't it make sense if Rockstar would use something in GTA SA to refer to this mystery in GTA V? San Andreas was the most popular GTA! What do you guys think?
