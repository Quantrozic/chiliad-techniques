(experiment) Altruist camp, need testers

Documented under:Statistical Power Analysis for Spawn Experiments

First, I'd like to apologize for not flairing the title correctly, I'm on my phone and don't know how.

Second, I tried posting this a few weeks ago and reddit did not want me to (kept saying I was doing something wrong)

Third, my experiment: I thought to myself while playing with Trevor at the Altruist camp that the baseball bat on the sacrificial stone looked rather precariously place. What if I sacrificed someone myself? It proved to be harder then I expected; getting someone to walk to the stone required me to fight with them, and they all have guns.

Long story short, I was able to eventually get 3-4 cultists to the stone and bludgeon them to death before my ultimate demise. I respawned at the hospital hours later (because of how the hospital visit works), to an immediate and extreme straight up thunderstorm the likes of which I had never seen in the game (except for the scripted one during one of the lost missions). It lasted longer than any storm should have.

My test to you: try to sacrifice cultists with a bat on the stone. See if storm commences within 10 hours or so.

Tl;Dr: sacrifice cultists with bat on stone, watch for storm

EDIT(S) Did mine in the evening while sun was low but not setting, storm spawned with me at the hospital next morning.

May only work once per day

Maybe only cultists can be sacrificed
