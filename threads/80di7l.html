I created this thread for individuals to post substantiated claims of what data is sent to RockStar during single player.

There are a few methods to acquire this data; as I am currently game-less, I cannot provide any logs.

You can use a program called WireShark to log all communications. Then examine the logs.

There are some observations that should be noted:
Unmodified/unpatched versions of the game may act differently.

Systems that have never played online may act differently.

Systems that have exclusively played online may act differently.

It would be absolute bullshit if an always-on internet connection is required when the packaging did not state as such for Single Player!

Questions to answer:
