Kifflom hunters,

I was banned from this subreddit for disagreeing with a mod over a year ago, so I'm posting under my girlfriends name some things I wanted to share. Anyways, on to some curious observations.

There is a church in paleto bay , in which if you are present, the shadow of the sun on objects will not move until you start walking around. What I mean by that more specifically is if you stand still, the shadows made by the sun will not change, and if you move after standing still they will move rapidly, much faster than normal. Strange bug? Or something more?

This church is pictured in a few places in game such as the town logo for paleto bay. This same church is also visited with missions in the game, such as the paleto bay heist, much like other questionable sites like the observatory, and the FIB building. It's sign also has missing and deformed letters like other curious places within in the game.

The observatory shadow between two in the afternoon to about 6 will pause on its smooth transition every hour for about ten seconds as well. Another odd observation. My personal belief is the purpose of this monolith, while being accurate to real life, also is there to let us know of a certain time frame to which we can move forward with the mystery based upon its eye like shadow. but that's my opinion and what to do with it just leaves more questions.

Possibly a distraction but kinda strange in similarities is the reports of witnessing abductions. In its own right these observations are skeptical to believe at best, but all users reported it where the full moon party is, near the red lights track, late at night, and one user reported it was a Wednesday on a sliver quarter moon. Something to keep in mind.

Also, the 500,000 to the docks is something we should keep in mind that has dropped in this subreddit. Something isn't adding up with it.

I am on the Xbox 360, and the epsilon tract under chiliad mountain will not spawn to be collected. Ever. It's been like this for a long time and is a known glitch across a few platforms, but Rockstar still hasn't fixed it. This makes me think they aren't required for this mystery otherwise they would try to fix it at least in somewhat a decent time frame.

Once again, this is my opinion, I don't feel like the substitution cypher this subreddit has looked at lately has anything to do with the mystery, and is purely aesthetic. It's found in the nursing home or something, I'm sure you know what I'm talking about.

Last, It's very strange that Barry meets frank in textile city, right next to the textile city mural. He then also proceeds to say "can't you see all the aliens?"

To me it seems like the development team purposely is leading us to various locations around the map to find clues, but they are very well hidden and we probably as a whole missed some that aren't obvious.

Thank you.
