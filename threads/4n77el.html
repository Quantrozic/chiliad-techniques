Hey guys. I'm starting to really believe that the hippie camp (when looked at from a bird's eye view) is a map. Here is the best picture I could find of the bird's eye view of the camp.

Here's why I think this. So we start at the entrance. There is a big red arrow pointing to the right, so we take a right. There is then a big loop that goes past a dead tree and the WOW! Signal. In the middle of this loop is an old car with a UFO on top of it. After following this trail, we end up back at the entrance.

Follow the rest of the trail and you get to a fork in the road. The path on the left leads to a dead end which says "beam me up". The path on the right continues on.

If you follow the path to the right, you'll climb a staircase. There's an optional path to the right, but it leads nowhere. If you keep climbing the stairs, you'll eventually reach another staircase which leads to the top. Go to the right and there's a red glyph. This is where you leave the golden path and go down to where the trailer is. Next to the trailer is what appears to be a three-dimensional version of the mural, with six boxes instead of five. On top of this mural recreation is yet another glyph.

Guys, I think if we solve what this "map" is trying to tell us, we'll get somewhere. The things you encounter while following this map's trail are supposed to represent things you actually encounter in the game. For example, I believe the UFO Car is the Space Docker.

Also, as a side-note, I noticed that one dead-end path to the right while you're climbing the staircase is right in front of the word "SAVE". So, I started to wonder, could saving one of the guys be a dead-end? Then I noticed that if you keep climbing the staircase, you'll start to see drawings of two, only two aliens doing yoga and having sex or whatever the hell they're doing. Could this be telling us in order to advance in the mystery we need to kill either Mike or Trevor?

I have so many more thoughts on this, but I'll keep them to myself for now because I don't want to sound crazy, and this post is already pretty long and I can't really TL;DR it or you guys will miss a lot of good details.

So for now, Kifflom, brother-brothers. Let me know what you think about this.
