I saw "NotoriousVader" posted a map for everyone to check out a peice of the map and report back. That is a wonderful idea but maybe not the best application. A simple way for everyone to be able to work together while also hopefully turning this subreddit around is for us to all focus on specific zones at specific times.

Essentially we should pick a specific zone for everyone to investigate. We then sticky that post and everyone investigates that zone for 2 days. Anything found inside that zone that seems suspicious is then posted in that thread. After the 2 days we switch to a new zone and a new stickied thread.
