Understanding the Altruists

▲ 10 ptsu/LlamaGuy69Jun 15, 201511 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Mural & Map Mirroring ?

There are multiple sources in-game that allude to the fact that the Altruists are a cult of baby boomers that are set on destroying the younger generation, specifically anyone under the age of 40. Sources include cultstoppers.com, the Altruist website in morse code, and dialogue from the Altruist shootout. On cultstoppers.com it says that the Altruists are at war with the sub-40 generation at the request of the alien Lord Zapho, who is subtly referenced in several other places scattered throughout GTA V.

Trevor is the only character that the Altruists will welcome into their camp and not shoot at on sight, even before Trevor makes the first delivery and meets the Altruists. This has widely been thought to be because of Trevor's age, being older than Michael and Franklin.

However, when looking at the tattoo on Trevor's arm, that he originally got to mourn Michael's death, it says that Michael was born in 1963. This would make Michael 50 years old since the game takes place in 2013, a full 10 years older than the generation that the Altruists are at war against.

This in fact makes Michael a true baby boomer himself, since the Post World War II 'baby boom' is considered to have lasted from 1946 - 1964. So why do the Altruists shoot Michael on sight when he enters the camp?

The reason is not because Trevor delivers people to the camp, because they welcome him before he ever does this, and when T drops off the first victim his dialogue implies that this is the first time he has done so.

We all know there is a great deal of mystery behind the beliefs and intentions of the Altruist Cult, and that the Jet Pack shadow on their sacrificial stone directly ties them to the Chiliad mystery. Finding out the exact reason why they shoot at Michael and NOT Trevor could be the key to understanding their true intentions. Cheers! (http://i.imgur.com/tjH8caP.jpg)
