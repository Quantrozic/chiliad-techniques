It's in the 6th shot of the new trailer. You can see a big shadow pass from right to left over the Lumber Yard. It isn't very clear, but seems to have big wings, a body, and a tail like a jet.

Judging from the direction of the camera, the movement of the shadow, and the angle of the sun, I think it's the same Altruist jet that we've been discussing here. I've seen the Altruist jet shadow pass over the Lumber Yard in the current version of the game, too.

Here's a screen capture of that shot from the trailer: http://i.imgur.com/ukwPdyu.jpg

Here's where it is in the video: http://youtu.be/3DBrG2YjqQA?t=19s

What does this mean? Well, two things. First, the so-called Altruist jets will be in the new version(s) of GTAV (...not really a big deal in itself). And second, R* deliberately put a shot of the jet shadow in the trailer.

PS I tried tracking the Altruist jet course again the other day. I dropped POI markers every 5-10 seconds as I flew right behind it. They're not exact, but they are very close. (Ignore the first marker on the left--that's where my plane was when I spotted the jet, before I got behind it.)
