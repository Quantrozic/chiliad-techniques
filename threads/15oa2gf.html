I wanted to share some exciting news! I think I have found the inspiration for the Mural on Chiliad, and to add more excitement, even the artist acknowledged the similarities. His name is Daniel Martin Diaz and he released a book called “Soul of Science” a month before GTA V came out in 2013. I’m not sure what year the Chiliad looking diagram was made, but it was first published in that book.

This is what Amazon says about him on his Soul of Science purchase page:

‘In "Soul of Science," Daniel Martin Diaz examines the mysteries of scientific diagrams, secrets of symbols and their everlasting effect on our psyche. The inspiration for this new body of work comes from the mysteries of consciousness, self-aware systems, philosophy, cellular automata, phase transitions, time travel, and mystical behaviors at atomic and sub-atomic levels.

In recent years, Diaz became immersed in scientific and philosophical theories. In particular, he became obsessed with scientific diagrams, which explain theories and properties through drawings. Although these rudimentary drawings were without any leanings towards aesthetics, he found them to be beautiful, though that is clearly not their intention. He was inspired to use the simplicity of drawing to create his own interpretations of the concepts of consciousness and other theories on a scientific, philosophical, and spiritual level. All of the projects begin as drawings, which have a beauty and intimacy that paintings cannot capture. Graphite's allure comes from its subtle lines and the quickness in which one can capture an idea.’

The Chiliad mural is some sort of scientific diagram. I’m having an issue finding more on this specific piece, however, here is an IMGUR album with some similarities and a screenshot of me asking him about it on Facebook and his response.

One more interesting thing about Daniel Martin Diaz; He’s in a band called “Trees Speak”. Now, this band was formed well after GTA V released, but Trees Speaking is a theory that advanced civilizations are able to download and store information in trees and plants, like organic harddrives.

With the artwork and the references to Trees speaking, I think we found our guy. Now, I think the next step is to figure out exactly which scientific process he is trying to convey in the one that looks like Chiliad. It seems like it has to do with frequencies and how to alter them.

In “Cosmic Overture”, he has musical notes associated with moon phases. We can install musical notes as horns on our vehicles. Maybe the Spacedocker has been a red herring all this time?

I’ve got to get to work, but I’ll have more as time goes on. I encourage you to check out his Instagram and Facebook, there's a lot to delve into here.
