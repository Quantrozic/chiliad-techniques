Ok, I'm just going to clarify what we know about the glyphs so far, please correct me or tell me off, etc.

So there are 5 glyphs we know about from the game files as found here All these file names are appropriately dubbed as 'clues'.

Some people believe there's a two-three divide of the 5 as on the mural, local to the mountain (as illustrated here) The main issue is that there is no concrete relationship between them.

If they were clues would they form some kind of combined meaning to hint at a particular trigger, or each solve a fifth of a larger problem? Given that there's five and there is some sort of mountain divide which matches up with the mural, maybe the latter is more likely.

What gets me though, is the fact that we have 3 distinct groups of clues, two of which are in pairs:

  • http://rsg.ms/1bMNzja = cs1_10_clue_mountain01
  • http://rsg.ms/1bMPRP9 = cs1_10_clue_rain02
  • http://rsg.ms/1bMPWm0 = cs1_10_clue_moon02
  • http://rsg.ms/1bMREUm = cs1_10_clue_rain01
  • http://rsg.ms/1bMThS8 = cs1_10_clue_moon01

One represents weather, another is cosmic, and another a mountain. Both rain02 and moon02 do not have distinct symbols, the rest do. If all these mappings are correct, then why is that? It would seem to push away from the 5 distinct tasks; if the task needed 5 parts, how would a player recognize a second moon and rain clue if neither of them were decipherable?

If we remove these duds we still have rain01, moon01 and mountain01. The five-glyph theory is interesting, but it's hard to map to the mural, unless further glyph types are yet to be discovered.

On another note, I came across this image from a forum today, I was wondering if anyone knew where this was, or if it has any significance? It has an identical zig-zag structure up its center like the mountain01 glyph, in addition to the lines at its side which meet at its top.
