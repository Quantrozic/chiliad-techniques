Was the online content (all the mystery stuff) already in GTA?

▲ 31 ptsu/MatyDisteMay 15, 20209 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Cross-Platform Hash Verification ?Tunables Delta Watching ?

For those who had GTA V shortly after the launch, Were the updates for the online content big (in the order of GB)? Or rather lightweight (maximum of 1.5 gb)?

If the updates were small, that means the models and textures were already in the game, meaning the mystery was solvable probably and all the code walking failed to narrow down these files. On the other hand, if they were heavy there are 2 possibilities:

  1. The mystery wasn't present in the game because it was incomplete and all the early investigations and grinding to try theories weren't successful because there wasn't anything to achieve, meaning all the old theories could now work properly.
  2. (Most likely) The developers knew we would decompile and explore the code, so adding to the impossible to read code, the mystery wasn't even present because it would come as a "secret update" for those who own the game and trigger the mystery, making the download available and once and for all, the mystery solvable. This could also mean the reason why in single player it is difficult to find the mystery assets in the files and code, as the SP would still be needing of a secret update and the files for the mystery would be working as a bait for code walkers.
