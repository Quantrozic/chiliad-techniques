After that I filled in the blanks.

And then filled the blanks in to create words.

F6 is a block on the map, and because it's on both maps, unlike J6, I assume this would be more plausible. It's half land, half water in this block so possibly drowning all 3 characters at a specific time, or blowing them up on the coast line somewhere.

The reason is, I think they put "%oy" as a hint so we know the "oy" is a number, even after we rearrange it.
