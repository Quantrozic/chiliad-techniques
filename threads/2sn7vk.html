CRESCENT MOON

This brings me into part 2.

Q: Why is there more than 1 type of moon? (A moon cycle)

A: This game was made late 2013. As a community of gamers, we have expectations. Rockstar has obviously gotten our attention with the moon in the past, and would be normal for us to have our attention on it now. Given the stark realism of the entire game, it would be hugely disappointing if we had the same moon we had in GTA San Andreas, we would be staring at them blankly, that they gave us an 8 year old moon that increases when it gets shot. That's not what we want now. We want realistic. So they gave us a few moons, so that it demonstrates a real passage of time.

Find me a solid "FULL MOON" glyph, or a WANING or WAXING or NEW or anything besides crescent, and you've got yourself a believer.

I just want to narrow down peoples tests. I couldn't think it any sillier to test under different moons (most of those tests are done anyhow)
