Number one: Thunderstorms. ⚡️

To create a thunderstorm

  • Make sure you’ve done Franklins Weekly side mission to deliver the weed van safely to ‘Smoke on the Water’
  • All players must be in their main houses.
  • Franklin needs to smoke from his bong once.
  • Switch immediately to Michael, he needs to smoke from Jimmys bong twice.
  • Switch immediately to Trevor and he needs to huff the gas outside the trailer.
  • Drive all players back home from their spawn points. Repeat.
  • You will notice after each repeat, the amount of clouds (or ‘smoke’) will keep increasing. This is visible during character switching and outside Franklins apartment window.
  • The switching is ‘creating’ a mini hurricane.
  • If you fail at utilising the thunderstorm, repeat from the first step. I believe you get one Thunderstorm per week. Dont bother saving before a thunderstorm and reloading, it wont be there.

That is how to spawn a thunderstorm.
