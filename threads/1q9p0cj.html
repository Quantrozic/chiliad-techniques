Sand Glyph: Not an arrow?

▲ 18 ptsu/codeflexJan 11, 20267 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Optical Flow Glyph Motion ?

Hi everyone, long time no see. With the recent revelations about the Spiderweb mystery in RDR2, I thought it was time to revisit GTA 5 and give it a good ol' deep investigation like the old days.

Let me preface this by saying that, after following the mystery for so long, I think everything I find is Jesus Toast, and can totally accept that I may be long passed sanity when it comes to GTA 5's mysteries. So by all means, I am not married to the following theory by any means, and won't take offense if y'all don't think it's anything. That all being said...

I was looking at the central RDR2 spiderweb, the one with N and a strange F-like shape highlighted in it. The N obvious meant NORTH, while the F-shape thing was discovered to be a phone pole, which led to the next hint. And sure enough, going north to the first phone pole you found, had the next hint hidden there.

This reminded me of the Sand Glyph, and indeed when you look at it, it has an N with what we've all always assumed was an arrow-- but look closer at it. It's half an arrow, and given N means NORTH, seems kinda redundant. So i decided to use the same logic from the RDR2 web, and travel directly north to see if i found anything that matched it.

Directly north takes you thru the hippy camp (very close, if not directly over, the rain glyph), and a bit farther north still, you'll end up at the corner of a small building (Liquor Market) looking directly an antennae of some sort where the main vertical and horizontal bars make that same 'half-arrow' shape, same angle and all. It is also angled in a way that points directly at Chiliad.

Again, might not be anything, but it does make me think of the 'A dog named Skip' trailer, which featured the Space Docker doing donuts near this location.

Thing's ive already tried: Rainy weather, Thunder weather, 1-2 am, 3am, shooting it with a bunch of weapons including stun gun, space docker special horn at it, donuts in space docker in front of it.

Anyways, just thought I'd share. Probably nothing but never know. Always better to share info imo
