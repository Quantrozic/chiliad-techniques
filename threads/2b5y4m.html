Some speculation on the "bring 500,000 to the docks" UV Map message.

▲ 18 ptsu/13th_Tribe_Of_KobolJul 19, 201431 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:UV Light Analysis

I'm not sure where I really stand on the nature of this mystery. I have wildly conflicting thoughts on all of it that go back and forth between 'yes there is still a puzzle of some extremely complicated sort to be solved' and 'no this is all a trick by Rockstar to extend the life of the game, having seen the effect of 'urban legends' on continued playability surrounding their previous games and other titles.'I'd like to believe the latter is not the case. But to the point..

As far as I have gathered, there is no conclusive agreement on what the "bring 500,000 cash to the docks" message means, or refers to. The full message being:

X
Lago Zancudo

Great Ocean Highway North through north Chumash

0.5 miles past Raton Canyon ← when guard rail ends follow the dirt road down the Docks

Bring $500,000 CASH XXXXX

Since the UV Map is meant to be Trevor's I believe this is a message meant for him. Or it was a message for Abigail Mathers, who payed Trevor 500,000 to cause the death of her husband, but I don't think that is the case. If the message was for Trevor it would explain the confusion behind Michael being able to buy the dock for 240,000. It's just a coincidence I think that Trevor has a deal set up on those docks.

I apologize if this has been hypothesized or discussed before but I couldn't find anything on it. So assuming that Rockstar really wants us to work hard for any sort of real reward is it possible that as soon as we get ahold of Trevor, before we do any missions or heists or progress any of that storyline, we have to get him up to 500,000 and then go to the docks?

This would be achieved by hitting every single armored car, robbing every store, the weed farm, getting all the various scattered briefcases of cash, robbing people at atms, etc. Just random events and simple crime. it's interesting to note that Trevor has the most money of the 3 characters, I think around 180,000 when you first get him.

I haven't done the math, but many of those events are repeatable, so it's entirely possible I suppose given enough time. I suggest this theory/approach only in the effort of exhaustive thoroughness. If someone has tried this I'd like to know, I plan on an attempt in a new playthrough as soon as I finish experimenting some more on my 100% file.
