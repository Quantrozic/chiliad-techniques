1-999-EMPDROP at Galileo Observatory causes rain

▲ 59 ptsu/C_0n33Jan 21, 201621 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Orthographic Map-Tile Mosaic Stitching

tldr Phoning black cellphones at Galileo Observatory atop the highest climbable rotunda set my in-game weather to rainy. edit: clarifying this was only in my game and not yet generalized or reproduced.

*Game Day/Time: Saturday 8pm, Sunny weather with few clouds.

*Game status: 27.2% completion, last mission "Far Out"

*Notable events prior: Completion of 3 taxi fares, "No Country For Old Men" random event on Fri. Yoga and Green Juice at M's on Fri. 3 clothing stores visited: Posonby's, Subarban and Binco. Chicken Hawk peyote consumed at Mt. Chiliad.

New edit: Background I've been testing different non-100% scenarios to determine whether we get mystery-related clues before or during missions. Currently I am avoiding the Steve Haines phone call that sets up "By the Book" based on a insults T makes to M about his physique when they meet, if M starts the mission. As hunters know missing a phone call sets the black exclamation mark as the Contacts icon.

I have a 27.2% save (XB1) at Franklin's Vinewood house where he has just met Omega to start "Far Out". If I switch to T then M, M will be in bed and wake violently screaming (not waving gun). It was clear to me that he's becoming unstable, so I decided to try and mellow him out with exercise and good karmic deeds. First changed him to shirt and shorts, then drank the green juice, then did one round of yoga in his backyard.

The next exercise I was thinking was **"Exericising Demons". I wanted to see if Mary-Ann's dialogue about M being poorly dressed for activity would change at all, so decided I needed Prolapse running shoes. I tried 3 clothing stores in a row: Posonby's, Suburban and Binco's, taking a cab each time. No running shoes found.

Finally I got frustrated of cabbing and jacked a cab. I drove a couple of fares including one in the area of the observatory. I then did a run towards Mt. Chiliad, and after that last fare the "No Country for Old Man" Random Event (5 dead or dying gangsters off the Pacific Highway, plus 20k cash) spawned and I went for it.

Next I took the cable car up to Mt. C in clear conditions and found the peyote there. I became the Chicken Hawk and M has new dialogue that says this is "better than yoga", which led me to believe I was getting somewhere! I flew for a while, headed towards the observatory to try and land on the obelisk but crashed.

Getting back down of the mountain and to the observatory area took a while. I activated the Skyfall and invincibility cheats to help out and this probably was what led to what I saw at the Observatory.

However on repeated attempts, I am able to trigger the "Monkey Mosaic" tile in the place it should be using 1-999-EMPDROP. My conclusion is the Black Cellphones is a cheat but I am not going to stop trying it out. Hope this background clarifies where I'm coming from in my investigation.

*Outfit: Shorts and Polo

Steps to try and reproduce

*Collect the spaceship part on this level

*As M ascend to the telescope level of the Galileo Observatory to space telescope level and climb the highest climbable rotunda (the reddish one with the textured roof)

*Dial 1-999-EMP-DROP and observe pedestrians flee after explosion. Rainy weather will commence shortly after. No autosave will occur.

*Descend the rotunda and you can also photograph a monkey mosaic tile in the immediate area.
