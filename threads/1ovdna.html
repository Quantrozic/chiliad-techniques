R* Has put a ton of references into GTA V and I found either a cool little coincidence or a nice reference maybe involving Mount Chiliad.

In the second season of the X-Files there is an episode involving a man named Duane Barry who believes he has been abducted and will be abducted again, so he travels to a place called "Skyland Mountain" to which he believes is the next abduction site of the aliens. The attraction of this mountain in the episode is "Ascend to the Stars", a tram ride that brings you to the top of the mountain.

I posted this as meta as I thought that if this was of any significance, it might be reaching a bit, but either way intentional or not by Rockstar, I thought was a cool little tid bit.
