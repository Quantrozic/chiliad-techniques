My Trip To The Edge Of The Ocean

▲ 115 ptsu/stringydogAug 18, 202127 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Tide & Water-Level Observation ?

In my own curiosity, I've tried using a Toreador and a mod that gives me a vehicle boost that never turns off. As of the start of my typing, I am currently 70,000 ft away from Del Perro Beach and here's what I gathered:

At 15,000 ft., your vehicle will malfunction and will stop working. But the boost will still work.

At 25,000 ft., your vehicle will start to rapidly sink from surface to 0 ft in less than a second for unkown reasons (maybe water stops acting as water and the vehicle, in turn, "fell"?). But this will lead to vehicle's catastrophic hull damage. But the boost will still work albeit the darkness around you.

Beyond that? It seems the ocean is an infinite render or that it's just extremely huge. I'm 100,000 ft (as of the current point of typing) away from San Andreas and it appears that I'm still going further. Maybe I'll sit this one out while I do other things and come back later for results. My PC doesn't seem to have a hard time doing this little project either. I just hope that there are other players when I come back so I can have a reference to my distance.

Edit: The speed my broken vehicle is currently doing is 0.16mi. And since I'm not familiar with imperial I'm guessing that's fast.

Edit 2: At 200,000 ft., it looks like all the icons on the map starts to move on its own.

Edit 3: Passed the 1,000,000 ft. at some point and currently at 1,400,000. It doesn't look like the Toreador is sinking at this point and is now going straight. I can't tell because everything is just darkness around me other than the faint light above. (Theory is that somewhere below the 0 ft depth limit is something that blocks me from going further deeper down. Should be good for me as at least I go on a horizontal straight line for more speed efficiency.)

Edit 4: I woke up. There are no players in my session so I can't tell the distance. Maybe I'm just crazy but the light above me seems to be more noticeably brighter. My Toreador also seems to be blinking in and out of existence. I'm kinda curious to see what's on surface but then again I'll have to leave the vehicle. Since there are no players in the session, I can't see SA in the map since the icons all moved on its own towards southern direction back at 200,000 ft.
