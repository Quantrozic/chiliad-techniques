Using High-Reflective Vehicles Reveals Differences Between Reflective World and Real world

▲ 0 ptsu/mykeukFeb 1, 201718 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Frame-Difference Motion Isolation ?

https://youtu.be/UbKoSmstLHM

In the video, the guy checked out a number of locations with a high-reflective van and showed that there are a number of differences between what's in world and what's showing up in the reflection. Notable things were the appearence of what looks like openable doors at the observatory and the lack of Chiliad mural. Also, the tree at the alien hippy camp is reflected - the only tree in the entire game to do so.

I had a scan to see if this had been mentioned before but couldn't find anything, but I apologise if it had.
