Savegame editor reveals certain choices have their own variables

▲ 11 ptsu/MalcolmEggsJan 5, 201423 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Choice-Branch Outcome Tabulation ?Choice-Tree Ending Permutation MappingRockstar Editor Time-Lapse Capture

While fooling around with XB36Hazard's savegame editor, something stood out to me which I thought you all might find interesting.

In the "Unlocks" section, there are a few variables you can toggle which seem to illustrate that some choices you make in the game are more important than others.

In this first image, here's what I find interesting:

  • Chose firecrew for the bureau raid
  • Chose offshore approach for the merryweather heist
  • Chose smart approach for the jewel store job
  • Chose subtle approach for the big score
  • Chose to kill Al
  • Chose to kill Dr. Friedlander
  • Chose to kill Ortega

Now, that's interesting because your decisions about whether or not to kill Michael or Trevor are stored in a different way, as a mission with a named achievement. However, the choices listed above seem to have been given special treatment by having their own dedicated variables.

And then in this second image, it occurred to me that I'd never noticed the "We Come in Peace" achievement, which stores whether or not you ran over any aliens in the movie studio during the very first mission of the game.

Thoughts?
