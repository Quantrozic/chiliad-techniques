The hobo in the mission repossession.

▲ 35 ptsu/OneMintyBoiFeb 23, 202015 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Anagram, Acronym & Linguistic Pattern Analysis ?

When you have to search the garages in this mission you’re confronted by a hobo who seems drugged/drunk accusing us of being “Zapho agents”. Initially I went to punch him as Lamar was about to bluff him but Franklin slapped him instead and he didn’t get up (is slapping a regular combat mechanic?).

I retried the mission and I let him be. I stuck around and noticed after a while he walked into the garage and began walking in a pattern: LEFT> FORWARD> BACKWARD> BACK LEFT> RIGHT> FORWARD.

I wondered if it was just a glitch/NPC trying to escape a corner instead of a predetermined path. I got out of the way and even “reset” him with a punch but he went back walking in the same pattern.

If I blocked his path he would pretty much “walk through” me nudging me away as if I weren’t even there. I restarted the mission and the same thing. Same pattern. Not sure what it is but it’s intentional.

If anyone has experienced this let me know. I recently decided to redo all the missions and pay closer attention to minute details where I can.
