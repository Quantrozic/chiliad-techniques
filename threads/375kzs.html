Interesting observation with Random Events/Robberies

▲ 9 pts[deleted]May 25, 201532 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:NPC Pathfinding Observation Logging ?

I was michael, just after I ran the race with Mary, and I was driving home (my car stayed where I left it in the little overhang across the street from her). An event kicked in, it was one of the ATM robberies. I killed the guy, got the wallet back, and gave it back. Saved in extra slot, loaded up Social Club - no innocents killed - so far, so good. Next, after saving Jimmy with Mike, I walk home as franklin. My walk takes me south directly to Baygor, and we have a talk. Game tells me about EP.com and I see I am blocked even without any innocents (crossed off my things to check this playthrough, sweet). I continue beyond, make a left, then a right into the parking lot with a jump in a dumpster just north of the freeway. Hop the wall, cross the freeway, and head a bit further south until I make a left under an overpass (i'm on foot because I don't want to steal cars and am in the mood to walk anyhow so no taxi). As I am walking I hear "Take it, it's thrift store anyway!" and the guy comes running toward me (he always runs the same way, and I am under the underpass as Frank). He gets to me, I take a swing. I miss, he keeps running. This is where things get strange:

In my previous 2 playthroughs, I noticed that some criminals drop the wallet WITHOUT killing them. Others must be killed to drop it. I had been trying to figure out the differences, to no avail so far. Anyhow, I shoot him ONCE. He drops, then drops the wallet, gets up and starts shooting at me. I've got a little armor so I run up and grab the wallet and run back away toward cover. Call the cops. Before the cops get there, he dies, just bleeds out I guess since I had shot him once already. Save in extra slot -> Innocents: 1. The guy - who stole a wallet and shot me - is considered an innocent. Am I supposed to let him go? is the first thing I think. But no. Maybe just hit him once, and take the wallet back? Maybe I should be pulling up in a car for this, and try to honk to pick him up? Maybe if I do it as Michael, it won't be an innocent? Mind racing with this one. I might even avoid it for now and try to do others to get the 14 needed.

Franklin calls himself a Hypocrite for this one. I'm not sure if I should avoid them entirely (you only need 14 out of like over 50 or something like that, after all). So the game does tell me: You are a hypocrite, player. But, as michael, in seemingly the same kind of event, I am a hero.

These need more looking into I think.
