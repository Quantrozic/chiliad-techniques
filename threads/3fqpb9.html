Mural asymmetry and a theory about the Xs.

▲ 24 ptsu/dixiewhiskeyAug 4, 201524 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Mural & Map Mirroring

Take a look here: http://i.imgur.com/sjQtJkk.png.

I'm sure many of you already know this, but maybe this could be a hint of some sort. If we flip one half of the mural (either right or left), we get 3 lightning symbols that are perfectly identical (green) and 2 that are not (red). What's the reason behind this? It could be nothing, but for a key element in the game it sure looks suspicious.

The lightning symbols could also explain the Xs and the lines between them that may represent power stations. We have one near the observatory and many others spread throughout the map. Maybe the mural hints towards destroying 5 of the stations in order to gain access to certain locations. We already know that the 3 characters can perform different tasks at the same time by switching between them. Next step would be to map the stations and the links between them I think.

TL;DR: lightning symbols on the mural are all symmetrical except two, and the Xs in the mural could represent power stations.
