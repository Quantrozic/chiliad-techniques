##Purpose of this post
*
I am writing this post for a few reasons.

First I want to inspire people to stick with the hunt. This has been one of the largest video game hunts of all time. We should not be giving up at this point, asking Rockstar to solve it for us, or just backing off because we've found everything. This game is massive, and has lots of different factors effecting everything from conversations, to NPC spawns, to holograms appearing at the largest mountain in the game.

Secondly I am a firm believer that there is more to this game than meets the eye. To me the UFO's, the Easter Eggs found this far, are all neat, but none of them are that thing we are looking for. None of them give us an edge in game, they give nothing back to the player. We all have pictures of UFO's, get to show our friends what we have seen flying way high up in the sky, but I think there is more to it than that.

##Focussing our Search Efforts
*
I believe we need to narrow down where and what we are looking at, and we need to be noting all factors in play when we make observations and discoveries.

If the answer was simple, we would have found it by now. The types of testing we should be doing should include , but not be limited to the following (when possible):

  • Time of day
  • Day of week
  • Moon Phase
  • Character Used
  • Clothing Worn
  • Vehicle Driven
  • Hang out? (If so who? Remember, you can do a three person hang out if you chose C. Deathwish)
  • Chop Taken?
  • Soft or Hard 100% (Does your game say 100%? Or do you have nothing left to do in the game)
  • What major choices you made through out the game

Mike, that's a lot of things to pay attention to

It certainly is, but if we don't, how will we know exactly what factors have caused what we have observed in game. This brings me to another point.

##Reproduce Results
*
Games the size of GTA are bound to have glitches, and to differentiate between glitches and game mechanics we need to be able to reproduce results.

Reproducing results has a few stages:

  1. Reload your last save, and get back to the exact point you were at, following your foot steps as exactly as you can. Does the same thing happen again? If so, move onto the next step.
  2. Advance in the game, and come back and try to reproduce the results again on a different day. Has the same thing happened again?
  3. Repeat testing changing factors such as Weather, Character, Clothing, etc. and see what sort of results you can get. Does anything change? What changed it?

This sounds like my science class...

Yes, that's exactly what this is, it's Science. Approach in game tests scientifically. Have your Constant Variables, your Manipulated Variables, and have your Results. The more scientific, the more likely it is to help.

##Where should we be focussing
*
There are a number of places that a lot of people feel have been beaten to death but honestly that just isn't the case.

We need a better understanding of factors that can affect the following:

  • Weather
  • NPC behavior
  • Wanted level
  • The ability to save and switch characters

There are some other good theories that are linked to the following, but require further testing:

  1. The Chiliad UFO makes sound for an hour. What can we observe and find within that hour?
  2. The Green Light at Zancudo. Why is it there? Why is there a Cellphone Tower Directly beside the green light that says Badger on it.
  3. Observation Decks, there are a lot of them. Do any of these play a role in anything? Other than the one on top of Chiliad being a prime location to view a UFO. They all have telescopes facing different directions.
  4. What the hell is with the Hippie camp? The UFO Car, the power lines, the painted tree. What is going on?
  5. Mt. Gordo, why can I do Yoga here? Why does the camera sweep up to look at it when I sleep with Ursula, why are there lakes on this mountain, but no other mountain?
  6. The Satellite Array? Why does a single dish face it's own direction? Why are there FIB members hanging around in the day? Why does weather effect the number of FIB personnel checking on these things?
  7. Alien Silo's. Why do they have the same types of painting as the hippie camp? They are so far away from the hippie camp, and yet on the same rail line. Why does Trevor wake up here after huffing gas?
  8. The Altruist Camp. It's general proximity to Mount Chiliad. The types of stuff going on there. What do their murals mean? What is with their encrypted website?

Who died and made you the king of anything?

No one. I am simply compiling things I have seen over the past few months that have been looked at, but not investigated heavily. If you personally have investigated these thing, all factors considered, I will not only upvote you, I will also take things off the list. Notes, Pictures, Videos, and other evidence will be required. The more the better/

Have anything you think should be included up here? That's great, I'm always open to suggestions. Use common sense though.

##Conclusion
*
We are at a point in this mystery where there is no factor that can be ignored. We can all search in our own ways, in our own clothes, and in our own vehicles. If we aren't making notes, and recording information that can potentially be relevant to the resolution, then what good does it do us?

What good does this do us?

That is a question that I'd like to see more people asking themselves before posting here. If you can answer that question, then what you are saying might be helpful. If you can't, perhaps read this post and do a little testing, and then maybe you can answer that question.

##TL;DR
*

  • Pay attention to everything
  • Reproduce Results
  • Test, test, test!
  • Ask yourself How does this help us? before posting
  • Keep a positive attitude!
