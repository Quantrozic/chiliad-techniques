Unusual horn behavior

▲ 8 ptsu/BullsaverDec 29, 20154 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:NPC Pathfinding Observation LoggingShared-Engine Behavior Comparison

Hello, I apologize in advance if I'm posting this the wrong way, its my first time here on Reddit.
So, today I was playing in an online invite only session (on PC), wandering around Harmony near the Grand Senora Desert. I found a good looking Ubermacht Oracle XS parked and began driving it around. After a few minutes I noticed something weird, the horn was not acting like the other stock horns, I'll try to explain: instead of a continuous horn sound, it acted like it was a morse code. One quick press - a random sequence of long or short honking, like a single letter of the morse code; Holding the key - continuous sequence of these, impossible for me to translate. I managed to translate the short sequences, resulting in series of letters like this: "T A D T A B T A D T A N" and so on.
I decided to save the car in my garage, and to invite a friend online to hear his opinion. When he got into the car, the horn went back to a normal, boring horn. The car right now is safe, in my garage, and the "strange" horn is not working anymore.
Looking back at it I feel really stupid for not taking a video proof, I only have a picture of the car in the place where I found it. Haven't found anything about strange horns on the web, so I thought to post it here, not sure of what I'm expecting. I'll try to recreate this event when I'll have the chance.
