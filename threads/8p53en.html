Something is fishy about the way the Altruist website has been "decoded"

Altruist Website

Okay everybody, basically ever since the initial launch of the game, there's been this widely understood translation of the manifesto. Here are a couple examples:

forum post from September 21 2013

http://gta.wikia.com/wiki/Altruist\_Cult

It occurred to me recently that this translation has like never been called into question. This stone has long been considered "turned over," but for me there always seemed like there could be more to it. So with that, let me present to you two problems I recently found with it:

Problem 1: the first paragraph does not contain any slashes indicating word spacing. All the other paragraphs do.

Problem 2: when actually transcribed letter by letter from Morse code, the words "the stability of" from the first line are NOT THERE. The Morse code symbols for these letters are not part of the code on the in-game website.

So what does this mean? It means the first line of the manifesto, where it has been commonly understood to be "the industrial revolution has been a disaster to the stability of the human race," might actually be: "theindustrialrevolutionhasbeenadisastertothehumanrace." With this information in mind, let me present you with two ensuing problems with the translation of this thing:

Problem 1: whoever first translated this line or the whole text using an online decoder would immediately have found that it doesn't work, because the programs are not able to read it without the spaces and will give you an error message.

Problem 2: if any translation of this thing was done by hand, it would not have included the part "the stability of" because it is, again, not there.

Not to sound too much like Ron Jakowski here, but I don't see how the initial "decodings" that popped up online in the early days are not subtle, deliberately implanted lies. Anybody with skill in Morse would immediately see the spacing discrepancy in this first line, and anybody typing dots and dashes in order to run it through a translator would find it doesn't work.

If you're still not sold that there's more to this document, the very first line of the Cultstoppers page on the Altruists is "Other than what appears to be some kind of encrypted manifesto, we don't have any first-hand information on this group." Encrypted..................

Long story short, I strongly believe this is some kind of ciphertext involving Morse code and the sequence of colors, and I'm bouncing this out to anybody who has better knowledge/skill with this stuff than me, because I know fuck all about it.

Whoa, another realization, hot off the press: NOT ALL THE PARAGRAPHS END IN PERIODS. This also odd, because the first translation I linked actually has this correct, but it also has the words "the stability of." Also occurring to me now is that if you look at the first line in Morse code, there are totally spaces for the slashes to be, they just aren't there, almost suggesting they're supposed to be there, they're just missing. Perhaps we need to be adding the slashes but subtracting "the stability of" before cipher analysis?

More realizations, they just keep coming. I found this online Morse translator where the slashless Altruist line actually does translate properly. Further down the page, the guy says to NOT use slashes to indicate spacing, because in his Morse table the forward slash has its own encoding. He says that using two spaces between words is the best way to get natural looking English from his tool. Given that the slashless Altruist paragraph does have extra spaces in the correct spots, this isn't necessarily saying it's wrong, just showing you that some programs have different ways of understanding Morse code when converting it. Again however, anybody entering the whole manifesto into any online decoder would have immediately noticed problems with the word spacing discrepancy.

One thing we DO know is wrong is the inclusion of the words "the stability of" in these translations, and also four paragraphs in the Wikia translation that falsely end in periods. There may be more inconsistencies, and I get a sense that in order to function as a ciphertext, we need to get this exactly right. For all I can see, however, the following is the true translation, down to the last period, in English plaintext:

THE INDUSTRIAL REVOLUTION HAS BEEN A DISASTER TO THE HUMAN RACE.

ALMOST ALL WOULD AGREE THAT CONTINUED FOCUS ON THE SUPERIORITY OF THE SUB 40 YOUNGER GENERATION WILL RESULT IN THE CATASTROPHIC AND TRAGIC END TO OUR CIVILIZATION.

MAN IS A NOCTURNAL ANIMAL BY NATURE.

THOSE WHO EXIST IN THE INDUSTRIAL-TECHNOLOGICAL SOCIETY, BATHING IN SUNLIGHT AND ELECTRONICS, SUFFER FROM PSYCHOLOGICAL PROBLEMS.

HYPERVITAMINOSIS D CAUSES ABNORMALLY HIGH CONCENTRATIONS OF CALCIUM, WHICH CAN CAUSE HARM TO THE HEART, KIDNEYS AND BONES. TOXIC CONCENTRATIONS ARE A DIRECT RESULT OF EXPOSURE TO THE SUN.

CROWDING CAUSES STRESS AND AGGRESSION

PRIMITIVE SOCIETIES, ONES WHERE CLOTHES AND TOOLS OF THE INDUSTRIAL REVOLUTION WERE NOT NEEDED, PROVIDED A STABLE FRAMEWORK FOR THE FUNCTION OF SOCIETY

ONE CAN NOT COMPLAIN ABOUT THE DECAY OF TRADITIONAL VALUES YET EMBRACE TECHNOLOGY

A MAN AS AN INDIVIDUAL, WORKING WITH AN AUTONOMOUS GROUP OF INDIVIDUALS, ALL OF WHOM ARE NAKED IN SPIRIT AND IN THE CLOTH, REMAINS HEALTHY TO HIMSELF AND OTHERS.

Also, there are discrepancies in word spacing that would have been immediately noticeable and problematic for somebody translating with an online decoder.
