I seriously just had an "AHA!!!" moment.

I was thinking about straight lines and what forms straight lines in the game... Roads and other obvious things like that, because of the middle line that leads to the jetpack in the mural... And here's what popped into my mind.

What if, the outside two lines of the mural really IS the side view of Mt. Chiliad, and the straight line is the lift? It's the only thing that would make sense... And it would orient and put direction on the area the mural is pointing to.

Right now my husband is hogging the TV so I can't check it out, but I suspect the red X boxes are some kind of land marks you pass by while riding the cable lift.

If this is true, there must be something at the ground station that has been missed... Are we absolutely sure people have combed over that area and there is nothing left? Do the crooked lines of the mural line up with mountain paths or anything else that crosses the straight line the cable lift travels?
