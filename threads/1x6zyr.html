If there are any pairs or triplets of large numbers that have yet to be identified, they could be coordinates out to sea or a bearing to fly towards. The only way I imagine you'd be able to find something is if you were given coordinates or a direction to fly in. Unfortunately I don't think there's any way to definitively disprove this theory, either.

I know it's a longshot, but I just thought this might be something good to keep in mind while you're hunting.
