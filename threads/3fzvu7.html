Hey I just wanted to post some observations I made.

  1. The "eye" like image on the top of the mural is almost identical to the pillar at the observatory. If the mural can be read like a road, descending, the observatory could be the first step of solving the puzzle. Or maybe the mural is the observatory and not Mt.Chiliad
  2. The main doors of the observatory read "not an entrance", possibly referring to the fact that while the observatory may play a role in the mystery it is merely a stepping stone.
  3. If one follows the "to the telescope" signs it will reach a lone blue telescope on top of the establishment from which Mt. Chiliad can be seen. Moreover, on the roof one can see power sources with the a bent arrow on the representing much the mini "thunderbolts" on the mural, one can also be found on the bottom terrace as well as the balcony and they produce an electric shock when shot. There are also power generators right next to the facility. (Right of the pillar at the front there are stairs that take you down there). There are tons of the arrows that represent lightning, and many of the generators you shoot will produce electricity.
  4. There is also writing on the wall that claims "David Richard Knows." I don't know much on him but I have read that he is connected to the papers one can collect.
  5. At night time at the front door two of the "this is not an entrance" disappear and are replaced by a bright white light. (Edit: if you stand close to it you can hear a faint radio sound saying something, I can't make it out though. Someone please check this, I could be wrong and it could have been a far off car radio).

I understand these are just "facts" however I do not know how to put them together. Sorry for the lack of pictures but if you visit the observatory you will see that there is evidence to my claims, if anyone needs pictures though comment and I will post anything you'd like to see about my observations.

I hope I may have helped shed light on the mystery.
