I’ve been re-reading the Epsilon Tract and I dont think it’s meant to be decoded like a clean cipher.

I think its more like a “rulebook” that tells the player HOW to hunt EEs.

Not saying I solved anything btw.

The tract repeats the same ideas over and over, and if you strip the weird metaphors (apple/peach/eagle/dove/lake) you get a gameplay pattern:

UPWARD is the #1 keyword

\- Upward flows truth form

\- Upward generosity

\- Look upwards and see the stars

In GTA language upward basically screams high altitude + high vantage points.

Mount Chiliad, tall towers (Maze Bank), mountain ridges, etc.

COMPLETION GATES / end to beginning (Omega to Alpha, Z to A)

It keeps doing inversion stuff backwards, dead/not dead, end/start, etc.

To me that reads like you need to revisit things AFTER the story / after certain milestones.

Like start at the end type logic.

PAYING = PROGRESS (this one is literal)

The tract says seeking = paying, paying = understanding, etc.

Thats basically the Epsilon questline in plain sight. Donate, buy stuff, comply, move forward.

So one layer of the “mystery” is its literally teaching you the cult mechanic.

TRANSFORMATION / BECOME THE BIRD

Trees become birds, birds become trees, eagle/dove imagery, reality is flipped etc.

The closest actual mechanic in GTA that matches “become the eagle” is Peyote.

If you can become animals/birds, you can see stuff from angles you normally cant.

TIME + WEATHER WINDOWS

Rockstar loves conditions (time, weather, state of game).

The tract’s “paradigms/years” talk feels like hinting the world state matters, not just location.

So my interpretation is:

Finish / progress the game, go UPWARD return under exact conditions (time + weather) use transformation (Peyote) to see what you can’t normally see and expect inversion/backwards sequencing.

How this fits some common suspects:

\- Aliens/UFOs: “look upward”, stars, 9th paradigm stuff, cult obsession.

\- Maze Bank: obvious UPWARD test spot even if not named.

\- Peyote/Eagle: literally “become the bird” mechanic.

\- Farming/harvest metaphors: maybe points to rural locations / old machinery / orchards, but this one is weaker (could just be flavor).
