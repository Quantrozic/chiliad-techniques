A New Approach: One List of Theories; Rigorous Requirements to Get a Theory on the List

▲ 6 ptsu/RegulateAndEngageJan 31, 20156 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Citation-Network Mapping of Theories ?

Hear me out:

One list of theories, sticked, that verified 100% hunters can document their attempts at debunking. In order to submit a theory or something that you want to add to the list, you have to submit your findings/observations/speculation/theory in a strictly regulated, mod-verified standard form. That way only theories fleshed out enough will make it onto the list, where (ideally) a group of dedicated hunters can try them out, one by one.

Obviously this could exist to supplement the existing style of this subreddit (ie, you don't HAVE to submit your theories to the list, but you wouldn't necessarily get the benefit of some of the best hunters combing through your theory). This could also cut down on some of the clutter and continue to cultivate ideas (from non 100%ers) and testers (from the verified 100% players).

Thoughts?
