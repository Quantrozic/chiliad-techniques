Some (perhaps) overlooked thoughts on the mystery and the mural... (Theory)

After going through a lot of things relating to the mystery and the mural for about a year now, there are a few things which I think should be reconsidered and a few things which may have been overlooked. If this post will end up seeming very disorganized, I am very sorry - but there are quite a lot of aspects to cover.

First, about the meaning of the three symbols at the bottom of the mural:
Many seem to accept the theory (or view) that these represent each of the characters in the game, with the UFO referring to Michael due to the fact that his encounter with aliens. While that might be the case, I have always thought that the meaning would be a bit different. If we think of it as referring to the cults or "religious" groups in the game, then the UFO could refer to Epsilon, the Egg to Children of the Mountain (since a cracked egg is, for some animals, birth), and the "Jetpack" to the Altruists, due to the symbol on the big rock at their camp (more on that later). While it in terms of the characters would still point to the same people as in the other view, it might mean that certain things need to be done in terms of the missions / activities relating to these three groups. What it would be, I do not know. Perhaps certain things are mostly being done "wrong", if there is such a thing in the game. But let's assume for now that there is.

The five X's: Here there are quite a few things they could mean - and perhaps they refer to more than just one (and it is likely that I do not mention all of them here). Think of how many things come in fives, in the game. Heist missions, and in four of those there are choises to be made and X's connected to the choise (on the planning screen), granted that not all of them are red.
Another thing is relating to the people brought to the Alturist camp. 5 is the maximum number of people you can bring there, if you end with two at the same time. What if there is a certain combination to this, which obviously would be really hard to get right, but could be a trigger for an alternate endning to that part of the story?

The therapy sessions: Five times you have to accept or reject advice from Dr. F. What if there is a certain combination to these also? Perhaps combined with either killing him or letting him live in the end? This might be a bit far fetched, but when doing the phone sessions the reject option is a red x. Might not matter at all, but still.

The "Jetpack" symbol: I use " " because I am not sure if that is actually what it is supposed to be a symbol of. I have heard that it could be a diver, and thereby the three symbols at the bottom referring to the collectables in the game (spaceship parts, letter scraps and submarine parts).
A further note on this, relating to what is on the rock in the Altruist camp: When I look at it, they do not look all that similar to me. Rather, the symbol on the rock, I would say, look more like a backpack, which could somehow be pointing to Ursula (perhaps meaning that she will have to be taken there).

The giant bell at the chicken factory: This one has 1917 on it. While it obviously seems to be referring to a year, does anyone have any knowledge as to whether or not it could be a clue to a specific time, that is 19:17? Don't know if there is any significance to that.

I have heard a lot of people talk about overlays on the map. Just a thought here, since I am unable to do this myself: Has anyone tried to see if the X's would match all the UFO locations? With the crashed one from the Gunrunning DLC, there should be five, right?

In all honesty, I do think by now that whatever the mural is hinting at is something already found and the reason why it seems something still might be missing could very well be because it was interpreted wrongly.
Of course, I really hope that there still is something hidden to uncover, somehow - and sometimes the answers is right in front of us...
Perhaps it means doing a few things which most players would not really consider doing - like not stealing the money in the last of the Epsilon missions, actually sacrificing Ursula and so on.

One last thing: A video on YouTube, which I am quite sure is fake, it was mentioned that the picture from the chiliadmystery.com website (where it in the code reads "it is here", does not hint at the location in the tunnel, but rather at a place where that can be seen on a screen (as far as I remember). Now, while that might be a video with fake content, it still could be so that if (and this is indeed a big IF) that website really is a clue from Rockstar, then that could just be the way they did it. Question is then: Are there any screens in the game that shows something from inside the tunnel?

Obviously, I cannot provide any answers here (and I sure hope this did not come across in a bad way or in any way indicating that I would know better than others, since I clearly do not know the answers, and it would seem most people do not either) - but I think that the questions raised here would be of relevance if there indeed is such a thing as the Chiliad Mystery.
