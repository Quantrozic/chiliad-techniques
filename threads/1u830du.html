As you probably already know, I used to extract assets from that PS3 devkit. Of the various existing RPFs, there's a version from July 20, 2013 (the final version corresponds to August 1, 2013) of dt1\_tc.rpf.

The "beta" version consists of this:

Jul 20, 2013 version

While version 01.00:

Aug 1, 2013 version

The most notable difference of all is the absence of the CTD (and the mention on the cmap) associated with the lights of the Fort Zancudo UFO. It's worth noting that, curiously, although rsn\_os\_specialfloaty2 is in that ctd in version 01.00, in this version it's inside dt1\_tc\_dufo\_core.cdr. Furthermore, I don't know if dt1\_tc\_dufo\_core.cdr has any differences in your model.

This light, in the final version, points to the entrance of the Fort Zancudo bunker, but it seems this decision was made very late in the game's development. Remember that v\_int\_6 had already been removed from the game files (although it still existed in versions from Jul but before Jul 20).

Could the late addition of this light actually be just an internal easter egg by the developers to reference the cut interior?

It's worth noting that, apart from the cmaps, I don't know if the other files contain relevant differences or not.
