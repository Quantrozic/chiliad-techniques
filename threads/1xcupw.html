Yo people listen I've never posted on a forum in my life so go easy please, constructive criticism wouldn't go a miss though. And yes I do have 100%.

Right I want to start with the 5 mountain glyphs, thanks to Rafman posting the real positions of the glyphs we all know that the mt. Chiliad mural does not line up with them, from a birds eye view anyway.

But what I did notice using Rafs image is that they line up a lot better, almost perfectly with the white boxes on the "hippie camp" mound (mural) on the west side of the camp, with the red circle at the top being the red glyph under the viewing platform on mt. Chiliad.
There are 6 white boxes on the mound though, which makes me think that we are missing a glyph, my thoughts are that it may be hidden somehow or we can only see it at a certain time. Just putting that out there.
In my opinion the mural on mt. Chiliad is not showing us the location of the glyphs. What it is showing us I don't have a clue but we'll get there don't worry.

Okay just a little bit on the WOW! Signal, I think it's really important. I think it might be giving us a time and day to be in a certain place, I done some quick research and found out the WOW! Signal happened (in real life) at 22:16:34 EST that's 23:16:34 EDT on a Monday. And I think it was a new moon that night, I have tried standing in numerous places at this time and moon phase but found nothing yet. Again just putting it out there, planting ideas hopefully.

So last but not least the Space Docker, I know many people are doubting the poor thing but I think it may be key. I know this has been discussed many of times but the green lights and horn are defiantly there for a reason.
To me the circle disks sticking out the front bumper are not just decoration, it is there to protect the headlights, to an extent. Grill me all you want but I think we need to discover something with the space dockers headlights.
I've searched the mountain in the area where the "missing glyph" might be according to my "hippie camp" mural theory I mentioned earlier but no luck with the space docker lights.

If someone with photo shop skills could line up the "hippie camp" mural with the mountain glyphs somehow that would b great help.

Shout out Rafman and everyone else doing good work on this mystery, feels good to get my thoughts out to you guys, I have a few more things to say but I think this post is long enough already. Now i would like you lots thoughts?
