Misconception about Karma

▲ 19 ptsu/PooPGoddFeb 7, 202028 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Karma / Pacifist Playthrough Protocol ?

Everyone of you should be familiar with the Karma theory and how people believe we need to complete the game with positive Karma.
After reading paragraphs about different Karma related theories I noticed that little of you actually know the concept of Karma which is not "YOU GET WHAT YOU DESERVE" but more like an energy or action that you constantly create, allowing you to change your future by actions that you do in the present.

You can read it up in detail on this page for example https://ideapod.com/heres-great-explanation-karma-really-means-can-improve-life/

However, if we can let go of this understanding of happiness, we can see that all we really need is to live deeply in the present moment with mindfulness and discover our true nature.

With that definition of Karma some of you might already see a connection to the mural/the main story where we can change our path based on our previous choices in the game, best seen in the last mission where you literally can change the fate of all 3 characters by one decision, based on which Karma you currently have in your mind towards the 2 killable characters.
At the end of the game you are free to decide which characters you kill or spare with the mural representing those 3 ways of how your future might look.

\-A you kill Michael and stop your journey before reaching "enlightenment"

\-B you kill Trevor and do the same

\-C you spare both of them, thus keeping the balance in your life and reaching the end of the journey

So all in all with this post I wanted to point out that we don't need a Karma playthrough as such is based on a misconception of Karma, but in actuallity we already have a Karma playthrough by simply beating the game. The reason so many people have pointed Karma out as a possible solution is because it gets referenced throughout the game for the pure reason of us understanding the story on a deeper level.

Your Karma decides which ending you get and it actually doesn't matter which way you pick, all you need is to understand that Karma is the freedom to decide which way you pick and what future it will result it. And the best choice would be ending C where you keep the balance between the characters alive and get the best future, with this ending being a representation of spiritual enlightenment as you decided for the most dangerous yet the most rewarding way, showing yourself what you are capable of if you confront your fears rather than dodging them
