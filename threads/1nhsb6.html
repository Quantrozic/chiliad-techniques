Not sure if this is a re-post but I was looking at the map on top of Mt Chiliad this morning when I had a sudden realization, in my opinion the dinosaur egg could mean the dinosaur statue outside Rex's Diner, the jetpack could be a nod towards the statue of a rocket ship that can be found outside another diner (Of which the name I don't know) and the UFO could mean any number of things from the one at the bottom of the ocean to the one hovering above Blaine County (But most likely it's another diner statue that I have not yet discovered) I'm still hopeful for a jetpack in the game but I think that these symbols were just Rockstar's way of giving us incentive to follow the clues and discover Mt Chiliads secret (Because who doesn't want a jetpack?) I'm thinking that perhaps there may be something nearby those diners or in the exact center of the three that may be the key to solving this mystery and I'll keep you updated if I find anything :)
