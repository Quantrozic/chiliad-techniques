'Stay put' zones / interesting Hippie Camp findings (gameplay spoilers below for those who haven't completed all endings)

Right then... this might actually be something substantial!

I remember seeing a post a while back about a '60 second rule', that if a character stays in a spot longer than 60 seconds, then it's for a reason. I'd second this notion as it's a very rare occasion to switch away, switch back and find your guy in the same area let alone the same spot.

I found 3x spots in the Hippie Camp where each character stays put, seemingly forever. Just nipped downstairs to check and sure enough, all 3 of the guys are exactly where I left them, not moved a step.

Trevor: on the red dot in the middle (the one that has the big red arrow pointing to it)
Franklin: on the red circle on top of the right-hand-side mound with the windows on it (there should be an eye over a moon glyph)
Michael: just below the top of the hippie camp, below the two yogis, there is a small ledge with an alien in a faded circle

This alone I find highly interesting. What input are they waiting for? But there are two other things which REALLY grabbed me.

#1
That red dot T is stood on? There's an always-burning wastebin. T has death-by-fire as a potential story option. I did try pouring petrol to link the two red dots but nothing happened (not sure if it's noteworthy but seems incredibly easy to catch it a-fire on that red dot). The game records your deaths by fire.

#2
There are two glyphs at this hippie camp. One just below where M is stood, it's an eye above a raincloud... the other where I've positioned F (eye, 3 straight lines, crescent moon). If the glyphs are to designate UFO positions, then why are there two seperate glyphs?

To save people some time, here are things I already tried:

  • Altering the order / position of the guys. When I tried putting them in different positions, they just wandered off
  • petrol from small red dot to big red dot, surrounded, petrol against the car+ufo
  • having them all in position during a crescent moon at 3am
  • lobbing a grenade at T in frustration from F's position
  • be careful getting the guys there, if you get wasted they all reset
  • zooming into the crescent moon via heavy sniper

If this is a glitch, it's the oddest most circumstantial glitch ever. Sorry no pics, felt the text is self-explanatory.
