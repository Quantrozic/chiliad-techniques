Cloth strip flags at two interesting locations

▲ 5 ptsu/HuffheinzNov 6, 20234 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Memory Scanning for Hidden FlagsMemory-Scanning for Hidden State FlagsMission-Replay Flag Diffing

If you go up on the roof across from the Sinner's Passage Mural in Textile City you'll see a yellow cloth strip tied around a metal railing on the left.

At the Parson's Rehabilitation Center mosaic there is a black cloth strip tied around the wooden structure above and to the left of the mosaic.

These are two locations of artwork that have real-life parallels. Both locations have been speculated to be possible clues or Easter eggs. I searched around other artwork installations in-game such as the Rancho Towers and the Oeuvre Gallery for other cloth strips and haven't found any.

These cloth strips appear on the PS4, but not on the PS3. It's possible that these cloth strips were omitted from the PS3 due to lack of resources and included in the PS4 port thanks to its greater capability. It's also possible that they were added to attract our attention to the two places they were added.

Can someone with access to the internals check to see if there are any other instances of these cloth strip flags? If there are only a few other instances of these cloth strips it might be interesting to examine those locations.
