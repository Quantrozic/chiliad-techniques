Just an observation I thought was a bit strange...

I've been playing around in Director Mode, and noticed that most/all of the story characters but Cris share a neutral sun flare. Micheal, Franklin, and Trevor's are the same as in story mode, but Cris Formage's matches Michael's perfectly. (Sorry if this is known.)

I have no idea if these different flares have meaning, but this is just more evidence that they ARE intentionally different... with Michael and Cris's matching the Eye/Sun in the gyphs almost perfectly. Why would Rockstar have applied the same post-fx filter to Cris and only Cris?
