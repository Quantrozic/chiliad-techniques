Debunking the Maze Bank fountain

▲ 10 ptsu/cbwysicAug 21, 201656 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Debunk-First Review Workflow

Up until recently, I was a firm believer that the scrolling squares in the maze bank fountain were in some way related to the mystery. Today I concede that the patterns in the Maze Bank fountain are simply a texture glitch. Many have already claimed it was a texture glitch but offered no proof or anything really to support their claims. It's easy to say something is a glitch but if you offer nothing to support your opinion then your claims fall on deaf ears to those who chose to believe.

I previously posted a couple shots of the same squares showing up on the Union Depository (https://imgur.com/a/K50z8) but after looking at them for a while they don't seem to mean anything. But, why were they in the fountain? Probably for the same reason this floor (https://imgur.com/a/4u7vO) has a bunch of logos on it that keep changing based on the angle you look at it, it's a glitch.

The main thing that kept me believing for so long was the behavior of the texture. It scrolled for a short time, then stopped for a short time, as if it were on some sort of timer. I couldn't find any example of another fountain showing this behavior, it seemed to be unique to Maze Bank and the surrounding fountains (Eagle, etc).

Fast forward a bit and I find myself parachuting down to an area where I find a fountain that has a water texture with the same stop/start behavior. I didn't feel like setting up a youtube to post a video so here's a shot of the fountain and the location in game so you can check it out for yourself: https://imgur.com/a/vEPFv

After seeing this, and the other crazy glitches that still haven't been fixed in this game, I think the Maze Bank fountain is now debunked. Edit: Before I get tarred and feathered, I still believe there is something to be discovered at Maze Bank. The features seem like there is something more to them, I just don't think the squares in the water texture are part of it.
