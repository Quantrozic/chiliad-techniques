The Two Star Never Wanted Level

.

From what I've seen and done with this, it seems like Michael has a connection with the Chiliad cable cars. Playing normally, if you manage to get any of the three characters on top of the cable cars, it gets you an instant 2 star wanted level. After doing some experimenting I figured out that even with cheats or mods turning the police off completely, if you get on top of one of the cars with Mike, it still gives you 2 stars. This video shows me using the cheats and the stars showing up anyways. There are a few parts the video that drag a little, but I go through several variants of the cheats being on or off, and how the cops are affected on and off the cable car. Now, I haven't tested enough to see if Mike gets this every time, but every time I've tried with him this has happened. It's also happened once or twice with the other characters, but their results seemed random. Sometimes they would get the stars despite the cheats being active, and sometimes not. Also anytime you land on the top of a cable car, it freezes in its position, so I can't help but wonder if you're supposed to parachute onto it in a certain spot to see something or shoot at something. I dunno. So this may need to be explored more, and preferably on other platforms as well.

The Classic Art Frauds

.

Studying the paintings in the Pacific Standard Public Deposit Bank, I've found that they are all based on paintings in real life. There are 12 unique paintings in the building based on paintings from the 17 and 1800's. 6 of them are portraits and differ slightly from their real life counterparts. Each of the portraits have had their facial expressions changed, or the size of their noses or faces, and in one case a single hand was made bigger along with the facial changes. Here's a video where I go through each picture and show a transitional morph for the pictures with differences. I've placed these in in the video in chronological order.

My first thought is that they simply changed each of the portraits to try and get around copyrights. But why only the portraits? I don't think the copyright is the case because even the self portrait of one of the artists has been changed into a sly mischievous smile, but another painting the same artist did that wasn't a portrait (and a famous piece being the signing of the Declaration of Independence.) wasn't changed at all.

.

I don't know what any of this might mean for the mystery, but until we make real progress, we can't know for sure what is a clue and what isn't.
