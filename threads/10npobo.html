Rooster sound at 9.03am at Cluckin’ Bell factory

▲ 43 ptsu/doofy102Jan 28, 202332 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Alchemical and Esoteric Symbol Matching

I’ve been wondering why there are 9s on small stone plates on some of the cliffs around the map, then it clicked that maybe they’re a reference to Big Smoke’s famous fast food order. “Two number nines”. We know that rockstar is willing to go deep to reference GTA memes.

Anyway, this theory took me to Cluckin’ Bell at 9am. At 9.03 there’s always a rooster sound.

https://youtu.be/L56sfqPdhkk

If you aim your weapon while the sound is playing, the sound will abruptly stop, meaning it is very sensitive to what the player is doing.

Maybe the cock on the mountain is starting to make sense.

I’ve been checking out large sodas and so far there has been nothing.

EDIT

The sound triggers a couple seconds earlier when you're on the train tracks, right on 9 or 8.59. Maybe it's a good time to remember the shape of the train track. https://i.ytimg.com/vi/Bd1I6BPT_ow/maxresdefault.jpg Big Smoke is intensifying.
