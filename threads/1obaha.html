I mentioned this in the comments field in relation to The Hatch - but I feel there's enough to this for it to have its own thread. Sisyphus is mentioned in the game - both as the name of a theater, and in Franklin's Children Of The Mountain test.

So who is Sisyphus? Let's wiki: http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sisyphus

It's a fact that we can move both boulders and colored balls in GTA V. It isn't impossible to move them to the top of a mountain, though it would prove very difficult. The Hatch Morse Code was cracked - and it seems to be a gag about Roman Bellic's penchant for bowling. It was Sisyphus' curse to move a boulder to the top of a mountain and watch it roll down again... But he was also a symbol for the "treacherous seas", where the hatch is found, and at crushing depth, no less. He was also a symbol of the rising and setting sun, and we have a plethora of rising/setting sun symbolism at the Altruist camp.

This might earn me a tinfoil hat indeed, but I've been convinced for a while that Sisyphus was relevant to a lot of the easter-eggs' symbolism. The new discovery at the hatch - the cracked code - only made it seem more relevant. In other words - should we indeed "go bowling", with the colored balls or the movable rocks? If this all sounds tedious and insane, check this out (source is wiki):

"In experiments that test how workers respond when the meaning of their task is diminished, the test condition is referred to as The Sisyphusian Condition. The two main conclusions Of the experiment are that people work harder when their work seems more meaningful, and that people underestimate the relationship between meaning and motivation."

I'm going to start moving those balls up mountains. And if that doesn't work, I'm gonna start moving boulders.
