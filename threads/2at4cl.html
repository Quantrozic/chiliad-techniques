Legion Square

My comment got too big on the Legion Square thread so I figured I'd just make a thread for it

I was standing on top of the Maze Bank building and was convinced that the Egg has to be found there. When seeing the plaza from a helicopter the lines radiate outwards from the building almost like the bottom of the Mural. Also the main staircase which kind of makes a double helix with the suspicious fountain seems to be creating the pattern of a cracked egg almost (seen from close range in a helicopter)

The two fountains outlined in red I believe? One has multiple 'benches' surrounding it and almost looks like a sort of beam reticule or something like that. And the other smaller one is ringed with red color. Reminds me of the two big red dots at the Hippy Camp

From the top as its known you have a direct line sight to Chilad. The helipad marking 17 is within a small circle which is within a bigger circle. Two circles, so oddly I thought 1+7 is 8 and that within a circle made me think of the Infinite 8 Killer and then I realized something else

The 17 marking is aligned to Chiliad but the building itself is aligned to the 3 streets that run through Downtown LS:

  • Alta St runs from Davis Ave near the Maze Bank Arena all the way up to Clinton Ave and converges with Power Street and Baytree Canyon Ave
  • Power Street runs from Alta Street directly through the heart of downtown LS through Clinton Ave and converges with Alta Street and Baytree Canyon Ave, has a direct view to the Vinewood sign from almost any point along it's path, Maze Bank Building is situated between Alta Street and Power Street
  • Elgin Ave runs from Innocence Blvd in Davis--which itself (Innocence Blvd) runs from Puerto Del Sol to Sustancia Road--and runs up to Occupation Ave and veers right up to Senora Road. As far as I can tell, there is an unnamed stretch which could be called Meteor Street

There is a fourth street to the left of Alta St which begins from San Andreas Ave and also runs all the way up to Baytree Canyon Ave and converges with Alta St and Clinton Ave

What is important to me about this is all these streets are almost specifically aligned with the Vinewood sign like on a grid like pattern and the street that runs through the heart of it all and has a direct view of the Vinewood sign is called Power Street? Too coincidental to me.

Alta means High in Spanish, that street and Power Street basically say High Power. Idk I know its all loose ideas but still these alignments stick out to me.

Back to why I think things are downtown LS, when standing atop the Maze Bank Building or at least flying above it Legion Square faintly resembles the Jetpack Man seen on the Mural when it's lit up at night. There's this gate underneath which is what the main post is about but I always found it odd and intriguing. The site of Michael and Trevor's odd experiences and the big area with the little puddle and arches to me seems like a big sundial.

Maybe we should be there under certain moon light or sunlight and wait to see a specific shadow at a certain time. Under the right conditions only so many minutes could potentially produce a shadow so maybe it could be as easy as sitting there for 20 minutes in bright sun waiting for the shadow. Idk that place seems very mysterious.

If the Maze Bank Building is the Egg and Legion Square is the Jetpack it was kind of hard to think of what the UFO would be but with all this talk of a UFO mission with Solomon Richards makes me think that is what is meant to be the UFO possibly on the Mural

I know there's a line from the UFO to the Jetpack but maybe the mission or cutscene or whatever involves that location

Legion Square has those flags all along it's premises that are the same ones as the flags at the Arcadius Business Center. They can also be found at the Kortz Center. Odd. Idk where else they can be found though I kind of forget but I think it's just those places.

So it's kind of like as above so below, on the Mural you have the UFO and lightning bolts signifying the storm and power related trigger you need. The UFO shows up in rain but still the Mural bolts signify power.

You have the UFO above the shed of Chiliad with a line drawn through the shed straight to the egg. If they're telling us the Chiliad UFO is above Chiliad why draw a straight line through the shed and have seven dashes on it? Why have a drawing of the shed within the shed? We're gonna see the Eye and CBWYSIC?

That's why I think the Mural is telling us more then just the UFO will appear atop Chiliad, the Line is drawn through Chiliad and if you kind of do a top down view like in Character switch you can kind of see how the straight line can go directly to the Maze Bank building.

Chiliad is the highest point in the North and requires a storm or power to reveal it's egg.

Maze Bank Building is the highest point in the South of the map and is situated between Alta St and POWER STREET which basically spells out High Power. As above So below. The two badger radio towers on the building are suspicious given their rarity in game. They seem to be forming an angle and possible a search area to triangulate.

Idk but I am just very convinced for some reason that if you go to the Maze Bank building atop it in certain conditions which I'm sure has been tried but that you can unlock something.

Could be the reason for the different color in Altruist Glyphs. Yellow being for Chiliad and Red being for Los Santos? Ok I've said enough nonsense but I hope if you read through it something made sense or you were able to take something from it and apply it to your own thoughts.
