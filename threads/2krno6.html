This is more of a simple resource to easily get to the right spot of the FZ UFO. Some people use the altitude meter, some people are searching 10 mins for it and I thought I'd just share my own simple tactic which lets me fly straight to the ufo. This acts more as a resource for people having trouble with this. Maybe you know this trick, but some people don't and therefor it could come in handy.

Ok, so here it is:

  1. Go to your map, and to fort zancudo.
  2. You'll see 2 roads, the left entrance road to FZ and the right road to the water cleaning centre around FZ, draw an inmaginery line between these roads and place your marker right in the center of it, like this: http://imgur.com/8DWAcH2
  3. Fly with your helicopter to that exact spot, while elevating untill the text in your bottom right corner changes from "fort zancudo" to "san andreas". (you won't see "fort zancudo" but you will see "san andreas" appear)
  4. the ufo is only a little higher then the "san andreas" mark on your screen, so keep elevating a little bit more (you'll get the feeling) and fly straight to the spot you marked on your map.

you will fly straight to the UFO.

hoped this helped at least someone. :-)
