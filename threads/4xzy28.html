Attempting a Perfect Playthrough

▲ 6 ptsu/the-antithesistAug 16, 201636 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Karma / Pacifist Playthrough Protocol ?

I haven't seen much development on this idea lately, and as a lifetime gamer/part time completionist, I know that you are almost always guaranteed an easter egg or special reward for getting to 100% - now obviously this has been done with the UFO's and those were pretty easy to solve. So many of the games I grew up with had something like that for 100%, but most also had a special reward for those who went above and beyond to get 101%. My theory is that there has to be some way to take the 100% one step further, like back in the good old days.

My main goal here is to get 100% on the first play through, while applying the Karma theory for Michael, and trying to do everything the character would. I feel like there is a correct way to play the game - the way the cut scenes play out depending on which character it's triggered with, and what the individuals say in their dialogue outside of missions. Perfect example - I swear I heard Michael tell Franklin that Trevor was the one who was supposed to die So I've started writing everything down...

Obviously this is going to take some time, so any words encouragement or advice would be appreciated. I just didn't feel like this theory was properly put to rest. At the very least at least it will be a good file to test other theories against, knowing that everything in the game is completed to the fullest. If I'm going to put hours into this thing it's going to be for a real reason, this goose chase has gone on long enough.

TL;DR: 100% gold on first play through, no checkpoints. Play like the characters would, and still try to finish everything.

Kifflom sister-brothers. Pray for my sanity.
