Understanding the Altruist Cult through the myth of Icarus (or not)

▲ 14 ptsu/mosquito_mangeAug 24, 20164 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:San Andreas Legacy Myth Regression Testing

Preface before posting: I do not actually believe the following model will help us uncover any additional secrets. If anything, what the following model demonstrates is that R* made the world of GTA V so rich (and so mysterious) that it is easy to take even just a portion of the content and form one's own cryptic (but unverifiable) story around it. In some ways, it is a testament to the depth of the game that tinfoil hat theories come so easily and are so entertaining to consider (or dismiss).

We can perhaps use the mythological tale of Icarus to better understand the Altruist Cult. As you recall from the Greek myth, Icarus was the young man who ignored warnings and flew too close to the sun, resulting in his wax wings melting and him falling into the sea. The baby boomer-aged Altruists resemble Icarus' father, Daedalus. Just as Daedalus warned Icarus of displaying hubris in the path of his flight, so do the Altruists warn of hubris in their statement that "continued focus on the superiority of the sub 40 younger generation will result in the catastrophic and tragic end to our civilization."

Daedalus was the craftsman responsible for building the Labyrinth for King Minos of Crete to imprison the Minotaur, a half-man, half-bull monster. We will recall that one of the Altruist Cult members is a man with a bull skull tattooed on his forehead, and we will recall the large horned skull in the fire cave below their camp. When Daedalus and Icarus attempted to make their escape from the Labyrinth, Icarus' mistake led to tragedy. The Altruists resemble a shut-in version of Daedalus in that, having once tried to reach the outside world, but following a tragic setback, they have decided they must retreat back to the secure walls of their fortress and blame technology and youth for the problems of the world, rather than turning inward to see their contribution to the tragedy.

You will recall that another Altruist Cult member bears a tattoo of Jesus, in which the sun is depicted on Jesus' chest. Just as Jesus came to represent a sacrifice for our sins in the Christian religions, so too did he who flew too close to the sun come to be the symbol for the Altruist's sacrifice- the modern day version of wings of wax and feathers is the jet pack, and thus the jet pack is depicted on the Altruist's sacrificial stone. These sacrificial themes may also help us understand how, on one hand, the Altruists appear to be worshiping the sun and, on the other hand, describing ills associated with the sun on their website.

Now, let's stretch this even further for maximum tinfoil effect. Using the area of the Altruist's Camp as a starting point, a player could simulate Icarus' flight by flying as close as possible to the sun before descending into the sea (in the myth, he was to have crashed in the sea near the island Icaria, which now bears his name) or by falling back to the sacrificial stone. I can all but guarantee that nothing new would happen (after all, exhaustive Codewalker findings point to nothing of the sort), but this is a game in which we regularly test connections between even the smallest hints, even if it only leaves us splattered on the rocks below, haha.
