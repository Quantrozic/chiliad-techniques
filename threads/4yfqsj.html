The Doors of Perception.

▲ 52 ptsu/denturedocelotAug 19, 201635 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Eye-Tracking Attention Mapping ?

Since interactive doors have seen a recent resurgence in popularity, I started doing some in depth research on their associations in the files and scripts.

During this process I have come across a couple that immediately caught my interest. Both of these doors have writing that I have found quite hard to discern.

The first is named prop_ron_door_01. It contains some very pronounced writing on the top and bottom sections. This door seems to be used in multiple locations.

The second example is v_ilev_tort_door.ydr. Going by the file name alone, my assumption is that this door exists in the room where Trevor tortures Kerimov.

A closer look at this door reveals a small spot with faded writing.

Since my current project is only about 1/3 complete, I don't want to lose momentum trying to interpret these intriguing details. On the other hand, I would rather not overlook them completely.

I also had a feeling that some of the regulars here might appreciate a couple textures that may actually contain a clue of some sort.

Either way, it's nose back to the grindstone time for me.
