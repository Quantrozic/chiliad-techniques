"Good" Karma Playthrough

Documented under:Karma / Pacifist Playthrough Protocol

A while back somebody on here mentioned that there is a subtle change in one of the prologue cutscenes if you kill only with Trevor.

Inspired by that idea I wanted to start a new playthrough in which I would try not to kill at all if possible as Michael. The games story really tries to push that Michael is trying to be a good person so I believe that playing him as such is key to the karma theory.
This also means no womanizing, or pretty much any activity that would result in negative feedback from Dr. Friedlander.

This also brings me to an idea I had about the blue/red dot random events that happen that you can help out with.
I believe that these act pretty much identical to the events in Red Dead Redemption, including having point and an overall meter. The only difference being we don't see either in GTAV. These random events act as a way to balance out your karma if you do kill somebody as Michael (or Franklin, who I also try to use to help during random events to keep his karma from being too shitty).

At least I hope, because I did have a lapse in judgement and kill the boat abductors in one of the first missions before I realized that Franklin probably would have thrown them overboard himself. It then clicked that all of Michael's missions so far have non lethal ways of finishing them (at least up to the first heist, I'm still replaying so I can't 100% confirm they all do, almost positive they don't). This is not true for any other character, which again solidifies my confidence in Michael and blue/red dot random events being key to a good karma playthrough.

If somebody else would like to try this as well, preferably without killing the men on the boat then we might be able to figure something out, or at least eliminate what might be decent ideas at least.

There are also some theories I have about possibly having to sacrifice Trevor if we do have to sacrifice somebody. The game encourages murder while playing Trevor almost immediately in both his in game debuts. With you having to murder the guard and then him wrecking Johnny K with heavy kick overheads.

Sorry for the wall of text, this is my first post and hopefully at least some of these ideas will help edge us a little closer to the truth.
