Are ingame hints pointing towards using the cellphone camera?

▲ 115 ptsu/mrYellowCakeJan 9, 201761 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Snapmatic Photography & Location-Data Cataloguing

I have speculated before that the camera in GTA V is more important than what we may think.
As we have been clutching at straws for years now, why not try to interpret the mural like this:

  1. The lightning symbols can also represent a flash symbol. https://cdn3.iconfinder.com/data/icons/glypho-photography/64/camera-flash-auto-512.png
  2. The "Eye" could also represent the phi-looking symbol found on cameras. https://i.stack.imgur.com/fYoTa.jpg
  3. The glyph that we really do not know what it represents is a symbol for photographing something. http://2.bp.blogspot.com/-8FyOF8YUnKM/U0jh4Mf4AoI/AAAAAAAABN0/3rqYBdLVzGk/s1600/sand+glyph.JPG https://i2.wp.com/www.fdphotostudio.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/05/Rim-lighting-scheme.png
  4. "Shoot for the stars" message found on UV-map could actually mean to "photograph something". To document the conspiracy. Perhaps the PENRIS building? http://i.imgur.com/iFeKIed.jpg http://vignette1.wikia.nocookie.net/chiliad/images/b/b1/3-_HkmjSHu.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/245?cb=20131109055316

Put together with the fact that R* have included a secret collection of monkey mosaics to photograph, using the camera as a trigger could be feasible.
If we are not exploring the camera as a trigger, it could explain why we are not moving forward in this hunt for whatever the end goal is.

A potential starting point to investigate this is to photograph areas of interest in succession during conditions depicted by the other glyphs.
I do know that this post contain a lot of despised "what ifs" without any concrete proof, but I also hope that it sparks ideas and motivates.
