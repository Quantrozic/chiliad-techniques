The 'UFO' symbol on the Mural is a perfect circle!

▲ 6 ptsu/Sauceboss5Oct 23, 20158 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Comparative Mural Iconography Cataloging ?

Since the All-Seeing Eye symbol atop the mural represents a UFO, it is likely that the other 'UFO' looking symbol on the mural is actually something different. The Mural seems redundant having 2 different style symbols for a UFO, especially when there are only only several symbols total on the entire Mural.

It is likely possible that this 'UFO' symbol is actually something harder to see to the untrained eye. It is difficult to see, but the bottom and top part of the 'spacecraft' actually forms a perfect circle. The middle 'spinny' part of the spacecraft makes this hard to notice....but if you take the scope of a sniper rifle (which is indeed a perfect circle) and zoom into this symbol the player can see that the scope fits perfectly around this symbol, making it a perfect circle.

With this said.. what could this symbol possibly be if not a UFO? I think that it is likely to represent a figure of some kind in front of the Sun, do to it being a perfect circle and the fact that the image is implied to be in the sky, since there are 'hover' lines and since it does not have a shadow. (The cracked egg has a shadow, implying that it is an object on the ground).

Please share your thoughts and ideas on what this symbol could possibly be if not a UFO. Cheers!
