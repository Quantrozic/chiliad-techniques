So that overlay thing that was converted to flash kinda piqued my interest, and since I haven't done anything relating to all this in forever I thought I'd share this with you guys.

I was looking at it and basically I noticed that the "words" rotating at the top actually do look like words, and then I thought they might be a substitution cipher because they have pretty clear patterns.

I've tried solving it myself and tried solving it with an online solver but I think the answer might contain words that you only find in the game, like Chiliad for example.

Anway, here is what I'm talking about:

http://i.imgur.com/AYrl1sm.png

If you want to copy that easily:

B Z X C O Y Y K O
C O Y U I L W V
Y K O X W
U I L B Z X

Read the wiki article on what a substitution cipher is here:
http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Substitution_cipher

Basically the idea with solving these is you go for the most common, repeating letters and try to replace them with the most common letters in the english alphabet. So for example Y in the cipher could be a vowel since vowels are really common.

IDK if this will lead to anything but I figured it was worth a shot.

If nothing comes of this I have a few other ideas I might try and then I'm going back to thinking we've solved this as much as we were supposed to :/
