[Observation] Amanda works for Psychic Shoutout and it explains her license plate

▲ 55 ptsu/ClementsparrowDec 9, 201644 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:In-Game Internet & Website CrawlingNPC Pathfinding Observation Logging ?

Usual disclaimer: I have searched on this sub and google but could not find any reference about this, so I believe it's a new observation. Please correct me if I'm wrong. Also, I'm French, so please pardon my English.

So we all know the Psychic Shoutout in-game website that protagonists can use to have a funny chat with a "psychic". It occurred to me that I never tried it with Michael, so when I was bored watching youtube videos and a Trippy Commentaries video about this website popped in my recommendation list, I decided to have a look at it despite Trippy Commentaries videos being mostly bullshit. (Here is the video, at the point Michael visits the website). I think there are hints that the "psychic" Michael is chatting with is actually his wife Amanda. Here are the clues that make me believe that:

  • Trevor and Franklin chat with a psychic called "Marcy", but for Michael the psychic is called "Tammy"
  • Tammy knows Micheal's name before he said anything ("Hello Michael – I'm Ms Tammy"). Although she pretends to know that because she is a psychic, we all know that psychics do not exist and that she most probably knows Michael.
  • She is the one who suggest to talk about relationships.
  • She then makes very explicit comments about Amanda's various sex partners (some known from the story, like the tennis and yoga coaches ; some alleged from other dialogues such as the gardener ; and some new ones like a fireman).
  • Somehow, Michael acknowledges that all this is true ("I thought you'd tell me something I didn't already know, you ass."), which confirms that Tammy must be someone very close to him.
  • I think the tone of the chat is very similar to how Amanda and Michael fight in the story dialogues.
  • I think that the girl whose picture is on the website looks a lot like Amanda.
  • "Tammy" is a name that could be coined from the first and last letters of Amanda's family name (Townley, not De Santa) giving T and Y, and the first letters of "Amanda" in between.

External confirmations:

  • I don't know if this twitter account is official, but I have found this picture published by Amanda, where she has a similar makeup and wear exactly the same necklace than the girl on the website (only different color).
  • Amanda's license plate is "KRYST4L" and everybody wonders why. But the girl on the website has a crystal ball, so if that girl is actually Amanda, it would make sense for her to have a plate that says "crystal".
  • I believe I have seen a dialogue where Amanda says to Michael something like "I can read your mind", but I cannot find it back.

Another idea:

  • Having a stranger actually being Amanda reminds me of the interaction between Michael and the Zombie actor (with a line like "wait, Amanda, is that you?")

What to do about this?

  • First thing is that we now have an explanation for the "KRYST4L" license plate, no need to worry about that anymore.
  • Second thing is that it suggests new ideas, such as changing Amanda's car color to violet in Father/Son (Jimmy comments that she does not like the red, and she wears violet on the website) or visiting the website while hanging with Amanda. It could trigger new dialogues? (I tried neither, I cannot use the PS4 for now...)
  • Third thing is that booty calls and this website are believed by some to control weather and trigger rain (this is confirmed for Ursula). Knowing that it's actually Amanda chatting with Micheal could contribute to this analysis.
