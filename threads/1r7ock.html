My take on "Deciphering" the mural...

So many theories, some logical and some far fetched, have been thought of to explain the meaning of the mural.

While I may be a skeptic, still am, I find the whole thing fascinating.

I have been thinking about the mural, and I may have a rough idea of what it means.

Focusing on the mural I have a couple of questions I needed to ask before I can provide my theory.

Where did this mural come from?
Who drew the map?
Is the mural the original, or is it based on a design that has yet to be discovered?

We are over complicating the whole situation. The three UFOs that are airborne are man made. I believe that he Mt. Chiliad UFO is not the one depicted at the apex of the mountain. If that were the case, it would mean the mural itself would have been created by the government also. The UFO depicted at the top of the mural should hover directly above the highest point.

The placement of the five glyphs are accounted for. The meaning has been established.

Now...my theory.

The mural is a prophecy. The requirements are given to summon the UFO that is not man made. Now the three images the UFO, the Egg, the Rocketman, are not items that can be discovered.

Let's apply logic, imagine the trolley, the shed, and the observation deck were not there. How do you expect the indigenous folks of the time to scale the mountain at that time.

The sunken UFO, I argue, is the original and is the one that is depicted at the apex on the mural.

The three images from left to right, is a story.

The UFO on the left, was the original one that was seen from afar.

The cracked egg could mean rebirth after death.

The Rocketman while enticing, means the eventual transcendence to the ship.

Again this is a rough explanation...but this makes a lot of sense to me.
