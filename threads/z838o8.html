I have a bunch of theories but I’m trying them out now in between my regular story save and Director mode. Got 100% (on 3 different consoles lol) and I love looking for patterns. I think the doomsday mural definitely made it easier for us with color coding the mysteries.

Here’s some of my ideas

Yellow part of the DDH mural - UFO hunt, Race around the map finding the UFOs in the correct order and time (Underwater UFO looks like the one at Mt. Chiliad at 3am but it’s not physically there, it shows you the Sandy Shores UFO which is physically there and shows you FIB (could be next or last point), other UFOs are Zancudo, plus any UFO spots like Bishops WTF and Sightings at the airport and you start getting a loop around the state.

The reason I inverted the Chiliad mural is because on the yellow DDH mural it shows an eye with an arrow pointing backwards as well as the head facing the UFO the wrong way (Special note: The yellow mural has moons, waves, signals)

Red Mural - Infinite 8 killer, I don’t think Merle did it and I think the the cultists, the ghosts, the red arrows around the map and maybe clues on the in-game internet about murders all have something to do with it (Special note: The red mural has a Sun on it, rays, arrows and radioactive symbols)

The green mural has been solved and is the template for this mystery, collect things around the map (like spaceship parts) on certain days and times (like peyote), use what you’ve collected to follow a path to the next step (Bigfoot with the sounds, space docker and possibly its horn/lights), final confrontation (The Beast). Then an unlock (Beast in director mode. (Special note: The green mural has the earth on it, plants, peyote seeing a pattern?)

Then finally the purple mural, This one might be the hardest and probably has to do with all of the eyes around Los Santos and perspective with shadows based on the images in the mural, fun fact the building in front of the Penris building is shaped like a penis with the tip looking like a Mayan pyramid and, it lines up with the eye at the top. Lights, shadows, eyes, perspective (Special note: the murals have already showed us the moon, sun and earth, i think this one is the mind)

A couple of more notes, I think the colors on the mural correspond with the characters that are supposed to do them, green with Franklin because he talked to animals, red for Trevor because he was the only one that interacts with the cult/victims, and Michael for the purple mural because if the mural was blue it would’ve been too obvious and he had the most to do with psychology, the mind, karma and Epsilon.

I think once those three bottom mysteries are complete that will unlock the final mystery at Chiliad. If you notice on the Chiliad mural there’s three things on the bottom that work its way up top to the mountain UFO, Egg and Jetpack/diver (it could be an oxygen tank) same could be said for the mysteries.

Also last note I think strangers and freaks can also impact these, if you notice you have to do things a certain way in the final Bigfoot mission in order to complete the peyote hunt, you also get to choose who to sacrifice as Trevor (saving Ursula can make it rain, saving Packie adds him to your crew).
