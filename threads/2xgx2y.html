Some speculation on the definition of chiliad.

▲ 6 ptsu/SquaremupFeb 28, 201515 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Anagram and Constraint Word-Solving

Brother brothers,

I don't post many ideas in here I tend to keep them to myself as I'm sure most of them have already been covered but this may prompt a discussion so I digress.

I've had an idea regarding the definition of chiliad, I've seen the posts from a year ago but everyone seemed to be leaning on the idea that it refers to a thousand years, I say maybe its not years and its just a group of one thousand, maybe R* are trying to say that there are thousand specific things to do in this game and your story isn't complete / you haven't played the game / appreciated our hard work, until you've done them all..

What would the thousand things be? I'd go as far as to imagine collecting spaceship parts and letter scraps or nuclear waste only count as one thing each then gold in all the missions, how many awards are there? If they've made a game this size they surely want the hardcore player to really appreciate the magnitude and how else but to give them a huge amount of things to do and really simple instructions how to do it 'come back when your story (of a thousand things) is complete!! it's almost a ruse, it could be like they're saying; 'complete the game, do everything there is to do and come back to the mountain'
I really want to know if anyone has done a solid hard 100% with everything done and nothing skipped over. It's quite possible that it might only need one person to solve this just by playing extremely thoroughly and maybe triggering a thousand things in the background, there could be a cut scene, a phone call, a random npc, R* could have planned for the mystery to unravel it's self in any number of ways once all the triggers are met.

Why else would the mystery revolve around the mountain? Why is the mural at the top of it and not downtown somewhere in a conspiracy related area, I might be way off here but isn't the literal translation of Mount Chiliad either 'The mountain of a thousand years' or what I think is more likely; 'The mountain of a thousand things'
Is the mountain a thousand feet high? Am I missing something?

Of course this is broad speculation and I know I've done way more than a thousand different things in San Andreas but I know I haven't done the correct things and covered every base and achieved hardcore completion. The reason I think this way is because there has clearly been a huge amount of thought put into this mystery by its makers, surely the ultimate win for them would be to have everyone scrambling to solve something that's really simple and quite clever and just requires you to play the game to its fullest? Once you have appreciated their hard work, then you will be rewarded.

I'm sure a lot of people have brushed over this topic before without much thought but maybe this is the key? Just play the game and do it right, do everything that's available and a cool thing to do and the whole thing will reveal itself. What do you think guys? Sorry for rambling but you know how deep this mystery goes and how many little red herrings and dead ends there are and all those leads and suspicious things dotted around, Even if it is that simple, I'm never going to be the guy to be able to do it, I'm still only 36% on ps4 and i've already made lots of wrong decisions in the game, I'm not a very good hunter, but I like to think about the mystery a lot.

Happy hunting
