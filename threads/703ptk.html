According to the in-game Epsilon Website, the ultimate goal of going through the Epsilon Program is to turn yourself into a living God.

Cris Formage claims that achieving this will give you complete immortality and enlightenment, and will allow you to be saved from the upcoming Apocalypse that will soon destroy San Andreas as well as the entire Universe. Anyone who doubts this is “Unsaveable”.

In order to make it through the Program, followers must adhere to the ultimate moral code of Epsilonism: “Manifest Generosity”.

This phrase is mentioned constantly throughout the Epsilon Tract, and describes the ultimate rule that Epsilonists are expected to live by.

Ch.1 Vrs. 3 of the Tract says... “Upwards flows the truth form, and so upward flows manifest generosity”.

This is one of the most infamous phrases from the Tract, but in order to understand it we must first understand the metaphors within.

The Tract constantly uses the words “gravity” and “upwards” in a metaphorical sense. Ch.1 Vrs.1 says... “You are being controlled by lots of powerful forces you do not understand – like gravity.”

Within the Tract, Cris uses the word “gravity” as a metaphor to describe the natural force in your mind that causes you to question or doubt. It is the nature of a rational mind to question new information and to require some sort of evidence to justify believing it. Cris uses the metaphor of “gravity” to describe this natural tendency, known as skepticism or doubt...a powerful force that is holding us all down. To resist this force is to go “upwards”.

Ch. 1 Vrs. 2 of the Tract states that all Epsilonists must adhere to the concepts of “manifest generosity” and “upward gravity”.

Men of doubt are held down, while men of faith flow upwards. To go upwards is to go through the Program...scaling up the Epsilon Hierarchy. The word “upward” is used constantly in the Tract to describe Epsilonists that are higher up in the hierarchy than you.

Ch. 4 Vrs. 3 says, “Look upwards and see the stars.” This sentence, when taken literally, makes perfect grammatical and logical sense. However, we know that the Tract is spoken in metaphor. Cris is using the word “stars” here to refer to Vinewood celebrities...referencing the fact that many celebrities have joined the Program and are high up in the Epsilon hierarchy. There is an entire page of the Epsilon Website that is dedicated to vast amount of celebrities who have become Epsilonists.

Cris refers to the “truth” of Epsilon as the “true form”, or the “truth form”.

The phrase, “Upwards flows the truth form, and so upward flows manifest generosity” is the explanation behind the meaning of “Manifest Generosity”...the ultimate code of the Program.

The hierarchy of the Epsilon Program is largely a parody of the hierarchy system of the Church of Scientology. The higher up the hierarchy you climb, you more knowledge of the “truth” you are given....hence “upwards flows the truth form”.

In order to receive this knowledge, you must give everything you have to those that are above you in the hierarchy....hence “upward flows manifest generosity”.

After this explanation of manifest generosity in the Tract...it is simply referred to as “Upward Generosity”.

In Ch. 5 Vrs. 4 of the Tract...Upward Generosity is described as “the gift towards Cris, to Cris, or to any senior Epsilonist”.

This ultimate code is the foundation for living your life as an Epsilonist. The complete subservience to the Program and to the people who are above you. The giving of absolutely everything that you have to offer.

Not only does this involve giving large amounts of money to the people above you...but it also involves giving yourself to them sexually.

The Tract states that a woman must lie with “five upward men of Epsilon a day on the seventh month”.

Ch. 4 Vrs. 3 says that “Just as a man and a woman make water differently, they shall not do it in the marital bed or on one another unless they that are upwards within the hierarchy demands it, and even then not often. Hierarchy is all and upwards generosity is all.”

Here, Cris uses “water” as a metaphor for urination and states that it must never be done during a sexual encounter unless demanded by a higher ranking Epsilonist.

During the Epsilon Slide-Show on their website, a slide is shown of a male surrounded by two females. Cris Formage says... “What do you see here?.....potential?....generosity?.....Manifest Generosity!...and through generosity all things come to pass, including.....multiple women. Yes.....Yes!! Now, we are getting somewhere!”

Epsilonists are essentially sex slaves to the people above them. In addition to this, they are forced to offer their spouse sexually to the higher ranks as well.

The Tract states that “Your earth partner is to be cherished and therefore shared with the utmost generosity upwards like all things.”

It is clear that all the swinging that Cris Formage mandates is partially a means of increasing the population of Epsilonists.

The Tract explains this using the Plant metaphors (-mentioned in Part 2-)...of which Trees are female Epsilonists and the Thorn Bush and Cactus are female doubters.

The Tract states that the reader must cast out their partner if they become “like the cactus”....and says to “hold on to the peach tree and the apple tree and give all that you have...”

In the Epsilon Program, a man's semen is seen as a form of currency... which must be offered upwards in order to increase the Epsilon population.

This is explained within the Tract by using Fire as a metaphor for sex and the Dove as a metaphor for semen.

Ch. 1 Vrs. 2 of the Tract describes Manifest Generosity as when a “peach tree issues forth fire and a great dove becomes born.”

The Tract then says “That dove is money and the money should be spent by all to achieve manifest generosity”.

The dove is constantly referred to as being the “dove of love”.

In Ch. 2 Vrs. 4, the Tract tells the reader to “water the peach tree but do not quench the fire that brings the dove”.

This “Upward Generosity” is the ultimate moral code of the Epsilon Tract, a coded sexual manifesto that requires you to offer your body to the people above you.

Chapter 1 of the Tract ends by saying... “So let yourself know those upwards of you, and be known by them. There is Kifflom and there is Krant, and both be praised through manifest generosity.”
