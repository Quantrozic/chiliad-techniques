Step 1 is to make the UFO appear and I'm going to say that it HAS to be storming since there are lightning bolts, even though it still shows up during rain. The first two glyphs, Rain/Moon are located opposite one another because they are found this way at the Hippie Camp. Looking at Chiliad from the camp, Moon would be on your left and Rain to your right. These are also the conditions needed to make the UFO appear and one could even argue that the three dashes over the moon and the three rain drops are both indicating 3am.

Step 2 is the side with the UFO. Not only is there an actual UFO flying above the Hippie Camp but it also emits a beam just like the glyph is showing, Beam glyph is what I will call it. This side of the mural means something must be done at the Hippie Camp. There is too much proof: the UFO on the car looks identical to the one on the mural, there is a Beam Me Up message, a red dot with an arrow pointing to it saying "right here" (the mural is also covered in red) and finally the message in the sand with the Beam glyph, WOW signal and an arrow pointing at the camp (known as Sand Glyph). All of this plus the glyphs found there are proof that the camp is involved. The fact that the Beam glyph is drawn pointing there is why that glyph belongs to that X.

Step 3 is trickier, just as all things involving the Jetpack have been but like many have stated before me, this step involves Zancudo. Obviously there is the comparison to San Andreas and how the Jetpack was in a military base, but there is also a UFO there was well. The only two glyphs we are left with are the two which have no clear meaning, the Eye and the Half Eye. There are two ideas I had as to what they could mean, both using logic in game: 1. the Half Eye is the UFO's cloak and the Eye is the UFO without the cloak or 2. the Half Eye having only two dashes and the Eye having three has something to do with the bunker lights changing at 2am and 3am. The latter seems more obvious because the resoning for the lights is unknown unless they're just to show you there UFO above but why would they be so crooked on bunker if not to line up the center with the elevator and also, why even have the lights change every hour and then both shine in full the same time the Chiliad UFO appears and then disappear when the Chiliad UFO stops making a sound.
