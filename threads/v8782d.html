The LS Gas Company Building on Elysian Island is based on a real life building in Los Angeles.

Rockstar has a habit of taking real life company logos, landmarks and buildings and turning them into a penis. This real life building already looks like a penis so the work was done for them.

From a bird's eye view the “balls” form the astronomical symbol for opposition just like the Hippie Silos near Stab City. When the Sun is in direct opposition to the Earth relative to the Moon this creates a Solar Eclipse.

The “shaft” of the LS Gas Company Building faces directly East and the window that it leads up to faces directly West.

There are 6 different Eclipses in the Moon Cycle, 2 Sunrise Eclipses followed by 4 Sunset Eclipses. The window of the Gas Company building is in alignment with all of the Sunset Eclipses, making it possible to view any one of them through this window, though like many things related to the Chiliad mystery this window is centered on the 3rd Eye.
