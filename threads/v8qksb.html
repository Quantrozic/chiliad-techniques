Just north of the Vinewood Sign is the giant Vinewood Radio Tower which has a smaller radio tower beside it with a flat platform on top.

From the perspective of a player standing on top of this platform, the Galileo Observatory is in perfect alignment with the 3rd Eclipse in the Moon Cycle.

Here's a video showing this alignment.

This alignment was hinted at by Rockstar in a painting called "She's Loaded" that they added into the Casino Update in 2019.

The girl in this painting is a girl found on a Rockstar Wallpaper for the Highlife Update.

Besides the black, white and red color scheme of the girl in the painting, one major difference between her and the original image of her from the Highlife Wallpaper is that she's not wearing Sunglasses. Instead her pure white Eyes are visible which look similar to the Sunset Eclipses in the game. Behind her are two orbs in the sky which likely represent the Sun and the Moon.

A Letter Scrap is found on top of the “I” of the Vinewood Sign, possibly as another clue towards Seeing the “Eye” here.

In the painting the Vinewood Sign is slightly cracked, possibly representing some kind of trigger that might be behind triggering the Eye at this location.

There are several different movies in which the original “HOLLYWOODLAND” sign is destroyed in some epic action packed way and becomes the “HOLLYWOOD” sign instead of the reality of them taking down the word “LAND” by construction in 1949. One of these movies is the “Rocketeer”.

There's a picture of the original VINEWOODLAND sign on the wall inside of the barbershops in GTA V.

Perhaps Seeing the Eye in this location under the right conditions will trigger the “VINEWOOD” sign to destruct into a word or abbreviation related to the Chiliad mystery such as “--N-W-O-” an abbreviation for New World Order, or “--NE---D” as an homage to Ned Luke the actor who played Michael.
