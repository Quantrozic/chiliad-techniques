Ocarina of Time "Tri-force" all over again!

Documented under:Script-Decompile Keyword Scan

NOT a theory, just me rambling

Does anyone fondly remember Zelda 64: Ocarina of Time's great Tri-force goose chase? People searched high and low for a way to get it, playing the game thru 100's of times, creating online communities, hoaxes, experiments, etc. In the end, though, it was all for naught. Many raged of course but I feel it's what made the game so Legendary. Super hard secrets like this bring inspirational awe to the table where it may have never been. You have to admit, even if there's nothing to find in GTA5, this has made it (this subreddit as well) infinitely more fun and mysterious! This is the next Tri-force hunt.

I personally believe R* won't disappoint like Nintendo did, they're a gaming company for gamers BY gamers. They may have done this by inspiration from Zelda 64, but I think they'll deliver.

My only question is: if game code is easily decompiled by fans looking for easy access to these secrets, would this answer not have been found in week one? OR is it possible for good companies like R* to prevent all things from being found out through such methods?

Keep up the theories, guys! They make the game that was already epic into something more than words can explain. Let's hope I, we, aren't overestimating R*'s passion for details. I miss the days where secrets were hard to find!
