Trevor's multi-lingual dialouge "measuring knock and BANG" (possibly)

I apologize if this has already been posted, but I've found something odd about Trevor's "minute men" missions. In the first mission, Trevor meets Joseph and Jo. Jo seems like a redneck hellbent on ridding los Santos of illegals, and Joseph does aswell only Joseph speaks fluent russian. At the end of the first cutscene of the first mission Trevor says something in what seems to be a different language, "Merski knock en..." I hopped over to google translate and "merski" is Slovakian for measurment. "Knock en" is dutch for knock and. In the second mission, Trevor says "Brakan." at the end of that cut scene which is Icelandic for "BANG". Mind you, I'm only using google translate. Putting the whole phrase "merski knock en brakan" together doesn't yield anything helpful, but breaking it down part by part roughly translates it to "measuring knock and BANG."

These words spoken by Trevor appear in the subtitles as I have written them, but are not translated like Joseph's russian dialogue. I have tried diffent combinations of the phrase but "measuring knock and BANG." seems to be the English translation. If anyone has any idea what Trevor is actually saying or what the knock and BANG are, please share :)

EDIT: "merski" is SLOVENIAN for measurement, not Slovakian. Also, Trevor says these things after expressing that he cannot understand Joseph, who speaks fluent russian.
