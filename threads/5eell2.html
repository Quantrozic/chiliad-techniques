The Big ''Bang'' Theory (Bang=stickybomb)

yo, how to solve the chiliad mystery?!
There are 2 big Trigger you have to activate. not one like the most people think.
the 'moon-trigger' that affect the position of the moon
and the main trigger that open the observatory door.

Play with Franklin !

Step1:
The Moon-Trigger (Picture1)

this trigger is required to bring the moonshadow of the observatory-eye to the right spot.
i discovered a mysterious painting on the street between Franklin's house and the observatory.
it says: A3 <-> B3. So i tried something with the spacedocker and wtf, it really works...
A3 = Interact with chop 3 times B3 = interact with the crash barrier 3times

-go to chop's territory and franklin will talk to chop (interacting 1)
-drive with the spacedocker to the observatory
-before you're there, you come to the crash barrier that glow up in the night when you drive into that area (interacting 2)

do this 3 times and the moon is on another position in sky
(like a moon-time-travel some days back or to future)
i did this without crashing into other cars or things, maybe it dosn't work when you crash into something.
do this when it's dark, drive on the road and use the spacedocker !

Step2:
The Main Trigger (Picture 2)
You need sticky bombs. it's the only way to activate 5 things at the same time !!!!! (5 red X on the chiliad mural)

-after triggering the moon position you have to go on top of the hill to the observatory. put 1 sticky bomb
on every red X you can see on the mural (pucture 2).

(i discovered that 2 of them on the wrong spot. picture 2 is my version of the mural with the 2 wrong red X on the right spots. im not shure about the x 1,2 and 3 but i'm 100% sure that spot 4 and 5 is right, because of the graffitis
i discovered in the game. i didn't found more. i think only 2 of the red x symbols on chiliad mural are wrong)

-after you put the 5 stickybombs on the right spot in the right sequence you have to go to the tree with the x. (you can see the x-tree on picture 2)
detonate the sticky bombs from the x-tree when the shadow of the eye is on the spot of picture 3

The day and the time.
it only works on a special day at a special time.
to find out the day and the time, look at picture 3. there is a little piece of grass and a raised stone plate infront of the observatory.
when the moon-shadow of the observatory-eye hit this spot you know the day and the time. it only works on 1 day when the moon is on the north side of the map.
it needs a lot of time to investigate the moon and the shadow of the eye. i discovered that the orbit of the moon is changing over weeks. there is more than 1 time northside, 1 time southside and repeat...
it takes a couple of ingame-weeks till it's back at beginning of orbit !

look at the o in the mural-eye. it has a T behind it. when you see the lines between the stoneplates as a T, the
little piece of grass can be the hidden position of the o. the position of the eye-shadow we need to discovere for the day and the time we have to detonate the sticky bombs.
