I'm starting to see the usage of the mural as an overlay start to return as a solution more and more, and after posting a few comments explaining a few things, I feel it's probably worth putting this information into a single place for people to reference.

The Mural as an Overlay

If we assume, that the mural is 100%, without a shadow of a doubt, definitely and absolutely supposed to be used as an overlay, there are a few basic rules of thumb that should likely be followed;

1) You cannot tamper with the scale.

This means you cannot stretch the image either virtually or horizontally to better fit parts of the map.

If the mural is indeed supposed to be an overlay, it will have been drawn to fit something specifically (obviously), so by stretching the image at all, you're invalidating any and all overlay attempts you are working on, and essentially "making it" fit.

Here is an example:

[MANIPULATED] MY GOD! THE JETPACK IS HIDDEN INSIDE AN ICE-CREAM! - http://oi40.tinypic.com/2j1wps0.jpg

[TO SCALE] Oh wait... My bad guys! - http://oi43.tinypic.com/v7v6ro.jpg

2) Use the ripped texture file.

If it is an overlay, the only way to get a "correct" version of the image, is to use the ripped texture file. Using Snapmatic or Google images will not guarantee any form of accurate proportions, and can make a huge difference as to where each box lays when placed.

An Instinctive Look at the Mural (My Thoughts)

If we combine both 1 and 2, it makes little sense for the mural to be an overlay at all. Given the only way to gain an accurate proportioned mural is to extract it from the game, and that Rockstar more than likely does not want people doing this, an overlay makes little sense.

Looking at the puzzle logically from how I believe this is supposed to play out from instinct:

  • First off, We find the mural and messages from a compulsion to scale Mount Chiliad.
  • The message under the platform tells us to complete the game to access whatever it is referring to.
  • The mural instinctively represents Mount Chiliad and leads us to find the glyphs.
  • The glyphs give us 3am at night, rain/stormy weather and the mountain. We note that the eye is the same eye shown under the viewing platform, connecting the message "Come back when your story is complete" to the glyphs.
  • We do these things and a UFO appears. We recall the mural has three images, a UFO an egg and a jetpack. We now assume we can cross the UFO off of the list.

Things unaccounted for:

  1. A partially erased glyph.
  2. A supposed missing glyph.
  3. The egg. (the second thing to find?)
  4. The jetpack. (the third and final thing to find?)

Sandy Shores and Zancudo

Now these are sort of more debatable:

  • The glyphs on the mound/Wow! Signal/insane amount of alien graffiti in Sandy Shores lead us to the the UFO directly above.
  • The FIB presence at the dishes leads us to believe the satellites have importance, the single satellite in a unique direction points us to Zancudo/the hippy tanks, and we find the light beam above the bunker during early morning at Zancudo which leads us to the Zancudo UFO.

I personally don't at all think the mural is an overlay, but I'm open to the concept of the unexplained "lines" being used in some form.

Regardless of your stance, try to approach all efforts with very basic logic and reasoning. Each step will have a logical and natural progression that seems obvious once you know how. If what you are doing requires too much elaborate effort to connect the dots, it's probably not right.

The question is, based on what we've seen thus far in this submission, what would the game be telling you to do next?
