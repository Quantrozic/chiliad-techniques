[Theory] The Mural Paths.

Documented under:Route Theory ?

I posted an excerpt on this theory earlier today and after some research, have come to a conclusion on the Mural and in game choices.

First, the items belong to specific characters. That isn't to say other characters can't utilize them, they are just associated with these characters. Michael is the flyable UFO, Franklin is the egg and Trevor is the Jetpack. These are your in game rewards for proper execution. Whether all of these must be completed simultaneously or not is up to the individual, I make no allusions as to which.

The "x" on the path references an end. It is not "the" end, just an end to your search, based on choices you can make. The paths themselves fork every so often. These "forks" are your choices.

Now, lets introduce a human variable to the variables you can decide between. Do you act against the nature of the character during pursuit of your goal or do you act within the parameters of their defined personality? Do you ignore Gold Medal requirements or aspire to them? I'm not sure which. There are a few ways we can do this and the choice is yours.

Let's start with Michael, the most convoluted of all the paths and also the most difficult. On Michael's path, we have two Xs. The first X is for completely ignoring Dr. Friedlander or perhaps even killing him. If you ignore or skip any of his meetings, your path stalls and you're dead in the water. There is 7 meetings with Dr. Friedlander and 7 choices. (EDIT; After review of Dr. Friedlander's sessions, there is 7 meetings but 6 choices. Consider the choice in the Epsilon mission now part of the Equation). The correct application leads to the Chiliad UFO. Due to the number of ways we can go about this, I do not know what the correct choices are. It is worth noting that some responses seem to be copy cats of one another when making your choices with Friedlander.

The second X is the end game choice. It is mirrored by Trevor's own end game choice. Selection of either Michael or Trevor to die results in the end of their path (and perhaps the end of the mystery altogether). I'm spitballing here but I also think this results in the end of Franklin's path as evidenced by the split of his path.

Let's head over to Trevor. Trevor has 6 possible choices on his path before reaching the UFO. What are the choices? First, we have Ortega. You can choose whether or not to kill Ortega. I know that Ortega returns and you apparently have to kill him but this needs further testing. What if you fly away? Does Ortega's own choice to pursue you absolve you of his death? Who knows? What is Trevor's first X? Is it definitively Ortega? Is the second X, Al, at the end of the S&F missions? There appears to be global variables for Ortega and Al, after all.

After Ortega, you begin the Strangers and Freaks missions for Nigel. You have 5 choices here. Kill celebrities or don't? Which do you kill or which do you choose to release? The variables are endless.

Let's proceed to Franklin. He's the easiest as he has 3 choices. Beverly, Dreyfuss and Trevor/Michael. Obviously, from the overview of the path, you should avoid killing Trevor or Michael. What about Beverly and Dreyfuss though? What is the correct path for Franklin?
