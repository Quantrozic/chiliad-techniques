Has anyone experimented with the boundaries/edge of the map?

▲ 16 ptsu/DaxlApr 6, 201624 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Edge-Detection Glyph Tracing

I ask because I have done some experimenting and got some strange results. I do not read much on this thread about searching the water so I thought I would share.

I will use EAST for this observation. Let's call the natural edge of the map X (X=how far will the crosshairs go on map screen without any POI's on the map). If you fly a plane EAST for certain distance you can pass the natural edge of the map and can place a POI (Point of Interest) before your plane falls apart. Let's call that 2X. If you bail from plane at low altitude you can safely land in the water and can keep swimming EAST. The problem here is a Shark will appear almost immediately and you be shark food.

After several experiments I found ways to keep pushing that border further EASTWARD. By character switching when the shark showed-up, I managed to push that border to 4X before being eaten.

My last experiment had some odd results: I used to submarine and traveled EASTWARD until the submarine lost power and started to sink rapidly. I EXITED the sub in my wetsuit and swam down as far as I could before the water pressure killed me (apx. 500 feet depth). So at about 490 feet under the surface of the water I kept swimming EASTWARD. Apparently the sharks do not swim that deep and I was able to push the border to somewhere near 10X. I swan straight for 15-20 mins (realtime) past where the shark usually ate me before placing a POI getting wasted. Zoomed all the way out on map screen and Los Santos was way way off the map. The entire map screen zoomed out showed no signs of land...not even close.

I have no idea what this means… Just thought I would share my findings with the group.
