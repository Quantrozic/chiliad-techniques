This is something that has been bothering me and I wanted to get to the bottom of this.

At the bottom of the animal_controller script there are 306 lines of code with coordinates to the peyote locations. As people have been reporting of a possible chimp peyote and as I find this file really interesting I took the time to map out the coordinates of these lines on the map.

What I found was indeed all the peyotes, including the 7 different ones for the sasquatch. Also, I found copies of a lot of the peyotes but at slightly different coordinates. I don't know what they are for and why a peyote would need multiple coordinates.

Among all of the coordinates I found 3 coordinates that seem to be a bit too far from the peyote that is closest and one coordinate that is nowhere near a peyote.

Here is the link to the map with all the listed coordinates. I highlighted the 3 ones that are slightly off from the other peyotes and the single one that is completely off. http://i.imgur.com/BNoNi5R.png

rkRusty reported the peyote, that is completely off, at
1845.74, -1257.06298828125, 23.453
to be peyote number 7 ( see here http://imgur.com/a/777bD )
But no other map I've found online elsewhere report a peyote at this location.

What do you guys make of this? I'm very interested in hearing what you know or think.
