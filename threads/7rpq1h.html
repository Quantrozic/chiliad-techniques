Morse Code

▲ 21 ptsu/mr_chicotasoJan 20, 201812 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:DTMF and Morse Tone DecodingMorse Code AnalysisPitch-Contour Morse Extraction

Hey guys just wanted to put out an idea for someone with Morse code experience to try out (I'm not experienced in that).

A lot of the things that I see people talking about, recently, that really sticks out to me is when people talk about a code being broadcasted. The mural itself seems to show something being broadcasted, could just be a reference to a storm but what if the actual message IS being broadcasted at all times but R* hid it in the most ingenious way?

After the guru team was able to go into the world of audio and was able to see how sound was being broadcasted throughout the map, I decided to go to the spot of the very last image they put on their post(the one at the very edge of Old Man's Crack) and see if I could listen or see something.

At the moment I'm just standing on the edge observing everything in front trying to find something in the open but what I keep hearing that really gets to me is the sound of the wind. It's not a constant wind sfx, it sounds more like Morse code. It plays about 20-30 wind bursts (it kinda sounds like someone cranking up a lawnmower) and then stops after those bursts. Once it's done you hear a calm long pause of wind before it starts again. Is this just a sfx for the mountains winds or something else? If someone with Morse code experience would love to try this out I would really appreciate your feedback. If this has been talked about and debunked before I apologize.
