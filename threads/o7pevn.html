What are the curved lines? They could very well be flight paths. The question is where do they originate? Mars? If this is related to the UFO how does it fit in with the UFO being depicted to originate from one of the solar systems closests quasars?Are they related to probes from Earth?

Next thing I noticed is that the binary code beneath the hand holding up a globe is 0110100. That is interesting as the planetary data included onboard of Pioneer is given as binary values referring to the planets' orbital radii compared to the orbit of Mercury.
So they never have a 0 as first digit. If we take that as a hint to remove the first and last 0 (like brackets) we get 11010; which in turn is the value describing Earth's orbit.
