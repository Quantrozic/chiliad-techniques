Space-Docker - Close Encounters Movie Parallel

Documented under:Multi-Save Parallel A/B TestingRDR2 Parallel-Motif Survey

Firstly, I know that the space docker has been covered extensively, so please bare with me on this. I have a few key observations and hopefully something that will give food for thought:

  1. The reward of the space-docker seems disproportionate to the time and effort actually required to collect the spaceship parts. i.e. It's a rather small reward for something that takes a lot time and effort (Let's pretend we are back at 'Day one' and a map / guide isn't being used).
  2. Ergo - The space-docker must have another use to merit the time investment required to attain it.
  3. The space-docker is fitted with 'communication' devices i.e. the horn and lights. Indeed, quite a bit of work has gone into this model, this is what brings me to my main point...

I think the space-docker exists as part of a parody and cultural reference to the movie - Close Encounters of the Third Kind. I believe it can be used in some capacity, to communicate with the UFO on top of Mt.Chiliad. Why do I think this?

  1. What struck me as odd regards the various horn sounds, was the pitch changes. When played quickly in close sequence in the 'correct' order, sound rather like the melody used to communicate with the alien UFO in the classic sci-fi film 'Close Encounters of the third kind'. I could swear I heard the same variations in pitch between the different horn sounds.

http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rnYCboElgHs

  1. You will notice the box art / poster of close encounters has a road lit by a UFO - 'our path is lit'.

http://www.imdb.com/media/rm2586827008/tt0075860?ref=tt_ov_i

Also note the interesting 'classifications' of encounters on the poster.

So, I believe the horn and lights on the space-docker are key to communicating with the UFO atop Chiliad. Remember, many of the Rockstar game developers are actually quite a mature (old ;D) bunch and it wouldn't be uncommon to see something like this given we know something about their attention to detail in respect to the geeky sci-fi elements they cover.

I'll level with you guys - I don't have the space-docker to try this (shock horror, I know!), but I've watched enough videos and read enough theories, combined with some restless nights and independent thinking to come up with this theory. What do I think needs to happen? I think you need to drive to the top of Chiliad and docker beep the hell out of that UFO, maybe even toggle your lights if you can, as per the movie!

Again, thanks for reading this far, I hope this serves in the very least as an entertaining read :-) I may be wildly incorrect, but it's just yet another theory in this hunt!

Kifflom.
