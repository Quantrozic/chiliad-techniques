Director Mode Location Hints?

▲ 16 ptsu/UndeadOPOct 3, 201511 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Director Mode Scene & Spawn Testing

Hey guys. I was messing around on Director Mode today and I had this random thought that seemed to make a lot of sense.

I believe Rockstar is giving us hints on what locations are important in the history and which ones aren't. They are doing this through Director Mode. And it is my belief that Director Mode could actually be mystery-related in a way, especially since the Bigfoot mystery ends with you getting Bigfoot in Director Mode.

Anyways, let's skip to the point.

Here are the locations you can teleport to by default in Director Mode:

  • Los Santos International Aiport
  • Docks
  • Los Santos Downtown
  • Del Perro Pier
  • Vinewood Hills
  • Wind Farm
  • Sandy Shores
  • Stab City
  • Fort Zancudo
  • Canyon
  • Cable Car (location: summit of Mount Chiliad)
  • The Lighthouse
  • Vineyard
  • Richards Majestic
  • Golf Club
  • Altruist Camp
  • Power Station
  • Sawmill
  • Quarry Pit
  • Dam
  • Storm Drain
  • Observatory
  • Radio Telescope Array
  • Los Santos University
  • Cove
  • Mount Gordo

Okay, so some of you may be thinking why these locations matter. Well, most of them (if not all) are mystery-related, and "hot spots" for this hunting community. One location that really makes me believe that these locations were meant for us, is the Radio Telescope Array. Why is this a default place to teleport to? We already have "Sandy Shores" as a location, why did they feel the need to have this here? It's not like you can do much with these satellites anyways. Very interesting, imo...

Now, there's also some important areas missing that make me wonder why they weren't added in...

  • Dignity Village
  • Hippie Camp
  • Mount Josiah
  • N.O.O.S.E.
  • Paleto Bay
  • Lost Biker Gang HQ
  • Maze Bank
  • Construction Site
  • Playboy Mansion

These are just some of the locations that would've been way more helpful/fun to use in Director Mode, but weren't added in as default locations. Like, the average GTA 5 player will most likely want to make a clip at the Playboy Mansion instead of the Telescope Array, or at the Maze Bank instead of the Dam. You get what I'm saying?

Well, I hope you all got something out of this. Maybe we need to really explore these areas. Who knows? Well, Kifflom brothers-brothers!
