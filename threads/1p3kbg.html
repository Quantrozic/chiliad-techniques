I've been trying theories out all night and I've come up with one I think that is worth sharing. The lines on the mural have seemingly no meaning. Some people think tunnels, but I am skeptical of that. I think the are meant to tell us the objects we find are connected. The small boxes represent the minor glyphs that tell us how to start the mystery. This would mean that the eye, the egg, the ufo, and the "jetpack" are all a connected thing. I believe this is the case.

Now, here is what I think we need to do next. There is a line connecting the jetpack and the ufo, I think it is because jetpack is step two. Right now, if you observe the ufo on chilliad, then back up behind the platform and zoom in with the sniper scope

(Stream where I saw it, its long but he does it nearish the start :http://www.twitch.tv/cyberthunder/b/472230374 : have also done it myself)

the ufo moves closer to the platform. Standing on the edge of the cliff, it gets inches away from the platform containing the glyph of the eye (we all the know ufo emits a light out the bottom when you look close).

If you had access to the jetpack, and it functions like the parachute and you can use it and hold a weapon, you would be able to get into a position where the ufo would be directly above the platform and the eye. I think this is how we get to the egg.

Now, where to get the jetpack. Well the ufo shows us again, it is in a bunker in the military base. The ufo over the military base shines a light directly on a building at 3am. It is a bunker that leads into a room with an elevator that you cannot open (yet). A sign above the elevator says the elevator is at floor -1, implying there is a floor below ground.

Ive played around in the bunker a lot. Ive tried setting on fire (due to the several NO SMOKING/FIRE signs) I have even been inside of it with no alarm. (In two instances: I brought franklin there and when inside the bunker used the decrease wanted level cheat to get no stars however the alarm was still on, I then replayed the mission "Fair Game" a hunting mission with trevor, instead of killing the last dear I went to the military base and there was no alarm, however the bunker didnt respond. I quit the mission and it put me back at franklin, in the bunker, with no stars and no alarm. However this didn't help with anything either. The only other building in the military base that I have found you can go into is the control tower. Inside is a mini gun, a health pack, and several empty rooms. I have done many sweeps of the rooms trying to interact with things, and destroying things and nothing has proven successful. I even tried being in the control tower at the time the ufo appears, there was a commander NPC that was looking out the window with binoculars, but in a direction not of the ufo.

I still believe the answer iwill be achieved by getting in that bunker next. Anyway just wanted to tell you guys of my experiences, and maybe itll spark an idea or save you guys time if I am way off. Happy hunting!
