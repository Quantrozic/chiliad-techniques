Is previous gen dead in regards to the mystery?

▲ 37 ptsu/non_osmoticJan 12, 201631 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:DLC Manifest Pre-Drop EnumerationExpanded & Enhanced Build DiffingUpdate-Drop Timing Frequency Analysis

EDIT: Thanks, everyone, for your perspectives, and for keeping it relatively civil. Good stuff to think about. Happy hunting!

Given the fact that things like peyote and black cellphones* are only available in new gen, along with Rockstar's explicit concentration on these systems and PC (for online, anyway), is hunting a worthy effort on previous gen systems? If we assume there is something to be solved here, are these updates completing the mystery on new gen, or adding clues that can be applied to either set of systems? Or are these new things unconnected, isolated endeavors (monkey mosaics, murder mystery, etc...)? I realize these questions are largely a matter of opinion, but I thought it would be interesting to get a gauge of the sub's feelings on this.

Personally, I would be disappointed if previous gen was eschewed, but a lot of that has to do with my reluctance to shell out the cash for the new gen version. But, it appears this is Rockstar's focus, so I suppose I may not have a choice going forward if I want to continue the hunt.

*I've tried several different playthroughs at various stages of completeness and I've yet to be able to get the new number to function on the 360. If there is hard proof that it works on previous gen, I'd love to see it.
