Subway signs written in Latin

New guy here. Ive been lurking for a while and Ive finally decided to join in. It may be nothing related to the mystery, but its interesting regardless. So if you go down to the subway stations, you will find a sign. Its in all of them as far as I can tell and the only one thats legibl, at least on next gen (I'm not sure about last gen). Well, the sign is fairly typical for a subway station. The big letters say "Buses" and "Trains" in English and to the side it says "Translation" and lists several languages along with phone numbers. Thats not the weird part. The rest of the text is in Latin and very legible. When I put some of it into Google Translate, it says some strange things, yet the phrases and sentences actually make sense, i.e. not mumbo jumbo. I dont have my copy of the game right now so I cant dig any deeper into it. But I really hope one of yall can transcribe the entire Latin part of the sign and see if it leads us anywhere. I find it extremely weird that R* would put some of the sign in English and the rest in Latin. Like its not just random words thrown together in order to just be visual dressing to add a level of realism to the subway. No, somebody put alot of work into this. I mean, why not just make it English? That and the "Translate" next to it basically pointing us to translate the Latin. It could just be a little easter egg or it could be mystery related. I dont know. Your thoughts?
