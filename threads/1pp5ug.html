New user so I apologize about any formatting issues, but I've been following the hunt off and on for awhile. I recently achieved 100% and I noticed some things that I thought I would share. They may be nothing, a glitched save file or something, but they still struck me as odd

  1. Immediately after getting 100% I could view the FZ UFO with Michael and Franklin but I was unable to view the one on Chiliad or the one above the hippie camp until I had completed "The Last One."
  2. I am unable to view any of the UFO's with Trevor. I've tried everything I can think of, even reverting to a save from prior to achieving 100% and one prior to completing "The Last One," and progressing to completion from there.

This may be nothing but it struck me as odd that the FZ UFO (arguably the one with the least amount of clues) would be viewable before the Chiliad UfO, as in they may not be part of the same easter egg. It was also a little odd that I can't see any of the UFO's with Trevor, especially since there is option B in the last story mission.

Again these may just be glitches but I thought I'd share them on the off chance they might be significant.
