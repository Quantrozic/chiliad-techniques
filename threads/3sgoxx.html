Let's get organized!

▲ 24 ptsu/GoshentaNov 11, 201527 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Moon-Phase Condition Testing ?

Hey peeps. Long time lurker, second time posting. I was discussing methods in which to better orchestrate the "hunt" and came up with an idea my buddy said I should probably post. So here it is. If you guys like it, I'll see what's to be done about setting up a database. Anyway, here's the idea:

We need to be more organized in how we search. As most of you know, the golden 'squatch peyote gave us new information on how the jetpack could potentially be hidden within the game world, if it exists at all. My proposal is that we break up the in-game map into a grid of easily searchable small areas. Every time a person searches in a given area, they make a post about their finds and what in-game times they spent searching. This gets added to a list of people and times for that area. There is approximately 24 hours real-time in which something could happen ANYWHERE in the game. So I feel as if keeping track of this information would drastically cut down on the overall time required to solve this mystery and allow us to put it to bed one way or another. Of course, no one person is 100% reputable. Which is why it would be understandable if more than 1 person wanted to search an area at a given time. The point here is to keep track of where we search, what times we search, and how often we search.

Depending on the response I get to this idea, I might just set up a thread myself for people willing to undertake such a dauntingly large task. THIS post is just to bounce around the idea and refine it. If anyone thinks this would be a waste of time, fine. State your reasoning. Otherwise, lets figure out how to get this done!

As always, Kifflom and happy hunting!
