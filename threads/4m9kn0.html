Hi guys.

I just wanted to share a few more thoughts and see if anyone else could shed some light.

1- Has anyone considered that the Maze Bank logo is a pair of two interlocking epsilons? The wikipedia page for epsilon describes the symbols, but it also lists their significance in various disciplines. Interestingly, Epsilon has applicability in programing ("empty string"), and area of which I have no familiarity. Perhaps someone else can make some meaning of this. It also, however, sees widespread use in astronomy. Edit: Astronomy obviously has connections to UFOlogy, and epsilon seems to be used in an equation for axial tilt. What's interesting about axial tilt is that the sculpture on the obelisk at the Observatory, and at least one of the statues at the Kortz Center depict what appear to be orbital axes. Epsilon also has applicability in electrical circuitry, perhaps relating to the lightening bolt symbols on the mural? I dunno, just throwing this out there.

2- On a related note, there seems to be some connection between Maze Bank (and, transitively, Epsilon Foundation, if you give creedance to the above assertion) and the Kortz Center. On a literal level, there is an actual maze on the grounds of the Kortz Center, and, more abstractly, I can't help but see a similarity between the statue at the center of the Kortz Center round-a-bout and the Maze Bank logo. Also, one of the more southern statues is very akin to orbital statuary, images of which can be found on google. This is perhaps another connection back to Epsilon.

3- In an earlier thread, another user (forgive me, it was months ago and I can't remember your name) posted an analysis of the fountain at the Maze Bank office building and concluded (convincingly, IMHO) that, through the lens of Sacred Geometry, the fountain represented a cracked egg. I believe that many of the sculptures on the grounds of the Kortz Center likewise bear a resemblance to eggs. Perhaps investigating Maze, Epsilon, and Kortz may lead to furthered understanding of the "egg" aspect of the Chiliad Mural.

If anyone has anything to add, or can correct/redirect this area of inquiry, please chime in!

Edit: This is very unrelated to the above, but I thought I'd mention it. A ways back, some other users ran a search of anagrams for the Jack Sheep poster on the Mckenzie Field hangar. The most promising anagram seemed to be "Jetpack Confirmed." This has been connected to the Rocketeer film in various ways. Anyhow, underneath Jack Sheep on the poster was printed R-108. I'm curious if anyone else has looked into the significance of the number 108. After some cursory research, it certainly has connections to astronomy and yoga, both of which feature prominently in this game. See:

http://www.yogajournal.com/article/practice-section/the-number-108/
