With the latest "find" bringing attention to shadows, It made me think back to something from a long time ago.

When I first got the game (PC release), I already was aware of the hunt. Early in my first play through, I noticed what I would describe as a very complex shadow.

Location

Having some past experience in world building, from creating maps for an old mod, this shadow was obviously intended. Even in this day game engines cannot really replicate complex shadows like this naturally.

It takes an effort on the part of the designers to produce complex shadows using shadow maps or "invisible" models that help to provide these complex shadows.

Now I'm not saying this location is related to the mystery, but I believe it could be used as an example of shadows that cannot be "naturally" produced. (Without additional effort from the world builders.)

As to the complexity of the shadow, The one on the right of the "rigid" shadow in the middle, looks like a small girl with well defined pigtails. I'll leave the interpretation of the rest of the image to you.

My original interpretation of this was that it was just another dirty Easter egg courtesy of Rockstar. Open to interpretations.
