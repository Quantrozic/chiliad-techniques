Florida Joker Tattoo - segregate and rearrange

Documented under:Anagram and Constraint Word-Solving

So I haven't seen any significant mystery hints in the new games trailer other than a triangle sticker and a black flag with 'i' on it (monster truck mud place). The only one that caught my eye and I'm trying to make any sense of is the Florida Joker 'Impentinent' tattoo https://smallo.au/gtavi/trailer1/screenshots/trailer1\_frame1940.png

Impentinent is not a word, so is it just a misspelling of 1 word, i.e. impertinent, or something else?

Impenitent

Thesaurus.com. adjective. not feeling regret about one's sin or sins; obdurate.

Impertinent
1 : not pertinent : irrelevant 2 a : not restrained within due or proper bounds especially of propriety or good taste b : given to or characterized by insolent rudeness.

i.e., a reference to the meaning of two words to effectively describe this person, e.g., portmanteau.

Then there's another approach, i.e., Segregate and Rearrange, the phrase on the FIB UFO. Which was thoroughly analysed and determined to mean: Segregate the word Segregate from that phrase and then Rearrange it to solve, which is Easter Egg, i.e., the flying UFO is only an Easter Egg.

So if we apply this to 'Impentinent' in an angram solver we get: https://imgur.com/a/PRqrqnW

site - https://www.thewordfinder.com/anagram-solver/

Nothing is jumping out at me with the results of the anagram solver but thought I'd share in case someone else can notice something.
