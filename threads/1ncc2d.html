So, whilst checking different threads out on the sub yesterday, I stumbled upon this wiki article about the Wow! signal (6EQUJ5). The signal is referenced/hinted in the game here http://i.imgur.com/H2nj9wi.jpg and http://i.imgur.com/Ow6DHf0.png.

And is as far as I could tell (tl;dr version with the important parts in bold) about a Dr. Jerry R. Ehman who, in real life, discovered a strong radio signal whilst working on the SETI project which showed potential of origin outside of the solar system. Anyways, he found that the signal's location in the region of the sky was somewhere 'inside' the constellation that is Saggitarius. Also known as The Archer.

http://i.imgur.com/BkkX6KG.jpg

Keep that in mind. I then remembered the quote "shoot for the stars" on the UV/blacklighted map. If you investigate this point of the map, you will find that a sniper rifle is located on a view-point (with a telescope) - and when you look to the right you will see, yes, the Observatory.

Are these all coincidences? They could be, sure, I just think that too many things point towards stars and/or the observatory for it to be a coincidence. My theory is that something about "shoot for the stars" / the Observatory is an important clue to solving the thing.

Have anyone thoroughly investigated the area around/inside the Observatory, or even the skies above it?
