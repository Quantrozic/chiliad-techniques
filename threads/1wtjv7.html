Are the UFOs controlling or monitoring the characters?

▲ 63 ptsu/HootieWithBlowfishFeb 2, 201430 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Time-Lapse Environmental Monitoring

Just an idea, and a kind of ridiculous one at that. But we know that each of the UFOs that we've seen so far are controlled by the FIB, who obviously spend a lot of time during the game monitoring each of the characters. I've been thinking that each of the UFOs might be in use for monitoring each of the characters. Or perhaps even controlling them.

Now, you may be thinking that we've seen 4 UFOs, and you would of course be correct. However, one of those is the sunken UFO, which is clearly no longer in use. Could it be that it had previously been used to monitor Brad? It would make sense that once Brad is killed, the FIB would dispose of that UFO by sending it to the depths of the ocean, where (presumably) no one could find it.

There's a few different ways this theory could be used. It could just mean that the FIB is monitoring every single thing each of the characters does, which might point to an increased role for the FIB in solving the mystery. Maybe when the camera zooms out to shift characters, we're shifting from UFO to UFO. It could even mean that the player is actually representing an FIB agent, who is controlling the characters through the UFOs.

Those are all crackpot ideas to some extent, but I don't think it's an unreasonable premise. Perhaps more can be done to investigate the FIB and its many secrets.

Edit: Another interesting note: The sunken UFO, Sandy Shores UFO, and Chiliad UFO are all the same model, a slightly older looking version. While the Zancudo UFO is a much newer, much nicer model. This seems to fit with the idea that the sunken, Sandy Shores and Chiliad UFOs were used to monitor Brad, Trevor and Michael, while the newest UFO over Zancudo was used to monitor Franklin, since he just recently rose to prominence.

Edit 2: Just a thought, has anyone tried to place Franklin in the green light at Zancudo, while Michael is on top of Mt. Chiliad and Trevor is at the Sandy Shores UFO (maybe under at the spot in the Hippy Camp where it says "Beam me up". Also thinking that the other two might be the other way around)? Franklin is the green character after all.
