Rebel radio tower - Small plane guaranteed spawn

▲ 27 ptsu/StagnatedStagJul 15, 20198 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Peyote Spawn-Condition Logging ?

I've been testing things around the rebel radio tower and the morse code when i happened to notice a really reliable plane spawn that comes from it, I've tried searching for it online and in the reddit but can't find it mentioned anywhere so sorry if it has been mentioned before but its so reliable that i almost cant believe it hasn't been brought up, i'd heard of the altruist plane spawn before but i found that a lot less reliable than this and way more covered.

If you're near rebel radio tower (not any of the close by locations like the building site or ark shop only rebel radio) and switch characters, when you switch back to rebel radio tower there is nearly always a small plane flying from east to west of the map going directly over the rebel radio building with the skull and out to the edge of the map, you can keep switching and get the plane to spawn any number of times with an almost 100% success rate, has anyone else heard of this before?
