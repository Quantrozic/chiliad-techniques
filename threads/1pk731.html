Found a possible phone number hidden in the game? Think before you post

▲ 20 ptsu/lendotOct 30, 201321 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Hidden Map Art & Environmental Pattern Recognition ?In-Game Phone Dialing Sweeps ?

I just posted this in another thread, but given that I've seen this happen multiple times on multiple forums with multiple numbers, I think it warrants its own post. I put this warning out there not just as a common courtesy to North American phone-having people, but also as information for gamers not from North America and as a way for people to do a quick sanity check for whether something they've come across could viably be a phone number in the GTA V universe...

I would just like to interject a small note about posting phone numbers we think we may have found inside the game. The phone number listed in your post is a real phone number in Ohio. For those of you not old enough to remember this wonderful little song called 867-5309 (and don't know anyone in any area code who to this day is cursed if they have that number), there is a very good reason why it's extremely rare for people to use phone numbers in games/TV/other media that do or could map to a real person's number, especially if that piece of media is consumed by millions.

The reason why nearly all phone numbers in the game use the 555 exchange is because that is de facto reserved for fictitious use in North America. The 999 area code is currently not used by any carrier, so that also turns up in the game and enables other options for numbers. I seriously doubt that Rockstar would put a number in the game that doesn't follow these conventions. EDIT If you look at the other numbers in this game, not only do they use 555 as an exchange, but they use 555-01xx specifically, which is the "safest" of all 555-based fictitious numbers to use. This shows that at least one person at Rockstar was careful and did their homework on this topic.

So as not to run the risk of being a nuisance to anyone not in any way affiliated with the game, if you think you've found a phone number and it doesn't contain a 999 area code or a 555 exchange, please please please think twice before posting it. I say this for several reasons. 1) I, and I'm sure many others, have gone to pull up the in-game phone and absent-mindedly reach for our real phone instead. 2) People are already extending their search for clues beyond the game and out into the real world, sometimes based on very bizarre theories. It's just a matter of time before someone goes, "that phone number doesn't work in the game, but it looks like a real phone number. Maybe we should try calling it with a real phone? I bet it's a voice recording that will tell us where to get a jetpack!" 3) This is perhaps the most important one. Posting these numbers on forums can and will screw with google results for people who actually own those numbers. This is already starting to happen with the aforementioned number. EDIT The number I'm referring to actually belongs to a church, of all things. Imagine the field day the press would have if a GTA game were linked to a church's phone number.

Ok, I'll get off my soapbox now. Happy hunting!
