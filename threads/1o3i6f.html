Hey all. I was following up on the Penris building discovery (one star is illuminated when looked at from the observatory), when I decided to search the sky at night well above the building. I noticed that there are a few clusters of stars where some are coloured pink, some are coloured red, and one in particular is coloured blue. I tried shooting it to no avail, but when I looked down from its exact position, my view landed at around G5/H5 (one of two trails that end at the ocean).

What do you all think of this? I would take a picture of the star formation, but my phone camera is kind of crappy (and it's a little too late to bother, in honesty). You can find it yourself if you stand at the back of the observatory and look up in that general direction.

EDIT: It has to be through the sniper scope, the viewing stations don't go high enough.
