Quantum Immortality and Ending the mystery.

Documented under:Choice-Tree Ending Permutation Mapping ?Moral Choice & Playstyle Branching Analysis ?

First, acquaint yourself with the concept of Quantum Immortality:

http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_suicide_and_immortality

Now, go with me. Assuming Rockstar is also acquainted with this concept, then we are dealing with a simple situation of the final mission unfolding in only our personal universe. Let me use the analogy of "Players" and "NPCs" to relate this.

The "Players" are the ones in control of the situation. The NPCs are the people who are still "Plugged into the matrix of control".

Michael, Trevor, and Franklin have all been trying to unplug the whole time. Now, assuming that all possible universes exist, what happens is the final mission is as follows:

In an infinitely large number of universes, Franklin, Michael, and Trevor, the "Unholy Trinity" as Lamar says, are wiped out. As a video game, these universes are irrelevant, so when it happens we try again.

In an also infinite, but smaller number of universes, we successfully destroy the other "Players" and are left in a world with only the NPCs, and Us. This is when our story is over, and our mystery just beginning.

I don't think the game play choices have much to do with the mystery, I think the mystery is about realizing nothing in the past matters at all, as long as we destroy the other "Evil" players (Send them to their own parallel existence) and clear our Karma so the aliens can help us.

Keep in mind "Karma" translates to "Action", so what we are really looking to do is clear our actions of all negative things. Lots of yoga, lots of green juice, just like in real life. (For those of you who do not eat healthy, and do not regularly meditate, I highly recommend you pick it up. GTA 5 convinced me to get my life healthy and happy so I could solve this mystery, and I suggest you do as well, because it makes you smarter and more aware of the details.)

Once we are "Good people", the mystery should just fall open for us without any effort at all. If rockstar was going to let us find this thing through traditional exploration, we would have found it by now. The mystery is going to be an "Oh Duh" moment, and I think this is the place to start. At The End.
