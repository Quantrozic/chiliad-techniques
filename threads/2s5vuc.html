So, the results of 9 hours non-stop testing:

Mount Chiliad

  • The ufo (ofcourse) did not show up on other weather types than rain/thunder.
  • The ufo makes a noise untill exactly 3:37 AM. Which could indicate we need to do something in the 37 minutes after the ufo first appears.
  • after 4 AM, the UFO will dissapear and won't come back if you travel far enough away from it. This distance is futher than the ps3 version as as far as I know. It will still come back when you walk around the top area of mt. Chiliad. It will stay there as long as you are there as well.
  • Searched the area for entrances to the mountain when the UFO is active, we did not found anything yet.
  • Parachuted, and used a helicopter to fly towards the road which was visible on the map, but not ingame (http://imgur.com/cYLVWRw), nothing here happens within the timeframe 3AM-4AM.
  • Used a helicopter to fly to the FZ UFO in the timeframe 3AM-4AM, nothing happens there as well.
  • Used a motorcycle to drive down mount chiliad to the beach (east) within the timeframe 3AM-4AM, nothing there.
  • Used a helicopter to fly to mt. gordo in the timeframe 3AM-4AM and started doing yoga with michael there. Nothing happens, but its worthy to note that time stops when you do yoga.
  • Tried falling down beneth the ufo to let it beam you up. Trevor did not like that. (he died)
  • Tried using the cable car when the ufo was active. You will arrive at 4:30 am and nothing is downstairs.

Fort Zancudo UFO

  • We found out that the only places that gives you an electric shock on the UFO are the 'danger' marked outlets on the 'flaps' of the ufo.
  • Tried shooting it down, did not work of course.
  • Used 5 sticky bombs, did not work.
  • The fort zancudo lights won't appear if you fall down the ufo at 1-3AM
  • The bunker doesnt open while the lights are active, neither when shooting, using the jerrycan, waiting a full day for it to reactive or with a wanted level of 5 stars or without a wanted level.
  • The computer screens inside the control tower display a sort of readable email containing spam.
  • The elevators inside the control tower dont do anything when the doors are closed.
  • You can't lose your wanted level while in the control tower.
  • It appears that the ufo has some sort of electrical shield since shooting it causes electrical sparks. Maybe it is an idea to check if we can turn of the shields.

Mount Gordo

  • Tried doing yogo on sunrise and sunset, with rain and sun. Nothing happened.
  • We noticed that the Sun lines perfectly with the top of mount chiliad at sunset.

Observatory

  • Tried to put michael, franklin and trevor on the metal plates and let them stay there to active something. Did not work.
  • Tried a thunderstorm to see if a lightning strike would hit the roof of the planetarium.
  • Tried electrocuting the space docker at the electricity. So far I know this is the only place in the game aside from the ufos where you can get electrified. (nothing happend regarding the space docker.)
  • Tried opening the doors variable ways (shooting, kicking, punching, explosives, etc.), did not work.
  • Tried putting michael, trevor, franklin on the 3 different roofs. Other than a strange sound, did not work.
  • Tried other entrances during a thunderstorm, did not work.
  • Tried all entrances while having a 5 star wanted level (shoot for the stars) did not work.

Hippy Camp UFO

  • Tried inserting trevor with a blimp controlled by Michael -> Could still work but trevor respawned/dissapeared while flying to high.
  • Tried shooting the UFO down, causes a strange blue light (possibly sunrise) but did not work.
  • Tried shooting inside the UFO, did not work, possibly a collision map on the outside of the UFO.
  • Tried landing on top and entereing the UFO from top, did not work.

Sunken UFO

  • Tried finding the text reported earlier, did not work, will try that later.
  • Tried to get inside it, through glitching, did not work.
  • Enjoyed the marvelous sight. (at least something that did work.)

LOST Hatch

  • Found out that there is a minimum height for an animal to swim in the sea. The hatch is just below the minimum so not directly reachable. Conclusion: The hatch isn't meant to be reached.
  • The light of the Hatch turns off when the Morse code has played.

Scientists at the sattelites

  • Still no FIB, only scientists and white cars. This has 100% been changed from the ps3 version. Question = why?
  • Scientists run away when someone else shoots.
  • The 'lonely' scientist that walks towards the other 2 respawns.

Altruïst camp

  • Shooting a guy in the camp from the antennas will let him respawn for infinite times.
  • Altruist people keep respawning while in the camp itself.
  • The sun symbol on the water tower points towards the location of the sun while sunrise is going.

hippy camp

  • Nothing happens when Michael is at the hippy camp at both glyphs and Franklin is at the mount chiliad UFO.
  • NEW info The layout of the hippy camp represents an alien head. as proven by the board at the entrance of the hippy camp.
