Keep the focus on the Mountain

▲ 16 ptsu/blacksunalchemyNov 18, 201330 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Tide & Water-Level Observation

I feel like a lot of people on this sub are making more out of the Chiliad mural than need be. Overlaying it over the world map, and looking for clues that have nothing to do with the mural.

There are still symbols on this map that I don't see anyone addressing, primarily being the angled lines across the bottom right and left of the mural.

These have to symbolize something, why else would they be there?

First, I think the mountain shape represented looks a lot like the view of Mt. Chiliad from the watch tower at the Altruists camp.

Second, I think the lines on the bottom left and right of the map might symbolize the ridges or valleys of the mountain itself.

Which might indicate where a tunnel or entrance into the mountain could possibly be located.

So for instance an entrance to left side of the mountain might be on ridge 7 of 10.

Any thoughts?
