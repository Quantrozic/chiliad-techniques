Another interesting thing about the promotional poster is the airplane contrails or streaks that would intersect to form an 'X,' although it's not clearly visible on the poster itself. This is interesting because it's an event that frequently occurs in the game: eventually, two airplanes will cross paths and create an 'X' pattern of contrails, with a similar trajectory in all cases. You've probably seen it at different points on the map. I don't think it's a coincidence; they want you to pay attention to the sky.
