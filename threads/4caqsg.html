Altruist camp paintings hint at a secret island?

▲ 52 ptsu/DRUMIINATORMar 28, 201682 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Tide & Water-Level Observation

I just wanted to share a thought. I might be on to something. Hopefully I can explain it.

For starters, a lot of people believe the sun is involved in some way, be it at the camp or with shadows or even a certain time of day.

There are plenty of clues that tell us light and color are involved:

When you put the sun drawings in order by color it resembles a timeline from sunrise until noon.

When you put the writings together by color you get this:

See the eye! Embrace the light. He is risen! His light shines on us all. Praise him. We are his children! Son of God! Our path is lit!

The Altruist logo on their website has a sun rising and a rainbow (light/color spectrum). The morse code messages are also colored. A member of the camp tells us we will never find the spectrometer.

Finally, the water tower in the camp has a giant eye facing East.

But before I go any further I want to point out the ram skull. Being the first zodiac symbol (Aries) it embraces the warmth of the sun as the March Equinox approaches a week before Easter which just so happens to be when the son of God rose from the dead. Easter egg?

So there is a drawing of three flowers. Even after studying these images off and on for years, it never occurred to me that it looked like an island.

When the sun is rising (and also setting) it seems to create a path of light out into the ocean. When you fly in that direction it appears in front of you. I know it could be written off as simple lighting but what if there is something out there?

I have been trying this for the last couple days and I discovered things as I went along that might be factors.

What time should I start? What day? What vehicle do I use? Do I travel by air or by sea? I always run out of time long before hitting the barrier. If the run rises too high the path vanishes.

Is it possible to find a gap in the barrier, only open at a certain time that leads to a secret island?

Would love to hear thoughts on this. Kifflom.
