[discovery, speculation] some indicators that the observatory is the physical site that the mural references

▲ 5 pts[deleted]Oct 19, 201313 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:UV Light Analysis

Walking around in Vinewood today, a dog began barking at something I had completely forgotten, the UFO tree in the middle of that strange park with the dirt mound, the circles cut out of the turf and the warnings about horn honking. Just north of that park is another grassy park, and it also contains trees with these strange camouflaged ufo shapes inside of them. I saw at least 3 or 4. I seriously doubt that this was just some oversight by Rockstar, given their extreme attention to detail.

So I made my way up to the observatory to get a better look. I noticed some things about the compound that caught my attention. The three primary chambers, two on the outside sharing an adornment in the shape of the line at the bottom of the mural. The rather tomb-like feel to the place. The Mayan-esque border on the facade of the building. The exceptionally scrupulous attention to detail paid to the whole area, high even by R's standards. What appears to be the eye of Horus casting shadows throughout the day in a way very much consistent with the mural. The focusing of the light even begins to explain the heat lines at the bottom of the mural...and perhaps even the cracking of an egg (natural incubator anyone). There is also the prominent elevator, suggesting a long descent into the hill...at an observatory? Then there is the massive electrical array to the north east of the compound, which fulfills the electric bolt symbols in the mural. All these are straightforward hints that the observatory is the site referenced by the mural.

So if you walk over to the electrical array northeast of the observatory, look up and you will see yet another UFO in the tree directly above it. In this case(I was very tired) I was even able to see the matte black texture a bit through the canopy with my cell phone zoomed. Naturally I tried to destroy the electrical array and the ufo, but they were invincible/non clipping as usual. I noticed some music playing as the cops came for me. I honestly don't know if that music was unique to this situation or more common, maybe someone with a better memory can say.

So yeah, the observatory definitely deserves more attention, and we need to find a way to access those "space penetrating telescopes."
