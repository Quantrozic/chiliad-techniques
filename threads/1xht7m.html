I recently just started to replay the game, and have begun taking notes of every strange encounter, light, statement, literally anything out of the ordinary that sticks out I'm keeping record of. Today I came across the "Did somebody say yoga" mission. This mission sticks out for numerous reasons the biggest being that this is Michael's abduction. I searched around on this subreddit for anything about the yoga poses that are being used but found nothing.

The poses being used by Fabien, that he teaches Michael, are part of the Sun Salutation Sequence. This pose requires you to start in the "Chaturanga" pose which stands for mountain pose. As you go through the poses they revolve around the sun, with you finally finishing the whole sequence back on the mountain.

Now the second part is when you originally play this mission is that it will fast forward time so that you start at 12:00. And by the time you are all done doing the yoga it is 16:00.

Finally this all could mean absolutely nothing. But I'm just putting it up here to kick around some ideas with, because sure it could be a coincidence, or maybe not. But Michael can do yoga on a mountain, maybe the start point for his trigger?
