Alright guys. I was doing some research last night regarding how the conspiracies/occurrences in the game work together with IRL events. I've gathered a small list. Note that some of these may be from works of fiction that happened out of the game, but I'm still counting them because they may help us.

*First off, here is a mysterious mountain known for its UFO sightings. It also has a conspiracy of a lady being murdered by her husband on this same mountain, but the husband was never charged guilty...Jock Cranley much? Brown Mountain

*Here is talk of a sunken UFO. Note the part where the discoverers state that there was another smaller mysterious object next to it. This could, if Rockstar based the sunken UFO off of this one, where the alien egg could possibly be? Sunken UFO

*Egg Silos? In the original imagining of the movie "Alien," in which the aliens in our game are based upon, the alien hunters enter a giant pyramid (known by the writers as a silo) and inside they find a bunch of alien-made stuff. After rummaging through the stuff, they stumble upon the alien eggs. We also have alien silos in our game ("they're here!") so maybe Rockstar stuck the egg inside of these as a nod to Alien? Or the pyramid in the movie could be Chiliad, so we have to find the egg inside of the mountain...?

*Has anyone ever thought of linking the Altruists to the cannibal cult found in the "Wrong Turn" movies? I'm not a fan of these movies, but I did a Google search to see if the cannibals in those movies ever had a camp of their own in which they live, but my results came back negative. I feel like Rockstar did have some type of inspiration when designing the Altruist camp and cave, but I can't find it anywhere.

*Welp. They're definitely real.

*Desert alien structures. While this is interesting and all, what caught my attention was the part about the three spaceships the aliens were suspected to be using to invade our planet. In our game, we found three solid UFOs.

*L.A. UFOs. A lot people, including myself, already knew of this. But what caught my attention was the fact that people shot at it and it had no effect...much like our game's UFOs...

*Well then. This little guy looks a lot like our mural friend and hippy camp friend.

Alright. I'll come back with more, but this is all I found for now. It's really interesting if you ask me. And I really want to see my theory of what's inside Chiliad happen now! :p But anyways, do you guys know of any more irl events that go well with things that happened in-game?
