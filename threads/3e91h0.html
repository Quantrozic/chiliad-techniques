Fort Zancudo and Moon observations

▲ 18 ptsu/Senpai_KushyJul 22, 20155 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Hidden Map Art & Environmental Pattern Recognition ?Moon-Phase Condition Testing ?

After a cursory search through the Subreddit and spending many months lurking it, I decided to add something to the mystery in the hopes that it furthers progress - if not, at least it's something interesting!

http://imgur.com/a/lryKd

The pictures in the album that I have linked show my findings exploring and messing about with time and weather.

The first four screenshots are of the hangers that are either side of the Bunker with the elevator - if you're facing towards Los Santos, the first three screenshots are of the left hanger and the fourth is of the right hanger. The reason I'm showing these screenshots is because, despite searching the entire air base, these two hangers are the only ones that have broken electrical boxes. Both hangers have three, however the right hanger only has two that show sparks - the fourth screenshot shows the broken electrical box facing the elevator bunker without sparks (the other side of the right hanger has the same two broken electrical boxes but with sparks).

What does this mean? I have no idea, but I've always thought it's odd that the two hangers either side of the bunker are the only ones with these boxes.

As for the last four screenshots, I was messing around at the top of Mt. Chiliad with the ScriptHook mod, changing the weather and time as I saw fit and I stumbled across something odd. I know there have been posts before regarding moon phases and I remember seeing a video of a moon making an odd 'U' shape in the sky, however I believe what I show here might be of further interest.

The first of the moon screenshots shows the moon at 3AM with normal weather - nothing odd. The second I changed the weather to "Snowlight", the moon flips to the other side (as can be seen in the second moon screenshot) which I thought was extremely odd - it's the only type of weather that does this to the moon.

The third and fourth moon screenshots show the moon changing phase between 23:00 and 00:00. This too I thought was odd, especially since there was much discussion regarding moon phases and placement. Additionally, while the "Snowlight" weather effect was on and I was advancing time by the hour, the moon never moved - it appeared around 20:00/21:00 and stayed in that exact position until dawn and then disappeared - the sun however, had its normal movement across the sky.

Now, while I was taking screenshots, I observed something very strange indeed - Despite having the time paused via ScriptHook, the moon started moving from the position you see in the last two screenshots to its original place, as can be seen in the first two moon screenshots. This happened because the "Snowlight" weather effect was changing to normal weather. The moon moved quite quickly to its original place and then slowed until it was barely crawling, until it eventually stopped. It crossed above me (above Mt. Chiliad!) until it reached the normal position. I thought this was exceptionally odd.

So there you have it, it's probably nothing, but all certainly interesting and I personally believe worthy of note.

Any thoughts?

Thanks for your time. Kifflom!
