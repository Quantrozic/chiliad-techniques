This wild conjencture happened all of a sudden when I was looking the hd pics uploaded by the_monotonist. One image pertaining to Paleto Bay, Mount Chiliad North-Northwest side appealed my attention. I noticed some well known pattern on the streets, and to my excitement, something related to the egg was in the middle: the lowest line on the mural map. In the beginning, I was heavily inclined to Line A, but now I'm leaning to Line B. Anyway, both lines have in the middle what I suspect the egg stands for on the mural. You've guessed: Cluckin Bell! Some other redditors had pointed out the conspicuous egg imagery in that town. It took me a whole night to explore where both ends of the lower line might be. And then I found another pattern. If you see, both ends are symmetrical and therefore they could point to entries aligned as the mural shows. More or less, Entry A and Entry B are located in the same fashion. They are barred! So, some equipment must be necessary to gain access. I remember the mission to Humane Labs. It is the only instance when a GTAW, I guess, was available. The only thing that could let us gain access and confirm or debunk my wild, I admit, hypothesis, unless the mystey is solved in other way. I suspect that the remaining lines stand for tunnels inside Mount Chiliad. I have no idea what the saucer, the jetpack man, and the crossess stand for. Regarding Lines C and D, I suspect they are the tunnels where the road and the railway go through the mountain. I am not sure about, but those lines on the bottom half of the map, but they might be the sun rays. On the right side, they come from the east; on the left side, they go to the west. No idea about those 'rays' or 'clouds' on the upper half of the map could mean. Thank you for your interest and patience. Image: http://imgur.com/a/aTutC
