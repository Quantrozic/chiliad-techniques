Interesting news today in the International Business Times when talking about the new Rockstar Editor for PC version.

"Apart from the new feature, another interesting point that will be coming to “GTA V” is the supposed addition of story content to the title. Though still unconfirmed or unannounced official by the developer, Gamercenteronline has tapped a source close to the project who has revealed that they are working on something more than just Online Heists and PC version of the game.

“We’ve spent a good amount of time working on the online Heists and the remaster, however we haven’t forgotten about Michael, Franklin and Trevor’s story. It’s going to be monumental!” said Gamercenteronline’s source.

Currently, no other detail other than the nature of the new addition has been given. The source did toss another bone to fans, stating that the developer will be making an announcement “very soon.” Whether or not this comes out alongside the “GTA V” PC version and the newly announced Rockstar Editor remains to be seen. Of course, since Rockstar Games has not announced anything, best to take this with a grain of salt. Better to wait for any other announcements made in the coming weeks for confirmation."
