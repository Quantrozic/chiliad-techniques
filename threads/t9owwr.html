Casper: Spirit Dimensions & GTAV?

▲ 39 ptsu/XariesteMar 8, 202249 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Fractal Dimension Coastline Analysis

This is a bit more of a real life thread into the inspiration for this mystery, but bear with me a bit. I’m not sure if anyone here is familiar with the hidden room in Casper:

Spirit Dimensions for the PS2. If not, here it is (#4 in this list):

https://youtu.be/xYfCG_uT2LI.

I couldn’t help but be stricken by the cracked egg being a key feature of the secret area. I guess I got curious so I looked into it a bit. That game was published/licensed by no other than… Take-Two Licensing. Wait though, I thought I had seen a different publisher? Oh that’s right, it’s TDK Mediactive:

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Take-Two_Licensing.

This brings us to a man by the name of Vincent Bitetti. Vincent has a storied career in gaming, including being the founder and CEO of TDK Mediactive, which would go on to become acquired by Take-Two Entertainment. Vincent himself says he stayed on for another year as a consultant before moving on. However, you’ll note on his LinkedIn that he also did some mysterious and confidential “consulting” during the time period that GTAV would’ve been in development.

We also have another curious individual by the name of Matt Saia, who is oddly connected to all of this. He worked on games with Lucky Chicken, but also worked on the “Robotron” series, which is made by Crave Entertainment, which, if you look back at Vincent’s LinkedIn, he was the President of in 2004. In addition, Matt also worked on the movie “The Lawnmower Man II.” The predecessor to that movie, “The Lawnmower Man”, had its effects done by a gentleman named Diego Angel, whose company, Angel Studios, would eventually become Rockstar San Diego… in 2003 just after the acquisition of TDK. (He also worked on RE2, which itself was known for hidden secrets).

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rockstar_San_Diego.

My point is, our man Vincent worked more on GTA than he has ever let on. Here he is in an article referencing a high profile case related to Take-Two/Rockstar.

https://nypost.com/2019/07/13/how-grand-theft-auto-kingpin-robert-alexander-gambled-his-fortune-away/.

Anyway I just found out about this mystery today and thought this odd link could help uncover something, even if someone tries to reach out to Vincent directly. Anyway, cheers.

EDIT: Soooooo the company in Japan that did “The Lawnmower Man” video game is SCi (now owned by SquareEnix). They did a VERY VERY interesting game:

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_Slip
