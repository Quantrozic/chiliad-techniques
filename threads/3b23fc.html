Hey everyone,

I've been a part of this group for a long time now. Got my 100% on a ps3 AND ps4.. streamed a couple of times.. stopped it because there is nothing new to actually stream about. That is why I actually came up with the idea for this post: a roadmap of my current theories and the way to go from there.

Currently I have 2 theories, both of them stay practicly close to the original thing/idea of the mural. In the time that I have been here, there are lots and lots of stuff added to 'possibly' be related to the mystery, and stuff that is possibly related to possible stuff probably related to the mystery, but my personal beliefs are more close to the original thing. To be honest, theory 2 is my current most-likely-to-be-true-theory.

theory 1: Treasure Hunt
Basicly this theory is based upon a treasure hunt idea. We see the mural, we know how to activate the ufo on mt. chiliad, and the flying ufo and/or jetpack is the reward. Now why is this theory special? Because the ufo on mt chiliad is the first part of the mystery. And the mural is the way to get the ufo on top. the boxes below are the rewards. We see the ufo -> the next step is the FIB symbols -> we investicate the FIB more -> we found out the T01 and T02 doors -> we found out the FIB at the sattelites -> 1 sattelite points to the FZ ufo -> we follow the beam down -> we get to the elevator -> now what? (this is ofcourse 1 possible way to go, but theory 1 is purely based on the fact that clues point to each other and basicly point to a reward later on)

Theory 2: the ufo's are the reward, and flyable.
This theory has one base foundation, and that is the rain glyph. Why would the rain glyph be there, if the mural itself shows that a thunderstorm is needed? It is double, so this theory is based on 1 simple fact: the line in the middle from the mt chiliad ufo to the cracked egg is a spacer.
(picture for reference: http://i1171.photobucket.com/albums/r548/traehnogard/0ohz06P_zps09e27937.png~original )

The first 2 glyphs on the left are the ufo glyph and the rain glyph. Together with a faded ufo symbol. The hippy camp ufo (the symbol looks like the ufo on the car) has 2 conditions: 1. you need to get close to it to see it (worn glyph) 2. the rain glyph. Since it is directly above the other rain glyph, these 2 are linked.

The FZ ufo is on the right, symbolized by the jetpack symbol. Why? We see the 'mountain glyph', which is the symbol for the light beam created by the ufo until 3 am (other glyph) at fz. It also has a slightly worn glyph, indicating its cloacking device.

Now, basicly in this theory, the mural has been explained, but not the mystery.

Thank you for the info, but what do we do next?

The point for me to explain my theories, and to write this post, is to get our focus back to the points that, in my oppinion, really matter. If you agree with eather theory 1 or theory 2, our focus should be on the UFO's, (the bunker in) FZ, and finding out how to unlock them.

We haven't got real proof of stuff, but the proof we HAVE is that these ufo models ARE in the game. And the line of having the ufos and being able to fly them, is a lot smaller, then looking for a lot of other stuff that might be possibly related to something probably related to the mystery.

We need to get our focus back on the ufo's, and test out lots and lots of (probably earlier tested, or new) theories related to Those. or stuff that the ufo's point towards. The answer should be a lot closer then where we look.

Thanks for reading.
