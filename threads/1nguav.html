Documented under:Histogram Equalization Pass on Dark Captures

Before I begin, as a disclaimer, this post will have some mild spoilers, and I am not yet 100% done the game.

So Last night I was completing the Return to North Yankton mission, after the mission was mostly over, and Trevor has to fly back to the airfield to land, I saw was what I assumed to be a fighter plane, something I have not yet encountered in the game. I take off after it, but it is going fast, as I get closer I notice it is a round oblong shape with a ring of flashing lights around the edge. It continues off the coast and seems to hover about 2 miles outside of the map proper, except that everything about it has vanished, except for the ring of lights. I am never able to get any closer to the lights, they seemed to hover on the horizon. It looked like the UFO I'v seen in videos of Mt Chiliad, but I can't be sure. Has anyone else encountered anything similar?
