Hi there, i was thinking of something. I know some of you would downvote because i suggest without having tried myself. In fact i'm not asking, simply suggesting, and most importantly, breaking down numbers.

Some have draw a parallel between Epsilon tractor, John Deere IRL, Jack Sheepe IG, the supposed jet pack, R-108, the movie "The Rocketeer" which features a jetpack, the Griffiths Observatory, and has a runtime of 108 minutes.

I casually mention that: in Buddhism, as you may know, lots of meditation is involved. Sometimes you even do yoga, even if it's more from Hinduism. BUT, anyway, in Buddhism when you gather and focus and repeat a mantra, yo do it 108 times. It's a well known "cosmic completion" number amongst buddhists.

So I wanted to know: could we actually perform 108 yoga sessions during the 10 days in Epsilon robes???

In theory, yes. As long as a yoga session lasts less than 4min26sec, that is 266sec by the way.

Why didn't i try this yet? simple: an uninterrupted sessions of 10 ingame days equals 8 straight hours. Daaaayum.

So:

  • 1 IG minute = 2 IRL seconds
  • it follows that an IG day is 48 min IRL
  • 48*10 = 480 min > /60 = 8 hours

here is a cool video that you don't have to watch though. Yoga session starts at 0:35(when custscene begins), is quite nicely executed, and ends at 3:45(when protag control comes back, when message for yoga comes back).

Feel free to Watch how long your yoga lasts, if it's different on Gordo than at Michael's, and if there's something indicating a "perfect" session. Typically, when is the best moment to exhale? I feel it would be just when the slightly darker blue circle expands(for example at 0:59 or 1:36 in the above video)

So i was just calculating:

  • 8 IRL hours means 28 800 seconds
  • 3min10sec yoga session means 190 seconds
  • 108 sessions like this mean 190x108 = 20 520 seconds
  • that means we have 8 280 spare seconds

Now the fun part:

  • 8 280 IRL seconds is 2 hours and 18 minutes > 138 minutes
  • 138 IRL minutes is......69 IG hours.

69 hours is 2 days and 21 hours. it's 10 times 6 hours and still we have 9 hours left. 9 hours are 10 times 54 minutes, 20 times 27 minutes. And 27 minutes is 54 IRL seconds.

I took this 10x6 hours (instead of 11,5 as 69/6 is) because it would be 10 days, with one sleep session per day, with Michael who sleeps for 6 IG hours.

I took 20x27 because it's the spare time we have after 10 sleep sessions of 6hrs each and 108 yogas of 3min10 each. It's actually 10x2x27. 10 because 10 days and 2 because 2 times a day : it's the time(27 IG minutes) you could take as Michael from your bed to yoga spot and yoga spot to your bed.

Obviously 108/10 = 10.8, it means that if you only do 10 yogas a day you won't make it. If you do 11, then the 10th day you only have 9 sessions to do. Meaning that you have(2 less sessions at 190 IRL seconds each) 3 hours and 10 minutes IG spare time on the last day. Maybe to reach whatever final spot to trigger whatever.

Of course, it's only when a session lasts 3min10. But still i found it to be curious. And wanted to do the maths just in case someone could achieve a 8 straight hours session before i could.
