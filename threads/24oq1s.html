Hi all, I am frequent hunter in GTA5 and reader of this thread. Rare poster.

I was trying to check what would happen by successfully riding a motorcycle down Eastern face of Chiliad into the Alamo sea via the creek while the UFO was around. Who knows why, I just find it odd that there is a mapped river/creek down that side. Really no reason to think much of this theory. Just something that I didn't see anyone else doing.

Playing as Michael. Unfortunately it stopped raining just before the UFO was due to appear (typical) just as I landed on Chiliad. It was about 2am. Irritated as anyone would be. I went on the viewing platform and looked through the telescopes for a while then jumped onto the table that has the map on it. About 2:30 am now, just pressing random buttons I pressed the two sticks down. Xbox 360 so this would be special capability. M did not have any guns drawn.

Inexplicably clouds change colour and get a lot darker, I thought it was going to start raining again, unusual pulsing sounds come out of the TV and camera angles change. The whole thing lasts about 10 seconds then stopped. Checked time - 2:36 a.m. WTF just happened? I wish I knew but I will be trying to replicate this and test further. Something strange just happened and it was not a glitch!!

For the record - F had a fun time with Ursula at about 4 pm. I immediately switched to M, drove from M's house to helipad and flew his buzzard to Chiliad. This is my normal method of getting the UFO to appear and I make it there before rain stops about 1 out of 3 times.

Hope this helps the cause somehow.
