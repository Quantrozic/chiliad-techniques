Ok, so let's get on to proving that the assets behind the locked doors by the mural & the one by the beach rocks are significant & yet to be triggered.

So after finding the asset box under the rocks the other day, I decided to do some digging in the files for anything similar.. A lot of digging!..

I found a handful of other significant asset boxes & by finding these, can prove what they do.

Early in the game, this: https://imgur.com/hXi57wc is what it looks like.

Now the fx\_helper 1-4 asset boxes here are triggered when the correct sequence of events have been fulfilled, in this case, because of story progression.

Then after said sequence has occurred, the asset boxes are then triggered, resulting in A PERMANENT CHANGE to the linked area.

Also, it may be worth noting that these asset boxes are ALWAYS in close proximity to the affected area.

This could also be visual and sound effects, but as the asset cubes are also layered with certain textures, shows that once triggered said texture & effects are applied.

This could also be in the form of smoke from the window, or a burning sound etc.

What is clear though is that these asset cubes apply lasting visual changes.

So now that a permanent visual/audial change occurring with these asset boxes has been established, let's now look at the box on top of Chiliad & the box by the rocks I found the other day;

Starting with the rocks box: https://imgur.com/u9BXGBF

As you can see from the previous screenshots, this box doesn't say "on fire", but in its place is instead "sea storm".

So from this we can now understand that when triggered, this area of rocks is affected by a sea storm of sorts, that will trigger a PERMANENT change to the affected area.

Now the Chiliad cube: https://imgur.com/ZVsChbd

And once again, you can see where "sea storm" was written previously, there is now "storm 4".

So this COULD mean the rocks and Chiliad are somehow linked, but that is just speculation at this point, but what we do know is that when this asset box is triggered, a certain type of storm is triggered (possibly an extreme storm, e.g. level 4) due to the file name, but once again, speculation.

So all of this together shows that the box in Chiliad and by the rocks remains untriggered due to the fact there is no observable permanent change that occurs in those areas that I am aware of.
