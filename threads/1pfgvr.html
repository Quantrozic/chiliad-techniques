Alamo Sea Tourism Site

▲ 134 ptsu/loopingleapheadOct 29, 2013127 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Convex-Hull Boundary Analysis of Glyph Sites ?

While restocking at the Sandy Shores Ammunation the clerk mentioned that the Alamo Sea has its own tourism site, so in the spirit of leaving no stone unturned, I decided to take a look.

First thing I notice on the header banner is a picture of a sunset over the Alamo Sea... but not just a sunset, an impossible sunset that appears to be in front of the mountains, AND matches perfectly the red glyph with the blue line under it at the Altruist camp cave. Where does this light come from if not the sun?

The rest of the site is mostly standard GTA satire: hipster/meth/trailer park/etc. jokes. But the one other thing that stood out to me? The weather forecast. It basically says "Same as always, triple digit heat and moderate to severe chances of earthquakes". Suddenly I remembered all the rumors about "real-time weather effects, and dynamic natural disaster systems" that would be designed for GTAV, and would change the way the game was played, and how we've seen pretty much none of that.

I thought of how this could be related to the search, and it hit me! We've been wondering what the squiggly lines at the bottom of the mural could represent. Well: Squiggly=Earthquake! Could it be possible we need to wait for, or somehow trigger an earthquake? Earthquakes tend to cause damage to things. Perhaps a quake could knock off the grates on those suspicious tunnels we've found, crush the mountain mural to reveal an elevator, punch a hole in a building somewhere, or even just throw a boulder aside to open up a path.

TL;DR - Alamo Sea website depicts Altruist cave glyph, and implies possibility of earthquake.
