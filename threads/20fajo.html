The mural and 'Come Back When Your Story is Complete' are clearly suggesting, to me, that you are not only supposed to return to Mt. Chiliad after reaching 100%, but also (based on the X[boxes] and lines within the mural) reach 100% in a certain way... whatever that means.

Essentially, I think that we need to combine several theories-- I don't know why but I feel like I'm starting to ramble, so let me condense my thinking:

  1. All evidence points to "THERE'S SOMETHING WE'RE MISSING..."

I just feel like the 'search' has been exhausted. We know triggers are involved (3AM, raining, etc.)... we know about weird rain coincidences (Ursula, Altruist)... but nothing has amounted to any new concrete direction for the search.

I also think it's completely believable, based on the random encounter with Simeon being time-sensitive, the FIB at the satellite dish, and the possibility of MC's being tailed throughout the game lend credence to the fact that there's something else out there that we're missing, and it might be because the game requires you to RolePlay it to achieve transcendence.

I think that it's possible that a TON of combinations of completing the game 'perfectly' can result in something different happen on top of Chiliad or at the military base.

That mural that said something about the 'Grand Scorer?' Saying shit, like, 'He's watching HOW you play the game?' I think Rockstar wants us to impress them... :|
