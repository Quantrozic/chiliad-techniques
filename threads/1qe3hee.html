So there is this doctrine called the Serpent Seed. In it, and it has been reworked a number of times so some versions will differ, the serpent in the garden of Eden mated with Eve to produce Cain which lead to another 'impure' line of beings. Serpent doesn't always mean snake it seems, and the serpent has been described as Satan or the devil, a lizard, or a human-like ape creature (Yeti?).

From https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Branhamism

'Branham taught an unorthodox doctrine about the source of original sin. He believed that the story of the fall of man in the Garden of Eden is allegorical and interpreted it to mean that the serpent had sexual intercourse with Eve, and their offspring was Cain. Branham also taught the belief that Cain's modern descendants were masquerading as educated people and scientists, and they were "a big religious bunch of illegitimate bastard children" who comprised the majority of society's criminals. He believed that the serpent was an intelligent human-like ape which he described as the missing link between the chimpanzee and man. Branham believed that the serpent was transformed into a reptile or a snake after it was cursed by God.'

Note above the part about 'educated people'. On the Epsilon website, they describe the Unsaveables as:

'Unsaveables are, unfortunately, all around us. They are silent. They are noisy. They ignore the truth. They want for everything and yet they seek nothing. Your wife may be Unsaveable. Your son may be. So may Grandma. Your therapist is doomed, as are most of the doctors, teachers, priests, lawyers and judges.' Educated people.

I can't remember on which page I saw it, but one pastor described the race of Cain as 'irredeemable', which would be a synonym of Unsaveable I suppose?

Afterwards Eve and Adam had Abel who produced the fully human or pure line or whatever. Unfortunately some groups aligned this story with race (look up British Israelism) and it became a part of the teachings of some White Supremacy groups. Maybe this is why there are few Nazi references in the game.
