Hi guys, I'm not sure if this has yet been discussed. At this location on a ranch in Grapseed, you'll find a big red barn with "Cousins Co Holding est. 47" on the roof.

Location: http://i.imgur.com/jGWcC5L.jpg
Red barn: http://i.imgur.com/BDsTTSA.jpg

At the front, you'll see a yellow half sun "glyph". Maybe sunset or sunrise, or even a solar eclipse?. I'm not sure this is just part of the logo or this is a undiscovered glyph: http://i.imgur.com/idZAtHw.jpg

I mention this because the name on the roof says est. 47. And ofcourse that year reminds us of the Roswell incident.

Roof: http://i.imgur.com/BDsTTSA.jpg.
Roswell wiki: http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roswell_UFO_incident

Also on that exact location, you'll find a water tower with the words "Milling co, Feed & Seed since 1962. That year reminds us of the dead of Marilyn Monroe, the beginning of the cold war (the atomic war between US and Russia). It also reminds us of this annular solar eclipse that toke place on July 31st 1962: http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_eclipse_of_July_31,_1962.

An annular solar eclipse can be observed when the Moon moves centrally in front of the Sun, but it is so far away from Earth that it appears smaller in the sky and cannot cover the Sun completely. The Sun's outer edges are then still visible and form a “ring of fire” around the Moon. Source: http://www.timeanddate.com/eclipse/annular-solar-eclipse.html)

Maybe this has something to do with the mural at Mount Chilliad and/or the Mural at the rehab. Or maybe it leads us to another quest? Maybe a annular solar eclipse will unveil lots of alien activity?
