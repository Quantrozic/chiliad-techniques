Greetings Uncle-brothers,

Some people here may already be aware that this custom Albany Alpha spawns at the epsilon HQ. There isn't much info on this sub regarding the car and I couldn't find any information on the spawn conditions. After some testing i found that the car spawns daily (I've tested Tues, Wed, Thursday and it has spawned) between 16:00 - 17:30. You need to park a couple of blocks away and wait for the clock to hit 16:00 then drive there. If it's not there immediately, circle round a couple of blocks and it will spawn. I thought i would share this in case anyone else was interested in obtaining this car.
