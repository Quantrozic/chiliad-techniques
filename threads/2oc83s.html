Alright, so I haven't played the game at all and am eagerly waiting for the PC release which will ofcourse blow the lid off the entire mystery if there is indeed something more to be found. I have however been following this sub and feel clearly there is something more teased.

So anyway, I was recently reviewing alien abduction films for youtube and had a chance to rewatch Close encounters of the third kind. R* is all about their film references, and I noticed the Fort Zancudo UFO makes a noise similar to the ship in the flim. Which got me thinking.

Spoilers for 30+ year old movie, but the plot involves humans who are contacted by an alien species and compelled toward a certain location for a meeting between races. Its a good movie, and worth checking out, at anyrate whats important is the method of communication used in the film, the aliens output a simplistic pattern of sounds, which the humans learn to mimic. I'm sure I saw a post with players attempting to use the Space Docker in a similar way to establish contact, but what other ways does the player have to generate sounds in game? Their phones. There is a number you can dial to get a strange offkey fax-machine/dial up type noise, has anyone attempted playing that audio in the presence of a ufo?

You could also try to mimic the sounds using dial tones, the sounds from the ufo seem very whitenoisy, but perhaps could be slowed down or speeded up to reveal some kind of pattern. Each character has a different model of phone as well, perhaps certain phones correspond to a specific UFO.

Like I said, I have zero in-game time, so it might just be the ravings of a lunatic, if someone wants to take this and run with it before I get my hands on a PC copy, feel free. :D
