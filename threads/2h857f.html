Speculation on hippy camp full moon party and alien symbol connection

▲ 14 ptsu/youngame23Sep 23, 201417 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Moon-Phase Condition Testing

I dont know if anybody has mentioned the hippy camp full moon party and a possible connection to the alien symbols. At the hippy camp there is a guy sitting on the car with the ufo on top. When you interrupt him by standing close to him, he will walk away and his phone will ring. During a full moon and only a full moon he will mention about a full moon party tonight in the desert and how its spiritual and how "Ken" was "visited" by aliens.

A seperate conversation he says "I want to show you some symbols...have you watched the documentary on symbols found in Guatemala? In the desert landing strip... ok bye" maybe a possible connection to a map.

I googled mayan symbols in Guatemala and came up with maya script. In the mayan script somewhere it says lines, dots and shells are numbers. it talks about how it is used for chronology and astronomy measurements. One of the hippys at the camp talks about how if you are looking from a satellite view something matches up. Maybe the desert party is linked to the chiliad mural or the game map can be used with the mayan measurements to produce a location to something bigger.

Also there are two npc's that are not on the map until you walk up to them. Andy Moon is the first and Clinton the war Vet is the second. They both talk about aliens and an apocalypse of some sort. I havent found any other characters that do this.

I think that the symbols might make a connection with the mayan numerical system somehow, or possibly the symbols can be a longitude latitude type of measurement on the game map. With all the illuminati and alien connections, this mystery is a fun scavanger hunt type of addition to make one of the best video games in history. Even better. Gta possibly made the greatest mystery in all of videogame history with this one. Even if our speculations don't eventually lead to anywhere, it has been really fun hunting. Gta rocks!!!!
