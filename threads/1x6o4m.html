Greetings fellow space travelers of Starship Earth!

So, like a lot of hunters, I play this game with my eyes and ears open. I completely understand that things can be a one off instance caused by a coincidental timing of a separate event, but this fellow hunters, is not that.

Here and Here are two separate occasions that I've managed to record a noise overheard, mixed into the radio. At first I thought it was just on Rebel Radio as there has previously been morse code messages found that reflect their website. This on the other hand, seems to happen more frequently than the previous morse message.

I can't seem to isolate the exact circumstances for reproduction, so I'll tell you guys what I usually do that causes them to be heard in my game.

  • Time does not appear to be a factor, as I've heard it at varying times through the day.
  • Non-vehicle specific, as I've heard it in both the Bifta and the Space Docker
  • To hear the noise, I drive through a tunnel on the Northern half of the map at full speed, without any collisions.
  • I've heard a noise when exiting three separate tunnels, the one that runs under fort z, the T01 & T02 tunnel under chiliad and the tunnel to the east of Chiliad near Gordo.
  • The first video example is from the Fort Z tunnel, while the second is T01/T02 tunnel

I'm going to learn Morse code, to see if I can translate this myself. Where I've noticed disinformation in the past, concerning things I've heard and noticed it on the sub in the form of the SCHWA video, I'm curious of its origin, so I'm going to learn the Morse Code myself.

No offense to anyone who might try to decode it and posts it in the comments. It'd be better to have two independent sources analyze the data anyway, for verification purposes. I'm also of the "gotta see it myself" line of thought, so frankly even if a few of you translate it, I'm inclined to still give it a go myself.

Just wanted to share this with you all & thanks for reading. If anyone with a capture card or has a way of recording/isolating the audio and are capable of capturing/recording this, let me know. I'd appreciate having a cleaner copy to work with.

These noises also might be completely unrelated to the mystery, but no stone unturned, I say.
