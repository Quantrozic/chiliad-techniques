My theory on the role Kyle P. Slater would have taken

▲ 7 ptsu/iiwis_now86Jul 21, 20253 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:UV Light Analysis ?

I guess most of you already know about the character of Kyle P. Slater, he was a cut character from the game that was meant to be a Lieutenant from Fort Zancudo, which you would of most likely had to help get to the base on a random event (A basic one where his car malfunctioned or something so you'd give him a ride, there are plenty of events like that)

We don't know much else about him besides his cryptic voiceline when you call his number which is actually in the game for some reason, but I've came to the conclusion that the reason for him to exist, would have been to give us a clue or tell us something about the Fort Zancudo UFO, so we'd have known that it existed, since it's the hardest one to find given the in-game clues.

Think about it, the Chiliad and the camp one have clear symbols signaling where there is a UFO, and it's something we would have been able to figure out, yet the Fort Zancudo one only indication are the lights above the bunker, which is something very difficult to spot or find for yourself through normal play (because of the hour it spawns at, just after 100% and in a locked area like the base)

So if we consider it to be the only clue given to us about there being a UFO in Fort Zancudo is kind of odd, but I guess you could also count the Omega Spaceship parts missions, since it's heavily implied it's the same kind of technology, sharing both aesthetic and lights to the FZ one, yet even then in those mission there is no indication or anything pointing at Ford Zancudo, not even a piece of the spaceship thrown in there, nothing.

All this leads me to believe, that perhaps Kyle Slater, in his random event, would have played the role of a dork oficer, like a run off the mouth kind of militar, that would have spilled the beans to us the players on the ride to the base, giving us hints or at least commenting about there being something in the base, this is speculating but I see it fitting for the character since his only line of dialogue makes him look a bit "unserious" or just "cryptic", he may even be a conspiranoic himself who distrusts what he is working on. So I don't think it's too crazy that his random event was meant to clue us in on the right direction to know there was something to look for in Fort Zancudo, with the bunker lights being a second hint signaling exactly where it was.

It at the very least is something to think about cause it's already odd that we would have kept his number or contact after just helping him for a random event, maybe that indicates that he at least got along very well with the player and had some reason to keep contact (it very likely being in the case that he already told us plenty of confidential stuff)
