Five endings...five X's...

▲ 21 ptsu/BiggestERBFanDec 31, 20149 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Choice-Tree Ending Permutation Mapping ?Moral Choice & Playstyle Branching Analysis ?

Alright, guys. I read a comment on YouTube last night and it got me thinking...

Someone was talking about how there are not only three endings to the game, but actually five. The user (and everyone else who responded) must not be that well into the mystery, because they forgot to mention that there also five X's on the mural!

I searched through this reddit and could not find this exact topic being discussed, but sorry if this has been discussed thoroughly elsewhere.

Anyways, yeah, there are five endings to the game. But usually, people only think there are three - Kill Trevor, Kill Michael, or Deathwish.

While those are the endings you can pick to do, while inside the two of missions you are able to make a different choice to make a different ending happen. In Kill Trevor, Franklin can either shoot Trevor himself or let Michael do it. In Kill Michael, Franklin can either kill Michael or try to save him. In either ending for both missions, though, Trev & Mike end up dying somehow.

So, these five X's must correspond to the endings with all this talk about the lines being mission choices. Here are my opinions:

The first two X's at the top that line up are to kill Mike/Trevor yourself. Mike is the left one, because it leads to the UFO (Epsilon). Trevor is the right one, because it leads to the jetpack (Altruists).

The X to the bottom-right of those two X's is try to save Mike. It stops at a dead-end, so this most likely means this ending is a wrong ending.

The X right above the egg represents letting Mike kill Trevor. I only say this because it is faded away...like how Trevor is being burned in the fire after getting killed by his best friend. Just a small theory. Again, this one stops at a dead-end, so this is also a wrong decision.

The X to the top-left of the UFO is Deathwish. I say this because unlike the other two that stop at dead-ends, this one has lines coming out of it. that leads to nothing. Not a dead-end, just nothing. So this could either mean that this ending doesn't reward you with a special prize like the other two (and the unfinished line possibly means that there are still loose ends or it means that there is nothing to find furthermore because you've completed the story the "true" way.)

So, how do all these lines connect? I'm not sure. On the same line as let Mike kill Trevor and try to save Mike, there is another unfinished line. This could also prove that these endings have no reward. Another interesting thing to point out is that Kill Mike leads onto the line that goes into the UFO, but you can steer off this path and go to Deathwish...does this mean that there is a way to resurrect Mike?

It's all just so confusing. Mainly because the first two X's, Kill Mike & Kill Trevor, are both connected, which means that those two endings may be the same and reward the same prize? I really have no clue.

All I could say is, the egg connects to every path. The jetpack connects to Kill Trevor. The UFO connects to Kill Mike.

But if we're supposed to be seeing the X's as starting points (meaning the two X's at the top aren't the "start" of the puzzle), then that means there are no dead-ends, only beginnings.

If that is true, I'll only point one thing out about this...the Deathwish box leads right into the eye's path. So hmmmm...
