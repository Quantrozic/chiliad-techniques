The Chiliad and Hippy Camp UFOs: One and the same? A fairly large theory based entirely on game logic.

▲ 26 ptsu/RdEydFcdMar 11, 201425 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:UV Light Analysis

So I've spent the last week trying to see what, if anything, I could come up with before the search for secrets in Dark Souls 2 eats up my gaming time, and honestly, I couldn't come up with anything concrete in the game (what's new?).

However, the recent posts on the nature of the mural got me thinking. I've said in the past that whether or not the mural was meant to break the 4th wall would be an important piece of information to have, but I think it could be a bit of both. It could be that it is meant to break the 4th wall, but as a result of in-game entities rather than the devs themselves.

There are only 2 places in the game where the clue glyphs are present: Chiliad, and the hippy camp (and its surrounding area). This would suggest pretty strongly that the glyphs, and the mural itself were painted by the in-game UFO enthusiasts. I think this changes the nature of the search immensely, as it would concretely connect 2 elements in the game that are otherwise seen as somewhat separate or arbitrary. This isn't groundbreaking stuff, but let me take it a few logical steps further.

The red eye and UFO glyphs on the mural are separate entities, which is something that has been cause for speculation for as long as the mystery has been around. Many still see the red eye glyph as being the UFO itself, though if they were painted by in-game characters, this wouldn't be the case -- they would have painted them separately for a reason. What we'd have then is an "eye in the sky", and what would these people see as an eye in the sky? The UFO above their camp. I believe, in the game world, the UFO enthusiasts are so enthusiastic because they're aware of the in-game UFOs.

So here's what I'm suggesting: The UFO hippies are aware of the UFO above the camp, but don't/can't communicate with it. Where better to try to communicate with "aliens" than on top of the tallest mountain? Here's where some 4th wall breaking stuff comes in. Instead of the devs setting up the mystery as just the devs, what if they connected it to the game world by setting things up so that it's actually the UFO enthusiasts who set it up for the player?

This would make sense, as it's pretty clear the UFO hippies see the mountain as important (see: the mountain glyph with the wow signal scrawled out next to it.) I think it's likely that the mystery was set up in such a way as to guide you from Chiliad to the camp UFO -- using the clue glyphs as intended (we didn't) would yield a UFO, and where are those glyphs much much easier to find? The hippy camp. Logically, people would have seen the glyphs at the camp first, but wouldn't think much of anything of them until they were then found on Chiliad, leading to a UFO. Solving the mural itself using the glyphs would almost certainly have led people back to the camp.

With this in mind (and this is speculation, not statement of fact,) the red eye glyph would be a more all-encompassing symbol for aliens, signifying "watchers" of sorts. The UFO glyph wouldn't be referencing the UFO on Chiliad, but the hippy camp UFO. This is why the they look the same, because they are the same. The UFO is obviously not there, it's only an image of the one that looks nearly identical to the one above the camp, and I submit that this is because they're the same UFO. The presence of the glyphs would lead you back to the camp, and the image of the UFO would tell you what to look for. Remember, if we had done this the correct way, this would have been the logical next step to take. It would also explain why the glyphs appear at the camp, despite them having seemingly no purpose there. It would also explain why there's an identical UFO elsewhere, and why it's objectively so much easier to find than the one on Chiliad, which has been another point of contention.

Of course, we found all the UFOs before we found all the clue glyphs, and before we attempted to apply any of them, so we did things ass backwards in regards to how it was designed for us.

Following what I believe to be the dev's intentions, the mural may not be exactly as many of us figured. If we were indeed meant to go back to the camp and connect the given dots, it would mean that something more is indeed supposed to happen at the top of Chiliad. This is something most of us believe for one reason or the next anyway, but it seems like most believe it just because they want to, and not because there's any compelling evidence of it. But I propose this:

The red eye glyph is whatever's inside the Easter egg, which is why they're connected. The clue glyphs were meant to reveal the Hippy camp UFO, represented by the UFO glyph, and the jetpack represents the weaponized military UFO at Zancudo. Whatever might be at the top of Chiliad involves something relating to these 2 UFOs.

Using the game's logic, it would make sense. It would explain why the Chiliad and hippy camp UFOs look the same, and why the glyphs appear in both places. It would explain why the UFO on Chiliad was set up to be found as a result of investigative work, yet the other 2 could be found easily without any investigative clues of their own. Most importantly, it would offer a strong explanation for the the jetpack glyph. Without using obvious military imagery, what better way to send people to Fort Zancudo to search than using an image of a jetpack (for obvious reasons)? It would also explain why all the UFOs can only be seen with 100% files, a clue only found on Chiliad.

Logically, I think the process was meant to look like this: 100% the game, use the clues to find a UFO --> with the knowledge that there is indeed UFOs in the game, go to the only other place the glyphs appear and find another UFO --> with the understanding of how to find the ambient UFOs, go to where a jetpack would logically be, and find another UFO the same way.

Do I have any idea what to do at this point? Do yoga in the Epsilon blues on a full moon with a thunderstorm after killing someone on the rock at the Altruist camp... just joking, I have no idea. However, if I'm right, or even near right, it would fairly adequately explain a lot of the lingering questions we've come to brush off. It would also be pretty strong evidence of a UFO-based DLC. Personally, I think this is the most logical view of the mural, even if all the clues are actually just 4th wall breaking stuff placed there by the devs for the player to find.
