Weird Things I Noticed About the Altruists' A.I.

▲ 17 ptsu/UndeadOPSep 27, 20156 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Director Mode Scene & Spawn Testing ?

Hey guys. While messing around on Director Mode today, I discovered some strange/mysterious things about the A.I. of the Altruists.

  • The first time I ever saw this happen was today on Director Mode. So I was using my Railgun and C4s to blow random stuff up around the Altruist camp. The third explosion scared all of the Altruists, which is something I expected to happen. However, they all jumped the wall surrounding their fort to run away. And when I say they jumped the wall, I literally mean they all ran up to the wall, climbed to the top of it, jumped, and then ran off into the woods. I tried to jump the wall with my character, but the wall is too high for the player to jump over. Just thought it was interesting that the Altruists could jump over it with ease but the player is unable to.
  • So after the aforementioned events, I re-loaded back into Director Mode again to do some more hunting at the camp. I decided to see how far I could run away from the camp until the angry Altruists stopped chasing me. So I shot one of the Altruists and got them mad at me, and ran out of the camp. I decided to climb up to the radio tower looking down upon the camp. Once I got up there, I hid by the tower to see if the Altruists would climb up after me. And what do ya know, they did. But here's the strange thing. They climbed up after me and as soon as they were situated in front of the Altruist house up there, one by one they each squatted down and took cover in front of the house, even though by doing this I had a clear shot at them. It's as if they were scripted to do this once they got up here. It really makes me wonder if Trevor is supposed to do something up here during the shootout. Unfortunately, I cannot test that since my shootout has been done already.
  • Something else to note. As I previously just mentioned, my shootout has been completed already, which means that your playthrough does not affect the map on Director Mode. I suggest that we may take this to our advantage if you want to do a before/after observation of areas that get affected during playthroughs (Kortz Center, IAA building, Altruists camp, that one hospital, etc.)

I was using an Xbox One while doing this testing, I don't know if that matters or not. But anyways, let me know if you guys find this stuff interesting. If you do, drop some requests below on what I can use Director Mode to search for. Kifflom, brother-brothers!
