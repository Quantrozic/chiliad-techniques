So basically I was wondering how we would know if we had triggered something in game. This made me think of the two things that rockstar has added for SP since re-release on next gen. The murder mystery and the director mode or more accurately - the golden peyote.

I think they were trying to show us the different ways that things are hidden and the mechanics we can use in game to find such triggers. The murder mystery has the writing on the walls which we aim at in order to 'trigger' and the dead woman that we have to do the same thing to. The same applies to the dead body in the mine. We also have a hidden time and place which is Solomons movie studio at 9pm with a hidden yellow marker.

As for the golden peyote - with them putting in the director mode, this indirectly got people looking for the final peyote that they really really hinted at. This showed us about the time and weather variables.

I think if we were to put all these things into consideration this could possibly help us to narrow down things we can actually test out such as weather and the aiming at things to 'trigger' something.
