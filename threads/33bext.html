Usually when I go to the observatory, the satellite dish and security camera are not visible on the small elevator building out front. Not only that, but even stranger, the electrical box that's behind a grate on the southeast lower side of the observatory appears and/or disappears along with the radar dish and camera on the elevator EVERY SINGLE TIME! If I fly directly above the observatory at a fairly high altitude and then go back down, everything will either appear or disappear, depending on how it was when I first got there. I can leave bullet holes anywhere around and they will stay if I don't go too far away, but the other things I mentioned always appear or disappear together. Has anyone else ever noticed this?
