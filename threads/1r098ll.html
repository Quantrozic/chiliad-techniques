I wanted to share my thoughts a little more of the purple mystery and the epsilon tracts.

So what importance could they hold to the mystery? Well as I mention above they seem to connect through the different elements extremely well as you will see going through but also might shed some light to how Kraff is god like rather than actually being gods.

"Kifflom, brother. You are now truly one of us and are ready to write the tract, which has already been written..WE WORSHIP KRAFF WHERE THE EARTH REACHES OUT TO THE 9TH PARADIGM."

This would mean 100% completion I.e Everything has already been done and the top of Mt Chiliad would be where the earth reaches the 9th paradigm.

What this means though is subjective as one interpertation I believe could be that they realise they are in a video game or at least the world is constructed through code which they manipulate to enter God Mode which is the 9th Paradigm view. This could then directly link to how they identify their game universes 2D, 3D and HD since 3D used Renderware and the HD universe using RAGE. This would explain their game universes and why they are actually separate universes and why whilst Red Dead and GTA are separate if entities could "pass through the game engine" then they could very well end up in Red Dead redemption and vice versa.

"Where the first of the fleet succumbed to the waves, there the message is seeded."

The first fleet would be the spaceship in the water and there being a message around there somewhere.

"When they sail from the North, they will find this offering."

This could be referring directly to the UFO position for how to find it since it is found sailing north or there being something north of the UFO. I would most likely suspect it's the directions to the UFO.

The next two I think are directly connected

"In the core of the mountain, where the blast is not felt, there you shall find it"

"In the rubble of the old religion, will be the basis for the new."

This is think implicates the Altruist Camp, with the Altruists being the Old religion with their worship being more primal in nature. Aside from the Altruists there are a few spots that reach into the mountain however the UV map does seem to direct us towards the Camp.

"Where the first fleet sailed, the new fleet will find its map"

This again is confusing and could be referring to heading back to the underwater UFO at this point as you have collected something by then as mentioned by the past tact "There you shall find it"

This map mentioned in the previous tract will lead us to a building.

This I have no clue on but by the time this part is reached may hold greater meaning or become more clear depending on where the map sends us. Maybe this indicates some audio we're meant to activate that gives us something on meaning.

"The tallest obelisk of glass and steel holds no comparison to the word of Kraff"
Clearly referring to Maze Bank as this is the tallest building and is mainly glass windows and steel beams.

"Where they discard their earthly prisons, there you may find the germ of a higher civilization."
This last part could have many meanings however, I feel this directly relates to the empty grave "Where they discard their earthly prisons" the place of bodies without souls.
