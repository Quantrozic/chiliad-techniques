Kifflom,

You guys may have seen that R* updated news, saying that this year they'll launch their "biggest ever DLC", and a heist in a "complete new location", and there's a lot to talk about here.

To begin with the biggest DLC ever, I would like to remind you about the alien "Los Santos 2020" poster we can find near Omegas trailer, considering they are supposedly working on that content for 7 years, and so far have not released anything about that so far, I guess is safe to assume they'll be releasing whatever they plan on that biggest DLC (also the last DLC of 2020, and possibly the last one from this gen of consoles). Also we have Gerald last call, saying that something big is about to hit the city.

Now about the "complete new location", the word complete really stands out here, many people are speculating about a map expansion (our biggest hope I guess), but could also mean opening a previous closed place like they did with the cassino and arcade, so let's talk about both of those.

The most likely place to open is the big construction building near maze bank (let's face, it's 7 year under construction, that's 210 years in game time), in the last update they made a big deal about blueprints, and this building having the most blueprints in the game (as well as a suspiciously high amount of connections with mystery involved corporations), I would bet on that opening with a bunch of clues on it.

But in terms of a new map the imagination can go really far, such as a new island, north Yankton, or even the previous maps, but there are things here that called my attention and I would like to share.

You guys probably remember the blond girl in bikini during the load screen (yes, the D cups) in the background you can see the giant whel from Del Pier Pier, and in the back the city of Los Santos. Except that this is completely non sense, the whel IS in the city, you shouldn't be able to see it from that angle.

And it becomes even more suspicious when you enter the Ateist community website, where you can simulate a tsunami hiting Los Santos, and the interesting thing, it's that Los Santos map is completely different than the one we know, summing up with everything we found in the ocean we could assume the tsunami actually happen.

And if that's not enough, differently from all other GTA games, Los Santos ocean is unbelievable big, it pretty much fits all other GTA maps inside of it with ease, doesn't it seen way too much waste of deployment to simply leave it without a use? All exclusive maps in online, are also invisible hidden in story mode by going under the map and landing in the right place, you guys think we are meant to find something there? Either a clue, or even...if I may dream, Vice City?

I know this whole thing is happening in online, and I too rather believe the real mystery is in story mode, yet since R* is sending clues there, I believe we should talk about those things, they're quite huge.

I appreciate your constructive input.
