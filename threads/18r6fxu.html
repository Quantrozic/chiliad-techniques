As you all know yesterday source code from gta 5 was leaked, 8 single player dlc’s were scrapped, we don’t know much about them but we have confirmation about 4 of them

Confirmed dlc’s:

liberty city, we have confirmation about Niko in gta 5, like his wanted poster, or him being mentioned.
Agent Trevor which is now the doomsday, so that has to be the dlc related to the jetpack like it is in gta online (there are files about jetpack in the source code)
Prologue dlc, maybe connected to the frozen alien
Norman apparently was focused on zombies just like the rdr1 dlc.

Non confirmed DLC’s (yet)

Now we are left with assassination, manhunt, relationship and enterprise.
