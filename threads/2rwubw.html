These symbols tie-in with the game!

Documented under:Symbolic Glyph Frequency Counting

*A UFO symbolizes a creative mind. It denotes a spiritual journey into the unknown and signals self-development and self-awareness. It also symbolizes that you need to take a different perspective on things, no matter how bizarre or unusual it may be. One last meaning for a UFO is that you are currently too 'spaced out,' and need to be more grounded in your journeys.

*A broken egg has a couple of meanings. It could mean that you are realizing your goals and discovering what you really need to accomplish. It could mean you are beginning to face the consequences of your actions.

*A jetpack symbolizes that you are accomplishing your goals and overcoming your difficulties in pursuing said goals.

*The symbolic explanation for an eye is interesting. The right eye represents the sun, while the left eye represents the moon. This could be very, very helpful in the hunt. There is a line going through the eye on the mural. The symbolic explanation for having something going through your eye is obstacles in one's path. It could alternatively stand for your view on things compared to others'.

*The meaning of the Eye of Providence is spiritual awareness, and new understandings on things. Alternatively, it could mean that no detail can get past you.

*An eerie-looking sun represents a block in your path that needs to be pushed away to become better, or in this case, lighter.

*A crescent moon symbolizes changes, renewal, and movement. In other words, it means you are progressing smoothly toward your life path.

*Lightning symbolizes awareness, insight, spiritual revelation, truth, and purification. Alternatively, it could stand for a shocking turn of events. There are many forces governing your life that may be beyond your control and even destructive. (This seems to be Michael's case with his fear of aliens.)

*A mountain represents many major obstacles and challenges that you have to overcome. If you are on top of the mountain, then it indicates that you achieved and realized your goals. You have recognized your full potential. Alternatively, mountains denote a higher realm of consciousness, knowledge, and spiritual truth. Climbing a mountain symbolizes your determination and ambition to solve a puzzle. Going around a mountain (you must do this to find the glyphs) implies that you are tackling some obstacle in a roundabout manner. You are taking an indirect approach. Alternatively, it can mean that you are pacing yourself and you know your limitations.

*Aliens taking part in sexual acts represents the need or desire to try something new, something really risky. You need to open up and realize there are way more opportunities.
