This guy outlines all the vehicle defs in next gen. One of them is called seraph, which he speculates could be a 2 door rolls royce, most likely based on a quick google search for "seraph car" https://www.google.com/webhp?sourceid=chrome-instant&ion=1&espv=2&ie=UTF-8#q=seraph%20car

My thought is that they rarely use actual model names in GTA, so its unlikely to be the Rolls Royce Seraph.

Another take on the word seraph could interpret as short for seraphim. Seraphim = winged angel. A search for "seraph jetpack" and "seraphim jetpack" reveals it is named after a model of jetpack in several video games, and a search for "seraph plane" returns a NASA built craft named the seraph.

So it is a common name for flying vehicles.
