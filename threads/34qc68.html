Going back to the beginning: The Mural, the Mound and where we should be looking.

▲ 23 ptsu/TheBitingCatMay 3, 20159 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Edge-Detection Glyph Tracing ?Noclip Boundary Mapping ?

It's been a long time since this mystery surfaced, and after what, two years of exploring Los Santos and Blaine County to no end we have discovered...what? A holographic UFO, two different flying UFO's, one crashed UFO, a crate at the Altruists, several glyphs on Chiliad, matching glyphs on The Hippie Camp mound, the sand glyph and whatever you want to consider the text at the Altruists. Essentially, no rewards as of yet. It makes me think that we've missed something fundamental.

I went back and looked at the mural, compared that to the locations of the five glyphs we know, and while there's some corellation to their positions, there's physically no way to line up an overlay on top of the map to make them all fit. However, I do hold the belief that there is some point in space in the direction of Paleto Bay that if someone were to noclip fly and position themselves there that not only would the mural align perfectly with the edge of the mountain but the glyphs would also align with the mural and we would know where the bottom three boxes lie. But I don't think it needs to come to that.

We have a second representation of the mountain, in the mound that is at the Hippie Camp. On that mound there are indentations. The ones on top of the mound do not exactly line up with the glyphs or the mural, but remember these are being built by people who could care less about accuracy and scale. The significant portion is on the side, three large indentations, sitting aligned next to each other. The leftmost falls within a blue area of the mound; the middle one falls at the top part of the red area; the rightmost falls within a green area; and they're pretty much equidistant from one another.

Here's where theory comes in: The three indentations and the three bottom boxes on the mural represent the same locations. The one in blue, the box with the spaceship in it, represents the sunken crashed UFO's location. The middle indentation, the box with the cracked egg, represents Paleto Bay, specifically a location along the northeast side near the train depot. The rightmost indentation, the box with the "jetpack" in it, represents a spot in the Chiliad National Forest. Now here's the interesting thing - if you draw a line on the map from the crashed UFO down through Paleto Bay along the rail line and extend it down, the spot that is equidistant from the crashed ship to Paleto Bay is...The Altruist Camp. From these three points and the observation point atop Chiliad, I'm willing to bet that a point in space can be triangulated to match the Mural's representation of Chiliad.

As it stands, I believe that we've found the UFO on the mural. Paleto Bay, with all of its egg references, is where the Alien Egg still resides. The "jetpack" or whatever is in the third box, is in or around the Altruist camp. It may even be in that locked box that can appear on completing the camp. There's already enough speculation about the Altruist graffiti towards witnessing a UFO.

I know that the community has likely picked apart the camp, and probably spent a good effort in Paleto Bay. I'm willing to bet we've still missed something.
