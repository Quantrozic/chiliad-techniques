Praise the Eye! - Sacrificing Altruist victims to the Eclipse

▲ 47 ptsu/LlamaGuy69Oct 19, 20216 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Interrogative Prompting of NPC Trigger Conditions ?

It's highly likely that the Chiliad Mystery involves several other conditions that need to be met during Trevor's interaction with the Altruists in addition to bringing them the correct victims. Many clues point to the fact that delivering these victims during an Eclipse is one of these necessary hidden conditions.

The Altruist graffiti tag that says “See the Eye!” is the most direct reference to the Eclipse, as half way through the appearance of the Eclipse it will form the shape of an Eye.

Here's an image of the Altruist that spawns on the edge of the cliff staring directly at the Eclipse which is in perfect alignment with him.

When Trevor delivers the final victim to the camp the in-game time will continue to flow normally during the intro cutscene where he delivers the victim and then gets forced into the camp but the time will freeze during the main cutscene where the Altruists begin to perform their sacrificial ritual on Trevor.

The intro cutscene lasts for 10 in-game minutes, which is the exact amount of time it takes for the Eclipse to form into the “Eye” after it first appears.

This means that if you deliver the victim at the exact moment that the Eclipse forms it will be shown growing into the Eye in the background of the intro cutscene and by the time the main cutscene is triggered the Eclipse will be frozen in the formation of the Eye in the background for the entire cutscene.

I think that it's highly likely that delivering victims under this condition is one of the necessary conditions that will trigger something deeper within the mystery.
