Now in accordance with the "6 minute rule", Mike and Trevor stayed where they were, and I'm not sure if where Frank was was a legitimate puzzle point, but he lasted over a minute in the location when not active, so I don't know.
Anyways, I did the storm cheat (separate save for testing purposes) and was able to hop between characters and everything, however the saucer wasn't visible from the a.c. water tower and definitely not from f's location (even with scopes)

However, I noticed when I was round that way that there's a small replica silo/tower thingy near the station wagon which has a signpost on top of it, which bears a red arrow. My main question about that is...does that make it relevant?
