I know it's out there but here goes...
What if the Space Docker is an in-game time machine that's supposed to send Franklin back in time to the Prologue? Suppose Franklin chooses option B and kills Michael De Santa and starts feeling guilty . What if Omega sends him the "coordinates" to send Franklin back in time via the Space Docker so Franklin can go back and stop Michael from betraying his friends and live a better life in North Yankton?

I know it's far fetched but it's not impossible since the North Yankton map is in the game and it seems to be a complete waste to just use it for a lousy two missions so briefly. This is just a theory based on the fact that there are several ways to trigger a thunderstorm in-game and lightening played a huge role in Back to the Future and Michael is a huge fan of 80's films.
