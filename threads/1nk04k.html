[SPECULATION] The Chiliad Mystery could very well be as alive as ever, let me tell you why. Punch, pie, and free hats at the table by the door.

Documented under:Vehicle Spawn-Table Auditing

There seems to be a ton of discouragement popping up, and rather than continuing to respond to individual comments, I thought I'd go over the logic behind why it's completely reasonable to continue searching for further eggs. I want to preface this by saying the idea that there's nothing more is completely valid, and I am very much not offering irrefutable proof to the contrary. I'm only offering the reasons why -- at least to me -- the idea that there's nothing more is equally as refutable. I will be doing so while wearing a dinosaur costume because dinosaurs are awesome.

Why does it stand to reason why the mural was not designed specifically to see the Chiliad UFO? Let me explain:

• The mural itself was largely unnecessary in finding the UFO. The most important clue as to how to make the UFO appear was not located on the mural at all, rather it was etched into the back of the observation deck, and reads Come back when your story is complete. It is there in plain sight, without any symbolism or cryptic hints, and also told you exactly where to stand with equal clarity. The only roles the mural played was in hinting that it needed to be raining, and to look for a UFO.

•The glyphs on the mountain were also completely unnecessary. The Chiliad mural displays a sort of map with many red boxes, most of which coincide with glyphs on the sides of the mountain, though they were all redundant clues if they were meant to show you how to make the UFO appear.

-The moon glyph: There need not be a clue that it needs to be night time to see the UFO, that's just the nature of UFO sightings in popular culture. You assume it has to be night by default, and people did

-The rain glyph: Completely redundant, the rain clue was on the mural itself.

-The mountain(?) glyph: Why would the mural show the location of a clue hinting at the location of the mural? This is easily the most redundant glyph, as you would have known to go to the top of the mountain by virtue of it being where the mural was, not to mention the fact the the top of the tallest mountain in any game is often the first place people look for things in video games. This is all assuming this glyph is of the mountain to begin with.

-The faded eye glyph: Unknown, though some theorize it's in reference to the holographic nature of the UFO, though this doesn't make even the slightest bit of sense. The mural hints at the UFO itself, why would there be a glyph in relation to a seemingly irrelevant fact you could only come to learn after solving the mystery?

-The face/missing glyph: Your guess is as good as mine here.

•The Big Glyphs. Of course, there are the big'uns.

-The UFO glyph: This is the one we believe we have the answer to, but do we? The placement of the UFO glyph on the Chiliad mural is at the bottom, not the top. If the lines on the mural lead to a single point at the top, and all the red boxes coincide with the placement of the glyphs on the side of the mountain, why would this UFO glyph not do so as well? I agree that it's likely the UFO glyph is a reference to the UFO (though this isn't rock solid), but its placement begs the question of why this glyph is the only one that doesn't work the same as the others if that UFO was indeed the end goal of the mural.

-The cracked egg glyph: This one could very well be just a reference to breaking open an Easter egg, and in my opinion, likely is. But the prominence of an egg in the Epsilon mystery raises the specter of doubt.

-The jetpack glyph: We don't know anything about this glyph other than it is, without a doubt, a dude with a jetpack. That is not scuba gear, or a backpack with propane and propane accessories. Stick figure drawings are meant to put things in simple, understandable terms, and that is obviously DJ Qualls riding a jetpack (DJ Qualls unconfirmed). Besides this, it has absolutely nothing to do with seeing the UFO.

Now back to the obvious: The UFO on Chiliad was found without the help of all but one glyph, including all the mountainside glyphs. In fact, the mountainside glyphs were found after the UFO, which isn't surprising, as they were much harder to find. To argue that the UFO is truly the end of the mural mystery is an argument for R* constructing an Easter egg hunt that was completely broken at nearly the exact same time people first started getting 100% completion, making the mural itself instantly useless. Is it possible? Sure, but it would involve R* half assing it to such a degree that I find it hard to believe.

Some assorted other stuff:

• There are glyphs at or around the hippy camp that have nothing to do with seeing the UFO located there. The moon, rain, and mountain glyphs are in the area, yet none of them are necessary for seeing that UFO.

• There are no glyphs at the Zancudo UFO, only a strange light.

• The location of the underwater UFO is spelled out for you on the blueprint map.

• Fucking magnets, how do they work?

• Why would R* design a big clue scavenger hunt for one UFO Easter egg, then make absolutely no big deal out of the 3 others? What makes the Chiliad UFO so much more special than the 2 others that are flying, and the one in the ocean you don't even need 100% completion to see? What do the glyphs even have to do with the other hippy camp UFO in the first place if they were designed specifically for the Chiliad UFO?

None of it makes perfect sense, as much as some claim it does. Is it possible R* half assed this whole thing so badly, and they really did expect their Fisher Price puzzle to hold up? Absolutely, but does that make perfect sense? No, it just doesn't, and I'd go as far as to say it makes less sense than R* making that puzzle even more complicated than it seems. In the lead up to the game's release, they talked about making the Easter eggs harder to find than they have made them in past games. Again, the Chiliad UFO Easter egg was cracked in 3 days, at roughly the exact same time players first started getting 100% completion. There were probably only hours between the first small wave of 100%ers, and the discovery of the UFO. The best R* could do was a few hours, after explicitly stating a desire to really hide them better? That'd be a hard case to make, in my opinion.

Anyway, we shouldn't be discouraged. All Easter egg hunters hunt knowing that they're mostly doing so on faith, sometimes completely. It has always been this way, and failing to find something should never discourage people from looking. There is always a gold rush after the first big discoveries in a game, followed by a huge dip into the deep whisky bottle of despair when that gold rush doesn't immediately pan out. It happens, and it always sucks, but remember: there have been Easter eggs that went years and years without being discovered. Sometimes the game's code eventually reveals something, sometimes the developer comes out and tells people about it, and sometimes they're found organically by players. People have been Easter egg hunting in GTAV for only about 2 weeks.

I welcome anyone to tell me I'm wrong, but if you do, tell me why so we can have a legit discussion about it. It would probably be a lot more fun than than covering a Slip 'n Slide in sandpaper and salt, then breaking out the ol' speedo. It would also probably be a lot more fun than talking about how we're all wasting our time.
