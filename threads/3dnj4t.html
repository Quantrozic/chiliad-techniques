I've been going through the sub and I've seen a lot of great ideas and some really creative ones as well. There are a lot of posts about possible triggers and a special order they'd have to be done. I thought we should have a Kaizen type post where we just start spit balling ideas and maybe brainstorm our way to a jetpack.

The point of Kaizen is to bounce ideas off each other and build into something productive. No matter how crazy an idea is, it may have a little sense it it that could trigger a relevant solution. So lets all try to be positive and encouraging.

  1. Yoga on Mt gordo seems to be a promising.
  2. I think the chart next to the crossword puzzle suggest we need to solicit the services of a working girl or one of the girls from the V.Unicorn. The white board says "Steps to Recovery"... maybe as in recovering the Jet Pack. #1 on the board is hate yourself, and after Trevor is done he says "and I hate myself.
  3. I think burning yourself on the sacrifice stone might be something. If you die you respawn (Be reborn). Also the cave says "your path is lit"... you can't get more literal than that.

If they are triggers what order and what time would we do all these things.

Alright lets get some ideas going!

   (Edit) Thought of another one, probably nothing (the whole mystery could be nothing) but how about this. The Maze bank fountain has been thought to be the egg or maybe The Maze bank itself. I noticed when you do the base jump the cut scene just before you jump has a close up of the "crack" in the fountain.  
   Could that be something? I tried base jumping from the bank onto the crack, but that was before 100%. Is it possible all characters have to die on certain locations, maybe at a certain time? As speculated if Franklin is the egg; then he dies on the crack. Mike gets mauled doing yoga on Mt Gordo and Trevor dies on the sacrifice stone?!?! 
   Can't wait to try these... If you have any alternate theories I'd love to hear them.

As a reminder this post is for ideas of what you think might work, please don't post why you don't think it'll work unless you've already tested it. Lets bounce crazy ideas off each other and maybe something not so crazy will come emerge.
