I've been looking into the possibility of an alternate ending for the altruist shootout and have observed something a little odd. I have no knowledge on game files at all but read something back along about there being some reference to different endings to the shootout, which has fuelled my quest. I'd imagine most people kill everyone there and get the altruist cleared message on the screen and maybe some have not cleared them all then left the area therefore not getting the message on screen. I finished it in a different way entirely which I'd imagine the majority havent. Whether it matters or not, its probably worth mentioning just in case that I took Castro, then drunk couple followed by the other drunk dude. When the cutscene begins and T is taken to the stone, the leader says something along the lines of 'He must die to become pure' or some shit, at that point I thought maybe I should just let them kill me, so I did. The failed screen popped up and instead of pressing retry I pressed cancel. All of a sudden there was this noise, like a continuation of the noise you get when the wasted screen pops up. I have never before experienced this continuation of sound when exiting a mission, or not what I can remember anyway. What strikes me as odd is, when you complete the shootout by killing them all it doesn't autosave even if its turned on, however when I let them kill me and cancelled instead of retry and spawned at paleto hospital it autosaved instantly. Has anyone noticed this before? Could this be an alternative ending to it? I'm carrying on to the end now to see if anything happens in regards to altruists, I'm as T at the mo and haven't turned up at Ms yet and have completed all currently available random events to take to altruists, so am going to see if I can continue to take people there when the hitch lifts become available. Thought I'd just put this out there while its fresh in my mind. No I don't have pics or videos or the means to do so, but try it for yourself and see if you find it odd.
EDIT : Just to add, the drunk guy random event was classed as completed afterwards.
