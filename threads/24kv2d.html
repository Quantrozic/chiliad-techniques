[Motivation] GTA V Art Director Garbut: "There's stuff that's so obscure that most people will never see it.[...]I think everything in IV has been found, I'm not sure if the same thing will happen for 5."

Documented under:Director Mode Scene & Spawn TestingHidden Map Art & Environmental Pattern Recognition ?

I just found this interview from last year with GTA V art director Aaron Garbut, who has designed the whole GTA V world and it's secrets. Lately I've been wondering if there really can be anything more to this mistery, as the search already goes on for such a long time. But he talks explicitly about the obscurity of the hidden secrets in this game.

"There's stuff that's so obscure that most people will never see it," admits Garbut. "I love the connection that social media has given us to the people that play our games. We can see what they like and what is interesting them as it happens, and this really has helped push more of these things into the game. I think everything in IV has been found, I'm not sure if the same thing will happen for 5."

Section three on this page.

I think it's really interesting that he mentions that some stuff might never be discovered. Makes you think that the search might not be that pointless after all.
