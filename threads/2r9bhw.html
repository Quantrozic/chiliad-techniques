The UV map is still a mystery...

When people discuss the UV map, they usually brush it off as a map for collectibles and weapons, etc. Pretty much a map for cool things to find around the map, plus some tips. But this theory is wrong... Sure, it helped us find some stuff like letter scraps, alien parts, etc. but we have to remember something...

Trevor Phillips is the one who wrote the map! How would Trevor know about the letter scraps, alien parts, etc? And who was he writing this for? People say it's supposed to be for our online character, but how could it be if we can't find alien pieces in the game?

The map depicts how to get money, places to live, etc. So I kind of think this map may be for Omega. He lives in a trailer, he wants to steer away from the government, and he is involved with the aliens. Not sure what the letter scraps have to do with him, though.

But if the map isn't for him, it's possibly for Trevor himself! Maybe he was just plotting out some ideas on this map. But how would he know about the letter scraps, alien parts, etc? It makes me wonder...

If we can find out why Trevor wrote this and to whom he was writing it for, we may be able to really get somewhere in the mystery.
