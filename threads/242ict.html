Space Docker Theory

Hi all, this theory is a part of something I've been working on for a while but as I'm still on my second playthrough and don't have the Space Docker yet I was hoping enough people with enough alternate endings or playthrough types can test this. I do apologise if this has been mentioned but did a good search here and on Google but found nothing. So here it goes...

There are those of us who kind of believe ELF (Extremely Low Frequency) waves are being used by the FIB/Government for weather or even mind control and perhaps beeping the Space Docker horn will result in something... Maybe, maybe not. Upon researching the Space Docker I realised that the antenna being used are helical antenna commonly used in VHF (Very High Frequency) communication, and maybe this has something to do with disrupting the ELF signals that are being emitted by various satellites scattered around San Andreas... as I'm sure this has been realised before perhaps the reason we've yielded no results is simply because the Space Docker's circuit is not complete or just bad in general. What I mean by this is below.

Explanation...

The Space Docker's antennas are Helical antenna which are commonly used in "mobile and portable high-frequency (HF) communications" and that "In most cases, helical antennas are mounted over a ground plane." My thinking is that the Space Docker... being made basically from trash cans and wood, is not the greatest ground plane itself.
So what to do about this you may ask? My only thought is that the RUBBER (which is an electrical insulator) on the tyres(tires) must be shot-out in order for it to act as a "ground plane" more efficiently. The article on ground planes mentions "metal disks" being used as ground planes... when I think metal discs I usually think UFO's but in this case I thought of a car wheel with no tyre. But basically a solid metal connection with no electrical interruptions to the ground, like said tyre. Could the lightning bolts on the wheels be a clue?

And a further point... when we take the Space Docker to LS customs, the only mod we can make is BULLETPROOFING THE TYRES! I remember when I got my SD on my first playthrough the second thing I did (after saving it in my car hole) was to see how I could modify it! Big mistake perhaps? Could it be a reason the SD hasn't found anything?

Also to mention that ELF waves are used to communicate with Submarines below a certain depth so it could be a connection between the Space Docker and doing something with the Submersible which both Mike & Trevor can own... so it wouldn't matter if one character was killed off. Obviously the only thing here that matters is if you have an un-modded Space Docker!

For people who would like to test this - the simplest way would be to do a Quick-save sometime before blowing out your tyres, as a reload will fix them. It could be important that the tyre has to be completely off the rim, this is done by driving a short distance after shooting them or just doing some donuts.

Anyway it's just a theory, perhaps someone with more intimate knowledge of how VHF communications work can disprove it.

Good luck!

Edit: I know allot of us believe the Space Docker is NOT related to the mystery itself or just strongly feel it isn't simply because it is easily lost, or that apparently R* said that it wasn't involved (can anyone prove this?) but I think for the most people out there who can manage to get your Docker back to our garage before the temptation to attack or kill something comes along is really not that difficult... just stop for a second, deep breath and set your safest GPS route back to (may I suggest) your Grove Street garage... "your path is lit!"
If you knew for a fact that it does have something to do with this whole mystery, wouldn't you be allot more careful with it? And yes I know some have lost theirs due to system failure... may I suggest turning off your AUTO SAVE and doing a QUICK SAVE just before you meet Omega. Just incase. :)
