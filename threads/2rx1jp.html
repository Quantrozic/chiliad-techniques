The division between Last Gen and Current Gen.

▲ 0 ptsu/ConorsteveyJan 10, 201522 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Old-Gen vs. New-Gen Version Diff Analysis ?

A lot of people don't seem to take into account the fact that Rockstar is a business. This means one of their top concerns (along with customer satisfaction) is profit. Having this big mystery be teased in Last Gen is a great way to get people to keep playing the game, exploring the massive world they've created.

It's also a great way to earn money, by having Current Gen being the only way to unlock the full mystery. (Peyote Plants are an example of new things that only exist in Current Gen). A lot of posts ignore this fact, and some people even say it's "stupid" for Rockstar to only make the mystery available in Current Gen.

There may still be things to discover in either game. I'm not saying to stop searching in Last Gen, I'm just saying that Current Gen does offer new opportunities.

As a side note: There is a lot of fact-stating in terms of speculations. A lot of things in this sub are not confirmed, and should still be treated as possibilities. This distinction can help us as we progress through the search.

Happy Hunting guys, Kifflom!
