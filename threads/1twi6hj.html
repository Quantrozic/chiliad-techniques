I was thinking how it's strange that the graffiti that says "karma it's a chameleon" aren't the lyrics to the song. It's obviously a reference to the song but at no point do they say those exact words in the song. Which would mean "it's a" were put there intentionally to potentially spell something.

I ran it through an anagram solver and the top suggested solution was; "Michael's karma atone."

Thoughts? The graffiti now feels like two messages in one. Both pointing us in the direction of a karmic compass or path.
