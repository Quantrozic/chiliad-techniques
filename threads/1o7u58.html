I did a quick overlay found here and it got me wondering ...

Maybe we're not looking at things correctly. Why are we trying to overlay the mural from a "God" perspective? A normal human being cannot obtain such a view from the sky that we have in the form of the maps, not even the aircraft can go high enough.

Therefore I think the key is to take a picture of the view of the mountain from one of the the hippie camps. Does this view line up with the mural and the found "glyphs"? I think the boxes on the mural are there to represent elevation on the mountain and the lines could possibly be tunnels or other ways to maneuver on the mountain to find these glyphs
