Hippy camp/tanks "alien language" - Gibberish?

▲ 12 ptsu/RdEydFcdNov 7, 201311 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Vision-Language Model Mural Auditing

I've been trying to figure out if there's something to these symbols, and it just hasn't been going well.

I searched every symbol at symbols.com, but barring the few somewhat connected ones the wiki links to, there's not much there. I tried searching for symbols with elements that overlap and found a handful, most of which ended up either astronomy/astrology or chemistry based. It kinda lends a little credence to my notion they're atomic/molecular structures, but these elements are just pieces that, on their own, don't match perfectly, let alone when connected to other symbols (connecting them being something that doesn't seem like was done at all). I'd link, but honestly, almost all the decent ones there have been already.

Many of the symbols, and their elements, don't come up with any matches at all. This is just one source, so that may not mean a ton, but it's a pretty comprehensive source.

I even noticed some symbols that looked like the ones in-game while watching the hilarious Plinkett reviews of the Star Wars prequels, but they didn't end up matching (look up the Naboo font, if at all interested.)

I stumbled across this, though. I know something along those lines have been posted, but I can't find the thread, so I don't know if that specific pic is a repost. Anyway, there's more than a few elements in there that match the symbols on the tanks, which lends some credence to the idea they're crop circle based. Now, that's a pic of a font -- one that I can't find -- but I don't think it would be as simple as just a font-like translation. Thing is that these must be based on something, but I can't figure out what. Most major crop-circlists base their work on something more than just pretty designs, it might help if we knew what those things were. Even if we didn't get any direct matches, knowing what the constituent parts might mean could help us figure it out ourselves. This would involve sending some emails and hoping someone gets back to us, which may or may not be likely. I know someone drew out all the symbols from the tank somewhere, but I can't find it. If anyone has a copy, or knows where it is, I'd love to get my hands on it.

That said, I'm starting to think it's all just gibberish written by a bunch of dirty hippy idiots trying to sex up some aliens. Using game-world logic, it'd make sense. In a real world sense, it just doesn't seem to me like these symbols are based on anything. I could be dead wrong, but if they are, it's something at least relatively obscure. The more I dig into it, the more I think these symbols are just decorative, as even if they did represent something definitively, it seems like something that would raise more questions rather than answer any existing ones. Seems like a lot of work expected for just a clue, especially when it would have been harder to find than any of the actual eggs we've found so far. It just doesn't seem very plausible.

I'm not saying we should quit on it, only that, from the looks of things, these symbols look like if we could connect them with something, we'd only find some stuff a group of alien enthusiasts in the desert would be interested in.
