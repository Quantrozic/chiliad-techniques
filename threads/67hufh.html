The Answer Is Right In Front of Us

▲ 5 ptsu/PugslysparksApr 25, 20179 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Shared-Universe Location Geometry Correlation ?

Hey everyone, been following the mystery for quite sometime now. This isn't a theory but it's something that I find interesting. I haven't seen anyone talk about this yet either but if it has been done in the past then I'm sorry and this can be taken down.

ANYWAY I was messing around in the airport and remembered there's an airline called FlyUS. Now of course the first thing that came to mind was "the answer is right in front of US". With this thought it my head, I went to the FlyUS hangar to see if I could dig anything up. No luck. I then forgot about it and started going on a killing spree around the airport. after doing this for a while I ended up on the roof of the FlyUS hangar and tried to snipe some cops. when I zoomed in with the sniper, the first thing I saw was Devin Weston's hangar. Which is the first thing directly in front of the FlyUS hangar. With this in mind I continued scoping out his hangar when I saw toward the back of his hanger is this added on looking building and the roof of that add-on has pillars crossing through each other and a border around the roof. But the border breaks in this one spot in front of the pillars where they cross forming an X and the border breaks just perfectly enough for you to see the X. After I saw this of course the first thing I did was run to the hangar. I mean the guy was this sketchy antagonist in the story and hes rich enough to own his own hangar. what else could be in there? Right?

Inside the hangar, there are 2 blue caution lights in front of this small hall way. down the hall way is nothing but boxes up against the wall. But at the end of the hallway, the right wall has a forklift which is blocking a garage that's half way open. High enough that you can see in it and of course there's something in it but low enough to not be able to get in. Its a big rectangular box. I didn't see any symbols or anything on it but it is in the same shape as the "jetpack box" from the Altruist camp. I tried everything I could to maybe open it up more for me to get in but nothing worked. ON THE LEFT WALL is a double door that you can walk through and it leads to a small locker room that has 4 vending machines on one wall and a row of lockers on the opposite wall. At the end of the locker room is yet another door that you can walk through that leads you to like a small back room/supply room that doesn't really have anything in it. There's a door in that room as well but it just leads outside to the opposite side of where you first entered.

Also on the very back of the hangar is "FlyUS" painted along the wall all blown up but looking in that direction leads to the ocean. I tried diving around in that general location too but so far nothing.

I didn't 100% the game or anything so if this is actually something and not just toast, that might be why I haven't found anything else while in there. I just wanted to get this out.
