Ft. Zancudo oddities and explanation - exploding radar dishes and DLC pointers on the mural - VIDEO

Ok, so I've been reading here for a while and finally have something to contribute, though it may already have been found/noticed by some people.

Here's the video (sorry for the iPhone camera quality, I don't have an Elgato): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qUFb8eMdC6M

Obviously, cheats were used (Invincibility and Bang bang!), but that illustrates the point. Explosions from Bang bang! occur normally on static geometry like buildings, walls etc. but when shot at NPCs, cops, cars... anything destructible, the explosion relocates to the base of the object. You can try this yourself quite easily.

The video shows two radar dishes in Zancudo. One appears static (on the left in the video) and bullets shot at it explode normally at the aim point. The second dish is non-static, and explosions are relocated to the bottom of the object.

Now, this pretty much confirms to me that there was originally a Zancudo assault (Take Over) that was either cut permanently or cut for DLC. The interiors, non-static radar dishes, hangar doors that should open... etc. all point to an assault on Zancudo to recover something.

I think it would be quite interesting for people to explore using Bang bang! to see if there are other non-static geometry that might relate in someway to this mystery.

Relating this back to the mystery, something else occurs to me that I haven't seen here. The FIB logos all over the 'UFOs' and the hidden texture under Zancudo clearly relate that these are not UFOs, but Drones. There is content in the game relating that Zancudo may soon be expanded to include a drone base (see here in the trivia section): "An internet article from Weazel News after the mission Blitz Play, reportes that Fort Zancudo has plans of opening a drone base near the fort. The article points that the base is going to bring needed jobs to the area and that they are also probably going change the law to enable the drones to help fight the menace of illegal aliens."

I'd wager this indicates that the mural is a description of the main game and DLC. On the left, we have the UFO, found at the end of the main game. On the right, we have the jetpack, part of the SP DLC. Then in the middle we have the cracked/cracking egg, the final DLC. Alien invasion, Godzilla... whatever... It looks like a simple flow chart, with the main game (complete with DLC included) up top, then it's broken down into it's individual pieces (the DLC).

Below lies speculation on the DLC.

From this, I can only see Zancudo and the jetpack/alien egg/drone/UFO as being a part of the DLC. The San Andreas missions involving the jetpack were missions that involved an assault on a military base to recover some technology or documents, and I can only see this coming content as an homage to that. But it would be much more complex. The drones are clearly FIB, and we have connections to that through Michael, and an interest in keeping them down from Trevor. We have the alien egg, something hidden in Zancudo that can be found when it is revealed that the technology is reverse-engineered from some alien tech. We have the HUD overlay, as well. The Hydra was located in the military base in San Andreas, and there's no doubt that we will see a Hydra at some point in upcoming DLC (it was in the trailers before release, AFAIRemember).

The assault is nearly a definite for me. The interiors clearly show that our story would involve the take over of the radar tower (as option A) or the destruction of the radar dishes (option B). From there, the object would be stolen by Titan, with the jet pursuers rendered useless from the destruction of the radar. Either that, or the object is to steal a drone. In the base, I'd imagine that the jetpack would be found, along with the alien egg.

The drones play a pivotal role in the next section of the story. They'd effectively shut down TPI's smuggling, Michael and Franklin's criminal enterprises and the bent FIB agent's ability to deal secretly. They also are a huge cultural reference to an ongoing debate today, something easily poked fun at in R* fashion. They could be the basis for another heist (Zancudo) and form a great part of the DLC story.

Small rant

Just remember, you won't find a jetpack yet. It's not even in the game files. It appeared as a mention in 1.12 and 1.13 and then was removed when discovered in 1.14. This can be found here. While the alien egg may be in the shipped game (there were some photos a while ago), after all the searching and map glitching and code hunts, it should have shown up within this past year. It's not accessible.

Basically, my point is to give up and wait for the DLC. The jetpack doesn't exist, the alien egg remains unfound by players and code readers, and there's nothing left to find. The easter egg of the UFO was the mystery, it's just the build up was so complex and the solution so simple. 3AM, 100% and raining. As this community has died down from the quality posts in the beginning to the random, unfathomable and untestable 'theories' that are out now, I think most users accept this.
