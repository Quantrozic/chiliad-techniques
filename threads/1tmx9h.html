EDIT: Here's my findings after investigating Yoga.

You have to tap your foot along with the yoga music, 4/4 time. The rhythm is easy to follow at first, but gets more difficult as the session goes on because you don't always have an instrument playing on every beat.

"1 - and - 2 - and - 3 - and - 4"

you inhale on the 1.

the circles should be full on the 3.

exhale on the 4.

I think the better you do, the smoother and quieter the breathing sound. the timing of it must be in the tenth of a second range or better.

AAAAAGH - awful

hiss - average

whoooh - good

If you do really good you might get a rain shower shortly afterwards.

Dr Ray DeAngelo Harris recommends you do Yoga in the morning, before anything else. 6am would be good to meet the morning sun - wake him up for the day! Ray also reports that it is "how you cleanse yourself"... for accidentally killing innocents I think.
