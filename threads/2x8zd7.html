Greek Stuff and the Galileo House signs.

▲ 1 ptsu/TehSecretHunterFeb 26, 201519 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Anagram and Constraint Word-Solving

After doing some research, I realized that a lot of mystery related things represent something in Greek.

  • The prefix Chilia in the word Chiliad is Greek.
  • We obviously have Alpha, Omega, Psi, Chi, and Tau which are letters in the Greek alphabet and also are in the Epsilon tracts.
  • The Greek word Μίασμα meaning pollution, translated to English is Miasma.
  • Arcadia is a regional unit of Greece. This word is very similar to Arcadius (The Arcadius Building) who was emperor of the eastern Roman Empire.

On the Galileo House, there are signs of different companies who use offices there. Each of these companies have an odd name and meaning. Isomorph, Queefstat, Miasma, Flojoscope, and Thermoskite.

  • Isomorph is short for isomorphism which is a Greek mathematical term talking about a morphism that admits an inverse. I'm gonna leave that one there as an do not want to get too technical.
  • I couldn't find much on a Queefstat but apparently it is some kind of medical instrument.
  • As I said above, Miasma is a Greek word meaning pollution.
  • Once again, I could find no meaning at all for Flojoscope or Thermoskite.

Putting them into an anagram solver?

  • Not a lot here, but a 9 letter anagram for Thermoskite is meteorist.

Something I was thinking was about how anagrams that don't use up all the letters from the original word get downvoted into oblivion. Maybe there is a specific number of letters we need to solve for.

  • 8 Is just infinity stood up.
  • 9 is God.

I will add more as I find out more.
