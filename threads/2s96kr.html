Is the Hippy Camp an elaborate sundial?

▲ 14 ptsu/theglugJan 13, 201511 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Sun-Shadow Azimuth Triangulation ?

Hi, fellow hunters! I urge you all to travel to the Hippy Camp and spend time observing the shadows on the mound.

At certain times of the day, the telephone pole shadows line up with the boxes in the mound, almost resembling a vertical sundial.

This could be telling us specific times to do specific things, to unlock each of the boxes...although I'm not sure which.

And one final thought...

The dead tree in the Hippy Camp has bothered me forever. What purpose does it serve? Well...it might be Jesus toast, but if you look at the mound through the branches in the tree, the lines of the branches divide up the mound similar to how the mural is depicted. It's a stretch, but I wonder if there's a connection there.
