Probably a dumb question, but does the sun move on the same path every day across the sky, or does it vary?

Documented under:Moon-Phase Condition Testing

I have this question because I'm trying see if I can catch the sun coming over the back side of chiliad to try to match one of the paintings in the little cave under the altruist camp.

Edit: After a couple of googles, apparently not. The sun is on a fixed track, the vector of the sun always follows exactly the same path every day, but the moon does change.
