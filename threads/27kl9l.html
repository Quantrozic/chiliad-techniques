In December of last year, I was playing around one of the waterfalls in Tongva Valley before the bridge, in a small pool of water that leads to another waterfall (links to exact location below). Near the base of the fall, after surfacing for air, an Eye Glyph appeared in the ripples of water and pulsed.

I tried to recreate this effect in different water locations across the map, checking to see if this was a stock occurrence or my eyes playing tricks on me... nope, only happens here in Tongva Valley.

Posted at gtaforums about this and it fell on deaf ears (as far as I could tell, no one even went to investigate this for themselves, just brushed my finding to the side).

Since then, I have gone back and fooled around some more and today noticed something pretty interesting… there are Eye Glyph and Jetpack symbols EVERYWHERE

It’s like where’s waldo… how many eye & stick figures with jetpacks can you see in this image alone?
