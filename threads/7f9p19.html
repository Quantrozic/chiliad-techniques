When you sleep with Ursula, time usually advances 7-8 hours and it's reliable, so take her home at say 02:01am, time will advance to 10:00am or say take her home at 02:45am and time will still advance to 10:00am after sleeping with her as it advances 7 hours and then rounds up the rest so you'll exit Ursula's house at the beginning of the hour.

But I found whilst testing that you can also advance time by sleeping with Ursula by 31-32 hours too (That's the 7-8 hours and another 24/day) and at first I thought it was simply just a glitch type thing but after testing it I'm not sure it is at all, so that leaves the question, why is it there/why does it do it??

To create this=Pick up Ursula, drive her home for a booty call and arrive between 08:00am and 16:00pm, then when the cut scene happens afterwards, it shows your character leaving the house and it pans down to show your character standing outside, press the X button to skip the rest of the scene and bam it's advanced 31-32 hours!?!

If you press the X button to skip the whole cut scene this doesn't work, if you don't press the X button and watch all of the cut scene then this doesn't work, but if you do it as your character leaves, it works every single time.

I tested each hour (All 24) and it only seems to happen if you drop off/arrive at Ursula's between 08:00am and 16:00pm for the booty call.

I tested trying it on different days, weather conditions (Rain/No rain), Trevor and Franklin, their cars and the SD, before/after 100 percent, 1st-4th date with Ursula, half health and more and the only conditions to this seem to be the timing (Arriving at her house) and when you press the X button, which is a long window of about 3-5 seconds so easy to hit every time.

I really, really don't think this is just a glitch or why would it only happen between 8 hours of the day and not the rest? Also the latest you can drop Ursula off and have this happen is 15:59 which means you leave Ursula's at 23:00 right but if you do it later, like 16:45 it won't work as you'll leave Ursula's at midnight/00:00am the next day so again why such a neat cut off point if a glitch? Adding to this it only happens when you press the X button to skip part of the cut scene and not all of it for that matter, it's strange and this seems all to be deliberate to me.
