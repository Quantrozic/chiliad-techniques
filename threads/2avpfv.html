Some notes regarding the odd geological structures under the sea

▲ 6 ptsu/ArchieDuke1914Jul 16, 201417 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Geological / Topographical Realism Analysis

Right, here are the basics; the San Andrean landmass is about 640 feet high (that is, the edges of the main landmass rise up 640 feet above the bottom of the ocean, on average). The mainland starts about 150 feet above the edge of the landmass. That's all fairly normal.
There are some strange rock structures that litter the Eastern coast and the coast around Zancudo (i.e to the west), and a little bit of the Paleto coast, though not much. These are enormous protrusions of rock that come up from the landmass but do not rise above the surface of the water. Many variations of the map, including the map you see when you go into the pause menu of the game, show the depth of the water, and looking along the Zancudo and Eastern coasts, you can see these rocky protrusions indicated by lighter areas in the water. With a bit of submarine-ing, using its sonar depth-detection device to measure, I worked out that the protrusions along the East coast, i.e near Davis Quartz and Humane labs, are on average 365 feet high, relative to the sea floor around them. That's incredibly high; that's about the same depth as you have to travel to find the underwater UFO, just to give you an idea. Around Zancudo, the protrusions are more frequent and only rise about 110-120 feet above the sea floor around them. They're a very odd shape, particularly around the east coast; just big lumps rather than sloping hills. These might have something to do with the suggestion that Chiliad was once a volcano (as implied by Merle Abrahams' message, 'where water meets land and fire once spewed forth' - this refers to the northern Paleto coast, just off Mt. Chiliad), so do you think these structures could be to do with that, or something stranger? I'm no geologist, so I don't know whether this is normal or not, but it's as though the island is surrounded by a strange barrier of the things. Perhaps I'm reading too much into this, but it seems odd to me.
