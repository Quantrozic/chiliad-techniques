Was doing some research on the Wow! Signal today when I came across the idea that it may have been an earth-bound signal that was bounced off of a lighthouse, so I decided to search both in google and this paper came up which states quite the opposite, saying it in fact could have come from an extra-terrestrial type 'lighthouse'. The paper is from June 2010, but have found other references mentioning this idea from well before 2010. Either way, we all know that Rockstar has pointed us to the Lighthouse many times in the Epsilon Promo pictures and we also know the Wow! Signal is in game atleast 2 times, once on the Hippy Mound, and again in the sand not to far from there. Thought some people might find this interesting and helpful, maybe give them some new ideas. Here is the link to the Paper - http://www.seti.org.au/The%20Wow%20signal%20as%20an%20interstellar%20lighthouse%20_20100628_.pdf

Edit 1 - To anyone who is unfamiliar with the Wow! Signal, it is what the 6EQUJ5 on the Hippy Mound is referencing.
