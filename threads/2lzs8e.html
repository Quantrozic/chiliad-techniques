The Last One never actually happens?

▲ 1 ptsu/_KrantNov 11, 201414 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Manhunt-Universe Easter-Egg Cross-ReferenceSan Andreas Legacy Myth Regression Testing ?

I've discovered something that now leads me to believe that this mission never actually occurs and is just an illusion or a hallucination by Franklin.

The Sasquatch Hunter who gives you this mission is the great-grandson of the Sasquatch Hunter from Red Dead Redemption, this is a fact since he says so and he is also dressed the same. As we all know, this entire mission is a reference to the Bigfoot mission in Red Dead, which itself is actually a reference to the Bigfoot myth in San Andreas. An Easter Egg within an Easter Egg I guess?

Anyway, the events in Undead Nightmare aren't canon and never actually occur. This is what leads me to the idea that The Last One never happens either. Simply because of the existence of the Sasquatch Hunter character. Like I said it could just be a hallucination, perhaps from the weed Franklin smoked in his Grass Roots mission, which if you recall seemed to have no effect on him. Could this also be the reason he can talk to that dog? If that's the case then the dog isn't real either.

My main question is why is this mission even in the game? If the Sasquatch Hunter is indeed a real character outside of these illusions then wouldn't that be a true link between the Red Dead and GTAV universes? Perhaps a missing link?

Then there is this:

"Although no definitive proof of Bigfoot's existence was ever produced and Rockstar Games eventually confirmed that Bigfoot did not exist in the game. However, they hinted something was there, which fueled many gamers to continue the search. However, the release of modifications made the search near-impossible, as anything considered "evidence" of his existence was most likely taken out of a modified version of the game."

Something is in the game, just not Bigfoot? Was this something ever officially found?

Finally this could just be a bunch of nothing and the entire mission is a Rockstar Easter Egg because some believe that the man wearing the Bigfoot suit is Jimmy from Bully.
