I used to post here (Smellslikearmpit/GambitPS) when I played on the PS3. Recently got the game again on PS4 and was testing a few things that I found on the PS3, to see if they worked on the PS4 version.

If you play or replay the mission Friends Reunited, it storms during that mission. The storm seems to last forever and doesn’t seem to stop until you go to the biker camp and do the mission. On PS3, I noticed that when you drive to, then away from the Altruist camp during that mission, it would stop the storm (There is a “bubble” around the Altruist camp, if you drive into, then outside of that “bubble”, it’ll stop the storm).

So, I just got to that mission on PS4 and tested it again to see if it still worked and it does, but I just noticed something else too that I must have missed before, it doesn’t just stop the storm, it also turns the weather to the sunny symbol (When you look at the weather on your phone). I tested it 10-20 times, the same thing, the sunny symbol.

The Altruist camp is covered in “Sun” symbols, they are everywhere in the camp, also, one says “Sun of God! Our path is lit” (Not my link)=

http://4.bp.blogspot.com/-X1EzFobkjqc/U3rmrUuooII/AAAAAAAAAHI/D30jwb3-EeI/s1600/\_03\_altrusits-camp-glyphs.png

The “O” in “Our path is lit” is a sun symbol. So is this what “SUN of God!”, “Our path is lit” could mean then, as their path can turn the weather sunny?? I uploaded a clip on YouTube to show what happens. In the clip, I’m on the mission, haven’t gone to the biker camp yet, just went straight to the Altruist camp where I sat just inside the “bubble” around the camp, then I drive just outside the “bubble” (The 2 rocks I drive between are the edge of the “bubble” around the Altruist camp) and show the weather change within 1 in game hour.

It could make sense, our path is lit just means they can make it sunny, not sure what the exact conditions are yet as no point in me testing (Is it because Wade is with you, can it be any passenger, does it work with Mike/Frank, does it work only on storms, or any weather, or just that mission, how long does the sun last, etc), I have to complete the game again first ffs (I didn’t think of that when buying it again), but when I 100 percent it, I’ll check it out/test properly.
