Kifflom, fellow hunters. I'm fairly new to the hunt and I got to say, the amount of research and theories provided by this community is damn impressive! I have read up on a lot of things and find numerous theories quite interesting. My focus lies mainly on trying to fuse the multiple theories in one that can make sense of all the clues spread in all layers of the game.
Anyway, the theory I like to present is not a new one but rather an alternative to the existing "Mural represents characters"-theory.
For those who are still reading, thank you and sorry for making this a huge post. So...What if the cracked egg represents Mike? It makes sense to me, seeing as Mikey cracked on multiple occasions in more ways than one and he is the one who brought T and F together. So, the mural originates from the egg, connecting T( the UFO) with F(Jetpack). Before you guys start saying "No, it's the other way around!", hear me out. T could be the UFO because he got alienated from M, believing he was dead and also from Brad, who turned out to be the dead one. Is it also possible that T is originally from North Yankton and had to leave his old life behind because of the failed depot job? And F is the perfect example of social elevation, in a way. From a black dude in "da hood", repoing cars for some evil, Slavic fraud to a black (ex-)crook with a big ass house on the hills and enough money to retire. It's pretty "out there" but not impossible. As for the X' , perhaps they suggest specific times to visit Friedlander, which might tie in with the popular karma theory. Going to Friedlander after a specific "sin"(heist, perhaps?), wipes the karma-slate clean, kinda like a priest. I have not speculated any further on this because I would like the community's input on this. I am not conviced this is any sort of answer to the mystery but like I said earlier, I'm trying to tie the theory-ropes together. Once again, sorry for the long post, hope it was at least a little interesting. Kraff be praised!
