New Generation Players

With the release of GTAV on next generation machines we have a whole crew of new hunters on the site (as well as returning hunters) who are taking a run at mystery on the new console.

We should capitalize on this opportunity. We have the opportunity to push through many different play styles, orders of missions, hypothesis testing etc.

I wanted a thread where people could post different recommendations on the above along with helpful save points throughout the game (e.g. save before each heist, have a save before Michael is abducted the first time). We will likely not get another boost in interest again until GTAV comes out on PC so let's utilize this opportunity while it is upon us [granted I'm a few weeks late to getting this post out]

I have just begun my second play through and am focused on strategic mission saves surrounding when I complete all collectables etc.
