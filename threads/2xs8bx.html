Mt Gordo yoga

I'm positive that yoga on Mt Gordo will trigger something, but what? And how? Firstly I find it very odd that Mt Gordo is the only place you can do yoga other than michaels house. Yoga has a huge link to the alien camp, there are people doing yoga just above the rain glyph. What's even more interesting is the clues in michaels house. Firstly there are the stairway clues. In the morning, sunlight shines through the window by michaels front door leaving what looks very much like a lighthouse on the floor, El Gordo lighthouse maybe?? Then there's the pictures on the wall, the first looks like water running past the bottom of a mountain, then there's a picture of trees with mountain in the background, then there's a picture of a zig-zag pathway up a mountain, very similar to the path up Mt Gordo. Is this telling us to start at the lighthouse, walk past a river at the bottom, ascend up the mountain, pass an area with trees and make your way up the zig zag path? The fact that these pictures are placed the way they are going up the stairs could mean we have to follow a certain route up the mountain. Even more interesting is the pyramid of light on the stairway wall where the sun shines through the roof window. Notice how it casts dark lines onto the pyramid which looks ever so similar to the second pose Michael does in the first yoga routine. There seems to be too many clues pointing Michael to Mt Gordo for it not to be relevant.
