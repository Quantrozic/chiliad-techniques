So I'm new to this subreddit, only just started playing the game myself (I always save myself money by buying games way after release) and to be honest I highly doubt I'll ever reach 100%. But this subreddit got my attention and now I've been thinking about this way too much.

So basically, from what I've seen of videos online of what happens when you get near the FIB UFOs, the camera starts to go glitchy and you as a character seem to get kicked off. I'm particularly keen on the glitchy camerawork part for one reason.

Is it possible that in R* doing what they do best (let's be honest, GTA is always a more hyped up version of America, the ridiculous radio stations, that woman in GTA V who is running for president on talk radio, that governor radio ad where that guy talks about how he has no experience in politics but will be 'your asshole'), that actually they're going full meta by allowing the game characters themselves to realise it's a simulation?

It sounds bizarre, but think about how much you could unlock if you could fly around the map in a spaceship, switch on and off police and NPCs at will without mods or cheats, that kind of thing.

When the characters get close to the spaceships, is that going to be like a Truman Show type deal? You're possibly getting close to 'breaking' the simulation, so that's why it wants to kick you off?

There's been a lot of talk about this in the tech community since Elon Musk and scientists got together a few years ago to say that we are 'most likely' in a simulation, so what if R* went the whole hog and incorporated this into the game?
