New theory and possible lead.

In the prologue of the game there is a room that has been modeled with an interior that has yet to be seen, it also has a animated door behind the static mesh.

The door I'm talking about is their surveillance door.

Why I think it matters: at first I didn't think it mattered at all, but after playing around as Trevor after he shoots the security guard I decided to go over and check out this room that on the map shows a interior that we can't access. The first thought this was just a room to hold the model in the game prior to the guard taking control of Michael after he comes out of the vault. It seemed like an easy explanation so I just stuck with that however while screwing around today after Trevor shoots the guard in the head and Michael says it didn't have to go down like that I went to the door and while aiming down sites, I started hitting the B button on Xbox One and I could hear a door opening as my body clipped through the door frame to throw a elbow attack.

My new theory: in the opening prologue you need to destroy all of the surveillance cameras before collecting the money, this will then Force the security guard inside of the surveillance room to come out of the surveillance room.

How could this have been missed for so long? Well I've actually tried to do this multiple times just on a whim, and there is one camera inside of the vault in the back left hand corner that has an invisible box around it and you actually have to get quite close to it in order to actually shoot it. You have to get kind of get underneath it. This poses an actual challenge because in order to get close enough to shoot the camera you also have to get close enough to the trigger that forces you into the cutscene where Michael and Trevor start taking money.

Couple tries and a couple fails. But I feel if a lot of people try it maybe someone will get into the vault destroy both cameras and back out without triggering the cash grab cutscene which resets the entire environment.

Also someone with PC could possibly wall breach the room prior to the cash grab and see what is inside?
