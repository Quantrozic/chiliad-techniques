I've encountered some mystery dialogue a couple of times in Online. The first time was when I was looking for the serial killer clues to unlock the Navy revolver. I got the dialogue a second time after I'd killed the killer. It was something to the effect of "Is anyone there?" or "Can someone help me?"

Unfortunately, I don't have any clips or proof of this, nor do I know exactly where it happened. What I do know is that they both happened in the same area, which was a hilly area in the northern part of the map, and also off to the west. Not extremely north or west, though, certainly well south of Paleto.

I looked it up and it didn't find any results. I tried to find it again, but I haven't been successful yet.

It only just occurred to me to post it here since someone might know something about it. Thanks

Edited to add: Both times I looked around and there was no-one around and nothing out of the ordinary
