Credit goes to Antasurris from GTAForums. Here's a short summary:

The Zancudo Alien facility interior is way closer to an unfinished mockup than an actual finished location ready for a mission.

https://youtu.be/iNZuTdoZ1AU

Realistically, only the main corridor and the dissection lab have any real level of detail. The rest looks more like a prototype that probably got abandoned after a few tests.

https://imgur.com/a/jOFApJT

There’s no sign that this interior was ever meant to appear in a playable mission in this state.
