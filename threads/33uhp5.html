Studios are always rushed to release on time (Look at Bethesda with Skyrim, rushing for the 11.11.11 slot.) and this more often than not means missions or sections are left out, and never really placed in a patch later on (Again with Skyrim, look at the College of Winterhold questline, cut way too early when there were more ambitious plans for it.)

I'm willing to put money on it that this is what happened with this. I mean, I know it's Rockstar and they're probably the best studio out there for Quality/Quantity > Release Date, but everyone has their limits.
