Ok so as you know the new mural has a lot of stuff hidden inside it... A LOT. Some of which has been solved, like the big foot stuff. Some of which hasn't. One of those things is on the "alien/ufo" yellow tab. The original image can be found here:

New mural

Now on this pic is a wave form. As an indie game dev myself, I wanted to see what this was. So, I took the image, adjusted it, and levelled it out, to get this:

IMAGE

Now sadly boys and girls this is a low resolution image. Also, the demo of the programme cuts out every so often. But, using the above, I managed to get the following sound file:

WAVE
WAVE2

I've uploaded two, because you get slightly different results depending on how you save the original image.

Now, from what I can tell, its the same sound repeated twice. Also, if you listen, you can make out some sort of speech. So I think the key is to getting a good version of this, and then changing pitch, speed etc, until you can hear whats being said. ATM, it could all just be like those weird blokes of Ghost Hunters who hear a noise and insist its somebody taking a dump in a haunted oven.

Anyway, hope this at least points people in some sort of direction for a possible new clue.
