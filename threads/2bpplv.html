Hello all,

I know the general sentiment towards Karma Theory, and posts that essentially don't bring any new information to light, but I can't resist posting my thoughts as of now.

Followed the mystery since "OMFGWHAT'SINTHESHED"
Beaten the main campaign three times now.
Working on my fourth playthrough. (My first 100%)

For starters, I had three goals when I booted up this fresh playthrough-

  1. Kill zero (0) police/NOOSE officers w/M... (IAA Agents excluded)
  2. Mind all Heist decisions in relations to the "too-obvious-to-be-coincidental" X's on the planning boards.
  3. Ultimately follow where the game seemed to be leading me at any given time, making choices based on where it seemed R* was leading us, with the end-goal in mind of killing Trevor.

I've been taking notes after every mission, and also on anything else that seemed to be worthy of note... instances of occult/illuminati/conspiracy references, subtle character and story details that weren't wholly evident/obvious on previous playthroughs, etc.

Currently at ~48%, just scouted out Paleto Bay Bank, and have now taken a break from the main story to do all the tedious boring shit... space-ship parts, letter scraps, mini-games and activities.

HERE ARE SOME THINGS I'VE DONE/NOTICED--

  1. In the opening mission, I killed all police w/T. Why would M kill local PD when working with the Federal Government? The foreshadowing in this opening mission in pretty fucking blatant, although you'd never notice if you weren't already familiar with the story... T: "There's enough $ here for all of us!" M: "Depends on how you look at it." --- T: "Come on! There'll be time for grieving later!" M: "Oh, you got that right!" --- Clearly, Dave Norton's first bullet was meant for T, not for Brad (who crosses in front of T a moment before the first shot.) Also, M is presumably wearing a vest so as to take a shot himself and survive... hence why in the loading-screen images he is pulling his shirt to the side to reveal one.
  2. Almost immediately after tuning in to the radio during "Franklin & Lamar" we hear about the FIB/IAA funding war on the radio. This rivalry is pretty much the source of all primary conflict in the game world. Two towers right next to each other...

Also, the "We Come in Peace" gold-medal requirement is another "too-obvious-to-not-be-related" moment.

  1. In "Repossession," the homeless guy in the first garage you pass calls F&L Zappo (Zapho?) agents. I know people have gone heavy in depth about the two feuding religious entities in the game... I'm assuming this is one, as there are several other mentions to this (mostly by crazy homeless peds, but still.)
  2. Literally every mission has some impact on the world... instantly after completion our actions are being discussed on Bleeter, LifeInvader, Weazl, etc. I've been keeping an eye out for anything of worth, but to no avail yet. Still... very likely there could be some clues hidden within.
  3. During M's "Grass Roots" mission, there is very warped audio. I know we have some transcripts somewhere of the dialogue that transpires later during "Did Somebody Say Yoga" between the four "aliens," but I cannot recall anybody looking into hidden audio during this. Also-- when F returns later, Barry says "Don't you see the aliens?" ... yet F sees nothing, and T sees clowns...

When I returned to M's house immediately after completing "Grass Roots" there was ambient soundtrack music playing... I was a little drunk when I wrote this note, but I described it as "Indian/YOGA" music.

OKAY-- KARMA--

I know everyone hates Karma Theory, but hear me out.
I think it's a lot simpler than we're making it. Obviously this is GTA. It's fucking IMPOSSIBLE to play through the game without killing anyone. It's REALLY EASY to play through the game only killing when prompted. Friedlander's psych reports should be our biggest clue here. The game is constantly doing checks, monitoring you, the player...

There are SOOOOO many decisions to be made. I don't think the answer lies in a single, rigid structured playthrough... but instead, simply consistency.

Think about this, everyone says "millions of people bought the game, we should've found the answer if there was one by now." Yet how many of the people who bought this game actually completed the main story? How many of those people then went on to get 100%?

I decided to do my playthrough focusing on M, killing T, as it seems like this was always M's goal... get rid of him. Not killing cops because he's a fucking Narc... hoping that siding with the government and helping them would ultimately unlock a secret tied to the UFO.

Reverse... I think someone might want to try a playthrough fucking M over as T... possibly this path leads to the jetpack.
