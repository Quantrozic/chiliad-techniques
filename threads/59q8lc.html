So, San Fierro and Las Venturas. Sure sounds like a backstory location for Trevor, filling in the nine years before his reacquaintance with Michael. North Yankton and Liberty City? Sure sounds like a way to tell the story of Michael entering witness protection and having to flee the city under the pursuit of various government organizations.

These are going to be full price paid expansions to GTA Online. Rockstar is happy to charge $60 for the best single player experience ever made and GTA: Online San Andreas, so if GTA Online Liberty City and San Fierro are both going to be worth $60, they're also going to come with single player stories. It's the reason GTA made the billions it has and why it continues to grow online. The immersion the single player experience crafts for the player allows them to full engage online where there are few cohesive stories to introduce and immerse the player. They know this, they made a billion dollars from it and allowed GTA Online to pay for itself with micro transactions and be profitable enough to sustain four years of development on RDR2 and presumably GTA VI and these expansions. There are two, notably, like there were for GTA IV. Rockstar has established patterns of success that they follow.

There's no doubt in my mind that, should this rumor be true, we're also going to get single player stories, two brand new ones. I'd wager they are for Trevor as he meets Ron, Chef and Wade, or perhaps its the story of Ron, Chef and Wade as they meet Trevor. Either way, considering they'll be part of the same game, I'm a hundred percent sure that they are going to be mystery related, and likely contain more mysteries themselves. The article discusses how R* had originally planned these expansions for single player, but delayed them to incorporate them into GTA Online due to the success there. We can read that as R* delaying SP DLC because online is more important, or we might look at it as R* making sure that when the single player DLC comes out, all of the online players aren't enraged that it took so long and they don't get the massive expansion. The same perception that R* is abandoning one group for another lead them to delay the product for both to make sure neither is left out. It's quite nice of them.

Still, I just wanted to let you guys know. I stay off the GTAV sub, so I didn't learn about this until today. Any thoughts?

My Take

This might be very disappointing for this sub. It likely means that the mystery was meant to be solved in three pieces, the UFO and aliens/abductions in Yankton, the Egg in San Andreas and the jetpack in San Fierro. The entirety of the mystery was likely split into these three components of GTA V, Los Santos, Yankton and San Fierro. This does mean, though, that the egg is in the current version of GTA V. I'm sure of that. The hunt is still on, in fact it's even better than before because there's two brand new chapters coming to it soon.

The fact that it was likely delayed also explains why new mysteries have been popping up here. They are our DLC. The Peyotes, Sasquatch, The Beast.. those have been the Bikers DLC for Online that keeps people playing there, waiting for expansions like we are. This is really, really good evidence that single player DLC is on it's way, and the mystery will continue, with three more years of layers and intrigue added, specifically because they have been paying attention to this community and this hunt exclusively in their development of single player. In addition, obviously, to the story they want to tell. This community has shown them what the core of GTA V is about, the immersion, realism and connections in the world and to ours, and they've been watching and giving us DLC to satisfy that. The resolution to the mystery in the single player DLC will only be the more satisfying for it.

Also, as I said, there's still an easter egg in GTA V Los Santos. It's the Egg. Literally, the mural points down from the visible UFO to the Alien Egg that is still in the game. Where? What triggers it? Who knows. But that's part one, with two parts yet to come, the UFO and the jetpack.

So, who's up to make an omelette and finally crack the Egg? What do you think, of both my analysis and the possibility of finding the alien egg?
