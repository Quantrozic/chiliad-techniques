Concerning the Maze Bank and possible hypotheses

▲ 6 ptsu/laikamonkeyMay 6, 201516 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Hidden Map Art & Environmental Pattern Recognition ?

Those lines on the fountain and around the Maze Bank seem oddly familiar with the ones present at the mural. (side by side comparison in image above)

I did a reckon around the building and noticed that the Maze bank logo is stamped 4 times around the building and one big 3D logo right on the bottom of the stairs, near the main entrance and the so called 'egg of life'. So that makes 5 logos, just like the five tick boxes on the mural (although I admit that is also kinda over stretching it).

Also the red line goes around the building and it does seem to have a particular pattern on the back part of the building, but on the front, near the staircase and the fountains, it's chaotic, all lines are leading to different places, and some of them even intertwine and go around the fountains.

I just think that if we want to place the Maze Bank as a center piece of this mystery, it shouldn't be with an argument stating the fountain looks like an egg or that the line signifies the crack mark on the mural egg. That just seems really stretching too far, I mean I know about the Eagle and the Nest, but that doesn't automatically tell us the egg is the fountain.

Just food for thought, I know that the fountain has been considered to be the egg for some time now, but it's about time that someone more expert/dedicated than me on the matter decides to meticulously explore any options of pattern in that 'Maze' around the Maze Building.

I read some comments around the subreddit and it looks like some of you actually tried similar approaches. Can you post your findings? Or lack thereof?
