When I look at the satellite map, I see quite a lot of relief in the ocean off the coast of the docks and airport, extending to a point square with the far-west and far-south of the landmass. That area on gta-5-map.com (visit it, and click to the Satellite mode to see the corals)

But there are no collectibles in those regions, according to that same site. Has anyone bothered to explore further out than is required to collect all the bits?

I'm also curious about the reservoir in the Tataviam Mountains: its analog in real life, Morris Reservoir was used as a weapons test facility. As far as I can tell, no freshwater is connected to it so it wouldn't be explorable underwater without using Cargobob to drop the Submersible there - has anyone explored it? (I haven't even come across Cargobob yet)

(Sorry if the answer to both of these is yes!)

I feel like Rockstar might give clues in what they decide to call stuff - GTA IV used loads of Native American and foreign names e.g. Algonquin for Manhattan, Hove for Brighton, etc. I looked up Tataviam and it mentions the Chumash were their neighbours, and they occupied an area near Palm Springs. According to GTA Wiki, the real life Palm Springs is represented by Pala Springs, where the Aerial Tramway is... although parts of that article are more than a bit confusing:

In the peak of Mount Chiliad, there is a shed in which Pala Springs operates.
