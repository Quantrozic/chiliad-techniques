We need to stop ruling out things based on platform or version of the game.

▲ 6 ptsu/DU_HA55TJan 9, 201724 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Cross-Platform Build Comparison ?Old-Gen vs. New-Gen Version Diff Analysis ?Shared-Engine Behavior ComparisonTrainer-Based Weather Cycling ?

There are a few ways to look at this. Firstly I ask how do you know that the mystery is contained to the OG version of the game? Just because it started there doesn't mean it will end there. The OG consoles have all but been abandoned, yet with each new update on NG there are clues, winks and nudges from the devs, i.e. the Alien Invasion mural for your custom auto garage, the Sniper skin with the UFO above Chiliad, etc. They have even gone so far as to add additional steps to existing mysteries, i.e. Bigfoot peyote and the Teen-Wolf hunt. Hell, clues in plain text in the game's update files have been found, but only on the NG versions.

The best reasoning I see behind culling the NG versions of the game is because the "mystery started here so it must contain the solution, too." I see where you're coming from but its nothing more than an assumption and I think it needs to be treated as such. It's possible for Rockstar to get a ping when some requirements are met, triggering an update to the game. Hell, it doesn't even have to update the game, a sever could send a ping back to you unlocking the event trigger. I'm not saying that the OG version of the game is useless, or anything like that, including that the mystery can't be solved there. I'm just saying we need to use all of the tools we have to the fullest extent, rather than throwing away a ton of potential leads. There is a new perspective, first-person. There are new vehicles. There is a new director mode. There are tons of things that can help us to the common goal.

All in all, I think its willfully negligent to ignore the new platforms and what they bring to the table or rule out things that are specific to them. It is a restriction you, the player, are putting on yourself, when nothing proves that it is the case. So many promising leads are kicked to the curb because a 3.5 year old version of the game that has been abandoned for a solid year doesn't react or present something the same way. You all are assuming a control when an assumed control isn't a control at all.

I'd also like to add that MP could be a possibility, however unlikely. Just think how amazing the reveal would be if you're going about your business when a massive tsunami hits, Chiliad erupts, aliens invade and it happening worldwide to anyone playing online.
