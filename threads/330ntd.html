I have a mural theory but I don't know if it's debunked, and it's mainly speculative

Ok before I start I just want to say that I'm aware of how outlandish this sounds, so I'm going to ask you to read this like it's fiction, but I have an idea about the mural and how to read it, and what it could mean...so if this is a repost then sorry, but if it's not, get your tinfoil hats on:

Ok, so we know that as far as the mural goes, that it's not a 2d image, in that the markers (x's) on the map correlate with the locations of the condition markings on chiliad (rain glyph/moon glyph etc), meaning that the mural image, if "perceived" vertically, point out the glyph locations although the glyphs are on separate sides. We know all this part, and have done for long enough, but the kicker is that if the mural is a representation of the mountain, it would put the rewards (egg/jetpack etc) underneath chiliad which is unlikely.

Now that that bits been caught up with, let's assume that instead of being one 3d image, the mural is actually 3 maps. Hear me out here:

Map 1: the glyph locations as marked around the mountain with the eye at the summit - makes sense, and I'll get to the glyphs later, but for now, map 1 covers this. Eye at the top, glyphs in the x's, cool.

Map 2 - just the grid:
For now, just so I can point out what I'm looking at, imagine the mural without the eye or mountain outline and remove the rewards boxes so all you have is the x's (it's getting really difficult to not write "Xboxes", by the way) and the paths that connect them. This gives us a kinda top down map with an entry point at each end, a long flat edge at the bottom, and the lines around the base of the mural.
This is where it gets a bit Jesus toasty, but I think that the "grid" section of the mural is a representation of the road and building layout at fort zancudo, with the x's now representing 5 triggers which would activate the lift, and the lines around the base of the mural represent the water line around the shore-side of zancudo, and the long flat edge representing the runway, or land thereabouts.

Map 3: the eye and the egg.
So let's assume that you've found and activated the triggers for the zancudo lift, which I assume can only be done while the chiliad ufo is active, and this opens the lift and takes you down into the under levels. The eye-egg line is a representation of an otherwise vertical line, viewed from the top down, with a condition marker at the top so: while "eye" ufo is up, Easter egg is available by accessing the lift after triggers activated. This lift happens to be vertically beneath another ufo that lines up with the eye ufo. More on that later.

Theory on the glyphs: these are conditions that need to be met in order for the eye ufo to appear, so night/rain/3am. This works, but doesn't explain all the glyphs. The glyphs work as condition statements and as reps of the other ufos, so the faded glyph is the sunken saucer and the glyph of the ufo and the path is a rep of the light show ufo, while the plain ufo glyph seems to just rep the hippy camp ufo in that none of the other glyphs can, because they're busy specifying the conditions for the eye ufo, whose glyph is at the top.

So my theory is as follows:
While the chiliad ufo is active, the x triggers in and around zancudo need to the activated to activate the lift. This takes you down into the base for your Easter egg, and the pathways are a map to show the route to the rewards, which makes me think that the place they'll be keeping the jetpack is the hangar at the end of the runway.
I need to jump on gimp to be able to explain it in images and ill do that later, but any thoughts?
