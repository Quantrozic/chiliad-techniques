The moon has been overlooked for a while.

▲ 14 ptsu/TheHidestHighedNov 24, 201410 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Moon-Phase Condition Testing

About 8-10 months back there was talk about the moon being key to furthering the mystery that were really solid but seemed to just peter out from lack of initiative or what have you. I would link the posts but they can be found simply by searching for "moon". Basically the gist is that there is a pretty complicated lunar cycle that is different from the one in our own world that allows for full and new moons to fall on every day of the week after certain periods of time. I feel like this is pretty key to accessing something more than the Chiliad UFO. Maybe the Chiliad UFO was just a "marker" to let us know we were headed in the right direction with the variables needed to see it (hence it being there but as a hologram and nothing tangible) and to let us know to go further; changing the location, time, weather, day of the week and the stage in the lunar cycle. That's 5 variables, 5 x's on the chiliad mural, maybe a coincidence maybe not. I don't want to be the guy to just throw out some conjecture without testing it myself but this is a big undertaking that would be better taken on by more than one person for sure considering it takes something like over 200 sleep cycles to advance through one month of in game time. I hope this made a little sense to someone else but me lol feel free to comment your thoughts and any findings!

Edit: Forgot to add that it is believed the symbols around the hippy camp may allude to the cycle of the moon.
