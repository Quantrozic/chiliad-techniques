There lies true flies in rose clouds above. In sixty scond sentiment and shotgun sudden revelations. Though stormy, half nimbus, half clawed big saucer eyes that stare down intensely from the sky, we pass on various night myths to continue on this journey through razor winter. Passing through her 3rd God eye and into her maelstrom proper, we begin the process of rebirth unto giant painbows in the cracked, slathered uranium universe of her warrior heart.

"I call you out, those keepers of secrets.
Lord prophets and keepers of lore.
I call you out that we may rest from this endless speculation.
We ask only for confirmation of our efforts and nothing more.
Oh nameless ones we, the broken hearted sea of dispassionate grey faces beg of you to shine a light once more on our darkened path.

This is our plea to you, those who Rock the stars."
