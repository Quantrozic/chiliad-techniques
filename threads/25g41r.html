Hey there.

Today I was playing in free mode and a unusually large cluster of players were congregated in the middle of the map. I decided to patrol the area in my tank (I play a Merryweather character). I arrive in the area, and before I know what's going on, explosions start errupting everywhere. They are so strong, they propel my tank back, and some of the hits propelled my tank off the ground. Before I knew it my tank exploded and it said the player (who I never saw) "knifed me". When I respawned I was quickly bombarded again with a hail of explosions. I'd seen something similar before, it's different than the airstrike... I'd suspected jet glitchers had figured out how to do these crazy multi-explosion explosion airstrikes, because I've been inexplicably killed by these explosions before around jets.

I quickly retreated the area, and went to get my buzzard. I returned to the site, and the hail of explosions began to bat my buzzard around until it crashed. I respawned, and wanted to investigate what was going by going into passive mode. I moved closer, and saw a cluster of sports cars in a densely populated street. Several players appeared to be gathered around a bounty. I went out of passive mode and used grenade launcher on the congregation. The cars exploded, I got no Poor Sport message, and four players died. The bounty, which was in the center of everyone, and who was standing on the point of impact was unscathed. ANGEL something was her PSN. She began to fire at me with her explosive machine gun. Jesus Christ as I type this I'm assuming everyone reading this can scarcely believe it. Haha. Anyway...

I ran for cover and went back into passive mode. Most of the other respawning players went into passive mode too and started to congregate around the ANGEL character. I checked the player stats and she had a KDR of 17 and a level of 300. Obviously a glitcher, but I've never or heard of a glitcher being able to summon hellfire and survive a grenade to the face. Also, while all the other four in her posse shoed up in my players' met menu, she did not for some reason.
