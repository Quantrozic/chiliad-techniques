In the mission Life Invader where you plant the bomb to blow up Norris, if you look around Lester's room there's Yeti stickers on the wall, and then when you go to the urban outfitters to get the change of clothes, there's yeti on the wall in the cutscene with the cashier, then the guy that you take a smoke break with at the Life Invader has a Yeti t-shirt on? Why?

Also I think the map in the corner by Lester's bed with the sticky notes on it and the letters, and then you see a street square with diagonal lines going through it with a dotted line going to the urban outfitters shop... I know it goes to the urban outfitters shop because that's the same place the first yellow dot takes you as soon as you leave Lester's! My question is what's the dotted line? Underground tunnel? A getaway path for some other unseen reason? And why is that square with the lines going through it connected to the dotted line? Nothing special about that block except a hospital I think, and a parking lot, I also noticed there was a power box that was open on that square...
