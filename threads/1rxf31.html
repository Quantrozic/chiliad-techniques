Go down the staircase in front of the Eagle.
http://socialclub.rockstargames.com/games/gtav/snapmatic/myphotos/photo/sMqsZZz4iki7XrRtma2isw

You will come to this corridor.
http://socialclub.rockstargames.com/games/gtav/snapmatic/myphotos/photo/QV3gLfmA50uTnh3amBz6mw

Five squares from the first door (On the right) you will notice the Char make a step up motion and the foot falls sound change from Concrete Effect to a Metal Effect from this point to the handles of the next doorway (Middle of the doors) your Char will look as though he is walking on air.

It is the width of the hallway and when you get to the middle of the doorway you will notice your Char make a step down motion where the foot falls return to the Concrete sound.

Using the Jerry Can I was able to capture its height from the Concrete.
http://socialclub.rockstargames.com/games/gtav/snapmatic/myphotos/photo/8Blz85D2h0yvAtTZCjCHHg

I have tried the following, Shoot the overheads out, Blow it up, Light it on fire, Shoot it (Multiple ways) then I noticed with a pistol the bullet impact with the Metal caused a spark, where shooting just before the Metal rise made a small crack but no spark.

Could there be something interactive within the FIB/IAA grid that activates this? Thoughts?
