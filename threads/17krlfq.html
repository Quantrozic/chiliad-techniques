This is something I've mentioned in comments before, but nobody has really taken notice.

So, the question of this mystery?.... WHERE ARE THE ALTRUIST WOMEN?

You can only find them in Director mode. As far as I know they are the only DM characters that you can't find in game.

We've all seen the posts or comments from the past where people have claimed to see women at the Altruist camp, but they never mentioned they were giant fat ladies wearing bikinis, so I'm just a little skeptical. I've never seen them in my thousands of hours of playing since launch day in 2013.

Now, the most obvious and easy answer is "cut content", and that's a fine and justified assumption at this point.

It's just something that sticks out to me. Like an overlooked clue. All the other women are in game. It's only the women Altruists that have seemingly been "removed" from the universe.

Any thoughts on this?

Right now I'd have to go with unused content, but then why leave them in game?

Also, this is just based off my knowledge of the game. I don't know everything. I could totally be overlooking something or the reasoning for this. So of anyone has an explanation or if rockstar has explained already, please inform me.

To me, it seems like a clue I'm plain sight. But it could be a nothing-burger.
