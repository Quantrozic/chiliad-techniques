The web mystery is expanding in RDR2

▲ 85 ptsu/EnergyTurtle23Dec 24, 202524 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Cross-Mystery & Inter-Game CorrelationMount Shann Comparative Survey ProtocolRAGE Format Parity Auditing Against RDR2

Not sure if anyone here is following these developments, but recently a cable mesh web was found to spawn (for only about an hour each night) on a telephone pole in St. Denis in RDR2. These webs are identical to the webs in GTAV which fueled a lot of speculation around the Chiliad Mystery. Now eight of these webs have been found in RDR2, and they form a rough circle with an obvious center if lines are drawn between them. Well 10 hours ago somebody posted that they found a much larger web hidden in the trees in the center of the circle (link in the comments), and it has an inscription that directs you to go “N lines cross” (north to a point where lines cross). Here’s the crazy part: if you follow its instructions you’ll find a telephone pole, and if you shoot the telephone pole the damage pattern will reveal a new inscription/clue that tells you to travel to the fifth telephone pole to the west. This is actively developing right now and if anyone is still holding onto hope for secrets in these two games then I recommend that you get in on the hunt! I’ve been a member of this community since the very beginning and I honestly can’t believe that this is just being found in RDR2 so many years later.
