Possible hidden Morse Code in the T01/T02 tunnel?

▲ 0 ptsu/alex_the_dogeDec 15, 201611 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:DTMF and Morse Tone DecodingMorse Code AnalysisPitch-Contour Morse Extraction

In just a couple of days I managed to collect all the 50 spaceship parts for Omega and yesterday I received the Space Docker. Today I decided to make a few experiments with that vehicle and see what happens (because it's believed that the Space Docker is somehow connected to the Jetpack).

As I reach the tunnel where the T01 and T02 doors are, I can't help but notice that the lamps above me form a suspicious sequence of ON-OFF-SOFT (with some spaces between some of them) lights from the beginning to the end of the tunnel.

I immediately realized it was a Morse Code and wrote it down on a piece of paper. It still doesn't make any sense and I'm still working on it, but, from what I saw, it appears to be some sort of text...a message, probably.

This means that, once translated, we'll have in our hands 3 Morse Codes (this one, the audio one under the ocean and the one found on an in-game website).

Did you guys already know about that or it's something new?
