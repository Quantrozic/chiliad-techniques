Billboard numbers change when you get closer

▲ 44 ptsu/cbwysicJul 28, 201621 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:DDS Mipmap and Texture Atlas Inspection ?Modular-Arithmetic Coordinate Reduction ?

I was bored and blowing things up, running from the police and they chased me down into a subway entrance where I messed around for a while looking at this texture glitch (some pics included below). I had previously been looking at the squares in the fountain reflections as a clue, but after seeing the floor in this subway entrance and how the overlaid logos and other images changed when you look around, it made me second guess the fountains as not being a glitch.

So anyway, got bored with that and started walking around. I noticed the dark lines in the parking lot just outside the subway entrance and wanted to get a better view, so I got on top of the overpass above the parking lot. While up there I noticed the billboard number was 1346, which was odd because I usually see 1944. I wanted to get a closer look and by the time I got to the edge of the overpass, the number 1346 faded out and changed to 1944. Same thing happens when you zoom with the camera from the far side.

Here's an imgur album with a screenshot of the location: https://imgur.com/a/4u7vO

I was playing as Franklin, it's a Tues at 1130ish in game, sunny skies. I am going to go check out other billboards now. I just popped out real quick to drop this post, but the billboards in the immediate area had the same effect, though one off to the side was just 2312 and didn't change.

And before anyone dismisses THIS as a texture glitch, please take a look at the imgur album which includes the billboard texture numbers. You'll notice they are simply 123456 (6 is flipped upside down to make 9) so there was definitely some intention in displaying these numbers. The ted onion graffiti is also there which has always made me believe it carries some significance. Maybe these numbers will lead us to an .onion URL where we can continue the hunt, who knows.. anyway, back to the game!
