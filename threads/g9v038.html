Los Santos 2020 (All in-game references and clues)

▲ 17 ptsu/LlamaGuy69Apr 28, 20203 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Update-Drop Timing Frequency Analysis

The Los Santos 2020 Poster is likely one of the biggest and most direct clues that Rockstar has given us regarding the Chiliad Mystery.

This poster has been in-game since the release of the original versions of GTA V in 2013 and has long been speculated to be a teaser for some kind of alien content being released in the year 2020. This kind of teaser of future content is typical of Rockstar (The "See you in Miami!" billboard in GTA 3 that teased the release of GTA: Vice City is the most well known example of this).

Back in 2013 many hunters believed this was a reference to an upcoming GTA V Alien DLC similar to Red Dead Redemption: Undead Nightmare, however it now seems more likely that this is alluding to an upcoming update for GTA Online. This is due to the fact that Rockstar's main focus has been shifted to online and they have repurposed content previously intended for a DLC into online updates (ex Doomsday Heist). The many references to the Chiliad Mystery that have been added to GTA Online over the years seem to allude to this as well, and they have been slowly dropping these teasers since the very first update (Beach Bum udpate in 2013 relased a van with a hidden UFO drawn on the side).

Besides being a 4th wall breaking teaser, this poster has a great deal of in-game significance as well. Here are all of the clues and references to the poster that I have gathered.

Los Santos 2020 Clues:

This poster is actually a promotional poster for a movie called "Los Santos 2020" that is found to be playing at several movie theaters in-game, including the Oriental Theater.

This movie cannot be seen even though it is listed amoung the movies currently playing.

The Los Santos 2020 poster can be found in 2 locations in San Andreas. (One at the Hippie Trailer Park and one at the Lost MC Trailer Park)

The poster depicts a member the same reptilian alien species that Michael sees in his drug hallucinations, as well as an image of Los Santos.

This movie seems to be a sequel to the movie "Los Santos 2012", a movie that is mentioned on the billboard for the movie "Die Already 4" which can be found in several places in the city. This billboard says "From the producers of Los Santos 2012".

On Devin Weston's in-game website, it states that he has holdings in the "Die Already" movie franchise, implying that he himself may have been involved in the production of the "Los Santos 2012" franchise as well.

Theories:

"Los Santos 2012" seems to be a parody of the movie "2012", a movie where the alleged Mayan Apocalypse rumored to end the world in 2012 turned out to be true.

Instead of natural disasters, "Los Santos 2012" involves an alien invasion as the cause of the apocalypse.

Along with Devin Weston, it is likely that Cris Formage is involved in the production of this movie franchise as well... seeing as the content of these movies aligns perfectly with the beliefs of the Epsilon Program.

Cris constantly warns his followers of an upcoming Apocalypse that will destroy mankind. This Apocalypse is alluded to within the tenants and goals of Epsilon and is even directly referenced in two of the Epsilon missions. ("Unless you achieve MORE you shall not survive the Apocalypse!" Cris to Michael in Exercising the Truth and "I was speaking to Carpa Tom in the Paradigm 4, and he said to deliver the Apocalypse funds to the Cayman Islands" - Cris to Michael in Unknowing the Truth)

Cris claims that the only way to be saved from this Apocalypse is to join his Epsilon Program, and that anyone who doubts this is Unsaveable. He likely funded the creation of "Los Santos 2012" as a scare tactic to get more people to believe in and fear his alleged upcoming Epsilonian Apocalypse. When the world didn't end in 2012, he created a sequel to this movie.... "Los Santos 2020".

This is the reason behind the actors dressed as reptilian aliens found at Richards Majestic Studios. Cris is secretly filming a sequel to "Los Santos 2020" during the events of GTA V... set to release sometime after the world fails to come to an end again in 2020. The fact that an end-of-the-Earth Apocaylpse movie is part of a trilogy is textbook Rockstar satire.

The appearance of these reptilian aliens during Michael's drug hallucinations seems to imply that he has an extreme fear of them (Trevor sees clowns which he has a phobia of).

Due to the fact that Michael is a huge movie buff, his fear of these reptilian aliens likely stems from the fact that he has seen "Los Santos 2012" or "Los Santos 2020" himself.

Upcoming GTA Online Update:

If this poster is truly a 4th wall breaking teaser, then this update will most likely drop in December of 2020 for two major reasons.

  1. Rockstar has dropped a major update in December (right before Christmas) every year since 2014.
  2. The Mayan Apocalypse that is satirized in this movie franchise was allegedly going to take place on December 21st.... perhaps this will be the exact release date.
