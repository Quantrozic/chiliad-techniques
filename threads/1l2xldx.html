First of all I know this is a chiliad mystery Reddit but I think it’s worth posting it here.

This is just a lead that maybe someone else already hypothesized or maybe it’s just a very dumb idea. I just wanted to post here because I don’t bother investigating and maybe could be worth spreading these thoughts for someone else who have the interest to go further.

On the red wallpaper during the doomsday heist mission we can see the infinity 8 mystery section. Here we spot the number 8, we spot a triangle indicating x,y coordinates, we spot what appears to me train tracks, and we spot two lines of code pointing towards a map.
These lines of code have arrows isolating certain sections within those 2 lines of code, meaning that out of the whole line of code only those letters/numbers are important to know the coordinates/location on the map.

And speaking of 2 lines of code i can only remember the 2 codes on the highway, those highway codes that people tried to decipher, ended up deciphering only one using a A1Z26 Cipher that translated to a line of letters that translated to that iconic GTA San Andreas voice line “All we had to do was follow the dam train CJ” and also referencing a Trevor mission called “Derailed”.

Well speaking of missions involving trains, this correlates with the object on the red mural that appears to be train tracks, giving more correlation between the highway codes and the infinity 8.

What I’m getting at with this is that the highway codes, and the arrows isolating specific letters/numbers of those codes could give us the coordinates/location of the infinity 8 killer or some clue.
