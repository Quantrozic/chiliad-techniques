Strangers and Freaks Theory

▲ 4 ptsu/thegingergiantMay 15, 20158 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Route Theory ?

Before I start I want to state that I believe we have to have 100% completion when we go to the top of chiliad like the writing says in order for the Rockstar gods to bless us with a sacred Jet pack. I believe the Red X's to be requirments we have to meet before we recieve said prize, I've been lurking here for a while and have read about 100% being one, 3:00am being another and uninjured being a third but today I came up with a theory. Forgive me if it's already been debunked or mentioned already.

I have 100% on my Xbox 360 and PS3 but not my Xbox One copy so I was looking through the game guide in the Strangers and Freaks section and in order to acheive 100% completion you must do missions for 8 people. There are 15 listed in the index which made me think maybe some of the remaining may be the "requirments" whether it means making the right decision or staying away from them completely. This could link in with the idea of good karma.

  1. Abigal - Her husband, Frank Mathers was passionate about space before turning to the sea. She doesnt out right admit it but its easy to tell she was the one who tampered with his sub and caused his death. You have the choice to:
  • Kill Her: Bad Karma?
  • Let Her Go: Good Karma?
  1. Cletus - I'm having trouble thinking of a reason Cletus would be involved. But his missions are not required so that's why I include him.
  2. Epsilon - When I played through the game my first 2 times I didn't know they existed apart from their website and I still don't know much so beg my pardon but I'm pretty sure that they are just about as fascinated with the Aliens as we are. After completing a series of tasks for the program you are asked to help deliver the "apocalypse funds" to the Epsilon helicopter but you have a choice:
  • Kill all Epsilon sequrity and steal the money: Medal Requirment
  • Steal the money and evade Epsilon Sequrity: Bad Karma?
  • Follow Epsilon Security to the drop-off point and receive the Antique Tractor: Good Karma?
  1. Josh - He is a slimey realtor who wants you to destroy all of Lenny Avery's for sale signs, beat Avery up and burn his own house down for the insurance money. When the cops show up he fingers Trevor as the arsonist but actually calls him an Epsilonist, your left with the choice to:
  • Gun down Josh then flee in the parked police car: Medal Requirment
  • Sprint to your own car and flee: Good Karma?
  1. Mary-Ann - The fitness freak makes you race her a few times. She's only included in this because her missions are not required.
  1. Maude - I feel like Maude is more of a Hobbies and Pastimes classification and she doesn't really belong here either.
  1. Minute Men - The Minute Men are self proclaimed civil border patrol. They drive around in a Park Rangers SUV and capture immigrants before leaving Trevor stranded in the desert once they're caught. Edit 1: For the final mission in this sequence one of the "immigrants you previously apprehended convinces Trevor to kill the Minute Men for wrongly apprehending him. I haven't played this mission in a long time and am just going off the book but based on this the choice you get is:
  • Use the stun gun before you kill them before they leave the farm: Medal Requirment
  • [Based on the requirments I'm wondering what happens if they leave the farm, can you avoid killing them in which case] Let them leave the farm: Good Karma?
  1. Nigel - And Mrs.Thornhill are celebrity worshipers who ask Trevor to steal them a bunch of celebrity souvenirs, find Al Di Napoli, chase him and kidnap him. He of course isn't the most cooperative prisoner and Trevor offers a couple of solutions as to what to do with him before ultimately deciding on death by train. However on the route to the train tracks Napoli pleads his case and your presented with a choice:
  • Continue to the fatal destination: Medal Requirment
  • Stop the car and release Napoli: $10,000 Reward?

So my thoughts are that we should either try making all good karma decisions with Abigail, Josh, Nigel, The Minute Men and The Epsilon Program and if that does nothing maybe we have to avoid them all while completing the game.

Kifflom!

Edit 2: I added in all the decisions and their outcomes that I could come up with. I don't know if any of this is relevant as it is but Id like to hope that it's something simple like this that we've all overlooked if there was a chance of solving it before the PC version came out.
