I have recently returned to the Xbox 360 V. Of GTA San Andreas only to find an update again! I believe the chilliad mystery may lye in this game as well.. I think we should be looking here. the time in this game takes place (very) roughly when the Infinity killer starts and is the same location as GTA V (obviously). I believe we will find what we are looking for, or more importantly the lack of what SHOULD be there. Assigning dates to important monuments and changes. Ive already noticed small interesting differences (such as the differences in orientation/direction of the maps, and half of the surrounding towns that are in V are still underwater in SA due to what im assuming is damming and sea levels lowering). But i am not an experienced hunter. Nor am I very good at it. Currently looking at Mt. Chilliad and the obvious yet important fact is that in this time (1992-1993) nobody has drawn the chilliad symbols. Which means Whoever etched these into the mountain came about recently. Some time before V (2012-2013) but after SA (the last 20 years). And being that in 5 they are worn i would place their etching no less than 3 years before V which leaves roughly a 17 year window.

(Extras possibly worth looking in to)

Due to this game being what i guess is the Miami of GTA universe I am not sure as to what will be there clue wise although i am sure we will be able to catch mentions to LS and/or infinity 8 Killer via radio stations and dialogue.

I believe Liberty City is very important to this mystery, if you recall @CHILLIAD8888s Email to our Yugoslavian friend, along with this being the placement of Niko Bellics story before he moves to LS along with Sonny Bottino, Johnny Klebitz, Brucie Kibbutz, Packie McReary, Karen/Michelle, United Liberty Paper contact, Lazlow, Marnie (epsilon), and Fernando Martinez. Who moved TO LC? Carl Johnson, Franklins cousin AND the infinity killer for some time. COULD NOT POSSIBLY BE A COINCIDENCE.

CONCLUSION:
I think that in order to solve this mammoth of a mystery we need to cover all of our bases. Rockstar has claimed on multiple occasions that we havent found EVERY easter egg/clue in ANY of the GTA games. As far as we know this could track all the way back to their arcade version! I understand this mystery already had a lot of ground to cover and this adds about a million more acres, but i honestly believe Rockstar has been planning this for a long time. sketching it out.. back to Tommy and Claudes stories at very least. This mystery hasnt been solved for a reason. I think broadening our scopes could only help us understand better if not solve the entire thing.

I will continue looking into San Andreas and 5 (i have already re-completed both stories 100%). Possibly even buying Vice, Vice Stories, and 3 again. But any extra hands on this would be extremely helpful. as i said i suck at hunting for these clues and just dont have the eyes for it.
