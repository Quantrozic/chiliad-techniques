I hate to be the bearer of bad news (concerns looking for a secret cave)

▲ 0 ptsu/SilverkukiSep 28, 20133 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:DDS Mipmap and Texture Atlas Inspection ?Texture-Atlas Dump Inspection ?

While aimlessly exploring the Eastern mountain near the Yoga mat I noticed my character sink into the ground. Upon further inspection, I found that he wasn't so much sinking into the ground as he was stepping through the visual ground onto a more solid ground below. This visual trick is used when stepping into brush or leaf piles, but I had never experienced it on plain dirt path such as on the mountains.

Here is the picture of him mid-step: http://i.imgur.com/Cbrk6dK.jpg

What this means for our subreddit is a little disheartening; Helicopter drive by's looking for caves may be completely useless. The cave entrance (if there should be one) could easily be covered by this faux ground texture.

On the plus side, I also found out that by combining the moon gravity cheat with the bmx bike, you are able to effectively bike up any surface that is inclined. You can pretty much pick a spot at the base of Mt. Chiliad and bike to the top in a straight line.
