Mt Josiah similarity to Mt Chiliad mural

▲ 2 ptsu/BipolaricecapFeb 13, 20154 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Embedding-Similarity Search Over Thread Archives

Apologies if this is something that has already been covered, I searched on here for some time and found nothing on the subject. I was scouring the AC for possible leads and headed up the hill to the comms tower. From there I looked over towards Mt Josiah and noticed a lot of random people taking interest in the thin cliff face (just outside the perimeter of the AC) that points towards Mt Josiah. I decided to head over there to see what the fuss was about. I walked to the very end of the cliff and noticed that the path that runs along the bottom of Mt Josiah very much resembles the line that connects the UFO and the jet pack on the mural. Also, the randomers who walk up there all get their phones out and take photos. I zoomed in on their phone screens and it looks like an egg on the screen. I have a crappy little tv so I find it hard to make it out clearly, could someone with a decent tv have a quick look? Again apologies if this has been covered.
