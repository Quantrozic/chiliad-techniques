So after seeing this in another post - https://imgur.com/XA7CoYr

I had made this - https://i.imgur.com/EYfI9iQ.png to match up the colors. But I got to wondering if there would be an kind of patterns or anything if we tried all the different ways of connecting each colored box.

First I made this - https://i.imgur.com/o8swcIZ.png keeping in mind I only made the black boxes green to make them a bit easier to follow. Basically, it was a waste and pretty much proves the obvious that nothing is really cut off from each other.

So I tried to simplify it and made this - https://i.imgur.com/MihIQei.png again, black is purple just to be a bit easier to see. The idea beside instead to only draw connections that would probably be the shortest in terms of turns and overall distance.

What's interesting is, putting aside my potential bias for what's the "shortest" route, there seems like there's a few patterns.

Red/Yellow connect twice, on the bottom and above the UFO.

Blue/Purple (Black) don't really pair much at all with anything

Blue, Yellow, Purple all touch the point above the bottom X

I feel like if we can figure out what each of those points where multiple colors connect together are, that should tell us something. Especially with RY, after all they do match 2 of the colors on these murals https://i.imgur.com/Irm4kFb.jpg
