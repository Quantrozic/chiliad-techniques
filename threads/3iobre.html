But what I did find in the area is a near by nuclear power station which is now in a decommission phase until 2021

The place (Moreton) is also near the Bovington tank corps, there are also links to Lawrence of Arabia, I took a look online about info for him, his home was near by, look what was above his door, the first symbol...

And if you look on google maps, there is a weird looking dead tree on the way there, that kind of looks like the one at the hippie camp

Also what else I find odd is those coordinates are right on the trail nearly, the surrounding fields are private property to Moreton Manor, the pond with the Island nearby is part of a private shooting range, would R* put coordinates in a game without first checking they were not on private ground or outside someones house, knowing that people may try and go there, I doubt it.

Near the coordinates is this pillar as well http://medias.photodeck.com/d3a6671e-340c-11e1-b4b4-0578b4be085b/JasonHawkes-0635_xlarge.jpg

I started overlaying the map of dorset and aligning the pillar with the observatory obelisk, lining up the tank base with Zancudo, the nuclear core ends up a the vinewood sign, and some roads on the Dorset map kind of line up with the coast road on the game map http://imgur.com/flJyWwH

Could all be total toast (probably is) but just seems a bit odd.
