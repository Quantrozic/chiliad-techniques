New to the scene. I think we need to include GTA 4 in the search for answers about the spider web and possibly even the Infinity Killer.

TL;DR: I don't think the scope of the search has been large enough, and the proof may be in the story of the Infinity Killer, a prolific entity that has appeared repeatedly throughout Rockstar's games. Most importantly, I think we're missing an important clue from GTA IV based on connected clues found in GTA 5, GTA Online, and Read Dead Redemption 2, or perhaps have not made the necessary connection yet to understand the bigger picture being presented us.
___
Where do I start? I love hidden mysteries in video games, especially those that connect to in-game lore and story elements that would otherwise never come up in a regular playthrough for anyone but those willing to turn over every loose stone in the vicinity. It's how I found Oddheader's YT channel and first learned about the Chiliad Mystery. While I've never played much of GTA or RDR, Rockstar always goes the extra mile when it comes to secrets and puzzles so I've always tried to at least keep tabs on new developments.

Oddheader's Recap and Call to Action

A few months back, we got Oddheader's recap of the mystery and its findings to-date, ending on the suspicion that no, we have not in fact found everything on account of this very specific spider web appearing under the Chiliad station. For those who haven't seen the video, I'll link it here:

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9AnI5jxL-us

Fast forward to a few weeks ago and he posted a new video confirming to his followers that his doubts the mystery was solved were correct, as the figurative "web" started to unravel:

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gv24Q4EUp5Q

I'm not personally versed enough in either game to confirm what is or isn't consistent with everyone else's findings to-date, but his videos had a lot of solid evidence and at the very least, shared a call to action to resume the hunt for new clues related to this spider theming around the RDR and GTA series. Having nothing better to do, I've been watching people's explorations and tonight found perhaps the first video that made me pause and lean forward.

Strange Man's on the Trail

Shortly after seeing this video, I found Strange Man documenting an extremely thorough revisit of the clues related to this mystery in RDR2. As he's been making other updates since I first discovered his channel, I'm only linking to his latest video on the subject:

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=igKT4fNLeuk

The title's a bit click-baity in that we still have no real idea what Gertrude's counting meant, but it's an extremely tight review of the mysteries found so far and what may or may not be related.

Important details from the video (again, most of this information seems known or has at least been mentioned at one point in time or another, but I'm recapping for posterity):

  • Gertrude is presumably insane. Alternatively, she's been possessed, as the pentagram in Butcher Creek seems to suggest.
  • Gertrude actually leads us to the outhouse with the IV on it. This is also the only outhouse with initials (LJ SM) on it, assumed to be the game's devs leaving a signature. We'll come back to that.
  • Following the bread crumbs here, we find a trail with eight milestones/markers, with the last happening to be a spider.
  • The trail continues until we've found eight telephone poles that happen to overlap with the spider image, each pole featuring a spider web that appears at a fixed time in-game for one hour before disappearing. At the center of the spider's body is a final hidden web that points north to yet another telephone pole with a hidden clue revealed only by damaging it.
  • Following this trail eventually leads to Fort Wallace, where the trail has gone lukewarm at best... There are a couple of ongoing theories, like the heart symbols sharing the same curvature as the "birds" on the roof, or that perhaps there is some connection to Gertrude's rambling about birds and the Bird of Paradise plants scattered unnaturally throughout one part of the map, but the next clue seems to be as-of-yet unfound.

Bizly's Smoking Gun

... Which leads me to the most recent analysis I just happened to stumble on because it was covering GTA5 lore.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KedfEMhQdP8

It's his findings that the Infinity Killer may not be a single individual, but perhaps a curse passed from one killer to the next in sequence, with each killer sharing a distinct obsession with the number eight. More importantly, his search leads to an active serial killer who, when you kill them, instantly grants you a new revolver with the text 'You're next' on it...

... And if you get enough kills with it, you unlock the gun in RDR2 ...
... And it just so happens to have belonged to Edmund Lowry, one of the single most infamous serial killers in the story.

Which he then expands on in this video:

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dsgF4tjpARg

And if you scroll down a bit, you'll find this highlighted comment from Tktktkoi:

hey i just realized something interesting, you know eddie low the serial killer from GTA IV, he has a blog in the game? well in it he confesses that he used to wear a sailor suit when he was little!! he says that he always wanted to be a sailor but he was locked up in a camp for bad boys so he couldn’t!

so that kid that is locked up in a sailors suit has something to do with it!

Where do I remember seeing IV come up... ? Right!

... And I think the answer might be somewhere in GTA 4.

I have quite a bit of gameplay to catch up on to start contributing to this research myself, so I can't say with certainty I'm bringing any new leads to the table yet. But it seems odd to me that these letters were specifically left on the fourth outhouse and none of the others, and a mention from the Infinity Killer themselves that just so happens to align in a completely different time period with a stranger that's been kidnapped and forced to dress as a sailor in someone's basement while everyone in town pretends he doesn't exist.

Is it something? Maybe.
Is it exciting? Definitely.
