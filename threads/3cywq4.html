The source for the hologram UFO at Mt. Chiliad?

As most of you know holograms work by projecting a beam of light on to something or into the air. Since the UFO on top of Mt. Chiliad is definitely a hologram there has to be a source that is generating and projecting the needed light for it. I've been on top of the mountain and visited and watched the UFO multiple times and something that I noticed is that when it appears there always seems to be some kind of blueish light coming from the East or to be more precise from the direction of where the light house is located. So, my speculation is that the light house is actually the source for the hologram. Does that makes sense somehow?
