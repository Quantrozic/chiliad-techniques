We should make a sticky that compiles and sorts ALL clues and theories, as long as the person is not obviously lying. They would be sorted in order of relative importance. Here's kind of what I was thinking:

Platinum (purposeful, proven relationship): Some in-game feature that was unarguably included in the game on purpose. Must have a proven relationship to a previous Platinum clue. Examples include the Chiliad mural and the UFOs.

Golden (proven relationship): Something that is certainly unusual and very, very likely to have been included on purpose but is not necessarily related to the mystery. Examples include geometry hints and the Cultstoppers website and NPCs.

Silver (possible relationship): Something that is between Golden and Bronze, essentially. This is where we get into the clues that are most likely not going to lead anywhere, as well as being the majority of clues posts. Examples include X's on billboards.

Bronze (reaching relationship): Something that is definitely commonplace but may have a relationship to the mystery if you look at it the right way. Examples include strange noises in buildings.

Again, this is all an idea for now and maybe you guys could help flesh it out. I just thought of these quick off the top of my head so it's probably not perfect. Change it as you will.
