The Complete Tract Is An ARG Project The Early GTA 5 Community Literally Never Followed

After doing lots of research into Rockstar's projects having to do with the Epsilon Program, Its been both enlightening and really saddening to see what I believe is the most obvious lead into the mystery, however, time (and Rockstar) hasn't been too kind to much of the media associated with Grand Theft Auto 5.

For starters, Rockstar made an insane amount of extended lore for the Epsilon program that extends way beyond the scope of GTA 5. However, most of it is completely abandoned nowadays and forgotten. When the game First Released, there was a website you could visit that had information related to the Epsilon Program's beliefs and practices. This was called "epsilonprogram.com". On this website, it's about what you would expect. Alot of the scam ad stuff people usually associate with them.

HOWEVER, this website has a specific e-mail address that you can use for contact. "webminister@epsilon.com" is listed as a contact you can go to for "reservations and direction". Specifically referring to the email system as the enlightenment center.

This was not the only real world connection that the Epsilon Program had, as they also were active in an official twitter page that is still up today, but is also completely abandoned. As is the email address (it just returns and error email from rockstar now).

What is especially upsetting about the twitter is that they are clearly posting to fish for people actually looking to de-cipher the tract, but the concept of an "ARG" wasn't as big as it is nowadays. Pretty much all the replies are memes and such. Noone was really trying to figure out the tract since either people didn't do the mission to know about it, or they just wrote the whole thing off as a meme. And there just...straight up isn't much traffic.

What I believe the plan was:

It is my belief that after collecting the 10 tracts in story mode and unlocking the tract, players were supposed to interact with the real life e-mail and twitter resources in order to figure out what we needed to do as a community, and over time, it would be decoded piece by piece with some guidance from Rockstar.

(One of the main reasons is that the game gives no reward for the tracts completion besides the tract on it's own)

However, this never happened. People looked to the mural on Chiliad as the "big mystery". And as well as this, the concept of an ARG wasnt really popular in 2012/2013 as it is today. It would've been a massive short lived community effort to figure out the steps in uncovering it's meaning and an insane stunt by Rockstar. Hell, the site itself tells you in BIG BOLD LETTERS to read the tract. (There is a cool screensaver too but it is also abandonware because of flash player)

What now?

This is the really sad part. I don't know.

Assuming these resources were there to help us solve the mystery, it just shows if anything that we are up Krant's creek without a paddle in figuring out what the complete tract means. It's also hilarious that an email literally existed for asking for direction on completing the mystery but...noone effectively used it when it was active? Especially when now there are so many people going to Rockstar support to try and figure out hints.

They....literally had a hint line....

I'm currently working on deciphering the tract and Doomsday gave us some insightful hints with the purple mural, but I am just baffled that such an integral part of the Epsilon mystery wasn't tapped and now it's all just....gone. Into another paradigm.

My only hope is that Rockstar sees this in some way and revives the twitter or something but right now, I feel like our chance for direct contact with the program irl is snuffed. The Tract should still be de-codable, we're just on our own now. Its sad but digging through alot of this old content is pretty cool, and a nice time capsule.
