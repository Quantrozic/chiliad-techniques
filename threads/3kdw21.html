This tracking device bugged me from the first time I saw it in the game, and until now I've been trying to get it outside the epsilon mission and use it (with mods) but there's no hope.

anyway, what I was really wondering about is that in the beginning of the Chasing the Truth mission, Marnie introduces this device to micheal saying "this is the technology that the authorities are trying to suppress", I know it's a joke but I mean that they give you it after telling you it's an (kinda) important device, still in the end of the mission before they leave they take the alien artifact (the boot lol) and leave the device in micheal's hand.

I know that rockstar might've not made a scene of them taking the device back because they thought it might be obvious that they took it, or they forgot to do the scene, OR what I'm going for here, they did it on purpose to show the player that micheal still has the device with him. and they actually show you after the scene that Marnie and the other guy get in the truck and leave, not like disappear or something like that, so this might actually be a hint that they just took that boot and left without taking the device!

So the device might still be with micheal or in his house or somewhere I don't really know, but we have to find it.
Another thing that I have also been thinking about is that franklin sees the device in the hand of Omega when he first meets him and Omega trys to scan him with it, and micheal sees and uses the device. so what about trevor? he never saw/used that device.

I think that it might be in that box that appears after the altruists camp shootout.

anyway, these were my thoughts and I liked to share them with you all. Kifflom!
