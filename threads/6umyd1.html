My apologies if you’ve read any of this before, I just wanted to sum up some stuff.

First, to help with searches you can pause time on the PS3, not tested it with other consoles. Just start a golf game, pause and load a saved game, time should be paused.

Here goes, I believe karma is a big thing in the game, it’s written and referenced constantly and in the stats screen you have a crimes tab so you can keep an eye on your crimes.

So if karma is a thing then that would mean to me Trevor has to die I think. This would make sense because with Mike and Franklin you can control their karma, you can choose not to kill people that don’t deserve it, like Mikes gardener, when you knock him out whilst sneaking into Mikes, it says he dies in the stats screen, but you can also not kill him and still continue the mission. Trevor however he is beyond karma, you can not control his killing, for example he kills the couple in the flat, the strip club owner, throws people off of bridges and docks in cut scenes and even buries people in cut scenes, you can not include him in a karma type game.

It also makes sense that one of the three characters can not have a complete karma game as well, as to complete the 100 percent someone has to do some bad things, like go to the strip club, rob a store, maybe other mission related bad stuff, so it makes sense that the person to do these bad things, since beyond karma is Trevor.

So you can only control the karma of Mike and Franklin, so if karma is a thing it should be concentrated on these two alone and use Trevor to do the necessary bad things, he is truly un-saveable and no point trying to, you can’t control him and it makes sense to me.

That then leads to what karma is in the game. Well there’s a stat screen for crimes alone which you can check to see if you are good. I’d say no killing of those you don’t have to, so no killing someone because they asked if you “wanna go”, no killing animals as it comes up as a kill in stats and no one that you don’t have to on missions, people shooting at you is fine though I think, but only on missions. No stealing peoples cars/bikes etc, just use taxis/your own car and to back this up, if they ask you to steal a vehicle on a mission, like steal that van, then it doesn’t count in the crime stats, well not when I tested it, so that supports it too, I think.

That leaves the tricky question of what do you do about random events in a karma situation. Well in the mission “Marriage Counselling”-I think that’s what it is called, where Mike pulls down the patio, in that he mentions a quote from Matthew 7 something, anyway it says there somewhere about being a hypocrite and to do to others like yourselves and stuff. That would point to me that in most events, like robberies you should not get involved, firstly as you’d be a hypocrite being a thief yourself, secondly you wouldn’t want that to happen in a robbery you were doing and lastly if you think about it, nobody dies in the robbery until you get involved, then you are forced to kill the thief. There are exceptions like the construction yard guy you can save without hurting anyone, but mostly karma wise I’d say leave them alone.
