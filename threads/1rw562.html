Jetpack Shadow: A closer look.

At a cursory glance, it does seem to resemble the jetpack glyph. However, taking a closer look at it reveals just how far fetched it is to make any connection further than "Yeah, this thing kinda looks like that other thing."

The figure -- pulled from the recent video -- without crappy MS Paint outline

The figure with crappy MS Paint outline

The figure is clearly missing important elements, or contains elements that are not consistent with the glyph:

  • Arm, and jetpack control arm, are missing.

  • Nozzle and thrust lines are missing.

  • Legs are in the wrong position, and one has a connected rectangular box.

  • Figure has a tail.

There are a bunch of connected shadows/lines that are are being completely glossed over because they don't fit in with the preconceived notion of what the jetpack glyph looks like. When taking all connected elements into account, the figure only vaguely resembles the jetpack glyph.

The idea that this was an Easter egg was debunked a while ago, and for good reason. It's beyond a stretch. If we didn't already have a picture of a stick figure with a jetpack in the game, absolutely no one would be making a connection here. There's nothing about this shadow figure that resembles a guy with a jetpack -- maybe a guy with a backpack, at best. It's a total case of pareidolia.
