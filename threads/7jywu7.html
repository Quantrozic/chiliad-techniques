The RDR2 Treasure Hunt Details...

▲ 90 ptsu/SSj5_TaddenDec 15, 2017100 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:RAGE Format Parity Auditing Against RDR2

Hellooo everyone 😄 Team Guru is back!! We've also been up to our old tricks...

So I'm guessing you've all heard about this new RDR2 easter egg/gun unlock/freemode event? If you have then Google is your friend 😛

I'm here just to explain a little about it and how we uncovered it. As most of these things do, it all started with some new files and text that was released in the Doomsday update. Gramz and Kai, as soon as the update dropped, spotted some new props and so began our hunt through the files. We then uncovered some text in the gxt2 (text) files:

"0x4F9027CF = Because sometimes revenge is a dish best served six times, in quick succession, right between the eyes. Sign up to the Rockstar Games Social Club to get access to the Double-Action Revolver headshot challenge. Beating this challenge will unlock a GTA$ reward and an exclusive variation of the weapon in Red Dead Redemption 2. Sign up now at socialclub.rockstargames.com and link your account."

This gave us a rough idea of what was going on, but we did originally think this may be some kind of secret hidden mission (we do love and secret mission lol). After finding more and more about it though, it became apparent that it was in fact a new game mode appropriately called "Treasure Hunt".

In this mode the player is sent an email with a picture of a location they must go to. They then, upon reaching the location, will get another email and picture to lead to the next. The player needs to collect 3 treasures, the gun being the 3rd and last one that shows up, at any of the 20 possible locations.

Once the gun is found the player can then begin the 50 head shot challenge I mentioned before. Which when completed, will unlock a variation of the weapon in RDR2 when it comes out and also rewards you with $250,000 in GTAOnline.

This new mode however is NOT yet active in game and will most likely not be active until closer to the RDR2 release. We have however managed to force the mission to load, so we can give you all an exclusive look at what's goin to happen and how the weapon is obtained.
