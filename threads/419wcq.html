Documented under:Mural & Map Mirroring ?

I've always been a huge fan of easter eggs, and was recently delighted to come upon this subreddit. I had some free time today and decided to examine the mural in photoshop. While I doubt my results are of any value to the investigation, and by no means mean to disrespect anyone by triflin' with yo time, I think these pictures are at least entertaining, and it's important to post the little things to keep people engaged and keep the hunt going.

So here's what I did. I noticed in the mural painting that the lightning bolts are mirrored, i.e. the five (or three) bolts on one side are identical to the other. It got me curious, if that part of the painting was mirrored, what would happen if I just mirrored the entire thing? Now, the unfiltered results of that might be valuable to someone who is actually familiar with the game (I haven't played any GTA in the last decade), so I've included them in the hope that something more fruitful may come of my contribution than just a few interesting but seemingly over-edited pictures. In fact, I've included all the layers I used in the process. Here is the entire album. The results, as you can see, are awesome, ranging all the way from an image of the Egyptian sun-god to Darth Vader, but seem mostly irrelevant and frivolous. What does creep me out a little (but is still irrelevant), is this photo. In the middle, between the first two X-boxes coming from the top down, is a rather clear smiley face.

Now, before you insult me for making a cold, grey English morning look like a sunny afternoon in Disneyland, do realize that all I did was mirror each half through the middle, and adjust the threshold layer. What this does, is, well, I don't know, but it looks rad as fuck.

Stay spooky boys.
