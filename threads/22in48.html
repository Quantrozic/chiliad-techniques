The Eye in the Sky at Kortz Center.

▲ 4 pts[deleted]Apr 8, 20143 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Sub-Pixel Star Pattern Mapping ?

I was messing around with some friends of mine in the maze by the Kortz Center, when I noticed an eye I knew from somewhere else. The eye of Horus painted on some streamers (please someone, put a picture of it). This eye is well known, but it reminded me of the Eye in the sky from The Alan Parksons Project. So I grabbed my LP and gave it a listening. The song in itself didn't ring any bell to me, but the second called Children of the moon begins with this :

<<**Pay no attention to the writing on the wall,
The words seem empty cause there's nothing there at all,**
We let the wise men beat the drums too soon,
We were just children of the moon,>>

Maybe I'm going too far, but this song talking about children of the moon, this sentence and the fact that the eye is painted on a streamer at Kortz Center really make me think... Could R* go that far? I hope not. If this turns out to be a really nice coincidence... mmh? Should we still believe to coincidences? This sounds far-fetched but pease, tell me what you think about it.

Btw, there are plenty of statues shaped like eggs in this area. You guys should take a look (again) at this building. Kifflom!
