So I started replaying San Andreas a bit ago, I’m fairly close to completion 79%, jet pack, hydra, etc. I’ve noticed that their are tons of references in gta v to San Andreas both in mission structures of online/sp but also a lot in the cults of gta v and mystery related things.

But the things that stick with me the most are what The Truth says to you from time to time.

There are a bunch, but the one that sticks out the most is after you help him gain the jet pack and he then has you flying around collecting green goo(?) and if I remember vaguely you were trying to secure some alien specimen. (?)

In addition, Truth will call into WCTR and say that aliens are real and he’s held them (or their eggs) in his hands.
This reminds me of the Egg from the mural/the game files.

He also mentions messages in the sands, and making peace with the aliens.

There’s another mission called Don Peyote, wear you find to ppl that truth sent on a spirit quest with peyote (familiar?) that got lost, and you have to track them down.

I realize this is a shitty post, bc I don’t have too many examples off the top of my head, I’ll go back and add some more.

But it got me thinking that maybe the chilliad mystery could be in some way tied to the truth from the first game.

Esp seeing as the 3 boxes are the ufo, the egg, and the jet pack, which you could argue are all directly related to the truth from San Andreas.

Idk maybe it’s been said before, but it’s interesting to me at the very least.
