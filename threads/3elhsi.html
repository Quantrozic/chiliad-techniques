Found an interesting door at Fort Zancudo (may just be a developer error)

▲ 16 pts[deleted]Jul 25, 20157 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Collision-Mesh Boundary Probing ?Collision-Mesh Probing ?

So, I was messing around at the base on the Xbox 360 version with a health mod when I came across this.

I found the image on Google so the red box is the location of my find.

Basically there is a small building there just big enough for a garage door. Anyways there is an entrance door on the side that caught my attention because it is sort of small. Well, I went to shoot the door and I killed something. Basically on the door there is a small window portion, that portion is basically not existent. You can shoot through it clear out the other side of the building.

You can also get up right next to that area, aim your gun and actually shoot. Normally you can't do that with an object right in front of you. You can't throw objects through it though, nor can you shoot a flare through it. But the flare's get stuck on the ground and if you go to the large front door you can see the light around the side of the garage door.

I imagine it is probably just another glitch that went unnoticed but I haven't been involved in solving the mystery for a long time and didn't know if this would be helpful in any way whatsoever.
