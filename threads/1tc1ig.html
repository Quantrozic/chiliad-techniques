One very important subtle clue on the Mt. Chiliad mural

▲ 6 ptsu/LlamaGuy69Dec 20, 201327 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Mural & Map Mirroring

Has anyone else noticed that one of the 'lightening bolt' looking symbols on the mural is distinctly different than all of the others? The symbols are all perfectly symmetrical, except for the one on the top right. Not only is this one a little off in placement according to the line of symmetry, but the symbol is also reversed....(http://www.giantbomb.com/images/1300-2571016). Now if we interpret these symbols as radio signals, which they are a common symbol for, it would mean that the odd one out is going in the opposite direction, and could be symbolic for communication with the UFO.

It could be interpreted that majority of the radio waves are being picked up by Mt Chiliad and the UFO, and that the odd symbol out is a radio signal being emitted from the UFO. This is if we interpret the signals in the same way as we interpret the SoulWax logo (http://stoneyroads.com/media/Soulwax-fm.jpg), were the signals that are emitted from the tower 'zig' inward and then 'zag' outward in relation to the emitter... but it might possibly be the opposite.

Im pretty sure this is a very subtle clue and could possibly be a hint towards to location of were you might have to pick up the radio signal from the UFO. Im not sure about this, but I think that most people on here think that the best fit of the mural would be more or less the West side of Mt Chiliad. If the mural if a depiction of Chiliad from the West side, then the radio signal emitted from the UFO would be going South of Mt Chiliad. Either way I think this might be a very important way to interpret the mural (especially since we know we dont need lightning to spawn the UFO!) and it might lead to some great findings!
