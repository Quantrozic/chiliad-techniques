I remember like an year ago or something that i've seen those moving stars before, not at the altruist camp but at the amusement park with michael, and earlier this year with frankin at his house. I've come with a little theory here, throughout the game we go to different locations, some of which have telescopes. Del perro pier with michael(the bycicle mission), Franklin's telescope in his new home, gallileo observatory with michael, later with trevor, again at del perro pier(the 3rd way) + the side missions, the binoculars on mt chilliad with franklin searching for the peyote plants and so on. So my assumtion is that we should be looking at the stars whenever we encounter a telescope. The missions themselves might be the trigger we need in order to step further into the mystery and that trigger is once and only and thus, after the altruist shootout, there might be a new cutscene. As for how you should know where to look, which telescope to use, the lines below will describe it.

The segregate and rearrange might refer to the playthrough itself, something like->"okay, so you have completed the game the first time, now segregate this playthrough in a save file and start another one, then rearrange your actions with knowledge from the previous one, and so on.

What i'm trying to say is that, some games like the stanley parable, the end is not the end. In that game, sometimes when you started a new playthrough, there were different dialogue lines even from the beginning, showing that the game knew you ended it before, giving you new experiences.
Basically, starting a new game and doing things differently will be memorized by the game itself and it might not even be necessary to use the save slots which by the way, there are 15(it might mean something, it might not). I guess that's the main reason why you can check your progression online even though it's single player.

I really do see R* being able to do something this ridiculous but it may be just me and this would also give an answer to the question:"How is that no one solved the mystery by now?", well, no one would re-play the game more than one or two times in all these years that have passed.
