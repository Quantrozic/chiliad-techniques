Have you all lost your minds?

▲ 0 ptsu/fatthand9Jan 9, 201414 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Moon-Phase Condition Testing ?

Why all the hate for mollywoppin? So what he made three rather cryptic posts. Nobody here forced anybody to even read them and now people are saying he is Brian and calling for his account to be banned. Since when did everybody have to act all holier than thou about a subreddit seeking to find answers to a mystery, that may not even exist, in a video game. Everyone saying how counterproductive he has been should blow it out their asses. Nothing productive has happened here in over two months. NOTHING, with the small exception of the file guys who have unearthed some things and helped to debunk some wild speculation. Other than that all I see in this sub is people getting berated for voicing their opinions. Granted some posters need to do more research, but its almost as if every new theory that gets posted is shot down for being too ridiculous or outrageous. Notice to everyone-- we have tried nearly everything and nothing has worked, so its fair to say that pretty much any theory, regardless of how crazy it may sound, should not be dismissed, because even the most sound theories so far have been proven false.

Also, mollywoppin and myinnertrevor have shed some light on the moon phases of the game, and the moon possibly being a clock, so to say that they have been completely counterproductive is bogus.

Really people, lighten up, this hunt should be fun. I don't believe the aforementioned users are trolls, and I think they want to solve this mystery just like everyone else, so get off your high horses and stop being so serious. I feel like a rockstar employee will essentially give the answers here one day in a cryptic message, and instead of investigating and trying to solve the puzzle his posts will be deleted and his account will be banned.
