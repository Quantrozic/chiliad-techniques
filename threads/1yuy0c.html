So I spent a few hours at the hippy camp last night wracking my brain on what the graffiti and symbols actually mean. I got the idea that the aliens (especially the ones who look conjoined) look like they are doing yoga. Then, I realized that yoga only uses the two joysticks...which coincidentally look like the symbols all over the camp.

That being said, maybe it is a code for some way to use the yoga activity on our own, without doing the pre-programmed routine? It has to have some kind of significance other than the boring yoga mini game that no one plays.

EDIT: I also heard a hippy say something along the lines of, "you know they say if you look at satellite pictures of the desert, some of the rocks line up"

and also, if anyone is curious, the reddit reference newspaper that says "NARWHALE?" and "BACON?" are all over the Lost MC biker camp. Pretty much anywhere there is trash.
