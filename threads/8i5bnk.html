Sorry for the poor quality post, just wanted to ask for input in the following areas. They've been discussed many times before, but I feel they are all places where the trail goes cold, maybe we can churn something up.

  • CB Radio.

I have a spreadsheet with all the possible CB radio transmissions, I assume exported from the game files. I have also heard people talk about 'military chatter' that is not represented in this spreadsheet.

Do we have a reliable way of triggering CB radio, and has anyone found a way of getting the correct 'order' of transmissions? IE has anyone managed to get a whole conversation to play in order? Is it a case of being in a certain place at a time, then moving to hear the next transmission?

  • Trevors Mum

Where is she? I don't think she's dead. One of Trevor and Amandas secret dialogues has Amanda say 'How is your mother Trevor, I miss her' Seems a bit harsh if she's dead... Did Trevor kill her? I feel like the answer to this question will help us.

  • Standard Peyotes

How much exploring have people done as the peyote animals? 27 peyotes are directly referenced in the new murals, but we have only ever used the golden ones. I've tried to get into small places as an animal, but with no success. I feel like the birds and smaller animals give us the opportunity to get into small gaps. (The open pipe up at Land Act Reservoir).

Trevor encourages the rabbit to chew through electrical wires. Maybe each animal has something similar to the yeti growl that we have to use.

Kifflom brothers, happy hunting
