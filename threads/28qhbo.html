Strange lights under Zancudo?

▲ 2 ptsu/rosalindstarlingsJun 21, 20145 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Histogram Equalization Pass on Dark Captures

http://imgur.com/a/nbMyg

While I was exploring Zancudo, looking for a jet to steal during Trevor's second hunting mission (so that I could avoid stars) I noticed a spot where the seam for the outer wall around the fort and the ground didn't quite meet. I tried to take a picture with the snapmatic, but couldn't capture it and ended up stuck using my phone. The first picture of the gap looks like glare on my screen in the picture, but thats actually how widely I managed to open it by turning the camera. I don't know if this has been talked about before, but it seemed really weird to me. The pictures are low quality and don't really do it justice, but the lights were very blend-y and (for lack of a better word) psychedelic. At first I thought they must be tied to the lights around the fort--particularly the landing strip--but the lights i could see were also pink and black and green.

Obviously, it's probably nothing, but I would like to know if the gap is indicative of a weak seam that could be clipped through, or if it's just a texturing/lighting glitch. And if this has been talked about before, kindly ignore my dumb ass. Kifflom!
