Reconciling the mural with the mountainside glyph locations and a a newcomer's perspective on the mystery

I'm new to the mystery, but I've been working pretty hard on it. I am a firm believer in the tunnel hypothesis, where the mural represents an x-ray view through the mountain from some unknown point while looking up at it. I have been focusing my hunt on the 5 mountainside glyphs, their pattern, and relationships, as I really think it's no coincidence that there are 5 boxed x's on the mural, and 5 glyphs around the mountain.

I read the wiki which states that when you add them all together, you get the requirements for seeing the MC UFO. I have 2 issues with this though - only 3 of the mountainside glyphs adds up to these requirements (zigzag = top of MC, moon with III = 3 AM, rain clouds = while storming). The other two have sort of been "conveniently ignored" in this analysis. I am referring to the half glyph which is rotated on it's side, and the blurry/faded glyph. I've been digging pretty deep into the symbolism going on, and even used my school's library pretty extensively last night with no success. This mystery has gone on so long with so little success because no one has come up with definitive explanations for what I see as the most fundamental component of this whole thing, which is the recurring eye and/or UFO symbol.

The most interesting thing I've found about this is from the symbols.com website, where I found this symbol: http://www.symbols.com/old/encyclopedia/24/2413.html. Trying to solve this without knowing what this recurring symbol means is like trying to write without understanding what letters mean. The brute force approach has taken this thing in the wrong direction, and when the solution is found it will turn out to be so elegant that everyone will wonder why they didn't see it before.

With all this in mind, I ran into a problem last night when trying to combine the glyph locations around the mountain with the mural, and I can't seem to get them to match up. Several pictures from around the web seem to see something that I'm not, because they show the glyphs matching up with the mural. See this excellent album someone here posted: http://imgur.com/a/NxIWd. I can't get the glyph locations to correlate with the boxed x's on the mural like others show. Am I missing something here? Regardless of which way you view the mountain, it doesn't seem like the glyphs have the relationship shown in the mural. Maybe the glyphs and mural don't match up, and that's not what the x's represent. If the boxes represented rooms in the mountain, the glyphs obviously wouldn't match up with the mural. I would really appreciate your guys help on explaining this to me.

Anyways, concluding this long post, my pet hypothesis is this: the 3 glyphs that add up give the conditions for the UFO do just that, they tell you when the UFO appears. When this happens, I think we are exactly 3/5 of the way to solving the rest of the mystery. I think that if we knew exactly what the other 2 glyphs signify, and met their conditions or do whatever they say, the final requirements will be met, and the door to the left of the mural will open (or something else). I don't think they symbolize weather conditions. Maybe the blurry one means you get drunk? Maybe it needs to be foggy? These are just some explanations out of the blue, maybe there are nicer explanations for it. Maybe it's side. Again, maybe the fact that it's half a glyph is insignificant because it looks like the surface that the rest was painted on was damaged or something.
