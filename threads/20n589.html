Clue telling us that Franklin is key to the mystery

▲ 63 ptsu/LlamaGuy69Mar 17, 201482 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:UV Light Analysis

I think the biggest reason that are hunt has hit a 'plateau' of sorts is not because there are tons of undiscovered clues....but because the MANY clues that we already have are being analyzed the wrong way. Think about it....Rockstar has made almost all of the Mt. Chiliad mystery clues pretty ambiguous and able to be analyzed in a multitude of ways. Examples... Mural (could be a mountain or the top part of the map)..Red icon (could be an eye or a UFO)..Bolts (could represent lightning or radio broadcast)...Altruist glyphs (could represent the Sun or a UFO).

It is clear that R* has given most of the Mt. Chiliad clues multiple possible interpretations in order to keep us guessing and add depth to the hunt. For this reason, I believe that some of the clues that we have analyzed in the past and have been considered "solved clues" have been incorrectly analyzed. It's easy to place a meaning on a clue that makes some sense and then move on and forget about it, exactly how everyone assumed that the bolts obviously meant lightning back in September...before somebody realized that ONLY rain was needed, and so we changed our viewpoint.

I think the key is to change our viewpoint and re-analyze some of our past clues that might otherwise be considered solved. I think that the biggest one that needs some re-analyzing is the zig-zag glyph. (http://i.imgur.com/mbCptMM.jpg). This glyph has been widely accepted as a mountain with a zagging path (simply just telling you to go to Chiliad), and I believe it has been largely forgotten about because of this. The fact that this clue is titled 'mountain-clue' in the files has lead people to naturally assume that its a depiction of a mountain, since the other clues have obvious names like moon-clue and rain-clue. I on the other hand, believe that if this clue has an alternate meaning other than just "go to the mountain', it is obviously a lot more difficult than the other clues, and R* would have been more careful naming this clue in the files. Also, this glyph is the only glyph with it's counterpart found in the sand as a different texture, so its definitely possible that they are named for there location instead of what they depict (mountain-clue and sand-clue). I know this has been a touchy subject for those who firmly believe that the glyphs have ALL been named for what they depict, but all I ask here is that you keep an open mind.

That being said, I'm sure many of you know that I'm more in favor of the zig-zag glyph depicting a radio tower. (http://i.imgur.com/WR1n6tI.jpg). The biggest reason being the sand glyph. This glyph has an arrow that points directly at the radio tower in Sandy shores, and is accompanied by the Wow! radio signal. (http://i.imgur.com/l8rPMxt.jpg) (http://i.imgur.com/Xj5bERH.jpg). Along with the depiction...these three independent elements of the glyph can all be interpreted as meaning the same thing...and that is why I strongly believe this is a radio tower reference.

With this in mind, after analyzing the Shandy Shores radio tower that the glyph points at...it is actually more likely to be a cell phone tower. This tower has 'Badger' dishes on top of it, which is one of the cell phone companies in the GTA universe. (http://i.imgur.com/NeGLw2X.jpg). Since Franklin is the only playable character who is guaranteed to survive AND is also the only character with a Badger phone, I believe that this sand glyph could be telling us that Franklin is the key to the mystery. (Micheal has an iFruit and Trevor has a Fade phone). The Badger dishes on top of this tower are a key element to the tower (especially since Badger dishes are not a widely re-used texture), and I believe that part of the clue is telling you that Franklin has to be used in combination with his cell phone in order to produce some kind of signal (6equj5).

I think that the key to this line of thinking is that the Fort Zancudo bunker has a 'Badger' dish on top of it as well. (http://i.imgur.com/kMKp2OK.jpg). When the UFO shines its green light down at 3 am, it is literally hitting the Badger dish. It would be a lot harder to argue that this is a coincidence. (sand glyph points to badger tower AND Ufo shines light on a badger dish). I believe that this is definitely key and that you have to do something with Franklin and his Badger phone while the light is shining down at 3 am, or something to that effect.

Though I'm not sure yet how to apply this information...I do strongly believe that the sand glyph is telling us that Franklin and his Badger phone are the key. It is also worthy to note that I have searched the map high low and in the middle for Bagder dishes and have only found 5 locations where they have them. 1) Fort Z Bunker 2) Sandy Shores Badger Tower 3) Badger tower near satellite array 4) Maze building (tallest in LS) 5) Tinkle building (cell phone company in competition with Badger).
Please keep all of this info in mind, and lets try to help each other spark some new creative ideas. Happy hunting my friends (http://i.imgur.com/tjH8caP.jpg).
