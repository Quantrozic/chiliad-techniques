First we get several Native American references from Lamar and Franklin..

Lamar.. "that's apache blood in me, homie. You lucky I didn't do a flying tomahawk and scalp they motherfucking ass." - Franklin's response.. "We can't repo the assets of a dead man, big sitting chief asshole!"

followed later by Franklin sneakily dropping another Native American reference..

Franklin's aunt leaves to exercise with friends. Lamar arrives asking if that is F's aunt, Franklin responds "Yeah, thank god I missed her crazy ass. She spirit walking and shit now man.." This occurs while his aunt is exercising so the when you hear it, use of the phrase "spirit walk" seems to make sense like a clever turn of words related to her positive frame of mind and exercising. But if you look it up it the term "spirit walk" is used by Native Americans to refer to the religious act of communing with spirits, sometimes accomplished by taking peyote and hallucinating. Sound familiar?

Are there more peyote hunt clues elsewhere? I think we are all familiar with the peyote symbol included in the green section of the Doomsday Mural but I found it again as a symbol on a rack server during the Doomsday Heist.

peyote graphic for reference

If the peyote hunt was added years later but clues were already in the original single player game, are there more clues to be found? Seems like we should be able to find clues related to the murder mystery, also added as part of the enhanced version. If so then I'm shifting gears and using those examples as templates to look for clues to stuff mysteries we haven't solved yet aka The Hunt.

Lastly, has anyone else noticed anything interesting in game related to Native Americans? It seems like if you dig in, as we do, a lot of the game deals with cultures, heritage, and history. If you pay attention there are little Native American references sprinkled here and there like on signs, and but not really written into the story line. It like the ghost of their culture haunts the game, pretty much like real life. How interesting that they seem to play such a small part of the game's action yet some of the tiny amount of single-player addon content was basically the GTA take on a big part of their religion?
