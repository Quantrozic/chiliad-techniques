I have been focusing my hunt around mainly the hippie artwork. I had previously posted that I noticed the three stars in the ground, all different colors could potentially represent the three characters. I still haven't decoded which color belongs to which character. But upon looking at the artwork from the side view on top of the red circle with the yellow glyphs, I noticed a pattern in the color. Red and Yellow are used most signicantly throughout the entire artwork including the words and glyphs. I noticed that on the spot with the Yellow glyphs on red which is on a separate hill to itself has windows in it, to represent that this is a building to which in specific I'm not sure. On top of the artwork the color changes to Red on Yellow with the glyphs equaling rain (thunderstorm).

I'm working on a more solid find but I believe that the Yellow represents Franklin as he walks the path shown in the artwork. I also believe that Red is represents both Michael and Trevor depending on who you keep alive. Finally, I think the blue star that is with the Red and Yellow star that is found in the middle could be to represent if you had all three characters alive.

There are some flaws in this I realize and I will recieve hate for not fully testing this theory, but this sub needs to understand that we are here to help each other solve this not to criticize or doubt someones theory.
