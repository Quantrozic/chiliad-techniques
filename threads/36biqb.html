Hey guys. I am here with a question that I really hope gets an answer.

So about a month ago, my Xbone broke. So I had to get a new one. Something was wrong with its hard-drive, so I couldn't transfer the hard-drive to my new one. So lucky me, all of my saves from games like GTA 5 were gone. :P

So yeah. For the past month, I've been working on getting a new 100%. It actually feels good to play the story again with some of the new things I have in mind mystery-wise, like the Maze Bank's importance.

Anyways, I have completed all main missions, all Strangers & Freaks, and all random events. All that's left is Hobbies & Pastimes and Miscellaneous. Well, here's the problem I am encountering. I cannot get the last two Street Races from Hao to spawn in, and those are both needed for 100%. I've done almost everything I can. I've slept for like ten whole days with each character, I've called Hao at least 20 times per day, and I've even driven around the area where the street race is supposed to start to no avail, as there are no street-racing pedestrians there like there should be.

There's also two other problems I have. I suck ass at golf. It took me almost four whole months to finally get the score I needed in golf for the 100% last time. No matter how much video-watching and research I do on it, I still am very terrible at it. My second problem is that, like Hao's races, the Epsilon missions are also dead for me. I've done all of them up to the one in Grapeseed with the alien detector device, and after that I did what I was instructed to (I forget what it was actually XD) and I still haven't gotten anything telling me what to do next in the Epsilon storyline. Like with the races, I've done everything I can (calling people, sleeping, driving around, etc.) and I still cannot get the next mission to spawn in.

So after researching a little I realized that the street races and the Epsilon missions are the two of the five most common things to glitch out and not work during your playthrough. The other three things are Dom's parachuting missions, any random event involving the Altruists, and the Bigfoot mission.

So my urgent question is, does anybody here know a way I could transfer a complete 100% game save onto my Xbone? When I googled some 100% saves for GTA for the Xbone and tried them out, most of them glitched the hell of out of my computer and one of them almost gave me a virus. So I am not trusting anything like that anymore. So that's why I turned here. You guys need 100% to partake in this mystery, so I'm hoping by now one of guys has stumbled upon or even made a SAFE 100% save for us Xbone/PS4 users to use. I have heard that this type of thing isn't compatible with the Xbone/PS4 like it was with the 360/PS3, but I've also heard that it is compatible. :P

So yeah. Thanks in advance.
