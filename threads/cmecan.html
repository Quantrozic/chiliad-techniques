This might be a stretch but here me out here:

So at the bottom of the Chiliad mural we see 3 Symbols, being a UFO, Cracked Egg And a Stickfigure. My theory is that these 3 symbols represent the Heists that happen in the game.

The UFO could represent "The Bureau Raid" since the UFO we see at the top of Chiliad has the FIB logo on it.

The Cracked egg could either represent "The Paleto Score" or "The Big Score". I suspect this because the bank in Paleto Bay has an egg as a logo. It could also be "The Big Score" since the fountain represents a cracked egg.

The Stickfigure with something on it's back could represent "The Merryweather Heist" where Trevor steals a supposed nuclear device. The Device could be what is on the stickfugure's back, instead of a jetpack which seems unlikely since you can't get in in Singleplayer.

This probably makes almost to no sense but seeing as after years of deciphering and still not solving the mystery I think we need to maybe find a new meaning to what the mural could mean.
