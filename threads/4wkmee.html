I've extracted the coordinates for most of the interactive doors found in the game files. Doors for e.g. Ammu-nation is, for some reason, not listed in the files I extracted from.
The list can be used with the GTA V Map Multi-Find function over at http://gta5map.glokon.org/ (just copy/paste the list)

The game files list all interactive doors, including doors that are only interactive during certain missions. Texture-only doors are not included in the list.

This verifies that there are four interactive doors in Fort Zancudo. Two just by the tower and two beneath/besides the bunker that are highlighted by the UFO beam.

No door at T01 though, which - at least for me - finally debunks the chiliadmystery.com-site. Either that, or Rock* plans on adding the content at a later date.

There's also two mysterious doors at the Humane Lab. The coordinates are in the file but I can't find the doors in-game.
