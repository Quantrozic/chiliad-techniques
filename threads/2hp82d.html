Some of you may realize this, while others may not have made this connection yet...

In a recent conversation with some friends, we were discussing (opinion of course) - why GTA V was hands-down the best GTA title to date. Can you guess what the second best GTA voted was?

Yep... San Andreas.

Now of course these are the opinions of a mere 5 or 6 people; but when pondering "why" both GTA V and San Andreas are so memorable... the answer is obvious.

They are larger than their predecessors... and they included the most "stuff".

I have long argued that GTA IV's short-comings were strictly engine based... (*No need to hate; I know GTA IV is a fan-favorite among many). But the "short-comings" in GTA IV were not related to story, graphics or anything of that nature...

But to be blunt: Rockstar went from jetpacks, Hydras, parachuting, mountain scaling, weight lifting, etc... back to the 'bare bones'.

And this is understandable... When creating a new engine for a newer-gen console, there is already A TON of work to do - and it's not as easy as just 'adding new, fun things'...

Like we saw in GTA V.

Like we saw in San Andreas.

When speculating about GTA 6 - a friend suggested it would be 'lame again' because Rockstar would be back-peddling for next-gen engine development - and fans would get a watered down, city-only (helicopter only) GTA title again...

But we now know: this is not the case.

A lot of people are bashing Rockstar for 'adding some lighting effects' and porting GTA V to PS4 and Xbox One.

But do they not realize what's in store!?

By taking the existing GTA IV/V engine and all that GTA V put in motion... Rockstar is BEYOND ahead of the game.

And for this reason, GTA 6 will BLOW OUR MINDS.

To think - with the release of GTA V as a 'port' more or less (as opposed to a whole new development)... Rockstar just bought themselves a good 2 or 3 years to develop GTA 6. But in this scenario (until I hear otherwise)... They're not painstakingly reinventing the wheel...

They have years to JUST build on what's already there...

So unlike GTA III or GTA IV which were purely city-based, 'beginner' templates for later GTA titles (yes, I know; GTA III was not a template - but later became one with the vastness of Vice City)... But unlike GTA III and GTA IV, I believe GTA 6 is going to be the best Grand Theft Auto BY FAR... and not simply because "it's the newest one, so duh!"

Just wanted to throw this out there for those who hadn't considered this.

*Mystery related?

  • I personally think Rockstar 'missed the mark' with the mural mystery - BUT IN FAIRNESS to them, did not know a mystery would ensue...

However; after seeing people clearly have too much time on their hands (myself included lol) - and that the internet is bat-sh*t crazy...

We can prepare for GTA 6 to also have the kind of easter eggs we've been dreaming of... Cryptic - insane easter eggs, with elaborate clues leading to crazy finds...

So if Rockstar DID miss the mark with the Chiliad Mystery (less than fans expected)... Fear not. GTA 6 should be nothing but added content to the already-existing next-gen ports that are coming...

And Rockstar now knows they can add easter eggs as crazy as they imagine... And people will dedicate their lives to it.

For better or worse haha...

With GTA being so complex, I believe Rockstar made the mural SIMPLE (100%, UFO on top) for a reason... for the masses...

But they see now that they can cater to both.
