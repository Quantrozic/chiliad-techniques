Tithing Upwards/How to get the good dollar

▲ 16 ptsu/chummypuddle08May 9, 202025 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Mission Order / Narrative Flow Analysis

The epsilon tracts talk about money a lot, mainly in the context of handing it over to them. But money plays an intrinsic part of this story.

It pays a part in confusing ways, asking us to get steal money by stealing vehicles, by stealing vehicles in a heist. It lets us do stuff. But not helpfully.

We unlock story elements at seemingly impossible points in the story. I only unlocked businessess late game when they didn't seem relevant to the characters personal progression.

Lots of things are tied to money for seemingly no reason. Cars that are wrecked, the stock market that seems to only be half understood. Epsilon hangs a huge wad of cash over us that we can steal with no apparent consequence.

My question is this.

We have a rough mission progression map. But do we have a solid knowledge of how the characters can make as much money as possible, in which stage of the story?

The reasoning behind the questions is that there undoubtedly puzzle pieces behind a paywall. Franklin must buy the taxi company to get the missions, to get the new girlfriend, to 'complete his story' but this can't be at the obvious point. Need money in single player? Well then just wait for the Lester assassinations and bet big. Then you can just buy everything and play bars, weed and taxis as post game content.

I'm not saying there is a correct order. But it would be useful to have some suggestions as to how people could play this story in a more realistic order.

Michaels epsilon heist has always really interested me. If we could work out the earliest he could possibly get this money, it's a significant amount that we could use. Complete the sale of the golf course? We pick up a guy whos wife says he's cheating, but he says he has actually been at the golf course at night. Anyone ever actually been the owner of the course when he claims this?

These properties don't make sense. R* have had time to update the cost and return of them. Why lock people out of making a measly income until the Lester assassinations?

Sorry this post got rambling. I guess I'm asking if there's a resource that shows when properties can be purchased, and their price. I would also love to know how to make money, but in a realistic way for the characters.

Franklin is straight, or tries to be. Towing missions, taxi work, weed deliveries; then playing the market with his winnings. Mike is a theif, always has been. Cash truck robberies? But is he doing that and the going back to his family? I need to play options a and b again but maybe Mike is trying to repeat history. Betray Trevor again, steal the big score from epsilon and make a new life with his past erased.

Trevor has his gun running. You know, the one where we had to do 600 of them to get the egg in online? When should we do them though? Definitely not after we've cleared the epsilon camp, as thats one of the first places we arm up on our first airdop. So the timing of these things matter.

These are all puzzle pieces I swear but it's so tricky to correlate things like the mission flow chart with the variables. Ie you can have x amount with Michael by this point, if you go round the map and collect all the hidden money caches.

One of the eggs will be found by roleplaying a character. Living as they would live, working their way up, completing their story.
