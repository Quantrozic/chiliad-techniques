I know that my last post was a real downer for a lot of people. I felt like i needed to at least put some things back in perspective. I personally think that we could still expect a new Easter egg in a future patch just like they did for Bigfoot.

I've thought a lot about that one. The Bigfoot Easter egg will be one year old later this month , so it's not far fetched at all to think that they could add another Easter egg with the gunrunning update which should be a very big update in itself. Here's my own thoughts and what we could maybe expect :

For me it's clear that they chose to focus entirely on online after releasing the game on PC. The only addition that SP got is the Bigfoot vs beast Easter egg. Having studied that one intensively for a long time , even if i want to be optimist , the reality strikes me back into place.

The golden peyote Easter egg assets were entirely recycled from old and already existing assets from online and SP. Everything. The Easter egg itself is insanely long to go through but the actual script is very simple. The Bigfoot model is the one from the mission. They recolored already existing peyotes using a golden texture. They placed them with a time and weather condition and used the online beast sounds to produce the call from the peyote. The whole beast mini-game is just a random path generator using npc corpses / sound calls for checkpoints location. The final fight calls for Online Halloween music while setting the weather to a storm, and then spawning the beast model from online.

Knowing all this , can we really expect a complete new Easter egg using new assets never seen before ? Can we expect cut-scenes ? i don't think so. But since they will obviously introduce A LOT of new content for online , we could expect them to also use this material to build an SP Easter egg.

Let's not forget that there's still a prop UFO that was added a while back already and never used at all. A prop is not something we can interact with at all , BUT it could be used to set the scene just like they did at the end of the Bigfoot Easter egg during the beast fight. I could imagine a few of those UFOs around in the sky appearing with a thunderstorm during a certain fight with government agents and military forces. *_-;
