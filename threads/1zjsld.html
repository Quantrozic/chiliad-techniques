I am looking for areas in which all our hunters are testing, so if you really want to contribute to this thread, you should by all means meet the following:

  • Have at least one 100% file
  • Seen the 4 UFO's yourself.
  • Currently testing and evolving your theory yourself

So testing and evolving your theory yourself, what is that? It means that you have done things in game that have lead you to believe that what you are talking about is a plausible solution. If you are operating under the theory of "I bet no one has tried this" then please, save your time and bandwidth and don't post your "drive the space docker up and down Chiliad while honking the horn and getting a blow job from Ursula until the aliens beam you up" theories.

If you have looked at clues, and you put your hunters hat on to try and confirm or debunk your own theories, I would like you to share. In the interest of convincing me you're not trolling, if your theory is really out there, provide reasoning, pictures, and/or video to support your theory.
