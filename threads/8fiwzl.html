Four Dead Bodies Found Near Stab City

Documented under:Bot-Driven Seasonal Event Monitoring

I was playing around with GTA V's Director Mode and was being chased by cops up to a place in the map just West of Stab City. There were two silos there - I dunno what to call them but they were shaped like grain silos so I just called them that - covered with graffiti that seems to be some sort of aliens having sex or something. Anyway, the silos have ladders on the sides so I climbed up whilst being chased by in-game cops and as soon as I got to the top. I saw two dead bodies on each of the silos. One male and one female each. They looked like hippies.

My curiosity got the better of me so I disabled the Director Mode's 'Wanted Status" and proceeded to take photos of the bodies. I photographed the van parked just by the road near the location. I also took a screenshot of the location on the map. They can be found in the Google Drive link below.

I posted it here in he hopes of finding explanation about how they died and what they were doing there. Hopefully, you guys can help me on that.

If you're curious too, just click the link so that you can use the photos for reference.
