(Spoilers) Online Heists dialogue and interactions discussion

Documented under:Whisper Bulk Transcription of Dialogue Banks

There are several interesting story threads that are linked to in the online heists. The purpose of this thread is to collect and analyze them to see if they reveal any clues to our mystery.

  1. Fleeca Bank - Just bonds in a safe deposit box - nothing related to our mystery it seems
  2. Prison Break - The IAA hires a nameless person (1) to pretend to be a criminal and get together a gang of other criminals to break a valued asset named Rashkovsky out of prison. The function Rashkovsky plays to the IAA is unknown (2). Once he is safely out of the country, the next heist is made available, and his name is mentioned at least once in the next heist as well, so it can be assumed whatever function he plays for the IAA helps them in their raid on Humane Labs.
  3. Humane labs - Now the nameless person is no longer pretending to be a criminal (I think he just forgot) and now pretty much lets the facts hang out in the open - he is working with the IAA and this has all been orchestrated for a bigger purpose. He reveals the next target- Humane labs. In the first setup mission (key codes), we see the first chronological appearance of the IAA interrogator woman who we meet in story mode (the woman who wants to put a flashlight up the tech support guys bum). She is also the "Michelle" character from GTA IV who Niko meets early on, and later discovers that she works for the IAA (3). In this heist she becomes the face of the IAA - giving us the entry codes to the Humane labs (obtained from sources inside the labs) (4) - and at the end she meets us and thanks us for our help, saying we won't be seeing each other again (5). This is the last we see her until Story mode, where she is interrogating Mr. Tech support guy for an unknown reason (6).
  4. Series A Funding - This reveals some interesting character development and backstory of Trevor and his company. In the first cutscene, we might assume Trevor and Ron are fresh from a crystal party, and they are finally going to do what they have been talking about for so long - create their own drug ring. They have us steal drugs from the Vagos, bikers, and other gangs in Los Santos, and stuff all the drugs into Impotent Rage statues (7). Eventually Trevor tries to sell the drugs to a buyer who is clearly a Fed, and they spring a trap on him. He gets away with just one Impotent Rage statue full of drugs, which he still keeps until Story mode when it gets broken. At the end of the heist, Trevor gives all the money he had saved up to the heist crew, and he gets busted, leaving him with nothing but that statue - setting the scene for story mode events. UPDATE: Sometime during this heist, probably in just before the first cutscene where Trevor and Ron walk in all excited about their new company, and before story mode events (after prologue), Trevor creates the UV map. It has the TP industries logo, prior to changing to TP incorporated (10) which helps to place the map chronologically very early in his startup company's history.
  5. Pacific Standard Heist - There is some dialogue in the setup mission "Signal" where Lester talks about the best crew members he has ever worked with - The best hacker is himself, the best planner is a snitch (8), the best body man eats people (Trevor), the best crew leader can't stop lying (Michael), the best signal guy communes with animals (Avi). Need more analysis of this heist dialogue
  6. Evidence of future heists coming (9): After you complete Pacific Standard, Lester calls you congratulating you specifically on the Pacific standard heist, and during that phone call he also mentions there will be more work coming your way sometime soon - In the same way he says it after every other heist, but no dialogue is looped, it is uniquely written for that finale. We also know there have been significant changes to Fort Zancudo, a suspected heist area due to the unused interiors there (server room, tower, bunker, new hangar)

I have put several numbers throughout this summary, which can be jumping off points for discussion on aspects that are not made 100% clear to the player. If you have other points, please let me know and I will add to the OP. There will certainly be others, as we need to document the dialogue for all heists more intensely.
