Random thoughts and questions (motivation)

▲ 1 ptsu/TripleduzJan 9, 20158 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Trainer-Based Weather Cycling

I'm jotting down a bunch of random thoughts. Theres been a lot of negative thinking and approaches to this mystery lately, I needed some motivation. So below I'll ramble on in hopes of reminding myself there's a lot out there unexplained. Maybe some will be new to newer hunters and maybe some will reenergize past discussions that's been forgotten.

Wall of text upcoming:

  • I don't see how the glyphs are X's when there's more than 5 glyphs.
  • I don't see how the jet pack figure symbolizes Ft Zancudo. Put it this way; I played GTA:SA on release and again in 2010, beat it twice, and never saw the jet pack. Not everyone plays with online help. Kids 16-18yrs old playing V were 5-7yrs old when SA came out. A lot of people can't stand the graphics enough to go back and play it. SA was in a completely different "GTA Universe". I can't see Rockstar excluding some people from a reward based on knowledge of a distant game.
  • Most theories have no account for the lightning bolts other than rain at 3am. This "grand Easter egg" would be the only one of my knowledge that allowed you to use a cheat code to see. Cycling through the weather cheat you can clearly see rainy weather is different from thundery weather. If there's no chance of lightning on regular rainy weather... why would bolts be on the mural? And why aren't bolts used on the glyph???
  • Why are these "X's" much more faded than the rest of the drawing? Why do 4/5 look more like an airplane, a bird, or even a satellite? The bottom right X is just half a dash that isn't even symmetrical to the others.
  • Not many theories have credible explanations for the lines throughout the mural. If we are to believe this mural means anything then everything on it means something.
  • Im also not sold on the bottom left "UFO" directing attention towards the Hippie Camp. There's a glyph there already, why need the mural indicate it as well? If it's just because the eyes would relate that drawing to the UFO car at the HC, why not Bishop's WTF building?
  • Sticking with that, some theories think both the top pic and bottom left pic indicate UFO's. Well...why have two separate drawings on one mural represent the same thing, which ironically, are 2 UFO's that look exactly alike?
  • On that note, what kind of "grand Easter egg" did Rockstar bestow on us when it requires 100%, 3AM, AND rain at the top of a mountain ...but looks exactly the same as another Easter egg that only requires one of those variables?
  • Staying on the mural's bottom left supposed UFO, does it not look like someone is inside if this is indeed a UFO? does to me...
  • Why do beams of light come from the bottom of each UFO when zooming in? Doesn't it seem like the MC UFO is ticking?

(Side note ~Once I gained 100% my first (theory/goal/test) was to get all 3 characters hanging out on top of Chiliad at 3am during natural rain. I even managed to dress them all in black suits before meeting and stole a FIB car. This took quite a while, literally hung out for 4 in game days to the point nobody was even talking to each other as I drove aimlessly around...but I finally did it, with FIB sirens on and all...to no avail obviously!~)

  • Why does a crate, unique in appearance, show up during the mission where you clear the Altruist's camp with Trevor? Some say to provide cover, but using a crate model found nowhere else in the game? And for a a mission I've beat plenty of times without taking cover at all? Hmmm, ok...
  • Why do most people assume you have to be 100% to solve the mystery? A trigger couldn't be made at any other point in the game? Better yet a specific point in the game?
  • The game shows us early in its progression there is an unmarked "event" with Simieon that can actually be missed all together if you advance the story too far. Clearing the Acamp is also missable. Are there more? What parts of the storyline would these be more prevalent? How about when Mike and T are exiled from the city because of Martin? That'd be an ideal situation to hide a missable event in. (I've done some testing here. Flew a chopper straight to the LSPD you can enter and hung out while the gangbangers came for me. They were greeted with my personal police protection, but of course kept coming back. I stayed in the city for quite some time and had a few instances hiding out in parking garages where I thought I lost them.)
  • Sure, hackers have presented a large share of their code related discoveries, but have they came forth with everything? We recently had a guy here (DCMillions) that dove back into the code and found a lot of stuff that was either previously missed, or purposely not shared. Plus they are limited on console not to mention the many areas/ways R* could hide something should they choose to. Do people really think R* is going to label a major reward as "jet-pack.obj"??
  • Any mention of the known alien egg, alien HUD, FZ bunker having code for an openable door, Lt. Kyle P. Slater (?), collision detection on Observatory roof, downtown UFO, the _tc_ce objects over the ocean the hackers couldn't figure out, etc... will all be greeted with "cut content". That is half the battle here, relating what code was left in the game inactive, and why? For DLC? Purposely to confuse the hackers? Laziness?
  • Why were those things like Kyle P's # and an alien egg file nobody has seen but clearly has a model, left in the transition to ps4 yet the FIB interaction at the satellite array and FIB blueprints changed? It took more work to completely change those two things than to simply eliminate one of those codes so it can't be laziness.
  • And further, why did the other government interaction at the shack near the Humane Labs stay the same? Why do these agents (supposedly IAA) appear to be checking the ground with one even crouching down and grabbing a handful of dirt?
  • There was a post recently linked from a year ago where a hacker (GTA_Bumhoffer) talked about a uniformed Lt. Or General soldier at the fort he killed that remained dead. ALL others respawn. He even drove away a good bit, waited, came back and the dude was still dead.
  • GTA_Bumhoffer also mentioned what, to him, seemed like a sequence where a black SUV sped away from the apartments located behind the FZ hangars. The dude is definitely not a troll and only had 2-3 comments on his post that were demanding "pics or it didn't happen"...lol...
  • Some people try to stress the fact that "nothing has been found in over a year"...but that isn't true. There was just a post about weird sounds occurring at (and only at) specific locations on the map. There was just a texture model on a rock found in the ocean that I've never seen before underwater or on land. People will then say "well nothing important to the mystery has been found"....but who knows what's important and what isn't when the mystery is still....a mystery?
  • It wasn't that long ago many people didn't realize some of your contacts would actually answer at random times and contain dialogue not heard before. Particularly after doing a mission with this contact. Call Stretch after the mission you do when he's relased from prison.
  • It wasn't that long ago nobody knew that you could call the police during the random event (deal gone bad) that gives a nod to the movie "No country for Old Men" and it resolves the event in an entirely different way?
  • Why is the MC mural the only one taken seriously? The textile mural gets thrown away because people relate to it as a "karma theory". The initial reaction is "karma?!?! In a GTA game!!!" Well, not that I'm leaning this way, but it's not like we don't have 3 totally different characters to choose who does what with...right? Karma would be a first in a GTA game but so are the 3 playable protags. The wide range of decisions each are faced with (and optional endings) is fairly new to the series as well.
  • Alas, to me, I took the textile mural more as a challenge by difficulty if you will. Haven't GTA's always been ridiculously easy? Save for a few car chases here and there Ive never struggled through any mission...ever. But cut off the assisted aim, the radar, and the HUD...and I'm having problems at 27%. Could this game possibly be like Dragon Age 1, where if you play from the beginning on hardest difficulty (heroic I think) and never change it the ending is different? There's no text prompting you to do this like there was in Dragon Age, but could it still be in play...?...
  • Could a hard 100%, gold missions, heist choices, beating the game on a time limit, factor in with the difficulty theme?

My bottom line is NO THEORY IS FACT, by definition of itself. Question everything. Test and report back (including the intangibles) even if nothing is found. This may save time for others that are thinking the same. Forget what has been found and linking that to the mural. Nothing is concrete. The only reason people are taking the easy way out is from frustration...and I understand because the scale of variables that could potentially come into play is immense! IMO, and it's probably too late and/or too much work for this idea...but...the best thing for this sub would be to have a general discussion area, and separate area of test results only so hunters didn't have to wade through all comments in all threads to see if an idea was tested, how it was tested, and results of the tests.

That's it, believe it or not...carry on with more if you'd like...
