Not sure if anyone has found this yet or not but I figured I'd post it just in case it ends up being a clue to the Mt. Chiliad mystery. I was flying around the observatory at night when I noticed the sculpture at the top of the obelisk in the observatory courtyard. It looks like a sphere mysteriously floating in space with concentric rings orbiting around it. Around the obelisk on the ground is a series of pathways with embedded lines and shapes, and the main pathway leads from the Obelisk directly to two large ornate doors leading into the observatory. Anyways as you move around the obelisk and look up at the sculpture at the top the orbiting rings start to make a shape very similar to the all seeing eye or ufo symbol that is at the top of the Mt. Chiliad map in the cable car station. So I'm walking around looking at this thing from various angles and eventually I get on top of the observatory directly over the two main doors and guess what happens. The sculpture forms a nearly perfect eye shape directly over Mt. Chiliad way in the background! It's almost a perfect representation of what we see in the map painted on the cable car station wall. The top of the obelisk even creates the little flat part at the top of the mountain right below the eye. And it looks like if you were to draw a line directly from the two large front doors of the observatory, down the sidewalk to the obelisk, that line would continue all the way to the top of Mt. Chiliad. It may even connect with the sunken UFO beyond Mt. Chiliad but I haven't had time to verify that yet. Check it out

http://imgur.com/a/5c9FO

What's also interesting is that the two large doors to the observatory have what looks like 4 boxes or slots in the middle of them, and at night the two on the left door are glowing bright yellow while the two on the right remain closed. I'm wondering if the glowing boxes represent certain actions or objectives that need to be completed, and when all those objectives are completed and all 4 lights are glowing that the doors will open revealing either one of the 3 prizes or another clue to solving the mystery. If it were a prize it could be the cracked egg, since on the map the all seeing eye connects with the egg following a straight line. It might be that each of these three prizes are located behind large doors somewhere in the map. One in the large doors in the cable car station, one in the doors of the observatory, and possibly one in Fort Zancudo in the elevator that could lead to an underground bunker. I'm not sure.

What's also interesting is that the UV activated special addition map has the words "shoot for the stars" written over the observatory location. The obelisk sits on a base that is 3 concrete steps shaped like stars. Could those be the stars they are referring to? I tried to see if there was another UFO directly above the obelisk way up in the atmosphere but couldn't find one. Anyways I'm not sure what all this means but it seems too coincidental that the obelisk shape would randomly line up with Mt. Chiliad the way that it does for it to not be a clue. Anyone have any ideas about what it could mean?
