I don't think the whale is much related to the chiliad mystery, but I remember a while back, spectrograms of sounds heard were being analyzed for clues. Particularly, the sounds heard in the ocean. That spectogram was analyzed by some redditors and tried to be broken down and rearranged for something useful.
I think that 52-Herts spectogram is associated with the whale which is the "world's loneliest whale".

The 52-hertz whale is an individual whale of unidentified species, which calls at the very unusual frequency of 52 Hz. This pitch is a much higher frequency than that of the other whale species with migration patterns most closely resembling this whale's.
