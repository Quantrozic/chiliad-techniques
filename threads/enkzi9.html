I had been doing research on the “Defender of the Faith” and it lead to a few great finds. One of which helped us with placement of a mission we were having difficulty with for a long time. But the other find I thought this community could find intriguing.

http://degenatron.com

At first I thought this was a fan site that might of released recently because of the new Arcade DLC, but after digging further I found it’s legit from Rockstar and was posted about in a Rockstar News Wire... get this... in 2012.

https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/46681/rockstar-throwback-degenatron-vice-city.html

The description of Defender of the Faith was the exact same as it is now.

"save the green dots with your fantastic flying Red Square”

But go from 2012 to 2013 and GTAV released. We play a missions where Mike visits Lester house and for those who leave no stone unturned you may have seen this poster of Defender of the Faith on Lester wall.

https://imgur.com/VrrS4SY

Now you will notice that art work from Lester poster is different from the Epsilon enrich art found on the Arcade. There’s a reason for that. What I never read before was the smaller print above.

“The Degenatron classic comes to the big screen. Green Dots Fight Angry Red Square”

The art work for Lester Poster is for the Movie adaptation of the Arcade game and therefore different in almost every way.

Angry red square 🟥 fantastic flying square.

“Ahh who’s the square now?”

https://imgur.com/Jltc8N9
