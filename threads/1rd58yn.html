I’m new to the chiliad mystery so I’m sorry if this has been stated or answered already.

I was recently doing the ULP missions for career progress in online and during the final mission ‘clean up’ you go to the missile silo in mt chiliad. In the room with the murals the room is split into 4 sections 1 for each mural. On the red double doors on the bottom floor each door has a symbol. The purple section has the rotation maze (from the purple mural), the green section has the circle with 8 lines coming out of it and circles on each end (from the red mural), the yellow section has the DNA symbol (from the green mural), and the red section has the 4 level square (from the purple mural).

I’m not sure what it means or if it means anything but my guess is would be that purple is either the start or end of the whole puzzle and solving one will help solve the next. That’s my thinking because purple is the only mural to have its symbol pop up twice while yellow doesn’t have any symbols appear on the doors. Following this logic the order to solve the mysteries would be purple, red, green, yellow or reverse the order.

Please drop some feed back if you know what the symbols on the doors mean if it’s already been solved 🙏
