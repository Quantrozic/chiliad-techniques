Stream report - January 16th 2015

Documented under:Object-Detection Sweeps of Stream VOD Archives

Hey Everyone,

Today was another stream. This time 'only' 4,5 hours.
We tested a whole lot of stuff again, and actually found some stuff this time around. I'll sort the results this time by three categories:

Debunked Theories

  • We tested Franklin on hippy camp glyphs, Trevor at Chiliad and Michael at Mt. Gordo -> Trevor activates ufo, we switch to franklin, look around, switch to michael, do yoga, then look around and switch back to the other characters. We did this at 3 AM while the ufo became active, at 6 AM (someones theory) and at 7 AM (Jeff's Theory). Nothing happend unfortunetely. So basicly everything including the 3 characters standing on those 3 spots has been debunked.
  • The FZ ufo 'shield' is not destroyable by the stun gun or any other weapon.
  • Driving the space docker to the observatory at 2 PM doens't do anything.
  • Driving the space docker on max speed through a tunnel sounding the rare horn doesnt trigger a backtothefuture type reference.
  • Trying to initiate a conversation as franklin with the Altruïst guy standing at the cliff doesn't work, it will trigger a fight with all the altruist before you can reach him.
  • Using the space monkey mask while doing the mission "did somebody say yoga?" doesnt trigger anything extra.
  • There is nothing located on the most south area of the map, all the way to the west area of the map, to the north of the map (see finds for more info)

New finds

  • You can actually use a boat to go a large distance across the sea. We are currently looking for 'hidden' islands around the map borders. So far we have done south and west borders of the map.
  • potentially big find When at fort zancudo, having a 4 star wanted level, when you walk up the control tower, and go straight the first door left (to the minigun area), sometimes there spawns a guy, sitting on a chair, begging for his life. He is a marine, and he is begging for his life instead shooting you because you have a 4 star wanted level. You can stay there for as long as you want, but he won't shoot at you. Maybe we can trigger some sort of event with him, or conversation, or make him give us access somehow to the bunker?
  • 1 guy, just 1 guy at FZ has got officer clothes or army clothes, while all the other soldiers are marines.
  • The elevators in the control tower at FZ are different from the one in the bunker. If you see the 'floor -1' sign at the control tower, you will see that it is red, low-res and shootable (when you shoot it, it creates bullet holes). The one at the bunker is high-res, blue-ish and you can't shoot it. Even more, it is therefor probably the same one used in the human labs mission, which is confirmed to show both -1 and 0 as floor levels. To me, this is confirmation that the elevator is triggerable. when we trigger it, it will go to floor 0 in the game code and then the doors will open. (the fact that the model is already there, is just easier to code.)
  • When you use a cargobob to land a (SMALL!) boat in the lake at mt. gordo, when you pick it up again and fly down, it causes a glitch to spray water from the boat. Kinda neat.

New Ideas

  • investigate the east and north side of the map.
  • someone posted the idea of checking the top of the I from vInewood at 3am in a thunderstorm.
  • something more with the sattelites.
  • investigate fort zancudo (especially that dude sitting on his chair) more.

As for the rest, I still have some stuff to check on my list, so that will also be tested next stream.

Again, thanks for watching! I really love doing this all together guys. It makes the community better and we are really working together to solve this. Love it. Special thanks to Jeff for moderating! as well as to mpxgaming for the find with the boat. Sorry for the fail the first time haha! I think a cargobob was the only thing i did not try in the game yet.

See you guys hopefully this weekend when I'm streaming again!

-Q
