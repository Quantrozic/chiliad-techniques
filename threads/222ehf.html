Stealth mode and other stats

▲ 5 ptsu/ambullyApr 3, 201415 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Mission-Replay Flag Diffing

I have a 100% file with Ursula sacrificed. So I decided to start a new game to keep her alive and create rain storms for obvious reasons. As I restarted, I noticed in the mission to repo Jimmy's new car, the amount of emphasis used to teach players how to play using stealth tactics. It stated that stealth ability can be increased by stealth takedowns. Now I've always been a "go in guns blazing" type of GTA player so I never really paid much attention. But as I replayed it I thought to myself, what if coming back with your story complete includes full stats including stealth mode? What if a full stealth mode stat allows for easier access in ft. Z and acts as a trigger in and of itself? I ask this mainly because the game doesn't really need you to be a stealthy player at all. Maybe lack of stealth missions make think this way or again, my style of playing doesn't need stealth. So what are your thoughts on this reddit?
