Zancudo bunker elevator opens in Content Creator

▲ 52 ptsu/the_monotonistJan 2, 201421 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Livestreamed Group Hunts ?

It's been ages since I last visited this place, but I thought I would throw this out there in case in helps with future investigations.

If you take a look at 2:42:00 of my depressingly long GTAV stream last night (found here ) you can see that the elevator doors of the Zancudo bunker open when visited in the Content Creator.

Why this is significant:
All the other elevators that we've found so far (Zancudo control tower, for example) will open, but the elevator itself won't function. This elevator is normally locked shut, but opens when accessed in the creator. This means that Rockstar specifically scripted these doors to stay shut, and weren't there merely decoration.

Note:
The content creator allows you to access nearly all interior spaces that are normally locked shut in Online and Offline. This includes police stations, banks, stores, strip clubs, protagonist homes, and the entire humane research lab. The entire first 4 hours of my stream (linked above) consists of me checking these places out.
