Could Deaf Art and The Hand Eye symbol be related to the mystery?

I was doing some cult symbology and gta 5 art research and something took my attention. The idea was to find hidden clues in the art style from the oeuvre gallery and I started with Deaf Art (bizarre painting style based on emotions and represented in exaggerated forms and colours) because of the similarities.. Then I found this mural from 2013: https://handeyes.wordpress.com/tag/betty-g-miller/
This is a deaf art work about the Hand Eye cult and took my attention for obvious reasons.. So my curiosity made me search about the hand eye symbol meaning, origin, etc.. And I found this: https://m.warpaths2peacepipes.com/native-american-symbols/hand-eye-symbol.htm
I found interesting specially parts like "The meaning of the Hand Eye is obscure, its true meaning has been lost in the midst of time. However there seems to be a common belief that the Hand Eye symbol is related to gaining entry to the Upperworld (Heaven), in other words a portal. A portal is a magical doorway that connects two distant locations and provides an entry point from one world to another. The Eye-in-the-Hand symbol is believed to be representative of a supreme deity and is solar (and therefore Upperworld) in origin. To reach the Upperworld the deceased had to undertake a journey along the Path of Souls, the Milky Way." and "The Algonquin people believed that crossing a bridge in the shape of an enormous serpent would lead to the road of the afterlife. All those who had led and honest life would cross the bridge but others would fall into the waters below and turn into serpents.", which again made me click on the horned serpent symbol link: https://m.warpaths2peacepipes.com/native-american-symbols/horned-serpent-symbol.htm
If you take a look at the ancient rock painting you'll see the similarities with the snake on oeuvres pic. And if you read the meaning of the horned serpent in that mythology you'll find some intriguing connections...
All I want from all you reddit hunters is some additional opinions before going full crazy with this thing. Don't want to waste your time but I'll appreciate if some redditors give it a little look. Thanks for your time bro-uncles
