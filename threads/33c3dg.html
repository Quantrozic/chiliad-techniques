Sacrifices on the Rock and Weather

▲ 3 ptsu/Vaderfart500Apr 21, 20159 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Procedural Weather-Cycle TriggeringProcedural Weather-State Sweep ?Trainer-Based Weather Cycling ?

First off... Long time lurker, first time poster. I've been following the Chiliad mystery since around Feb of 2014. I got deep into it on PS3 and now, having bought a PS4, I am back on it. I am currently 80% complete, have done the story before everything else, already activated the Altruist camp, got Ursula as a booty call for Franklin, went for the ending that leaves all three alive and not killed any of the 'friends'.

I have an open mind, so all the theories I find interesting. Like most hunters I have my own pet theory which, after I've gained 100% on this playthough, I am going to try out on my next playthrough.

The Altruist camp glyphs and the glyphs on the mountain are, I feel, made by the same person/people. The mural is also by the same person/people.

The Altruists make sacrifices on the rock. The people you bring to the Altruist Camp are sacrifices. In order to unlock whatever-the-hell-it-is we unlock(jetpack, dinosaur, etc), we have to use the mural as guide to when to drop off sacrifices to the Altruists.

Each glyph represents a different type of weather/time of day/month. Ie, half saucer represents fog, mountain with trail equals clear, moon equals night, saucer on own equals day, and cloud equals rain.

As far as I know, you drop off four people before the cut scene and showdown. If we take this event to be a fifth sacrifice, ie. Trevor is sacrificing the Altruists, then the five sacrifices add up to the five crosses. So, at the end of the killing(which I think should include at least one of the Altruists killed on the rock with the baseball bat) you should get an event.

This, I believe, is one of the possible solutions. It is at least Trevor's solution. By doing this he gets a jetpack(possibly).

I personally feel that the mural has different results for all the characters, which means that killing one at the end of the story doesn't end the possibility of the mystery.

Franklin's part of the mystery probably involves the space docker and driving, with Michael some sort of shooting challenge(Shoot for the Stars, Penris[sniPer]), while Trevor is, as above, a killing rampage. This means that, in order to be successful, we will need to use the special abilities to some extent.

I have sadly watched a lot of great thinking gone out of this sub, with many, many people going down the 'stoner Jesus toast' route of just bunging anything in and seeing faces in clouds. I think a good rule of thumb should be that you need to be able to make a good story out of the solution; it needs to link with the characters and the themes of the game. If it sounds like random shit, it's probably just random shit. That being said, all the people bringing random shit help as it brings to attention elements that hunters may have missed.

The amount of detail I have taken in from this game due to thsi mystery is intense. It gives me a greater understanding of just how much work is in this game, how much detail and craft. I have spent hours just looking at stuff like the Maze building, looking at the designs and details, enjoying the depth of the art direction. I truly believe that the makes have embedded some really interesting stuff in here, and even if this mystery comes to naught, it's given me a profound understanding and appreciation of this game, and for that it is entirely worthwhile.

TLDR: This game is awesome. Drop people off at Altruist with mural as guide to weather. Kifflom!
