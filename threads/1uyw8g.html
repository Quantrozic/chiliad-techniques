I just wanted to take a moment to thank every single one of you here on this sub. Especially those who have questioned me or doubted me.

This investigative nature of this sub has me blown away. I've personally spoken with members of a global community that have come here to unify and work towards one goal. The community coming together is what will solve this.

In these upcoming days, I want to remind you all to remain vigilant. To think for yourself and to question EVERYTHING about this hunt. I've reason to believe there may be disinformation in the sub that dates as far back as the beginning. I've personally been fed some of this disinformation during the whole WoW! signal kick I was on back when investigating Gordo. The wow signal apparently cannot be transcribed into audio due to it's frequency being 1420mhz, which is roughly but I was still provided a link when asked. This says to me that we have to do our own research in this thing.

Recently someone posted this video about "Schwa". They're fake. The video is also rife with symbolism found in GTA. Most notably the rocket and the Bill Binder logo found on cultstoppers. I was born on a bed of tinfoil and I've never ever heard of schwa. I've spent many late nights awake reading about the different government conspiracies, especially with the advent of youtube. I tried going to the website provided at the end of the video and it was a dead link credited to "William Barker". A quick search turned up nobody in the conspiracy world.

Thank you for your time and remember space travelers. Do what you feel is right in your heart, but don't be afraid to question - even yourself. That's what this hunt is truly about. We're all seeking something, but none of us are sure exactly what we will find. Maybe we're meant to learn something about ourselves through this whole thing?

Kifflom, and don't be afraid to question - but question the answers you get too.
