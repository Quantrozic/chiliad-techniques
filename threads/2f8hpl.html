I'm just doing something i love : using google to find old web pages (older than GTA itself, for everything refering to UFO encounters etc). I hope to find IRL references published before the success of GTA completly "erased" them.

I try names of characters or places from GTA universe, sometimes i change a letter or two, i keep in mind that all this is a parody...etc. I simply change my search settings to ignore anything after the year i want. (Obvious, i know, just sharing the idea to do this for those getting bored).

So I was looking for solid references about Epsilon IRL, setting google search to ignore post-2004 results. For exemple, I found "My Epsilon Daily News"

This website which kept active for a long time is really nice with surprising links for those who were too young to use internet at this time (back in these years, internet was expensive too) Like this one, selling the book of Darius Fontaine http://www.fearitdoit.com/ Cris Fromage's ennemy (a kind of pre-cultstopper?).

Discovering all this is really nice, even if i do it years later. Here is the website > https://sites.google.com/site/programmeepsilonproject/

They went really deep into the Epsilon mysteries & origins. Here you can learn a lot about the famous "non-cult".

You can also find all of their Epsilon hunt in GTA SA.
Just wanted to share this nice website and the idea of searching for IRL infos older than GTA itself (obvious, yes, but recently we all were facing code lines or angry peds...exploring IRL references give some rest).
