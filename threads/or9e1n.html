Easter Eggs and Mysteries in the Los Santos Tuners DLC

Documented under:DLC / Cut Content Archaeology ?

In this post I'd like to address some of the more cryptic/mysterious things that have been added into the game as a part of the LS Tuners update.

  1. Alien Egg is back... again.

During the ECU Contract (the train robbery one), you can find the Alien Egg in one of the containers. Does this mean anything? Perhaps. Or perhaps not. It's the n-th time the Egg has been added into the game, so we can assume this is just an easter egg.

2. Don Percival's files

With this summer DLC we got brand-new Robbery Contracts into GTA Online. Most of them revolve around stealing money or prison breaks and so on, but there's one that definitely stands out from the rest. It's the Data Contract.

In this robbery, Avi Schwartzman returns once again, and wants us to steal Don Percival's (Merryweather CEO) files. They're "weighted in gold", and are kept in a secure nuclear bunker in the Grand Senora desert.

Avi doesn't tell us once what the files are - "the less you know is better". During the upload section, a whole Merryweather army raids the docks, trying to stop Schwartzman from uploading the files. They bring dozens of men, multiple choppers, or even a Half-Track and a few Weaponized Tampas with mounted miniguns.

The mystery of the files is often mentioned by Kenny and Sessanta, with them saying that maybe it contains something the general public needs to know.

What are the files? Perhaps we will find out when the two next Contracts release. Right now, only the Union Depository heist has been teased, no info whatsoever about the second one.

3. Cliffford's Juggernaut is back

During the Agency Raid Contract, you can find Avon's Juggernaut in a locked room in the IAA facility. Why is it there? The game indicates that the Juggernaut is dead, but why is it here?

We know for sure that back in 2019 during the Diamond Casino Heist, Cliffford's army was still roaming the streets, even after we killed Avon.

But Lester mentions that Cliffford's last drive, the one on Hertz's neck, was nowhere to be found.
