Mural Mini Breakdown

I’m going to break them all down individually using only in game references, except for the yellow/golden mural, it seems to be the only one that uses real world images. To keep things clear I’m separating facts from theories, for example…

Facts - Certain changes to the map online do not show up in story mode and vice versa, Diamond Casino, hatches on Chiliad, crashed Buzzard at Kortz etc.

Theory - The mystery is only available in story mode (parts of the mystery can NOT be seen online).

I’m going to try to base most of my theories on in game facts and under the assumption that it can be done on consoles without going into the code (I’m on PS5), so without further ado here we go…

The Green Mural - The Beast Hunt

The Beast Hunt

I started with this one because it’s the one that has been solved and in my opinion sets the template as for what to expect with this hunt.

Facts:

\-Features collectible (Peyotes) along with the number required (27)

\-Features DNA, molecular structures and evolution imagery (Peyotes turn you into animals)

\-Collecting all 27 Peyotes unlocks the Golden Peyote (Bigfoot)

\-The mural features a sound wave (Bigfoot has a special “growl” mechanic\_

\-You have to get the Golden Peyotes in the correct order on the correct days at the right time under specific conditions Sun-Sat, 5:00AM - 8:30 AM, weather must be fog or snow

\-That growl mechanic is used to find the next piece of the puzzle (The Beast)

\-Doing so leads you to a final showdown with the Beast, the sky changes and he jumps out (event triggered)

\-Beating the Beast unlocks him (in director mode)

So a bunch of takeaways here, first, the hunt was activated by collectibles, we had to get all 27 peyotes to “activate” the golden Bigfoot peyotes, then getting the golden ones in the right order and conditions to activate the beast fight. Collectibles, reward, mechanic, day, time, location, condition, hunt, event and unlock-able.

Collectible - 27 Peyotes

Reward - Bigfoot Peyote

Mechanic - Bigfoot growl

Hunt - Golden Peyotes

Day - Sunday

Time - 5am - 8:30am

Location - Raton Canyon Trail

Condition - Snow or fog

Event - Beast fight

Unlock-able - The Beast in director mode

This is how I’m approaching these mysteries and I believe the hints are in the in-game media/businesses/websites.

Theory:

If we use the Beast hunt as an example, it stands to reason that the peyote on the green mural is a required collectible, then, possibly the other 3 mysteries involve required collectibles and finding their version of the “golden peyote/Beast”. If your reward for the 27 collectibles was Bigfoot and his new growl mechanic (required to find the next step) then we should be looking for our “golden peyotes/Beast” using the collectible rewards.
