Observations at the Altruist Camp

▲ 83 ptsu/head_bussinFeb 21, 2017111 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Hidden Map Art & Environmental Pattern Recognition ?NPC Pathfinding Observation Logging ?Tide & Water-Level Observation ?

i recently spent 24 hours (in game) exploring the altruist camp. i feel this place is often overlooked, yet it's one of the few loose ends rockstar left open.

when you finish the shootout after delivering the hitch-hikers you get a message stating 'altruist cleared' and an unopened box is left in the aftermath. venture far enough away and the box de-spawns forever.

i have experimented taking the hitch-hikers here around 3am, tried different combinations of hikers, delivered only females then only males the next playthrough, completed the deliveries shortly after trevor get's the text from ron and even attempted the deliveries based on the different music cues. still no closure after the shootout.

feeling as i may have missed something, i came back to do some old fashion ufo hunting. my first observation was how similar mt chiliad is to the mural from this vantage point. it has been debated as to which angle we should look at chiliad from, but i have always believed we are to look at it from southwest to northeast if the mural is to be taken literally. my next best theory for the viewing location would be from rehab island (west of the camp).

is the mural even an outline of mt chiliad? if it is to be taken literally, it doesn't exactly match from any angle really. is it possible that the altruist cabin and the hill it rests on, is actually what the mural is supposed to represent? i mean it kind of matches up best with the chiliad glyph and the sand glyph's arrow points directly at the altruist camp.

i've always pondered if there is some sequence we are to follow after the shootout to unlock the box. i've tried overlaying the mural over the camp in my head, hoping something might jump out at me. this may be a bit toasty, but i cant help but wonder if the 5 cabins with chimneys emitting smoke 24/7 represent the x's on the mural. here is a better look from the water tower. these chimneys roughly mimic the placement of the x's on the mural when viewing from a few vantage points and the jetpack would appear to be where the crate is found after the shootout. i tried placing sticky bombs down each one, but of course nothing happened and i was testing on a 100% file with all hitch-hikers delivered.

while hanging out at the altruist cabin, i heard an explosion. the cabin is a perfect viewing area for thelma and louise easter egg random event. not much here that i can tell, but pretty cool none the less if you have never seen this.

the camp is riddled with clues, from the numerous glyphs, to the jesus toasty jetpack man on the on the sacrificial rock. there has to be something more here. remember this glyph from earlier and how it looks similar to our path up to the altruist cabin? i decided to count the number of stone steps and found there are 88 total.

now i am not sure that there being 88 stone steps means anything, but it's interesting that here, yet again, is a number that pops up everywhere around the map. it also represents how truly amazing and how convoluted this mystery really is. i am out of ideas of what to do here at this point, but i hope this post can possibly inspire more hunters to focus their attention on the altruist camp.

maybe we've been hunting on the wrong mountain all along. after all, this mystery may take 88 steps to finally solve.
