Topanga Canyon/Tongva Valley

Today, I was collecting for UFO parts using the R* map when I noticed one of the descriptions states the part is "Near Topanga Canyon", which I found a little strange since the area is called Tongva Valley on the map. Imgur After stumbling around for about 15 minutes, I finally found the stupid part and went to steal a car by the highway. The park sign also listed the name of the area as Topanga Canyon. Imgur and Imgur

I googled Topanga Canyon. Topanga was the name the Tongva Native Americans gave the land. Apparently, there were a ton of UFO sightings at Topanga Canyon in 1992. In fact, there's a book called "UFOs Over Topanga Canyon" (http://www.amazon.com/UFOs-Topanga-Canyon-Preston-Dennett-ebook/dp/B0046LVJIQ/ref=sr_1_1?ie=UTF8&qid=1431290336&sr=8-1&keywords=ufos+in+topanga+canyon). I'm not sure what to do with this information, but I definitely think it's interesting.
