Edit 1. I just want to point that this is only for looking at locations easily on console. I realise it won't trigger any single player content, but it's real easy to jump on creator mode if you want to see a location. You can just pick a point on the map, it warps you there instantly with no loading. You can swim forever. I swam down to the hatch and heard the tapping clear as day with no submarine echo. You can float around the sea if you think there's a secret island. It's cool!

Just want to throw out a quick post talking about tools that we use to hunt, again for knowledge sharing purposes and to possibly help with the hunt. Feel free to add any of your own tools, tricks, and methods when you're out hunting for breakthroughs as well.

A lot of people talk about using director mode, as you can become birds and whatnot to glitch in places, but no one talks about creator mode online. It's become one of my main tools I use when scoping out a location. For those on console it's as close to modding as well ever see. You can float high above in the air, zoom in real close, get any angle you can think of, set basic weather, time, etc. If something peaks your interest you can switch to on foot. It's pretty neat and very helpful as you don't need to steer a helicopter while trying not to crash into a glyph, and it gets rid of sniper scope distortion.

That's all I really have to work with as I'm on Xbox one. But one thing I also want to mention is some under utilized game features that get overlooked a lot. Things like the filters you get from the murder mystery give you a different perspective, and could possibly change things in game. Sunglasses in first person add another "filter" effect as well.

And my favorite one...using the microphone! Rockstar added this feature where the game will acknowledge you are speaking into the mic, and npcs will react (so far only seems to be strippers and store clerks, maybe during heists for crowd control I think) seems like a big feature with huge potential to hide a secret in. Can you talk to trees? Maybe! (Never worked for me)

That's it from me, I was online making a "slappers only" deathmatch custom match that takes place in the hedgemaze (awesome right?!) And I thought "I bet not too many people here know how useful this function is"

What tools do you use to help your hunt run a little smoother?
