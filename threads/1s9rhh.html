Clean-Up Time

▲ 6 ptsu/NesterGroomeDec 6, 201341 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Crowdsourced 100%-Save Testing Matrix ?

Months in, and here we are, with the hint of a ghost of a shadow of a promise that there's something...

...And we've got nothing. Nothing substantial, anyway.

What do we do? Where did we go wrong? Well, I have an idea.

A large majority of "investigators" here are actually just lurkers who are hoping one of us comes up with the answers. We know this.

Alright, let's move that factoid aside for a moment - who do we have left? Thirty percent of us, give-or-take, are actually investigating. And out of that fraction, how many are using cheats, modded games, or the same tired old 100% Option C save file that's been around forever (and is likely bugged)?

You're not gonna want to hear this, but I'm going to say it because it needs to be said - a vast majority of us are not using pure game files, guys. Each of us need to be using our own 100% file, and we need to refrain from using mods or cheats. I'll tell you why - cheats create too many unknown variables in a file, and can even corrupt it. Game events have been known, even since the 3D universe, to come out sketchy or not happen at all. Anybody remember the basketball mini-game disappearing once you used cheats in GTA: San Andreas? I had a save file in Vice City that went screwy and would no longer allow me to rob the drug-store... once I'd used cheats.

We know this game is buggy. All previous GTAs have been. I'm pretty sure we wanna stay as far away from DL'd saves, and mods, as possible, considering this fact.

In short - as referenced in a previous post, the most valuable investigators are 100-percenters who own their save, who did an honest play-through, and are cheat-free. This isn't to say that the remainder are useless; they can provide hints, find things from happy accidents, locate clues. But it's far less likely that using shortcuts of this nature will get you the damn jetpack.

Additionally, we've got a whole bunch of Option C players, and not nearly enough Option A / Option B players. There's a good chance that this might be the reason we haven't come up with anything yet. We need all three for this to be a concrete experiment.

Played the game legit, used no cheats, and got a hundred percent? Bless you.

Didn't play legit? Downloaded a save? Used cheats? Then make sure to mention that if you take offense to this post - because no offense is meant. We gotta make a dividing line, here.

:)

Kifflom.
