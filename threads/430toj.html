Crush depth turned off in Monkey Business - visiting the underwater hatch and other exploration opportunities discussed

▲ 33 ptsu/commissarioJan 28, 201640 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Coordinate Teleport Sweep

Tl; dr
Certain missions allow "exploration options" such as "No Crush Depth" and "No Prison/Fort Zancudo Security" - most likely for gameplay reasons - but, like the Alien we can find in Ludendorff, I suggest that maybe there are more Easter Eggs hidden/only found within actual missions.

Some observations (likely prior finds) during Monkey Business:
Like the Merryweather Heist (offshore option) I noticed that the "crush depth" is also turned off in Monkey Business (the story Humane Labs mission). This allows you reach the underwater "Lost" hatch in a scuba suit without cheats. Here is a video I made and you can skip to 3:10 secs for the hatch. You cannot interact with it.

Revisiting Humane Labs:
After you deliver the Nerve Agent you can return to Humane Labs with Trevor (during the Patricia part) and the underwater access tunnel to Human Labs is still open - it is impossible (on last gen) to make it through the tunnel because the invincibility cheat is denied and you cannot get scuba gear (Patricia won't leave Trevor's truck so you cannot use the Dinghy/sub because it takes you too far away from her).

Is it possible to spawn a sub or dinghy in current-gen? If so, I reckon you can explore the tunnel by spawning a Dinghy near the tunnel.

Edit: I've explored the underwater tunnel with Trevor but the first door (after the ladder) is locked once you get inside Humane Labs. In the day the particles/detritus in the water are bright green. Here's the method: You need to drive Patricia to Sandy Shores Airfield and use the cargobob to fly Trevor's truck (with Patricia inside) to the Sonar Collection Docks. Drop the truck at the end of the road leading to the docks. Then carefully land the cargobob in the shallow water in the cove; the water needs to be swimming depth but not too deep so you trash the cargobob. Exit the cargobob and use the sub or dinghy to equip scuba gear; if done right you get get back in the cargobob with the scuba gear and fly the truck (plus Patricia) back to Humane labs. Drop the truck near the back entrance of the labs and land the cargobob in the water - you should still have the scuba gear equipped to explore the tunnel.

No Prison Security:
The security at the Prison is also turned off during the Patricia part allowing you to thoroughly investigate the site. This is similar to Fort Zancudo during the Cletus Hunting mission. Basically, you cannot fail this part of the mission by exploring (providing Patricia stays within range which is quite large) - replaying the mission (post-100%) I even tried taking Patricia to the Chiliad UFO, to see if I could use her to spawn the UFO or get her abducted. Nothing happens.

Some ideas and questions:
My observations are likely prior finds but it made me think of what you might be able to find in other missions by going "off-piste". We know of the Alien in Ludendorff and it is found in the only place you are really allowed to deviate from the mission; in Bury the Hatchet you are barely allowed to deviate from the yellow path except for the Alien spot. Do they want us to find it?

Could the Alien and the even the Hatch be Easter Eggs for those that try to do something unintended? Are there more of these - what other deviations can me make?

Could the "Egg" literally mean "Hatch" and the "UFO" mean the Alien? Does this suggest there are Jetpack or CJ/San Andreas Easter Eggs still to find, even if not a actual Jetpack?

Why are we allowed to go wildly of-course in some missions, but restricted in others? I tried taking Devin Weston to the Altruists but I had already cleared the camp - what happens if it's not cleared?

Things I've tried
Post-100% you can make the Chiliad UFO appear in replayed missions - I've tried taking Patricia there, the nuke and even Devin Weston but nothing happens.
