Did you guys ever think of the possibility of the alien tatto Easter egg have a connection?

And the UFO, which can be seen in a lot of parts, and when acquiring the tatto.

If it's not in the game, it may arrive somewhere soon, see, the first part, the secret ressuply mission was added in the gunrunning update (june 13th, 2017), and the second part, added with the after hours dlc, the alien tatto, arrived way later (July 24th 2018).
