Now I'm not too sure about what significance this could have but in the past people have mentioned having to mirror things in game. Well I stumbled onto something which I'm not sure if anyone has noticed but I found it weird considering where it is, and I thought what possible reason could there be to have something like this where you have to mirror it to read it. Now if you go to where the horse statue is (faswan) on portal drive in Rockford hills, look straight on at the horse and on the pavement to the left there is a drain with a blue sticker of a fish on it that says (sea drain do not dump) now I very much doubt this has anything to do with the mystery but what I found interesting is that I had to hold a mirror towards my tv to read what it says as it was written backwards and the writing wasn't too clear but once I got my mirror it became very clear. But what I want to know is what is the reason for having to mirror a sign that is on the ground in the location it was? Why not just have it the correct way round ? It's baffled me since a spotted it a few days ago. Any info would be great, and sorry if this has been discussed before
