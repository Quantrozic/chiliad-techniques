###Epsilon tracts

I will refer to the 10 tracts that Marnie has you collect with a lower case t and by pluralizing the word, and the Tract that you unlock as a proper non-plural noun.

Yes, you have sat through countless posts from stoners trying to decipher the Tract that you unlock after collecting all Marnie's tracts. This is not one of those posts. So bear with me. What if it were as simple as using the 10 hints Marnie texts you to solve the mystery? Only under specific conditions, kind of like how we already know that tract 1 found at the top of Mount Chiliad (MC) has a certain set of conditions that produce extra terrestrial results (raining at 3 am for the UFO to appear). OR if that doesn't work, doing them in reverse order like the Epsilon Tract tells you to do? i.e. "Z to A."

Here is where the need for PC Users help comes in. We don't know where these locations are, what time they need to be activated, or what the conditions need to be. Mods are definitely going to be needed. In case you don't remember the tract hints I will post them.
