Bad phone reception at Maze Bank?

▲ 68 ptsu/Q-ChibMay 6, 202111 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Whisper Bulk Transcription of Dialogue Banks ?

Hey all!

Today I was messing around at Maze Bank and noticed that the two circular fountains with the red outer ends (the one's on both sides of the "egg of life" fountain) have a unique property.

Whenever you make a phone call, it'll simply keep on ringing. Some businesses and other contacts will appear as "Busy" but you'll never complete a call in that fountain.

But the strange part is.. If you walk to the outer red ring of the fountains while calling, it will then proceed to complete the call. But otherwise it's a literal dead zone.

Note: the black cell phone number still does the explosion.

Not sure if this happens anywhere else in the game or if it's been mentioned on here but seems odd in a place like Maze Bank.

\\\* At first I thought it was only the bigger fountain but tested it at both the "egg of life" and the other side fountain. I only got the same result at the other smaller fountain, both with the red ring and water spout.

Could be a bug from the way the fountains are designed (since their both similar) but thought I'd share and maybe get some thoughts?

EDIT: After further research and discussion, turns out that you aren't able to complete a call when in ankle-deep water, unless your character touches the adjacent edge/wall or object. This was tested at the Kortz fountains as well with the same results.
