Ok. We are searching through game files. We find intriguing clues that lead to dead(?) ends. Now, I understand the concept behind that and understand that even if we don't find like a complete UFO "mission", we can deduce clues from little bits and pieces left in the code. We use these clues and ultimately solve the mystery. In addition, I have personally put forth the idea with many others that this is an incomplete puzzle and that future updates/DLC will add new pieces or even be the subject of an expansion.

R* knows that. It is also fairly clear that they have people in the subreddit more or less monitoring our progress. What if the whole shebangabang is server-side? That fulfills the criteria of not being an incomplete story, but also explains why nobody can do anything but confuse themselves further once they start "putting the puzzle together".

Out of my ass example: We use the story characters to deduce clues and do missions and see UFOs (can you trigger a UFO on Chiliad online?). But then we have to "solve the puzzle" online (or in story mode, but with internet of course), so that we trigger an update or change. Is it not plausible that there is a plain and simple mission but you have to "trigger it's download" by doing something online? Or we have all been familiarized with GTA:O loading missions (or failing to), can we not do our crazy ritual or whatever and it load a mission for you? Even better in this example because it would remove you from the server loading a mission and not disturb the server with an earthquake or UFO crash. Or we could be graded on a curve and everyone get an update (probably like 2 fucking months after, I'm looking at you, stimulus package) when someone manages to trigger whatever it is we are looking for?
