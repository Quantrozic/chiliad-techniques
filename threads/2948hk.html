I wrote in the previous thread that there wasn't a tide, but ManiaFarm and Chatting_shit linked a video that showed pretty good evidence that there is. Explaining something is pretty flimsy next to a video, so I decided to seek out the exact spot the video was taken, and take some pictures. I snapped one every hour from 2am to 2am with clear weather, and this is what I got:

http://imgur.com/a/0hgaU#0

You can see that there isn't any rise and fall in the water level, sans some normal waves. I don't doubt that the video shows a real rise in water level, there's just a few factors that can exaggerate this under the right conditions. Weather, location, and unknown Poseidon magic that I can't explain that makes the water level real low every so often all seem to contribute to this.

I felt I'd put this in its own thread so it could be more visible, and in case anyone searched for it at some point in the future. Better than just a short blurb of text.
