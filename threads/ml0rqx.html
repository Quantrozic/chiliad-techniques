Lot of speculation recently on the idea of the yellow mural trying to communicate movement among potential energy states, moon phase tracking, etc. Appreciate the research. I think it's also important we work to understand what some of the real world images depicted in the mural represent, like the genius who figured out the Solar System/Galaxy pictogram and Sine waves are exact matches to what's found on the golden Voyager record.

Would love anyone to speak out if they disagree, but it appears the spiral directly above the human head represents the circular depiction of the Fibonacci sequence. I don't know what the circle and arrow represent on the ring itself, but the general picture is Fibonacci.

Example: https://static.thenounproject.com/png/43316-200.png (google 'circular fibonacci')

Background: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fibonacci\_number#Applications

One of the insane implications from Fibonacci's research is the discovery of a mathematical constant called the Golden Ratio. (I know we've come to love gold in the last few months \[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden\_ratio\]). The Golden Ratio represents "the limit of the ratios of successive terms in the Fibonacci sequence". Basically, the higher the numbers in the Fibonacci sequence, the closer the ratio between the two numbers gets to being the golden ratio. It's possibly the trendiest number in the history of math. I'm sure the Housers are fascinated by it.

It has a ton of implications, uses, and representations you can read on the wikis. It is rampantly observed in nature, especially in the seeding of flowers, which we know have at least some relevance to the mystery with the Peyote Hunt and the "Our Path is Lit!" message at altruist camp, with the 'O' stylized as a sunflower.

That's all I wanted to confidently contribute, that this is a Fibonacci Spiral, or a Golden Spiral. Hopefully someone among us figures out what the fuck we're supposed to do with it.

This is the end of the informational portion. Couple random notes of potential interest below.

\- It's greek letter is traditionally phi, adding to the family of already recognized greek letter in the game (and the mystery); can't remember if someone's already linked phi to another element in the game?

\- The Ratio is an irrational number, meaning it can't be represented as a standard fraction (like pi), so I doubt we'd see a direct numerical reference to it in the game

\- The Ratio has two possible values, or "states" \[1+sqrt(5)\]/2, or \[1-sqrt(5)\]/2, as these are the solutions to a polynomial representation of the ratio. Might help someone investigating the celestial images.

\- The Ratio is also recognized in pyramid construction, with one example of a near golden pyramid being the Great Pyramid of Giza. Imo this is what Chiliad itself represents, with the other two mountains representing the other two great pyramids.
