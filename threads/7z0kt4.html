I know that it may be getting boring to read about all of the mural theories, but this one makes a lot of sense to me. In Hebrew, they believe in the Tree of Life. The Tree of Life includes the steps that you have to follow in order to have a complete life. When I first heard this, I though of the Chiliad Mural. The Mural includes steps you have to follow that could lead to something bigger than a just a jetpack. As you look at the Mural, there are a lot of lines and Xs. On the Tree of Life, there are lines connecting steps for a complete life. Here are the steps you follow from the Tree of Life to have a complete life.

Keter-Crown

Chokmah-Wisdom

Binah-Understanding

Chesed-Mercy

Gevurah-Strength

Tiferet-Beauty

Netzach-Victory

Hod-Splendor

Yesod-Foundation

Malkut-Kingdom

I believed earlier that the Chiliad Mural were steps to unlock all of the objects drawn on the bottom of the Mural. To get the UFO, 100% completion. Cracked Egg would be the straightforward 600 gunrunning missions. Then the jetpack which proved my theory wrong about the steps. Connection As I said before, the Tree of Life has steps to complete for a complete life and after life. The Chiliad Mural has steps to solve the mystery around Mt. Chiliad. There is one Tree of Life, for one person, you. In GTA V, there are 3 characters. There are three pictures drawn at the bottom of the mural. The left side has steps to the UFO, which means a different way to end the game. The middle has a straight line which is just getting 100% completion of the game. The right has steps to the jetpack. The question is now, what are the other ways to end the game?

In game, Michael, has the right to join the Epsilon Program. This is a religious cult in-game. The Tree of Life is mentioned in-game but they don't use the term "Tree of Life." One Epsilon mission is when Michael meets Marnie in an alleyway and she is worshiping a sign that says "The Fruit Tree is a Lie." The Epsilon Program wants you to take their steps to have a fulfilling life. The Fruit Tree is the Tree of Life! They say that the Fruit Tree is a lie because they want you to follow their rules to a complete life! The Tree of Life could be known as the path to enlightenment. Franklin can become part of the Children of the Mountain that makes you complete steps and to follow the path to enlightenment. The steps that you have to follow include a segregate and rearrange step. It includes words that are similar to the Tree of Life steps. The character's lives are considered a story.

The Tree of Life is the way to have a complete story. One other connection is that on the top of Mt. Chiliad there is writing that says, "Come Back When Your Story is Complete." I hope this makes as much sense to as to you. Thanks for reading and leave your opinions in the comments.
