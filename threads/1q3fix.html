Maps within maps.

▲ 21 ptsu/GlenvilleNov 7, 20139 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:GIS Georeferencing of Community Maps

It occurred to me that the Mural on Mount Chiliad is needlessly elaborate, even if to find only the U.F.O using the five (V) x's.

Rather whomever has drawn this (some think Omega) has specifically drawn lots of lines connecting everything. However even though those lines are needless, they are also unnecessarily convoluted. Was the artist being deliberately complex?

Well I'm beginning to think not. Over behind Franklin's Vinewood house there is a reservoir. Carved onto the Hillside there is what looks like another partial map fragment. There is one round the other side of the hill, There is also the one painted on the ground at Vespucci Beach which has been noted.

Further to this I am sure I witnessed (while fleeing the cops) A red Brick building somewhere in or near one of the towns in Blane county which had an elaborate copper structure on it's back wall which also looked like a map fragment.

I may well be wrong, but what if the X's signify the UFO but that this is the start of a hunt for a treasure map that will lead us to something?

I'm still on my way to 100% However I've been following this silently and have seen no mention of any of these things being map fragments other than at Vespucci Beach.

Also bear in mind that in this game almost every mission ends up with your character being ripped off. From the story missions through the strangers and freaks all the way through to the end collections. Rarely do you end up with something that is impressive. A dead cultist camp, a t-shirt and a space docker, some robes and a rusty tractor. Your characters are always being ripped off. So even if I am on to something I still suspect any jetpack will be a toy, at best.

Apologies for lack of pics.
