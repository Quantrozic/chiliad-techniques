I loaded up my game during the 10days of wearing robes and encounter this event. My interest was piqued to replay this random event.

It is an even about a drug deal apparantly called Country side gang fight on gtav wiki.

Things you notice when arriving to this event if that the lost have a 2v1 advantage on this fellow of indigenous descent. Now if you jump right in and kill the red dots, blue dot will lead you to his car talking about better business only to have to hide because of more lost biker reinforcements. They spot you and you kill them, collect a reward and leave, but thats not the whole story. If you replay this scene from the beginning and let the conversation play out between red and blue only two outcomes exist, the blue dot dies at the hand of the lost or you kill the red dots and the blue dot runs away after you hear how he is late on payments for drugs on credit. The last option is to kill the blue dot yourself and then the red for no purpose.

That is 4 ways one random even can play out in this game. The guy you end up helping out talks about getting is Karma sorted.
