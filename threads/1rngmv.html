On the lawn in front of the observatory, you can see various lines on the concrete.

From the roof of the observatory, a panorama (sorry for the tire tracks):

  • looking left (NW): http://prod.cloud.rockstargames.com/ugc/gta5photo/9335/1xymm4O8q0i6Qqm_mE2cOw/0_0.jpg
  • looking right (NE): http://prod.cloud.rockstargames.com/ugc/gta5photo/8909/XZ4cnHpmFUuwx9DjQj-07w/0_0.jpg

I have made a terrible schema of the lines: http://imgur.com/UXye6lm

The grey lines are made of granit and the brown lines are bronze or copper.

In the real-life Griffith observatory, lines with identical symbols can be found: http://www.laparks.org/observatory/exhibits/bexterior.html But these lines are circular, indicating solar system orbits and sun/moon trajectories in the sky.

I wonder if the whole lawn and the central sculpture could be some kind of celestial calendar. I have read that the shadow of the eye is supposed to be aligned with the center of the lawn at 3pm, but I have observed this at other times (between 1:30pm and 3pm). Does the inclination of the sun change from one day to the other in the game? It would mean there are some kind of solstices which could have an influence on the triggering of some events...

I don't think at any point in the day the eye shadow can be projected on sun symbol on the ground, it would imply that the sunlight comes from the north...
