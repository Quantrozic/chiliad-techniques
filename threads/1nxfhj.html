[Speculation] The Penris Hypothesis (Need Help)

▲ 13 pts[deleted]Oct 7, 201321 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Hypothesis Pre-Registration Logging

Since hard data is coming in more slowly, I'd like to see more analysis of the data collected and related theories. In that spirit, here's one of mine. The following may contain spoilers, so don't read on if you haven't finished the game.

The Penris logo has an eye with five stars in it. I suspect Penris might be a combination of Pente (greek word for five) + Iris, meaning five eyes. Each star represents one of the "eyes" watching us in the game. In that area of downtown there are four tall buildings owned by the FIB, IAA, Union Depository, and Maze Bank.

The FIB and IAA buildings are both in the same plaza and both orgs are arms of the government, as evidenced by all the news radio chatter over the competition for funding between them. The FIB logo on the UFOs indicates a connection between these orgs and the aliens. They can be considered one of the five "eyes."

There are scattered references to the Illuminati throughout the game, but after the Union Depository heist, Lester yells something like, "Got you Illuminati bastards!" I think this is good evidence that the Illuminati is in control of the Union Depository and are another one of the eyes.

The Maze Bank is a little more ambiguous, but there are obvious links to the mystery: the "cracked egg" or lightning bolt on one of the stones in the fountain, the newspaper in North Yankton with the headline, "No Way Out for Maze Bank," and the fact that they have such a large building downtown. There also may be something significant with the Minotaur property management company, which is a subsidiary of Maze Bank (as revealed by a magazine cover in Lester's apartment). I can't tell who is behind Maze and how they might be connected. Maybe someone else has more clues? For now, I'm considering them to potentially be an "eye."

A fourth "eye" could be the Epsilon Group, as they too seem to track your activities, and clearly wield a fair amount of influence in the game.

So if this hypothesis is true, and all of these organizations actually do represent the five "eyes," then that still leaves one missing. My first thought was that it could be the Knights Templar, but the connection is kind of weak. From the rooftop of Lester's garment factory, you can get one of the best views in the city of the two Templar Hotel signs, which feature Knights Templar symbolism. Not much of a link, I know. But consider that Lester sells Franklin on the assassination missions by pitching them as being efforts for the greater good, but then he profits greatly from the schemes through the stock market. That could be a link to the Templars who went on religious crusades for "good," while looting and hoarding as much treasure as they could.

All in all, it's not the strongest or cleanest interpretation, but hopefully it's a jumping-off point for discussion.
