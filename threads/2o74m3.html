Indiana Jones Theory.

Documented under:Sun-Shadow Azimuth Triangulation ?

I believe that the altruist camp and the radio tower above are part of an Indiana Jones reference. Perhaps even the building on top of Chiliad too, however I am focusing mostly on the camp.

Some of the murals depict a deep red sun, like the one we see at sunset. However, I believe it is a reference to the tower because the light on top of it is almost the same color as the ones in the drawings. Furthermore, I think the tower has an unusually far draw distance. It was one of the furthest things I could see when approaching Raton Canyon by air. There are also power lines or cables that run up from the camp, to the shed, then more cables from the shed, to the tower, and then up to the light.

If you stand around the camp during sunrise you will notice that the sun rises from behind the mountain that the radio tower is on. I believe that at a certain time, perhaps 8 am, the shadow of the tower will light the way or mark a significant point.

If the shadow doesn't mark a significant object or collectable I will try to go to the same spot in the morning and look back at the tower at 8am.

"Our path is lit." "His light shines on us." "He is risen." "See the eye."

I am currently testing it now in the morning, however I do not have 100% completion. Would anyone be able to test this on the xbox1 or ps4 as well?

-Edit- You can track the shadow of the tower by zooming in to look at it, lower your weapon, then look at it again and the shadow would be moved a few feet. The shadow begins to appear around 5ish in the morning

-Edit- The altruist tower casts its shadow at 8am inside the curve of the road on the way to the Sonar Collections Dock. I went down there and explored the area intensely around 8am but found nothing. Now I am trying to test the shadow of the tower on the Chiliad lift.

-Edit- The shadow of the lift on chiliad doesn't become visible until almost 8am. However, when it does become visible it is out on the water. I found the shadow by following the mountain's shadow till the point where I thought it was the top of the shadow. I've marked it and I'm getting a sub to explore.

-Edit- FOUND A CAVE. It is underwater near where I was observing the shadow of the MT. Chiliad lift at 8am. I need a map to mark to show you guys! The cave has a tall opening and is very deep. I did not observe any markings or diagrams on the wall of the cave. Can someone with 100% explore it and also on the next gen version?

-Edit- 12/4 I'm going to try to track the altruist tower shadow all day. This is because the sun sets over the ocean thus it will cast the shadow of the tower towards chiliad...
