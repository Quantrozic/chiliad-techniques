A simple question for you all / my own "poll" : how many times did you finish the game ?

▲ 6 ptsu/AcolyteProdSep 15, 20146 comments at captureOpen original on Reddit ↗
Documented under:Community Polls & Heat-Map Aggregation

Because of the way i think we're all getting mad, like many others i want to get back at the roots of this game, its gameplay and its switchable characters system / coupled with the main story.

How many times did you finish the game ? Simple question, but maybe important answers. I just did my second run, and i'll make a third, fourth....etc. During this second run, i realised how deep is the importance of three characters, but NOT to make them stand i dont know where at 3 am or what ever... I think that just like me, some of you did this mistake : we are too connected to the reddit and the community. I think we all build a view of the game together, but we dont see ALL of the game by our own.

I watched the ending in which you kill M with Youtube, because this was not my first or second choice...i will kill him in my next run. I saved the two friends first, then i killed T. And when i read here, a lot of theories involve the three characters. Its just like we never try anything with ONE character only, forgetting that if you kill one of them, the world reacts : Jimmy gets mad because of its uncle T death, Lamar is surprised etc. We forget about the MAIN revolution in the GTA series.

Did you see this ending ? Isnt it "too big" to be true ? M receives a call from Amanda, and learns that Tracey will go to college etc. He's happy, he's really nice with his wife, then he talks about his new career in movies, how much he is happy etc. F must kill him. You think "he can't do it, i can't do it...this is the family man, I built his own dream during all the game, got back his family, he will live as a happy man now etc etc".

BUT F doesnt care. First surprise. At this moment, i thought : THIS IS KARMA. Its not in the way you drive or chose to kill people or not etc. This is too much...BUT, we have a choice to make at the end of the game. You kill the limitless maniac, or you kill daddy M. F is the only one to look like "out of the game" (of the gangsta sh*t nigga, of the T/M opposition, and at last, he is the one who will kill one of them...or not).

So, you chose to kill M. LOOK AT THIS ENDING if you didnt chose it. F shout at M something like "i was here a long time before you, i could have shoot before etc etc". He looks like posessed (do you agree ?). It reminds me the theory of a "new CJ". Well...i was amazed by this atmosphere, this editing, ambiance etc...

The moment i realised, really, that i'll need to go deeper in my own hunt by playing the game again and again and again, to finaly meet this zombie i never saw. And here, we are a lot to discover things through reddit/youtube/GTAF etc. Sometimes we ask "did you see this character before ? i didnt omg blablabla". Yes, we didnt, because we stand at the end of one or two save games, driving and searching everywhere, after we saw a new clue in reddit etc.

My point is : we all know the game very well, thanks to our sharing community (which i love dearly). But all alone, we dont know the game at all. Some were trapped by GTAO, in which the map/environement isnt the same (in SP the world evolves and reacts to our choices), some were trapped (like me) by the community, some by theories involving all three characters etc.

AND i believe, this is why i ask, that just a few people chosed to kill M, because we build him, we "save" him in the game. At last he can be happy, why should we kill him ? As we can kill the sad maniac who feels surrounded by liars and traitors.

SO / I shared my thoughts to push you all to think "wider", to get back at the core of this specific GTA episode and its new gameplay mechanics, and i just want you to share with me this answer : what choices did you make at the end ? How many times did you play ? Did you notice major differences regarding to these multiple way to complete your story ?

Have a nice day brothers, KIFFLOM ! <3
