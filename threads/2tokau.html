Greetings space travelers! Hope all has been well since the last time we've spoken. Noticed some similarities to this mystery in an early 90's TV show some of you may be familiar with, Twin Peaks. To break up the monotony of most of my posts, I figured I'd do it from the perspective of one of the characters that I think a few of us have inadvertantly been channeling, Special Agent Dale Cooper. So without further wait, I present to you, "Damn fine coffee: Chiliad Mystery Observations"

Hello Diane,

Coop here again. I was hoping I could have you send a couple files my way from the Twin Peaks investigation
from the early 90's. I'm on vacation and staying in a quaint little town in the beautiful state of sunny San Andreas, Paleto bay. I'm starting to notice many parallels to my time spent with Harry & the Bookhouse boys in Twin Peaks and any resources you can send along would be most appreciated.

Although, at first the similarities may seem superficial, they start stacking up as I dig deeper into the seedy underbelly & beautifully weird nature of this state. While waiting for a cab I hopped onto eyefind to see if there were any local events taking place. One of the suggested links was for a peculiar website that made the hair on the back of my neck stand up at the sight of it - www.whokilledleonorajohnson.com . Most of my time in Twin Peaks was spent finding out who killed Laura Palmer - with the first suspect being Leo Johnson. I shrugged it off when I got into the cab and set my way to Paleto Bay.

Upon arriving in Paleto Bay, I felt an instant connection with the place. Almost as if I've been here before. After checking in at my hotel, I took a stroll down the to look for the Famous Hamburgers restaurant. I must say Diane, I looked but could not for the life it me find it. Hopefully somewhere I can find a damn fine cup of coffee. When approaching the restaurant, I was reminded of the good ol' Double R diner. Ahh.. I can almost taste that pie.. Looking up from the sign, I saw a large mountain peak and felt I must ascend it next.

While walking through the forest I noticed a saw mill and smiled, smelling the fresh lumber, remembering the mill at Twin Peaks. The trees rustled in the wind and I heard an owl hoot. I couldn't find it anywhere, but one of the most fascinating pieces of advice I've ever received was that the Owls are not what they seem. I assure you Diane, I spent a great portion of my day looking and could not find the source of this owl.. but I digress. I took the lift up the mountain and wouldn't you know Diane, during the ride up, I noticed some strange glyph but even more strange in the shed at the top, there was a painting.. At this point Diane, I knew there was something strange afoot in this place. I'm going to have to remember to have Andy take a look at this, where he helped me solve the last strange painting I had needed to decipher in the case of Windom Earle.

Since it was around 2:50am, I decided to walk to the top of the mountain and watch the sun crest over the ocean. It was a beautiful view from up top, only enhanced by the calm as the rain started to pour. Suddenly, A bright light and this strange whooping noise started and in front of my very own eyes there was a UFO, with FIB markings on the side to note. I'll have to contact General Briggs and see if I can find any information from his experience with Project Bluebook to see if this is in his records. It was an astounding sight, but I had to return to sleep so I could process this all.

After doing a bit more research, I wound up at a local hotspot to the sandy shores patchouli crowd which was adorned with strange symbols and paintings of Aliens in various poses and positions, of different species too. Painted along the mound were the letters 6EQUJ5, which is affectionately called the WOW! signal from outer space, drawing the connection to the signals General Briggs provided me which came from deep space with no known origin. Recently read a book with a character Wade Owen Watts - Probably no connection, but felt it interesting to mention.

Alas, I've only been able to draw one other connection to my time in twin peaks, yet this one is beyond eery and is why I've requested all the information I can on the Twin Peaks case. I was invited into an apartment by one of the locals while visiting the city of Los Santos and the floor Diane.. the Floor was the exact same floor as that of the Red room from my dreams and when I entered from the 12 sycamore trees in Ghostwood Forest. The apartment even bore a similar color pattern with the red, black and white pillows in parallel with the red room and black & white lodge. I have no recollection of when or how I left there, but this case is starting to draw too many similarities and I must study this beautiful state.

Cooper out.
