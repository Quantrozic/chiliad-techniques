On the Christmas special of the quiz show QI they mentioned that the Capitol Records building broadcasts the word 'Hollywood' in morse code (and once the release date of a Katy Perry album but no one noticed) from the light atop its antenna.

Any chance it's representation, the Badger Building or any other building may be sending out such visual signals as opposed to the usual audio/radio messages that we've come to expect.

I'm pretty sure something so obvious would have been noticed already but no harm in keeping an eye out.
