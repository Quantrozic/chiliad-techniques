Just had a question about the Moon, normally I only notice the Moon and Sun crossing paths at sunrise. The Moon and Sun being closest to each other around I wanna say 6-7 am

But just now I noticed at 5 am the Sun is rising but the full Moon is still high in the sky on the West side, the side with the Altruist Camp, as any of you would've noticed on nights of a full Moon the game world is VERY BRIGHT. Makes me wonder if any of the Altruist Camp glyphs refer to a full Moon.

Anyway my main point of this post was to ask if this can be helpful in some way, I know everything has been tested but maybe it depends on having a full Moon. There's like I wanna say synergy with having a full Moon still high in the sky at the same time as the rising Sun.

My question about the Moon flip is has anyone tracked the progression of the Moon cycle. I am aware of the pictures that DEPICT the Moon cycle, but I am asking if anyone has TRACKED the Moon cycle and followed it's travels of rising and setting.

I hope there's someone that can give some positive feedback, I'm just curious and of course the new trailer reaffirmed my thought that this can be solved in the games current state. It must be a matter of just rearranging what we know.
