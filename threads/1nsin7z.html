Maybe the Sinners Passage/Textile Mural is telling you to Play Fair.

The video above from National Treasure says you need a 5 letter key word but that isn't generally the case. A good explanation of how the cipher works.

A few reasons I think this may a good lead:

  • There are marked letters with a line that runs through them around the outside of the mural. The pink marked letters seem clear to me, the green letters maybe.
  • The TUR-KEY to thanksgiving (from Republican Space Rangers).
  • The mural has similarities to the artwork in Masquerade by Kit Williams, which turned into a massive real life treasure-hunt a few decades back. Masquerade uses marked letters but a different method to the Playfair cipher.

A Playfair decoder, but there are many options online. Type your ciphertext in it's spot, then put a keyword in the box under the grid and some options with how to fill the rest of the squares will pop up underneath. As the cipher normally uses 25 squares one letter of the alphabet is dropped, Q and J seem to get dropped the most.

The marked letters around the mural, following the direction of the sentence: RNRmTGNTMhMKTuTOYDM. Well marked letters in upper-case, unsure in lower-case. The H bottom right just touches a line, while the M and U in green rectangles are hard to see.

I've had a go with not much luck, except for one maybe. If you use DEATH as the keyword, drop Q from the grid, and don't use the M that I've put the green square on, the first four letters RN RT will give you ULSA (where Nazi scientists may be hiding). You can also see maybe AIM, IM (I'm), MY, MESH, maybe NME. The method of decryption (under the letters) has to be the same also. But then the message goes to shit.

Some things I reckon might be worth considering: The lines have been put on the letters and then that coloured box/word has been condensed or stretched, which puts the marked letters in specific spots. So the order may be different than just going clockwise with the text. For example Masquerade uses lines drawn through two points in the images to point to letters, one of those points being eyes, one of which we have on the mural. There is also a only a limited number of colours used so a colour order could be involved I suppose.
