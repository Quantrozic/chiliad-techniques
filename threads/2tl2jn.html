I'm sorry, I know people are only spitballing, trying every possibility, turning every corner, but can we all please stop trying to overlay the mural to the map? It's illogical that that would be a thing. For one, the mural can be stretched out and moved around and resized to your hearts content, and so can the map. after all that resizing you're eventually going to find a way of having the X's sort of line up with the UFO locations...the size of the mural is irrelevant to the map. and irrelevant to the mystery. what matters is the mural itself. the glyphs around the mountain/map. the altruist CAMP and not the altruist people themselves, seeing as though it's an option to wipe them all out with Trevor.
My honest opinion, that I have once stated on here, is that the answer isn't in this version of the game. It's got DLC written all over it. But I'll keep searching anyway, because I hate online and there's nothing else to do in single player...and the thing's I'll be searching/testing will be; Altruist camp, Salvation mountain and the FIB

I feel like this is a bit of a rant, but the solving of the mystery has become a little desperate, and if we think, recap and try logical options, then an answer can't be too far away.
