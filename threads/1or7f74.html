My Theory (Thinking Simple)

Hey fellow hunters,

Watching the recent oddheader video, I became really interested in this mystery again and began thinking that, if this mystery is solvable, it must be solvable with the help of the mural and glyphs alone. So here’s my theory solely focused on the mural and the glyphs:

The Mural and glyphs are a multilayered puzzle

The first step to solve the puzzle is to find the glyphs on the side of Mount Chiliad in order to be able to match them with the red markings on the mural. (Which has been done)

Next the conditions shown on the glyphs themselves have to be met: Rain, 3am and the UFO on top of Mount Chiliad has to be spawned (which has been done)

Next is to find the glyphs in the game world (which they have been on top of Mount Chiliad where the UFO spawns and around the hippy camp, except the faded UFO one)

Next is to find the order in which you have to visit these glyphs’ locations and I’m thinking it’s by drawing a line along the ones found in the mural, without it intersecting with itself and ending in the cracked egg symbol. (I believe this because the UFO and jetpack symbol are linked by a line at the bottom letting you draw along it, while the cracked egg is a dead end). Since spawning the Chiliad UFO, which I think is one of the three starting condition for the secret to work, happens to make you stand on top of the plain UFO glyph hidden underneath the wooden platform, I think this is the starting point from where to draw the line. I also think that because the line to the left of the plain UFO glyph on the mural comes out of nowhere, basically being an entry point into the maze. You can start drawing a line from the plain UFO glyph to the right and then down to the red UFO in the box, from there follow the line that connects it with the jetpack symbol, continuing to the right and along the outline of the mountain to the left side where the line goes into the mountain again. From there you can continue drawing the line along all the other glyphs and have it end in the cracked egg without the line you’ve drawn touching itself. According to this the order is: Plain UFO glyph on top of Mount Chiliad, the rain glyph at the hippy camp, the 3 am glyph at the hippy camp and lastly the mountain glyph with the wow frequency on it that has been found in the sand.
The faded UFO glyph makes me think that you have to return to the Mount Chiliad UFO at the end and make it disappear by walking or flying close to it, since no such glyph has been found in the game world and it’s basically the plain UFO glyph fading away.

I might try this out over the weekend and report back how it went
