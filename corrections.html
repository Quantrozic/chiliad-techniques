Corrections & contributions

Errors are handled in public: report them on GitHub, watch them get fixed in a numbered release, and get credited in the changelog. Thread verifications and category reviewers wanted.

Report an error

All corrections run through a public, auditable process: open a GitHub issue on the site repository (a free GitHub account is the only requirement). Private DMs are deliberately not the channel; public error handling is part of what makes a reference trustworthy.

Worth reporting: factual errors in a technique entry; a case link whose thread does not actually demonstrate the method (false positive); a community method the encyclopedia is missing; wrong thread metadata (author/date/score/flair); broken pages or links; anything on the methodology page that does not hold up.

If you prefer, you can also submit the fix directly as a pull request against the repository; the published entry data lives in data/techniques.json.

What happens to a report

  1. The issue is triaged publicly on GitHub; discussion happens on the issue thread.
  2. Accepted fixes land in the next site version.
  3. Every correction is listed in the changelog credited to whoever reported it (GitHub or Reddit name, your choice; anonymity honored on request).

Verify a thread: become a citation verifier

The strongest way to improve this resource is converting machine matches into verified citations. A verification means: you read the full thread (on Reddit, including comments) and confirm it genuinely shows the community using that method, not merely mentioning its vocabulary.

To submit one, open an issue titled verify: <technique id> <post id> with three things:

  • your verdict (demonstrates the method / does not);
  • one or two sentences of justification (what in the thread shows the method in use);
  • the name you want credited.

Accepted verifications appear on the technique page as a green ✓ verified badge carrying your name and the date, sort ahead of all machine matches, and are recorded in the dataset. Rejections of bad matches are just as valuable: confirmed false positives get removed and credited in the changelog.

Named reviewers wanted

This site wants two or three recognizable r/chiliadmystery veterans as standing category reviewers: people who each own a category (audio forensics, file archaeology, overlay mapping, …), review its entries and matches, and are credited here and on the site by name. If that could be you, or you can vouch for someone, open an issue titled reviewer: <category>.

Current reviewers: none yet; the seat is open. Contributors of accepted corrections are the natural candidates.
