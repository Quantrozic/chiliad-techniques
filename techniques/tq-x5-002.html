Difference-of-Gaussians Edge Mapping

Applies two Gaussian blurs at differing radii and subtracts them to map sharp boundaries in the mural and signage. The resulting edge field highlights inscribed lines against painted noise.

#edge-detection#dog-filter#mural#boundary#image-analysis

Why It’s Used

To extract clean line geometry from a noisy painted surface for tracing.
