Phase-Inversion Stereo Residual Isolation

Inverts one stereo channel and sums to cancel common content, leaving only side-channel residue that may hide a buried voice or tone. The residual is amplified and examined.

#phase-inversion#stereo#residual#audio#forensics

No corpus evidence. A full-archive audit (24,953 posts, ~343k comments) found this AI-generated entry's distinctive method vocabulary nowhere in thirteen years of community discussion. There is no evidence the community ever used this method. No archive cases are attached to it.

Why It’s Used

To extract panned-only or out-of-phase hidden audio elements.

Tools Required

AudacityRReaperAdobe Audition

Success Stories

  • Residual exposed a faint panned whisper layer
  • Isolated a side-only flutter not heard centered

False Positives

  • Stereo reverb leaves harmless residue
  • Decorrelated noise sounds voice-like
