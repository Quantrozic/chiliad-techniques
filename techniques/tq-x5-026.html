Base-N Encoding Brute Sweep

Sweeps candidate strings through Base16, Base32, Base58, and Base64 decoders to catch encoded payloads disguised as text. Decoded bytes are checked for readable or structured content.

#base-n#encoding#decode#text#cryptanalysis

Why It’s Used

To rule in or out common encodings before deeper cipher work.

Tools Required

CCyberChefPPythonDdCode

Success Stories

  • A blob decoded cleanly from Base32 to ASCII
  • Eliminated Base64 for a non-padded string

False Positives

  • Random text sometimes decodes to garbage that looks structured
  • Charset overlap fakes a valid decode
