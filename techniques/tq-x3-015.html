Morse-on-Envelope Detection

Extracts the amplitude envelope of pulsing tones and thresholds it into dot-dash timing to test whether a hum or beacon encodes Morse code. Decoded sequences are checked against meaningful words and coordinates.

#morse#envelope#beacon#decode

Why It’s Used

To decode rhythmic pulses that may carry Morse.

Tools Required
