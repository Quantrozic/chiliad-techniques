Runs suspicious in-game audio through slow-scan television decoders to test whether transmissions encode hidden images, the technique behind the famous Mount Chiliad SSTV image claims and their debunks.

Why It’s Used

SSTV is how real spacecraft and ham operators send pictures over audio, exactly the kind of payload a UFO-themed mystery might hide in radio static or vehicle transmissions.
