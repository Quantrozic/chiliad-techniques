Glyph Angle Goniometry

Measures the precise angles between mural strokes and the painted eye using a digital protractor tool. Recorded angles are checked against compass bearings and in-game sun positions to test for directional encoding.

To determine whether mural geometry encodes specific headings to in-world sites.
