Hypothesis Pre-Registration Logging

Publicly record predicted outcomes before running any in-game test, timestamping the hypothesis to prevent post-hoc rationalization. Enforces scientific discipline on community experiments.

#pre-registration#methodology#hypothesis#rigor

Why It’s Used

To prevent hindsight bias by committing predictions in advance.
