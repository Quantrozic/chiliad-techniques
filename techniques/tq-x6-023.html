Bayesian Lead Prioritization

Assign prior probabilities to competing theories and update them as evidence arrives, producing a ranked list of leads worth pursuing. The model makes belief changes explicit and auditable.

#bayesian#prioritization#probability#leads

Why It’s Used

To rationally allocate effort across many competing theories.
