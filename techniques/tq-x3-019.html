Entropy Analysis of Assets

Computes Shannon entropy across asset files to distinguish compressed or encrypted regions from plain data, flagging unusually high-entropy blobs that might conceal a hidden payload. Outliers are carved out for deeper inspection.

#entropy#shannon#encrypted-blob#anomaly

Why It’s Used

To locate possibly encrypted or hidden data among assets.

Tools Required

BbinwalkPPythonHEhex editor

Success Stories

  • Isolated an unusually random texture chunk for review

False Positives
