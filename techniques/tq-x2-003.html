Rockstar Editor Time-Lapse Capture

Records long, controlled time-lapse clips with the in-game Rockstar Editor to compress a full in-game day or week of summit activity into a short, scrubbable video. Helps spot periodic events, lighting changes, or rare visual phenomena.

#rockstar-editor#time-lapse#capture#observation#summit

Why It’s Used

To catch infrequent or time-gated visual events at the mural without continuous live watching.

Tools Required

Rockstar EditorGVGTA VVSVideo scrubber

Success Stories

  • Documented the exact in-game hours of peak fog
  • Captured a rare clear-sky alignment window

False Positives

  • Compression artifacts read as moving objects
  • Editor's altered timescale misjudged as in-game timing
