Pitch-Shift Tempo Decoupling

Slows audio playback without lowering pitch (or vice versa) to scrutinize fast or compressed sounds in detail. Decoupling tempo from pitch reveals structure hidden by speed.

#pitch-shift#tempo#time-stretch#audio#analysis

Why It’s Used

To slow rapid blips or chirps enough to study or transcribe them.

Tools Required

AudacityPPaulstretchRReaper

Success Stories

  • Stretched a brief chirp into an analyzable tonal sweep

False Positives
