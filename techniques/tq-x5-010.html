Procrustes Shape Matching Against Map Outlines

Uses Procrustes superimposition to align glyph silhouettes with candidate landmass or road outlines after scaling and rotation. Residual fit error quantifies how well a shape maps to terrain.

#procrustes#shape-matching#map#geometry#alignment

No corpus evidence. A full-archive audit (24,953 posts, ~343k comments) found this AI-generated entry's distinctive method vocabulary nowhere in thirteen years of community discussion. There is no evidence the community ever used this method. No archive cases are attached to it.

Why It’s Used

To objectively test claims that a glyph traces a real map feature.

Tools Required

PPythonRRQQGIS

Success Stories

  • Low residual fit between the egg glyph and a lake outline
  • Rejected a weak coastline match statistically

False Positives

  • Free rotation and scaling fit almost anything
  • Sparse landmark choice biases the alignment
