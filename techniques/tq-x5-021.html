Caesar-Box Transposition Testing

Arranges candidate glyph text or collectible labels into rectangular grids of varying widths and reads columns to test for transposition ciphers. Each grid width is scored for emergent words.

#transposition#caesar-box#cipher#text#cryptanalysis

Why It’s Used

To uncover messages hidden by columnar rearrangement of letters.

Tools Required

CCyberChefPPythonDdCode

Success Stories

  • A width-5 grid surfaced a readable fragment
  • Column read produced a coherent short word

False Positives

  • Short text yields chance words at many widths
  • Confirmation bias picks the lucky grid
