Photogrammetry of the Mural

Captures dozens of overlapping screenshots of the mural at varying angles and lighting, then runs structure-from-motion to rebuild it as a flat orthorectified texture free of viewing distortion. Investigators measure true line spacing and symbol proportions on the corrected plane.

#mural#structure-from-motion#orthorectify#measurement

Why It’s Used

To remove perspective skew so the mural can be measured accurately.
