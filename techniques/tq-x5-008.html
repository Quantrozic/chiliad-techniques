Fourier Texture Periodicity Detection

Transforms the mural texture into the frequency domain to detect repeating spatial periods that may encode a grid or rhythm. Strong periodic peaks are mapped back to pixel spacing.

#fourier#texture#periodicity#frequency#image-analysis

Why It’s Used

To find hidden regular spacing or tiling inside painted detail.

Tools Required

PNPython NumPyIImageJMMATLAB

Success Stories

  • Detected a 14-pixel repeat in the background wash
  • Frequency peak matched the egg-row spacing

False Positives

  • Tiling of the source texture creates false periods
  • Mip artifacts add spurious peaks
