Loop-Point Seam Forensics

Locates the exact loop boundary of an ambient sound file to detect content hidden at the splice or audible only once per cycle. Examining the seam can expose a non-looping injected sample.

#loop#seam#ambient#audio#forensics

Why It’s Used

To find one-shot sounds concealed inside a repeating ambience loop.

Tools Required

AudacityHEHex editorWVWwise viewer

Success Stories

  • Found a rare non-looped layer in the cult camp ambience

False Positives
