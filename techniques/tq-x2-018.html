Dependency-Graph Prerequisite Mapping

Models each proposed mystery step as a node with prerequisite edges, then topologically sorts to reveal whether a claimed solution sequence is even logically possible. Exposes impossible or circular requirement chains.

#dependency#graph#prerequisite#sequence#logic

Why It’s Used

To test the logical feasibility of a proposed multi-step solution.

Tools Required

GTGraph toolSDStep datasetTSTopological sort

Success Stories

  • Exposed a circular prerequisite in a popular theory
  • Confirmed one sequence is order-valid

False Positives

  • Missing edge making an impossible plan look valid
  • Optional step modeled as mandatory

Example Source

Feasibility audits of community solution walkthroughs
