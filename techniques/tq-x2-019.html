Markov-Chain Sighting Sequence Modeling

Treats ordered sequences of reported events as a Markov chain to test whether one type of sighting statistically predicts another. Detects hidden ordering in otherwise chaotic reports.

#markov#sequence#modeling#statistics#network

Why It’s Used

To detect whether mystery events follow a predictable order.

Tools Required

SEStatistics environmentELEvent logTMTransition matrix tool

Success Stories

  • Found no predictive ordering, debunking a 'ritual sequence'
  • Quantified weather-then-hum likelihood

False Positives

  • Small sample faking strong transitions
  • Mislabeled events corrupting the chain
