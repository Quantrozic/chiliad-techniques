3D Pyramid / Volumetric Projection

Models Mount Chiliad as a 3D pyramid or volumetric shape to project glyph positions and mural alignments in three dimensions rather than on a flat plane.

#3d#projection#geometry

Why It’s Used

Overcomes the distortion of 2D flat overlays by accounting for the mountain’s contours and the viewing angle of each glyph.
