Brightness & Gamma Manipulation

Cranks display brightness, contrast, and gamma, in-game or in an image editor, to reveal faint textures, shadow detail, and night-time markings that are invisible at default settings.

#brightness#gamma#contrast#enhancement

Why It’s Used

Several glyphs and markings sit in shadowed or dimly-lit places; boosting brightness is the cheapest way to check whether a dark surface hides paint the renderer is technically drawing.
