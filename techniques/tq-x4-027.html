Audio Voice-Line Hash Matching

Hash extracted voice files and match them against subtitle and script tables to find unsubtitled or orphaned lines. Unmatched audio may be cut hint dialogue tied to the mystery.

#audio-hash#voice-lines#datamine#orphan-assets

Why It’s Used

To identify recorded but unused dialogue that scripts never play.

Tools Required

AEaudio extractorHThash toolSTsubtitle table

Success Stories

  • Found an orphaned hiker line about 'the lights'

False Positives
