Pathfinding-Cost Route Inference

Applies shortest-path algorithms over the in-game road and trail network to infer the intended or lowest-effort route between mystery sites. Tests whether clue locations form a deliberate traversal.

#pathfinding#route#cost#network#inference

Why It’s Used

To check whether scattered clue sites imply an intended path.

Tools Required

MGMap graphPLPathfinding libraryGVGTA V map data

Success Stories

  • Found a coherent low-cost loop through the glyph locations
  • Disproved a convoluted proposed route

False Positives

  • Forcing a route through unrelated waypoints
  • Terrain shortcuts ignored by the graph
