Orthographic Map-Tile Mosaic Stitching

Captures the in-game map at maximum zoom across a grid and stitches the tiles into one seamless high-resolution orthographic image. The mosaic supports precise distance and bearing measurements between landmarks.

#mosaic#stitching#map-tile#orthographic#cartographic

Why It’s Used

To build an accurate base map for plotting theorized routes and alignments.
