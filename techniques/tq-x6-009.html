Replay-Buffer Anomaly Capture

Keep a rolling replay buffer recording during long play sessions so any unexpected event can be saved retroactively and reviewed. This preserves fleeting anomalies that would otherwise be lost.

#replay-buffer#capture#anomaly#recording

Why It’s Used

To retroactively save rare events the moment they occur.

Tools Required

OROBS replay bufferHMhotkey macroSAstorage arrayVTvideo tagger

Success Stories

  • Saved a brief light anomaly after it appeared
  • Captured an unscripted NPC behavior

False Positives

  • Lens flare saved as an anomaly
  • Buffer drift losing the actual moment

Example Source

Overnight stakeout sessions on the summit
