Bayesian Theory Scoring

Assign prior probabilities to competing mystery hypotheses and update them as evidence accumulates. Produces a ranked, transparent confidence score rather than binary belief.

Why It’s Used

To rationally weight theories by accumulated evidence instead of popularity.
