Cross-Spectral Coherence Analysis

Computes coherence between two audio captures to find frequency bands where they share a common hidden signal. High-coherence bands flag possible synchronized or buried tones.

#coherence#cross-spectral#audio#frequency#forensics

Why It’s Used

To detect a shared concealed tone across separate recordings of the same scene.

Tools Required

MMATLABPSPython SciPyAudacity

Success Stories

  • Found a coherent 60 Hz component in both takes
  • Isolated a shared narrowband hum spike

False Positives

  • Mains hum produces spurious coherence
  • Identical music tracks force false agreement
