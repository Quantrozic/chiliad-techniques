Vigenere Key-Length Kasiski Examination

Searches repeated substrings in candidate ciphertext and factors their separations to estimate a Vigenere key length. Likely key lengths guide frequency-based key recovery.

#vigenere#kasiski#cipher#key-length#cryptanalysis

No corpus evidence. A full-archive audit (24,953 posts, ~343k comments) found this AI-generated entry's distinctive method vocabulary nowhere in thirteen years of community discussion. There is no evidence the community ever used this method. No archive cases are attached to it.

Why It’s Used

To break polyalphabetic ciphers suspected in long clue strings.

False Positives
