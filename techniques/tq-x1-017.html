Reverse-Playback Auditory Inspection

Plays suspect audio backwards to check for reversed speech or hidden phrases, a classic backmasking test. Any candidate phrase is then confirmed by independent listeners to reduce suggestion bias.

#reverse#backmasking#audio#speech#playback

Why It’s Used

To test whether radio or ambient clips contain reversed messages.

Tools Required

AudacityVVLCRReaper

Success Stories

  • Confirmed one DJ outro is gibberish forwards and backwards

False Positives

  • Reversed phonemes hallucinated into meaningful words
