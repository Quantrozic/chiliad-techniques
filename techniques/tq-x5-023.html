Zipf-Law Text Frequency Analysis

Compares the letter and token frequency distribution of compiled clue text against the Zipf and natural-language baselines. Deviations hint at ciphertext rather than plain English.

#zipf#frequency#linguistics#text#cryptanalysis

Why It’s Used

To detect whether collected strings are language, cipher, or noise.

Tools Required

PPythonRRSspreadsheet

Success Stories

  • Flagged one string as flat-distribution ciphertext
  • Confirmed another set matches English

False Positives

  • Tiny samples give unstable distributions
  • Mixed-language text skews the baseline
