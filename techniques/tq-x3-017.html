Beat-Grid Tempo Forensics

Locks ambient loops to a beat grid to measure exact loop length and tempo, looking for musically meaningful intervals or counts that line up with in-game timers like the rain-and-altitude conditions. Tempo ratios are checked for symbolic numbers.
