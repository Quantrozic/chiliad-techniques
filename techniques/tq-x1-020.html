DTMF and Morse Tone Decoding

Scans audio for dual-tone multi-frequency or Morse-code patterns and decodes them to letters or digits. Detected tones are matched against standard frequency tables for verification.

#dtmf#morse#decode#tone#audio

Why It’s Used

To check whether beeps in the soundscape encode phone digits or text.

Tools Required

Sonic VisualiserDDDTMF decoderAudacity

Success Stories

  • Decoded a ringtone as a valid but mundane number sequence

False Positives

  • Musical notes coincidentally hitting DTMF frequencies
