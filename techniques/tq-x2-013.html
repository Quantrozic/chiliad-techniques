Graph-Centrality Clue Ranking

Builds a network graph linking clues, locations, and symbols, then computes centrality measures to rank which nodes are most structurally important to the mystery. Prioritizes investigative effort on high-leverage clues.

Why It’s Used

To identify which clues are the hubs that most connect the evidence web.
