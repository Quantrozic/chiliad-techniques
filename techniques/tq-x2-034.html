Mythological Comparative Templating

Compares the mystery to world myths of sacred mountains, sky-gods, and ascension to test whether it follows a known mythic template. Provides interpretive scaffolding for ambiguous symbols.

#mythology#comparative#template#symbolic#interpretive

Why It’s Used

To map the mystery onto established mythic structures for interpretation.

Tools Required

MRMythology referencesSCSymbol catalogNNotes

Success Stories

  • Connected the ascent to a recognizable axis-mundi motif
  • Distinguished a borrowed myth from an invented one

False Positives

  • Universal motif treated as a specific reference
  • Cultural detail forced to fit
