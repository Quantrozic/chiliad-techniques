Fourier Transform Audio Spectral Analysis

Applies a Fast Fourier Transform to suspicious in-game audio to break it into its frequency components. Hidden tones, carriers, or non-random structure in the spectrum become measurable peaks.

To detect deliberately embedded tones or signals in ambient and radio audio.
